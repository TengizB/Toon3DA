TITLE: Enemy Variety — Eight Original Archetypes (Floors 1-10+)
---

OVERVIEW
========
Eight distinct enemy archetypes that fill out the bestiary the turn-based combat
core (enemy_system.txt) was built to run. Each is a different ANSWER to a
different question the player asks the tile grid: "can I outrun it? can I out-
range it? does the wall protect me? is it worth the ammo?" Together they cover
the role wheel — tank, ranged poke, light skirmisher, straight-line charger,
hovering area-denial floater, armored elite, mechanical gunner, and stealth
ambusher — so any floor's monster mix poses a layered tactical problem rather
than one repeated threat.

These eight map onto eight new enemy sprite-sheet regions (two 2x2 sheets) and
replace the old placeholder roster. This document OWNS: the eight archetype stat
blocks, their AI behavior, XP rewards (mirrored into order_11's GameBalance),
drop tables, floor-appearance bands, and the sprite-sheet TextureRegion mapping.
It does NOT re-define the turn engine, the perception/chain-alert phases, the
DDA LOS check, or Player.applyDamage — those are owned by enemy_system.txt
(IMPLEMENTED). Each archetype is a parameterization of that existing Enemy model
plus, where noted, one new behavior flag.

SETTING / LORE
==============
The run is a descent through a research-and-containment complex whose lower
levels have been overrun by an encroaching organic corruption — part biological
infection, part dimensional bleed. The corporation that ran the facility is gone;
what remains are the things its containment failed to hold and the salvage drones
that were repurposed by the spread. Every archetype is a different stage or limb
of that corruption: reanimated bulk, sensory horrors, ambush predators, and
machines turned hostile. No two answer the same tactical question, so the player
reads the silhouette and immediately knows the threat.

SPRITE-SHEET / TEXTURE MAPPING  (no PNG-per-enemy; two shared 2x2 sheets)
=========================================================================
Each archetype's billboard sprite is a TextureRegion sliced from one of two 2x2
sprite sheets. EnemyConstants.java holds the texture paths; EnemyRenderer slices
the region per enemy type. Each sheet is split into four equal quadrants
(Q1=top-left, Q2=top-right, Q3=bottom-left, Q4=bottom-right). For a sheet of
pixel size W x H, the region for a quadrant is:
  Q1: (0,        0,        W/2, H/2)
  Q2: (W/2,      0,        W/2, H/2)
  Q3: (0,        H/2,      W/2, H/2)
  Q4: (W/2,      H/2,      W/2, H/2)
(TextureRegion origin is top-left in LibGDX region coordinates; confirm against
the actual sheet dimensions when wiring EnemyRenderer.)

  textures/enemies/enemy_sheet_blight.png   (2x2)
    Q1 top-left     -> PLAGUE_HULK
    Q2 top-right    -> EYE_TYRANT
    Q3 bottom-left  -> IRON_STALKER
    Q4 bottom-right -> MIRE_WRAITH

  textures/enemies/enemy_sheet_infernal.png (2x2)
    Q1 top-left     -> GORE_BITER
    Q2 top-right    -> SHELL_BRUTE
    Q3 bottom-left  -> ACID_DRONE

SHARED MODEL RECAP (from enemy_system.txt — every archetype uses these knobs)
=============================================================================
  - hp, attackDamage, attackType {MELEE, RANGED, BOTH}
  - moveEveryNTurns (movement throttle; attacks are NOT throttled when adjacent)
  - alertRadius (Chebyshev proximity wake) + LOS wake
  - rangedRange (tiles) for ranged/both; hitscan in tile space, needs clear LOS
  - states DORMANT/ALERTED/CHASING/ATTACKING
NEW FLAGS introduced by this doc (small additions to Enemy):
  - boolean chargeAttacker         (SHELL_BRUTE: locks a cardinal line and rushes)
  - boolean canBeKnockedBack       (order_15 melee knockback gate; default true)
  - boolean hoverOffset            (EYE_TYRANT / MIRE_WRAITH: billboard drawn
                                     raised off the floor to read as airborne)
  - boolean silentWake             (VOID_SHROUD: wakes without firing the
                                     chain-alert scream that wakes its neighbors)
  - MovementPattern {CHASE, PATROL, AMBUSH, CHARGE, FLOAT} (perception/idle flavor)

ARCHETYPE 1 — PLAGUE_HULK  (spawn '1')   ROLE: slow attrition wall
=============================================================================
SPRITE: enemy_sheet_blight.png, Q1 (top-left). A bloated pink/fleshy spiked blob
  of fused, swollen tissue — a mound of corrupted bulk that lurches forward. Slow
  sway when dormant; lurches one tile per two turns when chasing.
HP: 60        DAMAGE: 14 (melee)      ATTACK TYPE: MELEE, range 1 (Chebyshev==1)
MOVEMENT: CHASE, moveEveryNTurns = 2 (slow tank).
XP REWARD: 8
AI BEHAVIOR: dormant until proximity/LOS wake. Once alerted, never loses interest;
  greedy-steps toward the player every OTHER turn but SWINGS every turn it is
  adjacent (you cannot out-DPS it by clinging — you must create distance). The
  tutorial threat: teaches "back-pedal and reload" because it is slow enough to
  kite but tanky enough to demand commitment.
RESISTANCES: none. Vulnerable to everything (incl. status). The honest baseline.
DROPS: 25% Stim pack '+', 10% Armor shard 'a'. (Roll on death.)
FLOORS: 1-6 (the bread-and-butter early threat; thins out by mid-game).

=============================================================================
ARCHETYPE 2 — EYE_TYRANT  (spawn '2')   ROLE: ranged poke / sightline punisher
=============================================================================
SPRITE: enemy_sheet_blight.png, Q2 (top-right). A floating red sphere with a
  single huge central eye and a fringe of writhing tentacles. hoverOffset = true
  (drawn raised off the floor). Telegraph: the eye constricts/pupil flares one
  turn before it fires (readable tell).
HP: 18        DAMAGE: 8 (ranged)   ATTACK TYPE: RANGED, range 5
MOVEMENT: CHASE/KITE, moveEveryNTurns = 1 (fast), hoverOffset = true.
XP REWARD: 6
AI BEHAVIOR: wakes on LOS at long range (it sees you first). If LOS clear and
  within range -> FIRE (hitscan in tile space, can be dodged via AGILITY, blocked
  by walls/columns). If too close it KITES one tile away to restore range; if too
  far it closes. The "break LOS behind a corner" trainer. Its bolt can apply a low
  chance of a status tick — see RESISTANCES note.
RESISTANCES: normal to everything (common chaff with reach, not durability).
DROPS: 15% Stim pack '+'. Low drop rate (common chaff).
FLOORS: 1-8 (the persistent ranged annoyance throughout).

=============================================================================
ARCHETYPE 3 — GORE_BITER  (spawn '3')   ROLE: fast light-melee skirmisher
=============================================================================
SPRITE: enemy_sheet_infernal.png, Q1 (top-left). An orange-red flesh-maw creature
  — a gaping mouth on a low body with claw/tentacle limbs that scuttles fast and
  low. Twitchy idle; rapid forward lunges when chasing.
HP: 20        DAMAGE: 10 (melee)   ATTACK TYPE: MELEE, range 1
MOVEMENT: CHASE, moveEveryNTurns = 1 (fast).
XP REWARD: 6
AI BEHAVIOR: a fast, fragile pressure unit. Closes every turn and bites the moment
  it is adjacent. Individually trivial, but the level generator places them in
  small packs (2-3 per cluster) and they wake together on the chain-alert scream,
  so they overwhelm a player who back-pedals into a corner. The counter is to thin
  them with a wide weapon (shotgun cone, blast) before they stack onto you, OR
  funnel them into a one-tile choke. Teaches positioning under swarm pressure
  without the wall-ignoring trick.
RESISTANCES: none. Dies to a single shell. Pure tempo threat.
DROPS: 10% Stim '+'. Chaff.
FLOORS: 1-8 (escalating pack sizes deeper in).

=============================================================================
ARCHETYPE 4 — SHELL_BRUTE  (spawn '4')   ROLE: straight-line charger
=============================================================================
SPRITE: enemy_sheet_infernal.png, Q2 (top-right). A brown armored spider/crab
  hybrid — a low, wide, plated body on heavy legs built to rush. Lowers and braces
  (telegraph) one turn before it charges.
HP: 45        DAMAGE: 18 (melee, +knockback on charge hit)   ATTACK TYPE: MELEE 1
MOVEMENT: CHARGE, moveEveryNTurns = 1 normally; CHARGE special below.
XP REWARD: 10
AI BEHAVIOR (chargeAttacker = true): when alerted and the player lies on a clear
  CARDINAL line (same row or column, unobstructed by wall/door/enemy), the
  SHELL_BRUTE enters a one-turn WIND-UP (brace telegraph, no move), then on the
  next turn RUSHES in a straight line covering up to SHELL_BRUTE_CHARGE_TILES
  (default 4) tiles in a single turn, stopping on the tile adjacent to the player
  (or the first wall) and dealing damage + knockback if it reaches melee range. If
  NOT on a clean line, it behaves as an ordinary moveEveryNTurns=1 chaser,
  repositioning to line itself up. The "don't stand in its lane; round a corner to
  cancel the charge" enemy. Charge cancels if the lane is broken during wind-up
  (door closes, another enemy steps in, player strafes out of the row/column).
RESISTANCES: resists knockback (canBeKnockedBack = false — too heavy);
  normal to all damage/status.
DROPS: 20% Stim '+', 15% Armor shard 'a'.
FLOORS: 2-9.

=============================================================================
ARCHETYPE 5 — MIRE_WRAITH  (spawn '5')   ROLE: hovering area-denial acid floater
=============================================================================
SPRITE: enemy_sheet_blight.png, Q4 (bottom-right). A dark yellow/toxic hovering
  blob trailing dripping tentacles. hoverOffset = true. Bulges/swells before an
  acid spit (telegraph).
HP: 70        DAMAGE: 12 (ranged acid spit, applies Poisoned)   ATTACK TYPE: RANGED 4
MOVEMENT: FLOAT (slow drift), moveEveryNTurns = 2, hoverOffset = true. Does NOT
  ignore walls (it's a hovering blob, not incorporeal).
XP REWARD: 16
AI BEHAVIOR: a slow, tanky ranged threat. Wakes on LOS, drifts to maintain a
  medium range, and spits acid (hitscan, needs LOS) that deals direct damage AND
  applies the Poisoned status (order_14, stacks) — so even after you break LOS the
  poison ticks. It does not kite aggressively (slow), so the counter is to close
  the gap fast (it is bad in melee) OR break LOS and out-wait the poison. Punishes
  ranged-only, sit-back play; rewards aggression — the inverse of the EYE_TYRANT.
RESISTANCES: immune to its own Poison; resists poison damage by 50% (it's made of
  the stuff). Normal to fire/physical.
DROPS: 25% Stim '+', 20% Armor shard 'a', 5% Field medkit 'H'.
FLOORS: 4-10.

=============================================================================
ARCHETYPE 6 — IRON_STALKER  (spawn '!' — NEW)   ROLE: armored elite, melee + ranged
=============================================================================
SPRITE: enemy_sheet_blight.png, Q3 (bottom-left). A dark armored spider with a
  massive toothy maw filling its front — heavy plating, eight braced legs, a
  cavernous mouth that both bites and spits. The "you are in trouble now"
  silhouette. Larger billboard scale than the rank-and-file to convey bulk.
HP: 120       DAMAGE: 22 melee / 16 ranged   ATTACK TYPE: BOTH
MOVEMENT: CHASE, moveEveryNTurns = 1 (fast for its size — relentless).
XP REWARD: 35
AI BEHAVIOR (attackType BOTH): the elite. If adjacent -> heavy maw bite (22,
  knockback-resistant). If not adjacent but within IRON_STALKER_RANGE_TILES
  (default 4) with LOS -> ranged spit (16). Otherwise closes aggressively every
  turn. There is no safe range: kiting eats spits, closing eats bites. The intended
  counter is BURST + cover dance + using floor geometry, not raw evasion. Spawns
  sparingly (1, occasionally 2 per floor) as a mini-elite; the level generator
  should treat it as a "budget spike" placement, often guarding a keycard or the
  exit.
RESISTANCES: 25% physical damage reduction (armored); knockback-resistant
  (canBeKnockedBack = false); normal to fire/poison. NOT immune to Stun, but
  Stun duration on it is halved (heavy frame).
DROPS: 60% Armor shard 'a' OR Security vest 'A', 30% Field medkit 'H', plus a
  guaranteed XP lump (already in xpReward). Often gates a pickup behind itself.
FLOORS: 5-10+ (the standard non-boss elite thereafter).

=============================================================================
ARCHETYPE 7 — ACID_DRONE  (spawn '$' — NEW)   ROLE: mechanical ranged gunner
=============================================================================
SPRITE: enemy_sheet_infernal.png, Q3 (bottom-left). A green cybernetic centipede,
  segmented chassis with a gun barrel mounted on the head segment — a salvage
  machine turned hostile by the corruption. Skitters between cover, levels the
  barrel (telegraph) before firing.
HP: 25        DAMAGE: 10 (ranged)   ATTACK TYPE: RANGED, range 4
MOVEMENT: CHASE/KITE, moveEveryNTurns = 1 (fast).
XP REWARD: 8
AI BEHAVIOR: a mechanical ranged unit that behaves like a disciplined skirmisher:
  it prefers to fire from medium range with clean LOS and repositions to keep the
  player in its sightline, KITING when the player closes. Distinct from EYE_TYRANT
  in that it is tougher and tends to relocate to flank corners rather than retreat
  straight back, punishing players who camp a single corner. Because it is a
  machine, it is a candidate for EMP/Stun synergy (order_14) — flag it as
  isMechanical = true if that doc wants a tag.
RESISTANCES: normal to fire/physical; if order_14 implements an EMP/shock status,
  ACID_DRONE takes extended Stun from it (mechanical). Otherwise no resistances.
DROPS: 15% Stim '+', 10% Cell ammo (mechanical salvage).
FLOORS: 3-10.

=============================================================================
ARCHETYPE 8 — VOID_SHROUD  (spawn '^' — NEW)   ROLE: fast stealth melee ambusher
=============================================================================
SPRITE: enemy_sheet_infernal.png, Q4 (bottom-right). A dark, shadowy amorphous
  blob with two glowing eyes — a low-visibility shape that blends into unlit tiles
  until it is almost on top of you.
HP: 30        DAMAGE: 12 (melee)   ATTACK TYPE: MELEE, range 1
MOVEMENT: AMBUSH/CHASE, moveEveryNTurns = 1 (fast), silentWake = true.
XP REWARD: 12
AI BEHAVIOR (silentWake = true): the only archetype that wakes WITHOUT firing the
  chain-alert scream — it stirs silently and does NOT wake its neighbors, so it can
  peel off from a group and reach you while the rest stay dormant. It closes fast
  and bites in melee. Reads dim on the billboard (dark sprite, faint eye glow) so
  it is easy to lose against unlit walls until close — the player should learn to
  watch the minimap and unlit corners. Pairs viciously with a loud threat: while
  you're trading shots with an EYE_TYRANT down the hall, a VOID_SHROUD closes the
  gap unannounced. The counter is awareness and keeping your back to a known-clear
  wall.
RESISTANCES: normal to all damage. No durability gimmick — its edge is the silent
  wake and low visibility, not toughness.
DROPS: 10% Stim '+'. Occasionally drops alongside a loud enemy's loot.
FLOORS: 3-10 (the ambush layer that escalates with depth).

BALANCE NOTES (all numbers placeholder)
=======================================
- HP/damage chosen against the existing shotgun (coeff 0.18, ~20 point-blank):
  EYE_TYRANT/GORE_BITER die in one shell, PLAGUE_HULK ~3, SHELL_BRUTE ~3,
  ACID_DRONE ~2, VOID_SHROUD ~2, MIRE_WRAITH ~4, IRON_STALKER ~6 (before
  perks/stats). This keeps the elite genuinely threatening.
- Each archetype answers a DIFFERENT question, so no two share a single counter:
  PLAGUE_HULK = distance, EYE_TYRANT = cover, GORE_BITER = thin-the-swarm,
  SHELL_BRUTE = lane-break, MIRE_WRAITH = aggression, IRON_STALKER = burst+geometry,
  ACID_DRONE = don't-camp-one-corner, VOID_SHROUD = awareness. Mixing two creates a
  real puzzle (kite the PLAGUE_HULK while a corner blocks the EYE_TYRANT, etc.).
- Telegraphs (eye flare, brace, bulge, barrel-level) are the readability contract:
  every hit the player takes should be one the telegraph warned about. Critical for
  the "I understand why I died" pillar. VOID_SHROUD intentionally bends this — its
  threat is the silent approach, so its readability moves to the minimap and the
  unlit-corner cue, not a wind-up animation.

INTERACTIONS
============
1. TURN ENGINE (enemy_system.txt): all eight are parameterizations of the existing
   Enemy + a few new flags (chargeAttacker, canBeKnockedBack, hoverOffset,
   silentWake, MovementPattern). The two-phase turn, chain-alert, and red-alert
   pulse are unchanged — except VOID_SHROUD's silentWake suppresses the chain-alert
   broadcast for itself only.
2. STATUS EFFECTS (order_14): each archetype's RESISTANCES block maps directly to
   that doc's resist/immunity table (MIRE_WRAITH half-poison, SHELL_BRUTE/
   IRON_STALKER knockback-immune, ACID_DRONE extended-Stun-from-EMP, etc.).
   order_14 owns the mechanism.
3. XP/PERKS (order_11): xpReward feeds awardExperience on death; the table above
   replaces the placeholder values.
4. STATS (order_12): player STRENGTH/MARKSMANSHIP scale damage DEALT to these
   enemies; IRON_STALKER's 25% physical reduction and SHELL_BRUTE knockback-
   immunity are read by the damage/knockback pipeline.
5. MELEE (order_15): knockback pushes only canBeKnockedBack==true enemies one
   tile; SHELL_BRUTE/IRON_STALKER ignore it. Chainsaw vs IRON_STALKER is the
   high-risk melee payoff.
6. MINIMAP / VISIBILITY: VOID_SHROUD's silent wake and dark sprite make minimap
   awareness the primary counter; EYE_TYRANT and MIRE_WRAITH hoverOffset must read
   as airborne on the billboard without breaking z-buffer occlusion.
7. LEVEL GENERATION: floor bands + threat budget drive spawn placement; IRON_STALKER
   as a keycard/exit guard; GORE_BITER and VOID_SHROUD placed in small clusters.
