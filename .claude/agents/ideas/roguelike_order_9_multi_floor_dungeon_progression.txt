OVERVIEW
========
The descent spine: a run is a sequence of procedurally generated floors connected
by the stairs-down tile ('>'). Stepping on the stairs ends the floor, shows a
short summary screen, and loads a new, harder floor. A persistent depth counter
is the run's score; difficulty escalates in tiers (1-3 easy, 4-6 medium, 7-9 hard,
10+ nightmare) by spawning more and tougher enemies and thinning out recovery. The
goal is simple and brutal: descend as deep as you can before you die.

This belongs at the centre of the roguelike because it converts isolated levels
into a run with shape and stakes (Pillar #8, Pacing & Escalation). The stairs
mechanic and the World floor-swap machinery already EXIST (level-transitions-
stairs-down.txt is IMPLEMENTED, with depth counter, fade, and rebuildForLevel).
This doc does NOT re-design the swap — it defines the DIFFICULTY TIER curve that
the swap feeds into the generator, plus the new inter-floor SUMMARY SCREEN that
punctuates each descent. It is the policy layer on top of the existing plumbing.


GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
1. Player spawns on floor 1 at 'p' with the starting loadout. The HUD shows a
   persistent "SUBLEVEL 1" depth readout (already added by level-transitions).
2. Player clears / explores the floor, finds the single '>' stairs tile (placed by
   the generator in the EXIT room, furthest from spawn).
3. Player steps onto '>'. The existing descent flow fires: old-floor enemies take
   their turn (no free escape), World enters FADE_OUT.
4. NEW: at full black, BEFORE generating the next floor, World shows the FLOOR
   SUMMARY screen instead of an instant swap:
       "SUBLEVEL 3 CLEARED
        DEMONS PURGED ...... 12
        SUPPLIES FOUND ..... 5
        TIME ON FLOOR ...... 2:34
        DESCENDING TO SUBLEVEL 4..."
   The summary holds for FLOOR_SUMMARY_SECONDS (or until any key), purely cosmetic
   — it advances zero turns.
5. World computes the next depth = depth + 1, derives the difficulty TIER from
   depth, and calls the generator with (floorSeed, depth, tier). The generator
   (order 10) reads the tier to set enemy count/mix, recovery density, lighting
   darkness, and room count.
6. FADE_IN reveals the new, harder floor. Per-floor RunStats counters reset for
   the summary (kills-this-floor, items-this-floor, floor-start-time); cumulative
   RunStats (order 1) keep accumulating.
7. Loop. There is no stairs UP. Death (HP 0) ends the run; depth reached is the
   score, shown on the order-1 death screen. A theoretical "victory" floor (a deep
   boss / the incursion source) can cap the descent — see OPEN QUESTIONS.


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
=================
Each descent is a held breath. The summary screen is the Hades-style beat between
rooms: a moment to read what you accomplished and brace for worse. Watching the
"SUBLEVEL N" number climb is the entire long-term hook — every run is a question
of "how deep this time." The tier boundaries are felt as gear-shifts: crossing
into floor 4 the player notices more enemies and tighter supplies; floor 7 the
lights start failing and the heavy enemies arrive; floor 10+ is a knife-edge where
one mistake is the run. Because recovery thins as enemies thicken, the player feels
the descent squeezing them — the exact "earned difficulty" the design voice wants,
where death always traces back to "I pushed one floor too far."


TECHNICAL NOTES
===============
DEPENDENCIES (must exist first):
- level-transitions-stairs-down.txt (IMPLEMENTED): the '>' tile, depth counter,
  RunPhase {PLAYING, FADE_OUT, FADE_IN}, rebuildForLevel, seedForDepth. This doc
  EXTENDS that machine; it does not replace it. HARD dependency (already met).
- ORDER 10 (procedural room-based gen): the generator must accept a depth/tier
  parameter and scale its output. This doc defines the tier curve it consumes.
- ORDER 1 (permadeath + RunStats): per-floor counters feed the summary; cumulative
  counters feed the death screen. floorReached is bumped here on each descent.
- ORDER 7 (recovery pickups) + enemy_system.txt: the tier curve sets their spawn
  density and enemy mix. Soft — the curve degrades gracefully to "same difficulty
  every floor" if those are stubbed.

NEW / MODIFIED CLASSES:
- world/DifficultyTier.java (NEW enum, ...toon3d.world):
      EASY (floors 1-3), MEDIUM (4-6), HARD (7-9), NIGHTMARE (10+)
  Plus a static fromDepth(int depth) mapping, and per-tier scalar getters the
  generator reads:
      float enemiesPerRoomBase, enemiesPerRoomVariance
      float toughEnemyShare          (fraction of spawns that are heavy variants)
      float recoveryDensityMultiplier
      float unlitTileShare           (lighting darkness)
      int   roomCountBonus           (longer floors deeper)
  All values are PLACEHOLDER (see BALANCE). Keeping this as a tier enum (not a raw
  per-depth formula) makes the curve readable and tunable; depth 10+ all map to
  NIGHTMARE but can still scale within the tier via a mild per-depth multiplier.
- world/FloorStats.java (NEW, ...toon3d.world): a small per-floor accumulator —
  enemiesKilledThisFloor, itemsFoundThisFloor, floorStartRealSeconds. Reset at
  the start of each floor (in rebuildForLevel). Distinct from order-1 RunStats
  (which is cumulative for the whole run). FloorStats feeds the summary screen
  only; on descent its kills/items are also added into RunStats.
- world/World.java: extend the existing RunPhase machine with a FLOOR_SUMMARY
  phase between FADE_OUT-complete and the level swap:
      ... FADE_OUT (timer done) -> FLOOR_SUMMARY (hold) -> [swap] -> FADE_IN ...
  In FLOOR_SUMMARY: input locked, summaryTimer advances, any-key skips. On exit:
  snapshot FloorStats into the summary text once (no per-frame string build),
  fold FloorStats into RunStats, reset FloorStats, then run the existing swap
  (seedForDepth(depth+1), generator with tier, rebuildForLevel, reposition 'p').
- render/FloorSummaryRenderer.java (NEW, ...toon3d.render) OR fold into the
  existing fade/HUD path: draws the summary text over the black fade. Owns/shares
  a BitmapFont + ShapeRenderer; pre-builds the summary string once when
  FLOOR_SUMMARY is entered (rare event, not a hot path). Implements
  Renderable + Disposable.
- HudRenderer.java: the persistent "SUBLEVEL N" depth readout already exists
  (level-transitions). Optionally add a small tier label / tier color tint
  ("NIGHTMARE" in red) once depth crosses into harder tiers, for dread.

CONSTANTS TO ADD (Constants.java; PLACEHOLDER):
  FLOOR_SUMMARY_SECONDS         = 2.5f     (auto-advance; any-key skips)
  TIER_EASY_MAX_FLOOR           = 3
  TIER_MEDIUM_MAX_FLOOR         = 6
  TIER_HARD_MAX_FLOOR           = 9        (10+ = NIGHTMARE)
  // per-tier scalars live as fields on DifficultyTier, but expose the BASE here:
  ENEMIES_PER_ROOM_BASE_EASY    = 1.5f
  ENEMIES_PER_ROOM_GROWTH       = 0.4f     (avg added per depth, soft-capped)
  ENEMIES_PER_ROOM_SOFT_CAP     = 6f
  RECOVERY_DENSITY_EASY         = 1.0f
  RECOVERY_DENSITY_NIGHTMARE    = 0.4f
  UNLIT_SHARE_EASY              = 0.05f
  UNLIT_SHARE_NIGHTMARE         = 0.22f

TURN-BASED FIT: the summary and fades are cosmetic real-time only — zero turns.
The descent turn itself is a normal tick resolved on the OLD floor before the swap
(inherited from level-transitions; prevents stairs-dodging a lethal enemy turn).
Tier selection is pure integer math (fromDepth). No real-time difficulty drift.

PERSISTENCE ACROSS FLOORS (inherited rule, restated for clarity): HP/AR, Loadout
(order 8), inventory + ammo pouch (order 5) ALL carry down. Keycards RESET per
floor (each floor has its own security). The marine carries wounds and loot
deeper — descent is free, the cost is what you leave behind.


VISUAL DESIGN
=============
- FLOOR SUMMARY screen: drawn over the full-screen black fade (so text pops),
  CRT-amber type matching the procedural HUD / death screen for continuity.
    Header (large amber):  "SUBLEVEL 3 CLEARED"
    Report block (monospace, dotted-leader rows):
        DEMONS PURGED ...... 12
        SUPPLIES FOUND ..... 5
        TIME ON FLOOR ...... 2:34
    Footer (dim, blinking): "DESCENDING TO SUBLEVEL 4 —  [ANY KEY]"
  Reuse the order-1 death-screen scanline overlay for visual family.
- Persistent depth readout (already present): "SUBLEVEL N" in the right HUD panel.
  Add a tier word + color once past EASY: MEDIUM (amber), HARD (orange), NIGHTMARE
  (pulsing red) — a quiet escalation cue the player reads without a tutorial.
- Optional tier-cross flash: when crossing INTO a new tier (entering floor 4, 7,
  10), the descent banner reads "ENTERING <TIER> ZONE" once, in the tier color.
  Cosmetic, ~1s, sells the gear-shift.


BALANCE NOTES (all PLACEHOLDER — tune in playtest)
=============
- TIER CURVE (what the generator reads via DifficultyTier):
    EASY (1-3):       1-2 enemies/room, mostly trash (Corruptor/Ghoul), full
                      recovery density, ~5% unlit tiles, standard room count.
                      Floor 1 must be clearable with the STARTING kit and zero
                      meta-unlocks (Pillar #6).
    MEDIUM (4-6):     2-3 enemies/room, ~40% tougher variants (Crawler/Vortex
                      Eye), ~0.7x recovery density, ~12% unlit, +1 room.
    HARD (7-9):       3-4 enemies/room, ~60% tough + first heavies (Revenant),
                      ~0.5x recovery, ~18% unlit, +2 rooms.
    NIGHTMARE (10+):  4-6 enemies/room (soft-capped), heavies common, ~0.4x
                      recovery, ~22% unlit, mild per-depth scaling continues so
                      floor 15 is meaner than floor 10.
- The two big levers are ENEMY DENSITY (up) and RECOVERY DENSITY (down). Move
  them in opposite directions with depth — that squeeze IS the difficulty.
- Boss cadence: floors that are multiples of 5 are BOSS FLOORS (order 10 places a
  boss room; stairs withheld until the boss dies, per the level-transitions hook).
  This doc just supplies the "is this a boss floor?" check: depth % 5 == 0.
- FLOOR_SUMMARY_SECONDS = 2.5 is long enough to read, skippable so it never annoys
  on a deep run. Time-on-floor is from FloorStats.floorStartRealSeconds (cosmetic
  only — never feeds simulation; the game is turn-based).
- Keep the EASY tier genuinely gentle. The death screen must let a new player say
  "I got to floor 4," not "I died on floor 1 to an unavoidable swarm."


INTERACTIONS
============
1. level-transitions-stairs-down (IMPLEMENTED): this doc inserts the FLOOR_SUMMARY
   phase into that doc's RunPhase machine and supplies the depth->tier policy the
   doc's "Depth difficulty ramp" section left as TODO. It consumes seedForDepth
   and rebuildForLevel unchanged.
2. Order 10 (procedural gen): the generator's depth/tier parameter is fed from
   DifficultyTier.fromDepth(depth). The tier scalars ARE the generator's spawn
   policy inputs. Boss-floor detection (depth % 5 == 0) tells the generator to
   build a boss room and withhold '>' until the objective flag flips.
3. Order 1 (permadeath/RunStats): floorReached bumps here on each descent; the
   per-floor FloorStats fold into the cumulative RunStats; depth reached is the
   death-screen score. The summary screen and death screen share visual style.
4. Order 7 (recovery) + order 5/8 (inventory/loadout): recovery density scales by
   tier; carried HP/AR/weapons/items persist across the swap (run-state, not
   floor-state). Keycards reset per floor.
5. enemy_system.txt: enemy count and the trash/tough/heavy MIX are tier-driven;
   the generator picks spawn markers (1-5) per the tier's toughEnemyShare.
6. boss-encounter-facility-overseer.txt: boss floors (every 5th) gate the stairs;
   the boss-room reward (guaranteed medkit/vest/weapon) is the payoff for the tier
   spike.


OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
1. Is there a FINAL floor / win condition (a deep boss = "you ended the incursion"),
   or is the descent infinite (pure score chase)? Recommend an optional deep boss
   floor (e.g. floor 15 or 20) as a "win" with the run continuing past it for
   score-chasers. Tie to boss-encounter doc.
2. Within NIGHTMARE, how aggressively does per-depth scaling continue? A gentle
   linear creep (e.g. +2% enemy density per floor past 10) vs a hard wall.
   Recommend gentle creep so deep runs end to attrition, not a brick wall.
3. Should the summary screen show CUMULATIVE run totals too (total kills so far),
   or only this floor? Recommend this-floor only for the descent beat; cumulative
   lives on the death screen. Keep the two screens distinct.
4. Partial heal between floors (Hades-style sip)? level-transitions recommends NO
   by default. Could be a NIGHTMARE-only mercy or a boon. Defer to progression.
5. Should crossing a tier boundary be telegraphed pre-descent ("the next sublevel
   is a NIGHTMARE zone") so the player can stock up first? Adds nice agency; flag.
6. Does time-on-floor pause during the inventory menu (order 6) and summary? It is
   cosmetic, so it does not matter mechanically; recommend pausing it during menus
   for an honest "active time" readout.
