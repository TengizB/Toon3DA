PURPOSE
The main, always-on storytelling channel: short one-line messages that pop up DURING
play without stopping the game. Carries ~80% of the story (mostly the AI assistant).
Depends on order-1 (visual language).

WHAT A BARK IS
- One speaker line, 1–2 lines of text, on the story panel from order-1.
- Non-blocking: the game keeps running; the player keeps moving/tapping.
- Auto-dismisses after a hold time, or when the next bark replaces it.
- Never has choices (choices are order-4 "exchanges"). A bark is read-and-forget.

ROGUELIKE STORY-GATING (critical — see order-7 Part A)
This is a permadeath roguelike with a branching route map, but the story is a linear
descent. Barks are gated by the DEEPEST STORY REGION ever reached (persistent), NOT by
the current run. One-shot beats fire ONCE (persistent "seen" flag) and never replay when
the player re-enters a region on a later run. Death never rewinds the story — ORA's tone
and the planet's voice stage only ever move forward. Full model + persistence in order-7.

WHERE IT APPEARS (screen position)
- Lower-third band, centered horizontally, ABOVE the HUD and ABOVE the thumb controls,
  BELOW the weapon sprite. Must never overlap touch buttons or the crosshair/weapon.
- Fixed anchor from StoryUiConstants. Same spot every time so the eye learns it.

QUEUE BEHAVIOR (so it never spams)
- One bark on screen at a time. New barks queue.
- Max hold ~3–5s (tunable), then fade. If a new bark arrives, current one fades early.
- Hard rate limit: no more than 1 bark per N seconds of gameplay (anti-overwhelm; see
  order-6 pacing). Low-priority barks are dropped if the queue is stale.
- Priority tiers: STORY-CRITICAL (mandatory beats) > REACTIVE (kills, rooms) > FLAVOR
  (idle quips). Critical never dropped; flavor dropped first under pressure.

WHEN BARKS POP UP — trigger moments (data-driven, via TickEventBus / game events)
Hook the narrative system to existing game events. Suggested triggers:

  Trigger event                         Typical speaker   Example (see dialog docs)
  ------------------------------------  ----------------  ----------------------------
  Enter a new room / region for 1st time AI               "Objective's down. Let's go."
  First time a new enemy FAMILY appears  AI / Planet       "These 'specimens' scream..."
  Kill (occasional, not every kill)      AI                "See? Contaminant. Sure."
  Low health crosses a threshold         AI                "Ten percent. Bold. Dumb."
  Pick up a log / data-slate             AI (the "take")   one-line lore take (order-5)
  Region gate reached                    Organization      a cold order (rare)
  Deep-strata proximity milestones       Planet            growing whispers/fragments
  Idle too long / backtracking           AI (flavor)       dry quip, low priority
  Boot / reprint                         AI + System       handled by order-3

RULES
- Every bark is tied to a MOMENT (an event). No free-floating lore on a timer.
- "First time only" flags for one-shot lines (don't repeat the room-intro every visit).
- Reactive barks (kills, low HP) are randomized from a small pool and rate-limited so
  they stay fresh and never chatter.
- The AI parrots Organization words early ("contaminant", "asset") and drops them late —
  the bark pool is REGION-GATED so tone tracks the descent (order-5 owns the schedule).
- Planet barks stay tiny and grow across regions (silence → whispers → address).
