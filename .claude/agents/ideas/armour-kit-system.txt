TITLE: Armour Kit & Armour System
CREATED: 2026-05-31
---

OVERVIEW
========
The companion to the medkit system: armour. Two tiers of UAC body-armour spawn
on the floor as walkable decal props (a small vest shard and a full security
vest), and — unlike a medkit — are applied INSTANTLY on pickup with NO turn
cost. Armour points (AR) form a second, parallel health pool that sits IN FRONT
of HP: incoming damage is split, a fraction soaked by armour (consuming it) and
the remainder spent on HP. Where the medkit is the active, turn-costing "patch
under fire" decision, armour is passive, pre-fight insurance — you grab it when
you find it and it quietly buys you survival later. This is the classic Doom
"green/blue armour bonus" loop: instant pickup, no menu, no turn burned, just a
rising cyan AR bar that means you live a little longer.

This doc owns: the two armour pickup props, the AR resource numbers (current /
max armour), the DAMAGE-ABSORPTION SPLIT, the F-key apply-on-pickup flow, and
the armour balance. It does NOT re-define the Player.applyDamage signature, the
HP pool, or the AR HUD bar geometry — those are owned by
scanline-crt-hud-marine-vitals.txt (which already declares `int armor, maxArmor`
and `applyDamage(int amount, float sourceDirX, float sourceDirY)`). This system
INSERTS the absorption math into that existing applyDamage path and CONSUMES the
existing AR bar. It mirrors and reuses the pickup-resolution order defined by
medkit-field-medicine-system.txt so the two pickup types live in one code path.


GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
ACQUIRING + APPLYING (automatic, instant, NO turn cost — the key difference vs
the medkit, which banks a charge then spends a turn to use it):
1. Player steps onto a tile holding an armour pickup ('a' shard or 'A' vest).
2. On move-completion, BEFORE the tick fires, World checks the destination tile
   for a pickup. REUSE the exact same resolution hook the medkit doc defines:
       settle door -> resolve medical pickup -> resolve ARMOUR pickup
       -> resolve other pickups (ammo/keycard) -> finishAction(true, MOVE)
   Armour resolves in this same pre-tick window so enemies see the post-pickup
   world on the same turn.
3. Armour is NOT banked into a stash. It is APPLIED IMMEDIATELY to the AR pool:
       newArmor = min(currentArmor + tierValue, maxArmor)
   The prop tile is converted to plain floor (the decal disappears).
4. A toast routes through the HUD banner ("ARMOUR SHARD +" / "SECURITY VEST +").
   The cyan AR bar glides up via the existing PlayerArmorChanged event.
5. OVERFILL RULE (differs from the medkit's "full stash, leave it" rule): if the
   AR pool is already at maxArmor, the pickup grants NOTHING and is LEFT on the
   floor (tile unchanged), with an "ARMOUR FULL" toast. This lets the player top
   up later. A partial pickup (e.g. AR at 48/50, a shard worth 5) DOES consume
   the prop and clamps to 50 — the player chooses to "waste" the overflow by
   walking over it, exactly like Doom armour bonuses. See OPEN QUESTIONS for the
   alternative (leave partials on the floor too).
6. Walking onto armour is NOT a separate action — it happens during the normal
   step. The step is the only tick. Picking up + applying costs no extra turn.

THE 'F' KEY (apply / inspect — see OPEN QUESTIONS):
- Per the brief, armour is auto-applied on pickup, so in v1 NO key press is
  required to gain armour. The 'F' key is RESERVED here (KEY_ARMOUR_APPLY =
  Input.Keys.F) for the manual-apply variant: if armour is later changed to bank
  as a carried pad (like the medkit stash), 'F' applies one pad with NO turn
  cost (armour is passive protection, not active healing — applying it does not
  advance the world). 'F' is chosen specifically because 'R' is the medkit HEAL
  key and must not be shadowed. For v1 (auto-apply), wire 'F' as a no-op-safe
  inspect/confirm and document it; do not burn a turn on it under any branch.

SPENDING (passive, during any incoming hit — no input):
1. An enemy or hazard calls Player.applyDamage(rawAmount, sourceDirX, sourceDirY)
   (HUD-doc method). This is where the absorption split lives.
2. If currentArmor > 0, split the raw damage: a fraction is soaked by armour and
   subtracted from currentArmor; the remainder is subtracted from HP. See the
   DAMAGE ABSORPTION FORMULA section for the exact split.
3. Armour is consumed as it protects — every hit that armour soaks lowers AR.
   When AR hits 0, all subsequent damage lands fully on HP until armour is
   replenished by another pickup.
4. The marine never gets a turn-cost or animation for being protected; armour is
   invisible plumbing inside the damage pipeline.


ITEM TIERS
==========
TIER 1 — ARMOUR SHARD ('a'): the common drip-feed armour, mirror of the stim.
  - A torn-off plate / vest fragment. Grants ARMOUR_SHARD_VALUE AR (placeholder
    5) on pickup, clamped to maxArmor.
  - Drops frequently: supply rooms, lockers, occasional enemy corpses (Scavenger
    boon synergy, same loot hook the medkit uses). The "chip away toward a full
    bar" beat — you rarely top out from shards alone.
  - Reads as the small, low pickup across a room.

TIER 2 — SECURITY VEST ('A'): the rare big slab, mirror of the field medkit.
  - A full UAC security vest. Grants ARMOUR_VEST_VALUE AR (placeholder 25) on
    pickup, clamped to maxArmor. The "now I can push the next room" item.
  - Rare: 0-2 per floor, hand-placed by the level designer near boss/objective
    rooms or behind a keycard door (pairs with uac-keycard-doors doc).
  - Same instant, no-turn-cost pickup as the shard — the vest isn't slower, just
    a bigger single grant. The tension is scarcity, not action economy.

Both tiers feed the SAME AR pool (capped at ARMOUR_MAX, placeholder 50). They
are not carried as discrete charges — armour is a continuous pool, unlike the
medkit's discrete stash. This is the deliberate design contrast: medicine is a
banked resource you spend on a turn; armour is a buffer you accumulate and bleed.

NO new wall chars, NO grid-format change beyond these two decal symbols.


PICKUP MECHANICS
================
POOL MODEL — continuous buffer, no carry slots:
  - There is NO armour stash and NO carry cap on "kits" because armour is applied
    instantly. The only cap is the POOL cap: ARMOUR_MAX (placeholder 50). You
    cannot carry armour for later; you bank it directly into the AR bar.
  - Picking up while at ARMOUR_MAX: prop is LEFT on the floor, "ARMOUR FULL"
    toast (GAMEPLAY LOOP step 5). Persists until AR drops and the player returns.
  - Picking up while below max: AR rises, clamps to ARMOUR_MAX, prop is consumed
    and the tile cleared.

PICKUP RESOLUTION ORDER (MUST match the medkit doc and tick doc exactly):
  settle door -> resolve medical pickup -> resolve ARMOUR pickup -> resolve other
  pickups -> finishAction(true, MOVE). Both medical and armour mutate the world
  and clear their tiles BEFORE the tick so enemies react to the post-pickup
  state on the same turn. Armour resolves AFTER medical purely for a stable,
  documented order; on any single tile only one pickup char exists, so ordering
  only matters for code structure, not gameplay.

NO MANUAL "PICK UP" KEY: stepping on the tile is the whole interaction (Doom RPG
instant-grab feel). The 'F' key is reserved for the future manual-apply variant
only (see GAMEPLAY LOOP / OPEN QUESTIONS), never as a required pickup step.


DAMAGE ABSORPTION FORMULA
=========================
This is the heart of the system. It lives INSIDE Player.applyDamage and runs on
every hit. The model is Doom's classic split-absorption (a fixed FRACTION of
each hit is soaked by armour, and armour is consumed 1:1 with the damage it
soaks), with a floor so armour can never fully negate a hit.

Inputs:  rawDamage (int, > 0), currentArmor (int, >= 0)
Constants: ARMOUR_ABSORB_FRACTION (placeholder 0.5f), ARMOUR_MIN_HP_FRACTION
           (placeholder 0.0f — see balance note on guaranteed chip damage)

Algorithm (compute in a GameMath helper, NOT inline):
  if currentArmor <= 0:
      hpDamage = rawDamage; armorDamage = 0
  else:
      // amount of the hit armour WOULD like to soak
      desiredSoak = round(rawDamage * ARMOUR_ABSORB_FRACTION)
      // armour can only soak as much as it has left
      armorDamage = min(desiredSoak, currentArmor)
      hpDamage    = rawDamage - armorDamage
  // optional guaranteed-chip floor: never let armour reduce a hit below a
  // minimum HP bite (prevents an armour wall trivialising every hit):
  minHpDamage = ceil(rawDamage * ARMOUR_MIN_HP_FRACTION)
  if hpDamage < minHpDamage:
      // shift the difference back onto HP, refund it to armour
      shift = minHpDamage - hpDamage
      armorDamage = max(0, armorDamage - shift)
      hpDamage    = minHpDamage
  currentArmor = currentArmor - armorDamage
  currentHealth = currentHealth - hpDamage   // existing HP clamp/death follows

WORKED EXAMPLES (ARMOUR_ABSORB_FRACTION = 0.5, ARMOUR_MIN_HP_FRACTION = 0):
  - rawDamage 10, AR 50  -> armorDamage 5, hpDamage 5  -> AR 45, HP -5.
  - rawDamage 10, AR 3   -> desiredSoak 5, but AR only 3 -> armorDamage 3,
                            hpDamage 7 -> AR 0, HP -7. (Armour partially soaks
                            its last points, then breaks.)
  - rawDamage 10, AR 0   -> hpDamage 10. (No armour, full hit.)
  Net: armour HALVES incoming damage while it lasts, and is drained by exactly
  the amount it soaks. 50 AR at 0.5 absorption buys ~100 raw damage of HP
  protection over its lifetime (50 soaked / 0.5) — a meaningful but finite slab.

WHY A FRACTION, NOT A FLAT REDUCTION: a flat "-3 per hit" makes armour worthless
vs big hits and overpowered vs swarms of weak hits. A fraction scales cleanly
with any enemy damage the enemy_system doc throws at it and is the readable Doom
behaviour the genre expects. Put the whole thing in GameMath.applyArmourSplit so
it is one tested formula with a derivation comment, never duplicated.

VISUAL DESIGN
=============
FLOOR PROPS: see TILE SYMBOLS. Steel/gunmetal bodies, cyan rim-light + cyan glow
pool, hard geometric silhouette (vs the rounded white medical kits). The cyan
glow pool is the read-at-distance cue in dark ('u'/unlit) rooms.

HUD: AR bar is the existing cyan gradient bar in the left panel (deep cyan ->
bright cyan -> near-white at full), sitting directly under the red HP bar. On
gain: bar glides up + a one-frame brighter cyan flash on the bar fill. On
absorb (taking a hit that armour soaks): a short cyan tick-down + a faint cyan
edge pulse on the bar to telegraph "armour just saved you." When AR reaches 0,
the bar empties and a subtle one-shot grey "ARMOUR DEPLETED" micro-toast can fire
(optional; flag as tunable) so the player KNOWS the buffer is gone and the next
hit is full HP — readability of why-you-died is a design pillar.

TOASTS (existing banner): "ARMOUR SHARD +", "SECURITY VEST +", "ARMOUR FULL", and
optionally "ARMOUR DEPLETED". All tinted cyan to reinforce the colour language.

NO new 3D effect, NO screen-space shader. Everything rides the existing prop
billboard pipeline + the existing HUD bar.


BALANCE NOTES
=============
All numbers are PLACEHOLDERS pending playtest against enemy damage output.

- ARMOUR_MAX 50 + ARMOUR_ABSORB_FRACTION 0.5 means a full bar absorbs 50 AR worth
  of soak, i.e. it eats ~100 raw damage of HP protection before breaking. Against
  a starting HP pool of ~20-30 (progression doc), full armour roughly TRIPLES
  effective survivability while it lasts — a big, deliberate swing, which is why
  armour is scarcer than medicine and never carryable.
- ABSORB FRACTION is the master lever. 0.5 = "halves hits." Lower it (0.33) if
  armour feels oppressive; raise it (0.66) only if armour spawns rarely. Tune
  absorb fraction BEFORE touching tier values — fraction changes the texture of
  every fight; tier values only change how often you top up.
- SHARD 5 / VEST 25 vs MAX 50: a vest is 50% of a full bar, a shard 10%. So a
  player chips up with shards and resets with a vest, mirroring stim/medkit
  pacing exactly — intentional symmetry so the two systems feel like siblings.
- GUARANTEED CHIP (ARMOUR_MIN_HP_FRACTION): default 0 (off). If playtesters find
  fully-armoured play trivialises early rooms, set ~0.2 so every hit still bites
  at least 20% to HP — keeps HP relevant and prevents an armour turtle. Flag as
  the first knob to try if armour feels too safe.
- OVERFILL: walking over a shard at 48/50 banks 2 and "wastes" 3 (Doom bonus
  behaviour). This is intended minor waste, not a bug — it rewards picking up
  armour at the RIGHT time, not hoarding routes. The full-bar case leaves the
  prop on the floor so a perfectly-full player loses nothing by ignoring it.
- DROP RATES (placeholder): shard ~1 per 2-3 rooms + small enemy-corpse chance
  (Scavenger boon scales it); vest 0-2 per floor, hand-placed. Keep TOTAL armour
  granted per floor below the total damage a careful player takes, so AR stays a
  managed buffer, not a permanent shield.
- NO TURN COST is the core contrast with the medkit. Armour is "free" to gain,
  so its cost is OPPORTUNITY (scarcity + you can't carry it) and the fact it
  only delays death, never reverses it (it never adds HP). The medkit reverses
  damage at a turn cost; armour prevents damage for free but runs out. The two
  must be tuned together: if both are abundant the marine is unkillable.


INTERACTIONS
============
1. MEDKIT / FIELD MEDICINE (medkit-field-medicine-system doc): the two are
   deliberate siblings sharing the pickup-resolution order, the prop billboard
   path, the Scavenger corpse-drop hook, and the toast banner. They are the
   complementary halves of survival: armour PREVENTS damage (passive, free, runs
   out), medkit REVERSES damage (active, one turn, banked). A common decision:
   low HP + full armour -> push on; low HP + no armour -> retreat and HEAL. They
   must be balanced against each other, not in isolation.
2. HUD (scanline-crt-hud-marine-vitals doc): consumes the already-declared
   armor/maxArmor fields, getArmorFraction(), the cyan AR bar, and a
   PlayerArmorChanged event for the bar glide/flash. No new HUD widget; this
   doc just supplies the gameplay reasons the bar moves.
3. ENEMIES (enemy_system doc): the absorption split runs inside applyDamage, so
   EVERY enemy hit (Corruptor melee, Vortex Eye ranged) is auto-split. The
   "armoured Corruptor" enemy is the marine's mirror — consider giving enemies a
   read-only armour value through the same GameMath.applyArmourSplit so player
   weapons and enemy armour share one tested formula (OPEN QUESTION).
4. HAZARDS (hazard-chain-reactions doc): decide whether fire/toxic ticks route
   through applyDamage (and thus get absorbed by armour) or bypass armour as
   "environmental" damage. Recommendation: hazards DO go through applyArmourSplit
   for consistency, but with a possible separate hazard absorb fraction later.
5. PROGRESSION / BOONS (levelup-stim-boon-choice doc): a future "Plating" boon
   could raise ARMOUR_MAX or ARMOUR_ABSORB_FRACTION; a "Salvager" boon could add
   armour to the corpse loot table. All read the ARMOUR_* constants — no new
   plumbing. maxArmor changes must re-clamp current armor.
6. LEVEL DESIGN: 'a'/'A' give the designer the same pacing tool as 'h'/'H' — a
   vest before a hard room signals "gear up"; a vest behind a keycard door
   (uac-keycard-doors doc) rewards exploration. A shard on an oil pool ('O') or
   beside a flickering tile makes a tense, readable grab.


OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
1. OVERFILL behaviour: consume-and-waste (Doom bonus style, recommended for v1)
   vs leave-partials-on-floor (only consume if the full value fits)? Recommend
   consume-and-waste for v1 — simpler, more Doom-like, rewards timing.
2. POOL vs BANKED armour: v1 applies instantly to a pool (no carry). Should a
   "carried armour pad" variant exist (bank it, apply with 'F', still no turn
   cost)? Recommend NO for v1 — instant-apply is the cleaner contrast with the
   medkit's banked stash. The F key is reserved in case this is revisited.
3. ABSORB MODEL: fixed fraction (recommended) vs Doom-2's two-tier green(1/3)/
   blue(1/2) armour where the tier sets the fraction? A two-tier fraction (shard
   = green = 0.33 absorb, vest = blue = 0.5 absorb) is a tempting depth knob but
   needs the AR bar to track "which absorb class is active." Defer to v2.
4. GUARANTEED CHIP: ship with ARMOUR_MIN_HP_FRACTION = 0 (off) and only enable if
   armour trivialises fights? Recommend yes — start off, tune in only if needed.
5. ENEMY ARMOUR: should enemies reuse GameMath.applyArmourSplit so the formula is
   shared? Recommend yes for consistency, but that is the enemy_system doc's call
   to wire; this doc just keeps the formula pure and reusable.
6. HAZARD damage: absorbed by armour or environmental-bypass? Recommend absorbed
   (through applyArmourSplit) for v1; revisit a hazard-specific fraction later.
7. "ARMOUR DEPLETED" micro-toast on AR hitting 0: helpful readability or HUD
   noise? Recommend a single subtle one-shot; flag tunable so it can be muted.
