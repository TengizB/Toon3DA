OVERVIEW
========
The GORE NEST is a SIGNATURE room where the demonic incursion physically won: the facility walls have
been consumed by a living flesh membrane (gore wall 'G') fringed by corroded, rusted steel (rust wall
'j'), and the floor is a carpet of corpses, blood, and dark bile around a ruptured bio-pod at the centre
— the wound the demons crawled out of. It fills a gap in the room catalog: every existing special room is
a CLEAN facility function (server, medical, armory, cryo, reactor, command, containment, research,
observatory) or GENERIC storage (salvage, supply). None is the AFTERMATH — the "this section is lost"
dread beat. Its identity is built entirely from WALLS and ENVIRONMENT DECALS (the two things the brief
asked for), reusing existing art only: it claims the currently-unclaimed gore wall 'G' as its signature,
so it costs ZERO new symbols and ZERO new sprites (no RECIPE A).

GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
1. Generator places rooms and runs the seeded weighted roulette (STEP A). On a floor at depth >= 3 with a
   candidate room >= 6x6 interior and no gore nest yet placed, GORE_NEST competes like any blueprint.
2. If selected -> build() stamps the room: floor lighting, gore/rust wall theming, then the nest prop pass.
3. Player enters a corridor that visibly darkens (nest rooms bias toward unlit/flickering floor). The far
   wall is wet red flesh, not steel. Player reads "something happened here" before any enemy is seen.
4. A ruptured bio-pod '&' sits near the room centroid; a dense ring of corpse 'm' + blood '.'/'s' decals
   surrounds it; radioactive barrels 'g' and toppled crates 'C' clutter the walls; oil 'O' bile pools bleed
   from the flesh seams. A walkable ring is always left around the nest (see TECHNICAL NOTES pathing rule).
5. Because this room is the natural home of the necrotic faction, the encounter budget tends to seed
   melee-heavy packs here (Ghoul '~', Crawler 'z', Blight Corruptor '*', Revenant 'K') — the room reads as
   their den. (Spawn placement stays the generator's global FIXED-symbol pass; the room only sets the mood.)
6. Player fights through, loots any pickups the global pass dropped, and exits. The nest is a memorable,
   dread-heavy landmark on the minimap and in memory.

PLAYER EXPERIENCE
=================
Dread and recognition. Every other room says "UAC ran experiments here"; the gore nest says "the
experiment got out, and this is where it nested." The wet-flesh far wall is an instant, wordless
storytelling beat — the player slows down, checks corners, expects a swarm. The decision it creates: push
in for the loot the generator seeded, or skirt the doorway and pick the pack apart from the corridor
(cardinal-line kiting) rather than walking into a corpse-strewn kill-box. It rewards the tactical
"read the room before entering" instinct the game already teaches with dark/flickering floor.

TECHNICAL NOTES
===============
Recommendation: SIGNATURE room (RoomKind.SIGNATURE). It must PIN the gore wall so the flesh membrane
actually renders (a GENERIC room has an empty demand and could not claim 'G'); pinning is the whole point.

NO NEW SYMBOLS, NO NEW SPRITES. All symbols and sprites below already exist (verified against
docs/tile-symbols.txt and tileset/TilesetRegistries). This is RECIPE B (a signature room with a bespoke
prop pass), NOT RECIPE A.

Claimed symbol -> sprite pairs (the room's symbolDemand().requiredSprites, all legacy bindings so the
allocator reserves the exact intended art via order-4 Step 2):
    WALLS (accent walls — the signature):
      'G' -> "wall_gore"      (primary perimeter: wet demonic flesh membrane — the room's face)
      'j' -> "wall_rust"      (accent: corroded steel where flesh meets the old bulkhead)
    SOLID PROPS (reused; the "nest" clutter):
      '&' -> "prop_biopod"    (ruptured specimen pod at the nest centre — the breach origin)
      'g' -> "prop_radioactive_barrel" (leaking drums that fed the rot)
      'C' -> "prop_crate"     (toppled supply crates — abandoned barricade debris)
    FLOOR DECALS (the environment heart of the room — dense):
      'm' -> "decal_corpse"   (corpse clusters)
      '.' -> "decal_blood"    (pooled blood)
      's' -> "decal_blood_alt"(spatter variant, for visual break-up)
      'O' -> "decal_oil"      (dark bile/fluid seep)

Clash safety: '&', 'C', '.', 'O', 'm' are also demanded by other signature rooms (cryo/containment/
medical/etc.) but ALL to the SAME sprite id — the allocator's Step-2 clash resolution only reroutes on
same-symbol/DIFFERENT-sprite, so there is no clash. 'G' and 'j' are currently claimed by NO signature
room (they live only in the generic-variety / harsh-depth pools), so reserving them here is free.

RoomBlueprint registration (drop-in, matching the file's existing style):
    registry.register(new LambdaRoomBlueprint(ID_GORE_NEST, RoomKind.SIGNATURE,
            RoomSymbolDemand.of(demand(
                    'G', "wall_gore",
                    'j', "wall_rust",
                    '&', "prop_biopod",
                    'g', "prop_radioactive_barrel",
                    'C', "prop_crate",

            context -> context.dungeonDepth()  >= LevelGenConstants.LEVEL_GEN_GORE_NEST_MIN_DEPTH
                    && context.interiorWidth()  >= LevelGenConstants.LEVEL_GEN_GORE_NEST_MIN_WIDTH
                    && context.interiorHeight() >= LevelGenConstants.LEVEL_GEN_GORE_NEST_MIN_HEIGHT
                    && context.alreadyPlacedOfThisKind() < LevelGenConstants.LEVEL_GEN_GORE_NEST_MAX,
            context -> LevelGenConstants.LEVEL_GEN_GORE_NEST_CHANCE,
            context -> {
                context.assignFloorLighting();
                context.themeNewRoomWalls();      // themeNewRoomWallsForRoom handles the GORE_NEST case
                context.placeGoreNestProps();
            }));

Prop layout concept (placeGoreNestProps):
  - Compute the room centroid tile. Place ONE ruptured bio-pod '&' at (or adjacent to, if centroid is on a
    door approach) the centroid — the single "origin" anchor, like the observatory's one gravity-well core.
  - Corpse ring: scatter corpse 'm' and blood '.'/'s' decals on the floor ring immediately around the pod
    (radius 1-2 tiles), at a HIGH decal density so the nest reads as a kill-site. Bile 'O' pools mixed in.
  - Wall debris: along interior walls, sparsely place radioactive barrels 'g' and toppled crates 'C'
    (reuse the standard perimeter-prop placement pattern; these are the "abandoned facility" leftovers).
  - PATHING RULE (mandatory): never fully seal the walkable ring around the nest and never block a door
    approach. Decals ('m'/'.'/'s'/'O') are walkable so they carry no collision risk; only the solid props
    ('&'/'g'/'C') need the standard "leave a 1-tile clearance / don't block the only path" guard the other
    prop passes already use. Reuse the existing prop-placement clearance check — do not invent a new one.

VISUAL DESIGN
=============
  - Walls: gore wall 'G' (existing high-res procedural flesh: metaball membrane, wet sheen, sinew vessels,
    pustules, blood-drip gashes, bone nodules) dominates the perimeter; rust wall 'j' breaks it up so the
    room reads as flesh EATING metal, not a solid meat cube. This is the room's signature silhouette.
  - Floor: bias toward unlit ('u') and flickering ('f') floor via assignFloorLighting for the GORE_NEST
    tag (darker than a clinical room) — sells the dread and makes the wet 'G' sheen pop against shadow.
  - Centre: the ruptured bio-pod '&' (green fluid tube, cracked glass) as the visual focal point, ringed by
    corpse 'm' billboards lying flat and dark blood '.'/'s' pools — a clear "nest" read at a glance.
  - Palette: deep reds (flesh) + oxide orange/brown (rust) + sickly green (barrels/pod) + black bile pools.
    Distinct from every existing room's cool steel/cyan/clinical-white.
  - No HUD changes. Purely environmental. Reads instantly on the raycast far wall from the doorway.

INTERACTIONS
============
  1. Allocator harsh-depth rule (R5): gore/rust carry the PALETTE_HARSH_THEME_TAGS ("gore"/"rust"). Deeper
     floors already trend grittier via the variety pool; this room CONCENTRATES that trend into one
     landmark rather than scattering it — coherent with the existing depth-influence system, not fighting it.
  2. Encounter budget / necrotic faction: the room is the thematic den of the necrotic spawns
     (~ Ghoul, z Crawler, K Revenant, * Blight Corruptor). Spawn placement stays the generator's global
     FIXED-symbol pass, but designers can bias the budget planner to seed a melee pack in a gore nest for a
     signature "swarm out of the flesh" encounter. Cardinal-line kiting from the doorway is the counter-play.
  3. Hazard tiles (fire 'i' / toxic 'q'): the nest is an ideal hand-seed OR runtime target for a toxic pool
     'q' (a Plague Hulk dying here leaves one). Its walkable decal floor and dark lighting make it the
     natural home for area-denial dread — the room and the hazard system reinforce each other.
  4. Freed-symbol mechanic: on floors WITHOUT a gore nest, 'G' and 'j' fall back into the generic-variety
     pool exactly as today (no regression); when the nest IS placed, its demand reserves them so a random
     corridor never accidentally sprouts a lone flesh wall next to a placed nest.
  5. Fog-of-war / minimap: as a rare, visually distinct landmark it aids player mental-mapping (the
     "meat room" is a memorable node) — synergises with any minimap-memory feature.

OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
  - Should the ruptured bio-pod '&' at the centre optionally be a guaranteed loot anchor (drop a stim/ammo
    beside it) to reward pushing into the kill-box, or stay purely atmospheric? (Leaning atmospheric;
    global pickup pass already handles loot.)
  - MIN_DEPTH = 3 vs 4: is floor 3 too early for the "lost section" beat, or does earlier corruption raise
    tension sooner? Playtest.
  - Should MAX be 1 (set-piece) or 2 (let deep floors feel overrun)? Starting at 1; consider scaling to 2
    at depth >= 7 via the eligibility lambda if deep floors want more dread.
  - Wall theming: reuse the shared themeNewRoomWallsForRoom switch with a GORE_NEST case (less code) vs a
    dedicated themeGoreNestWallsForRoom (cleaner if the raised-density fill logic diverges too much from the
    other rooms' sparse accenting). Implementer's call; dedicated helper is safer if fill density is special.
  - Does the darker floor bias hurt readability of the corpse decals? May need to lift a few centre tiles to
    normal 'l' lighting around the nest so the focal cluster stays legible.
