TITLE: Player Health, Damage & Hit Feedback
---

OVERVIEW
========
The foundation of all combat: the marine has a finite HP pool, enemies deal
damage when they attack, and at 0 HP the marine dies (triggering order-1
permadeath). This document owns the DAMAGE PIPELINE — how raw damage becomes lost
HP, how armor soaks part of it, how brief post-hit invincibility prevents
multi-hit chain-death, and how every hit is made readable (screen flash, floating
damage numbers, directional indicator). Without this, enemies (already
implemented) have nothing to hurt and the HP bar is cosmetic.

It belongs here because legible, fair damage is the precondition for permadeath
feeling earned rather than cheap. The player must always understand WHY they took
damage and from WHERE.

OWNERSHIP / WHAT ALREADY EXISTS — read before implementing:
- scanline-crt-hud-marine-vitals.txt declares the HP/AR pool fields
  (int health, maxHealth, armor, maxArmor) and the method signature
  Player.applyDamage(int amount, float sourceDirX, float sourceDirY). DO NOT
  redefine these. This doc INSERTS behavior into that applyDamage path.
- armour-kit-system.txt (IMPLEMENTED) owns the damage ABSORPTION SPLIT (how raw
  damage is divided between AR and HP). DO NOT duplicate that math; call into it.
- enemy_system.txt (IMPLEMENTED) defines the enemy ATTACK branch that calls
  applyDamage, and melee-vs-ranged attack gating.
This doc fills the gaps the brief asks for: HP definition + death threshold,
invincibility frames, hit-flash, floating damage numbers, and how melee vs ranged
damage values are sourced.

GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
1. During the enemy ACTION phase (order 2), an alerted enemy that can attack calls
   Player.applyDamage(rawAmount, sourceDirX, sourceDirY). sourceDir is the unit
   vector from the enemy tile to the player tile (used for directional feedback).
2. INVINCIBILITY CHECK (new): if the player is currently in i-frames
   (invincibleTicksRemaining > 0), the hit is IGNORED (zero damage, no feedback
   flash, but the attack still "happened" for AI purposes). Otherwise continue.
3. ABSORPTION SPLIT (existing, armour-kit-system.txt): if armor > 0, a fraction of
   rawAmount is soaked by AR (lowering AR), the remainder hits HP. If armor == 0,
   the full rawAmount hits HP.
4. HP is reduced: health = max(0, health - hpPortion). RunStats.totalDamageTaken
   += hpPortion (+ armor portion if counted — see OPEN QUESTIONS).
5. FEEDBACK (cosmetic, render-only, advances no turn):
     - Full-screen red vignette/flash pulses (alpha scales with damage fraction).
     - A floating damage number spawns in 3D space above the player's view edge
       biased toward sourceDir (so the player reads "hit from the left").
     - The Doom-face portrait swaps to a bloodier frame; HP bar glides down (owned
       by the HUD doc — fire a PlayerDamaged event).
6. INVINCIBILITY GRANT (new): set invincibleTicksRemaining =
   PLAYER_INVINCIBLE_TICKS after a damaging hit, so the player cannot be chain-
   killed by multiple enemies in the SAME tick beyond the configured budget. The
   counter decrements once per tick (a tiny TickSubscriber or inside applyDamage's
   owning entity update).
7. DEATH CHECK: handled by order 1 AFTER the full tick resolves — when
   health == 0, isDead() returns true and the run ends. applyDamage itself does
   NOT trigger the death screen mid-tick.

PLAYER EXPERIENCE
=================
Getting hit must be visceral and unambiguous. The red flash + directional damage
number tells the player instantly "you lost 14 HP, from the imp on your left."
HP scarcity drives the entire risk economy: with no regeneration (only medkits),
every point of HP is a spent resource, and the player constantly weighs "push or
retreat." Invincibility frames exist purely to keep death FAIR — they prevent the
unreadable "three enemies all hit me at once and I died from full HP in one tick"
death that would make permadeath feel arbitrary. The player should never die
without at least one tick of warning at low HP.

  HUD bars and Doom-face portrait. (HUD bar geometry exists in CLAUDE.md already.)
- armour-kit-system.txt (IMPLEMENTED): the absorption split.
- order 2 (turn system) + enemy_system.txt (IMPLEMENTED): the caller of

- order 1 (permadeath): consumes isDead() and the hit-flash overlay.

New / modified:
- entity/Player.java: add fields (per HUD doc if not already present)
    int health, maxHealth;            // owned by HUD doc — do not duplicate
    int invincibleTicksRemaining;     // NEW, this doc
  Add isDead() => health <= 0. Add invincibility decrement (once per tick).
  NEVER mutate health outside the applyDamage / heal paths.
- Damage pipeline lives in Player.applyDamage (HUD-doc method): order is
    (a) if invincibleTicksRemaining > 0 -> return early (no damage, no feedback)
    (b) absorption split (armour-kit-system.txt)
    (c) subtract HP, clamp at 0
    (d) record RunStats.totalDamageTaken
    (e) fire PlayerDamaged event (drives HUD + flash + damage number)
    (f) set invincibleTicksRemaining = PLAYER_INVINCIBLE_TICKS
- render/DamageFlashRenderer.java (new, ...toon3d.render) OR fold into HudRenderer:
  a full-screen red quad whose alpha decays over real time after a PlayerDamaged
  event. Pre-allocated white/red pixel texture; NO allocation in render(). This
  same overlay is reused by order-1's killing-hit flash.
- render/FloatingDamageRenderer.java (new): a small fixed-capacity object pool of
  floating number labels (e.g. 8 slots). On PlayerDamaged, activate one slot with
  the value, a screen position biased toward sourceDir, and a real-time lifetime;
  it rises and fades. Pool is pre-allocated — NO per-hit allocation. (This is the
  player-damage counterpart to enemy-hit numbers in enemy-hit-impact-effects.txt;
  share that pool/renderer if it already exists.)
- Damage values are SOURCED from the enemy, not invented here: each enemy type in
  enemy_system.txt carries a melee/ranged damage stat. Melee enemies deal their
  melee value when Chebyshev-adjacent; ranged enemies deal their ranged value when
  in range + LOS. This doc only defines the PIPELINE that consumes those values.

Constants to add (Constants.java):
- PLAYER_MAX_HEALTH = 100            (placeholder; may already be in HUD doc)
- PLAYER_INVINCIBLE_TICKS = 0 or 1   (see BALANCE — recommend 0 for v1, 1 if
                                       multi-enemy ticks feel unfair)
- DAMAGE_FLASH_DURATION_SECONDS = 0.25f
- DAMAGE_FLASH_MAX_ALPHA = 0.55f
- DAMAGE_NUMBER_LIFETIME_SECONDS = 0.9f
- DAMAGE_NUMBER_RISE_WORLD_UNITS = 60f
- DAMAGE_NUMBER_POOL_SIZE = 8

Turn-based fit: damage application is instantaneous within the enemy's turn (no
animation, no real-time cooldown). All feedback (flash, numbers, face swap) is
cosmetic real-time decay that advances ZERO turns. Invincibility is counted in
TICKS, never seconds, honoring the "no second-based cooldowns" rule.

VISUAL DESIGN
=============
- Red flash: full-screen red vignette, denser at screen edges, peaking at
  DAMAGE_FLASH_MAX_ALPHA scaled by (damage / maxHealth), decaying to 0 over
  DAMAGE_FLASH_DURATION_SECONDS. Stacks/refreshes on repeat hits.
- Floating damage number: chunky pixel font, blood-red, e.g. "-14", spawned near
  the top-center of the 3D view, nudged left/right/up by sourceDir so the number
  drifts in from the direction of the hit. Rises DAMAGE_NUMBER_RISE_WORLD_UNITS
  and fades over its lifetime.
- Directional damage indicator (optional v1.1): a brief red arc on the screen
  edge pointing toward sourceDir — strong Doom-style "you were hit from here" cue.
- HP bar + Doom-face: owned by scanline-crt-hud-marine-vitals.txt. This doc only
  fires the PlayerDamaged / PlayerHealed events that drive them.
- At critical HP (<= 20%) the HUD doc's portrait goes "critical" and the edge
  vignette may pulse faintly red even between hits (low-health heartbeat) — a fair
  warning before a permadeath.

BALANCE NOTES
=============
- PLAYER_MAX_HEALTH = 100 with starting enemy melee in the ~8-16 range means a
  fresh marine survives 6-12 unanswered hits — enough that death is always
  preceded by a readable low-HP state. Verify no enemy combination one-shots a
  full-HP marine in a single tick (the order-1 fairness rule).
- PLAYER_INVINCIBLE_TICKS: recommend 0 for v1 (turn-based games rarely need
  i-frames since the player chooses to walk into danger). Set to 1 ONLY if
  multi-enemy ticks produce unfair burst deaths in playtest. If used, i-frames
  must NOT make the player invincible to a deliberate second turn of standing in
  danger — they only cap damage within concurrent same-tick attacks.
- No passive HP regen: healing is medkits only (medkit-field-medicine-system.txt).
  This keeps HP a scarce, spent resource per the roguelike economy.
- Flag ALL numbers above as placeholders pending playtest.

INTERACTIONS
============
1. Armour (armour-kit-system.txt): applyDamage routes through the absorption split
   before touching HP. AR is the front layer; HP is the back layer.
2. Enemy system (enemy_system.txt): enemies are the callers; each type's melee /
   ranged damage stat feeds rawAmount. Melee needs adjacency; ranged needs range +
   LOS.
3. Permadeath (order 1): isDead() is the death trigger, checked after the full
   tick; the hit-flash overlay is reused for the killing blow.
4. HUD vitals (scanline-crt-hud-marine-vitals.txt): consumes PlayerDamaged /
   PlayerHealed events for the HP bar glide and Doom-face swap.
5. Medkits (medkit-field-medicine-system.txt): the heal path (USE a medkit costs a
   turn) raises HP and fires PlayerHealed; the inverse of this pipeline.
6. Turn system (order 2): invincibleTicksRemaining decrements once per tick via a
   subscriber or the player's per-tick update.

OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
- Does totalDamageTaken count armor-soaked damage, or only HP lost? Recommend
  counting HP lost only (the report metric players care about is HP), but expose
  both if the death screen wants "damage absorbed by armor" as a flex stat.
- i-frames in ticks: 0 or 1 for v1? Lean 0; promote to 1 only on playtest evidence
  of unfair concurrent-hit deaths.
- Should there be a separate "last damage source" stored for a death-screen "KILLED
  BY: REVENANT" line? Cheap to add an enemyTypeId param to applyDamage; recommend
  adding it so order-1's report can name the killer.
- Knockback on hit? Movement is tile-based and cardinal — knockback would be a
  forced 1-tile shove (a turn-space effect). Defer; out of scope for v1.
- Does the directional damage indicator (screen-edge arc) ship in v1 or v1.1?
  Recommend v1.1; the flash + floating number are sufficient for legibility first.
