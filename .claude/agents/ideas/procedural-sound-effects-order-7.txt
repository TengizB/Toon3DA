OVERVIEW
--------
Order 7 of the procedural-sound-effects series (1-5 in procedural-sound-effects.txt,
6 in procedural-sound-effects-order-6.txt). The owner's two asks:

  1. "Remove the cap on sounds at all." The distinct-sound cap (64, raised to 72 by
     order 6) was never the owner's rule — it came from the original design doc. It
     is deleted outright: the constant, every doc sentence that says "a new sound must
     replace a row", and the CLAUDE.md wording.
  2. "Add more if any action has no sound." A sweep of every player action, menu,
     world event and the boss fight found the gaps below; each gets a sound.

The standing rule still holds: every sound is redundant with something drawn.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - Delete `SoundConstants.GAME_SFX_MAX_DISTINCT_SOUNDS` and all "cap" language
    (docs/sound-system.txt, CLAUDE.md, catalog comments). The resident-memory
    guard it stood for is left to the per-sound 1.0 s duration limit (unchanged).
  - PLAYER ACTIONS that were silent:
      PLAYER_TURN        rotate left/right (quiet gear/cloth shuffle, lowest priority)
      PLAYER_WAIT        skip turn (the suit's soft "hold" tick)
      PLAYER_GUARD_RAISE guard stance (a braced metal clank)
      PLAYER_STUNNED     the input a stun swallows (a dazed ringing)
      ACTION_DENIED      heal with no medkit / at full HP, reload with a full clip,
                         a shop purchase that fails, an invalid route-console tap
  - MENUS that were silent:
      UI_MENU_OPEN / UI_MENU_CLOSE (order 6) now play for EVERY menu overlay — shop,
        pause, settings, codex, weapon inspect, inventory — from ONE phase-change
        detector in World, not per site.
      UI_CONFIRM   a pick: pause-menu row, level-up card, event choice, route ENGAGE
      UI_FOCUS     a focus change on the route console
      SHOP_DISPENSE a purchase landing in the vending tray
  - WORLD events that were silent:
      FIRE_IGNITE / TOXIC_SPILL  a hazard tile appears (fire also on ember collapse)
      IMPACT_BLOCKED (existing)  an enemy's Block buffer absorbs a hit
      CRYSTAL_SHATTER            shard / crust / frost shatter
      CRYSTAL_GROW               shard regrown
      FROST_CRUST                crust cooled onto an enemy
      HEAVY_FOOTFALL             a golem's heavy footfall
      LANCE_BEAM                 the Rimeshell lance beam
      ENEMY_SPAWN                a summoned enemy materialising
      SPIRE_GROW / STONE_CRUMBLE a spire born / shattered
  - THE BOSS FIGHT (BossFloorController, audio injected by setter, null in sim):
      BOSS_ROAR     awaken, and (pitched down) the phase-2 enrage
      ENEMY_WIND_UP (existing) the TELEGRAPH turn
      BOSS_SLAM     RESOLVE, MELEE, CHARGE
      HEAVY_FOOTFALL DASH / REPOSITION
      BOSS_REPAIR   HEAL
      BOSS_DEATH    defeat
      (CAST_HAZARD and SUMMON are heard through FIRE_IGNITE/TOXIC_SPILL/ENEMY_SPAWN.)
  - `GameAudio.playMenu(id)` — a menu sound that ignores hard-pause suppression
    (menus ARE the overlay) but still obeys the EFFECTS setting.

OUT
  - Story-channel taps (bark close, exchange answers, boot card, title menu) — the
    story layer already has its own cues (render/StoryAudio), separately switchable.
  - Music / ambient loops; voice.
  - Per-boss unique voices (one boss sound set, pitched per boss by size later).
  - Tests (non-balance).


RULES
-----
  R1  Nothing changes gameplay state; test + balanceSim identical before/after.
  R2  No package gains an audio import it did not have: golem/spire events ride
      ImpactEffectSystem (already holds GameAudio); hazards ride World's existing
      HazardVisualListener lambda; the boss controller lives in `world`.
  R3  Menu open/close is detected from the RunPhase change, once per transition.
  R4  PLAYER_TURN and FIRE_IGNITE are the quietest, lowest-priority and carry
      re-trigger windows so a held rotate or a spreading fire never drones.


ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
-------------------
  A1  `GAME_SFX_MAX_DISTINCT_SOUNDS` does not exist; no doc says a sound must replace
      a row.
  A2  Rotate, skip turn, guard, a stunned input and each refused action listed above
      play their sound.
  A3  Entering any of INVENTORY_OPEN, SHOP_OPEN, PAUSE_MENU, SETTINGS_MENU,
      CODEX_OPEN, WEAPON_INSPECT plays UI_MENU_OPEN; leaving one plays UI_MENU_CLOSE.
  A4  Pause row pick, level-up card, event choice and route ENGAGE play UI_CONFIRM;
      route focus plays UI_FOCUS; route INVALID and failed purchase ACTION_DENIED; a
      purchase SHOP_DISPENSE.
  A5  Every ImpactEffectSystem golem/spire/spawn/block callback plays its sound.
  A6  Fire and toxic hazard tiles appearing play FIRE_IGNITE / TOXIC_SPILL.
  A7  Boss awaken, phase 2, telegraph, resolve/melee/charge, dash/reposition, heal
      and defeat each play their sound.
  A8  Every registered sound renders <= 1.0 s without clipping (offline harness).
  A9  build green (mirror), test = baseline, balanceSim summary byte-identical.
  A10 docs/sound-system.txt documents order 7 and every new firing site.
