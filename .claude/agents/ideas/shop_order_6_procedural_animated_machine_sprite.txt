READ ORDER
==========
Part 6 (final) of the 6-part SHOP series (see shop_order_1 for the list + golden
constraint). Parts 1-5 built the machine entity, stock, upgrade/supply/ability
purchases, and the buy UI. THIS FILE is the machine's LOOK: a unique, high-detail,
ANIMATED procedural billboard sprite — a robotic UAC vending machine — with idle,
depleted, and dispense states. No PNG art; everything is drawn procedurally, cached,
and animated, in the spirit of the weapon FrameBuffer sprites and credit-chip
billboards (credits-currency-system.txt, weapon-ground-sprites.txt).


DESIGN BRIEF (from the request)
===============================
"A unique sprite must be generated with animations and high-resolution details... the
shop must look like a ROBOTIC VENDING MACHINE." So: not a humanoid merchant — a
chest-to-head-height automated dispenser bolted to a wall, clearly a MACHINE, with
enough detail and motion to read as alive/robotic at distance and up close. It should
feel like a cold UAC appliance: brushed steel body, a glowing product window, a
status light, a dispenser tray, small animated servos/arms, and an idle hum shimmer.


RENDERING APPROACH — HIGH-RES CACHED SPRITE + REAL-TIME ANIMATED LAYERS
======================================================================
Two-tier approach so we get both HIGH-RESOLUTION DETAIL and cheap ANIMATION with NO
per-frame allocation:

  TIER A — STATIC BODY (baked once, high detail):
    Draw the machine's detailed, non-moving chassis ONCE into a high-resolution
    Texture at machine creation (FrameBuffer + ShapeRenderer, or a large Pixmap),
    e.g. SHOP_SPRITE_TEXTURE_SIZE = 512 px tall (portrait, ~256 wide) with Nearest
    filtering, transparent background, cached and reused every frame. This holds all
    the fine detail (panel seams, rivets, bezel, brand plate, grille, tray) that would
    be wasteful to redraw per frame.
    (Mirror the WeaponHudRenderer FrameBuffer pipeline: draw with ShapeRenderer into an
    FBO, read into a Texture, cache; see docs/weapon-creation-guide.txt for the
    pixel-readback pattern.)

  TIER B — ANIMATED OVERLAYS (drawn per frame, allocation-free):
    A small set of MOVING elements drawn each frame ON TOP of the cached body as
    simple additive/alpha billboard quads whose position/alpha/scale come from
    sin/lerp of machine.animationTimeSeconds. These are cheap (a handful of draws, no
    allocation — reuse pre-generated small glow/segment textures):
      - STATUS LIGHT: a pulsing colored dot/halo (green = open/in-stock, amber =
        low/partly-sold, red-dim = DEPLETED). Pulse alpha via sin.
      - PRODUCT WINDOW GLOW: the glass display panel emits a soft cyan glow that
        breathes (sin alpha), suggesting fabrication energy inside.
      - HOLOGRAM SIGN: a flickering "VEND" / "REQUISITION" holo-glyph floating above
        the unit (BitmapFont or a cached glyph, pulsing alpha — the flicker sells
        "robotic" and marks it as interactable from across the room, like the credit
        chips' aura marks value).
      - SERVO ARM / TRAY: a small dispenser arm or tray element with a subtle idle
        twitch (tiny lerp offset), and a full DISPENSE motion on purchase (part 5).
      - AMBIENT AURA: a faint radial glow behind the whole unit (additive, tile-
        brightness aware via PropRenderer.getTileBrightness) so it reads as an emitter
        in a dark hall — reuse the credit-chip aura technique.

The machine does NOT bob/float (it is wall-bolted, unlike floating pickups). Motion is
in its lights, glow, sign, and servo only — reinforcing "heavy machine," not "hovering
loot."


ANIMATION STATES
================
Driven by machine.animationTimeSeconds (advanced by deltaTime) + machine.state:
  IDLE / IN-STOCK   : green status light slow-pulse; cyan window breathe; holo "VEND"
                      flicker; occasional servo twitch. The default alive-machine loop.
  LOW-STOCK         : status light amber; slightly faster/dimmer pulse (some entries
                      sold). Optional; can fold into IDLE if not worth the branch.
  DEPLETED          : status light dim RED, no pulse; window glow fades to near-dark;
                      holo sign reads "EMPTY" / flickers out; servo idle. Signals "no
                      more here" at a glance (part 5 also shows OUT OF STOCK).
  DISPENSE (one-shot, on purchase): triggered by part 5 on a successful buy — the
                      servo/tray extends and retracts, the window flashes bright, a
                      "product" glyph drops into the tray, status light blinks. ~0.5s
                      real-time; returns to IDLE/LOW/DEPLETED after.
State transitions are set by the shop logic (stock sold count from part 2); the
renderer only reads state + time. All timing constants in RenderConstants.


VISUAL DETAIL SPEC (Tier A body — high-res, procedural)
=======================================================
Portrait unit, read three-quarter/front like other billboards. Palette: cold UAC —
brushed steel greys, a warm UAC-orange accent stripe, cyan tech glow. Coordinates as
fractions of the 512px-tall box; origin = box center.
  - CHASSIS: a tall rounded-rectangle steel cabinet (mid-grey 0.42,0.44,0.47) with a
    darker base plinth and a slightly lighter top cap; 2-3 px cool-white top-left rim
    highlight down the left edge + top (the "interactable/lit" convention shared with
    weapon/credit sprites).
  - PANEL SEAMS & RIVETS: horizontal seam lines dividing top display / mid tray /
    lower plinth; small rivet dots along the edges (fine detail that justifies the
    high-res bake).
  - PRODUCT WINDOW: an upper inset "glass" rectangle (dark cyan-tinted 0.10,0.16,0.20)
    with a thin bright bezel; behind it, faint silhouettes of stock (ammo box / stim
    cross / chip) hinting at goods. The Tier-B window glow overlays this.
  - BRAND PLATE: a small UAC wordmark / logo plate (orange 0.80,0.45,0.15) — the
    corporate stamp.
  - STATUS LIGHT HOUSING: a recessed circular housing (dark rim) where the Tier-B
    pulsing light sits.
  - DISPENSER TRAY: a lower recessed slot (dark) with a lip — where DISPENSE drops the
    product; the Tier-B servo/tray animates here.
  - ACCENT STRIPE: a diagonal or horizontal UAC-orange hazard stripe across the mid
    body for readability and brand identity.
  - GRILLE / VENTS: a set of dark horizontal slits (the "hum" vent) low on the body.
Keep it unmistakably a VENDING MACHINE (window + tray + brand) and unmistakably
ROBOTIC/UAC (steel, rivets, status light, cyan tech glow, hazard stripe).


RENDER INTEGRATION
==================
  render/PropRenderer.java (or a dedicated render/ShopMachineRenderer.java if the prop
  pass is crowded — recommended dedicated renderer for clarity):
    - Constructor: bake the Tier-A body Texture once (FBO/Pixmap), build the small
      Tier-B glow/light/aura textures once, cache all. Dispose all in dispose().
    - Per frame, for each VendingMachine on the floor:
        project its tile to screen using WallRenderer's Z-buffer (same billboard
        projection + depth-cull + occlusion path props/enemies already use), then:
          1. draw ambient aura (additive, tile-brightness aware) behind,
          2. draw the cached Tier-A body billboard (bobless, ground-anchored),
          3. draw Tier-B animated overlays (window glow, status light, holo sign,
             servo/tray) using animationTimeSeconds + state,
        then restore blend mode after any additive layers.
    - The machine is SOLID (part 1), so it occupies its tile like a wall-mounted unit;
      ensure the billboard sits flush to its wall and is depth-sorted correctly
      against the player's view (reuse the prop Z-buffer occlusion so walls hide it
      properly).
  Render ORDER: draw machines in the PROP pass (step 3 of World.render, before enemies
  and the weapon HUD) so enemies/weapon draw over it correctly.

TURN-BASED / PLATFORM / PERF COMPLIANCE
=======================================
  - The idle/dispense animation is COSMETIC real-time decoration on a static tile —
    same class as flickering floor lights or credit-chip auras; it advances no world
    tick and violates no turn invariant.
  - NO allocations in render(): Tier-A body + all Tier-B glow textures are baked/cached
    at construction; per-frame work is a fixed handful of draws driven by sin/lerp.
  - All Textures/FrameBuffers/ShapeRenderer/SpriteBatch owned here are disposed in
    dispose() (Disposable rule).
  - FrameBuffer bake happens on the render thread at floor gen / renderer construction,
    not mid-frame during play (weapon-HUD FBO pattern).
  - Coordinate/viewport rules: billboard projects through the existing camera; no new
    camera/viewport; origin stays bottom-left.


BALANCE / UX NOTES
==================
  - The status light + holo sign are the machine's READABILITY: a player should spot a
    fabricator (and whether it still has stock) from across a room — green glow = "go
    spend," dim red = "already looted." This is the same value-at-a-glance principle
    the credit-chip auras use.
  - Warm UAC-orange accent + cyan tech glow visually distinguish the shop from cold
    grey walls and from the gold/green credit chips, so the player never confuses "a
    place to spend" with "currency to pick up."
  - Keep the DISPENSE animation short and punchy (~0.5s) so buying multiple items in
    one visit stays snappy (part 5 buy flow).
  - High-res bake (512px) gives crisp detail up close (the machine fills a chunk of
    screen when adjacent) without per-frame cost — the whole point of Tier A.


INTERACTIONS
============
  - PART 1 (machine entity): supplies tileColumn/row, facing, state (in-stock / low /
    depleted from stock sold-count), and animationTimeSeconds.
  - PART 2 (stock): sold-count drives the LOW / DEPLETED state.
  - PART 5 (UI): a successful purchase triggers the one-shot DISPENSE animation +
    "cha-chunk" sound cue.
  - PROP / ENEMY RENDER (PropRenderer / EnemyRenderer + WallRenderer Z-buffer): reuses
    the billboard projection, depth-cull, occlusion, and tile-brightness paths.
  - CREDIT CHIP AURA (credits-currency-system): reuses the additive tile-brightness-
    aware aura technique for the machine's ambient glow.
  - WEAPON HUD SPRITE (weapon-creation-guide): reuses the FrameBuffer bake +
    pixel-readback + cached-texture pipeline for the Tier-A body.


OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
1. Dedicated ShopMachineRenderer vs extend PropRenderer? Recommend a dedicated
   renderer — the machine has more animated layers than a simple prop, and it keeps
   PropRenderer lean.
2. Tier-A bake resolution: 512px tall enough for crisp adjacent viewing, or go 768?
   Recommend 512 for v1 (memory-friendly on phones); bump if it looks soft up close.
3. Should the machine have a subtle IDLE sound (hum) in addition to the DISPENSE
   sound? Nice ambiance; gate behind an audio-budget check (mobile). Defer if audio
   isn't wired.
4. Distinct body accent color per machine when a floor has 2 (e.g. one orange, one
   teal) to tell them apart on the minimap/view? Minor; recommend same look for v1,
   differentiate via minimap marker instead.
5. Minimap representation: add a small "$"/machine icon on the mini-map so the player
   can navigate back to spend accrued credits? Strong QoL — flag as a small follow-up
   (LevelRenderer minimap marker), out of scope for this sprite doc.
