CATEGORY: Systems
TITLE: Balance Rule System — the math contract for adding content safely
---

Idea 2 of 6 in the balance series (see balance_order_1 for the full list).
Depends on idea 1 (Unified Balance Config) being done first.

=== PURPOSE ===

A rule system + the math behind it, so that when a NEW weapon, enemy, item, or
upgrade is added, the designer can check it against fixed formulas and know in
advance whether it breaks balance — instead of finding out by feel after the
fact. This is the "balance contract." Every piece of content must pass it.

The whole system rests on FOUR primitive quantities. Everything else is derived.
All four become pure functions in util/GameMath.java (project rule: every
formula is a documented static method, never inline).


=== THE FOUR PRIMITIVES ===

  1. eHP   — Effective Hit Points (how much raw damage a thing really absorbs)
  2. DPT   — Damage Per Turn (output, the turn being the game's atomic unit)
  3. TTK   — Turns To Kill (= target eHP / attacker DPT)
  4. TP    — Threat Points (a single comparable "danger number" per enemy)

Because the game is strictly turn-based (one action = one world turn), TIME is
measured in TURNS, not seconds. This makes the math clean and exact: there is no
real-time DPS, only damage-per-turn. This is a huge advantage SPD-style games
have over action games — balance is arithmetic, not physics.


=== PRIMITIVE 1 — EFFECTIVE HP (eHP) ===

  Formula: effectiveHitPoints
  Derivation:
    Dodge multiplies survivability by 1/(1-dodge) because a dodged hit deals 0.
    Flat reduction R subtracts from each incoming hit of average size H, so it
    multiplies survivability by H/(H-R) for HP-bound damage.
      eHP = (rawHP + armorPool) * 1/(1 - dodgeChance) * H/max(1, H-R)
  Player example (MARINE start, no stat investment):
      rawHP 130, armor 75, dodge 0, R 0  ->  eHP = 205
  Player example (TOUGHNESS 6: +30 HP, R=6; AGI for 0.20 dodge), avg hit H=12:
      eHP = (160 + 75) * 1/0.80 * 12/6 = 235 * 1.25 * 2 = 587 eHP
  Enemy example (no dodge/armor): eHP == rawHP. Plague Hulk eHP = 50.

  RULE: enemies currently have NO dodge or flat reduction, so enemy eHP = HP.
  If an armored enemy archetype is ever added, its HP entry in BalanceConfig
  must be its RAW hp and its reduction declared separately so eHP stays derivable.


=== PRIMITIVE 2 — DAMAGE PER TURN (DPT) ===

Two flavors. Both are needed.

  BURST DPT = damage delivered in a single fire action (one turn).
      Shotgun 50, Railgun(full) 90, Grenade 30, Chaingun 10.

  SUSTAINED DPT = damage averaged over a full clip + reload cycle:
      sustainedDPT = (clipSize * damagePerShot) / (clipSize + reloadTicks)
  Worked from current numbers:
      SHOTGUN       (1*50)/(1+1)  = 25.0   <-- highest non-charge
      DBL_SHOTGUN   (2*32)/(2+1)  = 21.3
      PLASMA_RIFLE  (4*18)/(4+1)  = 14.4
      ASSAULT_RIFLE (30*14)/(30+1)= 13.5
      CHAINGUN      (24*10)/(24+1)=  9.6
      RAILGUN full  (1*90)/(1+2)  = 30.0
      RAILGUN min   (1*40)/(1+2)  = 13.3

  >>> BALANCE SIGNAL ALREADY VISIBLE: the SHOTGUN has both the best sustained
      DPT (25) of the non-charge guns AND (see primitive below) the best ammo
      efficiency. That is a real imbalance the current game has, surfaced purely
      by the formula. This is what the contract is for.

  Falloff: actual DPT decays with range via damageAtDistance(). Define
  EFFECTIVE DPT at the weapon's INTENDED engagement band (see weapon-role table)
  rather than at muzzle, so a 5-tile shotgun isn't credited with point-blank
  numbers it rarely achieves.


=== PRIMITIVE 2b — AMMO EFFICIENCY (damage per ammo unit) ===

  Formula: damagePerAmmoUnit = damagePerShot / ammoPerShot   (usually /1)
  Run-total potential = damagePerAmmoUnit * reserveCap:
      SHOTGUN   50/shell * 60 shells  = 3000 potential damage
      ASSAULT   14/bullet * 200       = 2800   (shares bullets w/ chaingun)
      CHAINGUN  10/bullet * 200       = 2000
      PLASMA    18/cell  * 120        = 2160
  This is the bridge to the scarcity model (idea 3): a weapon's "run budget" is
  damagePerAmmoUnit * total ammo the economy hands you, NOT its reserve cap.


=== PRIMITIVE 3 — TURNS TO KILL (TTK) ===

  Formula: turnsToKill = ceil(target.eHP / attacker.effectiveDPT)
  Player-killing-enemy examples (start shotgun, burst, point blank):
      Gore Biter 18 / 50  -> 1 turn
      Plague Hulk 50 / 50 -> 1 turn
      Iron Stalker 95 /50 -> 2 turns
  Enemy-killing-player (TTD, Turns To Die) examples vs 205 eHP start player:
      Shell Brute 13/turn -> 16 turns of continuous adjacency
      Eye Tyrant  7/turn  -> 30 turns

  THE GOLDEN RATIO (the single most important design target):
      For a FAIR 1-on-1 fight the player should win, but not trivially.
      Target:  TTK_player  <  TTD_player   (player kills enemy before dying)
      and      TTD_player / TTK_player  in [3, 8] for trash mobs
               (you can kill ~3-8 of this enemy back-to-back before dying if you
                take every hit — but you WON'T take every hit if you play well).
  This ratio is the knob that makes fights "tense but winnable." Below 3 = unfair
  / swingy. Above 8 = enemies are harmless damage sponges (the current problem).


=== PRIMITIVE 4 — THREAT POINTS (TP): one number per enemy ===

  Formula: threatPoints
  Derivation: an enemy is dangerous in proportion to (a) how much it hurts you
  per turn, (b) how long it survives your fire (= how many turns it keeps
  hurting you), and (c) whether it can hurt you without being adjacent.
      TP = enemyDPT * survivalTurns * positionalMultiplier
  where
      enemyDPT       = attackDamage / max(1, attackCadenceTurns)
      survivalTurns  = enemy.eHP / REFERENCE_PLAYER_DPT   (use 25, the shotgun)
      positionalMult = 1.0 melee, 1.30 ranged, 1.15 fast-melee(moveEvery1),
                       +0.25 if it applies a status effect (DOT/stun/slow)
  Worked (REFERENCE_PLAYER_DPT = 25, attack cadence: melee = moveEveryN, ranged=1):
      Gore Biter   (7/1) * (18/25) * 1.15 = 5.8 TP
      Eye Tyrant   (7/1) * (18/25) * 1.30 = 6.6 TP
      Shell Brute  (13/1)* (38/25) * 1.0  = 19.8 TP
      Plague Hulk  (10/2)* (50/25) * 1.0  = 10.0 TP
      Mire Wraith  (7/1) * (38/25) * 1.30 = 13.8 TP  (ranged+DOT could add 0.25)
      Iron Stalker (16/1)* (95/25) * 1.15 = 69.9 TP  (mini-elite — correct!)

  TP is the currency the encounter budget (idea 3/4) spends. It lets the level
  generator say "this floor's rooms may contain up to N total TP" so difficulty
  is a dial, not an accident of which enemies happened to roll.


=== THE CONTRACT — what every new piece of content must satisfy ===

------------------------------------------------------------------
A) NEW WEAPON must fall inside the POWER BAND for its role + tier.
------------------------------------------------------------------
  Define POWER SCORE:
      weaponPower = sustainedEffectiveDPT * sqrt(ammoEfficiencyNormalized)
  Each ROLE has a target band (so a new weapon can't quietly out-DPS the class):
      sidearm        : power 8-14    (pistol-tier, ammo-cheap, always usable)
      workhorse      : power 12-18   (assault rifle, chaingun)
      burst/specialist: power 18-26  (shotgun, plasma) - high DPT, ammo-hungry
      heavy/charge   : power 24-32   (railgun, grenade) - scarce ammo, slow
  RULE: a new weapon picks a role, then its numbers are tuned until weaponPower
  lands in that role's band. If it lands above the band it WILL invalidate the
  others. (The current shotgun is slightly over its band — a known fix target.)
  Higher RARITY does NOT raise the DPT band — it buys ABILITIES (idea 5), not
  raw damage. This keeps a legendary from being a strict numeric upgrade.

------------------------------------------------------------------
B) NEW ENEMY must declare a ROLE and land in that role's TP band.
------------------------------------------------------------------
      chaff       : TP  4-8    (dies in 1 hit, comes in packs)
      soldier     : TP  9-16   (the standard threat unit)
      bruiser     : TP 17-28   (forces a weapon swap / repositioning)
      mini-elite  : TP 40-75   (a mid-floor spike; rare)
      boss        : see idea 6 (own ruleset)
  RULE: choose role -> set HP, damage, cadence, range so TP lands in band ->
  THEN verify the golden ratio (TTD/TTK in [3,8] for chaff/soldier; [2,4] for
  bruiser; bruisers are SUPPOSED to be scary 1v1). An enemy that violates the
  golden ratio is rejected even if its TP is in band.

------------------------------------------------------------------
C) NEW CONSUMABLE/HEAL must be priced in "turns of survival bought."
------------------------------------------------------------------
      survivalTurnsBought = healAmount / averageIncomingDPT(floor)
  A medkit that buys >10 turns on an early floor trivializes scarcity. Heals are
  priced against the floor's average incoming DPT, not a flat number.

------------------------------------------------------------------
D) NEW UPGRADE/ABILITY must cost POWER BUDGET (full math in idea 5).
------------------------------------------------------------------
  Short version: an upgrade's value = (DPT or eHP gain) expressed in TP-equiv;
  a level-up grants a fixed budget; every offered choice must cost ~the same
  budget so no choice is a no-brainer. Detailed in balance_order_5.


=== DEPTH SCALING — keep the player/enemy curves coupled ===

The danger of depth scaling is the two curves drifting apart. Define them
relative to each other, never independently:

  enemyThreatAtDepth(d)  = baseTP * HEALTH_SCALE^(d-1) * DAMAGE_SCALE^(d-1)
                         = baseTP * 1.08^(d-1) * 1.06^(d-1)   (current values)
  playerPowerAtDepth(d)  = f(expected level-ups by depth d, expected weapon
                             upgrades found, expected eHP from found armor)

  THE INVARIANT (must hold or the game gets unfair-hard or trivial-easy):
      0.9  <=  playerPowerAtDepth(d) / enemyThreatAtDepth(d)  <=  1.2
  i.e. the player should stay roughly even-to-slightly-ahead of the curve,
  drifting NO more than +20% ahead or -10% behind. If found-loot and XP let the
  player outscale this, lower the loot/XP rate; if they fall behind, raise it.
  This is the formula that makes "add floor scaling later" (deferred) safe to do.


=== PROPOSED util/GameMath ADDITIONS ===

  effectiveHitPoints(rawHp, armor, dodgeChance, flatReduction, avgIncomingHit)
  sustainedDamagePerTurn(clipSize, damagePerShot, reloadTicks)
  ammoEfficiency(damagePerShot, ammoPerShot)
  turnsToKill(targetEffectiveHp, attackerDpt)
  threatPoints(attackDamage, attackCadenceTurns, enemyEffectiveHp,
               referencePlayerDpt, positionalMultiplier)
  weaponPowerScore(sustainedEffectiveDpt, ammoEfficiencyNormalized)
  goldenRatio(turnsToDie, turnsToKill)            // returns TTD/TTK
  depthThreatScale(healthScale, damageScale, depth)
All pure, all with the mandatory Formula/Derivation/Edge-cases comment block.


=== A LIVING SPREADSHEET (docs/balance-rule-system.txt) ===

Ship a companion doc that TABULATES every current weapon and enemy with its
computed power/TP, so the bands are visible and "out of band" content is obvious
at a glance. Regenerate it whenever BalanceConfig changes. (A tiny dev-only main
or unit test that prints the table keeps it honest — the numbers can't lie.)


=== PREREQUISITES ===
  - Idea 1 (Unified Balance Config) MUST be done — the formulas read its fields.
  - A "reference player DPT" and "reference player eHP" must be agreed (proposal:
    REFERENCE_PLAYER_DPT = 25 = shotgun sustained; REFERENCE_PLAYER_EHP = 205 =
    MARINE start). These become BalanceConfig constants the formulas anchor on.

=== DEFINITION OF DONE ===
  - The four primitive formulas exist in GameMath with derivations.
  - The contract (A-D) is written into docs/balance-rule-system.txt as a
    checklist any future weapon/enemy/item idea must pass.
  - The living table is generated and currently-out-of-band content (e.g. the
    shotgun) is flagged for a follow-up tuning pass.
