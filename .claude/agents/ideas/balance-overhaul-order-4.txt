OVERVIEW
--------
The owner: "it's a slow drain — I'd like some enemies to have stronger attacks
that have to be avoided and not tanked"; "no single hit takes too much";
telegraphs "work but should be communicated even better"; "toxic, beams,
weakening feel like they do nothing"; hazards are ignored but "I want them used
strategically"; "GUARD — never use it; armour doesn't matter; the intention is
that it must be useful".

All four symptoms have one root: nothing in combat is big enough to demand a
response. This order makes the game's EXISTING commit-then-resolve attacks —
WIND_UP charges, AREA_STRIKE slams, the Rimeshell lance, SELF_DESTRUCT —
genuinely HEAVY (roughly half an on-curve player's health), shows exactly which
tiles they will hit, and gives three answers: step off the marked tiles, GUARD
facing the attacker (which now also punishes it), or stagger it first with a
close shotgun blast (order 3). Armour becomes the shield against status effects
and the buffer for heavies; status effects and hazards scale with max HP /
enemy HP so they stay meaningful at every depth; barrels are placed where the
groups are.


SCOPE
-----

  - Sim policy behaviour for heavies; S-HEAVY band; HEAVY_DAMAGE_SCALE as an

GAMEPLAY LOOP
-------------
  1. A Shell Brute at 4 tiles in the player's column commits WIND_UP. Its intent
     icon is large, red, with "!!". The five tiles of its charge lane glow with
     red floor markers in the 3D view and fill red on the mini-map.
  2. The player's turn. Options, each one ordinary action:
       a. Strafe out of the lane -> the charge hits nothing and the Brute ends
          its charge exposed (existing recovery).
       b. GUARD facing the Brute -> the charge lands at x0.2 and the Brute is
          STUNNED for its next turn and VULNERABLE (+50% damage taken) for 2.
       c. It is within 2 tiles? A shotgun blast staggers it (order 3) and the
          charge is cancelled.
       d. Do nothing -> the charge lands for ~50% of max HP (armour absorbs part
          and is shredded).
  3. An Acid Drone's WEAK debuff hits the player while armour > 0: "ARMOUR HELD"
     appears, armour drops, no WEAK. With 0 armour: WEAK (-35% damage, 3 turns),
     shown on the status row with turns remaining; a STIM cures it.
  4. A pack of five chaff sits beside two barrels. One shot turns the fight.


RULES
-----
Heavies
  H1  HEAVY verbs (data flag on the existing mechanics): WIND_UP charges and
      lunges, AREA_STRIKE, the Rimeshell Lancer lance, SELF_DESTRUCT. Assigned
      heavies (existing verbs, existing code paths): Shell Brute (charge, today),
      Plague Hulk (slam + self-destruct, today), Iron Stalker (AREA_STRIKE,
      today), Rimeshell Lancer (lance, today), Revenant (WIND_UP lunge, NEW
      assignment), Cinderforge Colossus (AREA_STRIKE, NEW assignment).
  H2  Heavy damage vs an on-curve player (ExpectedPlayer at d): unguarded,
      unarmoured hit = HEAVY_DAMAGE_FRACTION in [0.40, 0.60] of expected max HP
      (default 0.50; SELF_DESTRUCT 0.55; AREA_STRIKE 0.45). Derived at spawn from
      the fraction, never a literal, times BalanceConfig HEAVY_DAMAGE_SCALE (1.0;
      the order-7 feel pass tunes it).
  H3  Every heavy is COMMITTED at least one full player turn before it resolves,
      with its exact DANGER TILES known at commit time (lane tiles for charges/
      lunges/lance, the strike cross/radius for AREA_STRIKE, the blast radius for
      SELF_DESTRUCT).
  H4  AVOIDABLE: from any danger tile, at least one single action avoids or
      blunts the hit — a move/strafe to a non-danger walkable tile, or GUARD
      facing the source (H7). If the player's tile has no walkable non-danger
      neighbour (cornered), GUARD must still reduce the hit to <= 12% of max HP.
  H5  A STAGGERED (order-3 STUNNED) heavy is CANCELLED, not delayed.
  H6  Un-telegraphed damage keeps the old cap: no single un-telegraphed hit above
      25% of on-curve eHP (R-TELEGRAPH unchanged for ordinary attacks).

Guard
  H7  GUARD (one turn, as today) facing the attacker (+/-60 degree front arc):
      ordinary hits x GUARD_FRONT_ORDINARY 0.50; HEAVY hits x GUARD_FRONT_HEAVY
      0.20 and RIPOSTE: the attacker is STUNNED for its next turn and VULNERABLE
      for 2 turns (existing statuses). Side/back unchanged (x1.0). GUARD
      remains usable every turn.

Armour
  H8  ARMOUR_ABSORB_FRACTION 0.50 -> 0.67 (share of each hit taken from armour
      while armour > 0). HEAVY hits cost armour x1.5 (shred).
  H9  STATUS SHIELD: while armour > 0, WEAK / SLOW / BLIND / POISON application
      from ENEMIES is blocked and costs ARMOUR_STATUS_COST_FRACTION (0.10 of max
      armour) per blocked application. Hazard tiles are NOT blocked (the floor
      is the counter-play there). Event text "ARMOUR HELD" on each block.

Status (all magnitudes fractions of max HP so they never go stale with depth)
  H10 POISON: POISON_TICK_FRACTION 0.015 of max HP per stack per turn, max 6
      stacks, stacks decay by 1 per turn; ranged acid hits apply 2 stacks; toxic
      tiles apply 2 stacks per turn stood.
      WEAK: player outgoing damage -35% for 3 turns.
      SLOW: unchanged mechanic (enemies act twice per player action), 2 turns;
      the Rimeshell lance applies it on hit.
      BURN on the player: 0.03 of max HP per turn.
      BLIND: unchanged mechanic, 3 turns.
      The unified BURN base (R-DOT) becomes BURN_TICK_FRACTION (player) with the
      enemy-side burn derived from the enemy's max HP; R-DOT's one-definition rule
      is kept.
  H11 STIM ('+') also cleanses POISON and WEAK when used.
  H12 HUD status row shows each active status icon with its turns remaining
      (and POISON stacks).

Hazards
  H13 Barrel explosion: centre = HAZARD_BARREL_CENTRE_FRACTION 1.2 of the floor's
      reference SOLDIER max HP; orthogonal ring 0.6; to the player: 0.30 / 0.15
      of max HP. Counts as EXPLOSIVE for matchups (order 3).
      Fire tiles: enemy burn x HAZARD_FIRE_ENEMY_DAMAGE_MULTIPLIER 2.5.
      Toxic tiles and the Plague Hulk death cloud poison enemies too (2 stacks
      per turn stood, enemy-HP fraction).
  H14 PLACEMENT: on COMBAT and ELITE floors, >= HAZARD_GROUP_ROOM_SHARE (0.40) of
      rooms holding a group of >= 3 get 1-2 explosive barrels within 2 tiles of
      the group cluster (placed by the order-2 encounter placement step, existing
      barrel symbol).

Communication
  H15 DANGER MARKERS: every danger tile of a committed heavy shows a red,
      pulsing, floor-height marker in the first-person view and a red fill on
      the mini-map, from commit until resolution (or cancellation).
  H16 HEAVY INTENT: the intent icon of a heavy is drawn 1.5x size, red, with a
      "!!" sub-glyph. If the source is off-screen (outside the view) and the
      player stands on a danger tile, the screen edge on the source's side
      pulses red (HitVignetteRenderer).
  H17 Sound: heavy commit reuses an existing telegraph/wind-up sound row if one
      exists; otherwise replace one row per docs/sound-system.txt (catalog is at
      its 64 cap). The game must be fully readable with sound off.
  H18 Teaching: the existing GUARD teaching topic (first heavy hit taken, re-
      taught after 3 unguarded heavies) and READ_INTENT cover this; their lines
      are reviewed so they name "red floor" as the thing to read (rewrite per the
      narrative add-a-line protocol, no numbers). No new topic.


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
-----------------
Fights gain peaks: most turns are positioning and shooting, and then something
winds up and the floor turns red. The player either reads it and wins the
exchange (sidestep, guard-riposte, or a point-blank stagger) or takes half their
health — and always knows exactly why. Armour becomes the thing that keeps
casters' debuffs off you; losing it to a heavy is a real cost. Status effects
now hurt enough to cure. Barrels next to packs make the player look at the room
before charging in.

VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
Danger marker: a flat-looking red ring/chevron sprite (procedural, 32x32 px
texture generated once) drawn as a low billboard at floor height at the tile
centre, alpha pulsing 0.45..0.9 at 3 Hz, colour (0.95, 0.15, 0.10). Mini-map
danger fill: same red at alpha 0.5. Heavy intent "!!": two vertical bars with
dots under the existing intent icon, same red. "ARMOUR HELD": event text in the
armour bar's blue.


BALANCE NOTES
-------------
Balance-bearing throughout (SECTION 23, GameMath, schema, sim). Heavy fraction
0.50 is a default; HEAVY_DAMAGE_SCALE lets the order-7 feel pass tune it. TP
pricing of heavies uses an avoidance discount, so heavy-bearing archetypes do not
flood the encounter budget; R-ENEMY TP bands re-checked. BalanceReport's existing
HAZARDS and TELEGRAPH AUDIT tables are re-based to the fractions and gain a HEAVY
column (damage fraction, guarded fraction per heavy verb).


INTERACTIONS
------------
  - Order 3: STAGGER cancels heavies (H5); barrels are EXPLOSIVE vs traits;
    shotgun knockback can push enemies into fire/toxic tiles.
  - Order 2: barrel placement joins the encounter placement step; supply of heals
    re-fits to the new incoming-damage shape (expected incoming uses the heavy
    avoidance discount).
  - Order 1: fractions are of on-curve max HP (ExpectedPlayer).
  - Bosses: boss verbs derive through BossBalance under the new caps (R-BOSS-
    VERB-CAP updated); danger markers apply to boss heavies automatically if they
    use the same verbs.
  - Story: GUARD/READ_INTENT line review only.


EDGE CASES
----------
  - Danger tiles through walls: charge lanes stop at the first wall/door/prop
    (GameMath geometry); markers never draw inside walls.
  - Two heavies overlapping: tiles union; H4 checked against the union (if no
    safe tile, GUARD must satisfy the cornered rule against each).
  - The heavy's source dies before resolving: markers vanish, no hit.
  - Player mid-animation when the heavy resolves: resolution happens on the
    world turn as today; animation is cosmetic.
  - Armour 0 exactly when a status arrives: applied (shield only while > 0).
  - Stim cleanse with no poison/weak: heals only (as today).
  - Barrel next to the start room: excluded (first-contact room only).
  - Hard-pause overlay open: markers freeze with the world.

ASSUMPTIONS
-----------
  AS1  "Must be avoided, not tanked" means about half of max HP; 0.40-0.60 is the
       enforced band, 0.50 the default.
  AS2  Reusing existing verbs (and giving two more archetypes one) gives enough
       heavies in every region without new mechanics.
  AS3  Floor-height billboard markers are an acceptable way to show danger tiles
       in the raycast view (no floor-casting change).
  AS4  Guard riposte (stun + vulnerable) is the reason to guard rather than just
       step aside when you are in a good firing position.
  AS5  Armour as the enemy-status shield is the identity that makes armour
       matter; hazards bypass it on purpose.
  AS6  STIM cleansing poison/weak gives stims a job distinct from medkits.
  AS7  40% of group rooms getting barrels is enough to make hazards a habit
       without every fight being a barrel fight.
  AS8  No new teaching topic: GUARD and READ_INTENT already exist and are
       re-worded.
