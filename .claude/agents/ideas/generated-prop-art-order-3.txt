VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
GENERATOR ('%', 0.85 wall height, canvas 176 x 216, 16 frames @ 0.1 s, loops)
  A heavy steel power unit on a skid. Cold steel body (gunmetal housing, darkmetal fin stacks both sides, a
  lab-grey control plate) and HEAT as the accent:
  - COIL BAY: a recessed bay down the front behind vertical guard bars; through the gaps, stacked induction
    windings glowing HOT IRON - dark red at the edges, orange, a white-hot band - with a heat wave rolling up.
  - TURBINE: a round fan housing on top; dark blades turn over a hot-iron glow (the fan pulls the heat up).
  - EXHAUSTS: two stacks at the top corners with glowing hot-iron lips and embers drifting up and fading.
  - CONTROL PLATE: a gauge whose needle trembles, two neon cyan status lamps (the cold counterpoint) and one
    hot-iron warning lamp that blinks.
  - A rad lime hazard strip on the skid. Stencil GEN-04.
  Static steel = one kit render; every moving or glowing part painted per frame.
