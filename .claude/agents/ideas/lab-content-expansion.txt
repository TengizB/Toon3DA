OVERVIEW
========
A bulk content pass that deepens the UAC facility vocabulary: 7 NEW procedural
wall types, 5 NEW solid billboard props, visual reworks of 4 EXISTING bare props,
6 NEW room templates, and the generator changes to weave them in. Everything
slots into the ALREADY-BUILT pipelines (per-char wall switch in WallRenderer,
billboard PropRenderer, RoomType decoration in LevelGenerator) with zero new
runtime architecture — it is content, not new systems. The goal: a player should
be able to name any room on sight (med bay, armory, cryo, reactor) and read its
danger from the walls before stepping in.

This document does NOT redefine any existing tile. It only ADDS. See
procedural-wall-types.txt (r/G/k walls — IMPLEMENTED), billboard-prop-sprites.txt,
and level-generation-improvements.txt (RoomType system — IMPLEMENTED) for the
plumbing this rides on. All new procedural textures reuse the established
"generate Pixmap once in constructor, Nearest filter, dispose in dispose()"
pattern and the shared radialFalloff / drawBeveledRect helpers from those docs.


CHARACTER AUDIT (what is already taken — DO NOT reuse)
=====================================================
Walls (solid):   x c v t w h r G k
Solid props:     b B T L C  (generator also emits g=barrel, E=explosive)
Decal props:     m s . O  + keycards r/y/b, stim +, medkit H, armour a/A, stairs >
Floor lighting:  (space) l u f
Special:         P (column) d (door) p (player) g E (generator prop aliases)
Enemy spawns:    1 2

Conclusion: lowercase r, G, g, E are ALL in use. New chars below are chosen from
the confirmed-free set:
  capitals  : N M Q R S U V W X Y Z   (R/S/U/V/W/X/Y/Z safe; M/N/Q safe)
  lowercase : e i j n o q z
  symbols   : * ~ % & = # @ ^
Each new symbol is justified in the assignment table (SECTION 6).


===================================================================
SECTION 1 — NEW WALL TYPES (7)
===================================================================
All are SOLID (Level.isWall -> TRUE), procedural Pixmap, 128x128, Nearest filter,
deterministic seed. Each adds one case to wallTextureFor / widthFor / heightFor.
Drawing layer order is listed per wall (back-to-front). Distance shading and
floor-brightness multipliers apply unchanged.

--- WALL 1.1 : 'N' — REINFORCED CONTAINMENT GLASS (cracked observation window) ---
THE READ: "something is (or was) being watched/held on the other side."
Base: dark tinted glass over a steel frame. A thick beveled steel FRAME border
(14px) like the bulkhead, then an inner glass pane that is semi-transparent dark
teal with faint diagonal sheen streaks and a spiderweb CRACK radiating from one
off-center impact point. Behind the glass, a few dim shapes (suggested, not
detailed): vertical bars or a pale silhouette blob.
COLORS (RGBA):
  GLASS_FRAME_STEEL   = (0.28, 0.31, 0.36, 1.0)
  GLASS_FRAME_LIGHT   = (0.46, 0.50, 0.57, 1.0)   raised bevel top/left
  GLASS_FRAME_DARK    = (0.12, 0.14, 0.18, 1.0)   bevel bottom/right
  GLASS_PANE_DARK     = (0.07, 0.13, 0.15, 1.0)   tinted glass body
  GLASS_PANE_LIGHT    = (0.16, 0.27, 0.30, 1.0)   sheen streak
  GLASS_SHEEN         = (0.55, 0.72, 0.74, 1.0)    bright diagonal highlight, 1px
  GLASS_CRACK         = (0.78, 0.85, 0.88, 1.0)    hairline crack (near white)
  GLASS_CRACK_DARK    = (0.02, 0.05, 0.06, 1.0)    crack shadow doubling
  GLASS_GHOST         = (0.20, 0.24, 0.22, 1.0)     dim shape behind glass
LAYER ORDER: 1 fill frame steel + grain  2 inner pane gradient (dark->light top
to bottom)  3 ghost silhouette blobs (2-3 soft radialFalloff smears in GHOST)
  4 sheen: 2 diagonal SHEEN streaks  5 crack: random-walk lines from one impact
point, each crack drawn 2px CRACK with a 1px CRACK_DARK offset for depth
  6 beveled frame border (drawBeveledRect helper, top-left lit).
ROOMS / FREQUENCY: Containment Cell block (cell fronts, 60% of cell-facing walls),
Cryo Chamber (pod-bay viewing wall, 1 band), Command Center (1 observation wall).
Rare elsewhere. ~2-4 tiles per level outside its native rooms.
LORE: UAC observation glass. Cracked = the thing inside got out.

--- WALL 1.3 : 'R' — EMERGENCY LIGHTING STRIP WALL ---
THE READ: "alarm state — this corridor is on backup power."
Base: standard dark lab steel with a single horizontal recessed channel at eye
height containing a glowing RED emergency strip. The strip casts a soft red
bleed onto the steel above and below it (gradient). Small caged-bulb housings
punctuate the strip every ~size/4.
COLORS (RGBA):
  EMERG_STEEL         = (0.20, 0.21, 0.24, 1.0)   dark steel field
  EMERG_CHANNEL       = (0.08, 0.06, 0.06, 1.0)   recessed channel
  EMERG_STRIP_CORE    = (0.95, 0.18, 0.16, 1.0)   bright red strip core
  EMERG_STRIP_GLOW    = (0.55, 0.10, 0.10, 1.0)   bleed onto steel
  EMERG_CAGE          = (0.10, 0.10, 0.11, 1.0)    bulb cage wire
LAYER ORDER: 1 steel field + grain  2 vertical glow gradient toward the strip row
(EMERG_STRIP_GLOW lerped by distance from strip center row)  3 recessed channel
(2-3px CHANNEL band) at row ~size*0.45  4 STRIP_CORE bright band inside channel
(2px) with 1px white-hot center  5 cage housings: small CAGE crosshatch every
size/4 over the strip.
NOTE: This is STATIC red (paints the alarm look). It composes with the global
emergency-red-alert-pulse tint — when that pulse fires, this wall reads as the
SOURCE of the alarm light. No animation code needed.
ROOMS / FREQUENCY: Power Plant approach, Command Center, any corridor flanking a
keycard door. Use in short BANDS (2-4 contiguous tiles) so a corridor reads as
"alarmed," not single scattered tiles. ~6-10 tiles/level.
LORE: Backup power only keeps the red strips alive. Main grid is gone.

  CRYO_STEEL          = (0.34, 0.40, 0.46, 1.0)   cold blue-grey steel
  CRYO_FROST_LIGHT    = (0.86, 0.93, 0.97, 1.0)   bright rime

--- WALL 1.6 : 'Y' — RADIATION-BURNED WALL (scorched, glowing cracks) ---
THE READ: "reactor breach — irradiated, do not linger."
Base: steel SCORCHED near-black, blistered and warped, with a network of glowing
GREEN-YELLOW cracks (Cherenkov-ish) bleeding light through the burned plate.
Heaviest scorch radiates from one hot-spot. Faint yellow grain dusting (fallout).
COLORS (RGBA):
  RAD_SCORCH_BLACK    = (0.08, 0.08, 0.07, 1.0)   burned steel
  RAD_SCORCH_BROWN    = (0.22, 0.16, 0.08, 1.0)   heat-stained edge
  RAD_CRACK_CORE      = (0.85, 0.95, 0.30, 1.0)   glowing crack core (hot)
  RAD_CRACK_GLOW      = (0.40, 0.62, 0.14, 1.0)   green-yellow bleed
  RAD_DUST            = (0.55, 0.58, 0.22, 1.0)    fallout speckle

ROOMS / FREQUENCY: Power Plant / reactor core (dominant, 60%), deep-level dread

LORE: The reactor went critical. This wall is cooking you.

===================================================================
SECTION 4 — NEW ROOM TEMPLATES (6)
===================================================================
Each extends the RoomType enum and gets a decorate() branch in LevelGenerator.
Dimensions are INTERIOR tiles. "Floor mix" uses the lighting chars. Spatial layout
is described relative to the room rectangle (the entry door side varies per
connection — patterns are door-agnostic, anchored to room corners/center).

--- 4.1 : MEDICAL BAY  (RARE — once per level) ---
LORE: Infirmary where the wounded were triaged before the place fell.
DIMENSIONS: 7x6 to 9x7.
WALLS: 'M' medical tile dominant; 1-2 'Q' bio-containment accent; 1 'N' glass
(lab annex view). Corners may be plain 'x'.
PROPS: rows of 'L' lockers (medical supply) along one long wall; 1-2 '@' stim
dispensers; 1 '&' bio-pod in a corner annex; scatter '+' stim and 'H' medkit
pickups on the floor (this is the level's reliable heal stop); a 'm' corpse on a
"bed" (corpse + blood '.' cluster).
FLOOR MIX: mostly lit ' '; one 'f' flicker tile at the door chokepoint; a small
'u' unlit corner (the morgue corner). Clean, bright overall.
CONNECTS: 1-2 doors; ideally off a main corridor, not a dead-end (player should
find it). Generator should bias it toward mid-distance from entrance.

--- 4.2 : ARMORY  (RARE — once per level) ---
LORE: Weapons locker; site of the marines' last stand.
DIMENSIONS: 6x6 to 8x7.
WALLS: 'X' blast-scarred dominant; 'h' hazard stripe accent near the gun racks;
'k' bulkhead framing the door (high security).
PROPS: 2-4 '=' weapon racks along the back wall; 'B' explosive barrels (1-2,
risky); 'C' UAC crates stacked; '#' security camera by the door; 'm' corpses +
's' dropped shotguns + '.' blood (the last stand). Loot-dense.
FLOOR MIX: ' ' lit with 1-2 'u' unlit (the back where bodies fell). Combat-worn.
CONNECTS: 1 door, often behind a keycard door (uac-keycard-doors). Bulkhead frame
sells "secure." Can be a rewarding dead-end (bulkhead at a dead end reads right).

--- 4.3 : CRYO CHAMBER ---
LORE: Cryogenic stasis bay; coolant failing, occupants thawing.
DIMENSIONS: 7x7 to 10x8.
WALLS: 'Z' cryo/frost dominant; 'N' containment glass (pod viewing wall); 'M'
tile on the lab-annex side.
PROPS: rows of '&' bio-pods / cryo-tanks down two parallel walls (the pod bay
look — like server room but tanks); 'T' terminals (control consoles, reworked
sprite); 1 '%' generator (coolant unit). Some pods broken (place near 'G' gore if
adjacent).
FLOOR MIX: 'l' normal + 'u' unlit lanes between pods (cold dark); 1-2 'f' flicker.
Dim and blue-cold in feel.
CONNECTS: 2 doors (it's a through-room). Pods create tight lanes -> tactical.

--- 4.4 : POWER PLANT / REACTOR ---
LORE: The facility reactor; breached and irradiated.
DIMENSIONS: 9x8 to 12x9 (LARGE-class footprint).
WALLS: 'Y' radiation-burned dominant near the core; 'R' emergency-strip on

PROPS: 2-4 '%' generator/reactor units in a central cluster or two rows; 'b'

FLOOR MIX: 'l'/'u' dominant (dark, only emergency-lit); 'f' flicker at chokepoints;

bulkhead.
PROPS: '&' bio-pods inside cells (some broken); 'm' corpses + '.' blood (failed
containment); '#' cameras; sparse 'L' lockers. A central walkway with cells on
each side.
FLOOR MIX: 'u' unlit + 'f' flicker dominant (power failing); minimal ' ' lit.
Maximum dread. Pair with gore walls for "the breach happened here."
CONNECTS: 2 doors (through-corridor block). High enemy-spawn bias (den).


===================================================================
SECTION 5 — LEVEL GENERATION UPDATES
===================================================================
Extends the IMPLEMENTED RoomType pipeline (level-generation-improvements.txt).
Generation structure (placement -> connectivity -> doors -> typed decoration ->
post-passes -> audit -> stairs) is UNCHANGED. We add enum values, an assignment
pass, per-type decorators, and a wall-reskin post-pass for new wall types.

5.1 ENUM ADDITIONS
  enum RoomType {
    ENTRANCE, STANDARD, LARGE, SERVER_ROOM,            // existing
    MEDICAL_BAY, ARMORY, CRYO_CHAMBER,                 // new
    POWER_PLANT, COMMAND_CENTER, CONTAINMENT_BLOCK     // new
  }

5.2 ASSIGNMENT PASS (assignRoomTypes, extends existing weighted logic)
  Order of assignment (after ENTRANCE = room[0]):
  STEP A — RARE UNIQUES (at most one each per level, claimed first):
    - MEDICAL_BAY:  pick one mid-distance room with interior >= 7x6. Guaranteed
      1 per level (heal economy). If no room qualifies, relax size to 6x5.
    - ARMORY:       pick one room >= 6x6, prefer a dead-end or keycard-gated room.
      Guaranteed ~80% of levels (LEVEL_GEN_ARMORY_CHANCE = 0.80); not strictly
      every level so finding it feels lucky.
    - COMMAND_CENTER: pick the DEEPEST large room (max MST distance from entrance),
      interior >= 8x7. ~50% of levels OR forced if boss level.
  STEP B — LARGE-CLASS SPECIALS (from remaining LARGE-eligible rooms, >= 9x8):
    - POWER_PLANT: roll LEVEL_GEN_POWERPLANT_CHANCE = 0.45 -> POWER_PLANT.
    - else LARGE (existing behavior).
  STEP C — SMALL/MEDIUM SPECIALS (remaining rooms):
    - CRYO_CHAMBER:        roll 0.16 (needs >= 7x7; else skip)
    - CONTAINMENT_BLOCK:   roll 0.16 (needs >= 8x6, elongated; else skip)
    - SERVER_ROOM:         roll 0.16 (existing)
    - else STANDARD.
  HARD CAPS (downgrade to STANDARD once hit):
    MEDICAL_BAY max 1, ARMORY max 1, COMMAND_CENTER max 1,
    POWER_PLANT max 1, CRYO_CHAMBER max 2, CONTAINMENT_BLOCK max 2,
    SERVER_ROOM max 2 (existing). STANDARD must remain the plurality.
  DEPTH SCALING (if/when level depth exists): increase gore 'G' and rad 'Y'
  reskin density, CONTAINMENT_BLOCK/POWER_PLANT chance, and enemy density with
  depth. Until depth exists, use the flat probabilities above.

5.3 PER-TYPE DECORATORS
  Each new RoomType gets a decorateXxx(room, grid, random) method called from the
  decoration switch, mirroring decorateServerRoom. Each sets: a dominant wall
  reskin char (its native wall), a prop layout (Section 4), and a floor-lighting
  bias. Reuse existing placeProps/assignFloorLighting with type-specific params:
    - MEDICAL_BAY:    bright bias (low 'u'/'f'), guaranteed 1-2 'H'/'+' pickups,
                      lockers along one wall, low enemy density.
    - ARMORY:         loot-dense props (=,C,B), corpses+shotguns, mid enemy.
    - CRYO_CHAMBER:   two pod rows (&), dim ('l'/'u'), mid enemy.
    - POWER_PLANT:    central % cluster + barrels, dark, HIGH hazard, high enemy.
    - COMMAND_CENTER: terminal row (T), cameras, clean, boss-candidate flag.
    - CONTAINMENT_BLOCK: cell layout (alternating N/x fronts + & inside), gore,
                      dark, HIGHEST enemy density (den).

5.4 WALL-RESKIN POST-PASS (new walls)
  Run AFTER assignWallVariety, as a TYPED region reskin (recommended in procedural-
  wall-types.txt OPEN Q6). For each room, with probability per its type, convert
  eligible 'x' perimeter tiles (those adjacent to the room interior) to the room's
  native wall char:
    MEDICAL_BAY      -> 'M' (70%), 'Q' (10%)
    ARMORY           -> 'X' (50%), 'h' (15%), 'k' on door-flank tiles
    CRYO_CHAMBER     -> 'Z' (70%), 'N' (15%)
    POWER_PLANT      -> 'Y' (40% near core side), 'R' (20% approach), 'k' perimeter
    COMMAND_CENTER   -> 'N' (30% on one wall as a window band), 'R' (15%), 't' (10%)
    CONTAINMENT_BLOCK-> 'N' (40% cell fronts, alternate with 'x'), 'Q' (15%), 'G' (depth%)
  GLOBAL accents (any room, low prob, after type reskin):
    'R' emergency strip in 2-4 tile BANDS along corridors flanking keycard doors.
    'X' blast-scar near any 'm' corpse cluster (combat memory), 10%.
  RULE: reskin in BANDS/CLUSTERS, never single scattered tiles (matches rust/gore
  discipline) so a wall reads as belonging to its room.

5.5 PROP DENSITY RULES (new rooms)
  Base LEVEL_GEN_PROP_CHANCE = 0.13 stays for STANDARD. Per-type overrides:
    MEDICAL_BAY      0.16 (orderly), guarantee >=1 medkit + >=1 stim.
    ARMORY           0.22 (cluttered last-stand), guarantee >=2 weapon racks '='.
    CRYO_CHAMBER     0.20 (pod rows are structured, place along walls not random).
    POWER_PLANT      0.18, guarantee 2-4 '%' central + 2-3 barrels.
    COMMAND_CENTER   0.15, guarantee a terminal row (3-5 'T') + 2 cameras '#'.
    CONTAINMENT_BLOCK 0.18, structured cells (& inside cell tiles).
  STRUCTURED placement (rows/walls) for rack/pod/terminal rooms should use a
  deterministic wall-hugging loop, NOT the random-scatter placeProps, so the
  "row of racks/pods/consoles" silhouette reads cleanly.

5.6 ENSURING RARE-ROOM UNIQUENESS
  Claimed-first + hard-cap-of-1 (Step A) guarantees Medical/Armory/Command appear
  at most once. Boolean flags (medicalBayPlaced, armoryPlaced, commandPlaced)
  short-circuit further assignment. Medical Bay is the only GUARANTEED room
  (heal economy); the rest are probabilistic so each run's room set differs.

   prime boss arena (terminal row, observation glass, deep placement); Containment
   Block / Power Plant are alt arenas. Generator can flag the chosen room.
4. HAZARD CHAIN REACTIONS (hazard-chain-reactions-fire-toxic-explosive.txt):
   Power Plant '%' generators + 'b'/'B' barrels + 'Y' rad walls form a destructible
   danger cluster — a future explosion chain set-piece.
5. MEDKIT / STIM ECONOMY (medkit-field-medicine-system.txt, levelup-stim-boon):
   Medical Bay is the guaranteed heal stop and stim/medkit source; '@' dispensers
   add an optional risk/reward heal interaction.

   (den), telegraphed by 'G' gore + broken '&' pods; Medical Bay = low density
   (relief beat). Gore/rad wall density doubles as the difficulty dial.

   the new deliberate walls (M/Q/R/Z/Y/N/k) read too "intentional" to be push-walls.


===================================================================
BALANCE / TUNING (all values PLACEHOLDER — verify in 3D view + playtest)
===================================================================
- Rare-room guarantees: Medical 100%, Armory 80%, Command 50% (or forced on boss
  levels). Tune so a run has 1-2 "destination" rooms, not five.
- Wall reskin probabilities (5.4) are per-room dominance targets, not per-tile
  noise — apply in bands. If walls look too busy at distance, halve the accents.
- Prop densities (5.5) trade clutter vs navigability. Structured rows (racks/pods/
  terminals) must leave a 1-tile walk lane — verify no room becomes impassable
  (the existing connectivity audit is the safety net but won't catch a room the
  player can enter but not cross).
- Texture sizes 128 default; raise rad 'Y' and glass 'N' to 256 only if their
  high-contrast cracks alias.
- Heal economy: Medical Bay should restore enough to matter but not trivialize a
  level (tune medkit count there against enemy density).
