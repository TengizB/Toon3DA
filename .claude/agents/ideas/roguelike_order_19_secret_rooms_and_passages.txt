TITLE: Secret Rooms & Hidden Passages — Procgen

OVERVIEW
========
Some wall tiles are lies. A SECRET WALL raycasts and looks exactly like a normal
wall, but conceals a hidden room behind it. The player reveals it by SEARCHING:
either an explicit Use/E press while facing it, OR an automatic detection chance
that ticks while the player stands adjacent (scaled by a Perception stat). On
reveal, the wall slides open and exposes a small hidden room with bonus loot
(extra ammo, a rare weapon, or a special artifact). Each generated floor seeds
0-2 secrets, and a secrets-found counter is shown on the floor-clear screen. This
is the classic Doom pushwall / DCSS hidden-door staple under the strict tile +
turn + cardinal rules (Roguelike Pillars: Exploration + Risk/Reward).

RELATIONSHIP TO EXISTING WORK: secret-rooms-hidden-passages.txt already specs the
core REVEAL MECHANIC (active search via E, the bump-hint, the recess animation,
the SecretManager). THIS document is the ROGUELIKE GENERATION + PERCEPTION +
REWARD-TIER + COUNTER layer on top of it. It does not re-spec the reveal engine;
it defines how the GENERATOR places secrets, the auto-detect/Perception option,
what loot tier goes in secrets vs normal rooms, and the floor-clear counter.
Implement the reveal engine from the existing doc; implement placement/percep-
tion/rewards/counter from this one.

DETECTION MECHANIC (two complementary paths)
============================================
PATH A — ACTIVE SEARCH (deterministic; from the existing reveal doc)
- Player faces a tile from an adjacent floor tile, presses E. One turn consumed.
- If the faced tile is a secret wall → reveal (recess animation, permanent floor
  conversion). If it is a plain wall → a dull "nothing here" thunk, turn still
  consumed (searching is never free; matters once alert/turn-budget systems
  exist).
- This always works: a thorough player who searches a suspicious dead-end WILL
  find the secret. No RNG gate on guaranteed-required progress.

PATH B — PASSIVE AUTO-DETECT (NEW here; Perception-scaled, convenience)
- While the player STANDS adjacent to (and ideally facing) a secret wall, each
  player turn rolls a detection chance:
      detectChance = SECRET_BASE_DETECT_CHANCE + perception * SECRET_PERCEPTION_BONUS
  (e.g. base 0.0-0.15, +bonus per Perception point). On success, the secret is
  AUTO-REVEALED (same recess animation) and a "you notice a seam..." toast fires.
- Rationale: rewards a high-Perception build with "the walls show themselves,"
  while a low-Perception player must actively search. If no Perception stat
  exists yet, set base to a small flat chance (or 0 → pure active search) via the
  constant; the system degrades gracefully.
- HINT FEEDBACK: when the player is adjacent to an undetected secret, a VERY
  subtle audio/visual hint may fire on the bump or on near-misses (a faint
  "hollow" thunk on bump-hint, per the existing doc) — never a blatant marker
  before reveal.
- Once detected (either path), the tile is flagged on the minimap (a "?" or a
  distinct passage marker) so the player can come back.


PROCEDURAL PLACEMENT
====================
PER-FLOOR SECRET BUDGET
- Each generated floor seeds 0-2 secrets (SECRET_COUNT_MIN=0, MAX=2). Weighted so
  many floors have exactly 1; some have 0 (so finding one feels special); rarely
  2. Depth can nudge the average up slightly.

WHERE TO CARVE A SECRET (generator algorithm)
1. After the main level (rooms, corridors, exit, locks) is built, the
   SecretPlacer looks for candidate walls: a wall tile that has FLOOR/walkable on
   the player-accessible side AND has room on the far side to carve a 1-3 tile
   hidden pocket WITHOUT breaking into another room or the level edge.
2. Bias candidates toward "suspicious" spots a player would test: dead-end
   corridors, alcoves, rooms with an odd asymmetry, the back wall of a small
   nook. Avoid placing a secret on a wall the player can never stand adjacent to.
3. Carve the hidden pocket (1-3 floor tiles) behind the chosen wall; set the
   wall tile to the SECRET-WALL char (looks identical to its neighbors — match
   the adjacent wall's texture family so it blends). Place the reward inside.
4. INVARIANT: secrets are NEVER on the required path. No secret may be the only
   way to reach the exit or a required key. Secrets are pure optional bonus.
   (Reachability check: removing all secrets must leave the floor solvable.)
5. Optional spice: a secret can connect to ANOTHER already-reachable area as a
   SHORTCUT (skips backtracking) instead of a dead pocket — but still optional.

SECRET-ROOM SIZE: small (1-3 tiles). These are caches and panic-pockets, not
full rooms. A "secret room" can occasionally be a 2x2 vault for a marquee reward.


REWARD TIERS — SECRET vs NORMAL ROOM
====================================
Normal rooms hand out the bread-and-butter (stims, small ammo, common armor).
Secrets pay BETTER and are the home of the rare stuff (risk/reward: you only get
it if you looked):
- COMMON SECRET (most): a generous bonus cache — large ammo pack, field medkit,
  security vest. "Worth the search."
- UNCOMMON SECRET: a CONSUMABLE/UPGRADE — grenades, a damage token, a spare
  key (ties into roguelike_order_17), a stack of credits (ties into
  roguelike_order_18).
- RARE SECRET (low chance, biased deeper): a RARE WEAPON or a SPECIAL ARTIFACT
  (a run-defining passive: extra max HP, fire resistance, a re-roll charm, etc.).
- Reward tier rolls per secret; deeper floors weight toward better tiers.
- A floor's BEST loot should disproportionately live in secrets, so the
  exploration pillar is genuinely rewarded — but never REQUIRED loot.


FLOOR-CLEAR SECRET COUNTER
==========================
- The run tracks, per floor, secretsTotal (seeded) and secretsFound (revealed).
- On the floor-clear screen (shown on descent, or as a small toast when stepping
  on '>'), display "SECRETS: 1 / 2" for that floor. Classic Doom intermission
  vibe. A "100% secrets" floor gives a small flair (a star, a one-line praise).
- The run total (e.g. "secrets found this run") can feed the death/score screen
  (roguelike_order_1) as a completionist score.

VISUAL DESIGN
=============
- SECRET WALL (undetected): visually identical to neighboring walls — the whole
  point. May carry an EXTREMELY subtle authoring tell (a hairline seam, a missing
  rivet) only a sharp-eyed player spots; never an obvious marker.
- REVEAL ANIMATION (from existing doc): the wall RECESSES/slides open like a
  pushwall over SECRET_REVEAL_DURATION, then becomes permanent floor. A puff of
  dust + a "stone grind / pneumatic hiss" sound. One-way (never re-closes).
- DETECT TOAST: "You notice a seam in the wall..." on passive detect; on active
  E-search success a "HIDDEN PASSAGE!" banner.
- "NOTHING HERE" feedback: dull thunk + faint screen-shake on a failed E-search
  against a plain wall (so searching has texture without revealing where secrets
  are).
- MINIMAP: revealed/detected secrets shown with a distinct marker (a "?" or a
  highlighted passage) so the player can navigate back.
- FLOOR-CLEAR: "SECRETS: 1 / 2" line on the intermission/clear UI.


BALANCE NOTES
=============
- 0-2 per floor, averaging ~1, keeps secrets special, not a chore. Tune the
  count weights so 0-secret floors exist (scarcity makes finds feel earned).
- NEVER gate required progress behind a secret. Removing all secrets must leave
  every floor fully solvable (enforced by SecretPlacer flood-fill).
- Active search must be reliable: a player who methodically searches dead-ends
  WILL find secrets. Passive detect is a bonus, not the primary path.
- If a Perception stat doesn't exist yet, ship with SECRET_BASE_DETECT_CHANCE
  small or 0 (pure active search) — the system works either way.
- Secret loot should be NOTABLY better than normal-room loot (the reward for
  looking), but optional — the floor's REQUIRED loot stays on the main path.
- Don't make searching mandatory everywhere: the subtle authoring tells +
  "suspicious dead-end" placement bias teach players WHERE to search, so they
  aren't forced to E-mash every wall.


INTERACTIONS
============
- LOCKED DOORS (roguelike_order_17): a secret can hide a SPARE single-use key or
  the key to an optional bonus door — secrets and locks layer (hidden key opens
  visible bonus).
- SHOP (roguelike_order_18): a secret can stash a pile of CREDITS, feeding the
  economy and giving the shop more to do.
- BOSS FLOORS (roguelike_order_16): boss arenas have NO secrets (the boss is the
  content); secrets live on normal floors.
- TRAPS/HAZARDS (roguelike_order_20): a secret can be GUARDED by a trap (a
  pressure plate just inside) — risk/reward made literal; or a secret can be the
  safe bypass around a hazard.
- XP/LEVELING (roguelike_order_11): Perception stat drives passive auto-detect;
  investing in Perception literally reveals more of the world.
- PERMADEATH/SCORE (roguelike_order_1): run-total secrets found feeds the score
  screen for completionists.
- FLOOR-CEILING LIGHTING: secret pockets can use unlit ('u') floors for a
  "forgotten maintenance crawlspace" feel.
