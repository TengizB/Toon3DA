CATEGORY: Combat
TITLE: Arc Cannon — chain-lightning anti-swarm energy weapon
---

CONCEPT
The Arc Cannon fires an electric bolt that strikes the first enemy in the facing line, then LEAPS
laterally to adjacent enemies, creeping sideways through a cluster. It is the anti-swarm specialist:
no other weapon in the arsenal chains between separated targets. Where the Incinerator sprays a fixed
cone and the Grenade Launcher lobs a plus-splash, the Arc Cannon dynamically follows the shape of a
mob — reward for letting enemies bunch up.

NICHE / GAP FILLED
Existing fire behaviours: single-target hitscan (Shotgun), line-pierce (Plasma, Railgun), burst-fire
line (Chaingun), fixed cone (Incinerator), bouncing plus-splash (Grenade Launcher). MISSING: a weapon
that chains damage between DISCRETE, SEPARATED enemies. The Arc Cannon is that mechanic.

FIRE BEHAVIOUR (turn-based, deterministic)
1. Primary bolt: standard hitscan, marches the facing line, stops at the FIRST enemy (no pierce),
   applies distance-scaled primary damage. Walls / closed doors / cover / explosive barrels stop it.
2. Lateral chain from the primary target's tile: up to ARC_CANNON_CHAIN_JUMPS (3) leaps. Each leap
   inspects the four cardinal neighbours of the current arc tile in a fixed order (left, right,
   forward, back relative to the player's facing) and jumps to the first neighbour that holds a
   not-yet-struck enemy and is not blocked. Each jump deals CHAIN_DAMAGE_MULTIPLIER^jump (0.6^n) of
   the primary damage. The chain dies as soon as no eligible neighbour is found. A visited-tile set
   prevents re-hitting the same enemy.

AMMO
CELLS (energy weapon) — shared with Plasma Rifle and Incinerator. Thematically correct; no new ammo
type needed, so the scarcity model and reserve caps are untouched.

BALANCE (docs/game-balance-authority.txt WEAPONS living table)
Role: BURST / SPECIALIST. Credited on the SINGLE-TARGET primary line only (same convention the
Grenade Launcher uses on its centre splash):
  damage 28, clipSize 7, reloadTime 2, dropCoeff 0.08, range 7.
  sustainedDPT = 7*28 / (7+2) = 21.8 ; ammoEff = 28 ; powerScore = 21.8*sqrt(28/40) = 18.2  -> band 18-26 OK.
The lateral chain (~61 dmg across 4 targets) is UNCREDITED bonus AoE, well under the heavy-band
Grenade Launcher's 130-dmg ceiling. Distinct by MECHANIC, not band.
