IMPLEMENTATION NOTE (2026-10-21) — where the build diverged from the plan, and why
  1. World.java is touched (TECHNICAL NOTES did not list it), paint only, 3 call sites: the death beat and the MERGE
     hold call FadeOverlayRenderer.render(..., showDepthReadout=false) - the old "SUBLEVEL N" banner sat on top of
     THIS BODY DIED in every death (A4: alone on the black); the settings screen opened from the title passes
     overLaunchScreen=true and draws the launch blackout, or the title showed through the 0.75 scrim.
  2. UiKit gained one overload, screenHeaderRule(shapes, x, y, width, ink..., alpha) (the R1 rule with a FACILITY_CYAN
     tick). TECHNICAL NOTES expected no new method.
  3. A "FACILITY plate" is UiKit.card inked FACILITY_CYAN_DIM (lit FACILITY_CYAN when SELECTED / PRESSED), not plate()
     with a cyan ink: an IDLE plate has a UI_LINE border and no ink, so it could not read as the machine's.
  4. CONTINUE is a SELECTED SUIT plate (the card's one way onward; an IDLE plate carries no amber). REPORT / BACK /
     RETURN have no PRESSED state: taps commit on touch, as at base.
  5. Pause / settings brackets are SUIT_AMBER_DIM (the order-5 outer-frame rule for suit screens; no outer panel, so
     they frame header + rows). Facility screens' outer brackets are full FACILITY_CYAN (R5 / R8 named it).
  6. Shop: offer frames are FACILITY_CYAN_DIM per R9, so the offer's rarity colour stays on its ICON only (R9 wins
     over the ink table's generic "rarity frames" SEMANTIC row). The offer name stays TYPE_CAPTION and the confirm
     prompt TYPE_CAPTION: their fixed bands collide at TYPE_BODY. A DISABLED (unaffordable / sold) card gets an opaque
     UI_PANEL underlay - most offers are unaffordable, and the half-alpha DISABLED fill let the HUD show through
     (reviewer non-blocking 1).
  7. EventChoiceOverlayRenderer TAP_Y bug fix: it was CARD_HEIGHT - 30 measured from the bottom, which drew "TAP TO
     CHOOSE" over the choice label; it is 30 from the bottom, as its comment says. Text position only, no touch rect.
  8. Android colours are written six-digit #RRGGBB (opaque): the old "#F5A623FF" was eight digits, which Android reads
     as #AARRGGBB. A8 was verified by reading the XML only - this container cannot build the android module, and no
     cold launch was seen.
  9. The death stroke, title and every framing text are unshadowed (they sit on black or a scrim); story panels over
     the 3D view use UiKit.text's shadow.
 10. Follow-ups (pre-existing, seen in the base build, not fixed here): the floor's event text is drawn over the shop,
     the event console and the exchange prompt; at LARGEST a bark wraps to 3 lines but holds 2; MAP framing truncates
     long node labels; the title's status block touches EREBUS at TYPE_DISPLAY and the settings header sits in the
     status lines' rows; the event console's left-aligned title starts over the mini-map (reviewer non-blocking 2);
     the HEAVY "#" reads as a blob; the A11 corridors are floor-1 geometry under forced moods with no close enemy.

OVERVIEW
--------
The last order. It applies the UiKit to every remaining screen, using the ink the
fiction assigns. FACILITY CYAN is for what the facility shows you: the route map's
nav console, the fabricator shop, event consoles, the reprint machine (title screen,
boot card status, run report, depth readout). SUIT AMBER is for what is yours: the
pause/settings menu, your answers in an exchange, CONTINUE. The story panels take
their brackets from the speaker, whose accents now come from the palette (ORA = suit
amber, the Organization = facility cyan). It also fixes the first impression before
the game draws a frame: the Android launch window is white today (a white flash
before a black horror title) and becomes near-black.

What was SAID: "menus ... store-screenshot quality". What is NEEDED: the title screen,
the boot card and the route map are the screenshots a store page leads with, and each
still speaks its own dialect. The launch flash is the first thing every player sees.

PLAYER EXPERIENCE
-----------------
Launch: black, then the machine's cold cyan-framed words telling you that you are
already dead. In play: your amber suit. When the facility speaks (the nav console,
the fabricator, an event terminal, the reprint machine), the frame turns cyan. When
ORA speaks, her panel wears her amber. When the Organization speaks, theirs wears the
facility's cyan, because it is the facility. None of this is explained; it is
consistent enough to be learned.
