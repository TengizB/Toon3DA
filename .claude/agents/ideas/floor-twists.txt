OVERVIEW
--------
Some generated COMBAT floors roll ONE "floor twist": a named rule change that alters HOW the floor
plays, not how it looks. The twist is printed on the route-map card BEFORE the player commits, so it
becomes a route decision ("the dark floor, or the one with a hunter on it?"). v1 ships three twists
that need no new tile symbol, no new enemy art and no persistence schema change: BLACKOUT, SALVAGE
and HUNTED.

What the owner SAID: five pitch twists (BLACKOUT, LOCKDOWN, HUNTED, FLOODED, SALVAGE), shown on the
map node. What the owner WANTS: floor-to-floor variety in decision-making, bought with rules rather
than art, and a map that offers more than "which node type". The design answers the need with the
ELITE affix machinery the route layer already has (NodeAffixRegistry), widened to COMBAT nodes,
instead of a parallel system.

WHY THESE THREE (and not LOCKDOWN / FLOODED first):
  - SALVAGE is pure data on machinery that already ships (encounter budget override, owned-ammo
    cache request, scattered pickup guarantees, the NodeEconomics ledger). Lowest risk, and it is the
    twist that proves the pricing path end to end.
  - BLACKOUT changes INFORMATION (no mini-map, dark) and is made fair by changing PERCEPTION for
    both sides (enemies wake from much closer). It is one render seam (tile brightness), one HUD
    toggle and one EnemyManager perception override — no art.
  - HUNTED changes TEMPO: a floor that punishes lingering. It reuses an existing archetype
    (IRON_STALKER, a MINI_ELITE, sprite already shipped) and the existing spawnEnemy path; the only
    new behaviour is "walk the player's own trail", which is robust precisely because every trail
    tile is a tile the player already proved walkable.
  - LOCKDOWN needs a per-door re-seal state machine, a terminal interaction and a no-softlock proof
    across all three generators (rooms, linear corridor, cave). FLOODED needs a new per-tile
    movement cost (almost certainly a new FIXED tile meaning plus water art). Both are the expensive
    ones; both are follow-ups (SCOPE OUT).


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - The twist is a SCOPE of the existing ELITE affix registry: NodeAffixDefinition gains a scope
    (ELITE_AFFIX / FLOOR_TWIST) and the twist's typed modifiers; three FLOOR_TWIST rows are
    registered next to the five ELITE affixes in RouteRegistries.registerAffixes.
  - Map-gen rolls at most one twist onto eligible COMBAT nodes (chance, per-twist weight, per-twist
    minimum depth), deterministically from the node's existing affix sub-stream.
  - The route console shows the twist on the card (corner tag, already drawn for any affix) AND as
    a third line in the confirm bar (name + one-line rule), for any focused node carrying an affix
    or a twist.
  - The COMBAT floor profile folds the twist into the floor plan; World (and SimWorld) apply the
    twist's runtime modifiers after the floor is built, through the existing FloorEffects seam.
  - BLACKOUT, SALVAGE and HUNTED, fully playable on device.
  - One ORA teaching line per twist (three new TeachingTopic rows), one-shot for the save, fired on
    the first arrival on that twist; an event-text sting naming the twist on EVERY arrival.
  - The balance contract: a NodeEconomics row per twist, one new audit rule (R-TWIST), the
    trajectory audit folding twists in, one new behavioural band (S-TWIST), a TWIST REPORT in the
    sim summary. Both gates green.
  - Run telemetry: the route string records the twist (e.g. "combat:HUNTED").
  - Docs updated in the same commits (see IMPLEMENTATION PLAN step 6).

OUT (deliberately not doing now)
  - LOCKDOWN (doors seal behind you, a terminal reopens them) — follow-up idea
    `floor-twists-lockdown`; needs a door re-seal state, a 'T' terminal interaction and a
    softlock proof per generator. DoorManager.lockArenaDoor is the obvious starting seam.
  - FLOODED (tiles cost an extra turn) — follow-up; needs a per-tile movement-cost meaning that is
    very likely a new FIXED symbol (STRICT RULE) plus water floor art.
  - Twists on any node type other than COMBAT. ELITE keeps its own affixes (one affix slot per
    node, and ELITE+twist would stack two threat multipliers past what R-RISK-PREMIUM prices).
    MYSTERY, CACHE, SHOP, REST, EVENT, BOSS, REGION_GATE never carry a twist.
  - Two twists on one floor. Never.
  - A hunter that follows you to the next floor, or more than one hunter per floor.
  - A HUD countdown widget for HUNTED. The warning is event text + sound (see RULES); a persistent
    clock is a later polish idea if playtests ask for it.
  - Twist-aware sim policies (TACTICAL does not prefer or avoid twists). The sim plays whatever the
    map deals it; S-TWIST checks no twist is a death trap.
  - New sounds. Every cue reuses a shipped GameSoundId.
  - Twists on hand-made / file-loaded levels (no route node, no twist).
  - Any change to existing ELITE affix numbers, MYSTERY weights or node-type prices.
  - The codex. No archive entry for twists in v1 (the card and ORA say everything needed).
  - Fixing ELITE affixes' card display beyond the shared confirm-bar line described below.


GAMEPLAY LOOP
-------------
1. Route select. The FACILITY NAV console shows the next layer. A COMBAT card that rolled a twist
   carries the amber affix corner tag. Focusing it, the confirm bar reads, top to bottom:
       RESISTANCE                       (threat word, node accent colour — unchanged)
       STANDARD SUPPLY                  (reward promise, grey — unchanged)
       HUNTED — A HUNTER WALKS YOUR TRAIL   (twist line, amber — new)
2. Player commits. The floor is built with the twist folded in (SALVAGE: more threat, more supply).
3. Arrival (first PLAYING frame after the floor fade): an amber event-text sting shows the twist
   name ("BLACKOUT" / "SALVAGE" / "HUNTED"). If the player has never been on this twist before, ORA
   says its teaching line (bark, CONTROL_HINT channel).
4. Play. Every player action is one turn, as always:
   - BLACKOUT: no mini-map ("NAV OFFLINE" plate in its place); the floor is dark except the exit;
     dormant enemies wake only when the player is within 2 tiles, or in their line of sight within
     4 tiles.
   - SALVAGE: more enemies and more supply lying around.
   - HUNTED: the floor counts the player's turns. At turn 40 - 5 = 35 a warning sting fires; at turn
     40 an Iron Stalker is printed at the tile the player arrived on, already alerted, and walks the
     player's trail every turn until it has a direct shot at them, then fights normally. Killing it
     drops a bounty. Taking the exit first means it never arrives.
5. Exit. The twist ends with the floor. Nothing carries over.


RULES
-----
GENERATION
  R1  Only COMBAT nodes may roll a twist. A node holds at most one affix-or-twist (the existing
      RouteNode.affix slot; no new field on RouteNode).
  R2  Roll: in RouteMapGenerator's finalisation pass, from the node's EXISTING affix sub-stream
      (GameMath.mixSeed(nodeSeed, AFFIX_STREAM_SALT)), with chance TWIST_ROLL_CHANCE_COMBAT; then a
      weighted pick among the FLOOR_TWIST rows whose minDepth <= the node's depth, by each row's
      weight. No eligible row -> no twist. The same seed always yields the same twists, and adding
      twists changes no other roll (node type, generator, ELITE affix all draw from other streams
      or from nodes of a different type).
  R3  Minimum depths: BLACKOUT 2, SALVAGE 2, HUNTED 4. Depth 1 never carries a twist (floor one is
      the teaching floor). Boss depths never hold a COMBAT node, so they never carry one; the COMBAT
      profile additionally ignores any twist on a boss depth (defensive).
  R4  A twisted node's map hint keeps the existing " — <NAME>" suffix (RouteMapGenerator already
      appends any affix's display name), so every place that shows the hint shows the twist.

FLOOR PLAN (headless, in the COMBAT profile)
  R5  The COMBAT profile folds a FLOOR_TWIST into the plan with the same typed-modifier idiom
      EliteProfile uses for affixes — no per-twist code path:
        - budgetScaleMultiplier -> an EnemyBudgetOverride on the plan (depth ramp still first);
        - extra scattered owned-ammo boxes -> the plan's AmmoCacheRequest (World fulfils it with the
          player's OWNED ammo types, exactly like the cache);
        - extra scattered pickups -> Guarantees.pickup(symbol, n, Placement.SCATTERED, seed) using
          existing symbols only ('+' stim pack, 'a' armour shard);
        - FloorEffects gains the twist id (new field + `withTwist(id)`), plus withSting(name) and
          withStatLog(twist id) — the run route string becomes e.g. "combat:SALVAGE".
  R6  Twists never change the floor's generator, layout, node type, danger pips or region.

RUNTIME (World and SimWorld both apply these after rebuildForLevel, in applyPendingFloorEffects /
its SimWorld mirror; every modifier resets on the next floor build)
  R7  BLACKOUT
        a. Mini-map: LevelRenderer is not drawn this floor; in its rectangle a static dark plate with
           the amber text NAV OFFLINE is drawn (HudConstants). The player dot is not drawn either.
        b. Light: every floor/wall/prop brightness read through Level.getTileBrightness is
           multiplied by TWIST_BLACKOUT_LIGHT_MULTIPLIER (0.45, RenderConstants, cosmetic) — EXCEPT
           the exit/stairs tile, which keeps its LIT brightness and becomes the floor's beacon.
           Carried as a per-Level light multiplier (default 1.0) so it dies with the Level.
        c. Never darkened: enemy health bars, intent/telegraph icons, the weapon HUD, HUD panels,
           event text, bark panels. Enemy billboards are drawn with a minimum shade of
           TWIST_BLACKOUT_ENEMY_MIN_SHADE (0.35, RenderConstants) so a body in the dark is a
           silhouette, never invisible.
        d. Perception (balance-bearing): for the dormant-WAKE check only (EnemyManager Phase A),
           the proximity radius is TWIST_BLACKOUT_WAKE_RADIUS_TILES (2, default 4) and the
           wake-by-sight range is TWIST_BLACKOUT_WAKE_SIGHT_TILES (4, default 8). Chain-alert radius,
           attack ranges, ranged LOS for attacks and isSameCardinalLine are UNCHANGED — an awake
           enemy fights exactly as it does anywhere else.
  R8  SALVAGE — no runtime modifier. Everything is in the plan (R5): threat x1.30 (more bodies,
      through the same override SWARM uses), +4 owned-ammo boxes scattered, +1 '+' stim pack,
      +1 'a' armour shard scattered. Numbers are placeholders tuned into R-TWIST's band.
  R9  HUNTED — the hunter clock (new headless class, enemy package):
        a. States: WAITING -> WARNED -> ARRIVED -> DONE.
           WAITING: counts completed player turns on this floor (turns, never seconds; overlays and
             menus advance no turns, so they advance no clock).
           WAITING -> WARNED at turn TWIST_HUNTED_ARRIVAL_TURNS - TWIST_HUNTED_WARNING_TURNS
             (40 - 5): amber event text "SOMETHING IS ON THE STAIRS BEHIND YOU"
             (HudConstants.TWIST_HUNTED_WARNING_TEXT).
           WARNED -> ARRIVED at turn >= TWIST_HUNTED_ARRIVAL_TURNS, on the first turn the spawn is
             legal: the arrival tile (the tile the player stood on when the floor began) is not
             blocked, not occupied, and is at least TWIST_HUNTED_MIN_SPAWN_DISTANCE_TILES (5,
             Manhattan) from the player. Otherwise it retries every turn, forever (a player camping
             the arrival tile delays it — and gains nothing, because the floor's loot is elsewhere).
             On arrival: EnemyManager.spawnEnemy(IRON_STALKER, arrival tile, depth) — normal depth
             scaling, alerted, relic spawn-health multiplier applies as for any spawn — flagged as
             the trail hunter; red event text "IT'S HERE" (HudConstants.TWIST_HUNTED_ARRIVAL_TEXT);
             the INSECT family ALERT voice plays at the hunter's tile through the existing
             EnemyVoiceListener (so it is heard from behind, panned by the existing perception).
           ARRIVED -> DONE when the hunter dies. Leaving the floor in any state discards the clock.
        b. One hunter per floor, never respawned.
  R10 THE TRAIL. From the floor's first turn, every time the player SETTLES on a tile different from
      the last recorded one, that tile is appended to a pre-allocated ring buffer of
      TWIST_HUNTED_TRAIL_CAPACITY (512, EnemyConstants) entries. Recorded only on HUNTED floors. No
      allocation per step.
  R11 THE HUNTER'S TURN (inside EnemyManager's existing EXECUTE/COMMIT phases; commit-then-resolve
      and intent icons unchanged):
        a. If the hunter has line of sight to the player (existing hasLineOfSight), or is within
           Manhattan 2 of the player, it behaves exactly as an ordinary alerted IRON_STALKER (moveset,
           melee, abilities) for that turn.
        b. Otherwise it follows the trail: its target is the NEWEST trail entry that is cardinally
           adjacent to its tile and newer than its current trail index (it cuts the player's loops
           and backtracks); if none, the next entry after its index. It steps there.
        c. Next trail tile is a CLOSED door: the hunter spends this turn forcing it; the door is
           passable for the hunter on its next turn regardless of animation timing. A forced door
           plays DOOR_OPEN at its tile, does NOT fire DoorOpenedListener (no relic door blast/heal),
           and does NOT count toward the player's opened-door count. A locked or arena-locked door is
           never on the trail (the player could not pass it), and the hunter may not force one.
        d. Next trail tile occupied by another enemy: it waits; after 2 waiting turns it takes one
           ordinary greedy step toward the player and re-acquires the trail from there (R11b).
        e. Two consecutive trail entries not cardinally adjacent (the player took a portal): when
           the hunter stands on the earlier entry, its next move places it on the later one if free
           (it "knows the way"); else it waits.
        f. Trail overwritten past the hunter's index (buffer wrapped) or empty: ordinary greedy
           stepToward behaviour.
        g. The hunter never uses the exit and never leaves the floor.
  R12 BOUNTY. When the trail hunter dies it drops, at its tile, TWIST_HUNTED_BOUNTY_AMMO_BOXES (3)
      owned-ammo boxes (same owned-aware resolution as the cache; tile full -> nearest free floor
      tile) in addition to its ordinary XP, credit and loot drops.
  R13 Ranged/cardinal rule: unchanged for every enemy, the hunter included (IRON_STALKER is
      melee-plus-ranged and already obeys isSameCardinalLine).

PRESENTATION
  R14 Route console confirm bar: when the focused, revealed node carries an affix or twist, a third
      line is drawn at LEGEND_TWIST_OFFSET_Y (placeholder -30, RouteMapConstants) in hazard amber:
      "<NAME> — <RULE LINE>". Rule lines (RouteMapConstants):
        BLACKOUT  "NO MAP · DARK · THEY SEE LESS"
        SALVAGE   "MORE SUPPLY · MORE RESISTANCE"
        HUNTED    "A HUNTER WALKS YOUR TRAIL"
      ELITE affixes get the same line with their display name and a rule line each (IRRADIATED
      "HOT BARRELS", OVERCLOCKED "MEANER RESISTANCE · BIGGER VAULT", SWARM "MORE BODIES · BIGGER
      VAULT", FORTIFIED "COVER · EXTRA ARMOUR", VOLATILE "EXPLOSIVE BARRELS") — today their name is
      hidden behind the C1 promise lines. Truncated with the existing truncateToWidth if too wide.
  R15 The hunter's health-bar frame is drawn in the twist amber (EnemyConstants colour) so the
      player can tell THE hunter from an ordinary Iron Stalker.
  R16 ORA: on arrival on a twist floor, World requests BarkTrigger.CONTROL_HINT with the twist's
      TeachingTopic subject key. One-shot for the save (existing seen-flag mechanism); no re-teach
      (threshold 0). Delivered even at the floor budget (CONTROL_HINT rows are STORY_CRITICAL).


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
-----------------
The map stops being only "which node type" and becomes "which problem do I want": SALVAGE when the
bag is empty and HP is high; BLACKOUT when the build is a short-range brawler that likes enemies
asleep until close; HUNTED when you are strong and fast, or when the twin card is worse. On the
floor, each twist asks one new question: BLACKOUT — "how do I navigate with no map, and can I sneak
past what I can barely see?"; SALVAGE — "can I afford this many fights for this much loot?";
HUNTED — "what is worth stopping for?" HUNTED's counter-play is readable: it walks where you
walked, so keep moving, or pick the ground and kill it for the bounty.

Teaching, for a player who never reads: the card says the rule in four words before the choice; the
sting names it on arrival; ORA's single line explains it once; the twist itself teaches the rest
(the dark is visible, the NAV OFFLINE plate is visible, the warning text and the alert voice from
behind are unmissable). No tutorial screen.

ORA lines (story-strings.properties; ids follow the TeachingCatalog control-hint pattern; plain
words only — legal from Region 1, no proper nouns, no numbers, no senses she lacks):
  story.bark.control.twist_blackout = Power's out on this floor. No map for you — but nothing down
                                      here sees far in the dark either. Go quiet.
  story.bark.control.twist_salvage  = Nobody cleared this floor out. More supplies lying around, and
                                      more of whatever stopped the last crew.
  story.bark.control.twist_hunted   = The floor sensors picked up something coming down behind us.
                                      It'll walk the way you walked. Take what you need and keep moving.
(If TeachingCatalog derives ids differently, use its derived ids — the TEXT above is the spec.)

BALANCE NOTES

NodeEconomics rows (route/RouteEconomics, Kind.TWIST):
  blackout  budgetScale 1.0, no guaranteed payoff (an information-for-perception sidegrade).
  salvage   budgetScale TWIST_SALVAGE_BUDGET_MULT, guaranteedAmmoBoxes TWIST_SALVAGE_AMMO_BOXES,
            guaranteedHealHitPoints = stim heal + armour shard value (the same conversion the
            FORTIFIED row uses).
  hunted    budgetScale 1 + TWIST_HUNTED_THREAT_SHARE, guaranteedAmmoBoxes
            TWIST_HUNTED_BOUNTY_AMMO_BOXES.

New audit rule R-TWIST (BalanceSchema RuleKind.TWIST), evaluated at every depth 1..RUN_FINAL_DEPTH
from the twist's minDepth, priced COMBAT + twist against plain COMBAT through
RouteEconomicsModel.price(ledger, combatRow, twistRow, floor, depth), worst depth reported:
  T1 a twist whose budgetScale > 1: reward premium / threat premium in
     [ROUTE_RISK_PREMIUM_MIN, ROUTE_RISK_PREMIUM_MAX] (the SAME band R-RISK-PREMIUM uses — a
     twist that adds danger must pay for it, never a sucker bet, never free).
  T2 a twist whose budgetScale == 1: total EV within +-TWIST_NEUTRAL_EV_TOLERANCE of plain COMBAT.
  T3 HUNTED honesty: the hunter's actual Threat-Point price at the depth (the same per-archetype TP
     the encounter planner charges for IRON_STALKER at that depth) divided by the COMBAT floor's
     TP budget is <= TWIST_HUNTED_THREAT_SHARE. The ledger may not under-price the hunter.
R-ROUTE-PRICED: already iterates RouteRegistries.affixes().ids(), so a twist with no row fails the
  build with no code change — confirm, do not duplicate.
R-TRAJECTORY: the trajectory walk prices a visited node WITH its twist row folded in (today it
  ignores affixes entirely; ELITE affix folding stays as it is — out of scope). All three policies
  must stay in band. R-PIPS-DERIVED is unchanged (pips are per node TYPE).

New behavioural band S-TWIST (sim/BehavioralBands), never waived, read from the TACTICAL row:
  ST1 for each twist with >= TWIST_SIM_MIN_FLOORS floors played: the floor-death rate on that
      twist's floors <= TWIST_SIM_MAX_DEATH_RATE_RATIO x the floor-death rate on plain COMBAT floors
      in the same depth range. Under-sampled twists are REPORTED "n/a (sample n)" and do not fail;
      the CP that turns the band on records the sample sizes in its handover note.
  ST2 every twist appears at least once in the TACTICAL row's played floors (the roll is live).
SimReport gains a TWIST REPORT block: twist, floors played, floor-death rate, mean turns on floor,
  mean HP drain, hunter arrivals / kills (HUNTED).

will read BLACKOUT as slightly EASIER. That is why BLACKOUT is priced neutral (T2) rather than
priced from the sim, and why the light/minimap half is a feel question for playtests.

INTERACTIONS
------------
  - Route map: twists ride the existing affix slot, sub-stream, hint suffix and corner tag; the
    confirm bar gains the third line for affixes too. Route guarantees (UPGRADE / CALM / PRE-BOSS /
    REAL CHOICES) are untouched — twists never change a node's type.
  - Encounter planner: SALVAGE's threat goes through EnemyBudgetOverride exactly like SWARM; the
    depth ramp is applied first (route invariant).
  - Supply: SALVAGE and the HUNTED bounty use the owned-aware ammo path, so neither dumps ammo the
    player cannot use; '+' and 'a' are existing pickups.
  - Enemies: wake override touches only Phase A; the hunter is an ordinary IRON_STALKER for combat,
    intent, telegraph, abilities, drops and voice. Relics that scale spawn health apply to it.
    Relics that fire on door opening never fire on a hunter-forced door (R11c).
  - Doors: auto-closing doors behind the player are what make the hunter pause (one turn per door)
    — a real, readable counter-play.
  - Narrative: three CONTROL_HINT rows; the floor bark budget is not consumed by them
    (STORY_CRITICAL); the vocabulary ladder is not touched (no proper nouns).
  - Perks/relics: `barrelsOnMinimap` has nothing to show on a BLACKOUT floor (accepted; the map is
    offline). BLINDED status stacks with BLACKOUT (both apply).
  - Telemetry/report: the route string shows "combat:<TWIST>" in the run report automatically.
  - Audio: DOOR_OPEN for forced doors, the INSECT family alert voice for the arrival. No new ids.


EDGE CASES
----------
  - Hand-made / route-map-less floors: no node -> no twist; all modifiers at defaults.
  - MYSTERY delegating to the COMBAT fallback profile: the node is MYSTERY, so no twist folds.
  - Player dies the turn the hunter arrives: normal death; clock discarded with the floor.
  - Player reaches the exit before turn 40: the hunter never arrives; no bounty; nothing carried.
  - Arrival tile permanently blocked (a barrel pushed onto it, an enemy parked on it): retry every
    turn (R9a); never spawns in a wall or on the player.
  - Hunter killed by a barrel, a hazard or a DoT: still DONE, still drops the bounty (it died).
  - Hard-pause overlays (level-up, relic offer, inventory, codex, pause, exchange): no turns pass,
    so the clock and the hunter freeze; barks queue as usual.
  - Action lock mid-animation: the trail records on SETTLE, never mid-slide.
  - App paused (onApplicationPause): nothing persisted; state is in-memory and per-floor.
  - Floor transition mid-effect: rebuildForLevel resets minimap flag, light multiplier, wake
    perception, trail and clock BEFORE the next floor's effects apply.
  - Save wiped (NEW OPERATOR): the three teaching flags reset with every other seen-flag.
  - Trail capacity exceeded on a very long floor: the hunter falls back to greedy (R11f).
  - Portals: R11e.
  - BLACKOUT + exit in an unlit dead-end: the exit is exempt from the dark (R7b), so it stays
    findable without the map.
  - Two COMBAT cards in a layer both twisted: allowed; still a choice (different twists or a
    twist vs a different node type). REAL CHOICES counts node TYPES, unaffected.
