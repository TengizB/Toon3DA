================================================================================
NEW GAME BALANCE — ORDER 2 of 8
THE EXPECTED POWER CURVE & THE GEAR GATE
================================================================================
Requires: new-game-balancr-order-1 (schema + consolidated BalanceConfig).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM (the user's headline complaint)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Once I was able to beat the boss with a level 1 weapon that I've got from the
starting room, and that must not be possible. Finding better weapons must be
essential for survival."

Root causes in the current model (knowledge doc SECTIONS 2, 6, 9; problems 3, 7):
  1. The whole contract anchors "the player" to a FIXED 25 sustained DPT (the
     depth-1 shotgun) forever. Enemies and bosses are priced against a player
     who never upgrades — so never upgrading is viable by construction.
  2. Weapon ability magnitudes are unpriced placeholders; tier/rarity has no
     enforced power meaning, and (before order 1) legendaries could roll at
     run start.
  3. Nothing in the game gets HARDER for a player whose arsenal stagnates:
     enemy HP compounds at only 1.042/floor, which a flat-DPT player out-heals
     with cards alone.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DESIGN — THE EXPECTED ARSENAL CURVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Replace the single fixed anchor with a per-depth EXPECTED PLAYER, made of two
explicit components:

  expectedPlayerDpt(depth) = REFERENCE_PLAYER_DPT
                             * gearCurve(depth)      <- THIS ORDER
                             * cardCurve(depth)      <- order 4 re-derives

  gearCurve(depth): the arsenal the game EXPECTS you to be holding at depth d.
    Modelled as a step-per-region multiplier:
      GEAR_CURVE_PER_REGION = 1.35   (each 5-floor region expects ~+35% weapon
                                      power from found/bought weapons + weapon
                                      levels)
      gearCurve(d) = GEAR_CURVE_PER_REGION ^ floor((d-1) / REGION_BAND_SIZE)
    All new constants in BalanceConfig SECTION 9 (anchors).

  Everything downstream re-prices against expectedPlayerDpt(depth):
    - survivalTurns inside threatPoints() (enemy TP at depth)
    - turnsToKill / golden-ratio checks at region-entry depths
    - the floor TP budget (order 5) and boss HP (order 6)

THE GEAR GATE (what makes upgrades ESSENTIAL, not merely nice):
  Because enemy eHP is priced against gearCurve, a player still holding the
  starting shotgun in region 2 fights at ~74% of expected DPT; in region 3 at
  ~55%. Their golden ratio (TTD/TTK) falls OUT of the fair band — fights become
  slow, ammo demand per kill rises past what the scarcity economy supplies
  (order 3 supplies ammo priced at expected DPT), and the boss fight becomes
  arithmetically unwinnable (order 6 asserts it). Falling behind the gear curve
  is FELT as mounting pressure, then becomes lethal — exactly the roguelike
  "find better gear or die" loop.
  SCHEMA RULE (register in BalanceSchema):
    R-GEARGATE: with the depth-1 starting loadout, turnsToKill vs the region-2
    entry SOLDIER must exceed the soldier golden-band max (i.e. the starting
    weapon must read UNDERPOWERED by region 2), AND region-1 content must
    remain IN band (the start is fair; stagnation is what's punished).

DELIVERING THE CURVE (the game must actually SUPPLY the expected gear —
a gate without supply is just a difficulty spike):
  1. DEPTH-GATED LOOT TIERS (BalanceConfig SECTION 15/loot):
       WEAPON_DROP_TIER_BANDS: region 1 -> COMMON..UNCOMMON, region 2 ->
       UNCOMMON..RARE, region 3 -> RARE..EPIC, region 4+ -> EPIC..LEGENDARY.
       Dropped weapon LEVEL rolls depth-proportional (weapon level already
       grants +10%/level damage — it becomes part of gearCurve, not a bonus
       outside the model).
  2. THE PITY RULE (no run starved of its curve by RNG):
       GUARANTEED_UPGRADE_PER_REGION = 1 — every region must PLACE at least one
       weapon at that region's tier band (armory room, boss-adjacent cache, or
       shop stock; the route generator already guarantees node variety, hook
       into it). Track "upgrades seen this region" in RunStats; the floor
       generator force-spawns one if the region is about to end at zero.
  3. TIER = PRICED ABILITY BUDGET (fixes problem 3):
       Rarity NEVER raises the power band; it buys ABILITIES — but now abilities
       are PRICED. Extend GameMath with abilityPowerPoints(ability, level)
       (reuse the PP pricing used for level-up cards: %DPT or %eHP equivalent).
       Tier ability-PP budgets in BalanceConfig SECTION 15:
         COMMON 0 PP | UNCOMMON 6 PP | RARE 12 PP | EPIC 20 PP | LEGENDARY 30 PP
         (tolerance ±20%)
       WeaponRoller rolls abilities until the tier budget is spent — never past.
       SCHEMA RULE R-ABILITY: every catalogued ability has a registered PP
       price; every rollable tier/ability combination fits its tier budget.
       This converts the whole "PLACEHOLDER — flag for playtesting" catalogue
       into priced content in one pass. Magnitudes may be tuned freely later —
       the price recomputes from the magnitude, so tuning stays free.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TECHNICAL NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - GameMath additions (pure, with Formula/Derivation/Edge-case blocks):
      gearCurveAtDepth(perRegionMultiplier, depth, regionBandSize)
      expectedPlayerDamagePerTurn(depth)   (replaces raw REFERENCE_PLAYER_DPT
                                            use in threatPoints/turnsToKill call
                                            sites that mean "player at depth")
      abilityPowerPoints(...)
  - REFERENCE_PLAYER_DPT itself stays (depth-1 anchor); audit compares depth-1
    expected == reference exactly (gearCurve(1..5) == 1).
  - WeaponRoller: budgeted rolling replaces free rolling; keep the roll
    deterministic per run seed (same discipline as SymbolAllocator).
  - The starting room offer stays COMMON..UNCOMMON (order 1) — the run BEGINS
    on the curve, at the bottom of it.
  - BalanceReport: new GEAR CURVE table (per region: expected DPT, band of
    droppable tiers, pity status) + ABILITY PRICING table.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] expectedPlayerDpt(d) monotonically steps per region; depth 1 == 25.
  [ ] R-GEARGATE and R-ABILITY pass in BalanceAuditTest.
  [ ] Every region can demonstrably supply >= 1 in-band weapon (generator test
      across 100 seeds: zero regions end with no upgrade placed).
  [ ] A legendary's total ability PP <= 30 * 1.2 for every possible roll.
  [ ] Starting-loadout TTK vs region-2 soldier is out of golden band (the gate
      exists) AND all region-1 content in band (the start is fair).

DEPENDENCIES: order 1. UNBLOCKS: order 3 (ammo priced at expected DPT),
order 5 (budget at expected DPT), order 6 (boss as gear check).
