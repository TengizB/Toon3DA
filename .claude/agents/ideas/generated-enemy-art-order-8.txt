OVERVIEW
--------
The last and most important creatures: the Void Shroud (the only regular DEMON) and the three bosses -
the Overseer (MACHINE), the Corruptor (ABERRATION) and the Hell Baron (DEMON). Two bosses have never had
their own art (the Corruptor and the Hell Baron draw a placeholder quadrant today). Bosses use the 240x360
canvas, and because a boss fight is built on telegraphed phases, bosses get PHASE clips: the body itself
shows which phase the fight is in and what is coming next.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - Kit growth: a SHADOW material (near-black charcoal with ember undertones - readable only through its
    rims and core, never violet), a smoke-taper helper (a body that dissolves into dithered wisps toward the
    floor), 240x360 canvas support everywhere (packer, sheets, GIFs), atlas budget check for bosses (split
    into a second atlas page if 2048x2048 is exceeded - the manifest supports pages).
  - Void Shroud: rig + full clip set + effects (shadow wisps, embers).
  - Each boss: rig + full clip set + PHASE clips (one idle and one telegraph pose per phase the boss has -
    read the boss's design docs and BossVisualState for the phase list) + its signature attack clips +
    effects + an ENRAGE/low-health variant + a death that lasts (1.5-2.5 s) and leaves boss remains.
  - Owner gate PER BOSS (each boss is its own approval).
  - Four registry rows (the boss rows replace today's placeholder/dedicated regions); docs.

OUT
  - Boss mechanics, phase rules, BossHudRenderer, arenas. Changing boss heightMultiplier (1.80/1.60/2.00).
  - The boss afterimage trail is a renderer feature: it keeps working by drawing the CURRENT frame.


GAMEPLAY LOOP
-------------
Rules unchanged. Visually: a boss's stance changes with its phase, and every telegraph is a held pose.


RULES
-----
  R1  Series G1-G13; runtime rules of orders 2-3.

VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
VOID SHROUD (DEMON, SOLDIER, 0.80 walls, melee flanker)
  The planet's pain given a shape: a tall hooded drift of shadow-stuff, an ember core where a chest would
  be, one long arm reaching out to the side - the flank - and a body thinning into smoke above the floor.
  The outline does the work (cyan rim + ember core). MOTION: idle = the smoke churns, the reaching arm
  drifts; move = it glides, smearing sideways; attack = the arm whips around; hurt = it tears and re-forms;
  death = it unravels into embers.
THE OVERSEER (MACHINE boss, 1.80 walls, ranged, telegraphed phases)
  The story says the Overseer is not a person - "It's a desk, not a person"; the chair is the cruelty. So
  the boss is the CHAIR made into a war machine: a monumental administrative chassis, a console-throne of
  Organization gunmetal and livery panels, a bank of cyan screens and lenses where a face would be,
  filing-arm manipulators, cables running up out of the canvas. Nobody sits in it. MOTION: screens cycle
  text-like glyph patterns per phase, arms re-file and re-aim, the throne swivels for telegraphs; death =
  screens go dark one by one, the chassis slumps, the cables fall.
THE CORRUPTOR (ABERRATION boss, 1.60 walls, ranged, telegraphed)
  Where the breach began: a towering mass of harvested tissue grown around the ruin of a containment tank,
  the tank's ribs and glass shards still embedded. Rows of eyes (order 5's eyeball helper) down its front,
  all aimed at the player - it "tells you what's coming" by looking where it will strike. MOTION: the eyes
  track and converge on the telegraph, the mass heaves, pods birth minions; death = the eyes close in a
  wave and the mass collapses into the tank's wreck.
THE HELL BARON (DEMON boss, 2.00 walls, melee, read the mark)
  The planet's immune champion - an antibody the size of a room, armoured in the planet's own crust
  (order 7's stone method, never slabs), an ember core, immense forearms. Deliberately NOT a horned devil:
  its menace is mass and patience. A broken-off Organization drill bit embedded in its shoulder - the wound
  that woke the planet. MOTION: slow, enormous anticipation on every strike (the mark is its body), the
  ground cracks under its steps, the core flares on enrage; death = it kneels and turns to stone.
