OVERVIEW
--------
The encounter planner fills most rooms with one archetype: five Vortex Eyes, four Ghouls. The cause is
in the templates. Every chaff slot is same-type, PACK (3-5 chaff of ONE type) is the heaviest-weighted
shape, the top-up step grows those same-type slots to their maximum, and the only variety rule (0.40 of
the floor's Threat Points) is per FLOOR and is priced in TP, so cheap chaff never trips it. This idea
rebuilds the group shapes so that every group mixes archetypes AND behaviours (ranged / fast melee /
slow melee). Each room then asks a tactical question: which one first, which lane to avoid, how to
move. Budget accounting (TP cap, body target, anchor, HUNTER cap, first contact) is unchanged.

What the owner said: "do not spawn same-type enemies in large numbers; make different enemies spawn
together; take balance into account."
What the owner wants: fights in which the player has to prioritise, because each enemy in the room
attacks differently. A monoculture is fine as an occasional texture ("ok if it happens by chance"),
but never the norm and never 4-5 of one type.

Measured today (planner, COMBAT, 300 seeds per depth). These are the numbers this idea must move:
  depth  1: monoType groups 36%  avgMaxSameInGroup 2.87  avgDistinct 1.74  floors with a >=4-same group 87%
  depth  5: monoType 51%  avgMaxSame 3.12  avgDistinct 1.53  floors >=4-same 95%
  depth 12: monoType 10%  avgMaxSame 2.33  avgDistinct 2.23  floors >=4-same 42%
  depth 25: monoType 41%  avgMaxSame 2.89  avgDistinct 1.72  floors >=4-same 95%


SCOPE
-----
IN

    (PAIRS / SINGLES / BROOD). Each constant carries its own data, so there is no switch.
  - A behaviour classifier, the EncounterBehaviour enum (RANGED / RUSHER / PLODDER). It is derived
    only from EnemyType data (isRanged, moveEveryNTurns).
  - Template catalog reshaped. PACK is deleted. RABBLE, SKIRMISH and BROOD are added. FIRETEAM, ESCORT,
    BATTERY and WARBAND are re-slotted. HUNTER is unchanged.
  - Planner rules V1-V7 (RULES): per-group same-type cap, one slot per archetype, behaviour mix, the
    rare brood, a top-up that adds a DIFFERENT archetype, a floor body-share cap, and placement that

  - Seven new BalanceConfig numbers, declared in BalanceSchema.CONSTANT_SUBJECTS -> RuleKind.DENSITY.

  - Docs updated (IMPLEMENTATION PLAN step 6).

GAMEPLAY LOOP
-------------
There is no new player action. The change is what the player finds behind a door:
  1. The player opens a room. Instead of five Vortex Eyes, they find e.g. two Gore Biters (fast melee)
     rushing in and two Eye Tyrants (ranged) holding the far row.
  2. The player chooses: break line of sight with the eyes (strafe out of their row/column, since ranged
     attacks need the same cardinal line) and kill the biters at the door, or push through the biters'
     charge to reach the eyes. Each action costs one turn, as today.
  3. A slow melee member (a Ghoul, a Plague Hulk) can be outpaced and left for later. A fast one cannot.
     Movement decisions now matter inside a single room.
  4. About one floor in four, a BROOD appears: three of one chaff archetype, alone. It is a deliberate,
     recognisable "nest" moment, never larger than three.


RULES
-----
  BEHAVIOURS (EncounterBehaviour, level package, per-constant matches(EnemyType)):
    RANGED   isRanged()
    RUSHER   !isRanged() && moveEveryNTurns() == 1
    PLODDER  !isRanged() && moveEveryNTurns() >= 2
  Classification for the current pool: RANGED = Eye Tyrant, Vortex Eye, Mire Wraith, Acid Drone, Auric
  Sentinel, Rimeshell Lancer; RUSHER = Gore Biter, Crawler, Void Shroud, Shell Brute, Revenant, Iron
  Stalker; PLODDER = Ghoul, Plague Hulk, Blight Corruptor, Cinderforge Colossus.

  SLOT FILL (SlotFill enum, per-constant membersPerArchetype()):
    PAIRS    every archetype in the slot appears exactly GROUP_CHAFF_SLOT_MIN (2) times; the slot's
             count range must be a multiple of 2; pairs within one slot are distinct archetypes.
    SINGLES  every member is a distinct archetype (1 each).
    BROOD    the whole slot is ONE archetype, exactly GROUP_BROOD_SIZE (3).
  Slot count ranges, random rolls, the instantiate "grow / shrink to leave a fillable remainder" step and
  the shrink-to-fit-threat step all move in membersPerArchetype() units. A pair is never split.

  TEMPLATES (EncounterGroupTemplates.registerAll, one register() row each):
    id        slots                                               kinds          anchor  lone  weight minDepth
    RABBLE    CHAFF PAIRS 4..6 (2-3 different pairs)              COMBAT/ELITE/CALM  no   no    3      1
    SKIRMISH  SOLDIER SINGLES 1..2 + CHAFF PAIRS 2                COMBAT/ELITE/CALM  no   no    2      1
    FIRETEAM  SOLDIER SINGLES 2 + CHAFF PAIRS 2                   COMBAT/ELITE/CALM  no   no    2      1
    ESCORT    BRUISER SINGLES 1 + CHAFF PAIRS 2..4                COMBAT/ELITE       YES  no    1      1
    BATTERY   RANGED_SOLDIER SINGLES 1..2 + MELEE_CHAFF PAIRS 2   COMBAT/ELITE       no   no    2      1
    WARBAND   MINI_ELITE SINGLES 1 + SOLDIER SINGLES 2            ELITE              YES  no    1      GROUP_WARBAND_MIN_DEPTH
    HUNTER    HUNTER SINGLES 1                                    COMBAT/ELITE       no   YES   1      1
    BROOD     CHAFF BROOD 3                                       COMBAT/ELITE/CALM  no   no    1      1
  (PACK is deleted. RABBLE takes its weight and its CALM eligibility. SKIRMISH is the new 3-body shape
  that keeps "a remainder of >= 3 is always fillable" true on CALM floors.)

  V1  PER-GROUP CAP. No archetype appears more than GROUP_MAX_SAME_TYPE_PER_GROUP (2) times in a group.
      The only exception is a BROOD group (exactly GROUP_BROOD_SIZE = 3). It follows that a group of size
      n holds at least ceil(n / 2) distinct archetypes.
  V2  ONE SLOT PER ARCHETYPE. When drawing a slot, archetypes already in the group are excluded. If that
      leaves the slot no admissible archetype, the slot shrinks to its minimum. If even the minimum
      cannot be filled, instantiate returns null (the shape is skipped for this pick, exactly like
      today's "no admissible archetype" path). This is deterministic, with no retries beyond
      GROUP_INSTANTIATION_ATTEMPTS.
  V3  BEHAVIOUR MIX. A group of >= GROUP_MIXED_BEHAVIOUR_MIN_SIZE (3) members, BROOD excepted, spans at
      least two EncounterBehaviours. Constructive rule: when drawing the group's LAST slot, if every
      member so far shares one behaviour, that slot's candidates are restricted to archetypes of another
      behaviour. If that restriction is empty, it is dropped (pool too thin) and the draw proceeds.
      Never a reroll loop.
  V4  THE BROOD (the owner's "by chance"). Once per floor, at plan start, the planner rolls
      random.nextFloat() < GROUP_BROOD_FLOOR_CHANCE (0.25). Only if it hits does BROOD become eligible
      for that floor, and at most GROUP_BROOD_MAX_PER_FLOOR (1) BROOD is picked. A BROOD is never
      topped up, is never the anchor and is never the first-contact group if another group of >= 2
      exists (EncounterPlacer already picks the LIGHTEST group of >= 2; if the brood is lightest, the
      placer skips it in favour of the next-lightest).
  V5  TOP-UP ADDS A DIFFERENT ARCHETYPE. Bodies short of the target after the main loop are added in
      this order, each step obeying V1, V2, V6 and the TP cap, and never touching a BROOD, HUNTER or
      WARBAND group:
        a. remaining >= 2: add one PAIR of a chaff archetype NOT in the group, to a PAIRS slot below
           its maximum (RABBLE's third pair, ESCORT's second pair);
        b. remaining >= 1: add one SINGLE of an archetype NOT in the group, to a SINGLES slot below its
           maximum (BATTERY's second gun, SKIRMISH's second soldier);
        c. otherwise stop. The floor may end one body short of the roll. This already happens today
           when no slot has headroom, and R-DENSITY's body band stays the judge.
      Groups are visited in plan order (the anchor first), as today. A chaff archetype never appears as
      a lone member of any group (no "stragglers").
  V6  FLOOR BODY SHARE. While an admissible alternative exists, no archetype may exceed
      max(GROUP_CHAFF_SLOT_MIN, ceil(GROUP_MAX_SINGLE_TYPE_BODY_FRACTION x bodyTarget)) bodies on the
      floor (0.30: 4 bodies on a 12-body floor, 9 on a 28-body floor). This is applied as a second
      preference filter in `admitted()`, beside the existing TP filter: the preferred set is the
      archetypes passing BOTH. If none pass both, those passing the body filter; then those passing the
      TP filter; then all.
  V7  ROOM CAP (EncounterPlacer). A LARGE room takes a second group only if the merged roster holds no
      archetype more than GROUP_ROOM_MAX_SAME_TYPE (3) times. Otherwise that room is not "free" for this
      group. When rooms run out (today's `roomiest(regions)` fallback), the fallback picks, among rooms
      with enough free tiles, the one whose merged max-same count is lowest; ties go to the roomiest.
  V8  UNCHANGED. TP cap, per-group TP cap (anchor exempt), fill aim, cheapest-body reserve, body target,
      HUNTER fraction, the anchor choice (heaviest eligible anchor shape), E4 shape rules, E5 first
      contact, GROUP_MAX_SINGLE_TYPE_FRACTION, threat and XP per archetype.
  V9  PACK COHERENCE HOLDS. Every chaff archetype on a floor appears >= GROUP_CHAFF_SLOT_MIN times. This
      follows from PAIRS / BROOD and from V5 never adding a lone chaff.
      BalanceAuditTest.chaffAlwaysSpawnsInPacksOverAHundredSeeds stays untouched and green.


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
-----------------
Every room is a small puzzle with a priority order. Eyes on the far row plus biters at the door means
"leave the lane first". A Ghoul pair plus a Void Shroud means "the fast one reaches you alone, kill it,
then kite the slow ones". A player who never reads learns it the way Doom RPG teaches: the first
mixed room punishes standing in the eyes' row while the biters chew on you, and the death is legible
because the mini-map shows the eyes in-lane. ORA's existing archetype lines (ENEMY_FAMILY_FIRST_SEEN,
keyed by type) and the READ_INTENT / BREAK_LANE topics already say exactly this. There are no new lines.
The occasional brood of three reads as a nest: a moment of relief, or of dread.

EDGE CASES
----------
  - Thin pool (a future depth band or region that removes archetypes): V2 shrinks or skips the shape,
    V3 drops its restriction, V6 falls back to "all". The planner never loops and never throws. A floor
    may then be less varied, which the audit's depth sweep would show.
  - CALM floors (no anchor, small body targets ~7): RABBLE 4 + SKIRMISH 3 = 7. If V4 rolls a brood on
    a CALM floor, BROOD 3 + RABBLE 4 = 7. Both fill exactly.
  - Body target 1-2 left with no headroom anywhere: V5c stops short (as today). The HUNTER lone shape
    still takes a single body in the main loop when the HUNTER fraction allows.
  - Two groups in a LARGE room that share a chaff pair: 2 + 2 = 4 > 3, so V7 refuses the pairing and the
    second group goes elsewhere.
  - All rooms full (tiny floors): the V7 fallback picks the lowest merged max-same. A 4-same room is
    then theoretically possible but must not appear in the R-DENSITY generator sweep (A6).
  - Determinism: same seed gives the same plan. The brood roll is consumed on every floor, so the
    sequence never depends on the roll's outcome.
  - Hand-made levels keep their authored spawns unchanged.

ASSUMPTIONS
-----------
  AS1  "Large numbers" means 3+ of one type in a group and 4+ in a room. A pair is fine and is the
       basic chaff unit.
  AS2  The owner's "by chance" is honoured by the BROOD: a 25% floor chance, at most one per floor,
       exactly 3, never larger.
  AS3  Behaviour (ranged / fast melee / slow melee) is the axis that creates "prioritise, avoid, think
       how you move". Family is cosmetic for this purpose and is not used.
  AS4  The synergy is not priced in TP or XP. The gates judge it, and pricing is a follow-up if needed.
  AS5  PACK is removed outright, not kept at a low weight. Its role (cheap chaff bodies) moves to RABBLE.
  AS6  RABBLE keeps PACK's weight (3) and CALM eligibility, so the body/TP profile stays close.
  AS7  SKIRMISH (1-2 soldiers + a chaff pair) is added because every CALM floor needs a 3-body mixed
       shape now that PACK's 3-body option is gone.
  AS8  ESCORT's screen becomes 1-2 pairs (3-5 bodies), up from 2-3 same-type (3-4 bodies). The anchor is
       TP-exempt, so its slightly larger size is acceptable.
  AS9  WARBAND and HUNTER are effectively unchanged. WARBAND only gains distinct soldiers.
  AS10 The room cap equals the brood size (3), so a brood is the largest same-type cluster in the game.
  AS11 The floor body-share cap is 0.30 of bodies (with a floor of 2), beside the unchanged 0.40 TP cap.
  AS12 A floor one body short of its rolled target is acceptable (today's behaviour); a lone straggler
       chaff is not.
  AS13 The audit runs on the planner (cheap) for group metrics and on the existing generator sweep only
       for the room cap.
  AS14 Hand levels, bosses, lockdown waves and twist hunters are out of scope (they do not use the
       planner).
  AS15 No ORA line is needed. The bestiary voice already covers every archetype.
  AS16 Template weights stay as inline data in the register() rows (today's idiom), not BalanceConfig.
