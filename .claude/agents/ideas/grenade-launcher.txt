TITLE: Grenade Launcher — bouncing lobbed splash weapon (GRENADES)
CREATED: 2026-06-05
---

OVERVIEW
========
The Grenade Launcher is the player's first true AREA weapon. Every existing gun
hits a single straight line of tiles; the grenade launcher instead lobs a single
grenade down the player's facing that TRAVELS, BOUNCES off the first wall it
hits (turning a corner once), and DETONATES in a plus-shaped splash that damages
every enemy in a 5-tile area (impact tile + the 4 orthogonal neighbours). It is
the answer to enemies clustered in a side room you cannot get a clean line on,
to packs around a corner, and to the player's recurring problem: "they are
bunched up but a wall is between us." It reuses the Weapon abstraction
(clipSize / reloadTime / damageDropCoefficient / range), the marchShot() tile
walk, and the procedural FrameBuffer sprite pipeline. Its identity is the
ARC-AND-BANK lob and the AoE blast — the only weapon that can hit a tile the
player has no line of sight to.

Why it belongs: the four existing weapons (shotgun cluster, double-barrel burst,
chaingun stream, plasma pierce) are ALL single-line, single-target-or-pierce
hitscan. There is no splash, no indirect fire, no "throw it around the corner."
The grenade launcher is the cleanest orthogonal addition — it makes geometry the
puzzle. Where the plasma rifle wants enemies in a straight line, the grenade
launcher wants them in a CLUMP, ideally one you cannot shoot directly.


GAMEPLAY ROLE
=============
- Niche: indirect-fire area-of-effect. The ONLY weapon that damages tiles the
  player has no straight line to (via a single wall bounce) and the ONLY weapon
  that hits a 2D cluster instead of a 1D line.
- Ideal geometry: 2-4 enemies bunched in a small room or around one corner.
- Anti-niche (deliberate weakness): single enemies in open corridors (splash is
  wasted, ammo is precious), and point-blank fights (the grenade must travel a
  minimum distance before it can detonate — see ARMING below — so you can NOT
  drop it on an adjacent enemy; firing into a wall 1 tile ahead is a dud or
  self-risk depending on tuning). This keeps it from replacing the shotgun.
- Role differentiation table:

                    Shotgun     Plasma Rifle   Grenade Launcher
  Damage pattern    1D cluster  1D pierce      2D plus-splash
  Targeting         line-of-    line-of-       can BANK around
                    sight       sight          one corner (indirect)
  Best vs           1 close     a line of      a CLUMP / packed room
                    enemy       enemies        / around-corner pack
  Range             5 tiles     8 tiles        up to 6 tiles travel
  Ammo economy      common      uncommon       RARE (3-4 per clip total)
  Risk              none        none           SELF-DAMAGE if blast tile
                                               is adjacent to the player


GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
PRE-FIRE (free aim)
- As with all weapons, rotating (A/D) to choose the lob direction is FREE and
  does not advance reload/turn. The player lines up the corridor or wall they
  want to bank off.

FIRING (one full turn)
1. Player presses KEY_ATTACK with the launcher equipped.
2. PlayerController checks state == IDLE and weapon.canFire() (grenadesInClip > 0
   and not RELOADING). If false: dry "clunk" (empty breech), HUD ammo flashes
   red, NO turn consumed.
3. If true: transition to ATTACKING. Weapon.marchShot() resolves the grenade
   path instantly in tile space:
   a. March forward from the player tile along (facingStepColumn, facingStepRow),
      one tile per step, counting traveledTiles.
   b. At each tile:
        - If the tile is a WALL or CLOSED door: the grenade BOUNCES (see BOUNCE
        below) IF it has not bounced yet AND traveledTiles >= GRENADE_ARM_TILES;
        otherwise it detonates on contact with the wall (a grenade that hits a
        wall before arming just blows up against it). Record this as the
        pre-bounce wall hit and switch to the bounced direction.
        - If the tile contains an ENEMY and traveledTiles >= GRENADE_ARM_TILES:
        the grenade detonates ON that tile (direct impact). Go to DETONATE.
        - If the tile contains an ENEMY but traveledTiles < GRENADE_ARM_TILES:
        the grenade is not yet armed — it passes THROUGH harmlessly (a thrown,
        un-armed shell). This is the minimum-range weakness.
   c. If traveledTiles reaches GRENADE_RANGE_TILES (6) with no impact: the
      grenade detonates on the final reached floor tile (air-burst at max range).
4. DETONATE (impactColumn, impactRow):
   a. Build the splash set: the impact tile + its 4 orthogonal neighbours (N/S/E/W
      one tile each) = up to 5 tiles. (Plus shape. NOT diagonals — keeps it on
      the cardinal grid and readable.)
   b. For each splash tile: if it is NOT a wall, apply GRENADE_SPLASH_DAMAGE to
      every enemy standing there. The CENTER (impact) tile gets full damage; the
      4 neighbours get GRENADE_FALLOFF_DAMAGE (reduced — splash edge).
   c. If the impact tile or any splash tile is an explosive barrel (B): detonate
      it (chain reaction — see INTERACTIONS).
   d. SELF-DAMAGE: if the player's own tile is within the splash set, the player
      takes GRENADE_SELF_DAMAGE. This is the high-skill risk: never bank a
      grenade into a wall one tile away from yourself.
   e. grenadesInClip -= 1.
5. Visual: weapon sprite plays FIRE (breech smoke puff), a small arcing grenade
   billboard travels the path (optional polish; v1 can use a center-screen
   "thunk" + a delayed blast flash), then an orange-white expanding blast ring
   at the impact location. Screen shake (heavy). A deep "THUMP ... BLAM."
6. Turn ends. Enemies act. If clip now empty, weapon -> RELOADING with
   GRENADE_RELOAD_TICKS armed.

RELOAD (tick-driven)
- Same tick model as every weapon: each COMPLETED tile step (W/S/Q/E that
  changes tiles) decrements ticksRemaining. Rotations and blocked bumps do NOT
  tick. When ticksRemaining hits 0: grenadesInClip = min(clipSize, pool); deduct
  from the GRENADES pool; state -> NORMAL. The reload is slow (4 ticks) because
  the weapon is so strong — break the breech, feed shells, you are exposed.

EMPTY / OUT-OF-GRENADES
- Fire while empty mid-reload: dry clunk, no turn.
- Reload finishes with 0 grenades in pool: clip stays 0/N, HUD red, keeps
  clunking. Swap to another weapon. Fists remain the never-softlock fallback.

BOUNCE RULE (the signature mechanic)
- A grenade bounces EXACTLY ONCE off the first wall it meets (after arming).
  The bounce reflects the travel direction by 90 degrees:
    - If the grenade was traveling along X and hits a wall, it continues along
      the player's facing-perpendicular axis. Because movement is cardinal-only,
      the deterministic rule is: on hitting a wall, the grenade turns toward
      whichever orthogonal direction has OPEN floor (prefer the side the player
      is "leaning," see OPEN QUESTIONS for tie-break). If BOTH perpendicular
      neighbours are open, default to the player's last-rotation bias; if NEITHER
      is open, the grenade detonates at the wall (dead-end bank).
    - After bouncing once, a second wall contact = detonation (no double bounce).
- This single-bounce "bank shot" is the whole reason to learn the weapon: you
  rotate to face a wall at an angle to the room you want to hit, lob, and the
  grenade rounds the corner into the pack.

SPECIAL MECHANICS
=================
1. INDIRECT FIRE / SINGLE BOUNCE — the only weapon that hits tiles outside the
   player's straight line of sight. The grenade walks, banks once around a
   corner, and detonates. Deterministic (cardinal grid), so a skilled player can
   PLAN the bank.
2. PLUS-SHAPED SPLASH — hits a 2D cluster on the cardinal grid. Center full
   damage, 4 neighbours reduced. No diagonals (grid-consistent, readable).
3. MINIMUM ARM DISTANCE — cannot detonate within GRENADE_ARM_TILES (2) of the
   muzzle. Lobbing into an adjacent enemy fails (passes through un-armed). Forces
   the player to keep the launcher for mid-range clumps, not point-blank panic.
4. SELF-DAMAGE — if the blast catches the player's tile (e.g. banked a grenade
   into a wall right next to themselves), they eat GRENADE_SELF_DAMAGE. The only
   weapon that can hurt its own user. High-risk, high-reward.
5. BARREL CHAINING — a grenade that lands on or splashes an explosive barrel (B)
   detonates it, and that explosion can chain further. The launcher is the
   premier barrel-trigger weapon.


VISUAL DESIGN
=============
WEAPON SPRITE (procedural FrameBuffer, 192x134 canvas, Quake-1 top-down angle)
  Identity silhouette: a STUBBY, FAT single-tube launcher — short and wide, the
  opposite of the rifle silhouette. A break-action grenade launcher: thick
  receiver block, a single very WIDE bore tube (the launcher mouth is the widest
  barrel of any weapon), and a hazard-striped collar near the muzzle.

  View mode: this is a FACE-ON exception candidate, but to stay consistent with
  the guide's default, render TOP-DOWN with convergence factor ~0.65 and a
  MUZZLE CAP (no bore ellipse). Rationale: a wide muzzle cap band reads as a fat
  grenade tube perfectly; a bore ellipse would imply it points at the camera.

  DRAW LAYERS (back to front, Y-up canvas, centerX = 96):
    1. RECEIVER BODY: a wide chunky trapezoid from Y=14 (lowest visible) up to
       Y=66. Dark gunmetal rgba(0.24,0.26,0.30,1). It is the WIDEST body of any
       weapon (~110px at base) to read as heavy/stubby. Add a 3px top highlight
       strip rgba(0.42,0.46,0.52,1) and a 3px bottom shadow rgba(0.12,0.13,0.17,1).
    2. HAZARD STRIPE: a diagonal yellow/black warning band across the receiver
       face (Y=30..40), alternating rgba(0.85,0.70,0.10,1) and
       rgba(0.10,0.10,0.10,1) — sells "ordnance / explosive." This is the
       launcher's signature color accent (no other weapon has yellow hazard).
    3. BREAK-ACTION HINGE: a small dark notch at the receiver/barrel join (Y=64),
       two rgba(0.16,0.18,0.22,1) rects flanking centerX — implies it breaks open
       to load. In the RELOAD frame, draw the barrel TILTED/split here.
    4. SINGLE WIDE BARREL TUBE: one fat tube from base Y=66 (half-width ~22px) to
       muzzle Y=124 (half-width ~22*0.65 = ~14px), drawn with drawSymmetricTrapezoid
       / drawGeneralTrapezoid for perspective. Crown highlight strip
       rgba(0.45,0.49,0.56,1) down the center; outer-edge shadow strips
       rgba(0.10,0.11,0.14,1) on each side. The tube is SHORTER and FATTER than a
       rifle barrel (ratio ~1:2.5, not 1:3) to read as a grenade launcher.
    5. MUZZLE COLLAR / HAZARD RING: a retaining band at ~Y=110, full muzzle width,
       3px tall, rgba(0.40,0.44,0.52,1) with a 1px yellow rgba(0.85,0.70,0.10,1)
       top edge — a second hazard accent at the mouth.
    6. MUZZLE CAP: 2px bright steel band rgba(0.40,0.44,0.52,1) at the barrel tip
       Y=124, muzzle width. NO bore ellipse (top-down rule).

  FRAMES:
    idle    — as above, breech closed, muzzle dark.
    fire    — a gray smoke puff (rgba(0.55,0.55,0.55,0.6) expanding disc) at the
              muzzle, a brief orange rgba(0.95,0.55,0.15,1) flash inside the
              collar, weapon kicks UP ~8px (heavy lob recoil).
    reload  — barrel broken open at the hinge (tube tilted up ~15px and gapped),
              a spent grenade casing ejecting up-right (a small brass
              rgba(0.70,0.55,0.20,1) cylinder), breech dark.

FIRE EFFECT (custom — distinct from the shared orange flame cone)
  - MUZZLE: a gray smoke puff + small orange core flash (NOT the rifle's bright
    cone). Use a custom renderGrenadeMuzzle(): an expanding semi-transparent gray
    disc with a brief orange center, fading over FIRE_FLASH_DURATION.
  - BLAST (at impact): an expanding orange-to-white ring drawn over the world for
    GRENADE_BLAST_DURATION. Since the impact may be off-center (banked), v1 can
    render a center-screen blast flash + heavy shake to imply it; a polish pass
    can billboard the blast at the projected impact tile. Palette: core white
    rgba(1,1,0.9,1) -> orange rgba(0.95,0.45,0.10,1) -> dark smoke
    rgba(0.3,0.25,0.2,0.5) outer.
  - SCREEN FLASH: warm orange, alpha ~0.35, with a fast fade (slower than the
    shotgun's snap — a blast lingers).
  - SHAKE: heaviest of any weapon — GRENADE_SHAKE_INTENSITY ~8px, decays over
    ~0.25s.

HUD
  - hudAmmoString(): "GRENADES <grenadesInClip>/<clipSize>", e.g. "GRENADES 2/3".
    Reuses the right-panel ammo digits + weapon name "GRENADE LAUNCHER".
  - Empty-state red pulse and change-glow logic reused unchanged.

AUDIO
  - FIRE: a hollow "THUMP / THOONK" launch.
  - DETONATE: a delayed "BLAM" with a low rumble tail (the gap between THUMP and
    BLAM sells the travel time).
  - RELOAD: "CRACK" (break open), "clink clink" (load), "CHUNK" (snap shut) over
    the 4 ticks.
  - DRY FIRE: a flat metallic "clunk" (empty breech).


TECHNICAL NOTES
===============
NEW CLASS
  entity/GrenadeLauncher.java extends Weapon
    Constructor:
      super("GRENADE LAUNCHER",
            Constants.GRENADE_SPLASH_DAMAGE,     // base damage = center damage
            Constants.GRENADE_CLIP_SIZE,
            Constants.GRENADE_RELOAD_TIME_TICKS,
            Constants.GRENADE_DAMAGE_DROP_COEFF, // 0.0 — no falloff
            Constants.GRENADE_RANGE_TILES);

  marchShot() OVERRIDE — this weapon does NOT use the standard line-pierce loop.
  It walks a path, handles ONE bounce, then applies a plus-splash. Pseudocode
  (cardinal grid, all integer tile math, allocation-free):

    int currentColumn = playerTileColumn;
    int currentRow    = playerTileRow;
    int directionColumn = facingStepColumn;
    int directionRow    = facingStepRow;
    boolean hasBounced = false;
    int impactColumn = -1, impactRow = -1;

    for (int traveledTiles = 1; traveledTiles <= range; traveledTiles++) {
        int nextColumn = currentColumn + directionColumn;
        int nextRow    = currentRow    + directionRow;
        char nextCell  = level.getCell(nextColumn, nextRow);

        if (Level.isWall(nextCell)) {
            if (!hasBounced && traveledTiles >= Constants.GRENADE_ARM_TILES) {
                // bank: turn 90 degrees toward an open perpendicular neighbour
                int[] turned = chooseBounceDirection(currentColumn, currentRow,
                                  directionColumn, directionRow, level);
                if (turned == null) { impactColumn = currentColumn;
                                      impactRow = currentRow; break; } // dead end
                directionColumn = turned[0]; directionRow = turned[1];
                hasBounced = true;
                continue; // re-evaluate from current tile in new direction
            } else {
                impactColumn = currentColumn; impactRow = currentRow; break;
            }
        }

        currentColumn = nextColumn; currentRow = nextRow;

        if (enemyHitTarget != null
            && traveledTiles >= Constants.GRENADE_ARM_TILES
            && enemyHitTarget.enemyAt(currentColumn, currentRow) != null) {
            impactColumn = currentColumn; impactRow = currentRow; break;
        }
    }
    if (impactColumn < 0) { impactColumn = currentColumn; impactRow = currentRow; }
    applyPlusSplash(impactColumn, impactRow, level, enemyHitTarget, player);
    return new FireResult(/* hit */ true, distanceTo(impactColumn, impactRow));

  - chooseBounceDirection(): returns the 90-degree-turned (column,row) step whose
    target tile is open floor. If both perpendicular neighbours are open, pick a
    deterministic tie-break (see OPEN QUESTIONS). If neither is open, return null
    (detonate at the wall). Pre-allocate the returned int[2] as a reusable field
    on the weapon to stay allocation-free; do NOT new int[] per shot.
  - applyPlusSplash(): iterate the 5 plus tiles. For each non-wall tile, for each
    enemy there, apply center vs neighbour damage. Check the player's own tile
    for self-damage. Trigger barrel chains. Use a preallocated 5-element offset
    table Constants.GRENADE_SPLASH_OFFSETS = {{0,0},{1,0},{-1,0},{0,1},{0,-1}}.

  hudAmmoString() override: "GRENADES " + grenadesInClip + "/" + clipSize, built
  into the weapon's preallocated StringBuilder (setLength(0); append...). No
  per-frame allocation.

  getNormalTexturePath()/getFireTexturePath()/getReloadTexturePath(): return the
  Constants paths (procedural sprite, so these are nominal — the FrameBuffer
  generator supplies the actual texture).

BALANCE NOTES
=============
- PER-SHOT CEILING: a perfect plus-splash hitting 5 enemies = 30 + 4*16 = 94
  distributed damage in one turn. That is the highest single-turn AoE in the
  game by design — but you only get 3 before a 4-tick reload, ammo is the rarest
  in the game, and you risk self-damage. Strong, rare, risky.
- THE RELOAD + AMMO RARITY ARE THE BALANCE. 6 grenades found per level beyond
  the pickup (suggested) means the player hoards them for packed rooms and
  barrel chains, not trash.
- SELF-DAMAGE (20) must be enough to PUNISH a careless point-blank bank but not
  instakill a healthy marine. Tune against player max HP (placeholder assumes
  ~100). If players never self-damage, the min-arm + self-damage are doing their
  job (the weakness is real and avoided by skill).
- ARM DISTANCE (2) is the anti-shotgun guard. If it feels frustrating that
  adjacent enemies are immune, lower to 1 — but do NOT go to 0 (point-blank
  self-detonation would make it a suicide button, not a skill).
- DIFFICULTY KNOBS: GRENADE_SPLASH_DAMAGE (power), GRENADE_RELOAD_TIME_TICKS
  (cadence/exposure), pickup/max ammo (availability), GRENADE_ARM_TILES (min
  range weakness), GRENADE_SELF_DAMAGE (risk).
- ALL NUMBERS ARE FIRST-PASS PLACEHOLDERS. Intended FEEL: "the heavy ordnance
  option — you save it for the right room, you respect the blast radius, and
  when it lands on a pack it is the most satisfying shot in the game."


INTERACTIONS
============
1. HAZARD CHAIN REACTIONS (hazard-chain-reactions-fire-toxic-explosive.txt):
   The grenade launcher is the PREMIER barrel trigger. A grenade landing on or
   splashing an explosive barrel (B) detonates it; that explosion can chain to
   further barrels and enemies. Banking a grenade around a corner onto a barrel
   in a packed room is the weapon's signature highlight-reel moment.
2. ENEMY SYSTEM + HIT REACTIONS (enemy_system.txt, enemy-health-bars-...):
   ONE shot can damage up to 5 enemies simultaneously (the plus splash). The
   multi-hit hit-reaction path already exists for the shotgun cone / plasma
   pierce; the launcher reuses it. Resolve splash hits center-first so death
   animations and corpse drops order sensibly.
3. DOORS (doors.txt, uac-keycard-doors-security-tiers.txt): a CLOSED door stops
   the grenade like a wall (it can bounce off a closed door, or detonate against
   it if unarmed/already bounced). The grenade does NOT open or destroy doors —
   keycard gating preserved. An OPEN door tile lets the grenade pass.
4. EMERGENCY RED ALERT PULSE (emergency-red-alert-pulse.txt): the launch THUMP
   and especially the detonation BLAM are loud noise events the alarm/hearing
   system subscribes to — the loudest in the game, raising local alarm state
   strongly. Indirect fire still announces your position.
5. ENEMY weakness multipliers: if an enemy type defines an "explosive" weakness
   (e.g. an armored type that is tough to bullets but cracks to blast), apply it
   in applyPlusSplash per target, mirroring the plasma/shotgun weakness pattern.


OPEN QUESTIONS
=============
1. BOUNCE TIE-BREAK: when both perpendicular neighbours are open floor at the
   bank wall, which way does the grenade turn? Options: (a) toward the side the
   player most recently rotated (requires PlayerController to expose last-rotation
   bias); (b) a fixed handedness (always CW, i.e. (dr,-dc)); (c) toward the
   neighbour with more open floor beyond it (a 1-tile lookahead). Recommendation:
   (b) fixed CW for v1 — deterministic and learnable; revisit if players want the
   lean-based control of (a).
2. TRAVEL VISUALIZATION: v1 stylized (center-screen thunk + delayed blast flash
   + shake) vs a true billboard grenade arcing down the projected path and a
   billboard blast at the impact tile. Recommendation: stylized for v1; upgrade
   to a projected billboard in a polish pass (reuse the prop billboard pipeline).
3. SELF-DAMAGE ON DIRECT WALL DUD: if an UNARMED grenade hits a wall < ARM_TILES
   away (point-blank), does it (a) detonate and self-damage, or (b) fizzle as a
   dud (no blast, no turn refund)? Recommendation: (b) dud — the turn is still
   consumed and the grenade is still spent (punishes the misplay) but no
   self-damage; reserve self-damage for ARMED blasts that catch the player.
4. MAX BOUNCES: confirmed 1 (single bank). A 2-bounce version is tempting but
   harder to read/plan on the grid — keep at 1 unless playtest wants more.
5. SPLASH THROUGH WALLS: the plus splash currently SKIPS wall tiles (no damage
   through walls). Confirm a blast does not hurt an enemy on the far side of a
   wall neighbour. Recommendation: YES, walls block splash tiles (a wall in the
   plus simply contributes no damage).
6. PICKUP TILE: what represents the launcher / grenade ammo on the map? Reuse a
   corpse `m` or locker `L` container, or a dedicated glyph. Defer to the
   inventory/loot system (inventory-loot-ransack-system.txt). Recommendation: a
   locker `L` drops the launcher; small ammo pickups drop grenades.
