IMPLEMENTATION NOTE (2026-10-20) — where the build diverged from the plan, and why
  1. R1 is two calls: UiKit.screenHeaderTitle (glyphs, SpriteBatch pass) + screenHeaderRule (ShapeRenderer pass),
     because a ShapeRenderer and a SpriteBatch cannot be active at once; screenHeaderRuleY gives the default rule
     position. Where a header band already has an edge, the rule IS that edge: the codex's rule runs along the
     header band's bottom (so it also sits under BACK), the ability window's on its header line, the inspect card's
     replaces the old header line (two lines 9 units apart read as clutter).
  2. Level-up and relic offer titles were centred; R1 says left-aligned, so they now start at the card row's left
     edge, at their old height, with the rule across the row (the subtitle follows, left-aligned, at its old Y).
  3. The inventory has no single outer panel: its SUIT_AMBER_DIM brackets frame the bounding rect of the header
     bar + both body panels (brackets only, no extra fill). The BUILD page replaces the two body panels while open.
     The card-choice screens (level-up, keystone, relic offer) have no outer panel either: their brackets frame
     the title + card row (+ LEAVE IT on the relic offer), UI_SCREEN_FRAME_MARGIN outside it (reviewer r1 B1).
     The level-up titles lost their asterisks ("LEVEL UP!", "KEYSTONE"): the HEAVY face turns "*" into a blob.
     The inventory's "n / 12" counter moved to the strip under the grid (inside the tab bar it crossed the
     MISC plate's new border); text anchor only.
  4. R3 "never stacked": the inventory's popup dims (INV_BASE_DIM_FACTOR / INV_ITEM_WIN_DIM_FACTOR) are removed;
     the item and ability windows are bracketed .94 panels straight over the layer below.
  5. D2 (rarity / tier colour on frames only) removed coloured TEXT and FILLS that the old screens had: the inspect
     card's tier stripe, tier-coloured title and LV/TIER tag; the level-up header strips, gradients and rank text;
     the relic header strips, gradients and rarity label colour; the BUILD page's rarity-coloured relic names (the
     rarity now frames the rarity label). Icons (tier icon, matchup glyphs, item icons, LV arrows) keep colour.
  6. Level-up: keystone cards are framed in their TAG ink (R2) - the gold double frame is gone, the gold halo
     stays; the SYNERGY corner ribbon is gone (it poked through the chamfer) and the amber word SYNERGY remains;
     the rank line is TYPE_LABEL (at the old 1.86 / TYPE_HEADING its glyphs ran into the pip row).
  7. Relic offer: the CURSED dark-red inner vignette is gone (a fill gradient); CURSED reads from its blood-red
     frame, its CURSED label and its red cost line. The quarantine title is primary like every R1 title. The
     trigger badge is an outline chip. Glow colour, LEGENDARY pulse and the 0.4 s lockout are unchanged.
  8. Codex: it has no list selection state, so R9's "selected row" is the LAST-OPENED entry (the row you return to
     from BACK); a read-only CodexSystem.getVisibleEntry(int) exposes the row's entry (pinned flag, identity) -
     no behaviour change. The close X sits on a 38-unit plate inside the panel; its touch rect is unchanged (larger).
     ORA's take keeps her speaker accent (a voice, not chrome).
  9. Text roles (R6) were applied where they fit the fixed rects; where they do not, dense data stays TYPE_CAPTION
     (weapon slot stat lines, item window stat rows and descriptions, keystone gain lines, the ability AFFECTS
     list). Red DAMAGE text is TYPE_BODY or larger (relic / keystone cost lines, the inspect card's deltas):
     DAMAGE is 4.1:1 on UI_PANEL, "large text only" in the order-4 contrast table.
 10. Semantic greens that were not health became suit amber: the grid's equipped star and "CURRENTLY EQUIPPED /
     ACTIVE" (in use) - and the PASSIVE badge is secondary / ACTIVE amber. The ability window's blue border is gone.
 11. Item category colours (grid dots, the item window's glyph-box frame) are semantic and kept, read from
     ItemConstants.INV_CAT_DOT_* through one helper (ItemGridPanel.writeCategoryColor).
 12. UiKit additions beyond R1/R2/R7: an alpha overload of plate (the codex fades in). Fix: screenHeaderTitle
     must restore the font's LOGICAL scale (GameFonts multiplies its base scale in again on setScale).
 13. Checkpoint shape: ItemWindow / AbilityWindow (CP3 scope) landed in the CP2 commit, because the coordinator
     constructs them with the new HEAVY font; CP3 is the inspect card.
 14. Capture driver (dev tooling): `additem:TYPE:QTY` and `inspectweapon` steps (SKILL.md).
 15. Not seen on screen: CURSED / RARE / LEGENDARY relic cards and the quarantine title (floor 1 only offers
     RUN_START COMMONs), pause over the offer, a completed codex tab, a long scrolled codex entry, the BUILD page
     UP/DOWN plates (needs > 11 rows), CONVERT on the inspect card (not offered in the start room), a SWAP row tap.

OVERVIEW
--------
Applies order 4's UiKit and SUIT ink to the player's own full-screen overlays: the
inventory family (grid, weapon slots, build page, item and ability windows), weapon
inspect, the level-up card choice, the relic offer and the codex (ORA's archive). After
this order, every screen the player opens from their own suit has the same frame,
header, card and plate language as the HUD underneath it.

What was SAID: "menus ... consistent art direction". What is NEEDED: each of these
screens was built by a different idea series (inventory-menu, xp-level-progression,
relics, story-ui order 6), so each has its own scrim alpha, title treatment, card
style and private colour constants. They should read as one product.

RULES
-----
  R1  SCREEN HEADER. UiKit.screenHeader(shapes, batch, font, x, y, width, title): the
      title in HEAVY at TYPE_HEADING in UI_TEXT_PRIMARY, left-aligned, then a
      UiKit.divider below it the header's width, with its tick in SUIT_AMBER. Every
      suit screen's existing title text uses it at its existing anchor.
  R2  CARD. UiKit.card(shapes, x, y, width, height, inkR/G/B, PlateState): a plate
      (order 4 R3) whose border and brackets take the given INK. For a relic card the
      ink is the rarity colour. For a perk/keystone card it is BuildTagPalette's tag
      colour (or SUIT_AMBER where no tag applies). For a weapon it is the tier colour.
      Pressed/selected = SELECTED state in that ink. This is the ONLY place a rarity or
      tier colour appears in chrome.
  R3  SCRIM. Every suit screen draws one full-screen UI_INK_BLACK scrim at
      UI_SCRIM_ALPHA 0.75 over the frozen view (relic offer 0.78 -> 0.75; the codex
      and inventory adopt the same value). One scrim per screen, never stacked.
  R4  PANELS. Every rectangular body panel becomes UiKit.panel in UI_PANEL at
      ArtDirection.UI_PANEL_ALPHA_SCREEN (0.94; the HUD's 0.82 is for in-play chrome
      only), border UI_LINE. SUIT_AMBER_DIM brackets go on the OUTERMOST panel of each
      screen only (brackets on every nested panel become noise).
  R5  PLATES. Every tappable control (tabs, close/back, LEAVE IT, CODEX, setting knobs
      on the codex footer, inventory actions, scroll arrows if any) becomes UiKit.plate
      with the four states mapped from that control's existing pressed/selected/
      disabled logic. Tabs: selected tab = SELECTED in SUIT_AMBER; others IDLE.
  R6  TEXT ROLES. Titles: R1. Section labels: TYPE_LABEL UI_TEXT_SECONDARY. Body text:
      TYPE_BODY UI_TEXT_PRIMARY. Captions, hints, footnotes: TYPE_CAPTION
      UI_TEXT_SECONDARY. Disabled: UI_TEXT_DISABLED. Numbers that are the player's
      resources (ammo counts, credits): their semantic colours (CREDITS_GOLD for
      credits). A relic's COST line keeps CombatPalette.DAMAGE red (it is a harm).
  R7  NEW DOTS / BADGES (codex NEW dot, level-up "new" markers if any): SUIT_AMBER
      filled 6-unit square with a 1-unit UI_INK_BLACK edge. Not a circle; squares fit
      the chamfer language.
  R8  RELIC OFFER specifics: the 0.4 s input lockout, glow and every timing constant are
      unchanged. The rarity glow keeps its colour. The LEAVE IT plate becomes a
      DISABLED-looking-until-pressed IDLE plate (UI_PANEL + UI_LINE), so it never
      competes with the cards.
  R9  CODEX specifics: tab column = plates (R5). Rows = flat (no plate) with a
      UiKit.divider between rows; the selected row's left edge gets a 3-unit
      SUIT_AMBER bar. The pinned WHERE AM I page keeps its pinned position with a
      SUIT_AMBER tick on its divider. The footer settings strip = plates. The body
      text keeps the story text size multiplier (accessibility) on top of TYPE_BODY.
  R10 INVENTORY specifics: item grid cells = plates (IDLE empty, filled = IDLE with
      the item icon, selected = SELECTED SUIT_AMBER). Weapon slots = cards inked by
      the tier colour (R2). Build page tag chips keep BuildTagPalette fills, framed by
      a 1-unit UI_LINE border.
  R11 Every literal colour in the touched files' chrome code is replaced by an
      ArtDirection / CombatPalette / existing semantic palette reference. Remaining
      literals are allowed only inside icon/art drawing routines (e.g. a procedural
      item icon), each marked with a short comment.

VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
Every suit screen, top to bottom: scrim -> outer panel with amber brackets -> header
(HEAVY title + amber-ticked divider) -> body panels without brackets -> plates for
anything tappable. Rarity/tier colour lives on card frames only. No gradients except
the scrim. No drop shadows on panels; text shadows only where text sits on the 3D
view (it does not, here).

ASSUMPTIONS
-----------
  AS1  The codex is a suit screen (ORA's archive, opened from the inventory), not a
       facility one.
  AS2  Rarity/tier colours are learned semantics and must not move. Framing cards with
       them strengthens the existing language without changing it.
  AS3  Brackets on the outer panel only. Nested brackets would turn the signature into
       clutter.
  AS4  Unifying scrims at 0.75 is imperceptibly different from today's 0.78 on the
       relic offer and makes every suit screen feel like the same layer.
  AS5  Screen panels use 0.94 alpha (nearly opaque) because their text is long. The HUD
       keeps 0.82 because the world should show through during play.
