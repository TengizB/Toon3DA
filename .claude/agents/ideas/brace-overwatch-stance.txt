TITLE: Brace — Overwatch Reaction-Fire Stance (turn-economy tactic)
CREATED: 2026-06-21

OVERVIEW
========
BRACE is a one-tap defensive stance that converts a "wasted" Skip Turn into a
loaded trap. When the marine braces, he plants in his current tile facing his
current cardinal direction and goes into OVERWATCH: the next enemy that steps onto
or crosses his facing line gets shot for free, before it can act. It is the
turn-based, cardinal-only answer to the classic FPS "hold this corridor" moment —
the player trades initiative for a guaranteed first strike against an approaching
threat. Brace is a pure turn-economy decision that uses systems the game already
has (facing line, weapon fire, the tick loop) with no new tile symbol.

This document OWNS: the BRACE player state, the OVERWATCH trigger resolution
within the enemy turn, the brace-break rules, the touch button, and the HUD/visual
feedback. It does NOT redefine weapon damage (weapon docs), the status-effect layer
(roguelike_order_8), or enemy AI movement (enemy_system.txt) — it hooks into them.

NO NEW TILE SYMBOL REQUIRED. Brace is a player-state layer; it reuses the existing
facing-line concept already used by ranged enemies (isSameCardinalLine) and by all
weapon fire.

GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
1. Player taps the BRACE button (replaces/augments Skip Turn — see OPEN QUESTIONS).
   -> If the equipped weapon has at least 1 round in clip and is not mid-reload,
      the marine enters BRACED state and the turn advances by exactly one (enemies
      act). Bracing IS the player's action for that turn.
2. While BRACED, the player's weapon is "primed." A primed shot is reserved: it
   will fire automatically as a REACTION during the enemy turn, not on the player's
   own turn.
3. On the following enemy turn (and every enemy turn the player stays braced):
   EnemyManager moves/acts enemies in spawn-index order. Before EACH enemy resolves
   its own action, the OVERWATCH check runs for that enemy:
     - Is the enemy now on the player's facing cardinal line (same column if facing
       N/S, same row if facing E/W) AND on the correct side (in front, not behind)?
     - Is there clear line-of-sight along that line (no wall, no closed door, no
       solid prop between the marine and the enemy)?
     - Is the enemy within OVERWATCH_RANGE_TILES?
   -> If YES: the primed weapon FIRES at that enemy immediately (one shot / one
      blast / one march, exactly as a normal player shot would resolve, including
      pellet spread, pierce, splash, status application). This happens BEFORE the
      enemy gets to move or attack this turn. The reaction is consumed.
4. After the reaction fires, BRACE ends (one reaction per brace by default — see
   BALANCE for the Marksman upgrade that grants more). The marine returns to IDLE
   and may act normally next turn.
5. If NO enemy triggers the overwatch during an enemy turn, the marine simply stays
   braced and the player chooses again next turn: stay braced (tap Brace again,
   costs another turn) or break stance by taking any other action.

BRACE-BREAK RULES (stance ends immediately if any of):
- The reaction shot is fired (default: 1 shot per brace).
- The player takes any non-brace action (move, rotate, strafe, fire manually,
  reload, heal, switch weapon, open inventory).
- The player is hit by an attack (getting shot/clawed shakes you off the sights) —
  this is a deliberate counter so Brace is not a free turtle button.
- The equipped weapon runs dry (last reaction emptied the clip).

PLAYER EXPERIENCE
=================
Brace creates the "I know something is coming" tension that turn-based combat
usually lacks. Heard a Gore Biter skittering toward the corridor mouth? Plant the
shotgun on the doorway and brace — when it rounds into your lane it eats a barrel
of buckshot before it can close. The decision is sharp and readable: bracing spends
your turn and your initiative, so if you guess the wrong lane the enemy comes from
your flank and you wasted a turn AND you're now adjacent to a melee threat. It
rewards map reading, corridor control, and weapon choice (a braced Double Barrel is
a corridor execution; a braced Chaingun overwatch is weaker per shot). It is the
tactical mirror of the Lurker's "facing is defense" — here facing is OFFENSE.

TECHNICAL NOTES
===============
Affected classes:
- input/PlayerController.java: add a BRACED flag (the controller already gates input
  in IDLE). Bracing is initiated from IDLE, advances one turn, then returns to a new
  IDLE-but-braced sub-state. Keep the state machine readable: add boolean isBraced
  plus the existing IDLE/MOVING/ROTATING. Any move/rotate/strafe/fire/reload/heal/
  switch clears isBraced before executing.
- input/touch/TouchAction.java + TouchControllerRenderer.java + TouchInputState.java:
  add a BRACE action and an on-screen button (thumb-reachable, large target).
- world/World.java: owns the overwatch resolution call inside the enemy-turn phase.
- enemy/EnemyManager.java: in takeTurn(), BEFORE each enemy resolves its Phase B
  action, call OverwatchResolver.tryReaction(enemy). EnemyManager already iterates
  enemies in spawn-index order and already owns isSameCardinalLine() — reuse it.
- entity/Weapon.java: the reaction reuses the SAME fire entry point a manual player
  shot uses (marchShot / marchBlast). No new firing code — the resolver just calls
  it pointed down the player's current facing line at the triggering enemy.

New class:
- combat/OverwatchResolver.java (new package ge.tbegvadze.toon3d.combat, or place in
  world if a new package is unwanted — document the choice). Responsibilities:
    boolean tryReaction(Enemy enemy, Player player, Weapon weapon, Level level)
  Steps: confirm player isBraced + weapon ready; compute facing axis from
  player.directionX/Y (snap-exact cardinal, already guaranteed by PlayerController);
  check enemy tile is in front on that axis within range; run a tile-walk LOS check
  along the line (stop at wall / closed door / solid prop); if clear, invoke the
  weapon's fire resolution at the enemy and return true.

Math / algorithm notes (give to math-expert for the LOS walk):
- Facing axis: if directionX != 0 -> horizontal (row-locked); else vertical
  (column-locked). PlayerController guarantees exact (±1,0)/(0,±1).
- "In front" test: for facing +X, enemyColumn > playerColumn and enemyRow ==
  playerRow; mirror for the other three directions.
- LOS walk: step one tile at a time from the tile in front of the player toward the
  enemy along the facing axis; if any stepped tile isWall / isPropSolid / closed
  door -> blocked, no reaction. This is a 1-D walk (O(range)), no DDA needed.
- Range clamp: |enemyColumn - playerColumn| or |enemyRow - playerRow| <=
  OVERWATCH_RANGE_TILES.

Data to store (per player):
- isBraced (boolean), bracedReactionsRemaining (int, default 1).
- Reset bracedReactionsRemaining when re-bracing.

Constants to add:
- WeaponConstants.java or a new GameBalance entry:
    OVERWATCH_RANGE_TILES            = 8     // placeholder; how far the lane reaches
    OVERWATCH_REACTIONS_PER_BRACE    = 1     // base; Marksman perk can raise to 2
    OVERWATCH_DAMAGE_MULTIPLIER      = 1.0f  // placeholder; see BALANCE (consider 1.25)
- TouchConstants.java: BRACE button position/size/alpha.

VISUAL DESIGN
=============
- WEAPON HUD (WeaponHudRenderer): while braced, the weapon sprite raises slightly
  and steadies (kill the idle bob), with a faint cyan "sighting" glint along the
  barrel — communicates "locked and waiting." When the reaction fires, play the
  normal muzzle flash for that weapon.
- 3D VIEW: a thin, subtle cyan scanline / aim-bracket pair at screen center while
  braced (the marine is sighting down the lane). Reuse the existing full-screen
  overlay-quad pattern from the status-tint system — no per-frame allocation.
- MINI-MAP (LevelRenderer): draw a short bright wedge/line from the player dot down
  the braced facing axis to OVERWATCH_RANGE_TILES, so the player can see exactly
  which lane is covered. This is the key readability element.
- HUD STATUS LINE: show a small "OVERWATCH" pip in the left-panel status row
  (reuse the status-icon row reserved by roguelike_order_8).
- REACTION MOMENT: on a triggered reaction, fire the existing screen-shake / muzzle
  flash and an EventText "OVERWATCH!" callout via EventTextSystem.

BALANCE NOTES (placeholders — flag for playtest)
================================================
- Brace costs a full turn and your initiative; that cost is the balance anchor. The
  payoff is a guaranteed pre-emptive hit on the FIRST enemy to enter your lane.
- OVERWATCH_DAMAGE_MULTIPLIER: start at 1.0 (a normal shot). If brace feels too
  weak versus just shooting on your own turn, bump to 1.25 (a braced shot is
  "aimed"). Do NOT exceed ~1.5 or it dominates manual fire.
- One reaction per brace by default. A Marksman/Marksmanship (MRK) attribute or a
  perk could grant OVERWATCH_REACTIONS_PER_BRACE = 2 (hold a lane against two
  rushers). Gate higher counts behind investment.
- Brace-break-on-hit is essential: without it, brace + a wall at your back becomes a
  no-risk turtle. With it, flankers and ranged enemies hard-counter a careless
  brace, preserving meaningful choice.
- Fast enemies (Gore Biter, Void Shroud) are the prime brace bait; slow tanks
  (Plague Hulk) are poor brace targets since you could just keep shooting normally.
  This naturally makes brace a SPEED counter, which is good design texture.
- Weapon interaction: charge weapons (Railgun) and lobbed weapons (Grenade Launcher)
  need a ruling — see OPEN QUESTIONS. Default v1: only hitscan/march weapons may
  overwatch; charge/lob weapons cannot brace (button disabled with a tooltip).

INTERACTIONS
============
1. ENEMY AI (enemy_system.txt / EnemyManager): the reaction resolves in the enemy
   turn BEFORE the triggering enemy acts, using the existing iteration order. A
   reaction kill removes the enemy before it can move/attack — clean and readable.
   Reuses isSameCardinalLine() and existing LOS conventions.
2. STATUS EFFECTS (roguelike_order_8): a braced shot applies the weapon's normal
   status (a braced Incinerator reaction still applies BURNING). If the player is
   STUNNED, brace cannot trigger that turn (you can't react while stunned) — the
   primed shot simply doesn't fire until the stun clears or brace breaks.
3. FACING-AS-DEFENSE / LURKER (lurker-crawler-flank-blindspot-system): brace locks
   your facing, which means a Lurker flanking around to your blind spot directly
   punishes a braced player from behind — the two systems create a deliberate
   risk/reward dance (cover the front, expose the back).
4. WEAPON SYSTEM (weapon docs): zero new firing code; brace calls the same marchShot
   /marchBlast the manual Fire button uses. Per-weapon brace eligibility is a small
   flag on Weapon.
5. DOORS (DoorManager): a closed door blocks the LOS walk, so an enemy opening a door
   into your lane only triggers overwatch once the door is actually open and the
   enemy has stepped onto the line — encourages doorway-camping tactics.
6. NOISE/STEALTH (noise-propagation): bracing is silent (you fire only on reaction),
   so a braced ambush does not pre-alert enemies the way pre-emptive shooting would.

OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
1. Does BRACE replace the Skip Turn button or sit beside it? Recommendation: keep
   Skip Turn (pure wait, no weapon needed) AND add Brace as a distinct button;
   they read differently (wait vs. armed wait). If button real estate is tight on
   small phones, make Skip Turn a long-press = Brace, short-tap = wait.
2. Charge/lob weapons (Railgun, Grenade Launcher): disabled while braced (v1), or a
   special "release on trigger" behavior? Recommend disabled in v1 for clarity.
3. Should a braced shot that MISSES (pellet spread, accuracy roll from
   weapon-system-order-3) still consume the brace? Recommend yes — you took the
   shot, the reaction is spent. Keeps it honest.
4. Multiple enemies entering the lane on the same enemy turn: only the FIRST (by
   spawn-index resolution order, i.e. effectively nearest-resolved) triggers the
   single reaction. Confirm this reads fairly, or switch to "nearest tile first."
5. Should Toughness/armor or an attribute reduce the brace-break-on-hit chance
   (steady nerves)? Possible perk hook; leave out of v1.
