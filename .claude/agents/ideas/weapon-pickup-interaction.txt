TITLE: Weapon Pickup Interaction — Inspect & Swap
CREATED: 2026-06-15
---

OVERVIEW
========
Replaces the current "step on a weapon tile and it auto-equips / auto-converts to
ammo" behaviour (the IMPLEMENTED flow in roguelike_order_5_weapon_pickup_and_
switching.txt and PlayerController.pickUpWeaponGroundItemIfPresent) with a
deliberate INSPECT -> DECIDE -> PICK-UP / SWAP loop driven entirely by on-screen
touch buttons. Standing on a weapon shows its name; an "Inspect" button opens a
procedural stats card; from the card the marine equips it (free slot), evicts a
chosen slot (slots full), or walks away leaving it on the floor. The weapon that
gets swapped out is dropped back onto the tile as a GroundItem so nothing is ever
silently destroyed.

This belongs here because the loadout is the player's identity for the run (only
WEAPON_SLOT_COUNT = 2 ranged slots today) and blind auto-grabs throw away that
decision — a DCSS/Hades player wants to READ the gun and choose, not have the
floor choose for them. It is the touch-native, mobile-only realisation of the
"full-capacity swap prompt" the order-5 doc flagged as an OPEN QUESTION (pick-
which-slot-to-evict).


GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
Precondition: a weapon GroundItem (ItemCategory.WEAPON) rests on the marine's
current tile (player settled onto it via a move turn). The OLD auto-equip path in
pickUpWeaponGroundItemIfPresent is REMOVED for the normal (non-start-room) case —
settling on a weapon no longer equips or converts anything by itself.

1. GROUND NOTIFICATION (passive, no turn). The instant the player settles on a
   tile that holds a weapon GroundItem, a small name label appears in the HUD
   centre gap (X 420..860, just above HUD_HEIGHT). Text: weapon display name, e.g.
   "COMBAT SHOTGUN" with a sub-line "[ INSPECT ]". The label is purely a readout;
   it costs nothing and clears the moment the player steps off the tile.

2. INSPECT BUTTON appears (touch). While (and only while) the player stands on a
   weapon tile AND no dialog is open, the TouchControllerRenderer shows a single
   contextual button labelled "INSPECT" in the contextual-action position (see
   VISUAL DESIGN — same slot the future LOOT/USE verb would occupy). Tapping it
   fires TouchAction.INSPECT_WEAPON (new). Inspecting opens the dialog and does
   NOT advance a turn (menu-class action, like OPEN_INVENTORY).

3. WEAPON STATS DIALOG (modal overlay). A new WeaponInspectOverlayRenderer draws a
   centred procedural card showing the ground weapon's stats (DAMAGE, FIRE/CLIP,
   RELOAD, RANGE, AMMO TYPE, current loaded ammo, a one-line type description) and
   a comparison column against the player's ACTIVE weapon (deltas coloured green/
   red). The card has two touch buttons:
     - "TAKE"  -> proceed to pick-up / swap resolution (step 4).
     - "CLOSE" -> dismiss, weapon stays on the floor, nothing changes, no turn.
   While the dialog is open, world simulation is paused (GameState set to a modal
   state, same as the inventory overlay): movement/fire/rotate input is ignored,
   enemies do not act, the tick clock does not advance.

4. PICK-UP / SWAP RESOLUTION (one turn on success).
   a. FREE SLOT (loadout.isFull() == false): the weapon is equipped into the
      lowest free slot via loadout.tryEquip(weaponInstance). The GroundItem is
      removed from the world list. Dialog closes. A pickup banner flashes
      ("<NAME> ACQUIRED"). This counts as ONE turn (the world advances once).
   b. ALL SLOTS FULL (loadout.isFull() == true): the dialog transitions to a
      SLOT-SELECTION PANEL listing each current ranged slot (slot 1..N) with that
      weapon's name and a compact stat line, plus a "CANCEL" button. The player
      taps the slot to evict. On tap:
         - the evicted weapon is removed: loadout.removeSlot(chosenIndex);
         - the evicted weapon is dropped on the current tile as a NEW GroundItem
           (step 5);
         - the new weapon is equipped: loadout.tryEquip(newWeapon) (now succeeds,
           a slot is free) and then loadout.selectSlot(chosenIndex) so the new gun
           lands in the freed slot and becomes active;
         - the ground weapon's GroundItem is removed from the world list;
         - dialog closes; banners flash ("<OLD> DROPPED", "<NEW> ACQUIRED").
      The whole swap is ONE turn.
   c. CANCEL at the slot-selection panel -> back to the stats card (no turn). CLOSE
      from the stats card -> dismiss entirely (no turn).

5. OLD WEAPON DROP. The evicted weapon must reappear on the floor. Map the evicted
   Weapon instance back to its ItemType (reuse the existing World.weaponToItemType
   mapping at World.java ~line 865), then
   world.spawnGroundItem(new GroundItem(tileColumn, tileRow, evictedType, 1)). If a
   weapon GroundItem already sits on this exact tile it is the one being taken, so
   the tile is free for the evicted gun — no stacking conflict in the normal case
   (see EDGE CASES for the rare exception). PropRenderer picks the new floating gun
   up automatically via the already-wired setGroundItems list.

6. WALK AWAY. If the player never taps INSPECT, or taps CLOSE/CANCEL, the weapon
   stays untouched on the floor. Stepping off the tile clears the name label and
   hides the INSPECT button. The weapon can be inspected again any time the player
   returns to the tile.


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
=================
The floor stops making decisions for you. Seeing "RAILGUN" under your reticle and
a glowing INSPECT prompt is an invitation, not an ambush. Opening the card and
reading "+18 DAMAGE, -2 CLIP vs your PLASMA RIFLE" turns a blind grab into a real
trade-off you understand BEFORE you commit — exactly the readable, earned decision
the design pillars ask for. With only two ranged slots, the slot-selection panel
is a sharp little gamble made explicit: which of my two guns do I trust less than
this new thing, and do I want to leave my old one right here in case I regret it
two rooms down? Because the dropped weapon stays on the tile, the choice is
reversible if you survive the trip back — that safety net is what lets the player
gamble boldly instead of hoarding. The whole flow is thumb-only, large-target, and
pausing-modal, so it never demands twitch input on a phone mid-fight.


TECHNICAL NOTES
===============
NO NEW TILE SYMBOL. Weapons are entity-side GroundItems keyed by ItemType, not
tile chars (confirmed against docs/tile-symbols.txt and the order-5 doc). Nothing
to add there.

REMOVED / CHANGED BEHAVIOUR:
- input/PlayerController.java :: pickUpWeaponGroundItemIfPresent(...) — REMOVE the
  auto-equip and auto-convert-to-ammo branches for the normal case. The settled-
  tile hook must NO LONGER mutate the loadout or inventory. Instead it records
  "the player is standing on weapon GroundItem X" into a small piece of state
  (e.g. GroundItem standingOnWeapon) and notifies the World/HUD so the name label
  and INSPECT button appear. The start-room weaponGroundItemPickedUpCallback path
  (World.handleStartRoomWeaponPickup) is a SEPARATE flow and must keep working —
  guard it: if a start-room callback is registered, keep the old immediate-pick
  behaviour (the start room is a curated "pick one of three" room, not the
  inspect flow). Recommend: the inspect flow is active only when
  weaponGroundItemPickedUpCallback == null.
- The "convert duplicate weapon to ammo" rule (weaponItemTypeToAmmoType) moves
  INTO the dialog: if the player already carries this weapon type, the stats card
  shows a "CONVERT TO AMMO (+N <AMMOTYPE>)" button instead of "TAKE" (see OPEN
  QUESTIONS — could also be left as a plain TAKE that the resolver routes to ammo).

NEW CLASSES:
- render/WeaponInspectOverlayRenderer.java (package ...toon3d.render)
    Modal overlay, modelled on InventoryOverlayRenderer. Implements Renderable +
    Disposable. Holds a ShapeRenderer for panels/bars and a BitmapFont (or the
    shared HUD font) for text. Pre-allocates everything in the constructor — NO
    allocation in render(). Public surface:
      void show(GroundItem groundWeapon, Weapon groundWeaponInstance,
                Weapon activeWeapon, Loadout loadout)
      void hide()
      boolean isVisible()
      enum Phase { STATS_CARD, SLOT_SELECT }      // internal screen of the modal
      void handleTap(float worldX, float worldY)  // hit-tests the modal buttons
      // result callbacks injected by World:
      void setOnTake(Runnable)        // free-slot equip OR opens slot-select
      void setOnConvertToAmmo(Runnable)            // duplicate case (optional)
      void setOnEvictSlot(IntConsumer)             // slot index chosen to swap out
      void setOnClose(Runnable)
    The renderer is pure UI: it never mutates the loadout. It reports the chosen
    action via the callbacks; World performs the mutation (single source of truth,
    consistent with "never mutate Player/Loadout outside the controller path").
- world/WeaponPickupController.java (OPTIONAL, package ...toon3d.world) — a small
  coordinator that owns standingOnWeapon state, opens/closes the overlay, and runs
  the resolve(equip/swap/drop) logic + turn advance. Keeps World.java from growing.
  If not split out, this logic lives in World directly. Recommend splitting it.

MODIFIED CLASSES:
- input/touch/TouchAction.java — add INSPECT_WEAPON. (One new contextual action.)
- input/touch/TouchControllerRenderer.java — render a contextual INSPECT button

- world/GameState.java (or HudState) — add a modal flag/state WEAPON_INSPECT (or

- render/HudRenderer.java — draw the ground-weapon NAME LABEL in the centre gap

DATA FLOW (per the existing wiring):
  groundItems : List<GroundItem> is created in World.buildLevelDependentResources

TURN-BASED FIT:
  - Inspecting (opening/closing the card, paging to slot-select, CANCEL) = ZERO
    turns, world paused. Same class as opening the inventory.
  - A successful TAKE / SWAP / CONVERT = exactly ONE turn: after the loadout
    mutation, World fires one tick (TickEventBus / advanceWorldOneTurn) so enemies
    get their move, matching every other world-advancing action. Picking up your
    gun in a hot room is a real cost — the enemy gets a step while you holster.
  All resolution is integer tile lookups + reference swaps; no allocation in the
  hot path beyond the single new GroundItem on a swap.


VISUAL DESIGN
=============
GROUND NAME LABEL (HudRenderer): centred in the HUD gap (X ~640), at
WEAPON_NAME_LABEL_Y. Amber text (reuse HUD amber), e.g. "COMBAT SHOTGUN", with a
dim sub-line "[ INSPECT ]" pulsing gently (reuse a sin clock already in HudRenderer
for the red-alert pulse). Never overlaps the weapon sprite (sits above the chrome).

INSPECT TOUCH BUTTON (TouchControllerRenderer): a chunky thumb-sized square in the
contextual-action position (recommend bottom-centre-right, clear of the movement
d-pad and the FIRE button). Amber outline, "INSPECT" label, faint pulse so it reads
as "new thing available". Appears only on a weapon tile with no modal open; hidden
otherwise. Large tap target (>= 110 world units) per the mobile button rule.

STATS CARD (WeaponInspectOverlayRenderer, Phase.STATS_CARD): centred dark panel
(alpha 0.92) with a thin amber border, drawn with ShapeRenderer + font, all
procedural (NO new texture asset).
  - Header row: weapon display name (big), and its ItemType glyph colour swatch.
  - Stat rows, each a label + a horizontal bar (filled fraction = stat / max) +
    numeric value:
      DAMAGE   bar = damage / WEAPON_STAT_BAR_MAX_DAMAGE
      CLIP     "N shots"        (clipSize)
      RELOAD   "N ticks"        (reloadTime)
      RANGE    bar = range / WEAPON_STAT_BAR_MAX_RANGE   "N tiles"
      DROPOFF  damageDropCoefficient as "x.xx /tile"
      AMMO     ammoType display name + current loaded count
  - COMPARISON: a right-hand column showing the same stats for the player's ACTIVE
    weapon, with the delta coloured (green = ground weapon better, red = worse,
    grey = equal). This is the heart of the "informed trade" experience.
  - One-line type description (from a new ItemType.getDescription() or a switch in
    the renderer): e.g. "RAILGUN — single-target slug, pierces, slow reload."
  - Footer buttons: "TAKE" (green) and "CLOSE" (grey). If the player already owns
    this weapon type, "TAKE" is replaced by "CONVERT (+N <AMMO>)".

SLOT-SELECTION PANEL (Phase.SLOT_SELECT): same card frame, title "SWAP OUT WHICH?".
A vertical list of WEAPON_SLOT_COUNT rows, one per filled slot:
  [ SLOT 1 ]  PLASMA RIFLE   DMG 22  RNG 12   (active marker if active)
  [ SLOT 2 ]  CHAINGUN       DMG 8   RNG 16
Each row is a full-width touch button. A "CANCEL" button returns to STATS_CARD.
Tapping a row evicts that slot. The active slot gets a bright amber left-edge so
the player sees which gun is currently in hand before deciding.

BANNERS: reuse EventTextSystem.spawnWithColor for "<NAME> ACQUIRED" (green),
"<OLD> DROPPED" (amber/red), "+N <AMMO>" (green) — already the convention in
pickUpWeaponGroundItemIfPresent.


BALANCE NOTES
=============
All PLACEHOLDER — tune in playtest:
- Turn cost of TAKE/SWAP = 1 turn is deliberate friction in combat; inspecting is
  free so the player is never punished for merely LOOKING. If 1-turn swaps feel too
  punishing in firefights, consider letting a swap done while no enemy is in LOS be
  free (flag — adds a LOS check).
- Stat-bar normalisers (MAX_DAMAGE 60, MAX_RANGE 20) only affect bar FILL, not
  logic; pick values that keep the strongest real weapon near (not at) full bar so
  upgrades still read as "more".
- The dropped-weapon safety net (evicted gun stays on the tile) is the key balance
  lever: it makes bold swaps low-regret, which is intended. If hoarding/return-
  trips become tedious, a future "weapons left behind despawn on floor descent"
  rule (they do not carry down) is the natural pressure valve — already implied by
  the order-9 multi-floor rule that only CARRIED weapons descend.
- With WEAPON_SLOT_COUNT = 2 the slot-select panel is tiny (two rows). If slot
  count grows later the panel scales vertically; cap the card height and scroll if
  it ever exceeds the screen (not needed at 2).

   resolution with the inspect flow. The Loadout API (tryEquip, removeSlot,

   makes standing on it ACTIONABLE. The bobbing sprite vanishes the frame its

   World.resolveEvict/dropEvictedWeapon helpers for consistency.

   Inspecting/cancelling fires no tick.

OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
1. Duplicate-weapon handling: show an explicit "CONVERT TO AMMO (+N)" button on the
   card, or keep a single "TAKE" that the resolver silently routes to ammo when the
   weapon is already owned? Recommend the explicit CONVERT button — it keeps the
   card honest about what the marine actually gets.
2. Does INSPECT auto-open the moment the player settles on a weapon tile, or only
   on the explicit button tap? Recommend explicit tap (an auto-modal that pauses
   the world every time you walk over a gun would be intrusive mid-fight). The name
   label is the always-on hint; the tap is the opt-in.
3. Should a TAKE/SWAP cost a turn at all? Recommend YES (consistency with every
   other world-advancing action) but flag a "free if no enemy in LOS" softening
   if playtest finds combat swaps too punishing.
4. Where exactly does the contextual INSPECT button sit so it never overlaps FIRE
   or the d-pad on small phones? Needs a real device layout pass; TouchConstants
   placeholders above are a starting guess.
5. Comparison column: compare against the ACTIVE weapon only, or against the
   specific slot the player is hovering in the slot-select panel? Recommend active-
   weapon on the stats card, and per-slot stat lines on the slot-select panel
   (covered above) so both questions are answered without a third screen.
6. Player dies while the card is open (covered in EDGE CASES) — confirm the death
   overlay takes precedence and force-hides the inspect modal; no half-resolved
   swap may persist.
7. Should weapons left on the floor persist across floor descent or despawn? Out of
   scope here; defer to the multi-floor doc, but the safety-net balance note
   assumes same-floor persistence at minimum.


EDGE CASES
==========
- WEAPON ON THE EXIT TILE ('>'/stairs): the stairs-descent check
  (checkStairsDescentIfPresent) currently runs in the same settled-tile block.
  ORDER MATTERS: if a weapon GroundItem and the exit share a tile, descending
  would skip the inspect entirely. Recommend the exit prompt and the inspect prompt
  are mutually exclusive on a shared tile, with EXIT taking priority OR (cleaner)
  the generator must never place a weapon GroundItem on the exit tile. Add that
  exclusion to the generator/seeder; flag for the level-gen owner.
- EVICTED WEAPON DROPPED ON AN ALREADY-OCCUPIED TILE: in the normal swap the tile's
  only weapon GroundItem is the one being TAKEN (removed first), so the tile is
  free for the evicted gun. But the tile could also hold a NON-weapon GroundItem
  (ammo/armour the player hasn't auto-collected, or that auto-collect was full).
  GroundItem has no stacking, and the renderers iterate the flat list, so TWO
  GroundItems on one tile both render (overlapping bob) and the next settle would
  try to collect/inspect them in list order. Recommend: (a) drop the evicted weapon
  on the player's CURRENT tile as specced (simplest, two sprites briefly overlap —
  acceptable), and define a deterministic "first weapon GroundItem on the tile" rule
  for the inspect lookup so re-inspecting picks the nearer one; OR (b) if a weapon
  GroundItem already occupies the tile after the take, search the 4 cardinal
  neighbours for the first walkable, non-wall, weapon-free floor tile and drop there
  (never into a wall, never off-grid). Recommend (a) for v1, (b) as polish.
- PLAYER DIES WHILE THE DIALOG IS OPEN: a successful TAKE advances a turn and the
  retaliating enemy strike could be lethal. Resolve mutation-then-tick atomically:
  apply the loadout swap, fire the tick, then if the player is now dead, force-hide
  the inspect overlay and let the death overlay take over (death state outranks all
  modals). If the player dies from a hazard/DoT WHILE the card is merely open (no
  TAKE), force-hide the card on death; no swap was pending so nothing to roll back.
  Never leave a half-applied swap (e.g. evicted removed but new not equipped) — do
  removeSlot + tryEquip + drop in one synchronous block before firing the tick.
- LOADOUT EMPTY (no ranged weapon, e.g. just after start room with only fists):
  isFull() is false, so TAKE always hits the free-slot path and equips — never the
  slot-select panel. Good; no special case.
- WEAPON TYPE WITH NO BACKING Weapon CLASS (e.g. WEAPON_PISTOL has no Pistol class):
  findWeaponInArsenalForType returns null. The card should still SHOW (read the
  stats from the ItemType / a stub), but TAKE must fall back to CONVERT-TO-AMMO
  since there is no instance to equip — exactly the old hook's pistol fallthrough.
- MODAL OPEN + LEVEL TRANSITION / WORLD REBUILD: hide and dispose-safe the overlay
  on world teardown; the groundItems list is rebuilt per floor, so a dangling
  GroundItem reference in a still-open card must be cleared on hide().
