TITLE: Railgun — charge-up infinite-pierce hitscan sniper (SLUGS)

OVERVIEW
========
The Railgun is the player's long-range precision power weapon and the only gun
in the game with a CHARGE-UP fire mode. Pressing attack does not fire instantly:
it begins charging the capacitor, and the shot must be RELEASED (or auto-fires at
full charge). When it fires, it sends a single hyper-velocity slug in a perfectly
straight line that PIERCES EVERYTHING — every enemy AND keeps going — for the
full length of the corridor (up to 16 tiles, double the plasma rifle's reach)
with essentially NO damage falloff, dealing massive single-hit damage scaled by
how long it charged. It is the answer to a heavily-armored enemy at the end of a
hall, to a perfectly-aligned column of foes you want to delete in one shot, and
to the player who wants to play patient and positional. It reuses the Weapon
abstraction and the marchShot() full-pierce loop (same line walk as the plasma
rifle) but adds a charge state to the turn machine.

Why it belongs: the plasma rifle pierces but is a sustained-fire mid-range gun
(4-shot clip, moderate damage). Nothing in the arsenal rewards PATIENCE, nothing
reaches across a long hall, and nothing delivers a single devastating burst at
the cost of a turn spent charging. The railgun is the "snipe the whole corridor"
fantasy: low ammo, slow cadence, enormous payoff, and a built-in risk/reward in
the charge gamble (do you take the half-charge shot now, or hold for full and let
the enemy close a tile?).


GAMEPLAY ROLE
=============
- Niche: long-range, high-single-hit, charge-gamble precision. Longest range in
  the game (16 tiles). Highest single-target damage in the game (at full charge).
  Infinite pierce like the plasma, but one big slug, not a stream.
- Ideal geometry: a long straight corridor with an armored or high-value target
  (or a column of enemies) at the far end. Reward for lining up and holding.
- Anti-niche (deliberate weakness): close-quarters swarms (the charge turn gets
  you mauled; you cannot react fast), and ammo-hungry trash clearing (slugs are
  the rarest ammo — one slug to kill one zombie is a waste). It is a SCALPEL, not
  a broom. This keeps it from overlapping the chaingun/shotgun roles.
- Role differentiation table:

                    Plasma Rifle    Chaingun         Railgun
  Range             8 tiles         8 tiles          16 tiles (longest)
  Pierce            unlimited       none             unlimited
  Damage model      18/bolt, x4     10x3 burst       40-90 single slug
                                                     (charge-scaled)
  Cadence           fast (4-clip)   fast stream      SLOW (charge then 1)
  Fire trigger      tap             tap (stream)     HOLD to charge, RELEASE
  Falloff           gentle 0.10     present          ~none (coherent slug)
  Best vs           a line of       sustained DPS    one armored target /
                    enemies         on one target    a perfectly-aligned column
  Player tempo      aggressive      aggressive       PATIENT / positional


GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
THE CHARGE STATE (the signature mechanic)
The railgun adds one concept to the turn machine: a CHARGING phase that lives
ENTIRELY on the player's own turns. Charging does NOT advance the world — it is a
"held action." Each press/hold of the attack key while equipped advances a charge
level. The shot fires on RELEASE or at max charge.

  CHARGE MODEL (turn-true, no real-time timers):
  - The railgun has chargeLevel in {0, 1, 2} (RAILGUN_MAX_CHARGE = 2).
  - Pressing KEY_ATTACK with chargeLevel == 0 and a loaded slug: begin charging,
    chargeLevel -> 1. THIS CONSUMES THE PLAYER'S TURN (the world advances; enemies
    act). You are exposed while spinning up the capacitor. This is the risk.
  - On the NEXT turn, the player chooses:
      (a) Press KEY_ATTACK again -> RELEASE the shot at the current chargeLevel
          (fires now). OR continue holding to raise charge (see auto rule).
      (b) Press KEY_RAIL_RELEASE (or simply KEY_ATTACK as "fire now") -> fire at
          current charge.
      (c) MOVE / ROTATE -> this RELEASES the charge as a shot at the current level
          ... OR cancels it (see OPEN QUESTIONS). Recommended: moving FIRES at
          current charge in the facing direction BEFORE the move resolves
          (snap-shot), so the capacitor is never wasted silently.
  - AUTO-FIRE AT MAX: holding/pressing attack again while chargeLevel == 1 raises
    it to 2 (max) and FIRES THAT SAME TURN at full power. So:
      Turn 1: press attack -> chargeLevel 1 (spin-up, world advances).
      Turn 2: press attack -> chargeLevel 2 -> AUTO-FIRE full slug this turn.
    A half-charge shot is available on Turn 2 by choosing "fire now" instead of
    charging further (release at level 1). This is the charge GAMBLE: full power
    costs two exposed turns; a half-power shot costs one.

FIRING (resolves the slug)
1. canFire() requires a slug in the clip (slugsInClip > 0) and chargeLevel >= 1.
2. On fire, marchShot() resolves instantly in tile space:
   a. March forward from the player tile along facing, one tile per step, up to
      RAILGUN_RANGE_TILES (16).
   b. At each tile:
        - WALL or CLOSED door: the slug STOPS at that tile (a railgun slug does
          not pierce walls — it pierces flesh). End.
        - ENEMY/ENEMIES: apply damage to every enemy on the tile and CONTINUE
          (infinite pierce — the slug keeps going through bodies).
   c. Damage = RAILGUN_DAMAGE_BY_CHARGE[chargeLevel] with falloff
      multiplier dropMultiplier(D) = max(MIN, 1 - RAILGUN_DROP_COEFF*(D-1)).
      RAILGUN_DROP_COEFF is tiny (0.02) so a 16-tile shot still hits hard.
   d. slugsInClip -= 1. chargeLevel -> 0.
3. Visual: a BLINDING thin white-blue beam snaps down the full corridor for
   RAILGUN_BEAM_DURATION (a true line, longest tracer in the game), a sharp crack
   of light, brief screen whiteout, a hard recoil kick. Every pierced enemy
   reacts simultaneously.
4. Turn ends (the fire-release turn). Enemies act. If the clip is now empty,
   weapon -> RELOADING with RAILGUN_RELOAD_TICKS armed.

CHARGE FEEDBACK (so the player always reads the gamble)
- While chargeLevel >= 1, the weapon sprite shows a rising glow in the rail
  capacitor (level 1 = dim blue hum, level 2 = blinding white-hot, ready). A
  rising electric whine plays one step up per charge level. The HUD ammo readout
  shows a CHARGE indicator (e.g. "RAIL >  " at level 1, "RAIL >> " at level 2).
- Because charging spends turns, the player can SEE enemies advancing while they
  hold — the tension of "fire the half-charge now or risk one more turn for full."

RELOAD (tick-driven)
- Standard tick model: each completed tile step decrements ticksRemaining;
  rotations and blocked bumps do not. When it hits 0: slugsInClip = min(clipSize,
  pool); deduct from SLUGS pool; state -> NORMAL. Long reload (4 ticks) — racking
  a fresh slug and recharging the rail takes time.

EMPTY / OUT-OF-SLUGS
- Press attack with no slug (mid-reload or empty pool): dry "no-charge" click,
  HUD red, no turn, no charge begins.
- Reload finishes with 0 slugs: clip 0/N, HUD red, keeps clicking. Swap weapon.
  Fists remain the never-softlock fallback.

SPECIAL MECHANICS
=================
1. CHARGE-UP / CHARGE GAMBLE — the only weapon that does not fire on the turn you
   press attack. Charging costs an exposed turn; full power costs two. The player
   constantly weighs "half-charge shot now vs full-charge shot next turn while the
   enemy closes a tile." A genuine risk/reward decision baked into the trigger.
2. INFINITE PIERCE + LONGEST RANGE — deletes an entire aligned column of enemies
   across a 16-tile hall in one slug. The plasma rifle pierces too, but at 8
   tiles with per-bolt damage; the railgun is one enormous slug that does not run
   out of clip mid-corridor.
3. NEAR-ZERO FALLOFF — damage barely drops over 16 tiles. The far end of the hall
   is as dangerous as point-blank. Rewards holding the line at maximum range.
4. SNAP-SHOT ON MOVE — moving while charged fires the slug at current charge
   before the step resolves, so a charge is never silently wasted (and gives a
   "fire and reposition" option). See OPEN QUESTIONS for cancel-vs-fire choice.
5. SINGLE-SHOT CLIP — clip size 1 means every shot is followed by a reload. The
   railgun's rhythm is charge -> fire -> reposition-while-reloading -> charge.
   Deliberately the slowest, most deliberate cadence in the arsenal.


VISUAL DESIGN
=============
WEAPON SPRITE (procedural FrameBuffer, 192x134 canvas, Quake-1 top-down angle)
  Identity silhouette: a LONG, SLEEK rifle — the opposite of the grenade
  launcher's stubby fatness. The signature feature is a pair of parallel
  CONDUCTOR RAILS running the length of the barrel with a glowing capacitor
  block at the receiver. Energy-weapon palette (steel blue body, electric cyan
  energy) but COLDER and whiter than the plasma rifle (plasma = cyan; rail =
  white-blue, almost ultraviolet).

  View mode: TOP-DOWN, convergence ~0.65, muzzle CAP (no bore ellipse).

  DRAW LAYERS (back to front, Y-up, centerX = 96):
    1. RECEIVER BODY: a narrow, long steel-blue block rgba(0.26,0.30,0.40,1) from
       Y=14 to Y=58. Narrower than other weapons (~70px) to read as sleek/precise.
       3px top highlight rgba(0.44,0.50,0.62,1), 3px bottom shadow
       rgba(0.14,0.16,0.22,1).
    2. CAPACITOR BLOCK: a rectangular energy cell set into the top of the receiver
       (Y=30..52, ~36px wide, centered). At idle it holds a DIM blue glow
       rgba(0.10,0.35,0.85,1). At charge level 1 it brightens to cyan
       rgba(0.20,0.65,1.0,1); at level 2 it is blinding white-blue
       rgba(0.80,0.92,1.0,1). This is the charge-state read.
    3. TWIN CONDUCTOR RAILS: two thin parallel bars running from the receiver
       (Y=58) to the muzzle (Y=124), one on each side of centerX (e.g. at
       centerX-8 and centerX+8 at base, converging to centerX-5/+5 at muzzle via
       the 0.65 factor). Drawn as two narrow tapered trapezoids (drawGeneralTrapezoid)
       in steel rgba(0.40,0.44,0.52,1) with a 1px electric-cyan top edge
       rgba(0.20,0.75,1.0,1). When charged, the rails GLOW (brighten the cyan edge
       toward white). The rails ARE the barrel — there is no enclosing tube, which
       is the railgun's unmistakable silhouette.
    4. RAIL CROSS-BRACES: 2-3 thin steel rungs connecting the rails at intervals
       (small rects spanning rail-to-rail at Y=78, Y=98, Y=116), rgba(0.36,0.40,0.48,1).
    5. EMITTER / MUZZLE GAP: between the rail tips at Y=122, a small bright energy
       point rgba(0.80,0.92,1.0,1) (where the slug accelerates out) — a tiny disc,
       brighter with charge level.
    6. MUZZLE CAP: 2px bright steel band at Y=124 spanning the rail tips. NO bore
       ellipse (top-down, and there is no tube anyway).

  FRAMES:
    idle    — capacitor dim, rails dark steel, emitter dark.
    charge1 — capacitor cyan, rail edges glow cyan, emitter point lit.
    charge2 — capacitor white-blue blinding, rails glow white, emitter bright,
              faint arcing spark pixels between the rails.
    fire    — the whole rail flashes white, emitter erupts; weapon kicks UP ~10px
              (hardest recoil with the grenade launcher); capacitor instantly
              dark (discharged).
    reload  — capacitor dark/refilling (a sweep of blue fills it bottom-to-top
              over the tick count), a spent slug casing ejecting up-right.

FIRE EFFECT (custom — distinct white-blue beam)
  - BEAM: a thin, BLINDING white-blue vertical line up the center of the screen
    (the slug travels dead-ahead = screen center) for RAILGUN_BEAM_DURATION,
    additively blended, with a white core rgba(0.95,0.98,1.0,1) and a thin cyan
    glow falloff rgba(0.20,0.70,1.0,0.6) on each side. The longest, thinnest,
    brightest tracer in the game — it should read as a hyper-velocity line, not
    a fat plasma streak.
  - SCREEN FLASH: a hard near-white whiteout, alpha ~0.45, instant on, fast fade
    (the crack of a railgun discharge).
  - SHAKE: a single sharp jolt RAILGUN_SHAKE_INTENSITY ~10px, decays fast (~0.15s)
    — a crack, not a rumble.
  - CHARGE GLOW (per turn while charging): a soft cool-blue vignette pulse at the
    screen edges that intensifies from charge 1 to charge 2, telegraphing the
    held shot. Implemented as a low-alpha blue edge gradient over the world.

HUD
  - hudAmmoString(): when NORMAL/loaded and not charging: "SLUGS <slugsInClip>/1"
    (effectively "SLUGS 1/1" or "SLUGS 0/1"). When charging, show a charge
    indicator instead: "RAIL >" (level 1) / "RAIL >>" (level 2, READY). Reuses
    the right-panel digits + weapon name "RAILGUN".
  - Charge level can also drive a small bar or arrow glyphs in the right panel.

AUDIO
  - CHARGE: a rising electric whine, one pitch step per charge level (charge 1 =
    low hum, charge 2 = high ready-tone). The ready-tone at level 2 is a clear
    "it will fire next press" cue.
  - FIRE: a sharp "KRA-KOW" crack with a brief ringing tail (the longest, most
    dramatic fire sound).
  - RELOAD: a heavy slug rack "CHA-CHUNK" + a capacitor recharge hum over the
    ticks.
  - DRY FIRE: a flat dead "click" (no charge available).

  marchShot() OVERRIDE — identical structure to the plasma rifle's full-pierce
  loop, with charge-scaled damage:
    for (int distanceTiles = 1; distanceTiles <= range; distanceTiles++) {
        int targetColumn = playerTileColumn + facingStepColumn * distanceTiles;
        int targetRow    = playerTileRow    + facingStepRow    * distanceTiles;
        char targetCell  = level.getCell(targetColumn, targetRow);
        if (Level.isWall(targetCell)) return FireResult.HIT_WALL; // slug stops at wall
        if (enemyHitTarget != null) {
            Object hitEnemy = enemyHitTarget.enemyAt(targetColumn, targetRow);
            if (hitEnemy != null) {
                float baseForCharge = Constants.RAILGUN_DAMAGE_BY_CHARGE[chargeLevel];
                int damage = Math.round(baseForCharge
                              * GameMath.railgunFalloff(distanceTiles,
                                   Constants.RAILGUN_DROP_COEFF,
                                   Constants.RAILGUN_DAMAGE_MIN_MULTIPLIER));
                enemyHitTarget.applyDamageTo(hitEnemy, damage);
                // CONTINUE — infinite pierce
            }
        }
    }
    return FireResult.MISSED;
    // After the loop, caller resets chargeLevel and decrements the clip.

  hudAmmoString() override: if RELOADING -> "RELOAD"; else if chargeLevel == 2 ->
  "RAIL >>"; else if chargeLevel == 1 -> "RAIL >"; else -> "SLUGS " + slugsInClip
  + "/" + clipSize. Built into the preallocated StringBuilder.

GAMEMATH ADDITIONS (util/GameMath.java)
  railgunFalloff(int distanceTiles, float coefficient, float minMultiplier) -> float
    /*
     * Formula: near-flat linear damage falloff with a high floor.
     * Derivation: multiplier = 1 - coefficient * (distanceTiles - 1); at D=1 ->
     *   1.0; with coefficient 0.02 a 16-tile shot = 1 - 0.02*15 = 0.70 (the
     *   floor), so the slug stays lethal at maximum range. Clamp to
     *   [minMultiplier, 1.0].
     * Edge cases: distanceTiles < 1 -> clamp to 1; coefficient 0 -> flat 1.0;
     *   large coefficient -> floor dominates (but railgun uses a tiny coeff).
     */
  (Mirrors plasmaDamageDropMultiplier; can share one generic linearFalloff helper
   if java-architect prefers — keep distinct constants either way.)

BALANCE NOTES
=============
- THE CHARGE IS THE BALANCE. A 90-damage full-charge slug that pierces a whole
  16-tile corridor is the strongest shot in the game — but it costs TWO exposed
  turns to set up (spin to level 1, then to level 2), then a 4-tick reload, and
  slugs are the rarest ammo. The half-charge 40-damage shot is available in one
  turn for players who cannot afford two.
- CHARGE GAMBLE TENSION: because charging spends turns and enemies advance, the
  player visibly trades safety for power. If full charge feels never-worth-it
  (enemies always close before turn 2), shorten the charge to 1 extra turn at
  higher damage, or let charge 2 be reachable same-turn-as-charge-1 by a
  hold-press (already supported by the auto-fire-at-max rule). Tune
  RAILGUN_DAMAGE_BY_CHARGE so full charge is clearly worth the extra turn against
  beefy targets but overkill on trash.
- AMMO RARITY: 4 slugs per pickup, max 12. Suggested ~3-5 slugs found per level.
  The railgun is a "boss / armored elite / perfect-corridor" tool, never a trash
  broom. One slug per zombie is a deliberate waste the player learns to avoid.
- RANGE 16 is intentionally double the plasma's 8 so the railgun OWNS long halls.
  If levels rarely have 16-tile sightlines, the effective advantage self-limits
  (good — it shines exactly where long sightlines exist).
- DIFFICULTY KNOBS: RAILGUN_DAMAGE_BY_CHARGE (power curve), charge turn cost
  (exposure), RAILGUN_RELOAD_TIME_TICKS (cadence), slug drop rate (availability),
  RAILGUN_DROP_COEFF (long-range potency).
- ALL NUMBERS ARE FIRST-PASS PLACEHOLDERS. Intended FEEL: "the patient sniper —
  you pick your corridor, you hold your breath through the charge while they
  close, and you delete the whole line in one crack of light."


INTERACTIONS
============
1. PLASMA RIFLE / WEAPON SYSTEM (plasma-rifle.txt): both infinite-pierce, but
   deliberately non-redundant. Plasma = fast, 8-tile, per-bolt, sustained.
   Railgun = slow charge, 16-tile, one huge slug, single-shot clip. The railgun
   reuses the plasma's full-pierce marchShot loop nearly verbatim (proving the
   pierce path generalizes) and adds only the charge state and damage scaling.
2. ENEMY SYSTEM + HIT REACTIONS (enemy_system.txt, enemy-health-bars-...): one
   slug triggers simultaneous hurt/death on every pierced enemy in the line.
   Reuses the existing multi-hit reaction path. Order hits front-to-back so death
   animations / corpse drops resolve along the beam sensibly.
3. ARMOR / ENEMY WEAKNESS: if an armored enemy type defines high bullet
   resistance, the railgun's huge single hit is the intended counter — apply any
   "slug / armor-piercing" weakness multiplier in marchShot per target, mirroring
   the shotgun/plasma weakness pattern. The railgun should feel like THE answer
   to heavy armor.
4. HAZARD CHAIN REACTIONS (hazard-chain-reactions-...): a slug crossing an
   explosive barrel (B) detonates it and CONTINUES (coherent slug pierces past
   the barrel, like the plasma bolt). Lining a barrel up mid-corridor lets one
   full-charge slug delete a column AND pop the barrel.
5. DOORS (doors.txt): a CLOSED door stops the slug like a wall (no opening/
   destroying — keycard gating preserved). An OPEN door tile lets the slug pass.
6. EMERGENCY RED ALERT PULSE (emergency-red-alert-pulse.txt): the discharge crack
   is a loud noise event the alarm system subscribes to. The rising charge whine
   is NOT a combat noise event (charging is silent to enemies — only the shot
   announces you), which rewards the patient sniper who fires from concealment.


OPEN QUESTIONS
=============
1. MOVE-WHILE-CHARGED: does moving FIRE the slug at current charge (snap-shot) or
   CANCEL the charge (capacitor bleeds off, no shot, no slug spent)? Recommendation:
   FIRE at current charge in the facing direction (never silently waste a charge;
   gives a "fire and reposition" option). If that feels punishing for accidental
   moves, switch to CANCEL with a brief warning cue.
2. CHARGE PERSISTENCE ACROSS WEAPON SWAP: if the player swaps away while charged,
   does the charge hold or bleed off? Recommendation: bleed to 0 on swap (you let
   go of the trigger). Confirm with the inventory/weapon-swap system.
3. SAME-TURN FULL CHARGE: should reaching full charge be possible in ONE turn
   (press = instant full) on lower difficulties, or always require the two-turn
   commit? Recommendation: always two-turn commit for identity; expose the auto-
   fire-at-max so a player who presses twice across two turns gets full power
   without a separate release input.
4. CHARGE DECAY OVER IDLE TURNS: if the player charges to level 1 then does
   nothing further (no input), does the charge decay? Since charging consumes the
   turn, "doing nothing" is not really possible — every action either advances or
   fires. Recommendation: no passive decay; charge only changes on explicit
   actions. Flag if a "wait" action is ever added.
5. KEY BINDING FOR RELEASE: reuse KEY_ATTACK for both charge-up and fire-now
   (press while charged = fire), or add a dedicated KEY_RAIL_RELEASE? Recommendation:
   reuse KEY_ATTACK (press while charged-but-not-max = fire now; press while at
   level 1 with intent to hold... ambiguous). To resolve the ambiguity cleanly:
   press at level 1 ALWAYS raises to level 2 and auto-fires that turn; a player
   who wants the half-charge shot uses a tap-and-move (snap-shot) or a dedicated
   release key. Decide with input/PlayerController owner.
6. BEAM VISUAL: stylized center-screen line (recommended, cheap, and correct
