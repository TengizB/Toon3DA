OVERVIEW
--------
Orders 1-6 set every number from arithmetic and the simulator. The owner's own
words set the bar: "I test new versions playing until I see what I want to see",
and the questionnaire's ammo (Part G) and card (J2) answers were left blank to be
filled from play. This order is the loop that turns the owner's ordinary phone
play into final numbers, using only what exists without any developer tooling:
  - the owner's short, structured PLAY NOTES after each session (the form is in
    this file — it takes two minutes, in the style of the questionnaire);
  - the simulator's reports added by orders 1-6 (LADDER, FLOOR, MATCHUP, HEAVY,
    BUILD, run-outcome, S-BOSS, S-SHOP) and BalanceReport's living tables;
  - the MASTER KNOBS: the dozen BalanceConfig constants orders 1-6 introduced as
    whole-system scales, which are what a round moves.
Rounds repeat at most three times. The order also fixes the one thing that has
kept most behavioural bands waived since new-game-balancr order 9: the
simulator's navigation, so the gates finally measure the game.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - SimNavigator improved until scripted policies reach the exit on > 90% of
    floors; the navigation waiver deleted; every depth-derived S-band enforced.
  - Playtest rounds (max 3): brief -> owner plays normally -> PLAY NOTES ->
    analysis (notes + sim + BalanceReport) -> tuning.
  - The PLAY NOTES form (RULES P4) and the MASTER KNOBS list (P6).
  - Sim policies calibrated to what the owner reports; sim bands re-centred
    where a target was the wrong proxy.
  - Filling Part G (ammo) and J2 (perk picks) from the notes and the sim.

OUT (deliberately not doing now)
  - Building any developer tooling. That is order 9, optional, and nothing here
    waits for it.
  - New mechanics, systems, content or UI. A finding that needs one becomes a
    new idea file (via creative-game-designer), not a change here.
  - Tuning anything outside balance (renderer, story text, sound).
  - More than three rounds. Remaining gaps after round 3 go to a follow-up idea
    with the notes and reports attached.


GAMEPLAY LOOP
-------------
  Round n:
  1. The implementer writes the round's PLAYTEST BRIEF: what changed since the
     last build, what to pay attention to (two or three focus points), and the
     PLAY NOTES form.
  2. The owner plays normally on the phone — at least ROUND_MIN_RUNS (5) runs —
     and after each session sends one filled PLAY NOTES form (typed into the
     chat).
  3. The main thread saves the notes verbatim; the implementer runs balanceSim
     and BalanceReport on the same build and writes the ANALYSIS: each target
     metric from the sim, each owner answer beside it, where they agree, where
     they disagree, and the TUNING PROPOSAL (which master knobs / constants
     move, why, expected effect).
  4. The owner approves the proposal (or edits it); the numbers move in
     BalanceConfig; both gates green; the build goes to the owner for the next
     round.


RULES
-----
  P1  Artifacts (written by the implementer, outside the ideas folder):
        .claude/balance-feedback/playtest-round-<n>-brief.md
        .claude/balance-feedback/play-notes/round-<n>/<date>-<k>.md  (verbatim)
        .claude/balance-feedback/playtest-round-<n>-analysis.md
      The analysis is the evidence for every BalanceConfig change of the round;
      each change's CHANGELOG line cites it.
  P2  ROUND SIZE: >= ROUND_MIN_RUNS (5) normal runs by the owner, noted on the
      form, plus one full balanceSim matrix and a BalanceReport regeneration on
      the round's build. No dev scenarios are required.
  P3  TARGET METRICS — each has a SIM source (always available) and an OWNER
      source (the form); a metric is in band when the sim is in band AND the
      owner's answers do not contradict it:
        T2  lag punished     sim LADDER REPORT: HOARDER hits-to-kill >= 1.5x
                             TACTICAL at depths 3-5 / owner Q8
        T3  floors full      sim FLOOR REPORT: floors 1-3 >= 12 enemies, first
                             damage exchange <= 15 turns, >= 2 groups of >= 3 /
                             owner Q2, Q3
        T4  fair supply      sim: zero floors below the heal floor, TACTICAL
                             median COMBAT exit HP 55-80%, <= 30% of deaths with
                             0 heals and < 20% ammo held / owner Q1, Q4, Q5
        T5  heavies          sim HEAVY REPORT: TACTICAL >= 80% avoided/guarded/
                             staggered, NAIVE <= 30% / owner Q7
        T6  weapon roles     sim MATCHUP REPORT: no DamageClass > 50% of TACTICAL
                             damage; >= 1 hint-driven switch per COMBAT floor /
                             owner Q6
        T7  builds           sim BUILD REPORT: no dominant tag on > 40% of seeds,
                             no keystone > 50% / owner Q9
        T8  run shape        sim run-outcome bands (order 6 F8) and S-BOSS /
                             owner Q11 and the per-run lines (deepest floor, how
                             it ended; first boss beaten in the first round?)
        T9  shop             sim S-SHOP / owner Q10
        G   ammo             sim FLOOR REPORT reserve at exit 10-60% of cap per
                             carried type, lifeline < 5% of floors / owner Q5
        J2  perk picks       sim BUILD REPORT pick rates (no perk > 60% of its
                             offers) / owner Q9 ("always picked", "never picked")
  P4  THE PLAY NOTES FORM (one per play session; short answers are fine; "don't
      know" is valid). The brief reproduces it verbatim:

        PLAY NOTES — build/date: ______   runs this session: ___
        Per run, one line:  deepest floor __ | ended: death / quit / victory |
          killed by ______ | main weapon ______ | did a level-up or a better
          weapon change a fight?  y / n
         1. Did your deaths feel like YOUR mistake?  yes / mostly / no — why?
         2. Floors 1-3 felt:  boring / good / too hard
         3. Any floor empty or overflowing? which floor number, which layout
            (rooms / corridors / cave)?
         4. Healing: ran out / about right / too much.  Arriving at a new floor
            you were usually at:  full / three-quarters / half / low
         5. Ammo: which type ran out? which piled up?
         6. Did you switch weapon because of an enemy?  never / sometimes / often
         7. Heavy attacks (red floor): you dodged or guarded  most / some / few.
            Did getting hit by one feel fair?  y / n
         8. When your weapon's LV turned red, was that:  too punishing / right /
            not punishing enough / never happened
         9. Builds: which keystone(s) did you take? which perk did you always
            pick, which never? did runs feel different from each other?
        10. Shop: bought per floor  0 / 1 / 2+ ;  prices  too high / OK / too low
        11. Bosses you met: which, and  too easy / OK / too hard
        12. Best moment, worst moment (one line each).

  P5  TUNING DISCIPLINE: a round changes only numbers (BalanceConfig, sim policy
      parameters, S-band values), each tied to a P3 metric or a P4 answer.
      A number moves toward its band; a band itself moves only when the owner's
      answers consistently contradict it across >= 3 runs (the band was a wrong
      proxy) — recorded as an override with the evidence.
  P6  MASTER KNOBS (preferred first moves, all ordinary BalanceConfig
      constants owned by the order that introduced them):
        LEVEL_GAP_STEEPNESS, LADDER_GROWTH, ENEMY_HEALTH_GROWTH,
        ENEMY_DAMAGE_GROWTH, PLAYER_VITALITY_GROWTH           (order 1)
        BODY_TARGET_MIN/MAX, NodeSupplySpec ammoRatio / drainTarget,
        SUPPLY_HEAL_FLOOR_FRACTION                            (order 2)
        MATCHUP_STRENGTH, per-AmmoType generosity             (order 3)
        HEAVY_DAMAGE_SCALE, GUARD_FRONT_HEAVY                 (order 4)
        perk / keystone magnitudes                            (order 5)
        REGION_TP_BUDGET_MULTIPLIER, SHOP_CREDITS_PER_POWER_POINT,
        BossTable target fight turns                          (order 6)
      A round moves at most ~5 of them, so their effects stay attributable.
  P7  SIM CALIBRATION: TacticalPolicy parameters (heal threshold, guard/avoid
      rules, shopping, switching) are adjusted so the sim's TACTICAL outcomes
      (depth reached, arrival HP band, death causes) resemble what the owner
      reports at the same depths. The sim is a proxy for the owner, not the other
      way round.
  P8  NAVIGATION: SimNavigator reaches the exit on > 90% of floors for all
      three policies over the full seed matrix; the [SIM_*] navigation waiver in
      BalanceSchema and docs is DELETED and every depth-derived band is enforced.
  P9  STOP: the order is done when every P3 metric is in band in one round and
      the owner answers Q1 "yes"/"mostly" and marks "a level-up or better weapon
      changed a fight" on most runs, OR after round 3 (then the remaining gaps
      are written into a follow-up idea request for the designer).
  P10 OPTIONAL EXTRA INPUT: if order 9 (dev tools) has been built, the owner may
      also paste copied run logs; they are saved under
      .claude/balance-feedback/logs/round-<n>/ and used to sharpen P3 (measured
      values replace the owner's estimates where both exist). Their absence
      never blocks a round, a checkpoint or the stop condition.

EDGE CASES
----------
  - The owner sends fewer runs than P2: the analysis marks each owner-sourced
    metric with its sample size and proposes only changes whose owner evidence
    covers >= 3 runs (sim-only evidence may still move a number that is out of
    its sim band).
  - Notes from an older build: excluded from the analysis.
  - Contradictory answers vs sim metrics: the answers win for bands (P5), the
    sim wins for numbers inside a band.
  - "Don't know" answers: the metric falls back to the sim alone for that round.
  - A metric that needs a system change (not a number): logged as a follow-up
    idea, never patched here.
