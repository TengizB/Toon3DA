OVERVIEW
--------
The owner's top complaint: "I can play with a level-1 weapon very confidently even
on higher levels. I want to feel that a level-2 weapon is actually much stronger
than a level-1 weapon, and enemy health must scale with it. I want to feel like I
cannot play with a level-1 weapon on a level-3 map." Today a weapon level is +10%
damage (linear), enemy HP grows 4.2% a floor, rarity is contractually forbidden
from raising power, and the player's HP never grows with character level — so the
gap between a lazy and a prepared player is a rounding error.

This order builds ONE steep, readable power ladder shared by both sides: every
floor has a THREAT LEVEL equal to its depth; weapon damage and enemy HP grow by
the same compound step; a weapon BELOW the floor's threat level takes a sharp
multiplicative penalty per level; rarity multiplies damage; character level
multiplies max HP/armour while enemy damage grows to match; heals become a
percentage of max. The result: an on-curve player's fights feel the same length
at floor 1 and floor 20, a player two levels behind feels it immediately, and the
game SAYS so on the HUD. Base enemy HP is rebased so fights are short (no more
sponges) — floors will get more bodies in order 2.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - Floor THREAT LEVEL = depth; RUN_FINAL_DEPTH = 25 introduced as the audit
    horizon for every depth-swept rule (the run itself ends at 25 in order 6).
  - Compound weapon level growth, level-gap multiplier, rarity damage multiplier.
  - Enemy HP / damage compound growth fitted to the ladder; rebased depth-1 enemy
    HP to the TTK targets.
  - Player max HP / max armour growth per character level; heals and armour
    pickups as fractions of max.
  - ONE expected-player model, GameMath.expectedPlayerAtDepth, used by the audit,
    the simulator and boss derivation (and, if order 9 is ever built, its
    ON-CURVE dev kit).
  - Shop weapon LEVEL-UP made a reliable, affordable ladder rung.
  - Communication: weapon level vs floor threat on the HUD, the weapon compare
    card, floor arrival, and one ORA teaching topic.
  - New schema rule R-LADDER replacing R-DEPTH and R-GEARGATE; every other
    depth-swept rule re-fitted to the new curves at horizon 1..25.
  - LEVEL_GAP_STEEPNESS as an ordinary BalanceConfig constant (1.0), the one
    knob that scales the whole lag penalty.
  - Sim LADDER REPORT: measured hits-to-kill per role for on-curve vs lagging
    play, so the ladder is checked in PLAY, not only on paper.

OUT (deliberately not doing now)
  - More enemies per floor, groups, supply planner — order 2 (floors will feel

  - Weapon roles, damage classes, matchups — order 3. This order keeps every

  - Heavy attacks, guard, armour rework — order 4.
  - Perks/keystones and ability rework — order 5 (abilities are only re-spanned
    to the longer level range here, not redesigned).
  - Ending the run at 25, bosses 20/25, full shop economy — order 6.
  - Any developer dial, DEV START kit or log field — order 9 (optional).
  - A weapon-XP / "weapon levels up by use" system. Levels come from finds and
    the shop only (see D-notes).
  - Showing numbers to the story layer. ORA never states a number.


GAMEPLAY LOOP
-------------
  1. Floor 4 loads -> event text "THREAT LV 4" for 2 s beside the floor name.
  2. HUD right panel shows "SHOTGUN  LV 2" with a red down-arrow: the weapon is
     two levels under the floor. Shots visibly take ~1.7x as many hits as they
     did on floor 2.
  3. First time this happens in the save: ORA (teaching topic UNDERGEARED):
     "That gun's falling behind what lives down here. Upgrade it or replace it."
  4. Player finds an ASSAULT RIFLE LV 4 (UNCOMMON). The compare card shows
     "LV 4 / FLOOR 4" and the effective damage of both weapons ON THIS FLOOR.
     Picking it up is a decision the numbers make obvious.
  5. At a Fabricator: "LEVEL UP: SHOTGUN LV 2 -> 3" is always one of the offers
     for the equipped weapon, priced so one floor's credits buy it. The player
     chooses: keep a favourite gun on the ladder with credits, or switch to finds
     and spend credits on supplies.


RULES
-----
  R1  THREAT LEVEL of a floor = its depth d (1..RUN_FINAL_DEPTH). Boss floors,
      gates and calm floors use the same value.
  R2  Player outgoing weapon damage per hit:
        base(weapon) * LADDER_GROWTH^(L-1) * rarityMultiplier(tier)
                     * levelGapMultiplier(L - d)   [then existing modifiers]
      L = weapon level (1..MAX_WEAPON_LEVEL). One GameMath method composes it.
  R3  levelGapMultiplier(gap):
        gap < 0 : max(LEVEL_GAP_FLOOR,
                      LEVEL_GAP_PENALTY_BASE ^ (-gap * LEVEL_GAP_STEEPNESS))
        gap = 0 : 1.0
        gap > 0 : 1 + LEVEL_GAP_BONUS_PER_LEVEL * min(gap, LEVEL_GAP_BONUS_CAP_LEVELS)
      Defaults: PENALTY_BASE 0.82, FLOOR 0.25, BONUS_PER_LEVEL 0.05, BONUS_CAP 2.
      LEVEL_GAP_STEEPNESS = 1.0 (BalanceConfig; 0 disables the penalty — the
      order-7 feel pass tunes it).
  R4  The FIST is exempt: its level always equals the floor's threat level and
      it takes no gap penalty (it is the never-softlock backstop, S-SOFTLOCK).
      Every other melee weapon follows R2/R3.
  R5  rarityMultiplier: COMMON 1.00, UNCOMMON 1.10, RARE 1.20, EPIC 1.32,
      LEGENDARY 1.45 (on top of the tier's ability budget, which stays).
  R6  Weapon levels: MAX_WEAPON_LEVEL = RUN_FINAL_DEPTH + 2 (27). Found / dropped
      weapons roll level d + offset, offset weights {-1: 30, 0: 50, +1: 20}
      (order 2 sets per-node offsets for ELITE/CACHE rewards). Start weapon: L1.
      Ability magnitudes, accuracy, reload and clip level-scaling are RE-SPANNED
      so their old level-1..10 curve now spans 1..MAX (same start and end
      values), keeping R-ABILITY's priced budgets meaningful.
  R7  Enemy max HP at depth d = rebasedBaseHP(type) * ENEMY_HEALTH_GROWTH^(d-1);
      enemy outgoing damage = baseDamage(type) * ENEMY_DAMAGE_GROWTH^(d-1).
      Both growth values are FITTED (math-expert) so R-LADDER L1 holds; starting
      points 1.09 / 1.07. The old 1.042 / 1.073 constants are replaced.
  R8  Rebased depth-1 TTK targets (on-curve player, reference workhorse = the
      Assault Rifle, COMMON L1, measured at 3 tiles including falloff):
        CHAFF 1-2 hits | SOLDIER 3-4 | BRUISER 5-7 | MINI_ELITE 8-12.
      Rebased depth-1 TTD targets (hits the player survives from full HP+armour,
      ordinary attacks only): CHAFF >= 10 | SOLDIER 7-9 | BRUISER 4-6 |
      MINI_ELITE 3-5. Base HP / damage of each EnemyType is set to land inside its
      role's targets; the new values live in BalanceConfig SECTION 2.
  R9  Player max HP = PLAYER_MAX_HEALTH * PLAYER_VITALITY_GROWTH^(level-1) + card/
      stat bonuses; max armour likewise with the same growth. Starting value 1.07,
      fitted with R7's damage growth so R-LADDER L1 (TTD half) holds.
  R10 Heals are fractions of max: MEDKIT_FULL ('H') 0.45 of max HP, STIM ('+')
      0.18, ARMOUR_SHARD ('a') 0.15 of max armour, ARMOUR_VEST ('A') 0.60.
      (Replaces the flat 90 / 32 / 14 / 62 values.) Rounded, minimum 1.
  R11 Expected player at depth d (GameMath.expectedPlayerAtDepth, pure): weapon
      level d, expected rarity multiplier for d's region (from the per-region drop
      tier bands WEAPON_DROP_TIER_*_BY_REGION), character level
      1 + EXPECTED_LEVELS_PER_DEPTH * (d-1), max HP/armour per R9, expected card
      offence/defence power at that level, reference weapon per R8. This is THE
      on-curve player for audit, sim and boss derivation (R-BOSS-*).
  R12 R-LADDER (new schema rule; replaces R-DEPTH and R-GEARGATE), for every depth
      1..RUN_FINAL_DEPTH and every non-boss role's reference archetype:
        L1 ON-CURVE FLAT  expected-player TTK and TTD stay within +/-15% of their
                          depth-1 values.
        L2 LAG PUNISHED   weapon level d-2 -> TTK >= 1.5x on-curve;
                          d-4 -> >= 2.5x; the start weapon (L1 COMMON) at d >= 5
                          -> >= 3.0x.
        L3 AHEAD BOUNDED  weapon level d+2 -> TTK >= 0.75x on-curve.
        L4 LEVEL FELT     on floor d, level d vs level d-1: DPT gain >= +25%.
        L5 RARITY FELT    each tier step >= +8% DPT; LEGENDARY >= +40% vs COMMON
                          at equal level (abilities excluded).
        L6 VITALITY FELT  character level d-3 -> TTD <= 0.80x on-curve.
      (Check with defaults: L4 = 1.08/0.82 = 1.32; L2 at d-2 = 1/(0.857*0.672)
      = 1.74, at d-4 = 3.0.)
  R13 SHOP LEVEL-UP RUNG: every Fabricator machine always offers LEVEL UP for the
      equipped weapon (a guaranteed slot, outside the random category roll) while
      its level < d + 1; a shop level-up can never raise a weapon above d + 1.
      Price: R-LADDER-AFFORD — at every depth 1..25 the level-up price is <= 50% of
      the modelled credit income of one COMBAT floor at that depth.
  R14 COMMUNICATION (must all exist):
        a. HUD right panel: "LV n" after the weapon name. Colour: normal when
           L >= d, amber when L = d-1, red with a down-arrow glyph when L <= d-2,
           green up-arrow when L > d. Glyphs are procedural (no textures).
        b. Floor arrival: event text "THREAT LV d" under the existing floor/node
           arrival text, 2 s (cosmetic).
        c. Weapon compare / pickup card: both weapons show "LV n" and their
           EFFECTIVE per-hit damage on THIS floor (gap and rarity applied).
        d. Inventory weapon rows show "LV n" with the same colour rule.
        e. ORA TeachingTopic UNDERGEARED (TACTICAL, re-teach threshold 3): first
           taught when the equipped weapon is >= 2 levels under the floor for a
           whole floor arrival; re-taught (different, shorter words) after 3
           further floors started that far behind. Lines follow
           docs/narrative-authority.txt: no numbers.
  R15 The TacticalPolicy buys the level-up rung for its main weapon when behind
      and it can afford it; HOARDER-START-WEAPON never does (unchanged). NAIVE
      never shops (unchanged).


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
-----------------
Every find and every Fabricator visit now asks "does this keep me on the ladder?"
Falling behind is not a silent drift but a red arrow and fights that visibly
drag — and the fix is always in reach (a find, one floor of credits, or the
rarity of what you already carry). Taught by ORA once (UNDERGEARED) and by the
HUD colour forever after. The floor's threat level is announced on arrival so
the player always knows the number they are measured against.

EDGE CASES
----------
  - Weapon picked up mid-floor above/below the floor: effective immediately; the
    HUD recolours on pickup.
  - Floor threat level changes only at floor build; a weapon levelled at a shop
    recolours immediately.
  - Level 27 cap reached: the rung offer disappears (not shown greyed).
  - Fist equipped with no ammo anywhere: exempt, never penalised (R4).
  - Heal fraction when max HP changes mid-effect (level-up this turn): heal
    resolves against max HP at the moment of use.
  - Existing saves: runs are not saved mid-run, so no migration; nothing
    persistent changes.
  - Enemies that summon: summoned units spawn at the floor's d like any other.

ASSUMPTIONS
-----------
  AS1  "Level" in the owner's answers means the weapon's LV and the floor depth
       ("level-3 map" = floor 3). The ladder makes weapon LV comparable to depth
       one-for-one.
  AS2  Weapon levels come only from finds and the shop; no weapon XP.
  AS3  A 0.82-per-level penalty with a 0.25 floor is steep enough ("cannot play
       with LV1 on floor 3" reads as ~1.7x longer fights on floor 3 and ~3x by
       floor 5 — lethal once enemy damage growth compounds). Tunable via
       LEVEL_GAP_STEEPNESS in the order-7 feel pass.
  AS4  The Fist is the only exemption; the Combat Knife and other melee follow
       the ladder like guns.
  AS5  Heals as percentages keep a medkit meaningful at every depth; the absolute
       numbers the player sees still grow.
  AS6  Rarity gives a flat damage multiplier AND keeps its ability budget
       (abilities are redesigned in order 5).
  AS7  A small "ahead" bonus (+5%/level, cap 2) is enough reward for an elite
       find without trivialising a floor.
  AS8  The floor threat level is a whole number equal to depth (no region jumps),
       so every floor steps the same amount.
  AS9  Keeping one favourite weapon on the ladder should cost about half of a
       floor's credits — a real trade against supplies, never out of reach.
  AS10 Bigger enemy HP numbers deep in the run are acceptable.
