OVERVIEW
--------
Every level generator is rebuilt around three guarantees. Each floor is laid out SPECIAL ROOMS FIRST, drawn
with equal chance from the whole catalog from floor 1. Every room opening is a one-tile doorway with a wall
on each side. Every object in a room is placed by that room's PURPOSE (a kit of designed placements, with a
little randomness) and never by a per-tile dice roll. A new dedicated agent, `level-generation-engineer`,
is created first and owns this work and all level work after it.

WHAT THE OWNER SAID (2026-10-23): level quality dropped sharply after the balance-overhaul ideas changed
generation. (1) Props must follow designer logic plus a bit of randomness, never pure chance: terminals
belong in labs and server rooms, never in front of a door. Drop the current random prop selection and
placement completely. (2) Props never block a door or any passage, and a non-walkable object is never
spawned right next to a door. (3) Doors always have walls on both sides; today you can walk past a door
into its room. (4) Special rooms seem never to spawn. Place them FIRST and build regular rooms and halls
around them. Every type gets an equal chance from level 1, with no depth gating and no unequal weights.
Every level has special rooms except boss areas. (5) Random props make rooms hard to move through. An
occasional tight room is fine, most rooms are not. Refine every generator, one at a time, with one
checkpoint each. No confirmations needed.

WHAT THE OWNER NEEDS: floors that read as a real, inhabited facility at a glance. You can tell which room
is the server room. A door means "a room starts here". You never have to fight the furniture to cross a
room. And the memorable rooms actually turn up, on the floors people play most (1-5).

WHY QUALITY FELL - the concrete causes, found in the code on 2026-10-23
  C1 PROPS ARE A PER-TILE DICE ROLL. LevelGenerator.placeStandardPropsForRoom rolls every floor tile of
     every STANDARD room at BalanceConfig.LEVEL_GEN_PROP_CHANCE (0.13). randomPropChar() then picks g/C/T/L
     (solid) or ./m/O (decal) by LevelGenConfig weights. LinearCorridorGenerator.placeStandardRoomProps does
     the same with {g, E, C, #}. SUPPLY_CACHE runs the scatter TWICE. MEDICAL_BAY and CONTAINMENT_BLOCK roll
     per tile too (randomMedicalPropChar / randomContainmentPropChar), so a medical bay holds random crates,
     terminals and oil, nothing medical. tryPlaceAtmosphericProp* drops props on a random interior tile.
     CavernGenerator.placeBiomeProps rolls 3.5% per cave tile, placeStalagmites 3%. The only guards are
     "not orthogonally next to a door" and "not on the door's axis" (isAdjacentToDoor /
     isAdjacentToDoorAxis). Nothing keeps a walking lane through a room, nothing stops props splitting a
     room into pockets, and diagonal tiles next to a door are allowed.
  C2 THE REPAIRS MAKE IT WORSE. Pocketed rooms are reopened by verifyAndRepairConnectivity /
     carveEmergencyCorridor and repairUnreachableFloorRegions. Both run AFTER placeDoors and carve straight
     through room walls, often right beside a door. Then FloorPopulator.clearPluggedPassages deletes props
     that plug a passage. The result: random holes in walls, beside doors, and props that vanish where the
     dice landed worst.
  C3 DOORS ARE DECIDED PER TILE, NOT PER ROOM. placeDoors (in both LevelGenerator and
     LinearCorridorGenerator) turns any 'l' corridor tile next to a ' '/'u'/'f' tile into a door, 75% of
     the time (LEVEL_GEN_DOOR_CHANCE). Corridors are L-shapes from room CENTRE to room CENTRE, and
     carveHorizontal/VerticalSegment turns every 'x' on the line into floor. So:
       - a corridor that runs along a third room's wall row opens that whole wall;
       - corridors cut straight through other rooms;
       - a quarter of room entrances are bare gaps with no door;
       - the 3-wide hall spine is carved as ' ', the room-floor character, so the door pass cannot tell a
         hall from a room;
       - the emergency carves (C2) punch new gaps next to existing doors;
       - LinearCorridorGenerator.carveSpinePlaza turns every wall within its radius to floor, eating the
         walls of side rooms that sit against the spine.
     Any one of these leaves a door that "blocks nothing".
  C4 SPECIAL ROOMS ARE A LOTTERY AFTER THE FACT. Rooms are placed at random sizes first. A blueprint is
     then rolled onto each room, weighted by size eligibility:
       - weights: STANDARD 5.0, special 1.0, set-piece 3.0, observatory 20.0;
       - depth gates: STELLAR_OBSERVATORY, GORE_NEST and ATMOSPHERIC_PLANT need depth >= 2, so they never
         appear on floor 1;
       - the observatory needs a 13x13 interior, which only the 16% LARGE modifier can produce;
       - balance-overhaul order 2 (E6) shrank the room window to about 50% on floor 1 (FootprintPlanner),
         so fewer rooms fit any special's size gate;
       - CavernGenerator ignores the registry. Its typing is hardcoded (medical bay nearest the centre,
         armory 80%, cryo/containment bands). Its chambers are at most 7x6, so CRYO (min 7x7) and
         CONTAINMENT (min 8 wide) can never appear in a cave, and only 4 of the 13 specials exist there;
       - LinearCorridorGenerator adds two local types (STORAGE_BAY, REACTOR) outside the registry;
       - LEVEL_GEN_MIN_SPECIAL_ROOMS is a best-effort backstop.
  C5 SPECIAL ROOMS DO NOT READ AS SPECIAL. Their dressing is the C1 scatter. generated-wall-art order 2
     retired their wall theming (the R1 ledger keeps the dice draws but writes nothing), and region sets
     B-E still alias set A. A medical bay that happens to spawn looks like any other room, which is half of
     why the owner says "they never spawn".
  C6 SYMBOL MEANING CAN DRIFT. 'T' is a FLEXIBLE prop symbol (TilesetConstants.FLEXIBLE_SOLIDPROP_SYMBOLS).
     On ROOMS_MST the SymbolAllocator may bind it to another sprite when no placed room reserves it. Yet
     World treats every 'T' as a story LOG terminal (StoryUiConstants.STORY_LOG_TERMINAL_SYMBOL). A
     "terminal" can therefore render as some other object. (CP2's audit confirms how often.)

GAMEPLAY LOOP
-------------
This is a generation change. There is no new player action, no turn cost and no new control. What follows
is (A) what every combat floor's generator does, in order, and (B) what the player experiences.

A. GENERATION PIPELINE (ROOMS_MST / LINEAR_CORRIDOR / CAVERN; the bespoke floors follow the same rules)
  1. SPECIAL PLAN. From the floor seed salted by LevelGenConstants.SPECIAL_ROOM_SEED_SALT (NOT the footprint
     attempt seed), SpecialRoomPlanner draws N special room types uniformly WITHOUT replacement from every
     registered SPECIAL blueprint. N = GameMath.specialRoomCountForFootprint(target walkable tiles) (R2).
     The draw is fixed for the floor before any footprint attempt.
  2. FOOTPRINT ATTEMPT (FootprintPlanner.buildToTarget, unchanged contract). For each attempt:
     a. Size each planned special from its window, scaled by the attempt's footprint scale (R4).
     b. PLACE THE SPECIALS FIRST, spread across the window (R5).
     c. Place the ENTRANCE room (R6), then fill REGULAR rooms into the space left (R7).
     d. Give every room its SOCKETS (doorway tiles in its wall ring) and route corridors socket-to-socket
        with CorridorRouter (R9-R13). Room shells are never cut except at sockets. Some MST edges become
        3-wide halls that taper to the 1-wide socket.
     e. Each room's blueprint builds its ARCHITECTURE (floor lighting, columns, observatory shell, lab
        alcove) and exports FEATURE slots.
     f. RoomDresser dresses every room from its kit (R15-R27): signature row first, then supporting rows,
        then decals. Every solid placement is checked against door clearance, lanes, plug rules, room
        connectivity and the fill cap.
     g. Hall pass: walkable decals only, plus the existing spaced columns on 3-wide halls (R28).
     h. DoorwayRules.validate: the final check on the finished grid (R14).
     i. Connectivity audit (must now be a no-op; R30), keycard gate, stairs.
     j. Encounter + supply + barrels through the unchanged shared pipeline (FloorPopulator, with
        GroupBarrelPlacer obeying R26).
     k. LevelFinisher dresses the walls (unchanged). The palette request carries every placed kit's demand,
        and always 'T' -> prop_terminal and 'E' -> prop_explosive_barrel (R29).
  3. The FootprintPlanner keeps the attempt closest to target, exactly as today.

B. WHAT THE PLAYER SEES
  - Entering a floor: a clean entrance room with no solid props, and one or more doors leading out.
  - A door is always set in a wall: two wall tiles flank it, a corridor is on one side, a room on the other.
    Walking through it is the only way into that part of the room.
  - Inside, the furniture hugs the walls, corners and back wall: lockers by the door wall, a console desk
    on the back wall. A clear lane runs from every doorway to the room's middle. A special room shows its
    signature at once: pod rows, console banks, a generator block, weapon racks.
  - At most one room per floor is deliberately tight (a server room's rack aisles, a salvage heap).
    Everything else is easy to cross.
  - From floor 1, any of the 13 special rooms can be on the floor, and every floor (bosses aside) has at
    least two.


RULES
-----
Definitions used below.
  ROOM: an interior rectangle plus its one-tile wall ring (the SHELL). Cave chambers are rooms too.
  SOCKET: a shell tile that is a doorway: a door leaf ('d', or 'R'/'Y'/'B' once gated) or an open doorway
    floor tile.
  FLUSH TILE: an interior tile with a shell wall as an orthogonal neighbour (not a socket).
  DOOR CLEARANCE ZONE of a socket: every tile within Chebyshev distance 1 of it (LEVEL_GEN_DOOR_CLEARANCE_
    RADIUS = 1), plus the two tiles at distance 2 straight out on both sides along its axis
    (LEVEL_GEN_DOOR_AXIS_CLEARANCE = 2).
  HUB: the room's interior centre tile, or, when a CENTRE feature claims the centre, the ring of tiles
    around that feature.
  LANE: the shortest 4-connected interior path from each socket's inner tile to the HUB (ties broken by the
    dresser's seeded stream).
  LANE BAND: the lane tiles plus their orthogonal interior neighbours (half-width 1,
    LEVEL_GEN_LANE_BAND_HALF_WIDTH). In a DENSE room the band is the lane alone (half-width 0).
  SOLID: a solid prop (Level.isPropSolid) or a column 'P'.
  FILL: SOLID tiles inside the interior / interior tile count.

SPECIAL ROOMS
  R1  SPECIAL pool = every registered RoomBlueprint other than "entrance" and "standard": today server_room,
      medical_bay, armory, cryo_chamber, power_plant, command_center, containment_block, research_lab,
      stellar_observatory, gore_nest, atmospheric_plant, salvage_bay and supply_cache (13). There is no
      depth input, no per-type weight and no per-type cap anywhere. RoomContext's depth field and
      RoomBlueprint.eligible / selectionWeight are removed.
  R2  Specials per floor N = clamp(round(targetWalkableTiles / LEVEL_GEN_WALKABLE_TILES_PER_SPECIAL_ROOM
      (260)), LEVEL_GEN_SPECIAL_ROOMS_MIN (2), LEVEL_GEN_SPECIAL_ROOMS_MAX (4)), as
      GameMath.specialRoomCountForFootprint with the standard comment block. Reference values from today's
      targets (original walkable x (1 - FOOTPRINT_REDUCTION_BY_DEPTH)):
        ROOMS_MST        d1 565 -> 2, d2 678 -> 3, d3 791 -> 3, d4 848 -> 3, d5+ 904 -> 3
        LINEAR_CORRIDOR  d1 350 -> 2 (min), d2 420 -> 2, d3 490 -> 2, d4 525 -> 2, d5+ 560 -> 2
        CAVERN           d1 1050 -> 4, d2+ -> 4 (max)
      The function reads the target FootprintPlanner hands the generator, so a later footprint change moves
      the count with it.
  R3  The N types are drawn uniformly WITHOUT replacement (no duplicate type on a floor) from the floor
      seed salted with SPECIAL_ROOM_SEED_SALT, before any footprint attempt. Retries never change WHICH
      specials appear, so small rooms are not favoured by rebuild bias.
  R4  Each blueprint declares a SIZE WINDOW (interior min W x H, max W x H, rotatable unless SQUARE). An
      attempt sizes a special uniformly in [min, min + (max - min) x clamp(footprintScale, 0, 1)] per axis,
      so small floors get near-minimum specials. Windows are in TECHNICAL NOTES. Every window's MINIMUM is
      chosen so its signature row fits with 3 sockets present.
  R5  Specials are placed FIRST in an empty window by best-candidate sampling: LEVEL_GEN_SPECIAL_PLACEMENT_
      CANDIDATES (12) random positions, keeping the one that maximises distance to the specials already
      placed, respecting LEVEL_GEN_ROOM_MARGIN. Every planned special MUST be placed. If one cannot be after
      LEVEL_GEN_PLACEMENT_TRIES, that footprint attempt shrinks it to its window minimum. If it still fails,
      the attempt is discarded (FootprintPlanner tries the next). A planned special is never swapped for
      another type or dropped. As an absolute last resort (every attempt failed), the floor is built at
      window minimums and the 1.0 scale.
  R6  ENTRANCE (ROOMS_MST): placed after the specials, never a special, interior 5x5..7x7, and within
      LEVEL_GEN_ENTRANCE_MAX_HOPS_TO_ROOM (one corridor hop) of at least one other room, so the
      first-contact rule (FIRST_CONTACT_*) stays reachable. LINEAR keeps its small entrance room
      (LEVEL_GEN_SPINE_ENTRANCE_MAX_DIM 7). CAVERN's entrance chamber is a regular-size walled chamber.
  R7  REGULAR rooms fill the remaining window up to the footprint's room target, interior 4x4..12x10
      (LEVEL_GEN_ROOM_MIN/MAX_WIDTH/HEIGHT become 4/12 and 4/10). The LARGE modifier is gone; big spaces
      come from specials and 3-wide halls.
  R8  Every ROOMS_MST, LINEAR_CORRIDOR and CAVERN floor carries exactly its planned N >= 2 specials. The
      bespoke floors (MED_BAY clinic, EVENT_ROOM chamber, GATE_AIRLOCK threshold, start-room staging
      armory) are each one purpose-built special room by definition. BOSS_ARENA is the owner's stated
      exception.

DOORWAYS AND CORRIDORS
  R9  A room's shell is never carved except at its sockets. No corridor, plaza, emergency carve or prop
      pass may turn a shell tile into floor. Corridors may run alongside a shell; they never touch it
      except at a socket's outer tile.
  R10 A socket is exactly one tile wide. It is never a corner, and is at least LEVEL_GEN_SOCKET_CORNER_MIN_
      DISTANCE (2) tiles from either corner of its side, so both flank tiles are straight shell walls.
      Sockets on the same side are >= LEVEL_GEN_SOCKET_MIN_SPACING (3) tiles apart. A room has
      1..LEVEL_GEN_MAX_SOCKETS_PER_ROOM (3) sockets; the entrance has at most 2.
  R11 The socket's outer tile is a corridor (or spine / cave) floor tile straight out from the socket. The
      corridor's last step INTO a socket is perpendicular to the shell.
  R12 CorridorRouter routes each MST edge and each loop edge (rooms/3 loops, LEVEL_GEN_LOOP_MAX_DISTANCE
      unchanged) socket-outer-tile to socket-outer-tile. It runs a seeded A* on the tile grid:
        - room interiors and shells are impassable except the two endpoints;
        - a tile orthogonally next to another room's shell costs LEVEL_GEN_ROUTE_SHELL_HUG_COST (4);
        - an existing corridor tile costs LEVEL_GEN_ROUTE_MERGE_COST (0.5), so corridors merge;
        - every other tile costs 1.
      Each new edge first reuses an existing socket on the facing side, else adds one (R10). An edge with
      no legal route is skipped. MST edges retry with the next-nearest room pair until all rooms are
      connected.
  R13 WIDE HALLS: LEVEL_GEN_WIDE_HALLWAY_COUNT MST edges (unchanged count) are carved 3 wide along their
      route wherever the 3-wide band keeps >= 1 tile from every shell. They narrow to 1 wide for the last
      LEVEL_GEN_WIDE_HALL_TAPER_TILES (2) tiles before a socket. The hall spine is carved as 'l' like any
      corridor, never as ' ', so no later pass can mistake a hall for a room.
  R14 DOOR RULE (checked by DoorwayRules.validate on every finished grid, every generator):
        - a door tile has walls on both tiles of one axis and walkable tiles on both tiles of the other;
        - no door is orthogonally next to another door;
        - every opening in a room shell is a socket satisfying R10.
      The validator is a last-resort guard. A door failing the rule is DEMOTED to open floor 'l' and
      counted. Construction must make the count zero (A7). Socket door leaves: a SPECIAL room's sockets
      always get a door (LEVEL_GEN_SPECIAL_DOOR_CHANCE 1.0); every other socket gets one at
      LEVEL_GEN_DOOR_CHANCE (0.75, unchanged); otherwise the socket stays an open doorway. The keycard gate
      still promotes one existing door (algorithm unchanged).

PROPS (the prop engine)
  R15 No solid prop or decal is ever placed by a per-tile chance roll. Every placement comes from a kit row
      (R16), a hall/cave rule (R28/G3), the boss arena's cover/barrel rules, or the shared supply/barrel
      pipeline. LEVEL_GEN_PROP_CHANCE, randomPropChar, randomMedicalPropChar, randomContainmentPropChar,
      tryPlaceAtmosphericProp* and the cave per-tile prop/stalagmite rolls are deleted.
  R16 A KIT is an ordered list of rows. Each row has:
        - a symbol (optionally with weighted alternatives);
        - an ANCHOR (R17);
        - a count range;
        - a row chance (default 1.0);
        - a mode: SINGLE, RUN (contiguous along one wall line), SPACED (every 2nd tile along one wall
          line), or CLUSTER (contiguous blob of up to the count).
      Row 1 of a special kit is its SIGNATURE: always present (chance 1.0, count >= its min) and placed
      before anything else. The randomness is in which candidate tiles are chosen, the counts within
      range, optional rows, alternates and mirror side. All of it comes from the dresser's own seeded
      stream (floor seed ^ LevelGenConstants.ROOM_DRESSING_SEED_SALT ^ room index), so dressing never
      shifts the layout's random sequence.
  R17 ANCHORS (PropAnchor, one enum constant per anchor with its own candidate-tile rule - the WaveformKind
      idiom, never a switch):
        BACK_WALL    flush tiles on the side opposite the room's PRIMARY socket (the socket on its corridor
                     toward the entrance; the entrance's first socket). Falls back to SIDE_WALL when that
                     side is too short.
        SIDE_WALL    flush tiles on the two sides perpendicular to the primary socket's side.
        ENTRY_WALL   flush tiles on the primary socket's side, outside every clearance zone.
        ANY_WALL     any flush tile.
        CORNER       interior tiles with two shell neighbours.
        CENTRE       the interior centre (a 1x1, 1x2, 2x2 or 1x3 footprint as the row says). Rooms smaller
                     than 7x7 interior skip a non-signature CENTRE row; a signature CENTRE row falls back to
                     BACK_WALL.
        ROWS         DENSE aisle pattern: parallel lines along the long axis with 1-tile aisles, a cross
                     aisle through the HUB, and the line broken in front of every socket's lane.
        FEATURE      named tiles exported by the room's architecture build (observatory core/spokes, the
                     research lab's alcove).
        NEAR         within Chebyshev 1 of a prop this kit already placed, symbol named by the row.
        FIELD        (decals only) any open interior tile, at a per-tile density given by the row - the ONE
                     density-based anchor, and decals are walkable.
        TRAIL        (decals only) 2-4 tiles along a lane, from the HUB side toward a socket, gaps allowed.
  R18 Every SOLID placement must pass ALL of:
        (a) not in any socket's DOOR CLEARANCE ZONE (this is the owner's "never right next to a door",
            including diagonals);
        (b) not in any LANE BAND of its room;
        (c) FloorPopulator.wouldPlugPassage is false for it;
        (d) after placing it, the room's open interior tiles stay ONE 4-connected region;
        (e) the room's FILL stays within its cap (R21).
      A failing candidate is skipped and the next one tried. A row that runs out of candidates places fewer.
      A signature row that cannot reach its min tries the next anchor in BACK_WALL -> SIDE_WALL -> ANY_WALL;
      the size windows (R4) guarantee it can.
  R19 Decals (m s . O e) are walkable. They may sit on lanes but never on a socket tile. They come only from
      kit rows (FIELD / TRAIL / NEAR / SINGLE) and the hall rule (R28).
  R20 TERMINALS. 'T' appears only:
        - in the kits of OFFICE, SERVER_ROOM, RESEARCH_LAB, COMMAND_CENTER, MEDICAL_BAY and
          CONTAINMENT_BLOCK, and the bespoke MED_BAY / EVENT_ROOM / GATE_AIRLOCK dressings;
        - always FLUSH to a wall, except in a server room's ROWS racks;
        - never in a hall, a cave body, the entrance room or a door clearance zone.
      Every ROOMS_MST / LINEAR_CORRIDOR / CAVERN floor carries >= 1 'T' with a standable orthogonal
      neighbour, because the story's LOG_FOUND beats (including the two mandatory reveal logs) fire off
      terminals. If no placed room's kit produced one, the dresser re-dresses one regular room with OFFICE
      (or, with no regular room, adds the OFFICE 'T' row to the first terminal-allowed special).
  R21 FILL CAPS: REGULAR rooms <= LEVEL_GEN_ROOM_FILL_CAP_REGULAR (0.12). SPECIAL rooms <=
      LEVEL_GEN_ROOM_FILL_CAP_SPECIAL (0.22). A DENSE kit (server_room, salvage_bay) <=
      LEVEL_GEN_ROOM_FILL_CAP_DENSE (0.40) with a lane band of half-width 0. A room dressed DENSE is "TIGHT".
      At most LEVEL_GEN_MAX_TIGHT_ROOMS_PER_FLOOR (1) per floor: when a second DENSE special is planned, it
      uses its kit's RELAXED variant (non-dense band and cap).
  R22 The ENTRANCE room holds no solid prop and no decal. (The start must read clean; the player spawns at
      its centre.)
  R23 Regular rooms draw one kit uniformly from OFFICE / STOREROOM / MAINTENANCE / AFTERMATH / BARE, with
      one exception: the floor's LevelGenConfig.preferredRegularKit (set by a route profile; R31) counts
      double.
  R24 A kit's solid props never leave its room. The only solids outside rooms are 3-wide-hall columns
      (R28), cave formations and debris (G3), boss-arena cover/barrels, group barrels (R26) and the runtime
      shop machine (R27).
  R25 Columns placed by ARCHITECTURE (BARE-kit pillars, LINEAR's interior layouts four-pillars / centre-
      avenue / altar, the start room's four framing columns, observatory pylons) are SOLIDs and obey R18.
  R26 GroupBarrelPlacer (balance-bearing, R-HAZARD) additionally rejects tiles in any door clearance zone
      or room lane band, and prefers flush tiles when both exist within its radius. Its count rules,
      Random and flood guards are unchanged.
  R27 The runtime shop machine (World.tryMakeMachineCandidate) rejects any tile inside a door clearance
      zone, on top of its current wall-mount and local-connectivity checks.
  R28 HALLS (corridor tiles outside every room): no solid props, ever. 3-wide halls keep their spaced spine
      columns (placeWideHallwayColumns / placeSpineColumns), now also kept out of door clearance zones.
      Walkable decals in halls only at LEVEL_GEN_HALL_DECAL_CHANCE (0.02) per hall tile, never on a socket
      or its outer tile.
  R29 MEANING IS PINNED. Every placed kit contributes its symbol -> sprite demand to the floor's palette
      request (exactly as a SIGNATURE room's RoomSymbolDemand does today). Every generated floor also
      reserves 'T' -> prop_terminal and 'E' -> prop_explosive_barrel. A kit-placed symbol therefore always
      renders its kit's sprite. Flexible-category slot counts (salvage_bay / supply_cache) are removed;
      their kits name concrete symbols.

PLAYER EXPERIENCE
-----------------
The facility starts to make sense. A medical bay has beds and consoles; an armory has its racks on the back
wall and the dead in front of them; a power plant is a humming block in the middle of a big room. Offices
have a desk against the far wall. Nothing stands in a doorway, and every doorway is a real one: the player
learns that a door means "new room", which is what gives first-person exploration its reveal.

The decisions this creates are spatial, not new mechanics. A room's lane is the fast way through. Its
furniture, all against the walls, is real cover from ranged lanes. Players reason about cover without
fighting it. Special rooms become landmarks the player can name when they talk about a run.

Teaching: none needed and none added. There is no new rule for the player to learn. The existing
DOOR / BARREL / MACHINE teaching topics still fire at their existing moments.

EDGE CASES
----------
- FOOTPRINT RETRIES: the special set is fixed per floor seed (R3). Sizes and positions vary per attempt.
  An attempt that cannot place every planned special is discarded (R5).
- TINY FLOORS: the FootprintPlanner minimum window (24x18) still fits a 15x15 observatory shell. On LINEAR d1
  (target ~350), two specials at window minimum plus a short spine must fit. If footprint precision and
  special count conflict, the precedence is: (1) passability invariants, (2) N >= 2 specials,
  (3) footprint within tolerance. FootprintPlanner keeps the closest build as today. R-DENSITY judges.
- TWO DENSE specials on one floor: the second uses its RELAXED kit (R21).
- A signature that will not fit (an odd shape after sockets): anchor fallback chain (R18). Windows are sized
  so it always fits. A7 checks this.
- A room with one socket: the lane runs socket -> HUB. With a CENTRE feature: socket -> the feature's ring.
- Sockets on adjacent rooms facing each other across a 1-tile corridor: allowed if neither door is
  orthogonally next to another door (R14) and each clearance zone is prop-free.
- Linear side room whose wall IS the spine edge: the socket's outer tile is spine floor. Spine columns
  avoid door clearance zones (R28).
- Cave chamber shell severing the cave: R9 / G3 bypass.
- Connectivity repair firing anyway: it must open a socket, never a raw gap (R30). A8 wants zero repairs.
- Boss floors: no specials (owner exception). The door rule and clearance still apply (G4).
- Bespoke REST / EVENT / GATE floors: zero enemies unchanged. Their one room counts as their special (R8).
- Start room: no route node, no specials beyond itself (G8). The shop machine still seeds there (R27).
- Hand-made levels (World(String)): untouched. They never pass through a generator.
- Save / persistence: no generated grid is ever persisted (floors rebuild from seed), so no migration, no
  schema bump, no "new game" wipe concern.
- Determinism: every new random draw comes from a seeded stream (layout RNG, special salt, dressing salt).
  LevelGeneratorSnapshotTest is re-baselined at CP6 with a dated note, as earlier behaviour changes did.
- app pause / overlays / action lock / turn economy: not applicable (generation runs at floor build only).

ASSUMPTIONS
-----------
  AS1  "Special rooms" = every registered blueprint other than entrance/standard: 13 today, including the
       GENERIC salvage_bay and supply_cache, which the code already counts as special.
  AS2  "Equal chance" = uniform draw without replacement per floor, so each type's probability of being on a
       floor is N/13. No duplicate type on one floor.
  AS3  "Every level must have special rooms" = at least 2 on every ROOMS_MST / LINEAR / CAVERN floor (R2), up
       to 4 on large floors. The bespoke REST / EVENT / GATE floors and the start room ARE one special room
       each. Adding an extra special to the REST clinic would break its "always safe and calm" read.
  AS4  Special room count scales with footprint (2-4), not depth. Depth stays fully out of room choice.
  AS5  Footprint targets, encounters, supply and first contact are balance contracts and stay exactly as
       they are. "Resize rooms" is used for room SIZES (regular rooms 4x4..12x10, special windows), not for
       floor size.
  AS6  "Next to a door" includes diagonals: Chebyshev 1, plus 2 tiles straight out along the door's axis on
       both sides.
  AS7  Door leaves: special rooms always get a door. Other doorways keep the 75% door chance; the rest are
       open 1-tile doorways in a wall, which satisfy "walls on both sides". Doorways wider than one tile no
       longer exist on rooms. Wide halls taper to 1 tile at the socket.
  AS8  The LARGE size modifier is removed. Grand spaces come from big specials and 3-wide halls.
  AS9  LINEAR's local STORAGE_BAY and REACTOR are retired. supply_cache / salvage_bay (STORAGE family) and
       power_plant / atmospheric_plant (MACHINERY family) cover them, and keeping them would mean an unequal,
       unregistered sixth and seventh special source.
  AS10 Cave chambers become proper walled rooms with doorways. An open, wall-less "chamber" cannot have a
       door rule or a recognisable signature.
  AS11 Regular rooms get one of five kits (OFFICE, STOREROOM, MAINTENANCE, AFTERMATH, BARE), uniformly.
       Route profiles that used the deleted scatter flags express their intent as a preferred kit at double
       weight (CACHE -> STOREROOM, ELITE -> AFTERMATH).
  AS12 Terminals are allowed in offices, server rooms, labs, command, medical, containment and the bespoke
       clinic / event / airlock floors. Every combat floor guarantees one, because the story's LOG_FOUND
       beats depend on it.
  AS13 One tight room per floor at most (the owner's "occasional"). Dense kits are server_room and
       salvage_bay. A second dense special on the floor goes RELAXED.
  AS14 Fill caps 0.12 / 0.22 / 0.40, lane band half-width 1 (a 3-wide clear lane in open rooms).
  AS15 The entrance room is left clean (no props, no decals).
  AS16 Kits use only existing symbols and sprites. Several room demands change accordingly (e.g. server_room
       drops 'g'; medical_bay gains '&' and 'I'). symbolDemand() always equals what the room can stamp.
  AS17 The R1 "2D game untouched" ledger from generated-wall-art order 2 is retired on purpose. This idea
       changes the 2D game by design. The LevelFinisher's own stream keeps walls deterministic.
  AS18 The audit is acceptable under the Testing Policy as a measuring instrument (like LadderReport and
       RenderPerformanceProbe). It has no assertions, no Gradle task and no CI hook.
  AS19 The new agent runs on opus, as the old level agent did, because generator design is judgement-heavy.
       The orchestrator may still escalate or downgrade per its model-tiering rule.
  AS20 Kit rows, counts and anchors in TECHNICAL NOTES are decided defaults. Tuning a kit row later is
       short-lane work (one register row, no balance file).
  AS21 Halls get no solid props except the existing 3-wide-hall columns. Walkable decals at 2% per hall
       tile.
  AS22 The research lab's alcove / field-wall architecture and the observatory rotunda are kept as
