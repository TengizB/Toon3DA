OVERVIEW
--------
Replaces the five UNDEAD textures - Ghoul, Crawler, Revenant, Vortex Eye, Blight Corruptor. In the
premise the undead are the facility's own staff, killed by the catastrophe and the Deepworks'
conditions and dragged back into motion by raw soul bleeding through the galleries: victims, not
monsters. They are people, so this order gives the kit a HUMANOID RIG and cloth, and introduces the
family's new glow ink, SOUL.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - Kit growth:
      * humanoid(pose) helper: builds head, neck, torso, pelvis, two arms with hands, two legs with
        feet as kit shapes from a small dict of joint positions - one rig, five poses, so the five
        read as the same species of victim.
      * cloth materials: Organization work overalls (desaturated slate), hazmat rubber (dull olive-grey,
        NOT green - G5), lab white (reuse livery), security armour (reuse gunmetal / darkmetal).
      * dead-skin ramp (ash grey with a cold undertone) and a cloth-fold helper (carved fold lines).
      * the SOUL glow ramp: pale bone-white with a faint cold tint, saturation capped low so it never
        reads as purple "special" (G5).
  - Five briefs -> five functions -> generated/ghoul.png, crawler.png, revenant.png, vortex_eye.png,
    blight_corruptor.png. Owner gate (G11), five registry rows.
  - Regenerate every earlier sprite byte-identical. Docs.

OUT
  - Faces with expressions; gore; visible wounds as the main read (restraint - premise tone).
  - Any change to SUMMON (the Corruptor's raised dead use the same types and therefore the new sprites).
  - Animation; renaming.

VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
DESIGN BRIEF - GHOUL (CHAFF, 0.90 walls, melee shambler)
  A labourer in torn work overalls, head lolled to one side, one arm reaching, the other hanging; a
  lanyard ID badge swinging on the chest. Soul light in the eye sockets and the slack mouth. Upright,
  thin, patient.

DESIGN BRIEF - CRAWLER (CHAFF, 0.55 walls, fast scuttler)
  A lab technician on all fours, spine arched high, head thrust forward, arms long and splayed - a
  low, fast, wrong silhouette. Lab-white coat torn at the hem and dragging. Full anatomy (L6): it crawls
  because the soul moves it that way, not because pieces are missing.

DESIGN BRIEF - REVENANT (BRUISER, 1.05 walls, fast heavy melee)
  Organization security in riot armour - broad pauldrons, a cracked visor with soul light behind it,
  a shock baton gripped low - caught mid-lunge, weight on the front foot. The armour stencil (a unit
  number) is the detail. The heaviest, most dangerous-looking humanoid.

DESIGN BRIEF - VORTEX EYE (CHAFF, 0.55 walls, short-range caster, DEBUFF_PLAYER)
  What is left of a researcher's head and shoulders, held aloft by a slow vortex of soul light that
  pours from the eye sockets and coils around it. Hover baked into the canvas (the renderer does not
  hover this type). Small, eerie, the one undead that is MOSTLY light - and the short reach of the
  vortex should read as short.

DESIGN BRIEF - BLIGHT CORRUPTOR (SOLDIER, 1.00 walls, durable carrier, SUMMON)
  A quarantine worker in a full hazmat suit, swollen from inside by spore growth that has burst through
  the suit in soft pods (soul-lit), a decontamination sprayer tank fused to its back. Thick and slow;
  the pods are where the raised dead come from.
