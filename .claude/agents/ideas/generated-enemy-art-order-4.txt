registry row - and sets the template every later family order follows.

VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
BRIEF - IRON STALKER (INSECT, MINI_ELITE, 1.10 walls, melee, BUFF_SELF + AREA_STRIKE)
  What it is: a harvest-line insect the Organization tried to use as a guard animal - bred from the
  planet's tissue, then FITTED with riveted iron plates to survive the pens. It outgrew them; chitin has
  grown around and over their edges.
  Silhouette: TALL and upright, unlike the low, wide tick - reared on four rear legs, the front pair raised
  as two long scythe blades. Narrow waist, plated thorax, small armoured head, ichor eyes set low.
  Materials: chitin / chitin_dk, gunmetal + darkmetal riveted plates, bone-pale blade edges, ichor eyes,
  a glowing ichor seam where chitin split around a plate.
  Story detail: a stencilled pen number on the largest plate; a snapped restraint collar on the neck.
MOTION
  IDLE: weight shifts between rear legs, blades twitch and re-cross, head tilts as if listening.
  ALERT: blades snap open wide. MOVE: a stalking high-stepping gait, body level, blades held.
  WIND_UP (AREA_STRIKE): both blades drawn far back, body coiled. ATTACK: single blade stab.
  AREA_STRIKE: a two-blade sweep with smear frames. BUFF_SELF: plates lift and lock, seam flares.
  HURT: plates ring, body sways back; HEAVY: a plate tears half off. DEFEND: blades crossed in front.
  WOUNDED: a blade drags, a plate hangs loose, ichor drips at the seam.
  DEATH: blades drop first, legs buckle, it folds onto its plates.
