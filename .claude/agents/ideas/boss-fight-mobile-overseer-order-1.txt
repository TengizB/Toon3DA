=============================================================================
THE FANTASY — WHY THIS FIGHT
=============================================================================
The current Overseer is bolted to the floor: it fires row/plus laser lanes and
never moves (see OverseerPhase1Pattern header). That is a fine "dodge the lanes"
puzzle, but it is a STATIC fight — the player parks behind a 'P' column and trades
shells. The user's brief asks for the opposite: a boss that is ALIVE in the room.

New fantasy: the Overseer is a security-core drone that has torn free of its
mount and now HUNTS. It closes distance, cracks you with a melee slam, then
DASHES several tiles away before you can retaliate — a real, visible sprint
across the arena, not a teleport. From range it seeds the floor with fire and
toxic clouds to deny you ground, and it vomits up minion adds (Corruptor-style)
to pin you while it repositions. Once, when badly hurt, it breaks off entirely,
retreats to a corner, and REPAIRS itself over several turns — daring you to chase
it down and interrupt the weld before it claws its health back.

The player emotion we want: "It won't hold still. I have to READ where it's going,
cut off its escape, and burn my window when it finally plants its feet." A cat-
and-mouse duel across an open arena — the exact opposite of the turret it is today.

Design lineage: Doom RPG floor bosses (telegraphed slams), Hades (bosses as a
rhythm of telegraph → reposition → punish window), DCSS uniques (readable, on-
your-turn threats; you can always see the kill coming), Doom Eternal Marauder /
Archvile (a mobile threat that summons and zones and must be denied space).

=============================================================================
THE CORE LOOP (turn-based, unchanged tick model)
=============================================================================
Everything still runs on the existing one-tick-per-player-action model. Each
completed player action (move / strafe / rotate / fire / reload / wait) fires ONE
tick via TickEventBus; BossFloorController runs the Overseer's single action that
tick, BEFORE normal enemies (it is subscribed first). NO real-time anything. The
"mobile" feel comes entirely from (a) multi-tile committed moves that are
ANIMATED across several frames of one tick (Order 2) and (b) an AI brain that
sequences move+attack+flee into readable tactics (Order 4).

One full cycle the player experiences:
  1. STALK   — Overseer paths toward the player over 1–2 turns (visible slide).
  2. STRIKE  — telegraphed charge or melee slam lands (one turn to dodge).
  3. FLEE    — Overseer DASHES several tiles away (visible sprint), often
               dropping a hazard on the tile it vacated so you can't just follow.
  4. PRESSURE— from range it summons adds and/or seeds a fire/toxic lane, forcing
               you to deal with the room while it lines up its next strike.
  5. (once) REPAIR — below a HP threshold it flees to safety and heals over 5
               turns unless you reach it and draw blood, which cancels the heal.

The player's counter-loop: predict the dash destination, use arena geometry to
cut off escape lanes, clear adds efficiently (Order 6 guarantees you never run
dry), and save a burst window for when the boss commits to a STRIKE or a REPAIR
(both plant it in place). This is the whole game of the fight.

=============================================================================
FAIRNESS CONTRACT (mandatory — every later order must honour this)
=============================================================================
A mobile boss is trivially unfair if it can move every turn (the player can never
land a hit) or teleport (the player can never predict it). These rules keep it
fair. They are non-negotiable; cite them in code comments.

  F1. NEVER MOVES TWO TURNS IN A ROW WITHOUT A PLANT.  The boss must spend a
      "planted" turn (STRIKE wind-up, RESOLVE, SUMMON, hazard cast, or HOLD) at
      least every BOSS_MAX_CONSECUTIVE_MOVE_TURNS. It cannot kite forever. There
      is ALWAYS a punish window arriving soon — this is what makes it beatable.

  F2. EVERY DAMAGING ATTACK IS TELEGRAPHED ONE FULL TURN AHEAD.  Reuse the
      DangerTileSet TELEGRAPH → RESOLVE rhythm already in BossMove. The player
      gets exactly one turn to react to any hit. No same-tick "gotcha" damage.

  F3. MOVEMENT IS ALWAYS VISIBLE.  A relocation of N tiles animates as a real
      slide/sprint across those tiles within its tick (Order 2). The boss NEVER
      pops from tile A to tile B. If a dash would be too fast to read, it is too
      long — cap the per-turn dash distance so the eye can follow it.

  F4. THE HEAL IS INTERRUPTIBLE AND ONCE-PER-FIGHT.  See Order 5. The repair is a
      player-facing objective ("go stop it"), not an unbeatable reset. Any damage
      cancels the current regen turn-chain and the ability is spent for the fight.

  F5. THE ARENA IS NEVER A DEATH BOX.  Adds must never fully surround the player
      with no escape, hazards must always leave a walkable path, and the player
      can never be locked out of ammo (Order 6) inside the sealed room.

  F6. READABILITY OVER SURPRISE.  Distinct telegraph colour per ability (Order 8).
      The player loses to being out-thought, never to being unable to see the
      threat.

WHAT EACH ORDER DELIVERS (dependency map)
=============================================================================
ORDER 2 (Locomotion) — the foundation. Adds a visible interpolated move to the
  boss (render-position lerp over the tick) and a multi-tile BossMove.DASH that
  path-steps through free tiles. Implements F1 (consecutive-move cap) and F3
  (visible movement). Without this, everything else looks like teleporting.

ORDER 3 (Ability kit) — the verbs. New BossMove kinds / pattern helpers for
  CHARGE, SUMMON_ADDS (Corruptor-style, reuses existing SUMMON plumbing),
  SPAWN_FIRE and SPAWN_TOXIC (reuse HazardManager.igniteFire / igniteToxic /
  spawnToxicCloud), and MELEE. Each with its own telegraph. Data only — the brain
  that chooses between them is Order 4.

ORDER 4 (AI brain) — the intelligence. A HunterKillerPattern (implements
  BossAttackPattern) that SEQUENCES the Order-3 verbs into named tactics
  (Ambush / Zoner / Attrition / …), one tactic per phase pool, honouring the
  fairness cadence. This is where "combine abilities for different strategies"
  and "be creative about boss AI" live.

ORDER 5 (Heal) — the once-per-fight repair state machine.

ORDER 6 (Ammo lifeline) — adds spawned by SUMMON drop ammo 100% when the player's
  matching reserve is empty, so the sealed arena is never unwinnable.

ORDER 7 (Arena) — replaces the fixed corridor+box BossArenaGenerator with a
  spacious, interesting procedural arena built for hit-and-run: open lanes,
  asymmetric cover, hazard-friendly zones, heal alcoves, guaranteed reachability.

ORDER 8 (Presentation) — sprite/texture confirmation, phase tint, per-ability
  telegraph colours, dash afterimage/dust so movement reads, HUD states
  (REPAIRING / ENRAGED), intro & defeat banners, event-text hooks.

BALANCE FRAME (placeholders — real numbers per order, tune in playtest)
=============================================================================
- Overseer HP today = EnemyConstants.OVERSEER_MAX_HP (250), depth-scaled. Keep
  order-of-magnitude; the mobility already lengthens TTK because punish windows
  are rationed. Re-tune only after Order 4 lands (the brain sets real pacing).
- Read the balance contract (docs/balance-rule-system.txt) and
  .claude/agents/ideas/balance_order_6_boss_balance_ruleset.txt before setting any
  damage/HP number in Orders 3–5. Slot every new ability into that contract's
  eHP / DPT / TTK / TP framing; do not invent free-floating numbers.
- The mobile boss's DANGER must come from POSITIONING pressure (fire/toxic/adds
  denying ground) more than raw per-hit damage. A single melee/charge hit should
  hurt (~quarter HP at most, telegraphed) but not one-shot; the fight is lost by
  slow attrition and bad positioning, not by one unreadable spike.
