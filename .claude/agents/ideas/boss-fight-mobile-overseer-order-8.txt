MOVEMENT FEEDBACK — sell the dash (F3, the "not a teleport" polish)
=============================================================================
Order 2 gives a smooth interpolated slide; this order adds the JUICE that makes it
read as a deliberate sprint, not a glide:
  - AFTERIMAGE / MOTION TRAIL: draw 2–3 fading ghost billboards along the slide
    path behind the boss during a DASH/CHARGE (reuse EnemyRenderer's billboard
    draw at the path's trailing tiles, decreasing alpha). Pre-allocated; drawn only
    while isSliding().
  - DUST / SCUFF at the start and end tiles (reuse ImpactEffectSystem particle
    pool if it fits) so departure and arrival have weight.
  - Optional slight sprite lean/squash in the travel direction (scale hint).
  - The minimap (LevelRenderer) already tracks the boss by tile; it will update on
    arrival — fine. Optionally interpolate the minimap dot along renderColumn/Row
    for consistency.
These are what stop the multi-tile move from feeling like a pop even at 0.30s.
