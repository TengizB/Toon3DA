OVERVIEW
--------
The walls are the one thing on screen every frame, so they make the atmosphere - and they are the
weakest art in the game. They are a mix of image-model JPEGs (lab_wall_*.jpg) and flat Java Pixmap
programs (rectangles, stripes, rivets), with no shared light, no shared palette and no sense of place:
the same "conduit", "hazard stripe" and "holo data" wall can appear on floor 1 and floor 24. Meanwhile
the enemies are being rebuilt as sculpted, lit, generated sprites (generated-enemy-art orders 1-5), and
the walls behind them now look like they come from a different, older game.

The need is not "new wall textures". It is that the WORLD SHOULD TELL THE DESCENT. The story is a
descent through five strata of a living planet (story/05-descent-structure.md): a company town, an
abattoir, the resurrection heart, the wound where the facility gives way to flesh, and the mind at the
core. Today one screenshot cannot tell you where you are. After this series it must.

This series REBUILDS THE WALL VISUAL LAYER from scratch:
  - every wall is redesigned from the story and generated with the enemy-art kit (no old wall is
    reference material, none survives);
  - walls are organised as five REGION STRATA, each a coherent kit of WALL ROLES (base, structure,
    light, service, marker, feature, breach, and five room roles), instead of one global grab-bag;
  - the level "wall randomizer" (SymbolAllocator's seeded pick + the generators' random reskins) is
    replaced by a STRATUM-AWARE ALLOCATOR and a rule-driven WALL DRESSING PASS: architecture rhythm,
    lights, door frames, features where a room faces you, and the planet breaking through more the
    deeper you go - decided by rules, never by a random grab;
  - a new wall runtime: one atlas per floor, several BASE VARIANTS per tile (no wallpaper repetition),
    an emissive GLOW layer exempt from the mood grade (like the enemy glow), a few ANIMATED walls, and
    distance LOD so detailed pixel art does not shimmer.


THE CONCEPT - "THE STRATA"
--------------------------
One region = one stratum = one material story. Moods (art-direction section 3) already colour the
regions; the strata give them a SURFACE. The descent reads as the facility being progressively
consumed: Organization hardware dominates at the top, the planet's body at the bottom, and the BREACH
role is how the bottom leaks upward.

  stratum  route region     story region            surface                                          mood
  -------  ---------------  ----------------------  -----------------------------------------------  -----------------
  A        OUTER FACILITY   1 Habitation Rings      painted Organization panels (livery), rubber      cool_steel
                                                    bump rails, riveted kick plates, cable trays.
                                                    An evacuated company town: ordered, clean, wrong.
  B        RESEARCH WING    2 Harvesting Galleries  glazed abattoir tile to shoulder height, hook      clinical_white
                                                    rails, form-tied concrete, steel drains, stains.
                                                    Something was grown and cut here at scale.
  C        REACTOR DEPTHS   3 The Reliquary         heavy riveted steel, cradle print bays with the   amber_hot
                                                    rendered SOUL light, pattern-archive racks, fuel
                                                    lines. The resurrection heart - where YOU come from.
  D        THE BREACH       4 The Wound             the facility torn open: plating peeled back,      corrupted_violet
                                                    muscle and glands pushing through, ichor veins,
                                                    dead drill bits buried in living tissue.
  E        THE CORE         5 The Core              no facility left: a nacre-bone lattice like a     core_white_violet
                                                    neural mesh, soul light in the hollows, synapse
                                                    nodes pulsing. The mind the Cradles were burning.

The five prototypes that define the look (approved or not - see DECISIONS D1) are the rest-state
textures of these five strata: ring panel (A base), gallery tile (B base), cradle bay (C feature),
breach (D base), lattice (E base). Their source is .claude/skills/enemy-art/walls_prototype.py.

What the in-engine prototype captures already proved (2026-10-22, run-game, every wall swapped to one
texture per region, under the region's mood):
  - the kit's style reads at wall scale and under every mood; the Wound and the Reliquary are the
    strongest; the soul light and ichor read through the grade;
  - a FEATURE used as every tile (cradle bays, the drill) becomes wallpaper: features must be rare and
    placed by rule (the dressing pass), and the base needs several variants (the runtime);
  - the old floor/ceiling materials (flat grids, tread plates) now clash with the walls (order 9);
  - at distance the detailed texture aliases; LOD is part of the runtime (order 3).


SERIES RULES (every order, by reference)
----------------------------------------
  W1  ORIGINALITY. No existing wall/door/column texture or generator is reference material - never
      opened for inspiration, traced, recoloured or re-created (the enemy series' G1, applied to the
      environment). Designs come from story/00-premise.md, 03-the-planet.md, 05-descent-structure.md,
      the region's mood and the room's purpose.
  W2  GENERATED IN CODE with the enemy-art kit (guide N1). A wall is a script; the game ships only the
      PNG atlases + JSON manifests it writes. Deterministic, byte-identical regeneration, no hand edits.
      Enemy sprites regenerate byte-identical after every kit change (new materials are APPENDED).
  W3  ONE STYLE LOCK. Same LIGHT / RIM / wrap / cavity / cast occlusion / ramps / cleanup / outline as
      the enemies. A wall never gets its own light rig. New environment materials are added to
      kit.MATERIALS (appended), never tinted per wall.
  W4  TILEABLE. A wall texture is one TILE FACE, 256x256 (row 0 = ceiling line, row 255 = floor line),
      and tiles HORIZONTALLY with itself AND with every other wall of its stratum (shared trim heights:
      the kick plate, rail and cornice lines meet across tiles). Built on a 3-tile canvas and cropped
      (the prototype's method). It never needs to tile vertically.
  W5  SEMANTIC COLOURS SACRED (art-direction V5, guide N4). No saturated red/green/blue/purple/yellow
      decoration; SUIT_AMBER never on a wall. Facility light = cyan; planet tissue = ichor; harvested
      soul = the new pale SOUL ink; reactor heat = magma. Hazard-stripe yellow is retired from walls.
  W6  READABILITY BEATS MOOD (V8). Walls are the backdrop: mid-value, low contrast at range, so every
      enemy, pickup, intent icon and door reads in front of them. A wall may never be brighter than
      the enemies standing before it; emissives stay small (lamps, seams, cores - guide lesson 12.8).
  W7  THE 2D GAME IS UNTOUCHED. No tile symbol is added or removed, no wall becomes walkable or vice
      versa, no rule, turn, balance number, spawn, supply slot or persistence key changes. The dressing
      pass only ever swaps one WALL symbol for another WALL symbol on a tile that was already a wall.
  W8  STORY ON THE WALL. Every stratum carries story details - Organization stencils and serials,
      quota boards, a cradle print, a buried drill - as small things a player notices, never text the
      player must read.
  W9  OWNER APPROVAL GATE (guide N7). Each stratum kit: contact sheet (every role, every variant, the
      tiled strip) + in-engine captures under its mood, then STOP until the owner answers; the answer
      goes into that order's DECISIONS. An agent's opinion is never the gate.
  W10 NO TESTS (Testing Policy). Not balance-bearing. Existing tileset / level tests that encode the
      old catalog are UPDATED in the same commit when behaviour legitimately changes - never deleted,
      disabled or weakened. Verified by build + run-game captures.
  W11 PERFORMANCE IS PART OF DONE. No allocation in render(); one wall atlas bind per floor; the base
      pass keeps its opaque/unblended + equal-column merge (render-performance-full-hd R16/R18);
      measured with run-game --probe against the order before it.
  W12 ALWAYS BUILDING, ALWAYS PLAYABLE. Until order 10 the game runs mixed: strata whose kit has
      shipped draw the new art, the rest draw placeholder rows that point at the old sprites.


THE WALL ROLE MODEL (decided here; order 2 builds it)
----------------------------------------------------
A level symbol no longer names a picture. It names a ROLE; the floor's STRATUM decides what that role
looks like. Every stratum supplies every role (or names a fallback role for it - data, never a switch).

  role         what it is                                                      typical share of a floor's exposed wall
  -----------  --------------------------------------------------------------  ---------------------------------------
  BASE         the ordinary wall. Ships 3 VARIANTS that tile with each other;  ~70-80%
               the renderer picks one per tile face (order 3), so a long
               corridor never repeats one picture.
  STRUCTURE    pilaster / rib / frame: the rhythm of the architecture.         every 4th tile of a long run + door frames
  LIGHT        the stratum's light source (lamp, lit seam, gland, synapse);    roughly one per 7 tiles of run
               emissive; may animate (flicker, pulse).
  SERVICE      pipes, vents, conduit, fuel lines - the building working.        scattered, 5-10%
  MARKER       signage / Organization stencil / quota board / region serial.   1-3 per floor
  FEATURE      the stratum's signature (A notice window, B hook rail bay,       0-2 per room, facing the room
               C cradle print bay, D buried drill, E synapse node). Rare by rule.
  BREACH       the planet coming through: A hairline seep, B chitin growth,    0% on a region's first floor, rising
               C soul leak, D open wound (D's base is already breach), E none. toward the region's last floor
  CLINICAL     room role: medical / heal rooms                                 signature rooms only
  CONTAINMENT  room role: cryo / bio / specimen / quarantine                   signature rooms only
  MACHINERY    room role: power / reactor / generator                          signature rooms only
  SECURITY     room role: armory / blast / command                             signature rooms only
  DATA         room role: server / research / terminal                         signature rooms only

THE SYMBOL -> ROLE TABLE (data in TilesetConstants; order 2). Existing symbols keep being WALLS; only
the meaning of their PICTURE changes. Default table (order 2 may adjust with a DECISIONS note):
  x BASE | k STRUCTURE | S LIGHT | c v w SERVICE | h MARKER | F N FEATURE | G j BREACH |
  M CLINICAL | Z Q CONTAINMENT | U MACHINERY | X SECURITY | t D DATA |
  " ' `  (the stellar observatory) -> STRUCTURE / FEATURE / BASE of the floor's stratum - the
  observatory keeps its room shape and props, but its walls belong to the stratum it is in.

THE WALL DRESSING PASS (decided here; order 2 builds it) - how a level's walls are arranged
  D1  Runs after every generator (dungeon, corridor, cavern, boss arena, med bay, event, gate, start
      room) except hand-made .txt levels; seeded from the floor seed; pure grid -> grid.
  D2  Touches only EXPOSED BASE wall tiles (an 'x' with a walkable 4-neighbour) and only ever writes
      another wall symbol (W7). Tiles that a signature room stamped keep their room role.
  D3  RHYTHM: along a straight exposed run of >= 5 tiles, every 4th tile (phase seeded per run)
      becomes STRUCTURE.
  D4  THRESHOLDS: the wall tiles flanking a door or a room entrance become STRUCTURE (door frames).
  D5  LIGHT: no exposed run of >= 7 tiles is left without a LIGHT; never two LIGHTs adjacent; a LIGHT
      prefers the middle of a run.
  D6  FEATURE: at most 2 per room, on the wall facing the room's long axis, never in a corridor.
  D7  MARKER: 1-3 per floor, near the arrival point and the exit.
  D8  SERVICE: seeded scatter on the remaining base, 5-10%, clustered in 2-3 tile runs.
  D9  BREACH: density = stratum.breachBase + floorIndexInRegion x stratum.breachStep; grown as seeded
      clusters (not salt), never on a threshold. Deeper floors of a region visibly rot toward the next.
  D10 Rates and probabilities live in LevelGenConstants (WALL_DRESSING_*), never inline.

must match. Retirement is last so the game is never broken.

ASSUMPTIONS (series-wide)
-------------------------
  AS1  Stratum = route region (RegionPlan.defaultPlan(): A..E), keyed by RegionAmbience like the mood.
       Story region names (Habitation Rings ...) are the design names; code ids follow the route themeIds.
  AS2  256x256 per tile face (enemy texel density: ~240 px per wall height), opaque, Nearest filter.
  AS3  Static PNG atlases are shipped (as the enemy art is). This overrides visual-identity V1 ("no new
       image assets") the same way the enemy series did: the PNGs are generator OUTPUT, never authored.
  AS4  Same LIGHT direction as enemies (upper-left-front). A directional light on a periodic height
       field shades periodically, so tiling is unaffected; Doom-era wall textures used the same convention.
  AS5  The wall glow layer reuses the enemy idea: emissive pixels copied to a glow atlas, drawn over
       the graded base with NORMAL blend, exempt from the grade (art-direction V6 gains "wall emissives").
  AS6  Animation = a short frame cycle of the GLOW layer only (2-8 frames); the base stays one frame,
       so animation costs one extra textured column, never a second base pass.
  AS7  Hand-made levels (assets/levels/*.txt) render with stratum A and are not dressed (D1).
  AS8  The 2D grid is the authority; the dressing pass is cosmetic and seeded, so the same seed gives
       the same walls (fairness, seed sharing).
  AS9  The enemy-art skill folder keeps its name; walls.py lives beside bestiary.py (guide 13 "OTHER
       CONTENT"). A rename to an "art" skill is out of scope.
