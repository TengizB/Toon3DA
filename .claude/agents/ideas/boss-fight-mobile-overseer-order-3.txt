=============================================================================
SCOPE
=============================================================================
Add the Overseer's action VERBS as data — each a BossMove kind (+ telegraph)
resolvable by BossFloorController. This order does NOT decide WHEN the boss uses
them (that is Order 4's brain). It just makes each verb exist, be telegraphed one
turn ahead (Fairness F2), and resolve correctly. The brief's required verbs:

  1. CHARGE          — lunge several tiles in a straight cardinal line, hit along it
  2. SUMMON_ADDS     — spawn minions (Corruptor-style)  [reuses existing SUMMON]
  3. SPAWN_FIRE      — seed a fire lane/patch            [HazardManager.igniteFire]
  4. SPAWN_TOXIC     — seed a toxic cloud                [HazardManager.spawnToxicCloud]
  5. MELEE           — adjacent slam                     [already in BossMove]

Every damaging verb uses the TELEGRAPH → RESOLVE rhythm already in BossMove /
DangerTileSet: the boss commits on turn T (marks danger tiles / crackles), damage
lands on turn T+1. The player gets exactly one turn to react.

=============================================================================
VERB 1 — CHARGE (mobile line strike; the signature "hit then flee" opener)
=============================================================================
The boss picks a cardinal direction toward the player, telegraphs the corridor,
then next turn LUNGES up to N tiles along it (a fast slide via Order 2), damaging
the player if they are on any traversed tile, and ending adjacent-or-past them.
Reuses the Shell Brute "charger" feel the game already teaches (EnemyType
SHELL_BRUTE tacticalVerb: "sidestep its charge, then punish the recovery").

BossMove: new kind CHARGE with a direction + range payload, OR model as:
  - Turn T:  TELEGRAPH — arm DangerTileSet with the line of tiles from the boss
             toward the player's current tile, up to BOSS_CHARGE_RANGE_TILES or
             the first wall/'P' column (stop at solid). Damage =
             BOSS_CHARGE_DAMAGE. Boss faces the charge axis (visual).
  - Turn T+1: RESOLVE + move — apply DangerTileSet damage if the player is on a
             marked tile, THEN slide the boss along the (now cleared) corridor to
             the last passable tile via beginSlide (Order 2). The lunge is the
             flee-enabling reposition: it ends the boss on the far side, set up to
             DASH away next. After a charge, force a 1-turn RECOVERY plant (the
             player's punish window — Fairness F1/F2).

CONSTANTS (EnemyConstants.java — reuse/extend existing OVERSEER_CHARGE_DAMAGE=30,
OVERSEER_RAM_COOLDOWN=3 which already exist):
  OVERSEER_CHARGE_RANGE_TILES = 5
  OVERSEER_CHARGE_RECOVERY_TURNS = 1
Escape: step OFF the charge line (one perpendicular tile). Telegraph colour: red
corridor (Order 8).

=============================================================================
VERB 2 — SUMMON_ADDS (Corruptor-style; pins the player while the boss kites)
=============================================================================
Already 90% built. BossMove.summon(EnemyType, count) exists and
BossFloorController.summonMinions() spawns into free cardinal-adjacent tiles,
capped by EnemyConstants.CORRUPTOR_MINION_CAP against countLiveEnemies(). Extend:

  - WHICH adds: spawn a small mixed pack of EXISTING chaff so it reads as the
    "Corruptor carrier" fantasy. RECOMMEND CRAWLER (fast pressure) + GHOUL
    (durable body-block), 1–2 at a time. Do NOT invent new enemy types here.
  - TELEGRAPH it: today SUMMON resolves instantly. For fairness, add a one-turn
    tell (boss "vents spores", adjacent spawn tiles flash) then spawn next turn,
    OR keep instant but cap to 1 add per summon on a slow cadence (Order 4 sets
    cadence). RECOMMEND telegraphed spawn tiles for readability (F2/F6).
  - CAP: keep a hard live-adds ceiling (reuse CORRUPTOR_MINION_CAP; expose as
    OVERSEER_ADDS_CAP) so the room never floods (Fairness F5).
  - Adds obey the same-cardinal-line ranged rule and all normal enemy AI — no
    special-casing (they are just EnemyManager enemies).
  - Order 6 makes these adds drop ammo 100% when the player is empty. Nothing to
    do here except spawn them; Order 6 hooks the drop.

CONSTANTS:
  OVERSEER_ADDS_CAP = 4                 // max live adds at once
  OVERSEER_SUMMON_COUNT = 2            // adds per summon action
  OVERSEER_SUMMON_TYPES = {CRAWLER, GHOUL}  // pick per summon (round-robin/random)

=============================================================================
VERB 3 — SPAWN_FIRE (zone denial; deny the ground you're standing on)
=============================================================================
HazardManager already exposes igniteFire(tileColumn, tileRow). The boss casts a
short FIRE LANE or PATCH to push the player off a position — classic "you can't
stand there" pressure, and a natural thing to drop on the tile it just vacated
when fleeing.

  - Turn T: TELEGRAPH — mark the target fire tiles (DangerTileSet, amber/orange).
    Target = either (a) a 3-tile line between boss and player, or (b) the player's
    current tile + orthogonal neighbours (a small patch). Choose per brain (Order
    4); the verb supports a "target tile + shape" payload.
  - Turn T+1: RESOLVE — for each marked tile call HazardManager.igniteFire(col,
    row). The hazard system then owns the burning DOT/lifetime (do NOT re-implement
    fire damage; HazardManager.tick handles it). The DangerTileSet resolve here is
    just "place the hazard", not a direct hit — the fire persists and chips anyone
    standing in it, per the existing hazard rules.
  - COMBO: dropping fire on the tile the boss flees FROM (Order 4 "Ambush" tactic)
    means the player can't simply chase into melee — they must path around.

New BossMove kind SPAWN_FIRE (payload: target tile + shape id), or a generic
CAST_HAZARD move carrying {hazardKind: FIRE|TOXIC, shape, targetCol, targetRow}.
RECOMMEND ONE generic hazard move (CAST_HAZARD) to cover Verbs 3 & 4 — less
BossMove surface area. BossFloorController resolves it by calling the matching
HazardManager entry point per tile.

CONSTANTS:
  OVERSEER_FIRE_LANE_LENGTH = 3
  (fire DOT/lifetime come from existing HazardManager/hazard constants — reuse.)

=============================================================================
VERB 4 — SPAWN_TOXIC (persistent area denial; slower, wider than fire)
=============================================================================
HazardManager exposes igniteToxic(col,row) and spawnToxicCloud(centerColumn,
centerRow, radius). Toxic is the WIDER, longer-linger zone — used to wall off a
region of the arena or cover the boss's retreat / heal spot.

  - Turn T: TELEGRAPH the cloud footprint (DangerTileSet, sickly green), centred
    on a chosen tile (often behind the boss as it flees, or on the player's tile).
  - Turn T+1: RESOLVE — call HazardManager.spawnToxicCloud(centerCol, centerRow,
    OVERSEER_TOXIC_RADIUS). Hazard system owns the linger + DOT.
  - COMBO with Order 5 heal: the boss can lay toxic between itself and the player
    to buy time while it repairs (the player must path around it to interrupt).
    Fairness F5: the cloud must NEVER fully seal the boss off — always leave a
    walkable approach (arena design in Order 7 guarantees room; the cast picks a
    radius that can't wall a corridor).

CONSTANTS:
  OVERSEER_TOXIC_RADIUS = 1            // 3x3 cloud (centre + ring); keep small
                                       // enough to leave a path (F5).
  (toxic DOT/linger from existing hazard constants — reuse.)

=============================================================================
VERB 5 — MELEE (the up-close punish; already exists)
=============================================================================
BossMove.melee(damage) already resolves: if the boss is Manhattan-adjacent to the
player, apply damage. Keep as-is for the "hit manually, then flee" opener the
brief calls for: STALK into adjacency → MELEE (plant) → DASH away (Order 2). Give
it a one-turn wind (reuse WINDING_UP / a short telegraph) so it obeys F2, OR keep
it as the fast reactive poke and rely on the charge for the telegraphed big hit.
RECOMMEND: melee is a quick adjacent slam telegraphed by the boss rearing for one
turn (EnemyState.WINDING_UP already exists for chargers) — modest damage
(OVERSEER_MELEE_DAMAGE ~ 14), lower than CHARGE, because it is harder to avoid.

CONSTANTS:
  OVERSEER_MELEE_DAMAGE = 14

=============================================================================
FAIRNESS / BALANCE
=============================================================================
- Every damaging verb (CHARGE, MELEE, and the initial placement of FIRE/TOXIC) is
  telegraphed one turn ahead. Standing in a resolved hazard chips you — that is
  your fault for not moving, and the hazard is visible.
- No single verb one-shots. Rough hit budget at full player HP (100): CHARGE ~30,
  MELEE ~14, fire/toxic tick per turn ~ small. The player dies from EATING several
  because they mispositioned, never from one unreadable hit.
- Slot every damage/range number into docs/game-balance-authority.txt and
  balance_order_6_boss_balance_ruleset.txt (eHP/DPT/TTK/TP) before finalising.
- Hazards must always leave a walkable path (F5). The cast targeting must refuse
  to seal a 1-wide corridor; Order 7's spacious arena makes this easy.
