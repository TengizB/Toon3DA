OVERVIEW
========
CREDITS are the marine's in-run spendable currency: UAC corporate scrip skimmed off
dead demons and scattered as glowing chips across the facility. They are earned from
enemy kills and from three tiers of pickable credit chips seeded on levels (small /
medium / large), and they accumulate into a single running total shown in the
inventory menu. Credits are the WALLET that future in-run sinks (the merchant room,
roguelike_order_11) will draw from. This doc owns ONLY the currency itself: the
total, how it is earned, the three pickup objects, their procedural visuals, and the
inventory readout. It deliberately does NOT design a shop — that is order 11's job.

CRITICAL DISTINCTION (do not blur these): Credits are IN-RUN and reset to zero on
death/new run. They are NOT the SALVAGE meta-currency (salvage-cache-meta-progression
.txt), which is PERMANENT and banked between runs via Preferences. Credits live on
PlayerProgress/RunStats (in-memory, per-run); Salvage lives on StatsStore
(Preferences). Keep the two visually and verbally separate — credit CHIP (cyan/gold)
vs. salvage SCRAP-CAN. Never persist Credits to Preferences.

NO NEW TILE SYMBOL REQUIRED FOR THE PREFERRED PATH. Credit chips are best implemented
as entity-side GroundItems (item/GroundItem.java), exactly like weapon ground sprites
(weapon-ground-sprites.txt) — keyed by ItemType, not by a map character, so no tile
char is consumed and the procedural-billboard + bob pipeline is reused wholesale.
A FALLBACK level-symbol path is described in TECHNICAL NOTES for hand-authored levels;
it would require new symbols 'i' (small), 'o' (medium), 'q' (large) — currently FREE
per docs/tile-symbols.txt (lowercase i o q z free). If that path is taken: requires
new symbols — add to docs/tile-symbols.txt and Level.java before implementing.


GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
EARNING FROM KILLS:
1. Player kills an enemy (any weapon, barrel, or hazard). EnemyManager already fires a
   kill event (KillEventListener / XP award path).
2. On that kill event, the credit system reads the dead EnemyType's credit value,
   scales it by dungeon depth, and adds it to the run Credits total.
3. A small floating "+N cr" event-text beat plays (EventTextSystem), tinted credit-
   cyan, distinct from the amber XP beat so the player parses two rewards at a glance.
   No turn is spent — this is a side effect of the kill, which already cost a turn.

EARNING FROM PICKUPS:
4. LevelGenerator (or a hand-authored level / an enemy drop) seeds credit chips as
   GroundItems: new GroundItem(tileColumn, tileRow, ItemType.CREDIT_SMALL/MEDIUM/LARGE,
   1). They are added to the World ground-item list and pushed to PropRenderer.
5. Each frame PropRenderer draws the chip as a procedural billboard with a vertical
   bob and a glowing aura (visual spec below). This is cosmetic real-time decoration on
   a static tile — it never advances the simulation.
6. Player steps onto the chip's tile. Credit chips are AUTO-COLLECT (like ammo/armour):
   on the settled-tile pickup hook (PlayerController -> World pickup resolution), the
   chip's credit value is added to the run total, the GroundItem is removed, the
   floating sprite vanishes that frame, and a "+N cr" beat plays. NO extra turn beyond
   the step the player already took.

SPENDING (out of scope here, defined for completeness):
7. A future sink (order 11 merchant) calls PlayerProgress.spendCredits(amount) which
   returns true and deducts if Credits >= amount, else false. This doc only guarantees
   the wallet API exists; it spends nothing itself.

VIEWING:
8. Player opens the inventory overlay. The Credits total is shown as a header readout
   ("CREDITS  1 240 cr") with a small procedural chip glyph. Updates live.


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
=================
Every kill now pays twice: XP (you, the marine, get stronger) and Credits (your
wallet grows toward the next purchase). That dual feedback makes clearing a room feel
materially rewarding the Hades way — numbers go up AND you bank toward a choice.
Credit chips glowing two rooms down create the same "is that worth the detour?"
navigation pull the weapon-ground-sprite bob does, but cheaper and more frequent: a
faint gold shimmer is a small reward, a fat green glow is a jackpot worth crossing a
guarded room for. Because Credits reset on death, they create in-run pressure — spend
now at the merchant or gamble on finding more deeper — which is exactly the tension a
roguelike wants, and it stays cleanly separate from the permanent Salvage progress so
neither system muddies the other.


CREDIT AMOUNTS PER ENEMY KILL
=============================
Credits roughly track threat (loosely correlated to XP, but rounder numbers so the
running total reads cleanly). Depth scaling mirrors XP: actual award =
round(base * (1 + (depth - 1) * CREDIT_DEPTH_SCALE)). All values PLACEHOLDER.

  EnemyType      XP (ref)   CREDIT base   notes
  GORE_BITER       10           5         trash; fast light melee
  EYE_TYRANT       10           6         ranged kiter
  PLAGUE_HULK      14           8         slow tank
  ACID_DRONE       14           8         ranged mechanical
  SHELL_BRUTE      18          12         heavy charger
  VOID_SHROUD      18          12         stealth melee
  MIRE_WRAITH      22          15         acid ranged
  IRON_STALKER     55          40         armored elite (big payday)
  BOSS (any)      500         250         OVERSEER / CORRUPTOR / HELL_BARON

CREDIT_DEPTH_SCALE = 0.12 (placeholder): a Gore Biter worth 5 cr on floor 1 is worth
round(5 * (1 + 9*0.12)) = round(10.4) = 10 cr on floor 10. Keep scaling modest so
late-floor kills don't trivialize the merchant economy.


CREDIT PICKUP TYPES
===================
Three tiers, weighted to favor small. Weights are RELATIVE (normalized at roll time).
Amounts have a small +/- jitter so totals don't look mechanical. All PLACEHOLDER.

  Tier    ItemType        amount (base)   per-tile jitter   spawn weight
  SMALL   CREDIT_SMALL          8          +/- 2  (6-10)        70
  MEDIUM  CREDIT_MEDIUM        25          +/- 5  (20-30)       24
  LARGE   CREDIT_LARGE         70          +/- 15 (55-85)        6

Normalized: SMALL 70%, MEDIUM 24%, LARGE 6%. A typical floor seeds
CREDIT_CHIPS_PER_FLOOR_MIN..MAX = 3..7 chips (placeholder), rolled per chip against
the weight table. Expect a floor to average ~4-5 small, ~1 medium, rare large —
so a full clean floor nets roughly 60-120 chip-credits plus kill credits.

In-fiction names (for tooltips / pickup beats):
  SMALL  = "Credit Sliver"   (a single chipped scrip token)
  MEDIUM = "Credit Stack"    (a banded bundle of tokens)
  LARGE  = "Credit Vault Chip" (a UAC high-denomination data-chip)


VISUAL DESIGN — PROCEDURAL CHIP BILLBOARDS
==========================================
Rendered the SAME WAY as weapon ground sprites: a per-ItemType Texture generated ONCE
at PropRenderer construction via a Pixmap (fillRectangle / fillCircle), Nearest
filtering, transparent background, cached and reused every frame (ZERO per-frame
allocation). Square pixmap, CREDIT_PICKUP_TEXTURE_SIZE = 64 px. RGB values 0-1.
Origin in descriptions = pixmap center; coordinates are fractions of the 64px edge.

Shared rules for all three tiers:
  - Read as a CHIP / TOKEN viewed three-quarter, never a flat coin-on-edge (a thin
    edge vanishes at range). Use a stacked-disc / hex-chip silhouette.
  - A 2-3 px COOL-WHITE top-left rim highlight on the chip body so it pops on dark
    floors and signals "interactable" (same convention as weapon sprites).
  - A tiny dark "UAC" notch / square cut in the chip center sells "corporate scrip"
    and differentiates from medkit/armour pickups.
  - Higher tiers are BIGGER, BRIGHTER, and add layers (more discs, more sparkle).

SMALL — "Credit Sliver" (gold, humble)
  - Single chip disc: warm GOLD circle (0.85, 0.68, 0.20), ~38% of box diameter,
    centered.
  - Inner ring: thin darker-gold circle outline (0.55, 0.42, 0.10) at ~28% diameter.
  - Center notch: 4x4 dark-bronze square (0.30, 0.22, 0.06).
  - Rim highlight: 2px pale-gold arc (0.98, 0.90, 0.55) top-left.
  - Fills ~40% of the box (smallest footprint of the set).

MEDIUM — "Credit Stack" (gold + cyan band, a bundle)
  - TWO stacked discs offset vertically by ~6 px to read as a short stack: lower disc
    gold (0.80, 0.62, 0.18), upper disc brighter gold (0.92, 0.74, 0.26), each ~46%
    diameter.
  - Banding strip: a 4px CYAN band (0.20, 0.80, 0.95) wrapped across the stack middle
    — the "this is more than a sliver" tell, and the cyan ties it to the credit-cyan
    UI color.
  - Center notch: 5x5 dark square (0.25, 0.20, 0.08) on the top disc.
  - Two 2x2 white sparkle flecks (0.98, 0.98, 1.00) at upper-left and right edge.
  - Rim highlight: 2-3px pale-gold top-left on the top disc.
  - Fills ~58% of the box.

LARGE — "Credit Vault Chip" (emerald/green high-denomination, unmistakable jackpot)
  - Hexagonal chip body: filled GREEN/EMERALD hexagon (0.20, 0.78, 0.42), ~64%
    diameter — green deliberately breaks from gold so a jackpot is readable at a
    glance from across a room (color-codes value: gold = small, green = big).
  - Inner hex outline: bright cyan-green 2px (0.45, 0.95, 0.70) at ~50% diameter.
  - Embossed center: small dark-emerald 8x8 square (0.06, 0.30, 0.16) with a 2x2
    bright-cyan core dot (0.60, 1.00, 0.85) — the "data chip" core.
  - Four corner studs: 3x3 pale-mint squares (0.75, 1.00, 0.88) at the hex points.
  - Several (4-5) white sparkle flecks scattered around the rim.
  - Rim highlight: 3px pale-mint top-left.
  - Fills ~70% of the box (largest, brightest, most layered).


AURA (glowing halo behind every chip)
======================================
A soft radial glow drawn BEHIND the chip billboard each frame so chips visibly emit
light, escalating with tier. The aura is a separate additive-blended sprite, NOT baked
into the chip texture (so it can pulse independently). Implement as ONE pre-generated
radial-gradient Texture (white center -> transparent edge, 64px), tinted per tier and
scaled per tier, drawn with additive blending (GL_SRC_ALPHA, GL_ONE) directly before
the chip in the same billboard pass, then blend mode restored.

  Tier     aura color (tint)        base radius (x chip)   pulse amplitude   pulse speed
  SMALL    gold   (0.95,0.78,0.30)        1.4                 +/- 12%          2.0 rad/s
  MEDIUM   cyan-gold(0.55,0.85,0.80)      1.8                 +/- 16%          2.4 rad/s
  LARGE    emerald (0.35,1.00,0.60)       2.4                 +/- 22%          2.8 rad/s

  - radius factor = base * (1 + pulseAmplitude * sin(lightingTimeSeconds * pulseSpeed
    + phase)). Larger chips pulse wider, faster, brighter — a vault chip throbs;
    a sliver gently breathes.
  - aura alpha also pulses with the same sin (e.g. baseAlpha 0.35 small -> 0.55 large,
    multiplied by 0.8..1.0 over the cycle) so the glow shimmers, not just scales.
  - Per-chip phase offset (e.g. + (tileColumn*7 + tileRow*13) * 0.3) desynchronises
    multiple chips so a credit pile shimmers organically, not in lockstep.
  - The aura is tile-brightness aware (reuse PropRenderer getTileBrightness): a chip on
    an unlit floor reads as a lone glowing point in the dark — desired mood.


FLOATING ANIMATION (bob)
========================
Identical math model to the weapon-pickup bob (reuse GameMath.pickupBobOffset):
  bobScreenOffset = sin(lightingTimeSeconds * CREDIT_PICKUP_BOB_SPEED + phase)
                    * CREDIT_PICKUP_BOB_AMPLITUDE_FRACTION * spriteScreenHeight
Applied to the chip's vertical screen position (and to the aura, so glow tracks the
chip). The chip already sits lifted off the floor by CREDIT_PICKUP_HEIGHT_FRACTION so
the bob never dips into the floor.

  CREDIT_PICKUP_HEIGHT_FRACTION         = 0.28   (lift off floor; below weapon 0.30)
  CREDIT_PICKUP_BOB_SPEED               = 2.4    rad/s (slightly livelier than weapons)
  CREDIT_PICKUP_BOB_AMPLITUDE_FRACTION  = 0.12   (12% of sprite height)
  CREDIT_PICKUP_PHASE_STEP              = 0.45   per-chip bob phase desync
Constraint (must hold): BOB_AMPLITUDE_FRACTION < HEIGHT_FRACTION (0.12 < 0.28 — OK),
so the chip never clips the floor at the bottom of its bob.
Optional stretch: a slow horizontal width-squash to fake a slow spin (Doom coin feel)
— flag OUT for v1, vertical bob only.


INVENTORY UI PLACEMENT
======================
The Credits total lives in the inventory overlay (InventoryOverlayRenderer), NOT in
the main combat HUD (the combat HUD stays HP/AR/ammo only — no clutter). Placement:
  - A single-line HEADER readout at the TOP of the inventory overlay, right-aligned
    in the header band, opposite any existing title: a small procedural credit-chip
    glyph (mini version of the SMALL chip, ~24px) + "CREDITS" label + the value with
    thousands spacing ("1 240 cr"), drawn in credit-cyan (0.55, 0.85, 0.95) so it
    stands apart from white item text.
  - Drawn procedurally (ShapeRenderer chip glyph + BitmapFont number), matching the
    overlay's existing no-extra-texture style.
  - Value reads live from PlayerProgress.getCredits() each time the overlay opens /
    is drawn; no caching across opens.
  - If/when the merchant (order 11) exists, the same readout appears in the shop
    header so the player always sees their balance while spending.

TECHNICAL NOTES
===============
PREFERRED IMPLEMENTATION — entity-side GroundItems (no tile symbol):
  - item/ItemType.java: add the three CREDIT_* entries, category MISC, flagged
    auto-collect (same flag ammo/armour use so the step-on hook grabs them).
  - render/PropRenderer.java: extend the existing ground-item pass (the one
    weapon-ground-sprites.txt extends). Build a Map<ItemType, Texture> of the three
    chip textures + one shared aura gradient texture in the constructor; dispose all
    in dispose(). For each credit GroundItem: draw aura (additive, pulsed) then chip
    (bobbed), reusing the existing projection / depth-cull / z-buffer occlusion /
    tile-brightness path verbatim. Restore blend mode after the additive aura.
  - util/GameMath.java: reuse pickupBobOffset(); add a pure
    creditAuraRadiusFactor(timeSeconds, baseRadius, pulseAmplitude, pulseSpeed, phase)
    helper (with the standard formula/derivation/edge-cases comment block).
  - progression/PlayerProgress.java: the WALLET. Add int credits; addCredits(int),
    int getCredits(), boolean spendCredits(int) (deduct iff >= amount, else false),
    reset to 0 on new run. This is in-memory per-run state — NEVER write to
    Preferences (that is Salvage's job).
  - enemy/EnemyManager.java (or the existing KillEventListener path): on kill, compute
    round(deadType.baseCreditReward() * (1 + (depth-1)*CREDIT_DEPTH_SCALE)) and call
    PlayerProgress.addCredits(...). Fire the "+N cr" EventTextSystem beat (credit-cyan,
    offset from the amber XP beat).
  - world/World.java: own the credit chip GroundItems alongside other ground items;
    on the settled-tile auto-collect hook, resolve credit value (base +/- jitter,
    rolled at spawn and stored on the ItemStack quantity field, OR rolled determinist
    -ically at pickup) -> addCredits -> remove GroundItem -> "+N cr" beat.
  - level/LevelGenerator.java: after enemy/item seeding, roll CREDIT_CHIPS_PER_FLOOR
    chips; for each, pick a tier via weighted roll (small/medium/large weights) and
    place a credit GroundItem on a random free floor tile (reuse existing free-tile
    selection). Store the rolled credit amount in the GroundItem's stack quantity so
    the value is fixed at spawn (the displayed "+N" matches what is granted).
  - render/InventoryOverlayRenderer.java: draw the credits header readout
    (chip glyph + label + PlayerProgress.getCredits()).

JITTER STORAGE: simplest is to store the rolled credit AMOUNT directly as the
ItemStack quantity (quantity = rolled credits, not "1"). Pickup then grants
stack.quantity credits. This keeps the GroundItem self-describing and avoids a side
table. (GroundItem constructor already takes a quantity >= 1.)

FALLBACK — hand-authored level symbols (ONLY if level designers need to place chips by
hand in .txt files): requires new symbols — add to docs/tile-symbols.txt and
Level.java before implementing. Proposed FREE lowercase symbols:
  i = small credit chip, o = medium credit chip, q = large credit chip
  (i o q confirmed free in docs/tile-symbols.txt SYMBOL BUDGET). LevelLoader would
  convert these tiles to credit GroundItems at load time (like enemy spawn markers are
  extracted), then replace the tile with floor ' '. This keeps ALL runtime logic on the
  GroundItem path — the symbols are purely an authoring convenience. If this path is
  taken, update Level.java (new pickup check), the SYMBOL BUDGET count, and
  PropRenderer is unaffected (it still renders GroundItems).

TURN-BASED FIT: kill-credit award is a side effect of an already-turn-costing kill;
pickup is the existing one-turn settled-tile event; bob + aura are cosmetic real-time
decoration on static tiles (same class as flickering floors). No constraint violated.
No allocations in render() — all chip/aura textures cached at construction.


BALANCE NOTES (all placeholder — flag for playtest)
===================================================
- Kill credits are intentionally LOWER than XP numbers and rounder (5,6,8,12,15,40,
  250) so the running total reads cleanly and a merchant can price items in tidy
  tens/hundreds.
- Weights 70/24/6 make LARGE chips a genuine event (~6% of seeded chips). If large
  chips feel too rare in playtest, bump to 70/22/8; if jackpots cheapen the economy,
  drop large to 4%.
- Per-floor 3-7 chips + kill credits should net roughly 100-200 cr on a cleanly
  cleared early floor — tune against order 11 merchant prices once that exists. The
  two systems share GameBalance so a designer can rebalance both together.
- CREDIT_DEPTH_SCALE 0.12 keeps late-floor kills ~2x early kills by floor 10 — enough
  to matter, not enough to break the merchant.
- Aura sizes/pulse: LARGE must be unmistakable at range (green, big, fast) while SMALL
  must NOT spam visual noise in a chip-dense room (gold, modest, slow). If a room with
  many smalls looks like a disco, lower SMALL base alpha to 0.28.
- Credits reset to 0 on death by design (in-run only). Confirm RunStats records
  "credits earned this run" for the death-screen tally even though the wallet zeroes.


INTERACTIONS
============
1. SALVAGE META-PROGRESSION (salvage-cache-meta-progression.txt): explicitly DISTINCT.
   Credits = in-run, in-memory, reset on death (PlayerProgress). Salvage = permanent,
   Preferences (StatsStore). Different icons (cyan/gold chip vs. scrap-can), different
   screens. This doc's "credits earned this run" can FEED Salvage payout indirectly
   only via the existing kill/floor stats — it does NOT convert credits to salvage.
2. MERCHANT / SHOP (roguelike_order_11): the consumer of this wallet. That doc calls
   PlayerProgress.spendCredits(); this doc guarantees the API and the balance. Shop
   header reuses the inventory credits readout.
3. WEAPON GROUND SPRITES (weapon-ground-sprites.txt): credit chips reuse the SAME
   PropRenderer ground-item billboard + bob pipeline and GameMath.pickupBobOffset.
   Build the chip textures the same way (cached Pixmap textures). The aura layer is
   the one new rendering element; it could later be back-ported to make rare weapon
   drops glow too.
4. XP / LEVEL PROGRESSION (xp-level-progression-system.txt): every kill awards BOTH XP
   and Credits from the same kill event. The two floating beats (amber XP, cyan
   credits) must be visually offset so they don't overlap/clobber each other in
   EventTextSystem.
5. EVENT TEXT (EventTextSystem / EventTextRenderer): new "+N cr" beat type, credit-cyan,
   spawned on kill-award and on chip pickup.
6. INVENTORY OVERLAY (roguelike_order_4 / InventoryOverlayRenderer): hosts the credits
   header readout. No new screen — just a header line.
7. LEVEL GENERATION (procedural-level-generation.txt): chip seeding hooks into the
   existing free-floor-tile selection used for items/enemies; one weighted roll per
   chip. Boss arenas may seed 0 chips (the boss credit payout covers it) — designer's
   call.


OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
1. Do bosses also drop a guaranteed LARGE credit chip on death (visual jackpot), or is
   the 250 cr kill award enough? Recommend: kill award only for v1; a dropped chip is a
   nice stretch but risks double-counting the payday.
2. Jitter at SPAWN (fixed, stored in stack quantity — recommended) vs. at PICKUP
   (rolled when grabbed)? Recommend spawn-time so the floating "+N" tooltip (if ever
   shown) can match the grant. Defaulting to spawn-time storage above.
3. Should the inventory show a per-run "credits earned" stat in addition to the live
   balance (for the death screen)? Probably yes on the death screen, optional in the
   live overlay. Defer to RunStats.
4. Color-coding: gold(small)/gold(medium)/green(large) — is the gold->green jump at
   LARGE clear enough, or should MEDIUM start trending green? Recommend keeping MEDIUM
   gold-with-cyan-band so green uniquely means "jackpot." Confirm in a visual pass.
5. Aura additive blending over the floor/wall backdrop: confirm it composites cleanly
   in the existing PropRenderer batch (may need a brief blend-mode swap window). Flag
   to libgdx-specialist.
6. Hand-authored symbol path (i/o/q): build it now or defer until a level designer
   actually needs to place chips by hand? Recommend DEFER — generator + drops cover the
   roguelike use case; add symbols only when authoring demands it.
