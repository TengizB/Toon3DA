TITLE: Cinderforge Colossus — magma golem that hardens when unharmed
---

OVERVIEW
========
The second ELEMENTAL GOLEM (EnemyFamily.GOLEM). A towering bipedal furnace of
red-orange magma crystal, spiked crown, molten seams running under a black crust.
Slow, enormous, and the first enemy in the game that gets HARDER the longer you
stop shooting it.

Two mechanics, one idea — THE GAME PUNISHES HESITATION:
  * COOLING CRUST — every enemy turn it takes no damage, its surface cools and it
    gains a stack of flat armour. Any damage shatters ALL stacks.
  * MOLTEN TRAIL — every tile it leaves behind ignites. It burns the ground you
    would have retreated across.
Ducking behind cover to reload is exactly the wrong move: it hardens while you
hide, and the corridor behind you is on fire when you come out.

FAMILY & ROLE
=============
  family()               EnemyFamily.GOLEM
  role()                 EnemyRole.BRUISER          (TP band 70-120, ANCHOR)
  positionalMultiplier() BalanceConfig.POSITIONAL_MULT_MELEE (1.00)
  isRanged()             false
  tacticalVerb()         "FORGE: keep the pressure on — it re-hardens the moment
                          you stop shooting, and it burns the ground behind it."

Distinct from the Shell Brute, the other BRUISER: the Brute is a POSITIONING puzzle
(sidestep the lane, punish the recovery). The Colossus is a TEMPO puzzle (never let
the damage stop). Two bruisers, two different failures.

MECHANIC 1 — COOLING CRUST
==========================
State: enemy.crustStacks (int, 0..CINDERFORGE_CRUST_MAX_STACKS = 3).

  GAIN   At the END of the Colossus's own turn, if it took NO damage since its
         previous turn, crustStacks++ (capped). Each stack adds
         CINDERFORGE_CRUST_ARMOR_PER_STACK (4) to enemy.armor.
  LOSE   The instant it takes ANY damage: crustStacks = 0, armour contribution
         removed, and that hit deals CINDERFORGE_CRUST_SHATTER_MULTIPLIER (1.25x)
         damage if it broke a full 3-stack crust. Breaking a fully cooled shell is
         loud and rewarding; it is not a punishment for having waited, it is the
         payoff for coming back out.
  FLOOR  crustStacks is per-instance and never persists across a level.

At 3 stacks its armour is 12 flat against a reference 25-damage hit — a ~48%
reduction. That is severe on purpose: the number exists to make "keep firing" the
right answer, and it evaporates on the first bullet, so it can never wall a player
who is actually engaging.

IMPORTANT: this must hook the SAME damage entry point that already exists
(Enemy.applyDamage / the flat-armour subtraction), not a new one. Direct HP loss
from status DoT (applyDoTDamage) BYPASSES block today — a DoT tick must still COUNT
as damage for the purpose of resetting crust, otherwise Burning/Bleed builds are
silently locked out of the counterplay.

MECHANIC 2 — MOLTEN TRAIL
=========================
When the Colossus VACATES a tile (in executeMove, after the move resolves), that
tile becomes a fire hazard via the existing HazardManager.spawnFire path — the same
'i' tile the hazard system already owns, with lifetime
CINDERFORGE_TRAIL_FIRE_TURNS (3).

  - Uses the SHIPPED hazard system. No new hazard type, no new tile symbol, and the
    fire it lays participates in the existing spread / barrel chain-reaction rules
    for free (which is a great emergent moment next to an explosive barrel).
  - The trail burns the Colossus's allies too — the hazard system already applies
    BURNING to ANY host on the tile, player and enemies alike. Do not special-case it.
  - It does NOT ignite the tile it is standing on, so the player is never denied the
    tile they need to melee it from without warning.
  - Cap: no more than CINDERFORGE_TRAIL_MAX_LIVE_TILES (6) live trail tiles per
    Colossus, oldest expiring first, so a long chase cannot carpet a floor.

Because it moves every OTHER turn, the trail is a slow-growing wall rather than a
sprint-line. Kiting it is still correct — it just costs you the ground you kite over.

BEHAVIOUR — COMMIT / EXECUTE INTEGRATION
========================================
The turn engine does not change (docs/enemy-system.txt, PRE-COMMITTED INTENT MODEL).

  computeNextPlan()  -> ordinary melee planning (computeMeleePlan). No new branch is
                        strictly required; the Colossus is a plain chaser whose
                        IDENTITY is in the two passive hooks.
  phaseBExecute()    -> executeMove: after the move resolves, ignite the vacated tile
                        (trail). End of the enemy's turn: evaluate the crust gain.
  damage path        -> reset crust on any damage taken, incl. DoT ticks.

Deliberately no bespoke plan branch: the Shell Brute and Void Shroud already own the
"clever melee AI" space. The Colossus is dumb, huge and relentless, and its pressure
comes from the board state it creates, not from outsmarting the player.

VISUAL EFFECTS (renderer spec — EnemyRenderer / ImpactEffectSystem)
===================================================================
Procedural only; no new texture assets.

  1. SEAM GLOW = SOFTNESS. The sprite tint is driven by crustStacks:
       0 stacks -> full-bright orange (CINDERFORGE_TINT_HOT), seams blazing
       3 stacks -> darkened, desaturated charcoal (CINDERFORGE_TINT_COOLED)
     Lerped over ~0.4s so a shatter is a visible FLASH back to hot, not a snap.
     CRITICAL: the cooled tint must stay distinguishable from the DORMANT_SHADE_DAMPEN
     (0.7) darkening used for sleeping enemies — keep a bright orange RIM highlight on
     the cooled state so "hardened" never reads as "asleep".

  2. CRUST PLATES. One additive dark-plate quad per stack, drawn over the sprite at
     fixed offsets (shoulders, chest, hips) — a literal count of the armour, readable
     at a glance and at distance. Pre-allocated quads; no per-frame allocation.

  3. SHATTER. When crust breaks: an outward burst of orange shard particles, a white
     flash frame, and a short screen shake (all existing ImpactEffectSystem verbs).
     A 3-stack shatter uses a bigger burst than a 1-stack — the reward is felt.

  4. HEAT HAZE. A subtle vertical shimmer band at the base of the billboard (a
     sin-offset sampling of the sprite's lowest rows, amplitude
     CINDERFORGE_HEAT_HAZE_AMPLITUDE). Sells the mass and the temperature.

  5. FOOTFALL. Each completed move: an ember burst plus a brief screen shake scaled
     by distance — the room announces it is coming even out of view.

  6. DEATH — COOLING. The corpse beat runs long: the sprite blackens over ~1s, seams
     going out one by one, then it collapses into a burst of embers, leaving one last
     fire tile under it. Distinct from the Rimeshell's blue shatter.

COUNTERPLAY
===========
  - NEVER STOP SHOOTING. Sustained-DPT weapons (Chaingun, Assault Rifle, Incinerator)
    trivialise the crust; burst-and-hide play feeds it.
  - Reload BEFORE you engage, not mid-fight — the reload turns are exactly when it
    cools.
  - Fight it in open rooms. In a corridor its trail takes your only exit.
  - Bait it across an explosive barrel: its own fire chain-detonates the barrel.
  - Its allies burn in its trail. Golems do not path around it.

BALANCE — TP DERIVATION
=======================
  Crust as TIME-AVERAGED flat reduction. Live crust reaches 12 flat, but only against
  a player who has stopped attacking. Against the modelled player (who is attacking
  every turn — that is what REFERENCE_PLAYER_DPT means) crust sits near 0 most turns.
  Priced at the honest average of ~1 stack of uptime:
      flatReduction() = 3f
  The comment on flatReduction() must state this derivation, because a naive reader
  will otherwise "fix" it to 12 and blow the bruiser band.

  eHP = enemyEffectiveHitPoints(110, 0, 0, 3, REFERENCE_PLAYER_DPT=25)
      = 110 * 25/(25-3) = 125.0
  survivalTurns      = 125.0 / 25 = 5.0
  basicDamagePerTurn = 18 / 1 = 18.0
  TP = 18.0 * 5.0 * 1.00 = 90.0     -> BRUISER band (70-120) OK, mid-band

  The molten trail is NOT folded into this TP. Terrain danger has its own priced
  channel — GameMath.hazardTileThreatPoints, printed by BalanceReport's HAZARDS
  section — and folding it into the enemy TP as well would double-count it. Confirm
  the trail's contribution shows up there once implemented.

  Credits 16 (above Shell Brute's 12, below Iron Stalker's 40) matches its TP.
  XP is derived; never add an XP constant.

  GATE: BalanceConfig change -> ./gradlew test AND ./gradlew balanceSim
  (docs/game-balance-authority.txt SECTION 7).

INTERACTIONS
============
  - HAZARDS (Pillar 3): the trail is the deepest integration any enemy has with the
    hazard system. Fire spread, barrel chains and the BURNING status all apply
    unchanged; the Colossus is immune to nothing, including its own fire — it is made
    of magma, but the hazard system has no exemption path and adding one for one
    enemy is not worth the special case. If playtesting says otherwise, add a
    HazardImmunity concept properly, for all hosts.
  - EXPLOSIVE BARRELS: the single best emergent counter. Preserve it.
  - STATUS: BURNING on the player from the trail stacks with everything normally.
  - ENCOUNTER BUDGET: anchor role, one per floor.
  - OTHER GOLEMS: the Colossus forces movement, which walks the player straight
    through a Rimeshell Lancer's lane. That pairing is the golem family's signature
    encounter — ship them on the same floors.

OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
  1. Should DoT ticks reset the crust? RECOMMEND YES (stated above) — otherwise
     Incinerator/Bleed builds cannot express the counterplay at all. Confirm the DoT
     path can signal "damage taken" without also granting the shatter bonus.
  2. Crust gain while DORMANT: a Colossus asleep since level load would wake fully
     cooled. RECOMMEND crust only accrues once ALERTED, so the opening burst is
     always worth taking.
  3. Trail lifetime 3 turns vs 4: 3 keeps corridors survivable; test at 4 on floors
     8+ if the late game needs teeth.
  4. Does the trail block the ENEMY pathfinder? RECOMMEND NO — allies walking into
     their own boss's fire is good comedy and good tactics for the player.
  5. If '{' collides with a shipped EXCAVATION_SITE room, fall back to '/'.
