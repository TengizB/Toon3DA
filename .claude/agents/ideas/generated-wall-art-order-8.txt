Series: generated-wall-art.txt (concept, rules W1-W12). Method: order 1's rules apply unchanged where

OVERVIEW
--------
Doors and columns are wall stripes too and are in view almost as often as walls. Today the four doors are
JPEGs or a procedural 512x512 Pixmap "sci-fi blast door" (WallRenderer.loadOrGenerateDoorTexture) shared
by every region, and the two columns ('P' round / square) are flat Pixmap programs. This order makes
them part of each stratum: every stratum ships its plain door, its three keycard doors and its column,
in the kit, in its own atlas.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - walls.py: per stratum a DOOR group (door, door_red, door_yellow, door_blue) and a COLUMN group
    (column_round, column_square), written into the stratum's atlas + manifest (manifest gains
    "doors" and "columns" arrays, same entry shape; version 2 - order 3's parser accepts both).
  - KEYCARD COLOURS ARE SEMANTIC (V5): the red/yellow/blue reads come ONLY from a keycard lock panel
    and a lit frame stripe in exactly the CombatPalette/ItemConstants keycard colours (emissive, so the
    mood never shifts them). The door body is the stratum's material.
  - The door open/close animation (DoorManager's slide) is unchanged; the door texture slides as today.
  - WallRenderer: door + column columns sample the floor's wall atlas (one more reason for one bind);
    the LevelPalette column binding ('P' round/square) resolves to the stratum's column regions.
  - Column texture height/aspect: as today's column path (RenderConstants) - only the pixels change.
  - Docs: art-direction WALLS, wall-renderer-guide (doors/columns), tileset section 15.
OUT
  - Any door rule, keycard rule, symbol or animation timing (W7).
  - New door types.


VISUAL DESIGN (per stratum, one line each - the builder expands them with the owner gate)
-------------
  A  a painted livery sliding door with a porthole and a rubber seal; column = a painted steel pillar.
  B  a stainless swing-door pair with a kick plate and a drain at the sill; column = a tiled pier.
  C  a heavy riveted pressure door with a soul-lit seam; column = a ribbed reactor stanchion.
  D  a facility door forced half-shut by tissue, membrane over its edges; column = a tissue-wrapped column.
  E  a lattice iris (nacre leaves meeting at a soul-lit centre); column = a nacre trunk.
  Keycard doors: the same body + a lock panel (keycard colour, emissive) + a frame stripe in that colour.


RULES
-----
  R1  A keycard door's colour must read at 6 tiles in every mood (capture each door x each mood).
  R2  The plain door must be distinguishable from walls of its stratum at 4 tiles (silhouette: a frame,
      a seam, a porthole - never colour alone).
  R3  Doors and columns tile like walls only where they need to (columns never neighbour each other).
