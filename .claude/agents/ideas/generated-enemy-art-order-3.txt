OVERVIEW
--------
Replaces the four regular ABERRATION textures - Plague Hulk, Eye Tyrant, Gore Biter, Mire Wraith -
with generated sprites. Aberrations are the containment breach: harvested planet tissue that escaped
the tanks and kept growing. Soft, fused, wet flesh is what the kit's smooth-union height fields do best,
so this is the first order that designs a whole family from briefs. (The Corruptor boss is order 6.)


SCOPE
-----

  - Four briefs (below) -> four bestiary.py functions -> generated/plague_hulk.png, eye_tyrant.png,
    gore_biter.png, mire_wraith.png.
  - Kit growth: flesh materials (a pale bruised flesh ramp and a darker raw one - desaturated rose-grey
    and violet-grey, never saturated red, G5), a wet sheen setting (small, very bright specular), an
    EYEBALL helper (sclera dome + iris + pupil + glint) and a TEETH-ROW helper (a curved run of small
    bone spikes, reusing order 2's spike primitive).

VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
DESIGN BRIEF - PLAGUE HULK (SOLDIER, 0.95 walls, melee, AREA_STRIKE, tank)
  A mass of tissue that grew inside a containment tank until it burst it. It still wears the tank's
  broken steel collar ring around its middle like a belt it outgrew; flesh bulges over and under it.
  Wide, low centre of gravity, no neck; two enormous forearms knuckled on the floor (the AREA_STRIKE is
  a two-fisted slam). A cluster of ichor blisters across one shoulder. Small head sunk in the mass.

DESIGN BRIEF - EYE TYRANT (CHAFF, 0.55 walls, ranged straight-line sniper, DEBUFF_PLAYER, hovers)
  One huge eye that was being studied: it hangs in a cradle of fleshy lids and petals, and a surgical
  speculum (gunmetal) still holds the lids open. Nerve tendrils trail beneath it. The iris is ichor and
  points straight at the player - the whole sprite is the "line" ORA warns about. Small: it is chaff.

DESIGN BRIEF - GORE BITER (CHAFF, 0.85 walls, melee swarmer)
  A lean runner that is mostly mouth: the head splits vertically into a jaw lined with teeth, in a low
  forward crouch on long digitigrade legs, ribs showing through thin flesh. An ear-tag clip from the
  breeding pens. Built to be seen five at a time: simple, readable, no fussy detail.

DESIGN BRIEF - MIRE WRAITH (SOLDIER, 0.80 walls, ranged acid artillery, DEBUFF_PLAYER)
  A drooping, half-liquid shape that lived in the facility sump: a translucent sagging belly of glowing
  acid glands (the stacking acid - the brightest thing on the sprite), a hunched draped body, and a long
  proboscis-like spout aimed forward. A length of drainage hose has fused into its back. It looks heavy
  with what it is about to throw.
