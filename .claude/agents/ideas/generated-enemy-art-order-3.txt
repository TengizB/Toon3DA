OVERVIEW
--------
Order 2 made enemies move; this order makes them ANSWER. Every way the simulation can touch an enemy
gets a distinct visual reply: a hit that hurt, a hit it shrugged off, a hit its block ate, a stagger, a
knockback, a burn, a poison, a stun, the slow slide toward death, the death itself and what is left
behind. Effects are generated by the kit from each family's own materials - sparks off a machine, ichor
off a tick - and drawn by a pooled effect system. Ranged enemies get generated projectiles and muzzle
flashes launched from their rig's anchors. Everything is proven on the Warden and the Harvest Tick;
every later order supplies the same set for its creatures.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - DAMAGE OUTCOMES (from order 2's DAMAGED payload):
      HURT        HURT_LIGHT / HURT_HEAVY + a short recoil (the billboard pushed back and down a few
                  pixels, eased out) + the family HIT effect at the hit_centre anchor + the white flash.
      RESISTED    no flinch: a hard armour spark + a small shove; the existing RESISTED word stays.
      BLOCKED     no flinch: a block shimmer over the body (Block absorbed it).
      ABSORBED    the existing Auric gold flash path (no new art for it here; order 7 owns golems).
      KILLING BLOW goes straight to DEATH with a stronger hit effect.
    Splash damage (grenades, barrels, relic blasts) recoils AWAY from the blast tile, not from the player.
  - WOUNDED: below 50% HP the creature idles with its WOUNDED idle (order 1) and periodically emits its
    family LEAK effect from a wound anchor (sparks / drips), so a hurt enemy looks hurt at a glance.
  - STATUS VISUALS: every StatusType an enemy can carry (today BURNING, POISONED, BLEED, STUNNED, BLINDED,
    SLOWED, EMPOWERED, VULNERABLE, WEAK - confirm against status/StatusType at implementation time) gets
    a body tint and/or generated particles from body anchors; STUNNED plays a dazed loop (HURT_HEAVY's
    last pose held with a sway). Colours obey the semantic table (art-direction V5).
  - DEATH AND REMAINS: DEATH plays (order 2), its last frame stays on the floor as the creature's own
    REMAINS billboard for the rest of the floor, in place of the generic corpse decal sprite for that
    tile (the simulation's corpse tile and its semantics are untouched - only what is drawn there). A
    death burst of the creature's own materials plays on the killing frame.
  - PROJECTILES AND MUZZLES: for migrated ranged types, the attack effect is a generated projectile sprite
    (with a short trail) launched from the rig's muzzle anchor at the ATTACK clip's emit frame, replacing
    EnemyAttackEffectSystem's procedural glob for those types; a muzzle/emit flash on that frame.
  - TELEGRAPH EFFECTS: WIND_UP emits a charge-up effect (the Warden's prod crackles; the tick kicks up
    grit) so the held pose also reads in peripheral vision.
  - The KIT: a new builder `.claude/skills/enemy-art/effects.py` generating small effect atlases + manifests
    from kit materials: machine (sparks, cyan arc, metal debris, corrosive glob projectile + trail, leak
    sparks) and insect (ichor droplets, chitin flecks, grit, leak drips). Every later family order adds
    its own family set here.
  - The RUNTIME: `render/enemyvisual/EnemyEffectSystem` - pooled world-space billboard particles (position,
    velocity, gravity, lifetime, frame clip, depth-sorted against the wall z-buffer), glow particles drawn
    additively and ungraded; `RemainsRegistry` (per floor, tile -> creature remains frame).
  - Docs: guide sections 8 and 9 lose their [PLANNED] markers and state the real rules; art-direction
    section 16; enemy-system.txt.

OUT
  - Other creatures (orders 4-8) - but the rules and systems here are what they plug into.
  - Changing damage, statuses, corpse semantics, or any rule (G9). Player-side effects (vignettes,
    screen shake) - unchanged.
  - Gore. Restraint is the tone (premise): ichor, sparks, dust - not blood sprays.


GAMEPLAY LOOP
-------------
Rules unchanged. Visually: shoot a Harvest Tick with a weak gun it resists - it barely shifts and sparks
off its plating; switch to a gun that bites - it flinches, recoils, ichor sprays from the hit; at half
health a blister is burst and leaking and it drags a leg; set it on fire - it burns; kill it - it
curls up, and its carcass stays on the floor where it fell.


RULES
-----
  R1  A reaction never changes simulation state, timing or input (G9).
  R2  Every reaction has a visual answer that differs from the others: hurt, resisted, blocked and
      absorbed must be told apart without reading a word.
  R3  Effects use only the creature's family materials and inks (guide 4.4); status colours follow
      art-direction V5.
  R4  All particles and remains are pooled with fixed caps; when a cap is hit, the oldest is recycled.
  R5  Remains are cleared on floor teardown; the simulation's corpse tile stays the authority.


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
-----------------
Feedback the player can read with their thumbs busy: did that hurt it? (flinch + spray) did it bounce
off? (spark, no flinch) is it nearly dead? (wounded idle + leaking) - the matchup system becomes
visible on the body, which is exactly what ORA's MATCHUP teaching asks the player to notice.


TECHNICAL NOTES
---------------
  - Reuse ImpactEffectSystem / EnemyAttackEffectSystem infrastructure where it fits; replace only the
    enemy-body-side effects for migrated types. Do not touch player-side effects.
  - The corpse decal draw lives in the decal/prop path; the remains swap is a lookup in RemainsRegistry
    at draw time for that tile (no symbol, no level change).
  - Measure (G13): a fight with 6 enemies, 2 burning, one dying: frame time vs order 2's number.


VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
WARDEN (machine): hit = a spray of white-hot sparks + a cyan arc across the hull; resisted = one hard
spark and a ringing shove; leak = intermittent sparks from the cracked visor; burning = smoke + flame
licks from the cable bundle; projectile = a corrosive glob in the Organization's cyan-tinted agent with a
dripping trail, launched from the prod's emitter; death burst = hull panels and a cyan flash.
HARVEST TICK (insect): hit = coral ichor droplets + chitin flecks; resisted = a dull chip off the shell;
leak = drips from the burst blister; burning = flames along the back, blisters dimming; wind-up = grit
kicked up by the dug-in legs; death burst = a pop of ichor mist as the blisters fail.
