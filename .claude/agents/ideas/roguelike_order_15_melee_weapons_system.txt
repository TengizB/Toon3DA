CATEGORY: Combat
TITLE: Melee Weapons — Fist / Knife / Pipe / Chainsaw (No-Ammo Fallback)
---

OVERVIEW
========
A melee weapon class so the marine always has a reliable, no-ammo answer when the
clips run dry. Melee resolves INSTANTLY in tile space — no marching projectile,
no travel — by hitting whatever living enemy occupies the single tile directly in
the player's facing direction. Four melee weapons cover a clear power/risk curve:
the always-available FIST (last resort), the COMBAT KNIFE (fast, low damage), the
STEEL PIPE (medium damage + knockback), and the rare CHAINSAW (high sustained
damage). Because melee is "face an adjacent enemy and swing", it slots perfectly
into the cardinal, turn-based grid the whole game runs on.

This document OWNS: the four melee weapons' stat blocks, the instant
adjacent-tile resolution, the FIST-always-equipped rule, knockback, attack-speed
(turns-per-attack) handling, and melee-specific feedback (screen shake, hit flash).
It does NOT redefine the abstract Weapon class, the equip flow, or the damage
pipeline — it EXTENDS the Weapon abstraction from shotgun-weapon-system.txt and
feeds the same EnemyManager.applyDamageTo path used by ranged weapons.

NO NEW TILE SYMBOL IS REQUIRED FOR THE FIST OR FOR SWINGING. Melee weapon PICKUPS
on the floor (knife/pipe/chainsaw lying in the world) WOULD each need a symbol —
see TECHNICAL NOTES "PICKUP SYMBOLS" (flag, do not invent here).

THE FOUR MELEE WEAPONS (stat blocks — placeholders)
===================================================
All have range 1 (Chebyshev == 1 to the target tile in the facing direction;
cardinal facing means it is the orthogonally-adjacent tile ahead). NO ammo.

1. FIST              ALWAYS EQUIPPED / FALLBACK
   - baseDamage      : 4
   - turnsPerAttack  : 1   (one swing = one turn)
   - knockback       : none
   - special         : none. The "out of everything" option; chip damage only.
   - Occupies the melee slot whenever no other melee weapon is held; CANNOT be
     dropped. Selecting "melee" with no melee pickup uses the Fist.

2. COMBAT KNIFE      fast, low damage
   - baseDamage      : 8
   - turnsPerAttack  : 1   (snappy)
   - knockback       : none
   - special         : fastest melee; slight bonus vs low-HP chaff (clean one-shots
     on Lost Souls / Imps after stats). Flavor: equipping it grants +1 AGILITY
     (order_12 equipment hook) — optional.

3. STEEL PIPE        medium damage + knockback
   - baseDamage      : 14
   - turnsPerAttack  : 1
   - knockback       : 1 tile (pushes a knockback-eligible enemy one tile directly
     away from the player, if that tile is free floor).
   - special         : a CHARGED swing (hold the attack: see CHARGE below) deals
     +50% and applies STUN (order_14) for 1 turn. The crowd-control melee.

4. CHAINSAW          rare, high sustained damage
   - baseDamage      : 12 per turn but it CONTINUES: see "continuous" rule
   - turnsPerAttack  : 1   (but holding the attack key keeps it revving — each held
     turn is another hit on the adjacent enemy, no wind-down)
   - knockback       : none (it grinds in place — keeps the enemy adjacent)
   - special         : "continuous" — while the attack key is held AND a valid
     adjacent target remains, each consumed turn deals baseDamage again with a
     short hit-cadence; great DPS but ROOTS you next to the enemy (you cannot move
     while sawing without releasing). High risk vs Hell Knight. Rare drop.

INSTANT ADJACENT-TILE RESOLUTION (the core difference from ranged)
==================================================================
MeleeWeapon.fire(playerTileColumn, playerTileRow, playerDirectionX,
                 playerDirectionY, level, enemyManager):
  1. Compute the single TARGET tile:
        targetColumn = playerTileColumn + round(playerDirectionX)
        targetRow    = playerTileRow    + round(playerDirectionY)
     (Facing is always an exact cardinal unit vector after rotation snapping, so
      round() yields one of (±1,0)/(0,±1). This is the tile DIRECTLY AHEAD.)
  2. Query enemyManager for a LIVING enemy whose tile == (targetColumn,targetRow).
     - If none: "whiff" — swing animation plays, screen does NOT shake, no hit
       flash, but the swing STILL costs one turn (you committed the action;
       enemies retaliate). Emit a soft "miss" feedback. Return FireResult(hit=false).
     - If a target exists: HIT (see DAMAGE FORMULA), apply knockback/status,
       trigger screen shake + hit flash. Return FireResult(hit=true).
  3. No march, no projectile, no z-buffer query, no distance falloff — melee is
     binary at range 1.

DAMAGE FORMULA  (GameMath.meleeDamage, with derivation comment)
===============================================================
  finalMelee = round( weapon.baseDamage
                      * stats.getMeleeDamageMultiplier()     // STRENGTH (order_12)
                      * weaponDamageMultiplier               // honed_edge/overcharge perks
                      * berserkerMultiplier )                // 2.0 if perk + HP<30%, else 1.0
  Then EnemyManager.applyDamageTo handles the TARGET's physical reduction (Hell
  Knight 25%) and Empowered/other outgoing buffs are folded in via
  weaponDamageMultiplier or a dedicated outgoing-buff read (order_14 EMPOWERED).
  Decision: melee DOES read weaponDamageMultiplier so damage perks feel universal;
  if that proves too strong, gate honed_edge to ranged only (OPEN Q).

ATTACK SPEED — turnsPerAttack on the GRID
=========================================
The grid is one-action-one-turn, so "attack speed" is expressed as turnsPerAttack:
  - turnsPerAttack = 1 (all four v1 weapons): each swing is a single turn; the
    enemy gets its turn after each swing. This keeps the invariant clean.
  - For a FUTURE heavy weapon (turnsPerAttack = 2): the swing animation spans the
    first turn doing nothing (a "wind-up" turn) and lands on the second — modeled
    by a small pendingSwingTurns counter on the weapon, decremented each turn,
    damage applied when it hits 0. v1 does NOT need this; the field is defined for
    extensibility. Faster-than-1 (a weapon that hits twice in one turn) is
    DISALLOWED — it would break the one-action-one-turn invariant.
  - CHAINSAW "continuous": NOT faster than 1 turn; it simply lets the held attack
    key auto-issue a new swing every turn against the same adjacent enemy without
    re-press, so the player chain-saws across multiple turns. Each turn is still a
    full world turn (enemy retaliates each turn) — that is the risk.
  - STEEL PIPE CHARGE: hold the attack key for MELEE_CHARGE_TURNS (default the
    swing is charged if the key was held through one idle turn) -> the next swing
    is the charged variant (+50%, STUN). Releasing early = normal swing. (v1 may
    simplify charge to "every Nth swing is charged" if hold-detection is fiddly —
    OPEN Q.)

KNOCKBACK (Steel Pipe)
======================
On a hit by a knockback weapon against a canBeKnockedBack==true enemy:
  pushColumn = targetColumn + round(playerDirectionX)
  pushRow    = targetRow    + round(playerDirectionY)
  If (pushColumn,pushRow) is in-bounds, NOT a wall (Level.isWall), NOT a solid
  prop, and NOT occupied by another enemy/the player -> move the enemy there
  (instant, this turn). Else the enemy stays (it hit a wall — optionally +small
  "crunch" bonus damage; defer). Knockback is an INSTANT position change, not a
  status. Pinky / Lost Soul / Hell Knight (canBeKnockedBack==false, order_13)
  ignore it entirely.

FIST-ALWAYS-EQUIPPED + SLOT RULE
================================
- PlayerInventory holds a meleeWeapon reference, defaulting to a FIST instance at
  run start. The Fist instance is never null and never dropped.
- Picking up a Knife/Pipe/Chainsaw REPLACES the meleeWeapon reference (the old
  melee weapon, if not the Fist, drops to the floor as a pickup — or is discarded;
  OPEN Q). The Fist returns automatically only if the held melee weapon is somehow
  removed (it cannot be in v1).
- Weapon selection: a "melee" weapon-select key (or cycling the weapon wheel)
  selects the current meleeWeapon. Firing with melee selected runs MeleeWeapon.fire.
- Ranged weapons and the melee weapon are SEPARATE slots; switching between "ranged
  selected" and "melee selected" is itself a free UI action (does NOT cost a turn —
  consistent with how weapon-switch is treated; confirm vs shotgun doc).

VISUAL DESIGN
=============
- WEAPON HUD SPRITES (bottom-center, 380px slot, same as ranged):
    FIST     : two armored gauntlets; SWING = one fist thrusts forward, knuckles
               flare on contact.
    KNIFE    : a combat knife held low-right; SWING = quick upward slash arc.
    PIPE     : a rusted steel pipe held vertical; SWING = horizontal sweep; a
               CHARGED swing has a brief white wind-up glow then a heavier sweep.
    CHAINSAW : center-held, teeth animate (2-3 frame rev loop) continuously while
               revving; SWING = teeth bite forward, blood/spark particles.
- HIT FEEDBACK (only on a connecting hit):
    * Screen shake: brief, MELEE_HIT_SHAKE_PIXELS amplitude over
      MELEE_HIT_SHAKE_SECONDS (camera offset, restored — NO origin move; reuse any
      existing shake hook or add a tiny one in the renderer).
    * Hit flash: a quick white full-screen quad at MELEE_HIT_FLASH_ALPHA
      (reuse the tint-quad pattern; no alloc).
    * Enemy hit-reaction (recoil/flash) via enemy-hit-impact-effects.txt.
    * Knockback: the target billboard slides one tile back over the hit window.
- A WHIFF (no target) plays the swing with NO shake/flash — readability: shake
  means you connected.
- CHAINSAW audio/visual: a continuous rev tone while held + grind sparks; the most
  "committed" feeling weapon.

BALANCE NOTES
=============
- Melee fills the "out of ammo" gap and a high-risk burst niche; it must be WORTH
  the danger of standing adjacent. Chainsaw vs a tanky enemy out-DPS-es most ranged
  options but roots you in melee range eating retaliation each turn — the classic
  Doom chainsaw gamble.
- Fist is deliberately feeble (4) so it is a true last resort, not a viable main.
- Knife one-shots chaff after a couple of STRENGTH points (8 * 1.25 = 10 >= Lost
  Soul 12? close — tune so a mid-run knife reliably one-shots the 12-HP fliers).
- Steel Pipe's knockback + charge-STUN is the tactical melee: create space or lock
  down a single threat. STUN sources are kept rare (order_14), so the charged pipe
  is a meaningful tool, not spammable (charge costs the hold time).
- Knockback into a wall doing nothing is fine for v1; "wall slam bonus damage" is a
  stretch goal.
- Because every swing (even a whiff) costs a turn and provokes retaliation, melee
  punishes mis-spacing — deaths stay readable ("I swung at nothing and the Hell
  Knight got a free hit").

INTERACTIONS
============
1. WEAPON ABSTRACTION (shotgun-weapon-system.txt): MeleeWeapon extends Weapon;
   reuses canFire/fire/HUD-state plumbing and EnemyManager.applyDamageTo. No ammo,
   no reload.
2. TURN ENGINE (enemy_system.txt): a swing is a FIRE turn — onPlayerTurnCompleted
   fires after resolution, so adjacent enemies retaliate; the chainsaw's held loop
   issues one swing per turn.
3. STATS (order_12): STRENGTH melee multiplier scales every swing; equipping the
   Knife may grant +1 AGILITY / Chainsaw +1 STRENGTH via the equipment hook.
4. PERKS (order_11): berserker doubles melee under 30% HP (the intended "go loud
   when bleeding" payoff); brawn/honed_edge feed the formula.
5. STATUS (order_14): Steel Pipe charged hit applies STUN; EMPOWERED buff scales
   melee damage; melee can KILL a Burning/Poisoned enemy and claim the XP via the
   normal death path.
6. ENEMY ARCHETYPES (order_13): canBeKnockedBack gates pipe knockback (Pinky/Lost
   Soul/Hell Knight immune); Hell Knight physical reduction shaves melee damage;
   Pinky/Hell Knight punish standing adjacent (the melee risk).
7. AMMO ECONOMY: melee is the pressure valve when ranged ammo is dry, reinforcing
   the resource-scarcity roguelike pillar — a guaranteed (if dangerous) option.

OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
1. Does melee read weaponDamageMultiplier (honed_edge/overcharge apply to melee)?
   Recommend YES for v1 (perks feel universal); gate to ranged-only if melee scales
   too hard.
2. Steel Pipe charge detection: true "hold the key across a turn" vs simpler "every
   3rd swing is charged"? Recommend the simpler every-Nth for v1, upgrade later.
3. When a new melee weapon is picked up, does the old one drop to the floor
   (re-pickable) or vanish? Recommend vanish for v1 (no slot juggling); revisit with
   the inventory screen (order_6).
4. Is weapon-slot switching (ranged <-> melee) free, or does it cost a turn?
   Recommend FREE (a UI action), matching the expectation that drawing a knife when
   out of shells is instant.
5. Source of melee weapons in v1: weapon racks '=' (no symbol) vs dedicated pickup
   symbols vs a perk? Recommend weapon racks '=' for the Pipe, a rare Chainsaw in a
   guarded room (Hell Knight loot), Knife as a common early rack find.
6. Should the Chainsaw consume something (fuel) to bound its power, or stay pure
   no-ammo? Recommend pure no-ammo for v1 (its risk is the root-in-melee), add fuel
   only if it trivializes elites.
