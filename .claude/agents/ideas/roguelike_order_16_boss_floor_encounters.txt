TITLE: Boss Floor Encounters — Every 5th Floor
---

OVERVIEW
========
Every 5th floor (depth 5, 10, 15, ...) is a BOSS FLOOR: the descent stops at a
single large arena holding one unique, named, multi-phase boss. The arena is
cleared of all trash mobs; the boss fills it. Each boss runs on the existing
turn-based Enemy system (one boss action per completed player turn) and the
existing depth counter from level-transitions-stairs-down.txt (IMPLEMENTED).
Three bosses ship in v1, keyed to depth: The Overseer (5), The Corruptor (10),
Hell Baron (15). Beyond depth 15 the boss table cycles with scaled stats.

This is the climax pacing beat (Roguelike Pillar #8, Pacing & Escalation): a run
is a staircase of trash encounters punctuated by set-piece fights the player
remembers and narrates. It is the payoff for the chain-alert dread the normal
enemies build.

NOTE ON EXISTING WORK: boss-encounter-facility-overseer.txt already specs a
deep, standalone Overseer fight. THIS document is the umbrella SYSTEM (boss-floor
cadence, shared phase/HP-bar/intro framework, the roster of three) and treats
that file as the detailed Overseer reference. When implementing the Overseer,
read both; this doc's Overseer section is the condensed contract, the other file
is the long form. The Corruptor and Hell Baron are specced fully here.

GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
FLOOR ENTRY
1. World finishes a descent (depth increments). World checks: depth % 5 == 0?
   If yes, the generator was told to emit a BOSS ARENA floor (see TECHNICAL
   NOTES → arena generation). The floor has: one spawn 'p', a short approach
   corridor, one door, one large arena room, the boss seeded at the arena
   centre, and the '>' exit hidden/disabled until the boss dies.
2. Player walks the approach corridor and opens the arena door.

AWAKEN + INTRO
3. Crossing the arena threshold trips the boss alert (proximity OR LOS, same as
   any enemy). On wake:
   a. The arena door LOCKS behind the player (arena lock — door state forced to
      LOCKED, treated as a wall, reopens only on boss death). No retreat.
   b. A one-time BOSS INTRO plays (see VISUAL DESIGN → Intro): screen letterbox
      bars slide in, boss name + epithet typed out, a 2.0s pause, bars slide out.
      Input is locked ONLY during the intro (the only non-turn-based moment in
      the game, justified as a cutscene; ~2.5s total).
   c. The full-screen BOSS HP BAR fades in at the top of the screen and stays
      until the boss dies.

PHASE 1 COMBAT
4. The fight runs on the tick model: every completed player action fires one
   tick; the boss acts exactly once per tick. Phase 1 attack pattern runs (per
   boss, below). The player reads telegraphs, repositions using arena cover
   (columns 'P'), and trades damage.

PHASE TRANSITION
5. When boss HP crosses its phase-2 threshold (default 50%), a PHASE TRANSITION
   fires on the NEXT boss tick:
   a. Boss becomes briefly INVULNERABLE for PHASE_TRANSITION_TURNS (default 1
      tick) while a transition animation/roar plays.
   b. A transition banner shows the phase name ("THE OVERSEER OVERRIDES THE
      DECK").
   c. The boss switches to its phase-2 attack pattern. HP bar gets a second
      colored segment marker.

PHASE 2 COMBAT
6. Phase-2 pattern (more aggressive / area-denial, per boss). Player keeps
   fighting until boss HP hits 0.

DEATH + REWARD
7. On boss death:
   a. A death animation plays (collapse + screen flash); a kill-screen flavor
      line shows in the HUD toast.
   b. The arena door UNLOCKS. The '>' exit tile is enabled (reveal animation).
   c. GUARANTEED RARE DROP spawns on the boss's last tile (a billboard pickup
      decal): a weapon/upgrade keyed to that boss (see per-boss REWARD).
   d. A large XP bounty is granted (if XP system present).
8. Player collects the drop, steps onto '>', descends. Run continues.

DEATH OF PLAYER
- If the player dies in the arena it is a normal permadeath (roguelike_order_1).
  The death screen reads "KILLED BY THE OVERSEER ON FLOOR 5" — bosses make
  memorable death lines.


SHARED BOSS FRAMEWORK
=====================
All three bosses share one base type and differ by data + an attack-pattern
strategy object.

PHASES
- Every boss has exactly 2 phases in v1.
- phase2HpThreshold: fraction of max HP at which phase 2 begins (default 0.50).
- On crossing, a one-tick invulnerable transition fires (PHASE_TRANSITION_TURNS).
- Phase is stored as an int (1 or 2) on the boss; the active attack pattern is
  selected by phase.

ATTACK PATTERN MODEL (turn-based, telegraph → resolve)
- Each phase owns an ordered list of MOVES. A move is one of:
    TELEGRAPH(move, turnsUntilResolve) — boss shows wind-up, marks danger tiles.
    RESOLVE(move) — danger tiles deal damage / spawn hazards this tick.
    REPOSITION — boss moves/teleports per its movement rule.
    SUMMON(type, count) — spawn minions (Corruptor only in v1).
- The boss cycles its phase's move list, one move per tick. Telegraphed moves
  give the player a readable window to step out of the marked tiles (the core
  fairness contract: the player should always know why they died).

DANGER-TILE TELEGRAPH (shared primitive — reused by all bosses)
- A boss can mark a SET of tiles as "will be hit in N turns."
- Marked tiles get a pulsing red floor overlay (drawn via the floor/decal
  pipeline or a sprite overlay; NO render() allocations — pre-allocate the
  overlay sprite/pool at floor load).
- On resolve, any actor (player OR enemy) standing on a marked tile takes the
  move's damage. This makes telegraphs usable to bait, and makes summoned
  minions vulnerable to the boss's own area attacks (emergent comedy).

FULL-SCREEN BOSS HP BAR
- A wide bar across the top of the screen (margin 80px each side, ~24px tall),
  drawn in HudRenderer AFTER everything (it is HUD). Shows boss name (left),
  current/max as a fill (center), phase markers (notch at phase2HpThreshold).
- Color: phase 1 = boss accent color; phase 2 = the same color but pulsing
  brighter / shifted toward white-hot.
- Fades in on awaken, fades out on death.


BOSS 1 — THE OVERSEER (depth 5)
================================
Reference: boss-encounter-facility-overseer.txt for full lore/flavor. Condensed
contract here.

IDENTITY: Security/logistics robot fused into the facility control core. Cannot
leave the arena (bolted in). Sprite: hulking turreted control-core mech with a
single glowing optic and articulated arm-cannons.

PHASE 1 — RANGED LASERS (HP 100%..50%)
- Move list (one per tick, repeating):
  1. TELEGRAPH "LASER LANE", 1 turn: marks every tile in the row OR column the
     player currently occupies (cross-arena beam line). The optic glows + a
     thin aim-line draws down the lane.
  2. RESOLVE: tiles in the lane take LASER_DAMAGE (default 20). Player must have
     stepped off the lane (sideways) during the telegraph tick.
  3. TELEGRAPH "TWIN LANE", 1 turn: marks the player's row AND column (a plus).
  4. RESOLVE.
- The Overseer does NOT move in phase 1 (it is a stationary turret). The arena
  columns ('P') are the only safe break in lanes — the player learns to fight
  from cover.

PHASE 2 — MELEE CHARGE (HP 50%..0%)
- The Overseer rips free of some mounts and gains 1-tile-per-tick movement
  toward the player (cardinal pathfinding, existing enemy pathing).
- Move list:
  1. REPOSITION: step one tile toward the player.
  2. Every 3rd tick instead: TELEGRAPH "RAM", 1 turn — marks the up-to-3 tiles
     directly ahead of it in its facing.
  3. RESOLVE RAM: those tiles take CHARGE_DAMAGE (default 30); the Overseer
     lurches forward 2 tiles (stops at walls/columns). Smashing into a column
     stuns it for 1 tick (punish window).
- Faster, scarier, but readable: the player kites around columns, baits the ram
  into a column, unloads during the stun.

REWARD (guaranteed): "Overseer Optic" — unlocks/grants a charge-laser weapon OR,
if weapon roster is full, a permanent +1 weapon damage upgrade token. Plus a
medkit. Big XP.

NUMBERS (placeholders, tune):
  Max HP 300 | phase2 at 50% | LASER_DAMAGE 20 | CHARGE_DAMAGE 30
  Ram cooldown 3 ticks | column-stun 1 tick.


BOSS 2 — THE CORRUPTOR (depth 10)
==================================
IDENTITY: A UAC lead scientist, mutated by exposure to the breach signal he was
studying. Hunched, swollen, dripping; trails a personal cloud of acidic vapor.
He is mobile and cowardly — he keeps distance, hides behind the monsters he
births. Sprite: a bloated lab-coated figure, one arm fused into a birthing sac,
acid bubbling at the hem. Reuses the 'Corruptor' enemy aesthetic family but
boss-scaled.

PHASE 1 — SUMMONER (HP 100%..50%)
- The Corruptor flees: each tick it REPOSITIONS one tile to maximize distance
  from the player (existing flee/pathing logic).
- Move list:
  1. REPOSITION (flee).
  2. Every SUMMON_COOLDOWN ticks (default 3): SUMMON(minion, 2) — spawns 2 trash
     minions on free tiles adjacent to the Corruptor (reuse base Corruptor or
     Ghoul enemy type). Cap total live minions at MINION_CAP (default 5); over
     cap, summon is skipped.
- The player must choose: chase the boss through its own minions, or clear
  minions first and let the boss heal distance. Classic add-management.

PHASE 2 — ACID SPLASH (HP 50%..0%)
- The birthing sac ruptures; he stops summoning and goes on the offensive with
  area denial.
- Move list:
  1. REPOSITION (still kites, but less).
  2. Every 2nd tick: TELEGRAPH "ACID BURST", 1 turn — marks a 3x3 block of tiles
     centered on the PLAYER'S CURRENT tile. Green bubbling overlay.
  3. RESOLVE: marked tiles take ACID_DAMAGE (default 15) AND convert to a
     lingering TOXIC POOL hazard for ACID_POOL_DURATION turns (default 3) — this
     reuses the toxic-pool hazard from roguelike_order_20. The arena slowly
     fills with acid; standing still kills you.
- Any minions still alive from phase 1 also take acid damage (they don't dodge
  well) — the player can lure the Corruptor into nuking his own swarm.

REWARD (guaranteed): "Mutagen Vial" — a permanent stat boost choice (e.g. +max
HP or +1 to a stat, routed through the levelup-boon system) OR a rare acid/toxic
weapon. Plus an armor pickup. Big XP.

NUMBERS (placeholders, tune):
  Max HP 450 | phase2 at 50% | SUMMON_COOLDOWN 3 | MINION_CAP 5
  ACID_DAMAGE 15 | ACID_POOL_DURATION 3 turns | acid burst cooldown 2 ticks.


BOSS 3 — HELL BARON (depth 15)
===============================
IDENTITY: An armored greater demon — the first creature the breach sent that was
purpose-built for war, not a corrupted human or machine. Slow, immense, armored;
hits like a truck. Sprite: a towering bipedal demon with bone plating, cloven
hooves, and fire leaking between armor seams. The "you have arrived in Hell now"
boss.

PHASE 1 — FIREWALL (HP 100%..50%)
- The Baron advances slowly (REPOSITION one tile toward player every OTHER tick;
  it is heavy). It controls space with fire.
- Move list:
  1. REPOSITION (every other tick).
  2. Every FIREWALL_COOLDOWN ticks (default 4): TELEGRAPH "FIREWALL", 1 turn —
     marks a full row OR column line of tiles (whichever the player is NOT on,
     chosen to cut the arena and herd the player). Cracks of fire on the floor.
  3. RESOLVE FIREWALL: the marked line becomes a WALL OF FIRE hazard for
     FIREWALL_DURATION turns (default 4) — lingering fire tiles dealing
     FIRE_DAMAGE (default 12) per turn entered/stood on. Splits the arena;
     player must navigate the shrinking safe space. Reuses the fire hazard from
     hazard-chain-reactions / roguelike_order_20.
  4. Melee: if the player is adjacent in the Baron's facing, CLEAVE for
     CLEAVE_DAMAGE (default 35), telegraphed 1 turn.

PHASE 2 — ENRAGE (HP 50%..0%)
- The Baron sheds armor (visual: plates fall, more fire). It gets FASTER and
  hits HARDER:
  - REPOSITION every tick (no longer every other).
  - FIREWALL_COOLDOWN drops to 2.
  - CLEAVE_DAMAGE +50% and cleave reach extends to the 3 tiles ahead.
  - Movement/attack speed expressed in turn terms only (more actions per player
    turn is NOT allowed — instead, fewer cooldown ticks and bigger hits).
- An "ENRAGED" banner + the boss sprite goes red-hot. This is the run's hardest
  sustained pressure: the safe space is tiny and the Baron is on top of you.

REWARD (guaranteed): "Baron's Heart" — a major run-defining artifact (e.g. a
fire-immunity charm, a big permanent damage/HP package, or the game's heaviest
weapon if roster allows). Plus a large medkit + armor. Largest XP bounty.

NUMBERS (placeholders, tune):
  Max HP 700 | phase2 at 50% | FIREWALL_COOLDOWN 4 (p1) / 2 (p2)
  FIREWALL_DURATION 4 turns | FIRE_DAMAGE 12/turn
  CLEAVE_DAMAGE 35 (p1) / 52 (p2) | move cadence every-other-tick (p1) / every-tick (p2).


BOSS ROSTER BEYOND DEPTH 15
===========================

    scaledHp = baseHp * (1 + BOSS_DEPTH_HP_SCALE * (depth - firstAppearanceDepth))

TECHNICAL NOTES
===============
PACKAGES / CLASSES
- entity/boss/Boss.java — extends the base Enemy. Adds: int phase, float
  phase2HpThreshold, BossAttackPattern phase1Pattern, phase2Pattern, boolean
  invulnerable, String bossName, String bossEpithet, String killLine.
- entity/boss/BossAttackPattern.java — interface: nextMove(Boss, Player, Level,
  int tickIndex) → BossMove. Concrete: OverseerPhase1Pattern, OverseerPhase2-
  Pattern, CorruptorPhase1Pattern, ... (6 classes).
- entity/boss/BossMove.java — small struct/enum result: {TELEGRAPH, RESOLVE,
  REPOSITION, SUMMON, MELEE} + payload (tile set, damage, summon type/count).
- entity/boss/DangerTileSet.java — holds marked (column,row) tiles + turnsLeft +
  damage + onResolve effect (damage / spawn hazard). Lives on the boss; advanced
  one step per tick.
- world/BossFloorController.java — on descent, if depth % 5 == 0, instantiates
  the correct Boss from a depth→boss table, applies depth scaling, wires the
  full-screen HP bar, arena-lock, intro, and the death→reward→exit-enable flow.
- render/BossHudRenderer.java (or extend HudRenderer) — full-screen boss HP bar
  + intro letterbox + phase/enrage banners. Pre-allocate all sprites/glyph
  buffers at floor load (NO render() allocations).

LEVEL.JAVA / GENERATION
- Generator must emit a boss-arena layout for depth % 5 == 0: one open room
  (e.g. 11x11 to 15x15 tiles) with 2-4 columns ('P') for cover, an approach
  corridor, one door, boss seeded at center, '>' placed but DISABLED until kill.
- Use a new generator mode flag, NOT a new tile symbol — the boss is an Enemy
  seeded at a tile, the arena is normal walls/floor/columns. NO NEW TILE SYMBOL
  REQUIRED for the arena itself.
- The boss enemy needs a spawn marker. Existing enemy spawns are '1'-'5'. A boss
  is selected by depth, so it can reuse a normal spawn marker placed at center
  and have BossFloorController REPLACE that spawned enemy with the depth-correct
  boss — OR add a dedicated boss spawn digit. RECOMMENDATION: reserve digit '6'
  as a generic "boss spawn (resolved by depth)" marker.
  => requires new symbol '6' — add to docs/tile-symbols.txt (ENEMY SPAWN
     MARKERS section) and Level.java/LevelLoader before implementing.

TICK INTEGRATION
- Boss subscribes to TickEventBus like any enemy: on each tick, if not in
  intro/transition, advance DangerTileSet timers (resolve expired sets), then
  ask the active phase pattern for nextMove and execute it. Exactly one move per
  tick. Phase transition checked after damage is applied each tick.

ARENA LOCK
- Reuse DoorManager: on boss awaken, force the arena door to a LOCKED state that
  isWall()-returns-true and ignores interaction until BossFloorController flips
  it open on death. If keycard doors present, can author it as a 'B' blue door
  pre-opened then re-locked by the controller.

VISUAL DESIGN
=============
INTRO (cutscene, ~2.5s, only input-locked moment)
- Black letterbox bars slide in top and bottom (each ~120px).
- Boss name types out large center-screen (e.g. "THE OVERSEER"), epithet smaller
  below ("SITE LOGISTICS CORE OVR-1").
- The boss sprite does an idle "wake" twitch / optic flare.
- 2.0s hold, then bars retract, HP bar fades in, control returns.

FULL-SCREEN HP BAR
- Top of screen, BOSS_HP_BAR_MARGIN inset. Dark backing, accent-colored fill,
  thin notch at the 50% phase line. Boss name top-left of the bar.
- Overseer accent: cyan-white. Corruptor: toxic green. Baron: ember orange-red.

DANGER TELEGRAPH OVERLAY
- Marked tiles: pulsing translucent overlay in the boss's accent color (red for
  imminent-1-turn). Drawn via floor/decal overlay sprites, pre-allocated.

PHASE/ENRAGE BANNERS
- Short centered banner ("THE BARON IS ENRAGED") for ~1.2s on transition, plus a
  full-screen color flash (red for Baron, green for Corruptor, white for
  Overseer).

DEATH
- Boss sprite collapse animation + a white screen flash; '>' exit reveal pulse;
  kill-line HUD toast (Overseer: "OVERSEER OFFLINE. Productivity has decreased
  by 100%."; Corruptor: "The experiment is concluded."; Baron: "The gate stands
  open. Keep descending.").


BALANCE NOTES
=============
- All HP/damage/cooldown numbers above are PLACEHOLDERS — tune against the
  player's expected DPS and HP at the relevant depth.
- Fairness contract: every damaging move is telegraphed at least 1 full turn
  ahead. A player who reads the telegraph and steps off the marked tiles takes
  zero damage from that move. Deaths must feel earned.
- Phase 2 must NEVER be expressed as "more actions per player turn" (breaks the
  turn model). Express escalation only via bigger hits, shorter cooldowns, and
  faster (per-tick) movement.
- Boss HP should require roughly 2-4 minutes of careful play, not a war of
  attrition — bias toward dodge-heavy, punish-window combat over bullet-sponge.
- Guaranteed drops must matter: a boss kill should visibly change the run.


INTERACTIONS
============
- DEPTH COUNTER (level transitions): the sole trigger. depth % 5 == 0 → boss
  floor; the '>' exit is gated on the kill.
- TRAPS & HAZARDS (roguelike_order_20): Corruptor acid pools and Baron firewalls
  REUSE the toxic-pool and fire hazard primitives — implement those first, or
  stub them. Pressure plates etc. are NOT placed in arenas (boss is the threat).
- KEYCARD DOORS: arena door can be a 'B' blue door; arena-lock reuses door state.
- XP/LEVELING (roguelike_order_11): bosses grant a large XP bounty; the levelup
  boon choice can be the delivery vehicle for Mutagen Vial / Baron's Heart.
- INVENTORY/SHOP (roguelike_order_18): boss reward weapons/upgrades enter the
  same item/inventory pipeline; a shop is a good pre-boss "loadout" beat (place
  a shop on the floor BEFORE a boss floor when possible).
- ENEMY HEALTH BARS: the per-enemy bar is suppressed on bosses (the full-screen
  bar replaces it); minions still show normal bars.
- PERMADEATH (roguelike_order_1): boss death lines feed the death screen.
