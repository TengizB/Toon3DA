OVERVIEW
========
RESEARCH_LAB is a high-tech UAC research facility room: alien specimen study,
energy/plasma experiments, and a humming AI core under holographic light. It is
the "what were they MESSING with?" room. Distinct from SERVER_ROOM (pure data /
racks) and COMMAND_CENTER (oversight / weapon racks): the Research Lab is where
the invasion was INVITED — experiments went wrong, a containment tank cracked, an
AI started thinking for itself. Tonally it pairs cold clinical light with arcing
energy and biological wrongness.

This document ADDS only. It reuses existing pipelines (per-char wall switch in
WallRenderer, billboard PropRenderer, RoomType decoration in LevelGenerator). No
new runtime architecture. It defines:
  - 1 new room type:  RESEARCH_LAB
  - 2 new walls:      D (holo-data display wall), F (force-field arc wall)
  - 3 new solid props: I (specimen tank), W (holo-workstation), J (AI core node)
  - 1 new walkable decal: e (energy scorch / plasma burn)

All chosen symbols are confirmed FREE in docs/tile-symbols.txt (uppercase D F I J
W available; lowercase e available). See TECHNICAL NOTES for the exact budget.


ROOM ATMOSPHERE
===============
You step in and the light changes character. Standard corridors are warm-yellow
fluorescent; the Research Lab is lit cold cyan-white from above, with a secondary
unstable cyan/magenta flicker thrown by holographic displays and a force-field
barrier somewhere in the room. The far wall is a wall of glowing data (wall 'D').
A bank of fluid-filled specimen tanks ('I') line one side, each lit from within,
some intact, some cracked and leaking onto the floor. A central holographic
workstation ('W') projects a slowly rotating wireframe — a molecule, a creature,
a facility map. At the heart of the room sits the AI core node ('J'), a tall
column of stacked translucent processing slabs pulsing with light, faintly
talking to itself. A blue force-field barrier ('F') seals off a back alcove
(usually the loot or the worst enemy). Plasma burns ('e') scorch the floor where
an experiment, or its subject, broke loose. It should read as: brilliant science,
abandoned in a hurry, now half-demonic.

The emotional beat: AWE then DREAD. The tech is beautiful; the cracked tank and
the scorch marks tell you the beauty already failed.


GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
1. Player enters a RESEARCH_LAB room (generated room template).
2. Room READ: cold cyan light + holo-flicker + tanks + AI core = "research; expect
   energy enemies and a sealed reward."
3. Player approaches the back alcove and finds it sealed by a force-field wall 'F'.
   The force-field is treated as a SOLID wall (blocks movement + raycast) BUT it
   is keyed to the AI core node 'J' (see INTERACTIONS).
4. Player presses W into the AI core node 'J' (interact, like a terminal). 'J'
   toggles to a "shut down" state -> every 'F' tile in the SAME room becomes
   passable floor (force-field drops). This advances the world by one turn.
   - If 'J' interaction is not implemented in phase 1, ship 'F' as decorative-only
     (always solid, no alcove gating) and the room still works. See OPEN QUESTIONS.
5. Dropping the force-field RELEASES whatever was behind it (commonly a Vortex Eye
   '2' or Revenant '5') AND opens access to a high-value pickup (medkit 'H',
   security vest 'A', or a keycard). Risk/reward: shut down the core to loot, but
   you wake what it was containing.
6. Cracked specimen tanks 'I' may, on a per-room roll, be "live" — stepping
   adjacent on the turn you damage one (splash) can spawn a Crawler '4' (phase 2,
   optional). Phase 1: tanks are pure atmosphere props.
7. Plasma scorch decals 'e' are pure visual; they hint where the force-field arc
   or a previous energy discharge struck — a breadcrumb toward the alcove.


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
=================
The core decision: "Do I shut down the AI core?" Shutting it down is the only way
past the force-field to the reward, but it unleashes the contained threat and
silences a (planned, optional) ambient benefit the running core might provide.
This is the roguelike "meaningful choice under pressure" pillar made physical: a
locked door whose key is also an alarm. Secondary feel: the room teaches enemy
expectation through architecture — cyan light = energy enemies (Vortex Eye), so a
trained player pre-loads the right weapon before opening anything.


NEW WALL TYPES (2)
==================

--- WALL 'D' — HOLO-DATA DISPLAY WALL ---
THE READ: "this is where the thinking happened — live data, still running."
SOLID: yes (Level.isWall -> true). Full-height wall.
VISUAL: near-black glass panel base. Over it, glowing cyan UI elements rendered
as a procedural Pixmap: 3-4 stacked horizontal data bars of varying fill, a grid
of small square "readout" cells (some lit cyan, a few amber-warning), a faint
rotating-arc ring motif in one quadrant, and 2-3 scrolling-look horizontal text
lines drawn as dotted cyan runs. A subtle vertical scan-band one shade brighter.
Edges framed by a thin dark steel bezel (4px) so adjacent plain walls read as a
seam, not a blur.
COLORS (RGBA):
  DATA_GLASS_BLACK   = (0.04, 0.05, 0.07, 1.0)   panel base
  DATA_CYAN_DIM      = (0.10, 0.45, 0.55, 1.0)   unlit/dim UI element
  DATA_CYAN_BRIGHT   = (0.35, 0.95, 1.00, 1.0)   lit bar / active readout
  DATA_AMBER_WARN    = (1.00, 0.65, 0.15, 1.0)   warning cells (use sparingly, ~10%)
  DATA_BEZEL_STEEL   = (0.18, 0.20, 0.24, 1.0)   frame
HEIGHT: 1.0x (standard full wall).
FREQUENCY: 1 full wall (or 1 wall band) per RESEARCH_LAB, usually the far wall the
player faces on entry. Max 1-2 tiles elsewhere per level. Looks best lit by the
room's cold-cyan lighting; do NOT place behind unlit 'u' floor (kills the glow).

--- WALL 'F' — FORCE-FIELD ARC WALL ---
THE READ: "energy barrier — do not touch / sealed beyond."
SOLID: yes by default (Level.isWall -> true). May become passable when gated by an
AI core 'J' (see INTERACTIONS). Full-height.
VISUAL: mostly transparent-dark base suggesting you can ALMOST see through it,
overlaid with a vertical lattice of bright electric-blue arcs: 3-4 vertical
"current" lines that zig slightly, crossed by 2 horizontal energy bands, plus a
scatter of small bright nodes where lines intersect. Two heavy emitter posts down
the left and right edges (dark steel with a glowing slot) anchor the field so it
reads as a deliberate barrier, not a glitch. Per-frame brightness can pulse via
the existing global-tick / flicker hook if available (optional polish).
COLORS (RGBA):
  FIELD_VOID         = (0.03, 0.05, 0.10, 1.0)   near-transparent dark base
  FIELD_ARC_BLUE     = (0.30, 0.70, 1.00, 1.0)   electric arc lines
  FIELD_ARC_WHITE    = (0.85, 0.95, 1.00, 1.0)   arc hot core (1px highlight)
  FIELD_NODE_GLOW    = (0.55, 0.85, 1.00, 1.0)   intersection nodes
  FIELD_EMITTER_STEEL= (0.16, 0.18, 0.22, 1.0)   edge posts
HEIGHT: 1.0x.
FREQUENCY: 1-3 contiguous tiles forming a single barrier across an alcove mouth,
1 barrier per RESEARCH_LAB max. Never use as a structural exterior wall (player
expects to be able to drop it; a force-field on the map edge is a trap).


NEW SOLID PROPS (3)
===================
All block movement (Level.isPropSolid -> true), rendered as billboard sprites by
PropRenderer, procedural Pixmap, Nearest filter, disposed in dispose().

--- PROP 'I' — SPECIMEN TANK (incubator) ---
NAME: Specimen tank
VISUAL: a tall cylindrical glass tube in a dark steel base + cap, filled with a
glowing fluid (sickly green or cold blue, randomize per instance via seed). A
suspended SILHOUETTE floats inside — a curled humanoid or a clawed organic blob,
drawn as a darker translucent mass with one or two faint highlight edges. A column
of rising bubble dots. Two randomized states: INTACT (clean glass, full fluid) or
CRACKED (jagged white crack line, fluid level lower, a thin drip and an 'e' or 'O'
decal likely placed on an adjacent floor tile by the generator).
HEIGHT MULTIPLIER: 0.85x wall height (tall, reads as a containment cylinder).
COLORS (RGBA):
  TANK_FLUID_GREEN   = (0.25, 0.85, 0.45, 1.0)   variant A fluid
  TANK_FLUID_BLUE    = (0.30, 0.65, 0.95, 1.0)   variant B fluid
  TANK_GLASS_SHEEN   = (0.80, 0.92, 0.95, 0.5)   vertical highlight streak
  TANK_STEEL_BASE    = (0.20, 0.22, 0.26, 1.0)   base + cap
  TANK_SPECIMEN_DARK = (0.06, 0.10, 0.08, 0.85)  floating silhouette
  TANK_CRACK_WHITE   = (0.90, 0.95, 0.98, 1.0)   crack line (cracked variant)
USAGE: place in a row of 2-4 along one interior side wall (against the wall, not
mid-floor). The signature furniture of the room. ~30% of placed tanks roll CRACKED.

--- PROP 'W' — HOLO-WORKSTATION (projection table) ---
NAME: Holo-workstation
VISUAL: a low dark console table (steel + a strip of cyan controls) projecting a
floating HOLOGRAM above it: a slowly implied wireframe drawn as thin cyan lines
forming a rotating ring/molecule/creature outline, brightest at the base where the
projector cone fans up, fading toward the top. A soft cyan glow halo sells the
light source. Because it is a billboard, the hologram is a fixed iconic shape (no
true rotation needed); the projector-cone gradient sells the "floating" read.
HEIGHT MULTIPLIER: 0.70x wall height (console is low, hologram extends the visible
sprite upward but stays below full wall height).
COLORS (RGBA):
  HOLO_TABLE_STEEL   = (0.18, 0.20, 0.24, 1.0)   console body
  HOLO_CTRL_CYAN     = (0.35, 0.95, 1.00, 1.0)   control strip
  HOLO_PROJ_BRIGHT   = (0.55, 0.95, 1.00, 0.9)   cone base / brightest hologram
  HOLO_PROJ_DIM      = (0.25, 0.70, 0.85, 0.5)   upper faded hologram
USAGE: 1 per RESEARCH_LAB, placed near room center as the visual anchor. Walkable
tiles all around it so the player can circle it. Distinct from 'T' computer
terminal (T is a flat wall-screen console; W projects volumetric light).

--- PROP 'J' — AI CORE NODE ---
NAME: AI core node
VISUAL: a tall vertical stack of 5-6 translucent processing slabs (like glowing
glass bricks) in a dark heatsink frame, with a bright vertical light seam running
up the center and small status LEDs (mostly cyan, one or two amber). Faint
horizontal scan pulses travel up the seam. The tallest/heaviest prop in the room —
it should dominate sightlines.
HEIGHT MULTIPLIER: 0.95x wall height (near-full; it is the "boss furniture").
COLORS (RGBA):
  AICORE_FRAME_DARK  = (0.12, 0.13, 0.16, 1.0)   heatsink frame
  AICORE_SLAB_GLASS  = (0.20, 0.55, 0.70, 0.8)   processing slab body
  AICORE_SEAM_BRIGHT = (0.60, 0.95, 1.00, 1.0)   central light seam
  AICORE_LED_CYAN    = (0.35, 0.95, 1.00, 1.0)   status LED
  AICORE_LED_AMBER   = (1.00, 0.65, 0.15, 1.0)   warning LED (1-2 only)
USAGE: exactly 1 per RESEARCH_LAB. INTERACTABLE (treated like a terminal): facing
it and pressing W toggles a SHUT_DOWN state that drops the room's 'F' force-field
barrier (see INTERACTIONS). If interaction isn't implemented in phase 1, it is a
pure tall light prop.


NEW WALKABLE DECAL (1)
======================
--- DECAL 'e' — ENERGY SCORCH (plasma burn) ---
NAME: Energy scorch
VISUAL: an irregular blackened scorch blotch on the floor with a desaturated outer
ring, overlaid by 3-5 thin radiating ELECTRIC-blue arc filaments (lichtenberg-
style branching) that have not fully faded, and a couple of bright white-hot
flecks at the center. Reads as: "energy discharged here recently."
HEIGHT MULTIPLIER: 0.18x wall height (flat ground decal, same band as '.' / 'O').
COLORS (RGBA):
  SCORCH_BLACK       = (0.05, 0.05, 0.06, 1.0)   burn center
  SCORCH_RING        = (0.20, 0.18, 0.16, 1.0)   desaturated outer ring
  SCORCH_ARC_BLUE    = (0.40, 0.75, 1.00, 1.0)   residual arc filaments
  SCORCH_HOT_WHITE   = (0.90, 0.96, 1.00, 1.0)   center flecks
USAGE: walkable. Scatter 2-5 per RESEARCH_LAB, biased toward (a) tiles adjacent to
cracked specimen tanks 'I' and (b) tiles leading up to the force-field 'F' alcove,
forming a visual breadcrumb. Distinct from 'O' oil pool (teal, no arcs) and '.'
blood (red, no arcs).


ROOM GENERATION RULES
=====================
SIZE: medium-to-large. Interior floor footprint 6x5 to 9x7 tiles (excluding the
wall ring). Smaller than LARGE/COMMAND_CENTER, bigger than a STANDARD room. Needs
room for one tank row + central workstation + an alcove.

LIGHTING: floor inside the room uses a mix of lit ' ' and a few flickering 'f'
tiles near the AI core and force-field (sells unstable energy). Avoid 'u' unlit
floor in the main body (it kills the cyan glow reads). 1-2 'f' tiles max.

LAYOUT TEMPLATE (conceptual, generator fills a rectangle then decorates):
  - Far wall (opposite the entrance): a band of 'D' holo-data display wall.
  - One long interior side: a row of 2-4 'I' specimen tanks flush to the wall,
    ~30% rolled CRACKED, each cracked tank places an 'e' or 'O' on an adjacent
    floor tile.
  - Room center: exactly 1 'W' holo-workstation, walkable on all 4 sides.
  - A back alcove (2x2 or 1x3 recess): mouth sealed by 1-3 'F' force-field tiles;
    inside the alcove place 1 high-value pickup (H / A / a keycard) AND 1 enemy
    spawn behind the field (commonly '2' Vortex Eye, sometimes '5' Revenant).
  - Exactly 1 'J' AI core node, placed against a wall in line-of-sight of the
    force-field alcove (it is the "key").
  - 2-5 'e' energy scorch decals scattered, biased toward cracked tanks and the
    alcove approach.

PROP PLACEMENT DENSITY (per room, placeholder values — TUNE):
  'I' specimen tank      : 2-4   (one wall-aligned row)
  'W' holo-workstation   : exactly 1 (center anchor)
  'J' AI core node       : exactly 1 (wall, near alcove)
  'e' energy scorch      : 2-5
  'D' holo-data wall     : 1 band / far wall (3-6 tiles wide)
  'F' force-field        : 1-3 contiguous (single alcove barrier)
Keep total solid props <= ~7 so the room stays navigable (no boxed-in player).

ENEMY SPAWNS (thematic fit): cold cyan energy theme leans ENERGY/PSYCHIC enemies.
  - Primary: '2' Vortex Eye (energy/psychic — perfect thematic match, behind 'F').
  - Secondary: '5' Revenant (a failed experiment / reanimated subject) behind 'F'
    in higher-difficulty rooms.
  - Ambient body of room: 0-2 of '3' Ghoul or '4' Crawler loose (leaked specimens).
  - Avoid heavy industrial spawns here (this is not a reactor floor).
RARITY: RESEARCH_LAB should be UNCOMMON — at most 1 per generated level, sometimes
0. It is a set-piece room, not filler.


VISUAL DESIGN SUMMARY (palette discipline)
==========================================
The room's identity is COLD CYAN + ELECTRIC BLUE with AMBER WARNINGS as accent and
sickly green/blue tank fluid as the only organic color. This palette deliberately
contrasts the warm-yellow corridors and the red/orange of hazard/armory rooms, so
the Research Lab reads instantly different on sight. Force-field and AI core share
the electric-blue family on purpose: the player learns "blue glow = energy system =
the AI controls it." Amber is the only warning color and must stay rare (~10% of
lit elements) or it loses meaning.

INTERACTIONS (with existing / planned systems)
==============================================
1. AI CORE 'J'  <->  FORCE-FIELD 'F': the core is the "key" to the field. Shutting
   the core down drops the field, granting the reward but releasing the contained
   enemy. This is the room's central risk/reward and the reason both share the
   electric-blue palette. (Pairs with any keycard-door / interactable-terminal
   system already designed in uac-keycard-doors-security-tiers.txt.)
2. SPECIMEN TANK 'I'  <->  HAZARD CHAIN REACTIONS: cracked tanks naturally invite
   the planned hazard-chain system — a live tank that is shot could splash and
   spawn a leaked Crawler '4' or apply a brief slow/toxic floor (ties into
   hazard-chain-reactions-fire-toxic-explosive.txt). Phase 1: atmosphere only.
3. RESEARCH_LAB LIGHTING  <->  LABORATORY-LIGHTING / colored-light systems: the
   cold-cyan room tint and holo-flicker should drive the existing/planned colored
   lighting (laboratory-lighting.txt, lighting-system-overhaul.txt). 'D' walls and
   'W'/'J' props are emissive accents the lighting pass can sample.
4. ENERGY SCORCH 'e'  <->  ENEMY THEMING: scorch breadcrumbs reinforce that this
   floor hosts ENERGY enemies (Vortex Eye), training the player to swap to plasma/
   energy weapons (plasma-gun / plasma-rifle / railgun docs) before opening 'F'.
5. ROOM TYPE TABLE  <->  level-generation-improvements RoomType system: slots in as
   one more RoomType.RESEARCH_LAB with its own decorate method; no generator
   rewrite, just a new low-weight entry capped at 1 per level.


OPEN QUESTIONS

1. Does an interactable-prop hook exist yet (press W facing a prop -> event)? If

   or fast-follow the hook. Which?
2. Should the dropped force-field 'F' stay down for the rest of the run, or
   re-raise when the player leaves the room? (Recommend: stays down — one-way.)
3. Live specimen tanks (shoot 'I' -> splash/spawn): in-scope for phase 1 or defer
   to the hazard-chain system? (Recommend: defer; ship tanks as atmosphere first.)
4. Should the running AI core 'J' grant a passive room buff (e.g. brief mini-map
   reveal / scan) that you LOSE by shutting it down, deepening the trade-off?
   (Recommend yes as phase 2 polish — strengthens the meaningful-choice pillar.)
5. Force-field VFX: animate brightness via the global-tick/flicker hook, or static
   Pixmap? (Static is cheaper and respects "no allocations in render()"; animate
   only by swapping/scaling brightness, never reallocating the Pixmap.)
6. Should 'D' holo-data walls ever appear OUTSIDE the Research Lab (e.g. 1-2 in a
   Command Center)? (Recommend: very rare, to keep the read mostly lab-exclusive.)
