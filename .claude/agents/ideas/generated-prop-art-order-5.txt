OVERVIEW
--------
The security camera ('#', sprite prop_camera; GENERATED placement: one or two per security / command / research
room kit, at a CORNER or on the ENTRY_WALL, flush - see RoomBlueprints) today is a box standing on the floor,
which no camera does. It becomes the Organization's eye HANGING FROM THE CEILING: a mount in the ceiling, an
arm, and a camera head that slowly pans the room with a blinking recording lamp. It is the first CEILING-MOUNTED
prop, so this order also adds the one small runtime idea that needs: a prop that hangs casts no floor shadow.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - make_camera() in props.py + its PROPS row; the sheet camera.prop.png / .glow.png / .prop.json.
  - GeneratedPropRegistry: a CEILING-MOUNTED registration (e.g. registerCeilingSprite("prop_camera", "camera"))
    and a query (isCeilingMounted) - data, not a switch; the next hanging prop (lamps, cable bundles) is one row.
  - PropRenderer: a ceiling-mounted sheet gets NO contact shadow (both the main and the dynamic pass). Nothing
    else in the draw changes: the frame is a full-height billboard whose lower part is transparent.
  - RenderConstants.PROP_CAMERA_HEIGHT 0.80 -> 1.00 (the billboard spans floor to ceiling so its top row is the
    ceiling line; only the top part is painted).
  - Delete PropRenderer.generateCameraTexture and its TextureGeneratorRegistry row (PS6).
  - Docs: guide 14 (ceiling-mounted props: the canvas rule + the registry flag; 14.7 entry), tile-symbols '#',
    CLAUDE.md propvisual row, enemy-art SKILL.md.
OUT
  - Gameplay: the '#' tile stays SOLID and keeps its placement and every rule (V4). It sits flush to a wall or in
    a corner, so the blocked floor tile under it is not a pathing problem. Making hanging props walkable is a
    gameplay change for a later, separate request.
  - No camera behaviour (detection, alarms, tracking the player) - the pan is a cosmetic loop.
  - Other ceiling things (lamps, ducts) - later orders, which reuse this order's flag.


RULES
-----
  R1  A ceiling-mounted prop's canvas is FULL HEIGHT (height 1.0): its top row is the ceiling line, the object is
      painted in the top part, everything below it is fully transparent (so the per-frame opaque-column masks keep
      it out of the occluder record below the camera, and enemies behind it are not cut).
  R2  isCeilingMounted(sheet) -> contact shadow fraction 0, in BOTH prop passes. Body / glow / muzzle passes are
      unchanged.
  R3  The camera head must stay readable at 3-4 tiles: a clear dark silhouette against the ceiling and one
      bright lamp.


VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
SECURITY CAMERA ('#', full height 1.0, canvas 128 x 256, 24 frames @ 0.1 s, loops; painted in rows 0-~110)
  Cold steel with a hot accent - the Organization's eye:
  - MOUNT: a gunmetal ceiling plate flush with the top row (rivets), a cable loop running back up into the
    ceiling, a short darkmetal drop arm with a clamp collar ending in a pivot yoke.
  - HEAD: an angled camera housing hanging from the yoke - livery_dk shroud with a sun hood over the lens, a
    stencilled serial (SEC-02). The lens: dark glass, a NEON CYAN iris ring, a small white glint.
  - REC LAMP: a HOT IRON (master D4) recording lamp on the housing, blinking.
  - PAN: the head pans slowly left and right over the loop (the housing foreshortens and the lens slides across
    it, then back - seamless), the yoke turning with it; the arm and plate stay still.
  - SCAN (subtle): a faint neon cyan cone of light fanning down from the lens and sweeping with the pan, GLOW
    sheet only, low alpha (cap ~0.25) so it reads as "being watched" without covering the room. Drop it if it
    reads as a beam weapon.
  Static parts = one kit render; the head is re-rendered per pan pose (cache identical poses); lamp, glint and
  scan painted per frame.


TECHNICAL NOTES
---------------
  - props.py: make_camera(); the mount + arm are a cached kit render, the head a kit render per pan pose (a pose
    parameter: yaw in -1..1 -> housing width scale, lens offset, yoke turn). Seamless: yaw = sin(2*pi*k/frames).
  - render/propvisual/GeneratedPropRegistry: a Set<String> of ceiling sheets beside the existing maps,
    filled by registerCeilingSprite; GeneratedPropSheet or GeneratedPropSheets exposes isCeilingMounted(sheet).
  - PropRenderer: in both passes, if sheet != null && ceiling-mounted -> skip drawContactShadow.
  - TilesetGfxBootstrap / TextureGeneratorRegistryTest need no edit (expectedGeneratorIds already drops every
    migrated sprite).
