VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
DESIGN BRIEF - IRON STALKER (INSECT, MINI_ELITE, 1.10 walls, melee, BUFF_SELF + AREA_STRIKE)
  What it is: a harvest-line insect the Organization tried to use as a guard animal - bred from the
  planet's tissue, then FITTED with riveted iron plates to make it survive the pens. It outgrew the
  plates; chitin has grown around and over their edges.
  Silhouette: TALL and upright, unlike the low, wide tick - a reared-up stance on four rear legs, the
  front pair raised as two long scythe blades (the AREA_STRIKE is a sweep of both). Narrow waist, a
  plated thorax, a small armoured head with a cluster of ichor eyes low on it.
  Materials: chitin / chitin_dk body, gunmetal + darkmetal riveted plates bolted across the thorax and
  the blade bases, bone-pale blade edges, ichor eyes and a glowing ichor seam where chitin has split
  around a plate (BUFF_SELF reads as that seam - nothing animates, it is a static promise).
  Story detail: a stencilled pen number on the largest plate, and a snapped restraint collar on the
  neck.
  Must differ from the Harvest Tick at two tiles: vertical vs horizontal mass, blades vs mandibles,
  plates vs blisters.
