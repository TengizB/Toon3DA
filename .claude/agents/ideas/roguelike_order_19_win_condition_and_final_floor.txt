TITLE: Win Condition & Final Floor — Boss, Extraction, Endless
---

OVERVIEW
========
A roguelike needs a finish line, not just a death line. This doc defines the
ENDGAME: a unique hand-crafted final floor (floor 15, configurable) built as an
arena, the floor-15 Hell Baron boss (from order 16), an EXTRACTION POINT that
activates only after the boss dies, the win cutscene/trophy screen on reaching
extraction, and the post-win fork: ENDLESS MODE (keep descending forever, ever-
rising difficulty) or NEW RUN. Reaching extraction is the canonical "you won this
incursion" state and the top of the leaderboard.

This is the payoff for the entire descent (pacing & escalation pillar). After
fifteen floors of one-way commitment, the player earns a real ending — escape the
facility — and then chooses whether to walk away a winner or push their luck into
the endless dark.

  entity, its attacks, arena hooks, and defeat trigger. THIS doc does NOT redefine
  the boss; it consumes the boss's "defeated" event to activate extraction and

- level-transitions-stairs-down.txt (IMPLEMENTED): the descent system. The final

- roguelike_order_23 (difficulty/scaling): the final floor uses the depth-15
  scaling profile for any minions; Endless mode feeds N>15 into the endless-overflow
  terms defined there.
- roguelike_order_24 (run stats): a won RunRecord (won=true) flows to the trophy
  screen and tops the board.
- roguelike_order_25 (main menu): post-win "New Run" routes through that menu's
  new-run flow; this doc owns the "Endless / New Run" fork screen.
- roguelike_order_1 (permadeath): winning is the OTHER terminal state besides death;
  it clears the active run identically (run is over, record persists).

NEW TILE SYMBOL — DECISION REQUIRED
===================================
Extraction is rendered as a glowing exit PORTAL. The existing '>' is the
descent-stairs (continues the run); extraction is semantically different (ends the
run in victory) and only the FINAL floor has it. Two clean options:

OPTION A (PREFERRED, NO NEW SYMBOL): reuse the existing '>' portal tile on the
final floor, but the final-floor / depth-15 context makes World treat stepping on
'>' as EXTRACTION (win) rather than DESCENT. The visual is re-skinned to the green
extraction portal on the final floor only. This requires zero new symbol and zero
new tile plumbing — only a depth-conditional branch in the step-on-'>' handler and
a final-floor portal re-skin in PropRenderer/WallRenderer.

OPTION B (new symbol): add a dedicated extraction tile (e.g. an unused uppercase
like 'K' or 'V', both listed free in docs/tile-symbols.txt). REQUIRES NEW SYMBOL —
add to docs/tile-symbols.txt AND Level.java (walkable special, like '>') AND the
renderer lookup AND the SYMBOL BUDGET count BEFORE implementing. Only choose this
if the final floor must ALSO contain a normal '>' for some reason (it should not).

RECOMMENDATION: Option A. The portal stays gated/inert until the boss dies; on the
final floor a '>' step = win. No symbol churn.

GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
1. Player descends to floor 15 (depth == FINAL_FLOOR_DEPTH). World loads the unique
   hand-crafted arena level (NOT procgen — a fixed asset file, see TECHNICAL NOTES)
   and sets a finalFloor flag.
2. The floor 15 banner reads differently ("SUBLEVEL 15 — THE OVERSEER'S THRONE" or
   similar) and the Hell Baron (order 16) is present in the arena.
3. The EXTRACTION PORTAL exists on the floor from the start but is INERT/SEALED: it
   renders dim/closed and stepping on its tile does nothing (or shows "SEALED —
   THREAT DETECTED"). The boss must be dealt with first.
4. Player fights the Hell Baron per order 16. On the boss's DEFEATED event:
     a. World flips extractionActive = true.
     b. The portal "powers up": the dim tile becomes a bright green pulsing rift,
        an audio sting + facility announcement plays ("CONTAINMENT BREACHED —
        EXTRACTION ONLINE"), and a HUD objective updates ("REACH EXTRACTION").
5. Player navigates to the active portal and steps onto its tile (W/S/Q/E). On the
   settled-tile frame, World checks: finalFloor && extractionActive && tile is the
   portal -> WIN.
6. WIN: World sets GameState.phase = WON (a terminal phase alongside DEAD), freezes
   the sim (pollInput early-return like death), plays a short win cutscene/beat
   (real-time, zero turns), builds a won RunRecord (order 24), and shows the WIN /
   TROPHY screen.
7. POST-WIN FORK (on the win screen or a follow-up screen): the player chooses
     - ENDLESS MODE: the SAME marine/run continues; World re-enables descent (the
       arena gets a now-active '>' or the portal becomes a descent again), sets an
       endless flag, and floors past 15 feed N>15 into order-23's endless-overflow
       scaling (ever-rising HP/damage, no further win). Endless ends only at death;
       depth keeps climbing as the score.
     - NEW RUN: clears the run (it's already recorded as a win) and routes to the
       main-menu new-run flow (order 25).
8. Death on the final floor (or in Endless) is a normal death (order 1) — the run
   is logged with its reached floor; a won=true is NOT awarded unless extraction was
   reached.

FINAL FLOOR STRUCTURE (how it differs from procgen floors)
==========================================================
- HAND-CRAFTED, FIXED ASSET: a single .txt level file (e.g.
  assets/levels/final_floor_15.txt) authored by game-level-designer, NOT generated.
  Deterministic so the climax is a designed encounter, not RNG.
- ARENA SHAPE: a large open central chamber (the throne) for the Hell Baron fight,
  with enough room to kite (cardinal-only movement needs space + cover columns 'P').
  Surround with a short approach corridor (tension build) and a few cover/pillar
  features for the boss dance.
- THE PORTAL: placed at a deliberate spot — ideally across the arena from the
  approach, so post-boss the player must cross the (now-safe) battlefield to
  extract, a quiet victory-lap beat.
- ATMOSPHERE: lean on the demonic wall set (gore walls 'G', blast walls 'X'), unlit
  'u' / flickering 'f' floors for dread, emergency-red pulse (emergency-red-alert-
  pulse.txt) before the boss, flipping to green portal light after.
- NO '>' DESCENT initially (the run ends here). Endless mode re-enables descent
  post-win.
- Reuses ALL existing renderers and the standard tile grid — it is just a fixed,
  carefully composed level, not a new system.

PLAYER EXPERIENCE
=================
Floor 15 should feel different the moment you arrive — the banner, the throne arena,
the sealed portal taunting you across the room. The Hell Baron is the wall the whole
run was preparing you for. The instant it dies and the portal roars to life is the
catharsis; the walk to extraction across the corpse-strewn arena is the exhale. Then
the fork: bank the win (top of the board, EXTRACTION achievement) or get greedy and
dive into Endless knowing every floor now wants you dead harder. That choice IS the
roguelike's long hook.

Turn-based fit: the boss fight is normal turn-based combat (order 16). Extraction
activation is an event fired on a resolved turn (boss death), never mid-tick. The
win check rides the settled-tile step (one action = the step that enters the portal).
The win cutscene/beat is real-time/cosmetic, zero turns. Endless re-enabling descent
just flips a flag read by the existing descent handler.

No allocations in render(): the portal state is a boolean toggling a pre-loaded
sprite/tint. The win screen builds its strings once in show() (winning is a rare
one-shot). The fixed arena loads through the normal level pipeline.

VISUAL DESIGN
=============
- Sealed portal: a dark, dormant rift in the floor/wall — dim red containment glow,
  flickering, "SEALED" energy lattice across its mouth. Reads as "off."
- Active portal: bright green-cyan vertical rift, strong pulse, particle shimmer,
  a beckoning light spill across nearby tiles. The visual flip on boss death is the
  reward beat — make it loud (light + audio sting + screen flash easing to green).
- Final-floor banner + objective: "SUBLEVEL 15 — THE OVERSEER'S THRONE"; objective
  HUD line toggles "DESTROY THE OVERSEER" -> "REACH EXTRACTION."
- Win cutscene/beat: the 3D view brightens/whites-out as the marine steps into the
  rift (cosmetic plane/tint lerp), then cut to the trophy screen. A grim-triumph
  flavor line ("You walked out. UAC will pretend the facility never existed.").
- WIN / TROPHY screen (gold/green palette, distinct from the red death screen):
  header "EXTRACTION SUCCESSFUL", full RunRecord (order 24 fields), any unlocked
  achievements (EXTRACTION, NIGHTMARE_CLEAR), and the fork buttons:
  "[ENDLESS DESCENT]  [NEW INCURSION]". A gold frame + the marine silhouette
  standing.
- Endless transition: the portal re-skins to a descent rift again; the banner from
  floor 16+ reads "SUBLEVEL N — NO BOTTOM" or similar, signaling there's no win now.

BALANCE NOTES
=============
- FINAL_FLOOR_DEPTH = 15 is the headline pacing number; it must align with order-23
  scaling so a well-built run can realistically survive to and beat the boss at the
  depth-15 profile. Verify against order-23's NORMAL survival target (~floor 6-8
  median death) — winning should be HARD but achievable, not statistically near-zero.
- The boss difficulty (order 16) is the true gate; this doc only ensures the floor/
  extraction framing is fair (the player can clearly see the objective, the portal
  is reachable post-boss, no unavoidable arena death).
- Endless overflow scaling (order 23) should ramp steeply enough that Endless has a
  natural wall (depth becomes the bragging score) without an artificial cap — death
  is the only end of Endless.
- Winning awards the EXTRACTION achievement and tops the board; on Nightmare it
  awards NIGHTMARE_CLEAR — keep these as the prestige peaks.

INTERACTIONS
============
1. Boss (order 16 / facility-overseer): defeated event is the single trigger for
   extraction activation. No boss = portal stays sealed.
2. Level transitions: final floor loads a fixed file instead of procgen; Endless
   re-enables descent past 15.
3. Difficulty/scaling (order 23): final-floor minions use the depth-15 profile;
   Endless feeds N>15 into endless-overflow terms.
4. Run stats / leaderboard (order 24): won RunRecord (won=true) -> trophy screen +
   top of board + EXTRACTION/NIGHTMARE_CLEAR achievements.
5. Main menu (order 25): New Run from the fork routes through the new-run flow;
   the win screen must be able to return there.
6. Permadeath (order 1): WON is a terminal phase parallel to DEAD; both clear the
   active run and persist a record. Dying on floor 15 (no extraction) is a normal,
   non-won death.
