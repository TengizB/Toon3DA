VISUAL DESIGN - STRATUM E, THE CORE (mood core_white_violet)
------------------------------------------------------------
A nacre-bone lattice grown like a neural mesh (the approved prototype), the hollows between filled with
deep membrane and soul light. The lattice's struts are larger and calmer than the prototype (it read
busy at range): fewer, thicker struts, bigger hollows.
  BASE (x3)     the lattice at three phases of the same mesh (the struts meet across tile edges).
  STRUCTURE     a thick trunk strut, vertical, like a spine.
  LIGHT         a synapse node: a bright soul core with dendrites (travelling pulse).
  SERVICE (x2)  dead Organization cable runs, pulled taut through the lattice; a bore hole with a
                drill sleeve, the lattice healing over it.
  MARKER        the last Organization plate: a survey marker ("SEAM" in stencil), cracked.
  FEATURE       a memory window: a large hollow where the soul light shows a faint human shape
                (the original self - story/03-the-planet.md, how the planet gives memory back).
  BREACH        none - fallbackRole BASE (the planet does not breach itself).
  CLINICAL / CONTAINMENT / MACHINERY / SECURITY / DATA
                the Organization's last forward camp, bolted into the lattice: one panel each of
                stratum A's design (livery, steel) clamped onto nacre - the facility as the foreign
                body now.
Palette: nacre, flesh_dk (hollows), membrane, soul, bone; livery/gunmetal only on the camp walls.
Purple is semantic (V5): the violet comes from the MOOD, the materials stay pearl-grey and pale.
