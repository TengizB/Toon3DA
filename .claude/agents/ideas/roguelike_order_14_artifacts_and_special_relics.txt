TITLE: Artifacts & Special Relics — Passive Always-On Boons
---

OVERVIEW
========
Artifacts are rare, named relics with powerful always-on PASSIVE effects — no
activation, no charges, just a permanent rewrite of one rule for the rest of the
run. The marine can carry at most TWO at once, so finding a third forces a brutal
swap decision. Artifacts are the run's "build-defining" loot tier: a weapon perk
shifts a number, an artifact bends a system. They are found in secret rooms,
dropped by bosses, or seeded only on deep floors (5+), so seeing one at all is a
small event.

This belongs to the Doom-RPG-on-DCSS/Hades brain: the Demon Core that doubles
your melee but bleeds you on every kill is a classic devil's-bargain relic; the
UAC Dog Tags are a clean stacking-economy boon. Two-slot scarcity keeps every
pickup a genuine decision rather than a strictly-better grab.

GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
PICK UP:
1. An artifact exists in the world as a ground item (glowing pedestal sprite) or
   appears at a boss's death tile.
2. Player steps onto the tile (W/S/Q/E). On the settled-tile frame, World checks
   the tile's ground item; if it is an artifact, control routes to ArtifactSlots.
3. If a free slot exists (fewer than 2 held): the artifact is equipped instantly,
   its onEquip() hook fires, a HUD banner reads "RELIC BONDED — <NAME>", and the
   passive is now live. Costs NO extra turn beyond the step that grabbed it.
4. If both slots are full: the world enters a brief RELIC_SWAP phase (turn engine
   frozen, same as the level-up overlay). The player sees the two held relics +
   the new one and picks "drop slot 1", "drop slot 2", or "leave it". Choosing a
   slot fires onUnequip() on the dropped relic (it returns to the floor as a
   ground item so it is recoverable until you leave the floor) and onEquip() on
   the new one. "Leave it" resumes with no change.

WHILE HELD (passive hooks, fired by the relevant system, never polled in render):
- onEquip(player, runState) / onUnequip(...): apply/remove static modifiers.
- onKill(enemy): Demon Core bleeds 1 HP; Dog Tags do nothing here (XP hook is in
  the award path, not the kill path).
- onFloorStart(): Invincibility Frame Module re-arms its one free hit.
- onIncomingDamage(amount) -> modifiedAmount: Invincibility Module zeroes the
  first hit of the floor then disarms; Berserker checks the HP threshold.
- onOutgoingDamage(amount, isMelee) -> modifiedAmount: Demon Core x2 melee;
  Berserker x2 all damage while below threshold.
- onFire(weapon) -> shotPlan: Reactor Shard adds a second projectile and doubles
  the ammo cost.

THE SIX SHIP-FIRST ARTIFACTS
============================
Each: NAME | RARITY | passive | hook(s) used | the cost/tension.

1. UAC DOG TAGS        | Uncommon  | +15% all XP gain.
   Hook: ProgressionManager.awardExperience multiplies by 1.15.
   Cost: none — the "safe" pick, but it occupies a precious slot a build-defining
   legendary could use. Opportunity cost IS the cost.

2. NIGHT VISION GOGGLES | Uncommon | World renders full-bright; darkness/unlit
   floors and flickering tiles lose their shading penalty.
   Hook: a global lightingOverride flag read by the shading multiplier in
   WallRenderer / FloorCeilingRenderer.
   Cost: removes atmosphere/dread and the lurker-blindspot advantage demons get in
   the dark; trades fear-management for total information.

3. REACTOR SHARD       | Rare      | Weapons fire 2 projectiles per shot but
   consume double ammo.
   Hook: onFire() returns a shotPlan with projectileCount=2, ammoCost x2.
   Cost: ammo economy halves. With order-4 scarcity this is a real gamble.

4. BERSERKER IMPLANT   | Rare      | While HP <= 20% max, all outgoing damage x2.
   Hook: onOutgoingDamage checks player.healthFraction() <= 0.20.
   Cost: rewards staying in the death zone — pure risk-for-reward, Doom Berserk
   energy. Synergy bait with low-HP relics; suicidal with the Demon Core.

5. DEMON CORE          | Legendary | Melee damage x2; lose 1 HP on every kill
   (any source, including ranged — the Core feeds on death itself).
   Hook: onOutgoingDamage (isMelee -> x2) + onKill (player loses 1 HP, cannot
   self-kill below 1 — clamps to 1, never lethal).
   Cost: a slow bleed that punishes clear-the-room sprees; pairs viciously with
   Berserker (drop yourself into the x2 window on purpose).

6. INVINCIBILITY FRAME MODULE | Legendary | Immune to the FIRST incoming hit of
   each floor.
   Hook: onFloorStart() re-arms; onIncomingDamage zeroes one hit then disarms for
   the floor.
   Cost: it only saves you once per floor, so it rewards pushing into the unknown
   (scout aggressively, eat the free hit) but is dead weight in a long grind on
   one floor. Reads level-transitions depth changes for the re-arm.

RARITY TIERS
============
- UNCOMMON: small, safe, stat-flavored boons (Dog Tags, Night Vision).
- RARE: build-shaping with a clear cost (Reactor Shard, Berserker Implant).
- LEGENDARY: system-bending devil's bargains (Demon Core, Invincibility Module).
Rarity drives drop weight and visual treatment (see VISUAL DESIGN) and gates which
sources can roll which tier (see BALANCE NOTES).

PLAYER EXPERIENCE
=================
Artifacts are the moments a run gets a personality. Finding the Demon Core on
floor 6 makes you rethink the next ten rooms — do I lean melee, do I grab
Berserker to live in the x2 zone? The two-slot cap means the third relic is the
hardest pickup in the game: you must drop power to gain power. The HUD indicator
keeps the bargain visible so a death never feels mysterious — "the Core bled me to
4 HP and the Revenant finished it" is a fair, legible story.

- A central damage path for onIncoming/onOutgoing hooks — Player.applyDamage and
  the weapon's marchShot/damage-apply step. Build the hook seams there.

New / modified classes:
- items/Artifact.java (NEW, ...toon3d.items — add this package; document it in
  CLAUDE.md package table): enum or class-per-relic. Recommend an enum
  ArtifactType holding id, displayName, Rarity, and lore string, plus an
  ArtifactEffect interface with the lifecycle hooks (onEquip, onUnequip, onKill,
  onFloorStart, onIncomingDamage, onOutgoingDamage, onFire, awardExperience
  multiplier). Keep effects as small stateless strategy objects; per-run mutable
  flags (e.g. Invincibility "armed this floor") live in...
- items/ArtifactSlots.java (NEW): holds Artifact[2], the equip/unequip/swap logic,
  and per-relic per-run state flags. Owned by the run/World, not the Level (relics
  persist across floor descents; they are run-scoped, not level-scoped).
- world/World.java: route artifact pickups; add RELIC_SWAP phase (mirror the
  LEVEL_UP overlay freeze pattern). Fire onFloorStart() from the descent path.
- entity/Player.java: damage path calls ArtifactSlots.modifyIncoming/Outgoing.
- Weapon fire path: call ArtifactSlots.onFire() to get the shotPlan (projectile
  count + ammo multiplier).
- render/HudRenderer.java: draw the two relic indicator icons (see VISUAL DESIGN).
- screen/ or render/: a RelicSwapOverlay (reuse the level-up overlay rendering
  scaffolding rather than a new Screen).

Turn-based fit: equipping is free (rides the pickup step). The swap overlay
freezes the turn engine and costs zero turns, identical to the level-up overlay.
All passive hooks fire inside already-resolved turn steps — no real-time, no
cooldowns.

No allocations in render(): relic icons and labels are pre-built once on
equip/swap; the HUD draw is texture/quad draws only.

VISUAL DESIGN
=============
- Ground form: a small glowing pedestal billboard, color-coded by rarity —
  uncommon = cool steel-cyan glow, rare = violet, legendary = pulsing blood-amber.
  A slow vertical bob + a faint particle shimmer sells "this is special." Reuse
  the PropRenderer billboard pipeline; no new tile symbol.
- HUD indicator: two small relic icon slots tucked into the LEFT panel under the
  status line (or just inside the left edge of the centre gap if the left panel is
  full). Each shows the relic glyph + a 1px rarity-colored border. Empty slot =
  dim outline. On a conditional relic going live (Berserker entering the x2 zone,
  Invincibility absorbing its hit) the icon flashes once.
- Demon Core kill-bleed: a tiny red HP tick + a brief vignette pulse so the cost
  is felt, not just numerically applied.
- Night Vision: when equipped, a faint green tint over the 3D view + the lighting
  override; on unequip the darkness snaps back (deliberately ominous).
- Relic swap overlay: three large cards (held A, held B, new), rarity-bordered,
  with name + one-line passive + cost text; "[1]/[2] DROP   [LEAVE]" prompts.

BALANCE NOTES
=============
- Drop sources / weights (PLACEHOLDER, tune in playtest):
  * Secret rooms (floor 1-4): uncommon only, ~60% to contain a relic at all.
  * Secret rooms (floor 5+): uncommon ~55% / rare ~40% / legendary ~5%.
  * Boss drops: guaranteed one relic, rolled rare ~70% / legendary ~30%.
  * Deep-floor ground seeding (floor 5+): at most ONE per floor, low base chance
    (~12%), rarity scaling up with depth.
- Two-slot cap is the master balance lever — do NOT raise it without re-tuning;
  three slots makes legendaries stack into degenerate combos (Demon Core +
  Berserker + Reactor Shard).
- Demon Core HP cost must clamp to never drop the player below 1 from a kill
  (death comes from enemies, not your own relic — fairness pillar).
- Berserker x2 window stacking with Demon Core x2 melee = x4 melee: this is an
  intentional "glass-cannon" combo and is fine BECAUSE you live at <=20% HP and
  bleed on every kill. Flag for playtest; it should feel insane, not safe.
- Reactor Shard with order-4 ammo scarcity should feel like a gamble, not a
  no-brainer — verify ammo drops do not trivialize the double cost.

INTERACTIONS
============
1. XP/Leveling (order 11): Dog Tags multiply ProgressionManager.awardExperience.
   Night Vision removing darkness indirectly speeds clears -> more XP/min.
2. Difficulty scaling (roguelike_order_23): deeper floors gate higher relic tiers;
   Nightmare's no-ground-pickups rule means deep-floor ground seeding of artifacts
   is suppressed there, pushing relics onto boss/secret sources only.
3. Combat / weapons: Reactor Shard rewrites the fire path; Berserker & Demon Core
   ride the outgoing-damage path; Invincibility & Demon Core ride incoming/kill.
4. Permadeath & run stats (order 1 / order 24): relics held at death are logged in
   the run record ("RELICS BONDED: Demon Core, Berserker Implant") for the story.
5. Secret rooms & bosses: primary high-tier sources, making exploration and boss
   fights the path to build-defining loot.
