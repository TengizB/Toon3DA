IMPLEMENTATION NOTES (what shipped)
  - Power budget + invariant live in BalanceConfig (LEVEL_UP_BUDGET_PP=12,
    LEVEL_UP_BUDGET_TOLERANCE=0.15, LEVEL_UP_CARDS_OFFERED=3) and are surfaced
    through GameBalance. PP is computed in GameMath.damagePerTurnPowerPoints /
    effectiveHitPointPowerPoints (offence in reference DPT, survivability in
    reference eHP via GameMath.effectiveHitPoints).
  - The flat 3-boon level-up is replaced by a branching CARD system:
      progression/UpgradeCardPool  — OFFENSE / DEFENSE / SUSTAIN / UTILITY
      progression/UpgradeCard      — 10 cards (incl. 3 trade-off cards), each a
                                     pure-data bundle of stat deltas + its own
                                     estimatedPowerPoints() priced to ~budget.
      progression/UpgradeCardDeck  — draws 3 distinct, distinct-LEVER cards,
                                     biased toward the player's emerging build.
    The old flat boons survive as VITALITY / COMBAT ARMOR / HOLLOW POINTS (one
    pool option each), re-priced to budget (armour 18->24, damage 8->6, HP=25).
  - LevelUpOverlayRenderer now renders the drawn cards generically (per-pool
    accent colours, a TRADE-OFF tag) and reports the tapped index. World draws a
    fresh hand when the overlay opens and applies the chosen card's deltas, then
    advances the level and records the pick (deck bias).
  - STRENGTH/MARKSMANSHIP damage multipliers were wired into the central damage
    point (EnemyManager.applyDamageTo, which already knows melee vs ranged) so
    those attribute cards actually change damage. Dodge / action-speed / flat
    reduction / max-HP / max-armour / flat-damage levers were already live.
  - Living table: BalanceReport.printUpgradeCardTable() prints every card's PP and
    flags any out-of-band card; mirrored into docs/game-balance-authority.txt.
  - DESIGN DEVIATION: the catalogue of conditional WEAPON abilities (bleed/
    executioner/hemorrhage/crit/point-blank/…) was NOT folded into the level-up
    deck. Those are per-weapon roll effects (WeaponAbility/AbilityResolver) and
    re-homing them into player-level cards would have been a much larger change
    than the "choice layer + re-pricing" this idea scopes. The level-up deck is
    built on the four ATTRIBUTES + the three flat boons (the "passive backbone"
    the idea names), which already deliver the three required archetypes. Folding
    the weapon-ability pile into the card pools is a clean follow-up.

Idea 5 of 6 in the balance series (see balance_order_1 for the full list).
Depends on ideas 1 & 2 (especially the power-budget contract, item D). This is
the user's explicit "build diversity / synergy" request, scoped so it does NOT
break the balance the earlier ideas establish.

=== THE PROBLEM ===

Two issues, both balance problems disguised as design problems:

  1. Level-up offers FLAT, mutually-redundant boons: HP_BOOST (+25 HP),
     ARMOR_BOOST (+18), DAMAGE_BOOST (+8 flat dmg). These are PURE UPGRADES with
     no trade-off, so the "choice" is just "which stat do I want more of." The
     project's own roguelike pillars demand "interesting trade-offs, not pure
     upgrades" (pillar 4) and "run identity through build diversity" (pillar 5).
     The current system violates both.

  2. There is a LARGE ability catalogue already in GameBalance (crit, armor
     pierce, executioner, lifesteal, cleave, point-blank, marksman, rhythm,
     static discharge, legendary signatures...) attached to weapon tiers/levels.
     The raw material for builds EXISTS — but nothing makes the player CHOOSE
     between synergistic paths, and nothing guarantees the choices stay on the
     balance curve.

This idea turns the existing ability pile into a CHOICE system that produces
distinct builds while staying inside the idea-2 power budget.


=== THE CORE PRINCIPLE — SYNERGY WITHIN A FIXED BUDGET ===

Build diversity that BREAKS balance is just power creep. The trick: each
individual upgrade costs the SAME power budget, but upgrades COMBINE
multiplicatively, so the FUN (a build coming together) is free while the POWER
stays bounded.

  Define UPGRADE VALUE in a common currency — "power points" (PP), expressed as
  %-increase to either effective DPT or eHP (idea 2 quantities):
      ppOf(upgrade) = (newPower - oldPower) / oldPower * 100
  A level-up grants a FIXED budget, e.g. LEVEL_UP_BUDGET_PP = 12.
  RULE: every offered choice must cost ~LEVEL_UP_BUDGET_PP (within +/-15%). So no
  choice is a no-brainer (all cost the same) — they differ in KIND, not amount.

  THE INVARIANT THAT KEEPS BUILDS BALANCED (ties to idea 2 depth invariant):
      total PP granted by level L  ==  L * LEVEL_UP_BUDGET_PP   (build-agnostic)
  Whatever the player picks, their total power at level L is the same; only its
  SHAPE differs. This guarantees playerPowerAtDepth stays on the 0.9-1.2 band
  (idea 2) REGARDLESS of build. That is how you get diversity without disbalance.


=== WHERE SYNERGY COMES FROM (without exceeding budget) ===

Synergy is NOT extra raw power — it is CONDITIONAL power that only pays off if
the player builds and plays toward the condition. Each piece is budget-priced
for its AVERAGE proc rate; a player who engineers the condition gets above-
average value, a player who ignores it gets below-average. The budget is set to
the average, so the SYSTEM stays balanced while individual builds spike.

  Example synergy chain (all pieces already in GameBalance):
    BLEED-on-hit (Rend)  +  "Executioner" (bonus dmg to low-HP targets)  +
    "Hemorrhage Harvest" (heal on kill).
    -> A "bleed/finisher" build: apply bleed, let it tick targets low, execute
       for burst, heal on the kill. Each piece is budget-neutral alone; together
       they define a playstyle. A player who instead took crit/point-blank pieces
       plays a "burst gunslinger" with the SAME total PP but a different feel.

  Synergy SOURCES to exploit:
   - status effects feeding finishers (bleed/poison -> executioner)
   - on-kill effects feeding sustain (lifesteal/hemorrhage -> aggression)
   - positional bonuses rewarding playstyle (point-blank shotgun vs marksman
     long-range — these PULL the build toward a range band, which then interacts
     with which enemies you can fight cheaply, looping back to scarcity/tactics)
   - the four ATTRIBUTES (STR/AGI/TGH/MRK) as the passive backbone builds lean on


=== REPLACE THE FLAT LEVEL-UP WITH A BRANCHING CARD SYSTEM ===

  On level-up, offer 3 cards drawn from POOLS, each costed at LEVEL_UP_BUDGET_PP:
    OFFENSE pool   — conditional damage (crit, executioner, point-blank, rhythm)
    SUSTAIN pool   — lifesteal, hemorrhage, second-wind, temp-armor
    UTILITY pool   — extended mag, scavenger (ammo refund -> feeds scarcity!),
                     stagger/stun, mobility (AGI speed)
    DEFENSE pool   — eHP via TGH/dodge/armor (the OLD flat boons live here, now
                     ONE option among many, not the whole menu)
  TRADE-OFF CARDS (pillar 4): some cards grant MORE than budget on one axis but
  SUBTRACT on another (e.g. "+25% damage, -15% max HP"). These are net-budget-
  neutral but high-variance — the risk/reward choices roguelikes thrive on.

  Anti-redundancy: don't offer two cards that do the same thing; bias the draw
  toward the player's emerging build to deepen identity (or AWAY from it for
  players who want flexibility — pick one philosophy and tune).


=== KEEPING NEW ABILITIES IN BUDGET (the contract, item D, expanded) ===

When ANY new ability/upgrade is proposed:
  1. Compute its average-case ppOf() using idea-2 formulas at a reference build.
  2. If pp > LEVEL_UP_BUDGET_PP: either add a downside until net pp fits, or move
     it to a rarer source (legendary weapon) that costs more to obtain.
  3. Check for DEGENERATE SYNERGY: does it combine multiplicatively with an
     existing ability to exceed 2x budget in a reachable build? If so, cap the
     stack (e.g. crit-chance cap 0.30 already exists) or make them mutually
     exclusive in the draw pool.
  4. Add it to the living balance table (idea 2) with its pp value visible.

  The crit/pierce/executioner CAPS already in GameBalance are exactly this
  mechanism — formalize the practice so every future ability declares a cap.


=== BUILD ARCHETYPES TO VALIDATE THE SYSTEM ===

Ship the system only once at least 3 distinct, viable archetypes exist that a
player could intentionally build toward:
   - GUNSLINGER: crit + point-blank + rhythm + AGI. Burst, mobile, ammo-hungry.
   - REAPER: bleed/poison + executioner + hemorrhage + STR. Damage-over-time
     attrition with self-sustain.
   - JUGGERNAUT: TGH/armor/second-wind + cleave + slow guns. Face-tank bruiser.
Each must clear the golden-ratio fights (idea 2) but FEEL different and lean on
different weapons and different scarcity trade-offs.


=== PREREQUISITES ===
  - Ideas 1 & 2 (the power-budget math is load-bearing here).
  - The ability system already exists (GameBalance + WeaponAbility/AbilityResolver
    are present), so much of the engine is built — this idea is mostly a CHOICE
    layer (the card draw) + a re-pricing pass to a common PP budget.
  - The level-up overlay UI exists (LevelUpOverlayRenderer); extend it from 3
    fixed boons to 3 drawn cards.

=== DEFINITION OF DONE ===
  - Level-up offers budget-equal cards from multiple pools, including trade-off
    cards; the old flat boons are demoted to one pool among several.
  - LEVEL_UP_BUDGET_PP and the total-PP invariant are in BalanceConfig and
    enforced (total power at level L is build-independent).
  - At least 3 archetypes are viable and feel distinct; all pass the golden ratio.
  - Every ability has a declared pp value and cap in the living balance table.
