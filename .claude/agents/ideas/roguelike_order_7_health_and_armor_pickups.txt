TITLE: Health & Armor Pickups (floor spawns + enemy drops)

OVERVIEW
========
The concrete recovery economy of the run: six consumable pickups that restore HP
and AR, appearing as floor tiles in generated levels and dropping from ransacked
enemy corpses. Health items (Stimpack, Small Medkit, Large Medkit) refill the HP
bar; armor items (Armor Shard, Combat Armor, Security Armor) refill the AR bar.
This is the supply side of the survival pillar — every point of HP/AR the player
finds is weighed against the damage the floor will cost them.

It belongs here because permadeath (order 1) makes recovery the single most
important resource in the game. Doom RPG's design DNA is built on the medkit /
stimpack / armor-shard / armor-vest ladder, and the existing tile alphabet
already reserves four pickup glyphs for exactly this (`+ H a A`). This doc maps
those glyphs to a six-item ladder and defines how much they heal, when they
auto-use versus go into the inventory, and how densely they spawn per floor so
the descent stays tense but fair.


GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
1. The generator (order 10) stamps recovery pickups onto floor tiles using the
   existing pickup glyphs. The player sees them in the 3D view as low, glowing
   billboard decals (reusing the GroundItem billboard path from order 5).
2. Player steps W/S/Q/E onto a recovery-pickup tile. On the settled-tile block in
   PlayerController.advanceMove (same hook as keycard pickup and the order-9
   stairs check), the controller asks the pickup system to resolve the tile.
3. Resolution branches by item and by current bars:
   - INSTANT items (Stimpack, Armor Shard) ALWAYS auto-apply on step: add their
     value to HP/AR (clamped to max), clear the tile, flash the HUD bar. They
     never enter the inventory. Rationale: tiny top-ups should be frictionless.
   - MEDKITS and ARMOR VESTS (Small/Large Medkit, Combat/Security Armor) follow
     the "smart pickup" rule:
       * If picking up now would WASTE more than WASTE_TOLERANCE of the heal
         (i.e. current bar is near full), AND the player has a free inventory
         slot, the item goes into the inventory for later use (via order 6 menu).
       * Otherwise it auto-applies immediately and the tile clears.
       * If the bar is near full AND inventory is full, the item is LEFT on the
         floor and the HUD shows "[full] LARGE MEDKIT" so the player can return.
4. Whichever branch fires, stepping onto the tile is a NORMAL move = one turn.
   Picking up is free relative to the move (the move already cost the turn).
5. Enemy drops: on enemy death (order 5 corpse + LootTable), the loot roll may
   produce a recovery ItemStack. Small drops (Stimpack/Shard) spawn as a fresh
   GroundItem on the corpse tile; the player ransacks/steps to collect it under
   the same resolution rules in step 3.
6. The HUD HP and AR bars (already designed) animate toward the new value and
   pulse on gain (green flash on heal, blue flash on armor) so the gain is felt.


ITEM LADDER
===========
Glyph mapping (uses ONLY existing tile-symbols.txt pickup glyphs — NO new
symbols required):

  Health:
    +   Stimpack        +10 HP   INSTANT, auto-use, never stored.   (existing '+')
    H   Field Medkit    +75 HP   smart-pickup; the "Large Medkit".  (existing 'H')
    --  Small Medkit    +25 HP   smart-pickup.   SEE NOTE A.

  Armor:
    a   Armor Shard     +10 AR   INSTANT, auto-use, never stored.   (existing 'a')
    A   Security Vest   +100 AR  smart-pickup; the "Security Armor".(existing 'A')
    --  Combat Armor    +50 AR   smart-pickup.   SEE NOTE A.

NOTE A — the symbol budget problem:
The tile alphabet currently reserves exactly TWO health glyphs ('+' small/stim,
'H' large) and TWO armor glyphs ('a' shard, 'A' large). The six-item ladder the
brief asks for needs SIX. Two clean resolutions — pick one:

  OPTION 1 (recommended, ZERO new symbols): collapse to a 4-item floor ladder
    that matches the existing glyphs exactly:
        +  = Stimpack     (+10 HP, instant)
        H  = Medkit       (+50 HP, smart-pickup)   <- merge Small/Large into one
        a  = Armor Shard  (+10 AR, instant)
        A  = Armor Vest   (+75 AR, smart-pickup)   <- merge Combat/Security
    The "Small vs Large" and "Combat vs Security" distinction then lives only as
    ENEMY DROPS / SHOP items (which are entity-side ItemTypes, NOT tile glyphs —
    they never need a level symbol). Floor spawns stay at 4 glyphs; the full
    6-item ladder still exists in item/ItemType.java for drops and shop.

  OPTION 2 (full 6 floor glyphs): add TWO new pickup symbols. Candidates from the
    free list in tile-symbols.txt: lowercase 'i' or 'n' (small medkit) and 'V'
    (combat armor). REQUIRES NEW SYMBOLS — before implementing this option, add
    each to docs/tile-symbols.txt (PICKUPS section), to Level.java's pickup
    check, to PropRenderer's decal lookup + height, and bump the SYMBOL BUDGET
    count, all in one commit.

Recommendation: ship OPTION 1. It is fully implementable today with no symbol
churn; the six-item granularity is preserved through drops and shop where it
adds the most variety and costs nothing in the tile alphabet.


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
=================
Recovery pickups are the exhale between fights, and the agonizing math of the
descent. The instant top-ups (stim, shard) reward aggressive sweeping — grab
them on the way past, no menu, no friction. The big medkits and vests create the
signature roguelike decision: a Field Medkit glowing in a side room when you are
at 90 HP is a trap and a promise — taking it now wastes 65 HP, so the smart
pickup auto-banks it into your inventory for the floor below where you will bleed
for it. When inventory is full the player feels real loss leaving a Security Vest
on the ground. The green HP pulse and blue AR pulse give the body-memory of
"I am safer now," which is the emotional payoff permadeath needs to stay fair.


TECHNICAL NOTES
===============
DEPENDENCIES (must exist first):
- ORDER 5 (inventory-loot-ransack-system.txt): item/ItemType.java (the recovery
  ItemTypes already listed there: MEDKIT_SMALL, MEDKIT_LARGE, STIMPACK,
  ARMOR_SHARD — extend with ARMOR_COMBAT, ARMOR_SECURITY), Inventory (for the
  smart-pickup "store instead of use" path), GroundItemManager (drops on death).
  This is the hard dependency for storage + drops. WITHOUT order 5, the doc still
  works in a reduced "always auto-use, never store" form (instant resolution
  only) — graceful degrade.
- medkit-field-medicine-system.txt: the actual heal application logic. USE/auto-
  use of a medkit routes through that system so HP gain is computed in one place.
- armour-kit-system.txt: AR application + the HP/AR clamp + any armor-absorption
  rules. Armor pickups route through it the same way medkits route through field-
  medicine. Single source of truth for "how much does +100 AR actually add."
- Player.java must expose health/maxHealth and armor/maxArmor getters and a
  controlled heal()/addArmor() path (mutated only via the medkit/armour systems,
  consistent with the "never mutate Player directly" rule).

NEW / MODIFIED CLASSES:
- item/ItemType.java (order 5): add ARMOR_COMBAT (+50 AR), ARMOR_SECURITY
  (+100 AR) constants; confirm STIMPACK/MEDKIT_SMALL/MEDKIT_LARGE/ARMOR_SHARD
  exist. Each stores: restoreAmount (int), restoresArmorNotHealth (bool),
  isInstant (bool), and the existing glyph/color/name fields.
- item/RecoveryPickupResolver.java (NEW, in ...toon3d.item): the smart-pickup
  decision. Pure-ish helper:
      resolve(ItemType type, Player player, Inventory inv) -> enum
        { APPLIED, STORED, LEFT_ON_GROUND }
  Encodes: instant -> always APPLIED; otherwise compute wasted = restoreAmount -
  (max - current); if wasted > WASTE_TOLERANCE and inv has a free slot -> STORED;
  else if bar not near full -> APPLIED; else LEFT_ON_GROUND. No render state, no
  allocation; safe to unit test.
- Level.java: pickup glyphs ('+ H a A') are already recognized as pickups — no
  change for OPTION 1. (OPTION 2 only: add the new glyphs to the pickup check.)
- input/PlayerController.java: in the settled-tile completion block (same place
  as pickUpKeycardIfPresent and the order-9 isStairsDown check), call a pickup-
  recovery hook that delegates to RecoveryPickupResolver and the medkit/armour
  systems. Order: pickups resolve BEFORE the stairs check (so a pickup on the
  same tile is collected first — defensive; generator should avoid co-placing).
- render/PropRenderer.java: the recovery glyphs render as walkable billboard
  decals (height ~ LOOT_GROUND_ITEM_HEIGHT_FRACTION from order 5). Procedural
  Pixmaps: medkit = white box + red cross; stim = small white vial + red band;
  armor vest = blue/green chevron plate; shard = small blue triangle.
- HudRenderer.java: HP/AR bars react — lerp to new value, brief tint pulse on
  gain (green for HP, cyan/blue for AR). Add a transient "[full] <ITEM>" status
  line message when a pickup is LEFT_ON_GROUND.

CONSTANTS TO ADD (Constants.java; all PLACEHOLDER):
  STIMPACK_HEAL                 = 10
  MEDKIT_SMALL_HEAL             = 25
  MEDKIT_LARGE_HEAL             = 75        (OPTION 1 merged medkit: use 50)
  ARMOR_SHARD_AMOUNT            = 10
  ARMOR_COMBAT_AMOUNT           = 50
  ARMOR_SECURITY_AMOUNT         = 100       (OPTION 1 merged vest: use 75)
  RECOVERY_WASTE_TOLERANCE      = 20        (HP/AR points of waste before banking)
  RECOVERY_PICKUP_HEIGHT_FRACTION = 0.20f   (matches loot decal height)
  RECOVERY_BAR_PULSE_SECONDS    = 0.35f     (cosmetic HUD flash)

TURN-BASED FIT: a recovery pickup is collected as a side effect of a normal move
turn — it never adds an extra turn. Auto-use is instantaneous within that same
turn. Using a STORED medkit later from the inventory menu costs its own turn
(per order 5/6). All tile lookups are integer; no LOS, no float.


VISUAL DESIGN
=============
- Floor billboards (all low, walkable decals, gentle 1-tile shimmer reused from
  the flickering-floor sine so fresh pickups read at a distance):
    Stimpack ('+')      : small white vial, red band, faint warm glow.
    Medkit ('H')        : white box, bold red cross, slight green glow.
    Armor Shard ('a')   : small blue/steel triangle, cool glow.
    Armor Vest ('A')    : blue-green chest plate with a chevron, cool glow.
- HUD reaction (HP/AR bars already defined in CLAUDE.md HUD layout):
    On HP gain: HP bar fills toward new value over RECOVERY_BAR_PULSE_SECONDS and
      the fill flashes bright green once.
    On AR gain: AR bar fills and flashes cyan/blue once.
    The marine face box can flick to a healthier frame on a big heal (cosmetic,
    ties into the procedural-hud face states if those exist).
- Left-panel status line: "STIMPACK +10", "FIELD MEDKIT +75", "ARMOR +100".
  When banked: "MEDKIT -> PACK". When left on ground: "[full] MEDKIT" in dim
  amber. All transient, ~0.4s real time, cosmetic only.


BALANCE NOTES (all PLACEHOLDER — tune in playtest)
=============
- SPAWN DENSITY PER FLOOR (the key lever; consumed by the order-10 generator,
  scaled by depth per the order-9 ramp):
    Floors 1-3 (easy):    2-3 health pickups + 1-2 armor pickups per floor.
    Floors 4-6 (medium):  2 health + 1 armor (scarcer; more damage incoming).
    Floors 7-9 (hard):    1-2 health + 1 armor.
    Floor 10+ (nightmare):0-1 health + 0-1 armor (recovery is precious).
  Bias the MIX toward smaller items as depth rises (more stims, fewer Field
  Medkits on the floor) so big heals must be earned via drops/shop instead.
- ENEMY DROP CHANCE (rolled by order-5 LootTable; per enemy archetype):
    Trash (Corruptor/Ghoul):   ~8% stimpack, ~3% small medkit, ~4% armor shard.
    Mid (Crawler/Vortex Eye):  ~10% stimpack, ~6% medkit, ~5% shard, ~2% combat.
    Heavy (Revenant/boss):     guaranteed 1 medkit + chance at a vest on death.
  Drops skew SMALL/INSTANT so grinding trash never fully refills the bars; the
  bulk of recovery should come from deliberate floor pickups and the shop.
- WASTE_TOLERANCE = 20 means: a Field Medkit (+75) at >55 current HP banks
  instead of wasting; a Stimpack never banks (it is instant by rule). Tune so
  the "bank vs use" choice feels smart, not naggy.
- Target survival math: a careful full-clear of an easy floor should leave the
  marine at roughly 70-90% HP entering the stairs; a hard floor should routinely
  cost real HP even when played well. Recovery should never let the player sit at
  100/100 indefinitely past floor 3.


INTERACTIONS
============
1. Order 1 (permadeath): HP reaching 0 ends the run. These pickups are the only
   thing standing between the marine and the death screen — the central tension.
   totalDamageTaken in RunStats is unaffected by heals (it counts gross damage).
2. Order 5 (inventory/loot): big medkits/vests are stored as ItemStacks when
   banked; all enemy drops route through the LootTable. The smart-pickup "store"
   path requires a free slot, so inventory pressure (order 5) directly throttles
   how much recovery the player can hoard.
3. Order 6 (inventory UI): a banked medkit/vest is USED from the TAB menu, which
   spends a turn and routes through the same medkit/armour heal logic — identical
   result whether used from floor or from pack.
4. Order 9 (multi-floor progression): the depth tier sets spawn density and the
   small/large mix; recovery scarcity is a primary difficulty knob of the ramp.
5. Order 10 (procedural gen): the generator places these glyphs inside rooms
   (never on '>' / 'p' / doors / prop tiles), at the per-floor counts above.
6. medkit + armour-kit systems: the SINGLE place HP/AR are actually mutated —
   this doc only decides WHEN and HOW MUCH, never the raw application.


OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
1. OPTION 1 (4 floor glyphs, 6 ItemTypes via drops/shop) vs OPTION 2 (6 floor
   glyphs, +2 new symbols)? Recommend OPTION 1 — zero symbol churn, full
   granularity preserved off-tile. Confirm with whoever owns the symbol budget.
2. Does AR have its own max separate from HP, and does armor absorb a % of damage
   or just act as a second bar? Defer to armour-kit-system.txt — this doc assumes
   a simple second bar with its own max.
3. Should instant items (stim/shard) be wasted if the bar is already full (lost),
   or refused/left on ground? Recommend: at FULL bar, an instant item is LEFT on
   ground (not wasted) so the player can return for it.
4. Overheal? Doom classic lets stimpacks/medkits push past 100 with a soft cap
   and large medkits cap at 100. Recommend NO overheal for v1 (clamp to max);
   revisit as a boon ("medkits overheal to 125") in progression.
5. Auto-use vs always-store toggle as a player preference / boon? A "field medic"
   boon could flip big medkits to always-bank. Out of scope; flag for progression.
6. Should the banked-vs-used choice respect a player-held modifier key (hold a key
   on step to force-bank)? Nice-to-have; defer — the WASTE_TOLERANCE heuristic
   should cover the common case.
