OVERVIEW
--------
The captures of 2026-10-22 show the old floor and ceiling materials (flat tile grids, tread plates, neon
lattices drawn as lines) clashing with the sculpted walls. The ten 64x64 surface materials
(render/SurfaceMaterials, art-direction section 6) are regenerated with the kit so floor, wall and
ceiling read as one place. The GPU floor/ceiling pipeline (render-performance-full-hd) is NOT touched -
only where its texels come from.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - walls.py: a SURFACES group per stratum: one floor + one ceiling, 64x64, TILING IN BOTH AXES (a
    3x3 canvas, cropped to the centre), opaque, in the same light rig (a floor seen from above: the
    kit's light hits it as a flat face, which is what the pipeline expects - its shading is the
    vertex colour on top).
  - Shipped as PNGs; SurfaceMaterialRegistry loads them into its packed int[] layout (row 0 = the
    tile's south edge, column 0 = west - convert from the PNG's top-left origin once, at load) and
    builds its 4 mip levels exactly as today.
  - The ten material ids keep their names and their mood bindings (cool_steel -> deck_grating_steel ...)
    but their pixels are the strata's: A deck plates with a rubber runner + ceiling cable trays and
    strip lamps; B tile floor with drains + ceiling hook rails; C grating over soul-lit channels + fuel
    manifolds; D membrane over deck fragments + torn conduit and tissue; E lattice floor + lattice
    ceiling (matching the walls).
  - Value bands R9 (floors 0.10-0.24, ceilings 0.06-0.18 average luminance) are kept - floors and
    ceilings stay below the walls.
OUT
  - Any change to FloorCeilingRenderer's geometry, batching, mip selection or the far band.
  - Emissive floors (no glow layer for floors in this series).


RULES
-----
  R1  Each material tiles seamlessly in both axes at 1x (3x3 preview).
  R2  No moire at the far band: avoid periods of 8 texels or less (art-direction section 6 learned this).
  R3  Value bands kept (measured by a throwaway script on the shipped PNGs).
