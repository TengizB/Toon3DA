TITLE: Rimeshell Lancer — armoured ice golem with a molten lance
---

OVERVIEW
========
The first of the four ELEMENTAL GOLEM archetypes (EnemyFamily.GOLEM). A squat,
quadrupedal mound of blue-violet ice crystal with a furnace burning somewhere
inside it: two orange eyes and a molten LANCE it fires straight down a cardinal
lane.

Its whole design is one trade the player can see and exploit: the ice shell makes
it the most damage-resistant non-elite in the game, but the shell must OPEN for
the lance to fire. It is armoured OR dangerous, never both at once. Every other
ranged enemy in the bestiary punishes you for standing in its line; the Lancer
punishes you for shooting at the wrong moment.

FAMILY & ROLE
=============
  family()               EnemyFamily.GOLEM
  role()                 EnemyRole.SOLDIER          (TP band 36-66)
  positionalMultiplier() BalanceConfig.POSITIONAL_MULT_RANGED (1.30)
  isRanged()             true
  tacticalVerb()         "LANCER: armoured until it fires — bait the lance, step
                          off the lane, then burst it through the open shell."

The verb is distinct from every shipped archetype. Eye Tyrant = "break its line".
Mire Wraith = "prioritise it, don't let the DOT stack". The Lancer = "attack it on
the RIGHT TURN" — a timing puzzle, not a positioning one (though the lane still has
to be respected, which is what makes both halves solvable at once).

THE ABILITY — RIME LANCE
========================
A two-turn channelled beam down one cardinal lane, with the shell as its cost.

  TURN 1 — CHARGE (IntentVerb.WIND_UP, EnemyState.WINDING_UP)
    Precondition (all must hold at COMMIT time):
      - player is on the same tile column OR the same tile row (isSameCardinalLine
        — MANDATORY, the project's ranged rule),
      - Chebyshev distance <= attackRangeTiles(),
      - tileLineOfSightClear along the lane,
      - the Lancer is not already channelling.
    On commit it captures lanceDirectionColumn / lanceDirectionRow (the same field
    pattern as Enemy.chargeDirectionColumn/Row) and calls triggerTelegraph().
    THE SHELL OPENS: the live per-instance mitigation (Enemy.armor) drops to 0 for
    the whole of the following player turn. This is the punish window.

  TURN 2 — FIRE (IntentVerb.ATTACK_RANGED)
    Re-validation at EXECUTE time (the standard fairness rules R1-R6): the lance
    fires down the COMMITTED lane regardless of where the player now stands. If the
    player stepped off that row/column the beam simply misses — no damage, no free
    re-target. Bait-and-sidestep is the counter, and it works because the lane was
    locked a full turn earlier.
    The beam is PIERCING: it resolves every tile from the Lancer to the first WALL
    (Level.isWall / a closed door / a solid prop), not to the first body. It damages
    the player for attackDamage() AND every OTHER ENEMY standing in the lane for
    RIMESHELL_LANCE_FRIENDLY_FIRE_FRACTION (0.5) of that damage. Golems do not care
    about each other.
    The shell RESEALS at the end of this turn (Enemy.armor restored).

  COOLDOWN
    RIMESHELL_LANCE_COOLDOWN_TURNS (2) enemy turns before it may charge again. While
    on cooldown, or with no clean lane, it behaves as an ordinary slow ranged unit:
    it steps toward cardinal alignment (never diagonal-fires) and does not kite.

The friendly fire is not a bug to be balanced away — it is the second half of the
puzzle. A Lancer at the back of a corridor packed with its own family is a resource
the player can USE: pull the pack into the lane, let the beam fly, and the room
thins itself out. This is the first enemy in the game whose attack the player can
weaponise, and it is the reason the golem family is fun to fight in a corridor.

BEHAVIOUR — COMMIT / EXECUTE INTEGRATION
========================================
All of this lives in EnemyManager's existing three-phase turn (see
docs/enemy-system.txt, PRE-COMMITTED INTENT MODEL). Nothing about the turn engine
changes.

  computeNextPlan()  -> add a computeLancerPlan(enemy, ...) branch, keyed on
                        type == RIMESHELL_LANCER, sitting beside the existing
                        computeChargerPlan / computeFlankerPlan branches. Order of
                        preference: continue a committed channel > start a channel
                        (canBeginLance) > step toward cardinal alignment > wait.
  phaseBExecute()    -> executeWindUp() already covers the charge turn for the Shell
                        Brute; add an executeLanceBeam() for the fire turn (lane walk
                        + player hit + ally splash + shell reseal).

Shell state is per-INSTANCE, not per-type: set enemy.armor = RIMESHELL_SHELL_ARMOR
at spawn (beside the existing depth-scaling assignments), zero it on the charge
commit, restore it after the beam resolves. The type-level flatReduction() is only
the TP-pricing average (below) — the two must never be wired to each other.

TELEGRAPH & READABILITY
=======================
Contract: no hit the player takes should be one the telegraph did not warn about.

  - The charge turn is a full player turn of warning, drawn in the existing WIND_UP
    intent frame with the predicted-damage number (order-1/order-2 machinery, free).
  - The lane itself is drawn (see VISUAL EFFECTS) so "am I in it?" is answered
    without counting tiles.
  - The shell opening is shown on the SPRITE, not only in the intent frame, so the
    punish window is legible even when the intent icon is off-screen.
  - The lance damage (14) sits far under TELEGRAPH_MAX_UNTELEGRAPHED_HIT_FRACTION
    anyway, so the telegraph audit passes on both counts.

VISUAL EFFECTS (renderer spec — EnemyRenderer / ImpactEffectSystem)
===================================================================
Procedural only; no new texture assets beyond the shared sheet.

  1. IDLE — FROST BREATH. A slow cyan rim-light pulse on the sprite edge
     (sin(clock * RIMESHELL_FROST_PULSE_SPEED)), amplitude small. Cold, patient,
     inert-looking. Reuses the existing per-enemy tint path — no extra draw call.

  2. CHARGE TURN — SHELL OPEN. Three simultaneous, all readable at 6 tiles:
     a. A white-hot mote grows between the eyes: an additive whitePixelTexture quad
        scaled from 0 to RIMESHELL_CHARGE_MOTE_SIZE over the turn's telegraph timer.
     b. CRACK GLOW: the sprite tint lerps from ice-blue toward orange
        (RIMESHELL_SHELL_OPEN_TINT) — the body visibly "heats". This is the
        shell-is-down tell.
     c. AIM LINE: a thin cyan line drawn tile-by-tile down the committed lane from
        the Lancer to the stopping wall, alpha pulsing. Drawn in the same billboard
        pass as the sprite, at floor level, so it is occluded correctly by the
        wall z-buffer. Tile-quantised — never a free-floating diagonal.

  3. FIRE TURN — THE LANCE. An additive orange-white beam quad swept down the lane
     over RIMESHELL_LANCE_BEAM_SECONDS, with a white core and an orange falloff, plus
     a short screen shake (ImpactEffectSystem, existing) and an ember burst at each
     tile it passes through. Anything it hits gets the standard hit flash. A frost
     ring puffs off the Lancer at the muzzle — the ice/fire contrast is the identity.

  4. RESEAL. The tint snaps back to ice-blue over ~0.3s with a brief white frost
     bloom, which doubles as the "your punish window just closed" cue.

  5. DEATH — SHATTER. The kill burst uses cold colours: a spray of blue-white shard
     particles instead of the default gore palette, plus one bright orange flash as
     the core goes out. Corpse decal as normal.

COUNTERPLAY
===========
  - RIGHT TURN, not right position: shoot it on the charge turn and the shell is
    open; shoot it while sealed and you lose ~20% of every hit to flat reduction.
  - Sidestep the lane on the charge turn and the whole beam is wasted, then it is
    on cooldown AND out of position.
  - Corridor fights are in the player's favour: line its family up in the lane.
  - Burst weapons (Railgun, Double Barrel) are excellent INTO the open shell; chip
    weapons are the worst answer to it — the exact inverse of the Auric Sentinel,
    which is why the two make a good floor pair.

BALANCE — TP DERIVATION (numbers, not vibes)
============================================
Everything below is the existing model (docs/game-balance-authority.txt); no new
formula is invented.

  Shell as TIME-AVERAGED flat reduction. The live shell is RIMESHELL_SHELL_ARMOR = 7
  flat, but it is DOWN for 1 turn of every ~3 (charge turn out of charge+fire+
  cooldown-ish cycle), so type-level pricing uses the average:
      flatReduction() = round(7 * (1 - 1/3)) ~= 5
  That average is the honest number to price, because TP is a per-turn average model.
  The comment on flatReduction() must state this derivation.

  eHP = enemyEffectiveHitPoints(90, 0, 0, 5, REFERENCE_PLAYER_DPT=25)
      = 90 * 25/(25-5) = 112.5
  survivalTurns      = 112.5 / 25 = 4.5
  basicDamagePerTurn = 14 / 2 = 7.0
  TP = 7.0 * 4.5 * 1.30 = 40.95      -> SOLDIER band (36-66) OK, low-mid

  Friendly fire and the lane-lock are PLAYER-FAVOURING, so pricing them at zero is
  conservative in the right direction.

  Credits 10 sits between Acid Drone (8) and Void Shroud (12) — consistent with TP.
  XP is derived; never add an XP constant.

  GATE: this is a BalanceConfig change, so BOTH gates must pass —
    ./gradlew test         (unit tests + the full balance audit; every commit)
    ./gradlew balanceSim   (the behavioural bands; required for BalanceConfig edits)
  See docs/game-balance-authority.txt SECTION 7 — THE CHANGE PROTOCOL.

FLOOR BANDS & SPAWNING
======================
  Floors 1-10+. The golem family's baseline body; present at every depth.

  EVERY-FLOOR PRESENCE: the four golems are banded so at least one GOLEM archetype
  is in the candidate roster at EVERY depth (Lancer 1+, Spiresower 1+, Colossus 2+,
  Sentinel 3+). Until family-coherent floors ship they simply join the existing
  mixed roster and the EncounterBudgetPlanner spends TP on them like any other
  archetype — no generator change is needed for them to start appearing.

  FAMILY-COHERENT FLOORS (the staged rule EnemyFamily was added for): when a floor
  picks GOLEM as its family, its whole roster comes from these four. NOTE THE GAP —
  the family currently covers SOLDIER x3 + BRUISER anchor and has NO CHAFF, so a
  pure-golem floor would have no cheap bodies to spend the tail of the budget on.
  Adding a GOLEM chaff archetype (a "Shardling" — a knee-high crystal fragment,
  ~24 HP, fast, melee) is a prerequisite for GOLEM-family floors, not for these four
  archetypes to ship.

INTERACTIONS
============
  - CARDINAL-LINE RULE: mandatory and satisfied — the lance only ever commits down a
    true cardinal lane, and never re-targets after commit.
  - HAZARDS: the beam does NOT ignite fire tiles (that is the Colossus's job) —
    keeping each golem's floor-effect distinct is deliberate.
  - STATUS: no immunities in v1. If a later pass wants flavour, CHILLED-style
    resistance belongs to the shell, not to the lance.
  - ENCOUNTER BUDGET: an ordinary SOLDIER; no anchor privileges.
  - OTHER GOLEMS: pairs best with the Cinderforge Colossus (which forces you to keep
    moving, straight through the Lancer's lane) and worst-for-the-player with the
    Verdant Spiresower (whose spires give the Lancer cover to reseal behind).

OPEN QUESTIONS
==============

     sight and the player should be able to shut one on the lane) or burn through?
  2. Friendly-fire fraction 0.5 — tune against pack density once the golem floors
     exist; 1.0 may be more fun and is easier to explain.
  3. Does the open shell also increase damage taken (a VULNERABLE-style multiplier)
     on top of dropping flat reduction? Recommend NOT in v1 — dropping the flat
     reduction is already a legible ~20% swing, and stacking a multiplier makes the
     TP average harder to defend.
