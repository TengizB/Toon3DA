Order 4 of the generated-prop-art series. Read generated-prop-art.txt (brief, PS1-PS8, D1-D4) and
docs/sprite-art-creation-guide.txt section 14 first. Requested by the owner 2026-10-23: "Now do both barrels
and fire and toxic effect."


OVERVIEW
--------
Four objects that mean DANGER, rebuilt with the kit and animated: the explosive barrel ('E', sprite
prop_explosive_barrel, pinned on every floor), the radioactive barrel ('g', prop_radioactive_barrel), and the two
dynamic floor hazards - fire ('i') and the toxic pool ('q') - which HazardManager spawns, spreads and expires at
runtime (and which the Plague Hulk / Sump Wretch leave behind). Their meaning and every rule are unchanged; only
the picture is new.


SCOPE
-----
IN   props.py: make_explosive_barrel, make_radioactive_barrel, make_fire, make_toxic + PROPS rows; one painted-
     paint material appended to kit.MATERIALS if needed; four sheets. GeneratedPropRegistry rows: the two barrel
     sprite ids and the FIXED chars 'i' and 'q'. PropRenderer: the DYNAMIC-prop pass (runtime fire/toxic tiles)
     learns the sheet path too. Deleted: generateBarrelTexture, the hazard frame generators and arrays, their
     generator rows, and the old image asset textures/lab/barrel_radioactive.png (PS6). Docs.
OUT  Explosion, spread, damage, chain rules (HazardManager / ExplosiveBarrelManager) - untouched (V4).
     Heights unchanged (barrels 0.65, fire 0.30, toxic 0.16). Hazards stay full-tile-wide and ungraded.


VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
EXPLOSIVE BARREL ('E', 0.65, canvas 112 x 168, 16 frames @ 0.1 s)
  A steel drum in a dull oxide-red hazard paint (no saturated red) with darkmetal rib bands and a lid. What makes
  it read VOLATILE is HOT IRON (master D4): pressure-relief slots round its belly glowing hot iron and breathing,
  a hot seam at the lid, a blinking hot-iron warning lamp, stencil VOLATILE.
RADIOACTIVE BARREL ('g', 0.65, canvas 112 x 168, 16 frames @ 0.1 s)
  A lab-grey steel drum with a RAD LIME band and a dark trefoil, glowing rad-lime cracks at a dented seam, a
  glowing drip running down its flank once per loop into a small glowing puddle at its foot that pulses.
FIRE ('i', 0.30, canvas 160 x 72, 16 frames @ 1/12 s)
  A bed of charred debris with glowing cracks, and flames rising from it painted from a seamless looping noise
  field in the hot-iron ramp (dark red at the tips, orange, a white-hot base), sparks lifting. Fully emissive.
TOXIC POOL ('q', 0.16, canvas 160 x 48, 16 frames @ 0.1 s)
  A low pool of glowing TOXIN green (the POISONED status green - V5's own semantic colour, the right one here)
  with a dark crust at its rim, slow caustic ripples, bubbles that swell and pop, and a faint vapour lifting.
