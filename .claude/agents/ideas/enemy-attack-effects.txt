TITLE: Enemy Attack Visual Effects & Animations
CREATED: 2026-06-14
---

OVERVIEW
========
A purely-cosmetic layer that makes every enemy attack legible on the enemy itself.
Today an enemy attack produces only player-side feedback (red hit vignette + screen
shake) and the EnemyState.ATTACKING flag is set for exactly one turn — but the enemy
sprite gives zero sign it just struck you. This feature adds three readable visuals
driven entirely off that existing ATTACKING flag: a melee LUNGE/SLAM that shoves the
sprite toward the camera, a ranged PROJECTILE/BEAM that travels from the firing enemy
to the player, and an optional pre-hit TELEGRAPH (windup glow + scale-up) so a
prepared player can read the threat. Nothing here touches turn logic, pathfinding,
LOS, collision, damage, or input. All visuals are real-time wall-clock overlays that
live and die in under 0.5 seconds, rendered through the existing EnemyRenderer
billboard pipeline and a small new effect system. No new textures — ShapeRenderer
geometry, the white-pixel texture EnemyRenderer already owns, and the existing
BitmapFont only.

This belongs here because combat is mechanically complete but one-sided in its
feedback: the player sees their own hits land beautifully (enemy-hit-impact-effects)
and feels their own damage (HitVignetteRenderer), but the demon that bit them looks
inert. Readability is a core roguelike pillar — "the player should always understand
why they died." Right now they can't see which enemy hit them, or that two enemies
hit at once. This closes that gap.


GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
This is reactive and cosmetic — the player does not press anything. The trigger is
the game logic already in EnemyManager.

Per-turn (one player action = one world turn):

1. Player acts (move / rotate / fire / skip) -> World advances one turn ->
   EnemyManager.phaseB runs every alerted enemy's action.

2. For each enemy that lands an attack this turn, EnemyManager already does:
       player.applyDamage(enemy.scaledAttackDamage());
       enemy.state = EnemyState.ATTACKING;
   (melee ~line 351-352, ranged ~line 393-394).

   ADD, immediately after each of those two ATTACKING assignments:
       enemy.triggerAttackAnim();                       // melee + ranged
   and for RANGED only, also spawn the travelling projectile:
       enemyAttackEffects.spawnProjectile(enemy, playerColumn, playerRow);

3. enemy.triggerAttackAnim() sets enemy.attackAnimTimerSeconds = full duration.
   The enemy sprite now plays its archetype animation for the next ~0.30s of
   wall-clock time, independent of turns:
       - MELEE archetypes  -> LUNGE: sprite scales up + slides toward camera-bottom,
                              then snaps back (a "shove into your face" slam).
       - RANGED archetypes -> RECOIL: small backward kick + muzzle flash quad at the
                              enemy, while the projectile (step 4) flies to the player.

4. RANGED only: spawnProjectile() pushes one EnemyProjectile into a pre-allocated
   pool. Each frame it lerps its world position from the enemy tile center toward
   the player position over PROJECTILE_TRAVEL_SECONDS, is billboard-projected to
   screen by the same math EnemyRenderer uses for sprites, and is drawn as a glowing
   quad (ACID_DRONE/MIRE_WRAITH) or a single-frame beam line (EYE_TYRANT). On arrival
   it spawns a tiny impact spark at screen-center-ish and frees itself. It deals NO
   damage — damage already happened in step 2; this is pure cosmetics catching up.

5. TELEGRAPH (optional, see OPEN QUESTIONS for the gating choice): on the turn an
   enemy *enters* a state where it will attack next turn, EnemyManager sets
   enemy.telegraphTimerSeconds. The sprite shows a brief windup (rim glow + 1.08x
   scale pulse) so the player reads "this thing is about to hit me." Because the sim
   is turn-based and damage is instant, the honest, non-misleading version of this
   telegraphs on the SAME turn the attack lands (a "flinch before the slam" within the
   0.30s window) rather than predicting a future turn. The future-turn version is
   listed as an open question because it requires a one-turn "winding up, won't hit
   this turn" behavior change, which crosses out of pure-cosmetic territory.

6. Every frame World.update(deltaTime) calls:
       for each enemy: enemy.advanceAttackAnim(deltaTime);   // mirrors advanceHitFlash
       enemyAttackEffects.update(deltaTime);                 // advances projectiles
   Timers clamp at zero; when attackAnimTimerSeconds hits 0 the sprite renders normally
   again. The ATTACKING *game state* may already have been overwritten (enemy moves to
   CHASING next turn) — the animation is driven by the TIMER, not the enum, so it
   completes smoothly regardless of what the sim does on the following turn.


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
=================
- Instant readability of "what just hit me." Two PLAGUE_HULKs flanking you now produce
  two distinct lunges; an EYE_TYRANT across the room now visibly fires a beam down the
  corridor. The red vignette stops being an anonymous "you got hit somewhere" pulse.
- The melee LUNGE sells weight and aggression — the Doom-RPG demon literally shoves
  into your face on the slam. This is the "brutal, immediately readable" voice.
- The ranged PROJECTILE creates a half-second of dread as the acid glob crosses the
  room toward you, even though the damage is already locked in. It also teaches the
  player ranged enemies exist and where they are, encouraging them to break LOS.
- Decision created (with telegraph): when a sprite winds up, the player learns the
  rhythm of an archetype and can choose to retreat, strafe to break a cardinal line
  (defeating ranged fire), or pop a medkit pre-emptively. Even the same-turn flinch
  version builds a Pavlovian "lunge = I just lost HP, react now" loop.
- Nothing here punishes the player or changes difficulty — it only makes existing
  difficulty fair by making it visible.


TECHNICAL NOTES
===============
No new tile symbols required. No new textures. No turn-logic changes (except two
one-line trigger calls and optional telegraph flag — all cosmetic side effects).

--- GameMath additions (each with the mandatory comment block) ---

   attackLungeCurve(float strength) -> float in [0,1]
       Formula: symmetric lunge arc. The slam should ramp OUT fast and settle BACK.
       Derivation: phase = sin(pi * strength) gives 0 at strength=0, 1 at strength=0.5,
       0 at strength=1 — but strength DECAYS from 1->0, so as the timer winds down the
       sprite goes 0 (just hit, full) ... wait: we want full extension at the START.
       Use phase = sin(pi/2 * clamp01(strength*2 ... )). Simpler honest version:
       phase = (strength >= 0.5f) ? (2f*(1f-strength)) : 1f  -> snaps to full extension
       immediately at trigger (strength=1 -> 0), holds near full early, eases back to 0.
       Choose the easing the implementer prefers; document whichever you ship.
       Edge cases: clamp input to [0,1]; output never exceeds 1.

   attackRecoilOffset(float strength) -> float (signed pixel magnitude in [0, MAX])
       Formula: recoil = strength * strength * MAX_RECOIL_PIXELS (quadratic ease-out as
       timer decays). Caller multiplies by the away-from-player screen direction sign.
       Edge cases: clamp strength to [0,1].

   lerp(...) and clamp already exist — reuse for projectile position and strength clamps.

VISUAL DESIGN
=============
Melee LUNGE (PLAGUE_HULK, GORE_BITER, SHELL_BRUTE, IRON_STALKER, VOID_SHROUD):
   - Sprite scales up to +35% and slides ~18% of wall-height downward (toward the
     camera's lower edge) on trigger, then eases back over 0.30s. Reads as a forward
     shove. The existing white hit-flash on the enemy is unrelated (that's when YOU
     hit IT); the lunge has no white flash — instead a brief dark-red rim is acceptable
     via the tint pipeline if extra punch is wanted (optional, gate behind a constant).
   - VOID_SHROUD variant: lower scale bonus (it's a phasing wraith) — give it a slightly
     translucent flicker by dipping alpha during the lunge (optional polish).

Ranged PROJECTILE / BEAM:
   - ACID_DRONE:  bright acid-green glowing quad (~1.0, 0.85, 0.1 core fading to
     0.2,0.8,0.15 edge). Travels in 0.22s. Small green splat spark on arrival.
   - MIRE_WRAITH: sickly purple-green glob (0.55, 0.25, 0.6). Same travel. Pairs
     thematically with its POISONED application.
   - EYE_TYRANT:  hovering tyrant fires a BEAM, not a glob — a single-frame thick red
     line (1.0, 0.15, 0.1) from enemy screen-point to player screen-point that fades
     over the 0.30s anim window (alpha = getAttackAnimStrength()). No travel lerp; it's
     an instant lance. Add a brief white core down the beam center for heat.
   - All ranged enemies also get the RECOIL kick + a yellow-white muzzle-flash quad at
     the sprite for the first 45% of the window.

Muzzle flash: whitePixelTexture quad, size = spriteScreenWidth * 0.30, centered on the
sprite, color = warm white (1, 0.9, 0.6) with alpha = lunge-window envelope. Additive
look achieved by drawing bright over the sprite (no blend-mode change needed; keep it
simple and opaque-bright, or set batch additive only around this draw if desired).

Telegraph: a rim-glow ring is not feasible cheaply per-pixel; instead brighten the whole
sprite toward the archetype warning color (melee = deep red 0.9,0.2,0.15; ranged = the
projectile color) at ENEMY_TELEGRAPH_RIM_ALPHA, plus the small +8% scale pulse. Reads as
"charging up."

Depth/occlusion: projectiles and beams MUST respect WallRenderer.getZBufferUnchecked so a
glob vanishes when it passes behind a pillar. The lunge warp inherits EnemyRenderer's
existing per-column z-test for free.


BALANCE NOTES
=============
- Everything is cosmetic; "balance" = readability vs. visual noise. All numbers above
  are PLACEHOLDERS.
- Durations deliberately < 0.5s so visuals never bleed into the next turn's animation
  budget (player move/rotate = 0.12s; a 0.30s attack anim spans ~2-3 quick actions and
  that's fine because it's timer-driven, not turn-driven).
- LUNGE_SCALE_BONUS above ~0.5 starts clipping the sprite off-screen at point-blank;
  cap at 0.4 unless playtesting wants more menace.
- With many enemies attacking the same turn (a swarm), multiple lunges + projectiles
  fire at once. Pool size 16 covers worst case; overflow overwrites oldest gracefully.
- PROJECTILE_TRAVEL_SECONDS shorter than ATTACK_ANIM keeps the muzzle flash and arrival
  spark overlapping nicely. Keep travel <= anim duration.
- Telegraph duration should be short; a long windup on a turn-based game feels laggy.


INTERACTIONS
============
1. HitVignetteRenderer (player damage): fires the SAME turn as the enemy lunge/beam.
   Tune timings so the red vignette pulse peaks as the melee lunge reaches full extension
   / as the beam lands — they should read as one combined "I got hit by THAT" moment.
2. enemy-hit-impact-effects (ImpactEffectSystem): independent and non-conflicting — that
   system fires when the PLAYER hits an enemy (white flash, damage numbers). This new
   system fires when an ENEMY hits the player. They can co-occur (you shoot a Hulk the
   same turn it bites you) and must not fight over batch/color state — each owns its own
   batch begin/end, exactly like the existing renderers.
3. status-effects system: MIRE_WRAITH/ACID_DRONE already roll POISONED on a ranged hit
   (applyRangedAttackStatusEffect). The projectile's arrival visually justifies the green
   status tint that then appears on the player — wire the poison-applied moment to the
   projectile ARRIVAL if you want the cause/effect to line up (cosmetic only).
4. EnemyState.DORMANT shading: dormant enemies are dimmed; they never attack, so they
   never lunge — no conflict. ALERTED/CHASING enemies that DON'T attack a given turn show
   no animation, which keeps the lunge meaningful (only attackers move).
5. sentry-turret idea (if built): a stationary turret is ranged — it would reuse
   spawnProjectile/beam directly with zero extra work.


OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
1. TELEGRAPH gating: same-turn flinch (pure cosmetic, honest but reactive) vs. true
   one-turn-ahead windup (enemy "charges" on turn N, hits on turn N+1). The latter
   changes enemy behavior (a turn where it could have hit but doesn't) — that's a
   game-feel/difficulty change, not pure cosmetics. RECOMMEND shipping the same-turn
   flinch first; promote to true windup only via a deliberate design+balance pass.
2. EYE_TYRANT beam: single-frame instant lance (recommended, cheap, reads as a hitscan)
   vs. a fast travelling bolt. Lance fits the "tyrant eye laser" fantasy better.
3. Should the melee lunge also nudge the player's HitVignette intensity per-attacker
   (bigger enemy = stronger vignette), or keep vignette purely damage-driven? Likely
   keep separate to avoid coupling.
4. Muzzle flash blend mode: plain bright-opaque (simplest, zero state change) vs. true
   additive (prettier, needs setBlendFunction toggle + restore). Start plain.
5. Do we want a faint directional indicator when the attacker is OFF-SCREEN (you got hit
   from behind)? Out of scope here — a separate "directional damage indicator" idea —
   but flag it because off-screen attackers currently produce no enemy-side visual at all.
6. Pool overflow policy: overwrite-oldest (recommended) vs. drop-new. Overwrite-oldest
   guarantees the most recent attack is always shown.
