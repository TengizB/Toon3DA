Order 8 of 8 in the balance-overhaul series (index: balance-overhaul.txt).
Depends on orders 1-7 (everything; this order moves numbers, not systems).


OVERVIEW
--------
Orders 2-7 set every number from arithmetic and the simulator. The owner's own
words set the bar: "I test new versions playing until I see what I want to see",
and the questionnaire's ammo (Part G) and card (J2) answers were left blank to be
filled from play. This order is the loop that turns the owner's phone runs into
final numbers: a short playtest brief, logs copied from the order-1 DEV REPORT,
an analysis of every series target against those logs, dial settings baked into
BalanceConfig, and the simulator calibrated to what real play showed — repeated
for at most three rounds. It also fixes the one thing that has kept most
behavioural bands waived since new-game-balancr order 9: the simulator's
navigation, so the gates finally measure the game.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - SimNavigator improved until scripted policies reach the exit on > 90% of
    floors; the navigation waiver deleted; every depth-derived S-band enforced.
  - Playtest rounds (max 3): brief -> owner runs -> logs -> analysis -> tuning.
  - Every target metric (table in RULES) measured per round from logs.
  - Dial values baked into BalanceConfig; dials reset to 1.0; sim policies
    calibrated to logged behaviour; sim bands re-centred where the targets were
    proxies.
  - Filling Part G (ammo) and J2 (perk picks) from logs.

OUT (deliberately not doing now)
  - New mechanics, systems, content or UI. A finding that needs one becomes a
    new idea file (via creative-game-designer), not a change here.
  - Tuning anything outside balance (renderer, story text, sound).
  - More than three rounds. Remaining gaps after round 3 go to a follow-up idea
    with the logs attached.
  - Removing the dev tools: they stay for future passes (debuggable builds only).


GAMEPLAY LOOP
-------------
  Round n:
  1. The implementer writes the round's PLAYTEST BRIEF (what to play, which DEV
     START scenarios, which dials to try, the 5 feel questions).
  2. The owner plays on the phone, taps COPY THIS RUN / COPY LAST 5 RUNS after
     each session and pastes the logs to Claude with short answers to the 5
     questions.
  3. The main thread saves the logs; the implementer writes the ANALYSIS
     (targets vs measured, with the evidence lines) and the TUNING PROPOSAL
     (which constants move, why, expected effect).
  4. The owner approves the proposal (or edits it); the numbers move in
     BalanceConfig; both gates green; build shipped for the next round.


RULES
-----
  P1  Artifacts (written by the implementer, outside the ideas folder):
        .claude/balance-feedback/playtest-round-<n>-brief.md
        .claude/balance-feedback/logs/round-<n>/<date>-<k>.txt  (verbatim logs)
        .claude/balance-feedback/playtest-round-<n>-analysis.md
      The analysis is the evidence for every BalanceConfig change of the round;
      each change's CHANGELOG line cites it.
  P2  ROUND SIZE: >= 8 NORMAL runs plus the six DEV START scenarios:
        (a) depth 1 COMBAT BARE, (b) depth 5 BOSS ON-CURVE, (c) depth 8 ELITE
        ON-CURVE, (d) depth 12 COMBAT CAVERN ON-CURVE, (e) depth 12 COMBAT BARE
        (the "lazy player" check), (f) depth 20 COMBAT ON-CURVE.
  P3  TARGET METRICS (measured from logs; the band is what "in band" means):
        T2  scenario (e) dies or ends the floor below 25% HP; scenario (d)
            ends above 40% HP
        T3  floors 1-3: >= 12 enemies spawned, first damage exchange within 15
            turns of arrival, >= 2 groups of >= 3
        T4  every floor's "heals present" >= the heal floor; median HP at COMBAT
            exit 55-80%; <= 30% of deaths are "empty" deaths (0 heals and < 20%
            of any carried ammo type's cap held at death)
        T5  per run: heavies avoided/guarded/staggered >= 60% of heavies committed
            at the player by the owner's 5th round-1 run; GUARD used >= 0.5 per
            floor on floors with a heavy
        T6  no DamageClass > 50% of the round's damage dealt; >= 1 hint-driven
            switch per COMBAT floor on average; Shotgun family and Incinerator
            each deal >= 5% of damage in runs where they are carried
        T7  across the round's normal runs: >= 3 distinct dominant build tags;
            no keystone picked in > 50% of runs that reached level 5
        T8  first boss cleared in >= 1 of the owner's first 3 round-1 runs; the
            share of deaths on floors 10-15 is the largest of any 5-floor band
        T9  >= 1 shop purchase per floor with a Fabricator, median
        G   (ammo, questionnaire Part G) reserve at floor exit per carried type
            10-60% of cap; emergency lifeline on < 5% of floors
        J2  (perks, questionnaire J2) no perk picked in > 60% of the offers it
            appeared in
      Plus the owner's 5 feel answers per round (P4).
  P4  THE FIVE QUESTIONS (every round, one line each): (1) did your deaths feel
      like your mistake? (2) did a better weapon or a level-up change a fight?
      (3) were any floors empty or overflowing? (4) did you switch weapons for a
      reason? (5) which floor or fight felt best, which worst?
  P5  TUNING DISCIPLINE: a round changes only numbers (BalanceConfig, sim policy
      parameters, S-band values), each tied to a metric in P3 or a P4 answer.
      A number moves toward its band; a band itself moves only when the owner's
      P4 answers contradict it (the band was a wrong proxy) — recorded as an
      override with the evidence.
  P6  BAKING DIALS: a dial the owner kept at a non-1.0 value is converted into
      the constants behind its seam (e.g. ENEMY_COUNT 1.15 -> BODY_TARGET_* x
      1.15), then the dial is reset to 1.0 on the owner's device (TUNING -> RESET
      ALL, named in the next brief).
  P7  SIM CALIBRATION: TacticalPolicy parameters (heal threshold, guard/avoid
      rules, shopping, switching) are adjusted so the sim's per-floor HP-exit and
      supply metrics for TACTICAL fall within the range of the owner's logs at
      the same depths. The sim is a proxy for the owner, not the other way
      round.
  P8  NAVIGATION: SimNavigator reaches the exit on > 90% of floors for all
      three policies over the full seed matrix; the [SIM_*] navigation waiver in
      BalanceSchema and docs is DELETED and every depth-derived band is enforced.
  P9  STOP: the order is done when every P3 metric is in band in one round and
      the owner answers (1) and (2) with yes, OR after round 3 (then the
      remaining gaps are written into a follow-up idea request for the designer).

EDGE CASES
----------
  - Owner sends fewer runs than P2: the round's analysis marks each metric with
    its sample size and proposes only changes whose evidence is >= 3 runs.
  - A log from an older build (LOGv mismatch): excluded from the analysis.
  - Contradictory feel answers vs metrics: the answers win for bands (P5), the
    metrics win for numbers inside a band.
  - A metric that needs a system change (not a number): logged as a follow-up
    idea, never patched here.
