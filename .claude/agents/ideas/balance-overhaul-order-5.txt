CREATED: 2026-09-30

Order 6 of 8 in the balance-overhaul series (index: balance-overhaul.txt).
Depends on orders 1-5 (especially order 4's damage classes and scenarios and
order 5's guard/armour/hazard rules, which perks modify).


OVERVIEW
--------
The owner: "every run ends up the same — the intention is to build different
characters"; "weapon abilities do not provide meaningful bonuses"; "rare weapons
can be found but there is no reason to"; "levelling up, choosing weapons,
finding better weapons must matter". The current level-up system is built to
make every choice EQUAL: ten cards, all priced at the same 12 power points
(R-CARD), mostly generic stat sticks. Equal-value generic choices produce the
same build every run. Weapon abilities are 33 small, priced-to-be-harmless
effects.

This order replaces budget-equal cards with TAGGED PERKS that pay off inside a
style (close quarters, marksman, pyro, demolition, energy, bulwark, blade,
scavenger, vitality), adds KEYSTONES at character levels 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 —
rule-changers with a real cost that commit a run to a shape — and makes rarity
exciting by guaranteeing more and bigger abilities per tier with a legendary
signature that changes a rule. Offers are biased toward what the player carries
so synergy appears, and always include a pivot so a run can change course.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - BuildTag; Perk catalog (~26 perks, up to 3 ranks) replacing UpgradeCard;
    Keystone catalog (9) offered at levels 5/10/15/20.
  - Offer rules (synergy + pivot), level-up and keystone screens with tag chips.
  - Weapon ability catalogue pruned to felt effects; per-tier ability count
    guarantees and raised budgets; ability tags.
  - New schema rules R-PERK / R-KEYSTONE / R-CARRY; sim build-bias variants and
    S-BUILD.

OUT (deliberately not doing now)
  - Meta-progression between runs (unlocks, persistent perks). A run's build
    dies with the run.
  - New weapons, new enemies, new mechanics beyond what perks modify.
  - Respec / reroll buttons (a pivot slot in every offer does that job).
  - Attribute system removal: STR/AGI/TGH/MRK stay; some perks raise them.
  - Shop sale of perks beyond the existing "player ability" offer (order 7
    prices it).


GAMEPLAY LOOP
-------------
  1. Level up. Three perks appear, each with its tag chip. The player carries a
     Shotgun and an Incinerator: one CLOSE QUARTERS perk and one PYRO perk carry
     an amber "SYNERGY" marker; the third is from a tag the player has none of
     (the pivot).
  2. Level 5: the KEYSTONE screen (distinct frame) offers three: e.g.
     SCATTERGUN DOCTRINE, PYROMANIAC, IRON BULWARK. Each states its gain and its
     cost in one line each. Picking one shapes the rest of the run.
  3. A RARE Incinerator drops: two abilities, one of them PYRO-tagged, which
     stacks with the keystone. Swapping to it is exciting, not a spreadsheet.
  4. Next run, a Railgun and a Plasma Rifle early -> MARKSMAN/ENERGY perks and
     DEADEYE: a visibly different run.


RULES
-----
  B1  BuildTag: CLOSE_QUARTERS, MARKSMAN, PYRO, DEMOLITION, ENERGY, BULWARK,
      BLADE, SCAVENGER, VITALITY. DamageClass -> tag: SPREAD -> CLOSE_QUARTERS;
      BALLISTIC, RAIL -> MARKSMAN; FIRE -> PYRO; EXPLOSIVE -> DEMOLITION;
      ENERGY -> ENERGY; BLADE, BLUNT -> BLADE.
  B2  PERK CATALOG (register() rows; each rank's magnitude in BalanceConfig; the
      effect is typed data interpreted by per-constant modifier behaviour, never
      a switch). Defaults (per rank, max 3 ranks unless noted):
        CLOSE_QUARTERS  Buckshot Discipline  SPREAD damage +15%
                        Point Man            point-blank knockback +1 tile (r2),
                                             stagger also at 3 tiles (r3)
                        Breacher             SPREAD kills refund 1 shell, 33%
        MARKSMAN        Steady Aim           BALLISTIC/RAIL falloff -30%
                        First Blood          +25% damage to full-HP enemies
                        Lane Discipline      +1 range, +10% damage beyond 4 tiles
        PYRO            Accelerant           +1 max burn stack (FIRE)
                        Wildfire             burning enemies that die ignite
                                             their tile (1 rank)
                        Fireproof            immune to fire tiles (1 rank)
        DEMOLITION      Shaped Charges       EXPLOSIVE damage +20%
                        Blast Shield         own explosion damage -50%
                        Sapper               barrel damage +50%; barrels shown on
                                             the mini-map (r1)
        ENERGY          Capacitor            ENERGY damage +15%
                        Arc Jump             Arc Cannon +1 chain jump
                        Overcharge           Plasma pierce hits +15% per enemy
                                             already pierced
        BULWARK         Shield Wall          GUARD front arc +/-90 (r1), heavy
                                             guard x0.10 (r2)
                        Riposte Drill        riposte VULNERABLE +1 turn, +25%
                        Plate Carrier        max armour +20%
        BLADE           Butcher              melee damage +25%
                        Bloodletting         melee kills heal 3% max HP
        SCAVENGER       Pack Rat             ammo pickups +15%
                        Haggler              shop prices -10%
                        Salvager             carriers drop +1 item, 25%
        VITALITY        Hardy                max HP +10%
                        Field Medic          heals +15%
                        Second Wind          once per floor, a lethal hit leaves
                                             1 HP (1 rank)
      A perk rank may also grant +1 to one attribute (data), keeping STR/AGI/TGH/
      MRK meaningful.
  B3  OFFER RULES: 3 perks per level-up. At least 1 whose tag matches a carried
      weapon's tag (SYNERGY), at least 1 from a tag the player has no ranks in
      (PIVOT); the rest weighted by the existing draw bias toward picked tags.
      A maxed perk is never offered.
  B4  KEYSTONES at character levels 5, 10, 15, 20 (replacing that level's perk
      offer): 3 offered, at least one matching the player's highest-rank tag and
      one not. Each is a rule-changer with a stated cost:
        SCATTERGUN DOCTRINE  SPREAD reload costs no turn; point-blank kills
                             refund a shell. Cost: non-SPREAD damage -15%.
        DEADEYE              first hit on each enemy is a guaranteed critical.
                             Cost: clip sizes -30%.
        PYROMANIAC           immune to fire; burning enemies take +50% from all
                             sources. Cost: max armour -25%.
        DEMOLITIONIST        immune to your own explosions; barrels you detonate
                             deal x2. Cost: max HP -20%.
        STORM CONDUIT        ENERGY hits arc to one adjacent enemy at 50%. Cost:
                             BALLISTIC damage -25%.
        IRON BULWARK         a guarded hit reflects 50% of the blocked damage to
                             the attacker. Cost: all weapon damage -15%.
        BERSERKER            melee kills restore 8% max HP; below 30% HP melee
                             +50%. Cost: heals -30%.
        QUARTERMASTER        +1 weapon loadout slot; shop prices -25%. Cost: max
                             HP -10%.
        IRONHIDE             max HP +40%. Cost: heals -20% and STIMs no longer
                             cleanse.
      A keystone can be taken once; the four picks must differ.
  B5  RARITY: ability counts per tier: COMMON 0, UNCOMMON 1, RARE 2, EPIC 3 (one
      from the MAJOR list), LEGENDARY 3 + its signature. Ability budgets
      (R-ABILITY): UNCOMMON 12, RARE 25, EPIC 40, LEGENDARY 60 PP (+/-20%). Order
      2's rarity damage multiplier stays.
  B6  ABILITY PRUNE: every ability whose priced PP at the expected weapon level
      for its drop depth is below ABILITY_MIN_FELT_PP (8) is cut or merged into a
      stronger one; the survivors are re-tuned to their tier budgets and carry a
      BuildTag (e.g. Incendiary -> PYRO, Stagger Rounds -> CLOSE_QUARTERS,
      Scavenger Rounds -> SCAVENGER) so they count for B3 synergy.
  B7  R-PERK (replaces R-CARD and R-CARD-BREAKPOINT):
        PK1 FELT     every perk rank improves its niche metric by >= 10% (niche =
                     its tag's order-4 reference scenario TTK, TTD for BULWARK/
                     VITALITY, supply value for SCAVENGER) — and crosses >= 1
                     integer TTK or TTD breakpoint there by rank 3.
        PK2 FOCUSED  no perk (outside VITALITY) improves ALL eight reference
                     scenarios by > 10%; VITALITY perks are capped at 10% per
                     rank.
      R-KEYSTONE: every keystone improves its niche by >= 30% and worsens at
      least one other scenario or metric by >= 10%.
      R-CARRY: for every build tag, the expected player at depth 15 with that
      tag's perks at the expected rank count plus its keystone has TTK/TTD vs the
      depth-15 reference roster within +/-20% of every other tag's build (no trap
      builds, no dominant build).
  B8  COMMUNICATION: level-up screen shows each perk's tag chip, a SYNERGY
      marker (amber) when it matches a carried weapon, rank pips (1-3), and a
      one-line effect. Keystone screen: distinct gold frame, gain line + cost
      line (cost in red). Inventory gains a BUILD page listing picked perks by
      tag and the keystones.


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
-----------------
Every level-up asks "lean in or pivot?", and every fifth level asks "what kind
of marine is this run?". Weapons found stop being interchangeable: a rare gun
whose ability matches your keystone is a run-maker. Two runs with different
early finds end as different characters.

VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
Tag chips: small rounded plates in the tag's class colour (order-4 palette;
BULWARK blue, VITALITY green, SCAVENGER brass). SYNERGY: amber corner ribbon.
Keystone frame: gold double border, cost line in (0.95, 0.30, 0.25). All
procedural (ShapeRenderer + BitmapFont). Touch: perk plates keep the existing
level-up card size (thumb-sized).


BALANCE NOTES
-------------
Balance-bearing throughout (BalanceConfig SECTION 24, GameMath valuation,
schema, sim). The magnitudes above are defaults to satisfy R-PERK / R-KEYSTONE /
R-CARRY; math-expert tunes them together. Total player power is no longer
invariant across builds by construction; R-CARRY replaces that invariant with a
bounded spread, which is what makes choices real.

EDGE CASES
----------
  - Keystone level reached mid-fight: the level-up overlay behaves as today (it
    is already a hard-pause overlay); the keystone screen replaces the perk
    offer.
  - All perks of the synergy tags maxed: the synergy slot falls back to any
    unmaxed perk; the pivot rule still holds.
  - QUARTERMASTER extra slot then death: nothing persists.
  - IRONHIDE + VITALITY Field Medic: multiplicative (documented order: perk
    bonuses add, keystone costs multiply after).
  - Second Wind with a status tick as the lethal damage: triggers (once per
    floor).
  - Weapon with a pruned ability in an existing save: runs are not saved
    mid-run; no migration.

ASSUMPTIONS
-----------
  AS1  Tag-based perks + four keystones is the right depth of build system for a
       25-floor run (Slay the Spire's "commit to an archetype" feel, which the
       owner cites).
  AS2  Keystones replace the perk offer at their level (no double reward).
  AS3  The perk/keystone list above is a starting catalog; adding one later is a
       register() row plus magnitudes that pass R-PERK/R-KEYSTONE.
  AS4  Offering a guaranteed PIVOT each level-up is better than a reroll button.
  AS5  Abilities below ~8 PP are not felt and should go; exact cut list is
       decided by the valuation, recorded in docs.
  AS6  Attributes stay; perks may grant them.
  AS7  J2 (which card is always best) was left blank; R-CARRY and S-BUILD stand
       in for it until order-8 logs show real pick rates.
