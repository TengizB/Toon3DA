OVERVIEW
========
The arsenal layer: weapons exist as pickups in the world, the marine carries up
to four at once in numbered loadout slots, and switches between them with the
number keys or by cycling with [ and ]. Walking over a weapon either fits it into
a free slot, auto-swaps for the held weapon when full, or prompts a drop. A row
of small slot icons in the HUD shows which slots are filled and which is active.
WeaponHudRenderer swaps to the new weapon sprite on every switch.

This belongs here because the genre is built on the weapon ladder — pistol to
shotgun to plasma to BFG — and the existing codebase already has multiple weapon
designs (shotgun, plasma rifle, railgun, grenade launcher, incinerator, etc.) plus
a WeaponHudRenderer that draws a single weapon sprite. What is missing is the
carry/switch/swap layer that turns "the player has a weapon" into "the player
manages a loadout." This doc is that layer, and the source of truth the inventory
UI (order 6) and HUD read for weapon state.


GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
1. The generator (order 10) and enemy drops (order 5) place weapons as floor
   pickups (low billboard sprites, reusing the GroundItem path). Each weapon is a
   unique ItemType (WEAPON_PISTOL, WEAPON_SHOTGUN, WEAPON_PLASMA, ...).
2. Player steps onto a weapon-pickup tile (settled-tile block in advanceMove,
   alongside keycard/recovery/stairs checks). The Loadout resolves it:
   - If the player does NOT already own that weapon and a slot is FREE: the
     weapon goes into the lowest free slot, ammo (if any) is added, and a brief
     "PICKED UP: COMBAT SHOTGUN [slot 2]" banner flashes. Free.
   - If the player ALREADY owns that weapon: convert the pickup to its ammo type
     (per order 5: "picking up a weapon you already own converts to ammo"). The
     weapon tile clears; ammo is added to the pouch.
   - If the player owns a DIFFERENT set and all 4 slots are FULL: the pickup is
     NOT auto-grabbed. The HUD shows "[SWAP] STORM RIFLE for <current>?" The
     player presses F (interact/loot key from order 5) to swap: the currently
     ACTIVE weapon is dropped as a GroundItem on the player's tile and the new
     weapon takes its slot. If F is not pressed, the weapon stays on the floor.
3. SWITCHING (free, does NOT cost a turn — switching is a menu-class action, like
   opening the inventory):
   - Number keys 1-4 select that slot directly. Pressing the active slot's number
     again is a no-op. Pressing an empty slot's number is a no-op (soft buzz).
   - '[' cycles to the previous filled slot, ']' cycles to the next filled slot
     (wraps; skips empty slots).
   - On switch, WeaponHudRenderer.setActiveWeapon(weapon) swaps the sprite (with
     the existing weapon raise/lower animation if present), the HUD ammo digits
     update to the new weapon's ammo, and the HUD slot-icon row highlights the new
     active slot.
4. DROP to make room: from the inventory UI (order 6) the player can DROP a weapon
   slot (spawns it as a GroundItem, costs one turn on menu close per order 6). A
   quick in-world drop of the ACTIVE weapon could also be bound (see OPEN Q).
5. Firing the active weapon (existing weapon systems) consumes a turn and ammo as
   already designed. This doc does not touch firing — only carry/switch/swap.


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
=================
The loadout is the player's identity for the run. Four slots is enough to build a
kit (a crowd-clearer, a single-target punch, a panic button, a sidearm) but tight
enough that picking up a fifth weapon is a real decision: which of my four do I
trust less than this new thing? Switching is instant and free because hesitation
in a turn-based fight should be about WHICH tool, not about the cost of reaching
for it — the cost is the turn you spend firing the wrong one. The slot-icon row
gives the constant readout DCSS players expect: at a glance I know my options.
The "swap for current?" prompt at full capacity creates a sharp little gamble —
drop my plasma rifle here to grab the railgun, and pray I do not regret it two
rooms down.


TECHNICAL NOTES
===============
DEPENDENCIES (must exist first):
- Existing weapon systems: WeaponHudRenderer (single-weapon sprite + ammo), and
  at least the base Weapon abstraction the individual weapon docs assume
  (shotgun-weapon-system.txt, plasma-rifle.txt, railgun.txt, etc.). This doc adds
  a Loadout container ABOVE those Weapon instances.
- ORDER 5 (inventory-loot-ransack-system.txt): WEAPON_* ItemTypes, GroundItem /
  GroundItemManager (weapons spawn/drop as ground items), the ammo pouch (ammo
  lives in the pouch, NOT in weapon slots), and the "own duplicate -> ammo" rule.
  Hard dependency.
- ORDER 6 (inventory UI) for the EQUIP/DROP-weapon verbs from the menu. Soft: if
  order 6 is not built, switching via 1-4 / [ ] and in-world swap still work;
  only menu-drop is unavailable.

NEW / MODIFIED CLASSES:
- entity/Loadout.java (NEW, in ...toon3d.entity): the carry container.
  Fields: Weapon[] slots (size WEAPON_SLOT_COUNT = 4), int activeSlotIndex.
  Methods:
    boolean tryEquip(Weapon w)        -> lowest free slot or false if full
    boolean ownsWeaponType(ItemType)  -> dedupe check for "convert to ammo"
    void    selectSlot(int index)     -> switch active (no-op if empty/same)
    int     nextFilledSlot(int from)  -> for ']' cycle (wraps, skips empty)
    int     previousFilledSlot(...)   -> for '[' cycle
    Weapon  active()                  -> current weapon (may be null/fists)
    Weapon  removeSlot(int index)     -> for drop/swap; returns the removed weapon
  NO allocations in switch/select (hot-ish path on keypress). Owned by Player or
  World; Player exposes a read-only view, mutated only via the controller path
  (consistent with the "never mutate Player directly" rule).
- item/ItemType.java (order 5): WEAPON_PISTOL, WEAPON_SHOTGUN, WEAPON_PLASMA,
  WEAPON_RAILGUN, WEAPON_GRENADE, WEAPON_INCINERATOR ... each stores: display
  name, the ammo ItemType it consumes, the glyph/color for ground rendering, and
  a factory hook to build its Weapon instance on pickup.
- input/PlayerController.java:
    * In the settled-tile block: weaponPickupHook(settledTile) -> resolves equip /
      convert-to-ammo / full-capacity-prompt (sets a pending-swap target read by
      the F interact handler).
    * In the IDLE input poll: add SELECT_SLOT_1..4 (number keys), CYCLE_PREV ('['),
      CYCLE_NEXT (']'). Use isKeyJustPressed so one tap = one switch. These call
      Loadout.selectSlot / next / previous and then WeaponHudRenderer
      .setActiveWeapon — they do NOT call tryMove and do NOT advance a turn.
    * The full-capacity SWAP via F: F is the order-5 interact/loot key; when a
      pending weapon-swap target is set (player standing on a weapon at full
      slots), F performs the swap (drop active -> equip new) instead of/in
      addition to ransack. Disambiguation: weapon-swap prompt takes priority over
      ransack on the same tile, or use a different confirm key (see OPEN Q).
- render/WeaponHudRenderer.java: add setActiveWeapon(Weapon) (swap the procedural
  sprite + ammo source) and a small slot-icon strip renderer (see VISUAL DESIGN).
  Reads the Loadout for filled/active state. No per-frame allocation — pre-build
  the four slot-icon quads/labels once.
- world/World.java: owns or holds the Loadout (persists across floors per the
  order-9 / level-transitions rule that weapons carry down). Wires the pickup hook
  and the WeaponHudRenderer active-weapon binding.

CONSTANTS TO ADD (Constants.java; PLACEHOLDER):
  WEAPON_SLOT_COUNT             = 4
  WEAPON_PICKUP_HEIGHT_FRACTION = 0.30f    (weapons sit a touch higher than ammo)
  WEAPON_SLOT_ICON_SIZE         = 44f      (HUD slot icon square, world units)
  WEAPON_SLOT_ICON_GAP          = 8f
  WEAPON_SLOT_STRIP_ORIGIN_X    = 24f      (within left HUD panel, below face/bars)
  WEAPON_SLOT_STRIP_ORIGIN_Y    = 8f
  WEAPON_SWITCH_RAISE_SECONDS   = 0.10f    (cosmetic sprite raise on switch)

TILE SYMBOLS: weapons are ENTITY-side ground items (GroundItem), NOT tile glyphs,
so NO new tile symbol is required — the generator places a generic ground-item
spawn or the weapon manager seeds them; they never need a '.txt' map character.
(If level designers ever want a hand-placed weapon on a map, that would need a new
pickup glyph — flag it then; not needed for procedural placement.)

TURN-BASED FIT: switching weapons and cycling slots are FREE (zero turns), like
opening the inventory. Picking up / converting-to-ammo happens as a side effect of
the move turn. The full-capacity F-swap costs one turn (it is an interact action,
same class as ransack). Firing remains one-turn-per-shot in the existing weapon
systems. All resolution is integer tile lookups.


VISUAL DESIGN
=============
- Weapon ground pickups: low billboard sprites, procedurally drawn per weapon
  silhouette in PropRenderer (shotgun = brown/steel pump outline; plasma = cyan-
  glowing rifle; railgun = long thin barrel with blue coil; grenade launcher =
  stubby olive tube). A cool white shimmer marks an uncollected weapon so it reads
  as "grab me" at a distance.
- HUD WEAPON-SLOT STRIP (new): a horizontal row of WEAPON_SLOT_COUNT small icon
  squares in the LEFT panel, below the face box / HP-AR bars (the left panel is
  0-420; the strip lives in the lower-left, clear of the bars). Each square:
    * FILLED slot: tiny weapon glyph/silhouette in its category color + the slot
      number "1".."4" in a corner.
    * EMPTY slot: faint dashed outline, dim number.
    * ACTIVE slot: bright amber border + subtle pulse (reuse facilityTimeSeconds
      sin), so the current weapon is unmistakable.
  This strip is the at-a-glance loadout readout; it never occludes the centre-gap
  weapon sprite.
- On switch: WeaponHudRenderer plays a quick raise of the new sprite over
  WEAPON_SWITCH_RAISE_SECONDS; the right-panel ammo digits snap to the new
  weapon's pouch ammo; the active slot-icon border lights.
- Pickup/swap banners: amber centre-low text, transient (~0.6s real), e.g.
  "PICKED UP: COMBAT SHOTGUN [2]", "[F] SWAP STORM RIFLE for PLASMA RIFLE?",
  "DROPPED: PLASMA RIFLE".


BALANCE NOTES (all PLACEHOLDER — tune in playtest)
=============
- 4 slots is the cap. The starting marine has the pistol in slot 1 (always
  available, infinite or large ammo so the player is never weaponless). Slots
  2-4 fill from pickups.
- Weapon spawn density (order-10 generator, scaled by order-9 depth):
    Floors 1-3: 1 weapon pickup somewhere on the floor (the early arsenal).
    Floors 4-6: ~1 weapon pickup, biased toward mid-tier (shotgun/plasma).
    Floors 7+:  rare weapon pickups; heavy weapons (railgun/BFG-class) gated to
                deep floors / boss-room rewards / shop only.
  A weapon you already own converts to ammo, so duplicate spawns are never wasted.
- Switching is free BY DESIGN — the strategic cost is the firing turn, not the
  switch. Do NOT add a switch cooldown (would be a real-time mechanic; forbidden).
- The full-capacity swap dropping the ACTIVE weapon (not a chosen slot) is a
  deliberate friction: it makes the player switch to the weapon they are willing
  to lose BEFORE stepping on the new one. If playtest finds this too punishing,
  the swap could target the lowest-tier slot instead (flag).
- Durability/condition is an OPTIONAL stretch goal (see below) — ship without it.


INTERACTIONS
============
1. Order 5 (inventory/loot): weapons are GroundItems and WEAPON_* ItemTypes; the
   "own duplicate -> ammo" and ammo-pouch rules come from there. The Loadout is a
   sibling container to the slot Inventory (weapons live in the Loadout's 4 slots,
   NOT in the 8 inventory slots — keeps weapon management separate from utility
   items). Confirm this split with order 5's author.
2. Order 6 (inventory UI): the EQUIP path "delegates to order 8's weapon slot
   system" (order 6 says so explicitly) — the menu's equip = Loadout.selectSlot or
   tryEquip; menu drop = Loadout.removeSlot + GroundItemManager.spawn. The detail
   panel reads weapon stats from the same Weapon instances the HUD strip reads:
   ONE source of truth.
3. Existing weapon docs (shotgun/plasma/railgun/grenade/incinerator): each becomes
   a WEAPON_* ItemType with a factory that builds its Weapon. This doc is the
   carry layer; those docs own the firing/sprite/ammo specifics.
4. Order 9 (multi-floor): the Loadout PERSISTS across descent (you carry your guns
   down) — it is run-state, rebuilt-for-level renderers re-bind to the same
   Loadout after a floor swap.
5. WeaponHudRenderer: single-weapon today -> active-weapon-of-a-Loadout tomorrow.
   The slot-icon strip is the new HUD element this doc adds.


OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
1. Does the full-capacity SWAP drop the ACTIVE weapon, the LOWEST-TIER weapon, or
   let the player pick which slot to evict (a quick 1-4 prompt)? Recommend active-
   weapon for v1 (simple, forces a pre-swap decision); pick-which-slot is nicer
   but needs a small modal.
2. F-key disambiguation when a weapon-swap prompt AND a ransackable corpse share
   the player's tile: priority order, or a separate confirm key (e.g. G for grab/
   swap)? Recommend swap-prompt takes F priority when standing ON a weapon.
3. Should '[' / ']' cycle skip empty slots (recommended) or stop on them?
   Recommend skip — cycling should only ever land on a usable weapon.
4. Is the pistol slot 1 permanent (cannot be dropped/swapped out), guaranteeing
   the marine is never unarmed? Recommend YES — slot 1 is the locked sidearm.
5. STRETCH: weapon durability/condition. A weapon could degrade with use and jam /
   lose damage, repaired at the shop or by a kit. Adds a maintenance axis but a
   lot of UI and balance work. Recommend OUT for v1; design separately if wanted.
6. Quick-swap muscle memory: should the previously-active weapon be reachable with
   a single "last weapon" key (Quake-style)? Cheap and beloved; flag as nice-to-
   have once 1-4 / [ ] are in.
