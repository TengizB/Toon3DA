TITLE: Ammo as a Finite, Pickable Resource
---

OVERVIEW
========
Ammo is a finite, world-found resource — never infinite, never auto-refilled.
The marine starts each run with a small reserve per weapon, and every shot spends
a round. More ammo is found only by picking it up off the floor (level-placed
caches and enemy drops). Running dry is a real, scary failure state that forces
the player to switch weapons, melee, flee, or scavenge. This document defines the
ammo TYPES, their pools, the new floor-pickup tiles, drop rates, starting amounts
per difficulty, and the HUD readout.

It belongs here because scarcity is the engine of roguelike tension. An infinite-
ammo shooter has no resource decisions; a finite-ammo dungeon crawler makes every
trigger pull a small commitment and every ammo cache a meaningful reward. It is
classic Doom: shells are precious, you hoard rockets for the worst rooms.

OWNERSHIP / WHAT ALREADY EXISTS:
- The weapon system (shotgun-weapon-system.txt, plasma-rifle.txt, etc.) already
  fires, reloads (clip -> reserve), and reports ammo to the HUD. This doc defines
  the RESERVE economy those weapons draw from.
- inventory-loot-ransack-system.txt already proposes ammo as ItemType (SHELLS,
  BULLETS, ROCKETS, PLASMA_CELLS) living in a separate uncapped "ammo pouch" and
  rolled from enemy loot tables. This doc is COMPATIBLE with that and is the
  source of truth for ammo TYPES, pool caps, drop rates, starting amounts, and the
  floor-pickup tiles. If the inventory pouch ships, ammo pools live there; if it
  ships later, ammo pools live as simple fields on a small AmmoPool class — either
  way the numbers below are authoritative.

GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
1. Player fires a weapon (a FIRE turn, order 2). The weapon decrements its loaded
   clip; when the clip empties it reloads from the matching ammo RESERVE pool
   (existing weapon behavior). If both clip AND reserve are empty, the weapon
   cannot fire (canFire() false): a dry-click feedback plays and NO turn is spent
   (gate the FIRE tick on a successful shot — see global-tick-event-system.txt
   OPEN QUESTION 4).
2. Each weapon maps to exactly one ammo type:
     Pistol           -> BULLETS
     Pump Shotgun     -> SHELLS
     Plasma Rifle/Gun -> PLASMA_CELLS
     Rocket/Grenade   -> ROCKETS
3. Ammo is found two ways:
   a) LEVEL-PLACED CACHES: ammo pickup tiles seeded by the level generator (and
      hand-placeable by level designers). Stepping onto the tile auto-collects the
      ammo into the reserve pool (BEFORE the tick fires, same pre-tick pickup
      window as keycards/armour). Pool clamps at its cap; overflow is wasted (or
      the tile is left if already full — see OPEN QUESTIONS).
   b) ENEMY DROPS: on death, an enemy rolls its loot table
      (inventory-loot-ransack-system.txt). Ammo drops either auto-collect or spawn
      as a ground item to ransack. Tougher enemies drop larger/rarer ammo.
4. HUD shows the CURRENT WEAPON's clip + reserve. The right panel reads
   "08 / 24" (clip / reserve) and the ammo TYPE name.
5. Running dry: when the current weapon's reserve hits 0, the HUD ammo readout
   flashes red and the dry-click on the next fire attempt makes the threat
   explicit. The player must switch weapons, scavenge, or flee.

PLAYER EXPERIENCE
=================
The low hum of scarcity. Players learn to treat the shotgun as a precision tool,
not a hose; to save plasma for a Revenant pack; to ransack every corpse because
six shells might be the difference between clearing the next room and dying in it.
Finding an ammo cache feels like relief; the dry-click feels like dread. The core
decision: "Do I spend this round now, or will I need it more in two rooms?" —
asked on every trigger pull.

TECHNICAL NOTES
===============
DEPENDENCIES (must exist first):
- A weapon that consumes ammo (shotgun-weapon-system.txt or pump-action-shotgun;
  at least one weapon must read/decrement a reserve pool).
- order 2 (turn system): firing is a tick; pickup resolves in the pre-tick window.
- order 5 (inventory-loot-ransack-system.txt) is OPTIONAL but recommended as the
  home for the ammo pouch + enemy loot tables. If absent, use a standalone
  AmmoPool.

New / modified:
- item/AmmoType.java (enum) OR reuse the ammo subset of ItemType from
  inventory-loot-ransack-system.txt: BULLETS, SHELLS, PLASMA_CELLS, ROCKETS. Each
  stores: display name, max reserve cap, HUD color, the pickup-tile symbol, and
  the amount granted per box.
- item/AmmoPool.java (new, ...toon3d.item) — only if the inventory pouch is not
  yet built: a tiny int[AmmoType.count] of current reserves + add(type, amount)
  (clamps to cap) and spend(type, amount). No allocation. If the inventory pouch
  IS built, this is just the pouch.
- Level.java / LevelLoader.java: recognize 6/7/8/9 as walkable ammo pickups; on
  player-step pickup, grant the configured amount to the matching pool and convert
  the tile to floor. Same resolution order as armour/medkit pickups.
- PropRenderer.java: billboard sprites for the four ammo boxes (color-coded).
- Weapon classes: read/decrement the matching reserve when reloading the clip;
  canFire() false when clip + reserve both 0.
- HudRenderer.java: right panel shows clip / reserve + ammo type name for the
  EQUIPPED weapon; red flash when reserve == 0.
- LevelGenerator (procedural-level-generation.txt): seed ammo caches at a tuned
  density, biased to the weapon types the player is likely carrying.

Constants to add (Constants.java) — all PLACEHOLDER:
  Reserve caps (mirror Doom RPG ranges; align with the inventory doc's stack caps):
    AMMO_RESERVE_CAP_BULLETS      = 200
    AMMO_RESERVE_CAP_SHELLS       = 60
    AMMO_RESERVE_CAP_CELLS        = 120
    AMMO_RESERVE_CAP_ROCKETS      = 20
  Amount granted per floor box:
    AMMO_BOX_BULLETS              = 20
    AMMO_BOX_SHELLS               = 8
    AMMO_BOX_CELLS                = 20
    AMMO_BOX_ROCKETS              = 2
  Starting reserves (NORMAL difficulty):
    AMMO_START_BULLETS            = 50
    AMMO_START_SHELLS             = 16
    AMMO_START_CELLS              = 0    (no plasma until found)
    AMMO_START_ROCKETS            = 0
  AMMO_PICKUP_HEIGHT_FRACTION     = 0.20f  (walkable billboard)

Turn-based fit: spending ammo is a side effect of the FIRE turn (no separate
action). Picking up ammo happens during the normal step (the step is the only
tick). Nothing here is real-time; the only "timer" is the cosmetic dry-click
flash. No second-based cooldowns.

VISUAL DESIGN
=============
- Ammo box billboards (walkable decals, ~0.20x wall height), color-coded matching
  the inventory ground-item palette: BULLETS = copper, SHELLS = brass/yellow,
  CELLS = cyan, ROCKETS = olive. A subtle 1-tile shimmer (reuse the flickering-
  floor sine path) marks an uncollected box so it reads at distance.
- HUD right panel (existing geometry): large clip digits, smaller "/ reserve"
  below, ammo type name. On reserve == 0 the readout pulses red; on a pickup it
  glows yellow briefly (PlayerAmmoChanged event, per the HUD doc's ammo-changed
  feedback).
- Pickup toast through the HUD banner: "+8 SHELLS", "+2 ROCKETS".
- Dry-click: a brief desaturated weapon-sprite twitch + a thin "OUT OF [TYPE]"
  status-line flash (cosmetic, no turn).

BALANCE NOTES
=============
- The whole point is scarcity: target a full floor clear to refill roughly 60% of
  expended rounds (per inventory-loot-ransack-system.txt), never 100%. The marine
  should trend toward "lower on ammo" over a run unless they fight efficiently.
- Plasma and rockets start at 0 — those weapons are found, and their ammo is rare;
  they are "break glass in emergency" tools, not workhorses.
- DIFFICULTY SCALING (placeholder): starting reserves and cache density scale by
  difficulty. EASY = NORMAL x1.5 ammo, more caches; HARD = NORMAL x0.6 ammo, fewer
  caches and stingier enemy drops. Caps are fixed across difficulties (only the
  fill rate changes).
- Enemy ammo drop rates (placeholder, per common enemy): ~35% drop a small ammo
  stack of a random carried-weapon type; tougher enemies bias toward shells/cells.
- Flag ALL numbers as placeholders pending playtest.

INTERACTIONS
============
1. Weapons (shotgun-weapon-system, plasma-rifle, railgun, grenade-launcher,
   incinerator): each declares its AmmoType and draws from the reserve on reload.
   A new weapon must register its ammo type or it cannot be supplied.
2. Inventory loot/ransack (inventory-loot-ransack-system.txt): ammo lives in the
   uncapped-by-slots pouch; enemy loot tables roll ammo drops; the numbers here
   are the source of truth for caps and amounts.
3. HUD vitals (scanline-crt-hud-marine-vitals.txt): consumes PlayerAmmoChanged for
   the right-panel readout and reserve-empty flash.
4. Turn system (order 2): firing spends a round and a tick; a dry-fire spends
   neither (gated FIRE tick).
5. Level generation (procedural-level-generation.txt): seeds the ammo caches; must
   never strand the player with zero usable ammo for an entire floor (guarantee at
   least one cache of a usable type per floor).
6. Permadeath (order 1): RunStats.shotsFired is bumped on each successful shot;
   running dry is a common contributing cause of death (a fair, readable one).

OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
- Auto-collect ammo vs leave-overflow-on-floor when the reserve is at cap?
  Recommend: collect up to cap and CONSUME the box (Doom-style overflow waste) for
  simplicity; leaving partial boxes is a later nicety.
- Do all four ammo types share one set of pickup tiles, or is plasma/rockets cache
  -only (no enemy drops)? Recommend enemy drops for bullets/shells only; plasma and
  rockets are cache-only to keep them rare.
- Should the HUD show ALL ammo types at once (a column) or only the equipped
  weapon's? Recommend equipped-only on the main HUD; full breakdown lives in the
  inventory UI (order 6).
- Melee fallback when fully dry: is there a no-ammo melee attack (fists / rifle
  butt)? Strongly recommended so "out of ammo" is a setback, not an instant
  death sentence. Spec the melee in a follow-up combat doc.
- Confirm the FIRE-tick-on-dry-fire decision with global-tick-event-system.txt
  OPEN QUESTION 4 (dry fire should be free).
