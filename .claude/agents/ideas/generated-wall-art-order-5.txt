OVERVIEW
--------
The third stratum: the resurrection heart. Cradle banks, pattern archives and the refineries where
the planet's soul is rendered into printer fuel. This is where the player comes from - every cradle
bay is a print in progress - and the walls should make that land without a word.

VISUAL DESIGN - STRATUM C, THE RELIQUARY (mood amber_hot)
---------------------------------------------------------
Heavy riveted gunmetal and darkmetal, fuel manifolds under the ceiling, the pale SOUL light as the
stratum's only warm-cold glow. Under amber_hot the steel goes bronze and the soul light stays white.
  BASE (x3)     riveted heavy plate in three widths, a fuel manifold pipe along the cornice, a floor
                channel with a thin soul trickle (emissive, 1-2 px) in one variant.
  STRUCTURE     a massive rib with a hydraulic ram.
  LIGHT         a soul conduit window: a vertical slot of capsule glass with soul light flowing (pulse).
  SERVICE (x2)  a valve wheel cluster on the fuel line; a coolant jacket with frost-pale bands.
  MARKER        a reprint counter plate: stencil KR-xx and a column of tally marks.
  FEATURE       THE APPROVED PROTOTYPE: the cradle print bay - a half-formed body curled in soul light,
                feed line to the skull, cyan status lamp. Pulses (6 frames). Rare by dressing D6.
  BREACH        a soul leak: a cracked fuel line, pale light pooling down the plate.
  CLINICAL      a pattern-archive drawer wall: rows of small steel drawers, one open (dark).
  CONTAINMENT   a decommissioned cradle: the same bay dark, glass cracked, empty.
  MACHINERY     the renderer: a furnace door with a soul-white seam and gauges.
  SECURITY      an Organization seal plate over a shutter (livery, stencilled).
  DATA          a pattern bank: a lattice of small soul-lit cells (emissive pinpoints).
Palette: gunmetal, darkmetal, livery (seals), capsule, soul, cyan (status), rust (age). The magma ink is
NOT used here - the heat of this stratum is the mood's, the light is the soul's.
