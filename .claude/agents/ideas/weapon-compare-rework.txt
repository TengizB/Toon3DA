TITLE: Weapon Pickup & Compare Rework — Auto-Card, Same-Type Swap
CREATED: 2026-06-19
---

OVERVIEW
========
A ground-up rework of the weapon pickup / compare flow that replaces the current
INSPECT-then-decide modal (weapon-pickup-interaction.txt, IMPLEMENTED) with a
ZERO-TAP, single-screen "weapon card" that auto-opens the instant the marine
settles on a weapon tile. The card adapts to context: a FREE loadout slot shows one
big "EQUIP" button (fast lane), a FULL loadout shows inline "SWAP -> Slot X" buttons
next to each held weapon (one screen, no second phase), and the start room shows the
same card with "CHOOSE THIS WEAPON" wording. Crucially, weapons now carry their
ROLLED IDENTITY (level + tier + abilities) on the GroundItem, so a Level-1 Common
Chaingun and a Level-3 Rare Chaingun are genuinely different objects you can always
swap between — the "same-type weapons collapse into a convert-to-ammo dead-end" bug
is fixed. Convert-to-ammo survives as a small secondary option, never the primary CTA.

This belongs here because the loadout is the player's run identity (3 ranged slots),
and the current flow makes the single most exciting roguelike moment — finding a
better gun — feel like paperwork: step on tile, find an INSPECT button, tap it, read
a card, tap TAKE, navigate a SECOND slot-select screen, tap a slot. That is up to
five taps and two modal phases for "I want the new gun in slot 2." This rework
collapses it to: step on tile (card auto-opens) -> tap the slot you want to replace
(or tap EQUIP if you have room). One screen, one decision tap.

This document OWNS: the auto-open trigger, the single-screen card layout, the
free-slot vs full-loadout branching, GroundItem identity storage, the start-room
integration, and the same-type-swap fix. It REPLACES weapon-pickup-interaction.txt's
two-phase modal. It DEPENDS ON weapon-levels-tiers-abilities.txt for the rolled
stats it must display and store. It does NOT redefine the tier/ability catalogue,
the tick engine, or the FrameBuffer weapon-sprite pipeline.

NO NEW TILE SYMBOL IS REQUIRED. Weapons remain entity-side GroundItems keyed by
ItemType plus a new rolled-identity payload (confirmed against docs/tile-symbols.txt).
Nothing is added to that file.


WHAT CHANGES vs WHAT STAYS
==========================
STAYS THE SAME (reused verbatim):
- GroundItem is still the ground token; renderers still share the one groundItems
  list by reference (PropRenderer / LevelRenderer / PlayerController).
- Loadout API: tryEquip, removeSlot, selectSlot, isFull, getSlot, getSlotCount.
- The evicted weapon still drops back onto the floor as a GroundItem (nothing is
  silently destroyed).
- Modal pauses the world (same pattern as InventoryOverlayRenderer); a successful
  EQUIP / SWAP costs exactly ONE turn; opening/closing the card costs ZERO turns.
- Arsenal singletons: the SAME Weapon instance the arsenal holds is what gets
  equipped (no fresh Weapon constructed on pickup) — see TECHNICAL NOTES for the
  one wrinkle this creates for same-type rolled weapons.
- EventTextSystem banners for ACQUIRED / DROPPED / +AMMO.

WHAT CHANGES:
1. AUTO-OPEN replaces the INSPECT button. Settling on a weapon tile opens the card
   immediately. TouchAction.INSPECT_WEAPON and the contextual INSPECT button are
   REMOVED. The HUD ground-name label becomes a "no card open but weapon underfoot"
   hint only (e.g. after the player closes the card and stays on the tile).
2. SINGLE SCREEN replaces the two-phase STATS_CARD -> SLOT_SELECT modal. The slot
   choices live inline on the one card. WeaponInspectOverlayRenderer's Phase enum
   is removed; it becomes a flat layout.
3. SAME-TYPE SWAP is always allowed. The old "if you already own this type, the only
   option is CONVERT TO AMMO" rule is DELETED. Owning a Chaingun no longer blocks
   equipping a different Chaingun. Convert-to-ammo becomes an always-available small
   secondary button (useful when the ground weapon is a strict downgrade and you want
   the rounds instead).
4. GROUNDITEM CARRIES IDENTITY. GroundItem gains an optional rolled-identity payload
   so the card can show real, distinct stats per ground weapon and so the equipped
   weapon keeps that identity (fixes "same class = same stats").
5. START ROOM USES THE CARD. The start room no longer auto-equips on contact; it
   opens the same card with "CHOOSE THIS WEAPON" wording. The other two offers stay
   on the floor until one is chosen (you can step off, compare, come back).
6. FREE-SLOT FAST LANE. With a free slot, the card foregrounds one large EQUIP
   button; no slot list is shown (there is nothing to evict).


PLAYER EXPERIENCE — SCENARIO WALKTHROUGHS
=========================================
The throughline: the floor offers, it never ambushes. The card always tells you
what you'd gain or lose BEFORE you commit, and the commit is a single tap on the
thing you want to happen.

--- SCENARIO A: FREE SLOT (the gift) ---
You round a corner and a gun is bobbing on a tile. You step onto it. The world
freezes and a card slides up: "Lv3 RARE CHAINGUN" in plasma-blue, its stat bars,
its two ability rows ("CRIT 9.5%", "LIFESTEAL 7%"). Because you have an open slot,
the bottom of the card is dominated by one fat green button: "EQUIP -> SLOT 2". You
tap it. The gun is yours, the card closes, "CHAINGUN ACQUIRED" flashes, enemies take
their step. Total: one step + one tap. It felt like a reward, not a menu.

--- SCENARIO B: FULL LOADOUT (the trade) ---
All three slots full. You step on a Lv5 EPIC PLASMA RIFLE. The card opens and now,
below its stats, there's a compact column of your THREE held weapons, each a
full-width row with a "SWAP" button on its right edge:
  [SLOT 1 * ] Lv4 SHOTGUN      DMG 14  RNG 6    [ SWAP -> ]
  [SLOT 2   ] Lv3 CHAINGUN     DMG 9   RNG 16   [ SWAP -> ]
  [SLOT 3   ] Lv2 RAILGUN      DMG 30  RNG 20   [ SWAP -> ]
Each row's stats are tinted green/red vs the ground weapon so you can read the trade
at a glance (the Plasma out-damages your Chaingun: that row's DMG glows red = "you'd
lose less here"). You tap "SWAP ->" on the Chaingun row. The Plasma drops into slot 2
and becomes active; your old Chaingun thuds onto the floor where you stand. One step,
one decision tap. No "now pick a slot" second screen — the slot choice WAS the tap.

--- SCENARIO C: SAME-TYPE UPGRADE (the bug, fixed) ---
You hold a Lv1 Common Chaingun. You find a Lv3 Rare Chaingun. Old system: dead-end,
"CONVERT TO AMMO" only. New system: the card shows the Rare Chaingun as a clearly
DIFFERENT weapon (blue name, "Lv3", two ability rows your grey Lv1 lacks). Your held
Lv1 Chaingun appears in the slot list like any other weapon with a "SWAP ->" button,
its stats tinted red against the upgrade. You tap SWAP on it: the Lv3 Rare is now in
your hands, the Lv1 Common is on the floor. The convert-to-ammo button is still down
in the corner if you'd rather feed your reserve — but it is no longer your ONLY move.

--- SCENARIO D: START ROOM (the opening choice) ---
You drop in unarmed. Three guns sit in a row by the portal. You step onto the first.
The same card opens, but the loadout is empty so it shows one big button reading
"CHOOSE THIS WEAPON" (not "EQUIP" — the start-room wording cues finality). You don't
love it; you tap CLOSE (zero turns), step off, walk to the second gun, step on, read
ITS card, compare in your head. You like this one. You tap "CHOOSE THIS WEAPON." It
equips, the OTHER TWO despawn ("LOADOUT SET — DESCEND"), and you head for the portal.
For the first time you actually compared the three before committing.

--- SCENARIO E: STRICT DOWNGRADE / "I just want the bullets" ---
You hold a great gun; the ground Chaingun is a junk Lv1 Common but you're low on
bullets. The card opens, every slot row's stats glow green (your guns are all better).
You ignore the SWAP buttons and tap the small "CONVERT (+20 BULLETS)" chip in the
footer. The ground gun dissolves into reserve ammo, the card closes, "+20 BULLETS"
flashes. The downgrade became sustain.


UI LAYOUT SPEC
==============
One centred modal card, drawn procedurally (ShapeRenderer + shared HUD BitmapFont),
NO new texture asset. All coordinates in world units (1280x720, origin bottom-left).
All sizes below are PLACEHOLDER constants (see TECHNICAL NOTES).

CARD FRAME
  WEAPON_CARD_WIDTH  = 760f   (wider than the old 640 to fit the inline slot list)
  WEAPON_CARD_HEIGHT = 520f
  WEAPON_CARD_ORIGIN_X = (1280 - 760) / 2  = 260f   (centred)
  WEAPON_CARD_ORIGIN_Y = (720 - 520) / 2   = 100f
  Background: dark panel, WEAPON_CARD_PANEL_ALPHA = 0.94f, 18% see-through so the
  3D view bleeds faintly behind it (keeps the dread). Border: a 3px line in the
  ground weapon's TIER color (steel-grey..hellfire-gold), so rarity reads before any
  text — the whole card is "framed in gold" for a Legendary.

VERTICAL ZONES (top to bottom inside the frame):

  ZONE 1 — HEADER (top ~70px)
    - Big weapon name, tinted in the tier color: e.g. "CHAINGUN".
    - Left of the name: a small tier ICON SHAPE for color-blind safety
      (circle=Common, square=Uncommon, triangle=Rare, diamond=Epic, star=Legendary).
    - Right of the name: "Lv3 / RARE" tag in the tier color.

  ZONE 2 — STAT BLOCK (next ~150px), two columns:
    LEFT  = the GROUND weapon's effective stats (the thing you'd gain).
    RIGHT = a slim "vs ACTIVE" delta column (green = ground better, red = worse,
            grey = equal) compared against the currently active held weapon.
    Rows (label + horizontal bar + numeric value):
      DAMAGE   bar = effDamage / WEAPON_STAT_BAR_MAX_DAMAGE (=60)
      CLIP     "N shots"
      RELOAD   "N ticks"
      RANGE    bar = effRange / WEAPON_STAT_BAR_MAX_RANGE (=20)   "N tiles"
      ACCURACY "NN%"
      AMMO     ammoType name + loaded/reserve
    Below the stat rows: a one-line ability glyph strip (one tier-colored glyph per
    rolled ability; full ability names + magnitudes are NOT shown here to save space
    — they appear on long-press tooltip, OPEN QUESTION). Common weapons show "— no
    abilities —" in grey.

  ZONE 3 — ACTION AREA (bottom ~260px) — CONTEXT-DEPENDENT:

    CASE FREE SLOT (loadout NOT full) — FAST LANE:
      One large primary button spanning most of the width:
        WEAPON_EQUIP_BUTTON_WIDTH  = 520f
        WEAPON_EQUIP_BUTTON_HEIGHT = 84f   (well above the 54px thumb minimum)
        Label: "EQUIP -> SLOT n"  (n = lowest free slot), green fill, bright border.
        Start-room variant label: "CHOOSE THIS WEAPON".
      No slot list is drawn (nothing to evict).

    CASE FULL LOADOUT — INLINE SLOT PICKER:
      A vertical list, one row per held slot (getSlotCount rows; 3 today):
        Row geometry: full card width minus margins, WEAPON_SLOT_ROW_HEIGHT = 64f,
        WEAPON_SLOT_ROW_GAP = 10f. Three rows + gaps fit comfortably in 260px.
        Each row contains, left-to-right:
          - slot index chip "[1]" (active slot gets a bright amber left-edge + "*")
          - held weapon tier-colored name + "Lvn"
          - 3 compact stat values (DMG / RNG / one more), each tinted green/red vs
            the ground weapon (this IS the comparison — per-slot, inline)
          - a right-aligned "SWAP ->" button, WEAPON_SWAP_BUTTON_WIDTH = 150f,
            WEAPON_SWAP_BUTTON_HEIGHT = 54f. The whole row is ALSO tappable (the
            button is just the visual affordance) for a fat touch target.
      The tap target per row therefore is the full row (>= 520x64) — generous.

  ZONE 4 — FOOTER (a thin strip along the very bottom, ~52px):
    - LEFT: small grey "CLOSE" button (WEAPON_CLOSE_BUTTON_WIDTH = 150f, 52f tall) —
      dismiss, weapon stays on floor, zero turns.
    - RIGHT (secondary, de-emphasised): a small "CONVERT (+N AMMO)" chip, same height,
      muted color, only shown when the ground weapon has a backing AmmoType. This is
      the demoted convert-to-ammo path. If the weapon type has no Weapon class to
      equip (e.g. a pistol stub), CONVERT is promoted to the primary action instead.

COLOR LOGIC SUMMARY
  - Tier color drives: card border, name tint, level tag, ability glyphs, tier icon.
  - Delta color (green/red/grey) drives: the "vs active" column and the per-slot
    inline stat tints. Green ALWAYS means "the ground weapon is better on this stat"
    so the player reads green = reason to take it, consistently.
  - Primary CTA (EQUIP / CHOOSE) is green-filled and largest. SWAP buttons are
    amber-outlined. CLOSE is grey. CONVERT is muted. The visual hierarchy enforces
    "equip/swap is the main verb, convert is the side option."


GAMEPLAY LOOP (precise, turn-accurate)
======================================
Precondition: the player settles (finishes a move turn) onto a tile holding a weapon
GroundItem, and no other modal is open.

1. AUTO-OPEN (zero turns). On settle, PlayerController detects a weapon GroundItem on
   the new tile and signals World, which opens WeaponInspectOverlayRenderer with the
   ground weapon's rolled identity, the active weapon, and the loadout. World enters
   the WEAPON_CARD modal state: movement/fire/rotate input ignored, enemies do not
   act, tick clock paused. (The settle step that moved the player onto the tile was
   itself one normal turn — opening the card adds nothing.)

   GUARD against re-open spam: the card auto-opens only on the FRAME the tile is first
   entered. If the player CLOSEs the card and remains on the tile, it does NOT
   re-open; instead the HUD shows a small "[ weapon underfoot ]" hint and a single
   contextual "VIEW" button reopens it on demand (covers "I closed it, want it back").
   Stepping off and back on re-triggers a fresh auto-open.

2a. FREE SLOT -> tap EQUIP (one turn). loadout.tryEquip(groundWeaponInstance) into the
    lowest free slot; remove the GroundItem from the world list; close card; banner
    "<NAME> ACQUIRED"; fire exactly one tick (enemies act).

2b. FULL LOADOUT -> tap a slot row's SWAP (one turn). For chosen slot index k:
       - capture evicted = loadout.getSlot(k)
       - loadout.removeSlot(k)
       - drop evicted on the player's current tile as a NEW GroundItem carrying the
         evicted weapon's rolled identity (see step 4)
       - loadout.tryEquip(groundWeaponInstance) (now a slot is free)
       - loadout.selectSlot(k) so the new gun lands active in the freed slot
       - remove the ground weapon's GroundItem from the world list
       - close card; banners "<OLD> DROPPED", "<NEW> ACQUIRED"
       - fire exactly one tick.
    All mutations happen in ONE synchronous block BEFORE the tick (never a
    half-applied swap; see EDGE CASES — death mid-swap).

2c. CONVERT (+N AMMO) -> tap (one turn). weaponItemTypeToAmmoType + inventory.tryAdd
    (reusing the old hook's mapping); remove the ground GroundItem; close card; banner
    "+N <AMMO>"; fire one tick. Available whenever the ground weapon maps to an ammo
    type; the convert amount may scale with the ground weapon's level/tier (OPEN
    QUESTION — flat vs scaled).

3. CLOSE / step-off (zero turns). CLOSE dismisses, weapon stays on floor, no tick.
   The card also force-closes if the player somehow leaves the tile (defensive).

4. EVICTED-WEAPON DROP carries identity. The dropped GroundItem must reproduce the
   evicted weapon's level/tier/abilities so re-picking it later shows the SAME gun.
   Build the GroundItem with the evicted weapon's rolled-identity payload (the same
   payload type the spawn roller produces). PropRenderer picks up the new bobbing
   sprite automatically next frame; its tier-color glow reflects the evicted tier.

5. START ROOM variant. Identical loop, but: the action button reads "CHOOSE THIS
   WEAPON", the loadout is empty so it is always the free-slot fast lane, and on
   EQUIP the World start-room handler ALSO despawns the other two offer GroundItems
   and flashes "LOADOUT SET — DESCEND". CLOSE in the start room leaves all three
   offers on the floor so the player can compare and return.


TECHNICAL NOTES
===============
NO NEW TILE SYMBOL (verified against docs/tile-symbols.txt). Weapons stay entity-side.

DATA ADDED TO GroundItem (item/GroundItem.java):
  Today GroundItem stores only ItemType + a count — it is "a Chaingun is here" with
  no stats. Add an OPTIONAL rolled-identity payload so a ground weapon is a SPECIFIC
  weapon, not a class token:
    - field: WeaponRoll weaponRoll  (nullable; null for non-weapon ground items and
      for legacy/un-rolled weapons which fall back to Common Lv1 display)
  where WeaponRoll is a small immutable value object (NEW, package item/ or entity/):
    public final class WeaponRoll {
        public final int weaponLevel;            // 1..MAX_WEAPON_LEVEL
        public final WeaponTier tier;            // from weapon-levels-tiers doc
        public final AbilityInstance[] abilities;// rolled, pre-scaled
    }
  Rationale: GroundItem is the only thing that persists between "weapon spawned/dropped"
  and "weapon picked up." It must hold the identity or same-type guns collapse again.
  WeaponRoll mirrors exactly what weapon-levels-tiers-abilities.txt's configureRoll(...)
  consumes, so picking up = "apply this WeaponRoll to the arsenal Weapon instance."

ARSENAL SINGLETON WRINKLE (important):
  PlayerInventory owns ONE instance per weapon class. weapon-levels-tiers stores
  level/tier/abilities ON that instance via configureRoll. That is fine while you can
  only hold one of each class. But this rework explicitly allows a held Lv1 Chaingun
  AND a ground Lv3 Chaingun to coexist on screen, and a SWAP that replaces the held
  Chaingun with the ground Chaingun. Resolution rule (RECOMMENDED, simplest):
    - The GROUND weapon's identity lives only in the GroundItem.weaponRoll (data, not
      a live Weapon). The card reads stats from the WeaponRoll + base WeaponConstants
      via GameMath (no live Weapon needed to DISPLAY).
    - On EQUIP/SWAP, apply that WeaponRoll to the arsenal singleton via configureRoll
      and equip the singleton. The PREVIOUS roll on that singleton (if it was already
      in the loadout, i.e. a same-type swap) is what gets dropped — so before
      overwriting, snapshot the singleton's current roll into the evicted GroundItem.
    - SAME-TYPE SWAP SEQUENCE (held Lv1 Chaingun, ground Lv3 Chaingun, slot k holds it):
        snapshot oldRoll = chaingun.currentRoll()        // Lv1 Common
        drop GroundItem(WEAPON_CHAINGUN, oldRoll) on player tile
        chaingun.configureRoll(groundRoll)               // becomes Lv3 Rare
        loadout already references the singleton in slot k -> it now reflects Lv3
        remove ground GroundItem
      Because the loadout holds the singleton by reference, no removeSlot/tryEquip is
      even needed for the same-CLASS case — just re-roll in place and drop the old
      roll. For DIFFERENT-class swaps, use the removeSlot/tryEquip sequence in 2b.
      The implementing agent must branch on "is the ground weapon's class already in
      the chosen slot?" — if yes, re-roll-in-place; if no, evict+equip.
    NOTE: this means the arsenal stays one-instance-per-class (no architectural
    upheaval); the per-weapon ROLL is what moves around, carried by GroundItem.
    OPEN QUESTION flags an alternative (per-loadout-slot Weapon copies) if designers
    later want to hold two of the same class at once.

REMOVED / CHANGED CLASSES:
  - input/touch/TouchAction.java — REMOVE INSPECT_WEAPON. (Optionally add VIEW_WEAPON
    for the "reopen after close while standing on tile" button; small, contextual.)
  - input/touch/TouchControllerRenderer.java — REMOVE the contextual INSPECT button.
    Add the small contextual VIEW button shown only when (weapon underfoot && no card
    open && card was previously closed on this tile).
  - input/PlayerController.java — on settle, if the new tile holds a weapon GroundItem
    AND the world is not already showing this tile's card, signal World to AUTO-OPEN
    the card (instead of merely recording standingOnWeapon for a manual INSPECT tap).
    Keep the controller UI-free: it signals intent; World owns the overlay. While the
    card modal is open, the controller is locked (ignores movement/fire), same as the
    existing action-animation lock.
  - render/WeaponInspectOverlayRenderer.java — REMOVE the Phase enum (STATS_CARD /
    SLOT_SELECT). Becomes a single flat layout per the UI spec. New/changed surface:
      void show(GroundItem groundWeapon, WeaponRoll groundRoll,
                Weapon activeWeapon, Loadout loadout, boolean startRoomMode)
      void hide(); boolean isVisible();
      void handleTap(float worldX, float worldY)   // hit-tests all buttons on ONE screen
      setOnEquipFreeSlot(Runnable)        // free-slot fast lane (and start-room CHOOSE)
      setOnSwapSlot(IntConsumer)          // slot index chosen to replace
      setOnConvertToAmmo(Runnable)
      setOnClose(Runnable)
    Still pure UI: never mutates the loadout; reports intent via callbacks; World
    performs the mutation (single source of truth).
  - world/World.java (or a split-out world/WeaponPickupController.java, RECOMMENDED) —
    owns the overlay, wires the callbacks to the equip/swap/convert/drop resolvers
    above, performs the arsenal re-roll-in-place vs evict+equip branch, and fires the
    single tick on success. Reuses the existing weaponToItemType mapping and the
    arsenal lookup (findWeaponInArsenalForType). Adds the card to the render order
    after HudRenderer (drawn on top), before the touch controller.
  - world/GameState.java (or HudState) — the modal flag becomes WEAPON_CARD (rename of
    the old WEAPON_INSPECT) so the tick loop pauses and the touch controller hides
    movement buttons while the card is up. Only ONE modal at a time (mutually exclusive
    with the inventory overlay).
  - render/HudRenderer.java — the ground-weapon NAME LABEL now only appears in the
    "card closed but standing on weapon" hint state, not as the always-on pre-inspect
    cue (auto-open replaces the need to advertise INSPECT).

START-ROOM INTEGRATION (the key behavior change there):
  Today World.handleStartRoomWeaponPickup auto-equips on contact, bypassing any card.
  Change: in the start room, settling on a weapon GroundItem opens the SAME card with
  startRoomMode=true (button text "CHOOSE THIS WEAPON"). The "despawn the other two
  offers" rule fires on the EQUIP callback, NOT on tile-contact. This means the player
  can CLOSE the card, walk to another offer, and compare before locking in — directly
  fixing problem #4. The old immediate-equip path is removed; the start room uses the
  unified card flow with only the wording + despawn-others behavior differing.
  (Recommended: World passes a boolean startRoomMode into show(); the despawn-others
  logic lives in the start-room equip resolver, gated on that flag.)

TURN-BASED FIT:
  - Auto-open, CLOSE, VIEW-reopen = ZERO turns (world paused, like inventory).

    every other world-advancing action. The marine holsters; the demon gets a step.
  All resolution is integer tile lookups + reference swaps + one WeaponRoll apply; the
  only allocation on a swap is the single new GroundItem for the evicted weapon.


VISUAL DESIGN
=============
- The card is procedural (ShapeRenderer panels/bars + shared HUD font). No texture.
- TIER FRAMING: the entire card border and the weapon name are tinted in the ground
  weapon's tier color (steel-grey -> hazard-green -> plasma-blue -> demon-violet ->
  hellfire-gold), so the player feels the rarity the instant the card appears. A
  Legendary card is unmistakably gold-framed.
- TIER ICON SHAPE left of the name (circle/square/triangle/diamond/star) gives
  color-blind redundancy alongside the level tag text.
- COMPARISON GLOW: green = ground weapon better on that stat, red = worse, grey =
  equal — consistent everywhere (the "vs active" column AND the inline per-slot stats).
  Green is always "a reason to take it."
- PRIMARY CTA is the largest, green-filled element (EQUIP / CHOOSE). SWAP buttons are
  amber-outlined and live inside their rows. CLOSE is small grey. CONVERT is a small
  muted chip. The size/color hierarchy makes the intended verb obvious at a glance.
- ABILITY GLYPH STRIP: one small tier-colored glyph per rolled ability under the
  stat block; "— no abilities —" in grey for Common. Full names on long-press tooltip
  (OPEN QUESTION).
- BANNERS reuse EventTextSystem.spawnWithColor: "<NAME> ACQUIRED" (green), "<OLD>
  DROPPED" (amber), "+N <AMMO>" (green), "LOADOUT SET — DESCEND" (amber, start room).
- The faint 6% transparency over the 3D view keeps the dungeon visible behind the card
  so the modal still feels diegetic, not a full-screen menu.


BALANCE NOTES
=============
All PLACEHOLDER — tune in playtest:
- EQUIP/SWAP/CONVERT cost 1 turn (deliberate friction); auto-OPENING and CLOSING are
  free so merely LOOKING is never punished. If 1-turn swaps feel brutal mid-firefight,
  flag a "free swap if no enemy in LOS" softening (adds a LOS check).
- AUTO-OPEN intrusiveness: pausing the world every time you step on a gun could annoy
  in a hot room. Mitigations to consider: (a) auto-open only when NOT in combat (no
  enemy in LOS), and merely show the VIEW hint when enemies are near; (b) a settings
  toggle "auto-open weapon cards." RECOMMEND (a) as the default — it preserves the
  zero-tap reward feel in calm rooms (the common case) without freezing you mid-fight.
  This directly answers the old doc's OPEN QUESTION #2 ("auto-open vs explicit tap")
  in favor of context-sensitive auto-open.
- CONVERT amount: flat per ammo type (reuse AMMO_BOX_* values) is simplest; scaling by
  ground-weapon level/tier rewards converting rare junk but muddies the "convert is the
  consolation prize" read. RECOMMEND flat for v1.
- Stat-bar normalisers (MAX_DAMAGE 60, MAX_RANGE 20) affect bar FILL only, not logic.
- Dropped-weapon safety net (evicted gun stays on the tile, carrying its roll) keeps
  bold swaps low-regret — intended. If return-trip hoarding gets tedious, the
  multi-floor rule (only CARRIED weapons descend) is the natural pressure valve.

============
1. weapon-levels-tiers-abilities.txt (DEPENDENCY): this rework is the UI/flow half of
   that system. WeaponRoll mirrors configureRoll's inputs; the card reads effective
   stats via the same GameMath.weaponLevelX helpers; tier colors/icons and the ability
   glyph strip come straight from WeaponTier / WeaponAbility. That doc must ship (or
   ship alongside) for the card to show real distinct stats. Until then, the card can
   render every weapon as Common Lv1 (graceful degrade with weaponRoll == null).
2. weapon-pickup-interaction.txt (REPLACED): mark that doc REJECTED/SUPERSEDED by this
   one once this ships — its two-phase modal and INSPECT button are removed. The
   Loadout API reuse and "evicted weapon drops on the floor" principle carry over.
3. game-start-weapon-selection-room.txt (MODIFIED): the start room's "pick one of three
   -> remove the other two" rule now fires through the card's EQUIP callback in
   startRoomMode, not on tile-contact. The room layout, generator, and portal flow are
   unchanged; only the contact behavior (card instead of instant equip) changes.
4. Inventory overlay (InventoryOverlayRenderer): a sibling modal using the same
   pause-the-world + touch-hit-test pattern. Mutually exclusive — the weapon card may
   not open while the inventory is open and vice-versa (shared modal flag). The
   inventory's EQUIP/DROP verbs should route through the SAME World swap/drop resolvers
   for consistency.
5. weapon-ground-sprites.txt (COMPLEMENTARY): the bobbing ground sprite, tinted by tier

   bobbing, tier-tinted sprite appears the frame the evicted weapon's GroundItem is
   added. No conflict.
6. Tick system (TickEventBus): EQUIP/SWAP/CONVERT advance exactly one turn via the
   existing advance path; OPEN/CLOSE/VIEW fire no tick.


OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
1. Arsenal model: keep one-instance-per-class + moving WeaponRoll (recommended,
   minimal change), or move to per-loadout-slot Weapon copies so the player could hold
   TWO of the same class at once as genuinely separate weapons? The re-roll-in-place
   trick handles same-type SWAP cleanly, but if designers want "carry two distinct
   Chainguns simultaneously," per-slot copies are required. Recommend deferring;
   re-roll-in-place ships the requested fix without an arsenal rewrite.
2. Auto-open in combat: always auto-open (simple, but freezes mid-fight) vs auto-open
   only when no enemy is in LOS (recommended) vs a settings toggle. Recommend the LOS
   gate with a VIEW button fallback when enemies are near.
3. CONVERT amount: flat (recommended) vs scaled by the ground weapon's level/tier.
4. Ability detail: glyph strip only on the card (recommended, compact) vs full ability
   name+magnitude rows. If rows, the card height must grow / scroll for Legendaries
   (5 abilities). Recommend glyphs + long-press tooltip for full text.
5. "vs active" comparison column: compare against the ACTIVE weapon (recommended, the
   gun in your hands) — the per-slot inline tints already cover the other slots, so a
   single active-comparison column plus per-slot tints answers "what do I gain/lose"
   without a third surface.
6. VIEW reopen button: worth the extra contextual button, or should CLOSE simply step
   the player off the tile is enough? Recommend keeping VIEW — closing to glance at the
   room then wanting the card back is a real flow.
7. Weapon on the EXIT tile: keep the existing exclusion (generator never places a
   weapon GroundItem on '>') so descent never races the card. Confirm with the
   level-gen owner; same exclusion the old doc flagged.

EDGE CASES
==========
- PLAYER DIES ON A SWAP TICK: apply the full mutate block (snapshot old roll, drop,
  re-roll/equip, remove ground item) synchronously, THEN fire the tick. If the
  retaliating strike is lethal, force-hide the card; the death overlay outranks all
  modals. Never leave a half-applied swap.
- DIES WHILE CARD MERELY OPEN (hazard/DoT, no action taken): force-hide the card on
  death; nothing was pending, nothing to roll back.
- EMPTY LOADOUT (just left start room with no gun): isFull() is false -> always the
  free-slot fast lane; SWAP list never appears. Good, no special case.
- WEAPON TYPE WITH NO BACKING Weapon CLASS (pistol stub): no instance to equip; the
  card still SHOWS (stats from WeaponRoll/ItemType), but the primary action becomes
  CONVERT-TO-AMMO (promoted from the footer) since EQUIP/SWAP can't produce a real gun.
- EVICTED DROP ON AN OCCUPIED TILE: the ground weapon being taken is removed first, so
  the tile is free for the evicted gun in the normal case. If a non-weapon GroundItem
  also sits there, two sprites briefly overlap (acceptable for v1); define "first
  weapon GroundItem on the tile" as the one the next settle auto-opens. Polish option:
  drop the evicted weapon on the first walkable, weapon-free cardinal neighbour.
- MODAL OPEN + WORLD REBUILD / FLOOR DESCENT: hide and null the dangling GroundItem /
  WeaponRoll references in the card on world teardown; the groundItems list is rebuilt
  per floor, so the card must clear its held references on hide().
- LEGACY / UN-ROLLED GROUND WEAPON (weaponRoll == null): render as Common Lv1, no
  abilities, grey frame. Equipping applies a Common Lv1 roll. No crash path.
