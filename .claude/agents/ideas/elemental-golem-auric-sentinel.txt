TITLE: Auric Sentinel — gold golem whose shards are armour and ammo
---

OVERVIEW
========
The fourth ELEMENTAL GOLEM (EnemyFamily.GOLEM). An upright figure of amber and gold
crystal on darker violet limbs, a blazing spike-crown on its head — and three golden
SHARDS orbiting it in a slow ring.

The shards are the entire enemy. Each one absorbs ONE incoming hit COMPLETELY,
whatever its size, and each one is also a projectile it must SPEND to attack. A
railgun slug and a chaingun tick are worth exactly the same to a shard: nothing. It
is the first enemy in the game where the RIGHT WEAPON matters more than the right
position, and it is deliberately the mirror of the Rimeshell Lancer (which wants big
burst into an open shell).

FAMILY & ROLE
=============
  family()               EnemyFamily.GOLEM
  role()                 EnemyRole.SOLDIER          (TP band 36-66)
  positionalMultiplier() BalanceConfig.POSITIONAL_MULT_RANGED (1.30)
  isRanged()             true
  tacticalVerb()         "SENTINEL: its shards are armour AND ammo — strip them with
                          rapid fire, or bait it into spending them."

No shipped archetype poses this question. Eye Tyrant asks "where are you standing";
Iron Stalker asks "how much do you commit"; the Sentinel asks "WHAT ARE YOU HOLDING".
A player with a Chaingun laughs at it; a player with a Railgun has to think.

THE ABILITY — ORBITING SHARDS
=============================
State: enemy.shardCount (int, 0..AURIC_MAX_SHARDS = 3), enemy.shardRegenCounter.

  ABSORB    While shardCount > 0, ANY single incoming damage instance is NULLIFIED and
            consumes exactly one shard. Not reduced — nullified, whatever the amount.
            A 90-damage railgun slug and a 10-damage chaingun tick both cost the
            Sentinel one shard and cost the player one shot.
            THE HOOK GOES IN Enemy.applyDamage, BEFORE block and flat armour, and it
            must be per-INSTANCE-of-damage, not per-turn: a multi-pellet or multi-tile
            weapon that resolves as several separate applyDamage calls strips several
            shards in one trigger pull. That is the intended reward for a wide weapon,
            and it must fall out of the implementation rather than being special-cased.
            DoT ticks (applyDoTDamage) do NOT consume shards and are NOT absorbed —
            they bypass to HP. Burning a Sentinel down is a legitimate, distinct answer
            and keeps status builds relevant.

  SPEND     Firing consumes one shard. With shardCount == 0 it CANNOT attack at all: it
            retreats to maximum range and regenerates, fully defanged. Making the
            player's aggression directly disarm the enemy is the point — the counter to
            its offence and the counter to its defence are the same action.

  REGROW    +1 shard every AURIC_SHARD_REGEN_TURNS (3) enemy turns, up to the cap. The
            counter advances every turn, including turns it fired, so a passive player
            faces a permanently full ring.

  FIRE RULE Mandatory cardinal-line constraint (isSameCardinalLine) — the launched
            shard travels only along a true row or column, with clear LOS, within
            attackRangeTiles(). No diagonals, ever. The Sentinel steers toward
            alignment on its move turns like every other ranged archetype.

WEAPON MATH — WHY IT IS INTERESTING
  Against a full ring, per trigger pull:
    Railgun         (1 huge hit)          -> 1 shard,  0 HP damage. Terrible.
    Double Barrel   (1-2 hits)            -> 1-2 shards, 0 HP. Poor.
    Chaingun        (many small hits)     -> ring gone in a burst, then full damage.
    Shotgun         (1 hit, fast reload)  -> mediocre, but cheap to strip with.
    Incinerator/DoT (bypasses shards)     -> ignores the gimmick entirely.
  The Sentinel is therefore a LOADOUT TEST, and pairs beautifully with the Rimeshell
  Lancer, which inverts every one of those answers. A floor containing both cannot be
  solved with a single weapon — which is exactly what the build-diversity pillar wants
  out of an encounter.

BEHAVIOUR — COMMIT / EXECUTE INTEGRATION
========================================
The turn engine does not change (docs/enemy-system.txt, PRE-COMMITTED INTENT MODEL).

  computeNextPlan()  -> add computeSentinelPlan(enemy, ...) beside the existing ranged
                        planner. Preference: fire (shard available, cardinal line, LOS,
                        in range) > retreat to max range while shardless > step toward
                        cardinal alignment > wait. It KITES when shardless and only
                        then — a Sentinel with a full ring stands and shoots, so its
                        aggression level is itself a readout of its shard count.
  phaseBExecute()    -> executeRangedAttack decrements shardCount on a committed shot
                        (and still fires — the shard is spent even if the shot whiffs
                        under the standard fairness re-validation; committing to a shot
                        it cannot take is a real cost and a real bait).
  damage path        -> the absorb hook in Enemy.applyDamage, before block/armor.
  end of its turn    -> advance shardRegenCounter, grow a shard when due.

TELEGRAPH & READABILITY
=======================
  - The shard COUNT is visible at all times as the orbiting ring — this is the whole
    readability contract, and it needs no HUD, no bar and no text.
  - Shots are committed a full turn ahead in the standard ranged intent frame with the
    predicted-damage number.
  - 15 damage is far under TELEGRAPH_MAX_UNTELEGRAPHED_HIT_FRACTION, and is telegraphed
    regardless.
  - A shardless Sentinel visibly stops attacking and backs off, so "it is disarmed
    right now" is legible from across the room.

VISUAL EFFECTS (renderer spec — EnemyRenderer / ImpactEffectSystem)
===================================================================
Procedural only; no new texture assets beyond the shared sheet.

  1. THE ORBIT RING — THE SIGNATURE. shardCount additive gold quads orbiting the
     billboard: angle = wallClock * AURIC_SHARD_ORBIT_SPEED + shardIndex * (2*PI/3),
     with a slight vertical bob per shard and a size falloff for the far side of the
     ring so it reads as 3D orbit rather than a flat halo. Drawn in the same pass as
     the sprite, after it, respecting the same z-buffer occlusion test.
     Pre-allocate the quad transforms in the constructor — this draws every frame for
     every visible Sentinel and must not allocate.

  2. ABSORB — SHARD SHATTER. The struck shard flashes white, bursts into gold sparks,
     and the ring VISIBLY closes up (remaining shards re-space to even angles over
     ~0.25s). Plus a distinct "clink" of feedback: a bright gold ring-flash on the
     billboard INSTEAD of the usual red hit flash, so the player instantly knows the
     hit did nothing to its health. That distinction is the most important single
     effect in this doc — without it the enemy reads as a bullet sponge instead of a
     puzzle.

  3. LAST SHARD GONE — EXPOSED. The body's amber glow drops to a dull matte; a thin
     violet crack pattern lights across the sprite. It looks fragile because it is:
     70 HP and no answer. This is the "now" cue.

  4. REGROW. A gold mote rises from the ground, spirals up into the ring and
     crystallises into a new shard with a soft chime-flash. Long enough to see and
     interrupt-worthy — a player who sees it should feel the clock running.

  5. FIRE. The shard breaks orbit, streaks down the lane as an additive gold bolt with
     a short trail, and the ring closes behind it. The projectile is the armour
     leaving — the player watches the enemy disarm itself.

  6. DEATH — COLLAPSE. Every remaining shard falls out of orbit and shatters on the
     floor, then the body comes apart into gold shards with a bright amber flash.

COUNTERPLAY
===========
  - RAPID FIRE STRIPS IT. Chaingun / Assault Rifle / any multi-hit weapon deletes the
    ring in one burst.
  - Wide/multi-hit weapons strip several shards per trigger pull.
  - DoT bypasses shards entirely — Incinerator, Bleed, hazard fire all hit HP directly.
  - BAIT IT. Every shot it takes is one shard less. Dance in and out of its lane while
    a slow weapon reloads and it will disarm itself.
  - Do NOT open on it with a Railgun. Save the burst for AFTER the ring is gone —
    with 70 HP, one good hit into an exposed Sentinel is nearly the whole fight.

BALANCE — TP DERIVATION
=======================
  Shard absorption priced as an ARMOUR POOL. Absorbing a hit is exactly "extra
  effective HP", and armorPool() feeds GameMath.enemyEffectiveHitPoints directly.
  Against the modelled player, one absorbed hit is worth about one reference hit:
      shards absorbed over a fight ~= 3 (the ring) + ~0.5 (one regrow) = 3.5
      value per absorbed hit          ~= REFERENCE_PLAYER_DPT (25) * 0.8 = 20
      armorPool()                      = 3.5 * 20 = 70f
  The 0.8 factor is the honest discount for the fact that a player who reads the enemy
  correctly strips shards with CHEAP hits, not reference-sized ones. That derivation
  must be written into the comment above armorPool(), because the number looks absurd
  without it.

  eHP = enemyEffectiveHitPoints(70, 70, 0, 0, REFERENCE_PLAYER_DPT=25)
      = 70 + 70 = 140.0
  survivalTurns      = 140.0 / 25 = 5.6
  basicDamagePerTurn = 15 / 2 = 7.5
  TP = 7.5 * 5.6 * 1.30 = 54.6      -> SOLDIER band (36-66) OK, mid-band

  RISK, STATED PLAINLY: this is the archetype in the golem set whose TP is most
  sensitive to the player's loadout — its effective eHP swings hard between a Chaingun
  build and a Railgun build, and a single averaged armorPool cannot express that. The
  simulator is the right instrument: run ./gradlew balanceSim across the policy matrix
  and check the S-* bands per policy, not just the aggregate. If the spread is
  unacceptable, the lever is AURIC_MAX_SHARDS (3 -> 2), not the HP.

  Credits 14 sits between Void Shroud (12) and Mire Wraith (15) — consistent with TP.
  XP is derived; never add an XP constant.

  GATE: BalanceConfig change -> ./gradlew test AND ./gradlew balanceSim
  (docs/game-balance-authority.txt SECTION 7).

FLOOR BANDS & SPAWNING
======================
  Floors 3-10+. The last golem introduced: by depth 3 the player has a second weapon,
  which is precisely the decision the Sentinel tests. Shipping it on floor 1, against a
  single starting weapon, would be a wall rather than a puzzle.

  EVERY-FLOOR PRESENCE: the four golems are banded so at least one GOLEM archetype is
  available at every depth (Lancer 1+, Spiresower 1+, Colossus 2+, Sentinel 3+); they
  join the normal mixed roster immediately, with no generator change.

  BEST PAIRING: with the Rimeshell Lancer, whose counters it inverts. A room with both
  cannot be solved by one weapon.

INTERACTIONS
============
  - CARDINAL-LINE RULE: mandatory and satisfied — shard launches only travel a true
    cardinal lane and never re-target after commit.
  - STATUS / DoT: deliberately bypasses shards. This is the balance safety valve and
    the reason a status build is never hard-countered.
  - HAZARDS: fire tiles damage it through the shards (hazard damage is a DoT tick).
    Herding a Sentinel into a Colossus's molten trail is a genuinely clever play; keep
    it working.
  - BLOCK / ARMOR: shards resolve BEFORE both, so the ordering in applyDamage is
    shards -> block -> flat armor -> HP. Write that order into the method comment.
  - ENCOUNTER BUDGET: ordinary SOLDIER; no anchor privileges.
  - OTHER GOLEMS: pairs with the Rimeshell Lancer by design (inverted weapon
    counters). Behind a Verdant Spiresower's spires it is frustrating — the spires
    deny its own lanes as much as the player's sightlines, so the combination is
    self-limiting rather than oppressive.

OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
  1. Should a shard absorb a hit ENTIRELY (as specced) or cap the absorption at some
     value? Entire absorption is the readable, teachable rule and the reason the enemy
     is interesting; capping it turns the Sentinel into ordinary armour. RECOMMEND
     keeping full absorption and tuning AURIC_MAX_SHARDS instead.
  2. Do shards block the AREA/blast component of explosive weapons, or only direct
     hits? RECOMMEND blast counts as one instance (one shard) — simple and consistent.
  3. Should a shardless Sentinel flee the ROOM entirely rather than kite at max range?
     RECOMMEND kiting only; a fleeing enemy that leaves the encounter is a new
     behaviour the AI has no precedent for.
  4. Melee/backstab: does a backstab bypass shards? RECOMMEND NO — a universal bypass
     erases the puzzle for anyone holding a melee option.
  5. If '(' collides with a shipped EXCAVATION_SITE room, fall back to '_'.
