VISUAL DESIGN - STRATUM D, THE WOUND (mood corrupted_violet)
------------------------------------------------------------
Muscle under strain (dense swollen fibre bundles with constrictions - the prototype's third pass; the
lobe version read as sausages and the plank version as wood: see LESSONS below), translucent glands lit
by ichor, branching ichor veins, the torn facility edge along the top with rivets still in it.
  BASE (x3)     the approved prototype minus the drill (the drill on every tile was the wallpaper
                problem): torn plating at different heights, fibre bundles, veins; one variant has no
                plating at all (pure tissue).
  STRUCTURE     a surviving steel column, wrapped and half-swallowed by tissue.
  LIGHT         a large gland cluster, heartbeat pulse.
  SERVICE (x2)  a severed pipe still dripping into tissue; cables grown through like nerves.
  MARKER        a facility sign plate (D-xx) half-absorbed, readable only at the edge.
  FEATURE       the buried drill (prototype): the bit, its flutes and collar, tissue scarred around it.
  BREACH        (this stratum's base is breach) -> an open wound: a vertical split showing ribs
                (bone) and a deep ichor glow inside.
  CLINICAL      a medical panel overgrown: the white livery visible through membrane.
  CONTAINMENT   a membrane sac in the wall with a shape moving inside (glow frames).
  MACHINERY     a reactor housing crushed by a fist of muscle.
  SECURITY      a blast door section bent inward by the tissue.
  DATA          a terminal swallowed by membrane, its cyan screen still glowing through.
Palette: flesh, flesh_dk, membrane, ichor, bone, chitin_dk; gunmetal/darkmetal/rust for the dying
facility. No red (V5): wound reads through anatomy and glow, never blood.
LESSONS from the prototype (go into the guide): separate lobes read as sausages; parallel full-height
bundles read as planks unless constricted and varied in width; glands must be op="add" or they are
buried by the muscle's height.
