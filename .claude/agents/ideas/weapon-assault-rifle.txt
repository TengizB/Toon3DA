TITLE: Assault Rifle — precision automatic bullet rifle
---

CONCEPT
A bullet-fed automatic rifle that shares ammunition with the Chaingun (AmmoType.BULLETS)
but fills a different combat role. Where the Chaingun sprays a 3-round burst with low
per-bullet accuracy, the Assault Rifle fires one precise, hard-hitting round per press at
longer effective range. It is the player's reliable mid-range workhorse.

GAMEPLAY LOOP
- Single hitscan bullet per fire press (semi-auto feel), marching the facing direction
  tile by tile up to range, stopping at the first wall/door/enemy (no penetration).
- Larger magazine than the burst weapons so the player can stay in the fight; 1-tick reload.
- High base accuracy rewards lining up cardinal shots at distance, contrasting the
  Chaingun's "spray and pray" identity.
