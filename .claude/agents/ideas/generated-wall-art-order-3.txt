and ceilings must be created: the format, tiling, trim lines, the region set, the placement rules, the
design and repetition rules, materials, glow, output, verification, every lesson and every piece of owner

region: its RegionWallSet row stops aliasing set A and loads its own manifest (order 2's mechanism).


OVERVIEW
--------
The second and third region sets. B, the HARVESTING GALLERIES (route: Research Wing), is an industrial
abattoir passed off as a lab - the first crack in the briefing lives in its tiles. C, the RELIQUARY
(route: Reactor Depths), is the resurrection heart where the planet's soul is rendered into printer
fuel - where the player comes from. After this order three regions look like themselves; D and E still
borrow set A.

-----
IN
  - walls.py: the REGIONS rows for research_wing and reactor_depths, each a complete set: HALL (BASE x3, STRUCTURE,
    LIGHT, SERVICE, VENT, MARKER, BREACH), the six room families (BASE x2 + ACCENT each), SIGNATURE
    (BASE x2 + ACCENT), 4 doors, 2 columns, floor + ceiling.

VISUAL DESIGN - REGION B, THE HARVESTING GALLERIES (mood clinical_white)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Glazed greige-celadon tile to shoulder height, form-tied concrete above, a hook rail at row ~52, a steel
drain at the floor (the approved prototype). Clean surfaces, wrong stains (flesh_dk, never red).
  HALL      BASE x3 the prototype's tile field - chips/cracks/missing tiles and grime differ; ONLY ONE
            variant carries a hook (a hook on every tile was the wallpaper problem). STRUCTURE a concrete
            pier with a downpipe. LIGHT a caged bulkhead lamp (cyan-white lens). SERVICE a hose reel +
            standpipe. VENT a floor-level extraction grille with a stained mouth. MARKER a yield board
            (tally slots, some plugged - "quota" without words, stencil G2-xx). BREACH chitin plates
            pushing tiles off from below, ichor in the cracks.
  MEDICAL   a surgical bay: white tile full height, a stainless scrub trough, a floor drain.
            ACCENT a mirror panel over the trough (capsule glass, no glow).
  SECURITY  a sliding barred gate over tile, a guard's hatch. ACCENT a shackle rail with cuffs.
  CONTAINMENT a specimen wall: rows of small sealed hatches. ACCENT a recessed specimen tank, cloudy,
            drifting tissue strands, faint ichor glow (no shape, no figure - W14).
  MACHINERY rendering press housings, heavy steel, a hopper. ACCENT a gauge cluster (cyan needles).
  RESEARCH  dissection-lab panels with sample shelves. ACCENT a tally terminal with a paper strip.
  STORAGE   cold-store doors with frost bands. ACCENT a meat-hook rack of empty hooks.
  SIGNATURE THE KILLING FLOOR: a full hook bay - BASE x2 hooks on trolleys at different heights, stained
            tile below; ACCENT an empty hook bay: chains swinging at rest, a drip channel, a hose (no
            carcass, no shape - W14).
  DOORS     a stainless swing-door pair with kick plates and a drain at the sill; keycard panels.
  COLUMNS   a tiled pier (round: a tiled drum).
  FLOOR     tile with a drain grid; CEILING hook rails and caged lamps.

VISUAL DESIGN - REGION C, THE RELIQUARY (mood amber_hot)
-------------------------------------------------------
Heavy riveted gunmetal/darkmetal, fuel manifolds under the ceiling, the pale SOUL light as the region's
only light. Under amber_hot the steel goes bronze and the soul light stays white.
  HALL      BASE x3 the revised prototype reliquary_plate: riveted heavy plate in uneven widths, a fuel
            manifold along the cornice, a thin CONTINUOUS soul trickle in the floor channel (a line, never a
            spot - guide 8.3 R-3); variants move the plate seams and rivets only. STRUCTURE a massive rib with a hydraulic ram.
            LIGHT a soul conduit window (capsule glass, soul flowing - 6 glow frames, animate=all).
            SERVICE a valve-wheel cluster. VENT a coolant jacket grille with frost bands. MARKER a
            reprint counter plate (stencil KR-xx, a column of tally marks). BREACH a soul leak: a
            cracked fuel line, pale light pooling down the plate.
  MEDICAL   the pattern infirmary: drawer walls of stored patterns. ACCENT one drawer open, dark.
  SECURITY  Organization seal plates over shutters (livery, stencilled). ACCENT a sealed cradle-key
            lock with a cyan lamp.
  CONTAINMENT sealed fuel-holding tanks, dark. ACCENT a tank with cracked glass, the soul dregs low.
  MACHINERY the renderer: furnace doors with a soul-white seam. ACCENT a pressure gauge bank.
  RESEARCH  a pattern bank: a lattice of small soul-lit cells. ACCENT a pattern reader console.
  STORAGE   fuel canister racks (capsule glass, faint soul). ACCENT an empty rack, one canister broken.
  SIGNATURE THE SOUL REFINERY (replaces the dropped cradle bank - D13): BASE x2 refinery plating with
            condenser feed lines and outflow pipes (no focal point - W13); ACCENT the SOUL CONDENSER (the
            revised prototype: a glass column of rendered soul, a filtration coil, a gauge, a valve, a
            cyan status lamp; 6-frame pulse, animate=all; fully inside its face).
  DOORS     a riveted pressure door with a soul-lit seam; keycard panels.
  COLUMNS   a ribbed reactor stanchion (square: a fuel riser).
  FLOOR     grating over soul-lit channels (no emissive - texels only); CEILING fuel manifolds.
