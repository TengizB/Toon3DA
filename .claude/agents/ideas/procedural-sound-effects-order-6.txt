OVERVIEW
--------
Order 6 of the procedural-sound-effects series (orders 1-5 live in
`procedural-sound-effects.txt`; this is the next in order). Two jobs:

  1. MISSING MOMENTS. Several things the player does or suffers are still silent.
     The worst of them is HEALING: a medkit used from the INVENTORY and the auto-doc
     HEAL STATION both restore HP in total silence (only the HEAL button's path was
     wired). Low health, an enemy's WIND_UP telegraph, reading a terminal and
     opening/closing the inventory are also silent.
  2. A HALF-LIFE-INSPIRED RETUNE of what already ships. Not a copy: no sampled or
     recreated HL sound, no voice, no HEV speech. The INSPIRATION is the production
     language — an industrial research facility heard through its own hard rooms:
       - gunshots that REFLECT off concrete (a short damped room echo on every loud
         ballistic shot and on explosions);
       - STRUCK METAL everywhere (inharmonic bar partials): doors, reloads, ammo,
         shell casings, footsteps on grating, the guarded clang;
       - PNEUMATIC doors (hiss + slide + seated clank);
       - the SUIT's voice as a UI language: clean square/sine bleeps for heal,
         armour charge, level-up, low-health warning and a flatline under death;
       - mechanical afterthoughts that make a gun feel handled: the shotgun's pump
         after the shot, the pistol's casing tinkle, the melee WHOOSH before contact.

Underlying need (the owner's words: "add more sounds, like healing and so on",
"sound like a half life game"): the game should feel INHABITED and PHYSICAL by ear,
and every restorative or dangerous moment should have its own recognisable sound.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - Two synthesis-toolkit additions (content-agnostic, reused by many recipes):
      a) `WaveformKind.METAL` — a struck-bar oscillator (partials 1, 2.756, 5.404,
         8.933 at falling weights). One enum constant with its own body, no switch.
      b) A per-DEFINITION ROOM ECHO: `SoundDefinition.Builder.roomEcho(delaySeconds,
         feedback, dampingHz, wetMix)` — a feedback delay line with a one-pole
         low-pass in the loop, applied once to the summed layers before soft clip.
         The tail length is computed by a GameMath formula; the whole sound gets a
         short end fade when an echo is present so it still ends on zero (no click).
  - Six NEW sounds (`GameSoundId` + one `register()` each):
      HEAL_STATION        the auto-doc charger: pneumatic hiss, a rising charge
                          tone, two confirm bleeps
      LOW_HEALTH_WARNING  the suit's warning: three urgent descending square bleeps,
                          delayed so they follow (not mask) the hurt sound
      ENEMY_WIND_UP       ONE shared sound for every committed WIND_UP (charger rush,
                          spire sow, generic wind-up, self-destruct priming) — a
                          rising servo/whine at the enemy. Shared for the same reason
                          the ranged launch is shared: "it is about to do something
                          big" must not depend on the family. Pitched by size.
      TERMINAL_ACCESS     computer chatter: a quick run of data bleeps when the
                          player steps up to a log terminal or an EVENT console
      UI_MENU_OPEN        HUD blip up — the inventory opening
      UI_MENU_CLOSE       HUD blip down — the inventory closing
  - Wire the SILENT HEALS: inventory medkit use -> PLAYER_HEAL; auto-doc ->
    HEAL_STATION.
  - HL-style RETUNE of existing recipes (same ids, new layers):
      FIRE_PISTOL, FIRE_SHOTGUN (+ pump), FIRE_DOUBLE_BARREL, FIRE_CHAINGUN,
      FIRE_ASSAULT_RIFLE, FIRE_RAILGUN, FIRE_ROCKET (room echo; pistol casing tinkle),
      FIRE_FIST / FIRE_KNIFE / FIRE_HAMMER (swing whoosh),
      WEAPON_RELOAD_START / _COMPLETE (metal clacks), WEAPON_SWITCH (HUD tick),
      PLAYER_GUARDED (struck metal), PLAYER_DEATH (+ flatline bleep),
      DOOR_OPEN / DOOR_CLOSE (pneumatic), DOOR_LOCKED (deny buzz),
      PICKUP_AMMO / _ARMOUR (battery charge) / _WEAPON / _MEDICAL,
      PLAYER_HEAL (hiss + rising chime), LEVEL_UP (suit chime), FOOTSTEP (grating),
      BARREL_EXPLOSION (room echo).
  - Raise `GAME_SFX_MAX_DISTINCT_SOUNDS` 64 -> 72 (owner-requested growth; D1).
  - `docs/sound-system.txt` updated in the same pass.

OUT (deliberately not doing now)
  - Any voice or speech ("HEV" lines). There is no VO in this game and must not be.
  - Recreating or sampling any real Half-Life sound. Inspiration only.
  - Music, ambient beds / room tone loops. Every sound stays a one-shot under 1 s.
  - Fire ignition / hazard crackle, spire/shard/crust golem ability sounds, route-map
    and pause-menu UI sounds, a bullet ricochet off walls (no wall-hit event exists
    to hang it on) — each needs a new seam in another package; next order.
  - Per-family wind-up recipes (six sounds for no gain in legibility).
  - Any change to the mixer rules, distance model or the EFFECTS setting.
  - Tests (non-balance; see TESTS).


GAMEPLAY LOOP
-------------
Nothing changes in gameplay. Presentation only:
  - Player uses a medkit from the inventory -> inventory closes, turn is spent,
    HP rises -> PLAYER_HEAL plays (same frame as the green +HP text).
  - Player steps next to a charged auto-doc -> HEAL_STATION plays with the heal.
  - HP crosses DOWN through GAME_SFX_LOW_HEALTH_FRACTION -> LOW_HEALTH_WARNING
    plays once; it re-arms only after HP climbs back above the threshold.
  - An enemy COMMITS a wind-up (visible rim flash all next player turn) ->
    ENEMY_WIND_UP plays at the enemy on that same commit.
  - Player steps next to an unread log terminal (a take is actually spent) or an
    EVENT console (the event actually opens) -> TERMINAL_ACCESS plays.
  - Player opens / closes the inventory -> UI_MENU_OPEN / UI_MENU_CLOSE.


RULES
-----
  R1  The standing rule holds: every new sound is redundant with something drawn
      (green heal text + particles, the HP bar colour, the rim-flash telegraph, the
      bark/exchange/event overlay, the inventory screen itself).
  R2  Nothing here changes gameplay state. `./gradlew test` and `./gradlew
      balanceSim` results are identical before and after.
  R3  ENEMY_WIND_UP is fired through `enemy/EnemyVoiceListener` with a new
      `EnemyVoiceMoment.WIND_UP`; `EnemyManager` still never imports audio. The
      moment has NO per-family binding — it resolves through the per-moment
      fallback to the one shared recipe.
  R4  Self-destruct priming announces WIND_UP only on the FIRST prime (the
      countdown re-telegraphs every turn; the sound must not).
  R5  Menu sounds (UI_MENU_*) may play across the hard-pause boundary: they are the
      overlay's OWN sound, exactly like LEVEL_UP. They still obey the EFFECTS
      setting. Achieved by syncing suppression to the run phase at the open/close
      site, not by a bypass flag.
  R6  Every recipe (echo included) ends on exactly zero and stays within
      GAME_SFX_MAX_DURATION_SECONDS (1.0 s).
  R7  Room echo is used only where HL's rooms would ring: loud ballistic fire,
      railgun, rocket, explosions. Never on UI, pickups, voices or the player's body.


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
-----------------
Healing finally SOUNDS like relief on every path. Low health is felt as an alarm
rather than only a red bar. The charger's wind-up becomes something you HEAR coming,
which is exactly the read the telegraph system is trying to teach. The facility
sounds like concrete and steel: shots ring off the walls, doors hiss, the shotgun
pumps. No teaching topic needed — sound is a second channel.

INTERACTIONS
------------
  - Mixer: ENEMY_WIND_UP is ENEMY-category, so the per-turn cap (3) and stack
    falloff apply — a room of chargers committing at once is a crowd, not a smear.
  - Story duck: unchanged; TERMINAL_ACCESS usually precedes a bark sting and ducks
    under it naturally.
  - Noise seam: every new definition declares loudness (menu/heal/warning = 0,
    terminal 10, wind-up 35, heal station 20).
  - sim/SimWorld: no listener wired, no GameAudio -> unaffected.


EDGE CASES
----------
  - Medkit USE that heals 0 (full HP): no heal sound (the sound follows healAmount>0).
  - Low-health crossing on the killing blow: HP 0 is not "low" (fraction > 0 guard),
    so death is never stacked on a warning.
  - Auto-doc heal with healAmount <= 0: silent (existing early return).
  - Hard-pause overlay: all gameplay sounds suppressed as before; menu blips sync
    suppression to the new phase at the call site (R5).
  - App resume: mixer reset as before; nothing new to reset.
  - Cached WAVs: every retuned recipe changes its cache key, so stale WAVs are
    never reloaded; old files are simply orphaned (same as any retune).


ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
-------------------
  A1  Using a medkit from the inventory plays PLAYER_HEAL in the same frame as the
      green "+N HP" text (World.applyInventoryConsumableEffect).
  A2  Stepping next to a charged auto-doc plays HEAL_STATION with the heal.
  A3  Falling to or below GAME_SFX_LOW_HEALTH_FRACTION of max HP (and above 0)
      plays LOW_HEALTH_WARNING once; it does not replay until HP has risen above
      the threshold and fallen again.
  A4  Every setWindUp commit in EnemyManager, and the first self-destruct prime,
      reports EnemyVoiceMoment.WIND_UP; World plays ENEMY_WIND_UP at the enemy,
      pitched by its height multiplier. EnemyManager imports nothing from audio.
  A5  Spending a log-terminal take and opening an EVENT console each play
      TERMINAL_ACCESS.
  A6  Opening / closing the inventory plays UI_MENU_OPEN / UI_MENU_CLOSE, even
      though the inventory is a hard-pause overlay; with EFFECTS = OFF they are
      silent.
  A7  WaveformKind.METAL and SoundDefinition.roomEcho exist; the synthesiser holds
      no switch on waveform; every new GameMath method has the Formula/Derivation/
      Edge-cases block.
  A8  All 70 registered sounds synthesise to <= 1.0 s, peak below full scale after
      soft clip, and end on a sample of 0 (verified by a throwaway offline render,
      not a committed test).
  A9  GameSoundId.COUNT == 70 <= GAME_SFX_MAX_DISTINCT_SOUNDS (72).
  A10 The retuned recipes listed under SCOPE carry the HL traits named there
      (echo / metal / pump / whoosh / hiss / flatline), readable in the catalog.
  A11 `./gradlew build` green; `./gradlew test` green and `./gradlew balanceSim`
      bands unchanged.
  A12 docs/sound-system.txt describes the METAL oscillator, the room echo, the six
      new sounds, their firing sites, WIND_UP, the new cap and this order.

RISKS AND UNKNOWNS
  - Echo tails lengthen weapon sounds; with a 0.04 s chaingun re-trigger, stacked
    tails could build up voice pressure. Mitigation: short, low-wet echo on
    rapid-fire guns; the mixer's voice-pressure rule already bounds it.
  - An echo tail that does not reach silence clicks — hence the end fade (R6).
