VISUAL DESIGN - STRATUM B, THE HARVESTING GALLERIES (mood clinical_white)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Glazed greige-celadon tile to shoulder height (the approved prototype), form-tied concrete above, a
continuous hook rail at row ~52 and a steel drain gutter at the floor. Clean surfaces, wrong stains.
  BASE (x3)     the approved prototype: tile field (seeded chips/cracks/missing tiles), rail, drain; the
                three variants differ in which tiles are chipped and where the grime runs - only ONE of
                three carries a hook (hooks are FEATURE-rare, the prototype's hook on every tile was the
                wallpaper problem).
  STRUCTURE     a concrete pier with a drainage downpipe, the rail bracketed to it.
  LIGHT         a caged bulkhead lamp over the tile line (cyan-white lens), cage shadow carved.
  SERVICE (x2)  a hose reel + standpipe; a row of steel sampling ports with capped nozzles.
  MARKER        a yield board: a steel frame of tally slots (stencilled numbers, some slots filled with
                dark plugs) - the "quota" read without words. Stencil G2-xx.
  FEATURE       a hook bay: three hooks on trolleys, chains at different lengths, stains under them.
  BREACH        chitin growth: dark plates (chitin_dk) pushing tiles off the wall from below, ichor in
                the cracks.
  CLINICAL      white tile with a stainless scrub trough and a mirror panel (capsule glass, no glow).
  CONTAINMENT   a specimen tank recessed in the wall: membrane-like glass, a dark curled shape inside,
                faint ichor glow (emissive, small).
  MACHINERY     a rendering press: heavy steel, a hopper mouth, a gauge (cyan needle light).
  SECURITY      a sliding steel gate (bars, carved) over tile.
  DATA          a tally terminal: cyan screen, a printer slot with a paper strip.
Palette: ceramic, concrete, darkmetal, gunmetal, flesh_dk (stains), chitin_dk (breach), ichor. No red.
