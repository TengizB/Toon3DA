VISUAL DESIGN - REGION D, THE WOUND (mood corrupted_violet)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Muscle under strain (dense swollen fibre bundles with constrictions - the prototype's third pass),
translucent glands lit by ichor (op="add", or the muscle buries them), branching ichor veins, the torn
facility edge along the top with rivets still in it. No red: the wound reads through anatomy and glow.
  HALL      BASE x3 the prototype WITHOUT the drill (the drill on every tile was wallpaper): torn plating
            at different heights; one variant pure tissue. STRUCTURE a surviving steel column wrapped
            and half-swallowed. LIGHT a gland cluster (8 glow frames, a double heartbeat, animate=all).
            SERVICE a severed pipe dripping into tissue. VENT cables grown through like nerves. MARKER
            a facility sign plate (D-xx) half absorbed. BREACH an open wound: a vertical split showing
            ribs (bone) and a deep ichor glow.
  MEDICAL   a medical wall overgrown: white livery visible through membrane. ACCENT a dispenser
            swallowed, its cyan lamp still lit inside the membrane.
  SECURITY  a blast-door section bent inward by tissue. ACCENT a gun rack fused into muscle.
  CONTAINMENT membrane sacs in the wall. ACCENT a large sac with a shape inside (glow frames, animate=some).
  MACHINERY a reactor housing crushed by a fist of muscle. ACCENT a drill motor, still on its mount.
  RESEARCH  terminals swallowed by membrane, screens glowing through. ACCENT a cable bundle like a
            nerve trunk ending in a terminal.
  STORAGE   crates pulled into the tissue. ACCENT a shutter jammed open by a growth.
  SIGNATURE THE DRILL FACE: BASE x2 the buried drill bit (the prototype's), scarred tissue around it;
            ACCENT the drill's collar and a severed drive shaft.
  DOORS     a facility door forced half shut by tissue, membrane over its edges; keycard panels.
  COLUMNS   a tissue-wrapped column (square: a bone-ribbed pillar).
  FLOOR     membrane over deck fragments; CEILING torn conduit and hanging tissue.
  LESSONS (into the guide): separate lobes read as sausages; parallel full-height bundles read as planks
  unless constricted and varied; glands must be op="add".

VISUAL DESIGN - REGION E, THE CORE (mood core_white_violet)
----------------------------------------------------------
A nacre-bone lattice grown like a neural mesh (the approved prototype), calmer than the prototype -
fewer, thicker struts, bigger hollows (it read busy at range) - with deep membrane and soul light in the
hollows. Purple is semantic (V5): the violet comes from the MOOD; materials stay pearl and pale.
  HALL      BASE x3 the lattice at three phases of one mesh (struts meet across tile edges). STRUCTURE a
            thick spine-like trunk strut. LIGHT a synapse node: a bright soul core with dendrites (8
            glow frames, a pulse travelling outward, animate=all). SERVICE a dead Organization cable run
            pulled taut through the lattice. VENT a bore hole with a drill sleeve, the lattice healing
            over it. MARKER the last survey plate ("SEAM" stencil), cracked. BREACH none -> fallback
            BASE (the planet does not breach itself; breachBase 0, breachStep 0).
  ROOM FAMILIES - THE LAST FORWARD CAMP: the Organization's rooms here are prefabricated set-A panels
            CLAMPED onto the nacre (one builder per family reused from set A, with a nacre clamp layer
            and lattice showing at the edges): MEDICAL, SECURITY, CONTAINMENT, MACHINERY, RESEARCH,
            STORAGE each BASE x2 + ACCENT. The facility is the foreign body now.
  SIGNATURE THE MEMORY HOLLOW: BASE x2 large hollows of soul light with lattice framing; ACCENT the
            memory window - a faint human silhouette in the light (the original self, story/03; no face,
            no text; animate=all, slow).
  DOORS     a lattice iris (nacre leaves meeting at a soul-lit centre); keycard panels as nacre-set lenses
            in the keycard colour.
  COLUMNS   a nacre trunk (square: a camp-built steel post wrapped by lattice).
  FLOOR     the lattice's floor (pearl ridges over dark membrane); CEILING the lattice vault.
