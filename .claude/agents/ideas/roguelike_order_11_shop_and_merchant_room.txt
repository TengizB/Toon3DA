TITLE: Shop & Merchant Room — Credits Economy
---

OVERVIEW
========
A special merchant room that appears on some floors (30% chance from floor 2+),
holding one stationary merchant NPC. The player approaches, presses interact (F),
and a shop UI opens offering 4-6 items for sale priced in CREDITS — a soft
currency earned by killing enemies and grabbing pickups. Items: weapons, ammo
packs, medkits, armor, consumables, and rare upgrades. The merchant never
restocks: what's on the shelf at entry is all there is. This is the run's
controlled risk/reward valve (Roguelike Pillars: Risk/Reward + Resource
Management) — a safe room where the player converts a scarce currency into the
exact tool their build is missing, in the Hades/DCSS "spend on the way down"
tradition.

Lore: a holed-up UAC survivor (or a black-market scavenger drone) who'll trade
gear for credits, indifferent to the apocalypse as long as the books balance.
Dark corporate humor: "Demons? Above my pay grade. You buying or browsing,
marine?"


DEPENDENCIES (must exist first)
===============================
1. Interaction system (InteractionDispatcher from uac-keycard-doors, IMPLEMENTED)
   — F/E interact routing to a tile/entity in front of the player. The shop is a

2. Enemy system (IMPLEMENTED) — enemies grant credits on death (drop hook).
3. Inventory / item pipeline (roguelike_order_6 inventory UI; inventory-loot-

4. Procedural level generation (procedural-level-generation.txt) — must be able

5. Billboard prop/enemy rendering (billboard-prop-sprites.txt, EnemyRenderer) —

6. HUD renderer (procedural-hud-no-textures.txt) — credits counter on HUD; shop
   UI is a full-screen overlay.
SOFT: XP/leveling (roguelike_order_11) for upgrade-style purchases; armour-kit
and medkit systems for consumable definitions.


CREDITS CURRENCY SYSTEM
=======================
- Credits are a run-scoped integer stored on the player/run state (PERSIST across
  floors — unlike keys; you save up as you descend). Reset on a new run
  (permadeath).
- EARNING:
  - Enemy kills: each enemy type has a creditBounty (trash 3-8, tougher 10-20,
    bosses 50-100). Credited on death.
  - Pickups: a new CREDIT pickup decal (loose credits / a UAC cred-chip) found in
    rooms and dropped occasionally by enemies; small amounts (5-15).
  - Selling: OUT OF SCOPE for v1 (no selling back). Flag in open questions.
- DISPLAY: a credits counter on the HUD (small, e.g. top-right or in the right
  panel near ammo: "CR 142"). Pre-rendered glyphs, no render() allocations.


GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
1. Floor generates; with SHOP_SPAWN_CHANCE (0.30) on floor 2+, one room is a
   SHOP ROOM: a small safe room (no enemy spawns inside) with the merchant NPC
   billboard at center, maybe a counter prop and warm lighting.
2. Player explores, finds the merchant. The merchant has a subtle "open for
   business" idle (sprite sway / a glowing sign).
3. Player faces the merchant from an adjacent tile and presses F (interact).
   - The shop UI opens (full-screen overlay; the game is turn-based so no time
     passes while it's open — opening the shop does NOT consume a turn; it pauses
     the tick loop, same as the inventory screen).
4. Shop UI shows the merchant portrait, a flavor line, the player's current
   credits, and a list of 4-6 ITEMS, each with name, icon, short description, and
   price. The roster was rolled ONCE at floor generation and is fixed.
5. Player navigates (arrow/WASD in menu context) and confirms a purchase:
   - If credits >= price: deduct credits, deliver the item (weapon → roster;
     ammo/medkit/armor/consumable → inventory; upgrade → applied immediately).
     The item's slot in the shop becomes SOLD OUT (greyed; cannot rebuy — no
     restock).
   - If credits < price: a "INSUFFICIENT CREDITS" denial blip; nothing happens.
6. Player closes the shop (F or ESC). Returns to normal turn-based play, standing
   where they were. They can reopen the shop any time while on this floor (to buy
   remaining items as credits accrue).
7. On descent, the shop is gone (new floor). Credits carry over.


SHOP INVENTORY ROLL
===================
At floor generation, roll 4-6 items from a weighted pool, no duplicates of the
same exact item:
- 1 WEAPON (or weapon upgrade) — the marquee item; weighted by what the player
  doesn't already own when feasible.
- 1-2 AMMO PACKS — for weapons the player owns (don't sell ammo for guns you
  can't use).
- 1 HEALING item (medkit or stim bundle).
- 1 ARMOR item (security vest / armor shards).
- 0-2 CONSUMABLES / RARE UPGRADES — grenades, a skeleton key, a damage-boost
  token, a one-floor shield, etc.
Roll depth-scaled: deeper floors offer better/pricier stock.


ITEM PRICING FORMULA (placeholders — tune)
==========================================
Base prices, scaled by depth so credits stay meaningful as bounties grow:
  price = round( basePrice * (1 + SHOP_DEPTH_PRICE_SCALE * (depth - 1)) )
with SHOP_DEPTH_PRICE_SCALE ~ 0.10.
Suggested base prices:
  Stim pack            15
  Field medkit         40
  Armor shard          15
  Security vest        45
  Ammo pack (small)    20
  Ammo pack (large)    45
  Consumable (grenade) 30
  Skeleton key         60
  Weapon (common)      90
  Weapon (rare)        160
  Damage/HP upgrade    120
Compare to bounties: ~5-8 trash kills buy a medkit early; a weapon is a whole
floor's worth of kills — a real choice. Tune so the player can afford ~1 marquee
item per shop visit, not the whole shelf.

VISUAL DESIGN
=============
MERCHANT BILLBOARD (procedural FrameBuffer sprite — no PNG assets)
- A hunched humanoid figure: a grey-orange rectangle torso (UAC jumpsuit color
  #BB7733), a circular head with two amber goggle circles (filled circles, dark
  rim), and a pack/sack shape (dark rectangle) slung over one shoulder. A small
  rectangle "counter" in front at tile-floor level. Above the head: a flickering
  amber text glyph "OPEN" drawn with BitmapFont (pulsing alpha via sin-wave for
  the hologram flicker). 2-frame idle bob via sin offset on the torso Y position.
  Warm amber SpriteBatch tint to distinguish the shop room from cold grey halls.
- Drawn once into a FrameBuffer at floor generation; displayed as a stationary
  billboard via EnemyRenderer (or a dedicated PropRenderer path). No PNG needed.

SHOP UI OVERLAY (full-screen, on top of a darkened/paused game)
- Left: merchant portrait + a rotating flavor line ("Cash only. The afterlife
  doesn't take cards.").
- Center: item list/grid, 4-6 entries, each: icon, name, 1-line desc, price.
  Sold-out entries greyed with a red "SOLD" stamp.
- Top-right: "CREDITS: 142".
- Bottom: controls hint ("[WASD] move  [ENTER] buy  [F/ESC] leave").
- Procedural/vector styling to match the no-textures HUD; CRT/scanline accent.
- Purchase: a satisfying "cha-ching" + the credits counter ticks down; item icon
  flies to the HUD/inventory.

HUD CREDITS COUNTER
- Persistent small "CR ###" readout (right panel, near ammo). Pre-rendered
  glyphs. Briefly flashes/pulses when credits change.


BALANCE NOTES
=============
- 30% per floor from floor 2 means a shop roughly every ~3 floors — frequent
  enough to matter, rare enough to feel lucky. Tune SHOP_SPAWN_CHANCE.
- Price so a typical visit affords ONE marquee item, forcing a choice (weapon vs
  armor vs save for next shop). Never let the player clear the whole shelf in one
  visit unless they hoarded across multiple floors.
- No restock + no selling keeps it a clean one-way sink; revisiting only matters
  as credits accrue on the same floor.
- Deeper shops: pricier but better stock, so saving credits across floors is a
  valid strategy (Hades "should I spend now or save for the good stuff?").
- Don't sell ammo for weapons the player can't use; don't sell a weapon they
  already own (offer its upgrade/ammo instead).


INTERACTIONS
============
- ENEMY SYSTEM: enemies grant credit bounties on death; tougher enemies = more
  credits, rewarding fighting over fleeing.
- LOCKED DOORS (roguelike_order_17): shop can sell a "skeleton key" consumable
  (opens one locked door) — a credits sink with route value.
- BOSS FLOORS (roguelike_order_16): place a shop on the floor BEFORE a boss
  floor when possible, so the player can buy a loadout for the fight; bosses give
  big credit bounties.
- INVENTORY UI (roguelike_order_6): purchased items land in inventory/roster;
  the shop UI and inventory UI should share visual language.
- XP/LEVELING (roguelike_order_11): "upgrade" shop items can route through the
  same stat/boon application path.
- TRAPS/HAZARDS (roguelike_order_20): shop rooms are SAFE (no traps inside) — a
  deliberate breather between hazard-dense floors.
- PERMADEATH (roguelike_order_1): credits and bought gear are lost on death;
  spending vs hoarding is a real gamble.
