NEW GAME BALANCE — ORDER 5 of 8
TRUE THREAT PRICING: CYCLE-AVERAGED SPECIALS + ENCOUNTER BUDGET V2
================================================================================
Requires: orders 1, 2, 4 (schema; expected-DPT curve; honest power model).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The encounter budget spends Threat Points, but TP is computed from BASE STATS
only (knowledge doc SECTIONS 7, 8, 16; problems 9, 12):
  1. Casters, summoners and buffers raise EFFECTIVE damage far beyond their
     stat block (EMPOWERED +40%, summons add whole extra enemies, WEAK cuts
     player output 25%) — none of it priced into TP. Floors with casters are
     silently harder than their budget says.
  2. Enemy eHP == raw HP is hard-coded; the first armoured/dodging archetype
     breaks the model.
  3. Macro pacing (route regions) and micro difficulty (TP budget) are not
     unified: a "lethal" region is lethal only by node FREQUENCY. Region danger
     should be a budgeted number, not a vibe.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A) CYCLE-AVERAGED EFFECTIVE DPT (specials priced automatically):
   Every archetype's TP now uses a blended DPT over its special cadence cycle:
     cycleAveragedDpt = (basic attacks in cycle * attackDamage
                         + specialEquivalentDamage) / cycleTurns
   specialEquivalentDamage per verb, as pure GameMath conversions:
     AREA_STRIKE -> its predicted damage (already a number).
     BUFF_SELF   -> extra damage the buff adds over its duration.
     DEBUFF WEAK -> player DPT lost over duration * DEBUFF_TP_EQUIVALENCE (a
                    defensive verb priced as offense; start 0.6 — a turn of
                    your lost output is worth less than a turn of their damage
                    because you can rotate/heal around it).
     DEBUFF SLOW/BLIND -> enemy pack's bonus turns gained * pack average DPT
                    share (priced conservatively; band-checked).
     SUMMON      -> amortized: summonedTP * expectedSummonsPerFight /
                    survivalTurns, bounded by the existing hard caps.
   TP role bands stay AS IS — archetypes whose true TP now overflows their band
   get their base stats trimmed (that's the point: the budget stops lying).
   Schema R-ENEMY switches to cycle-averaged TP; the COVERAGE rule now also
   fails any special verb with no registered equivalence formula.

B) GENERALIZED ENEMY eHP:
   Enemy struct gains armor/dodge/flatReduction (default 0) and enemy eHP runs
   through the SAME GameMath.effectiveHitPoints as the player — one formula,
   both sides (the mitigation pipeline already is shared; survivability math
   should be too). No archetype uses them YET; the model just stops assuming.
   Iron Stalker (MINI_ELITE) is the designated first user in a later content
   pass — with flat reduction it punishes chip damage and rewards heavy
   weapons, deepening the gear gate.

C) ENCOUNTER BUDGET V2:
   Keep: anchor fraction 0.15–0.30, variety cap 0.40, per-room cap 0.25, fill
   target 0.95, ranged+melee mix. Add:
     1. TACTICAL ROOM CAPS: a room's spent TP is capped against its GEOMETRY —
        open rooms (no cover props, wide sightlines) get
        ROOM_OPEN_TP_MULTIPLIER 0.8 (nowhere to break ranged cardinal lines);
        corridor-adjacent chokepoint rooms 1.1. Computed from the generator's
        room metadata, not per-room hand tags.
     2. PACK COHERENCE: chaff spawns in packs of CHAFF_PACK_MIN..MAX (2..4)
        (the golden-band exemption assumes packs; the spawner now guarantees
        the assumption).
     3. THE REGION DANGER DIAL (fixes problem 12): route regions stop being
        frequency-only. Each region declares REGION_TP_BUDGET_MULTIPLIER in
        BalanceConfig (A "Outer Facility" 0.9 | B "Research Wing" 1.0 |
        C "Reactor Depths" 1.15 | D "The Breach" 1.25) applied ON TOP of the
        depth curve, and the depth-coupling audit (order 4) includes it — so a
        lethal region is EXPLICITLY lethal, budgeted, and provably fair. Node
        weight multipliers remain for FLAVOR (what kind of rooms), the TP dial
        owns HOW HARD.
     4. ELITE-GAUNTLET floors keep their 0.15 chance but must still fit the
        (region-scaled) budget — an elite floor is a composition choice, not a
        budget override.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TECHNICAL NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - GameMath: cycleAveragedDamagePerTurn + one specialEquivalent* method per
    verb; enemyEffectiveHitPoints (delegates to effectiveHitPoints).
  - BalanceConfig SECTION 2: per-verb equivalence constants
    (DEBUFF_TP_EQUIVALENCE etc.); SECTION 11: room-geometry multipliers, pack
    sizes, per-region TP multipliers (SECTION 16 region data references them).
  - EncounterBudgetPlanner: consumes cycle-averaged TP; pack-spawning; region
    multiplier input.
  - Expect a REBALANCE FALLOUT pass: casters (Acid Drone, Mire Wraith, Vortex
    Eye, Blight Corruptor, Eye Tyrant blind) will read OVER band once priced
    honestly — trim their base stats back into band rather than widening bands.
  - BalanceReport: ENEMIES table gains cycleDPT + trueTP columns; ENCOUNTER
    table gains region-multiplier sweep.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] Every special verb has a priced equivalence; audit fails on unpriced
      verbs (coverage).
  [ ] All archetypes back IN their TP band under cycle-averaged pricing.
  [ ] Chaff always spawns in packs >= 2 (generator test, 100 seeds).
  [ ] Region C/D floors show measurably higher spent TP than A at equal depth,
      and the depth-coupling audit still passes for depths 1..15 in every
      region lane.
  [ ] Enemy eHP path runs through GameMath.effectiveHitPoints (no raw-HP
      shortcut left in TP/TTK call sites).
