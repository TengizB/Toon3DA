OVERVIEW
========
DEEP EXCAVATION is the "origin point" room: not a clean UAC lab and not a
flesh-demon warren, but the raw dig site where corporate greed drilled straight
through bedrock into something that should have stayed buried. It is a subterranean
rock cavern — dark basalt walls, timber-and-steel shoring, industrial drill rigs,
harsh portable floodlights — built radially around a central excavation PIT from
which an ancient demonic MONOLITH has been unearthed and clamped with UAC sensors.
Every other room says "the facility failed." This room says "the facility CAUSED
it." Tonally: archaeological awe curdling into dread. Cold white work-light on the
outer ring versus infernal red glow rising out of the pit at the center.

This is a distinct sub-biome from all existing wall families (plain / conduit /
vent / terminal / wires / hazard / rust / gore / bulkhead / glass / bio-quarantine /
emergency / medical / cryo / radiation / blast / holo-data / force-field) — it is
the only ROCK / geological / archaeological theme in the game. It reuses existing
pipelines only (per-char wall switch in WallRenderer, billboard PropRenderer,
RoomType decoration in LevelGenerator). No new runtime architecture required for
phase 1.

Adds:
  - 1 new room type:      EXCAVATION_SITE
  - 3 new walls:          o (bedrock), [ (strata/vein reveal), { (shoring/pit rim)
  - 4 new solid props:    } (monolith), ( (drill rig), ) (rubble mound), / (floodlight mast)
  - 3 new walkable decals: , (gravel scatter), _ (catwalk grating), : (bore tailings)

ROOM SHAPE — RADIAL OCTAGON WITH CROSS-CATWALK CORE
===================================================
The silhouette is deliberately NON-rectangular so it reads instantly different on
the mini-map and in the 3D view:

  OUTER SHELL: an OCTAGON approximation on the square grid — a large square whose
  four corners are chamfered (staircased) with bedrock 'o' walls, giving eight
  visible wall faces instead of four. Reads as a round rock cavern, not a box.

  CENTRAL PLINTH / PIT RIM: a concentric inner block of shoring wall '{' (the raised
  rock plug the dig exposed). This inner mass is SOLID except for a PLUS/cross cut
  through it: the center row and center column are laid with '_' catwalk grating
  (walkable), forming four cardinal bridges that meet at the exact center.

  MONOLITH: the '}' obelisk stands on the exact center tile — the crossing of the
  four catwalk arms. To reach it the player must commit to walking a narrow bridge.

  So the floorplan is: octagonal open ring (walkable) -> four narrow catwalk arms
  cutting inward through a solid stone plinth -> monolith at the hub. The four solid
  quadrant blocks of '{' between the arms give the room a pinwheel/cross reveal in
  first person: from the ring you see stone terraces with a glowing obelisk framed
  at the end of each bridge.

Conceptual ASCII (o=bedrock, {=shoring, _=catwalk, }=monolith, ' '=lit floor,
d=entrance door; corners chamfered):

    o o o o d o o o o

(Real placement spans a 11x11..13x13 interior; the plinth is a centered 5x5 or 7x7.)


TEXTURE / SPRITE SPEC (procedural — detailed enough to implement)
=================================================================
General: walls are procedural tiling Pixmaps (Nearest filter, disposed in
dispose()); props/decals are procedural billboard Pixmaps in PropRenderer. Reuse
the existing wall/billboard pipelines. Palette discipline: the room's identity is
DRY EARTH TONES (basalt black, slate gray, ochre/rust dust) + COLD WHITE floodlight
+ INFERNAL RED-ORANGE emissive (vein + monolith). This trio contrasts every other
room (warm-yellow corridors, cyan labs, green radiation, red/orange armory hazard).

--- WALL 'o' — BEDROCK ---
Build (bottom to top, per tile, tiling seamless L/R):
  1. Base fill: vertical gradient BASALT_BLACK (top) -> SLATE_GRAY (bottom).
  2. Rock grain: 2-3 octaves of value/Perlin noise, multiply +/-8% luminance, so
     no two tiles look flat. Clamp to keep it dark.
  3. Drill striations: 6-10 faint near-vertical light scratches (1px, +6% luma),
     slightly wavering, evenly-ish spaced — the boring-head gouges.
  4. Aggregate: 20-40 small pale speckles (2-3px) in loose clusters (embedded stone).
  5. Sediment bands: 1-2 horizontal bands, 3-5px tall, hue-shifted toward OCHRE_DUST,
     at random Y.
  6. Damp patch: one darker blue-desaturated blotch low on the tile (~20% chance).
  7. Corner vignette: darken outer 10% border.
  NO specular sheen. Matte only.
COLORS (RGBA):
  BASALT_BLACK   = (0.09,0.09,0.11,1.0)
  SLATE_GRAY     = (0.22,0.22,0.25,1.0)
  OCHRE_DUST     = (0.42,0.34,0.22,1.0)
  QUARTZ_FLECK   = (0.78,0.80,0.82,1.0)
  DAMP_BLUE      = (0.10,0.12,0.16,1.0)

--- WALL '[' — STRATA / VEIN REVEAL ---
Build:
  1. Base: 4-5 stacked horizontal STRATA bands, top->bottom hues cycling
     OCHRE_DUST -> RUST_RED -> SLATE_GRAY -> BASALT_BLACK, each grained (noise) and
     separated by a 1px dark seam.
  2. Fissure: a jagged diagonal polyline crack from lower-left toward upper-right,
     width tapering 6px->2px.
  3. Vein glow inside fissure: fill with radial-ish gradient VEIN_CORE_WHITE (thin
     center) -> VEIN_ORANGE -> VEIN_BLOOD (edge), additive-look.
  4. Lichtenberg branches: 4-6 thin (1px) VEIN_ORANGE cracks branching off the
     fissure into the surrounding rock, fading out.
  5. Sigils: 2-3 tiny angular rune glyphs (5-7px) drawn in VEIN_CORE_WHITE over the
     vein, as if carved and backlit.
  6. Molten bead: 1-2 bright drips (VEIN_ORANGE) hanging below the fissure.
COLORS (RGBA):
  RUST_RED       = (0.35,0.16,0.12,1.0)
  VEIN_BLOOD     = (0.55,0.06,0.04,1.0)
  VEIN_ORANGE    = (1.00,0.42,0.10,1.0)
  VEIN_CORE_WHITE= (1.00,0.92,0.80,1.0)
(reuse BASALT_BLACK, SLATE_GRAY, OCHRE_DUST from 'o')

--- WALL '{' — SHORING / PIT RIM ---
Build:
  1. Base: darkened bedrock 'o' fill (multiply luma by 0.75 — it's in shadow at the
     pit edge).
  2. Timber lagging: 5-7 horizontal plank boards (TIMBER_BROWN, grained, 1px dark
     gaps between), each with 2 bolt-head dots (STEEL_GRAY highlight).
  3. Soldier piles: 2 vertical steel H-beams (STEEL_GRAY, edge highlight) at ~25%
     and ~75% width, drawn over the planks.
  4. Tension strut: 1 diagonal steel strut crossing the tile, HAZARD_YELLOW chips
     flaking off it.
  5. Base spill: dirt/spoil mound (OCHRE_DUST) piled along the bottom 4-6px.
  6. Placard (~25% chance): small hung warning sign, HAZARD_YELLOW with a dark
     glyph.
COLORS (RGBA):
  TIMBER_BROWN   = (0.34,0.24,0.15,1.0)
  STEEL_GRAY     = (0.40,0.42,0.46,1.0)
  HAZARD_YELLOW  = (0.85,0.72,0.12,1.0)

--- PROP '}' — MONOLITH (billboard, 0.95x height) ---
Build (tall narrow sprite):
  1. Silhouette: tapering obelisk, base wider than crown, canted ~4deg off vertical,
     rough-hewn edges (jagged 1-2px steps, not smooth).
  2. Body: BASALT_BLACK with subtle vertical grain; darker on the shadow side.
  3. Sigil grooves: dense vertical/angular carved glyph lines all over the body,
     filled with a glow gradient VEIN_BLOOD (deep grooves) -> VEIN_ORANGE (shallow)
     -> VEIN_CORE_WHITE (a few hot spots). Grooves brightest low, bleeding upward.
  4. Heat corona: faint additive VEIN_ORANGE halo (soft alpha) around the whole
     silhouette; 1-2px shimmer noise at the edges.
  5. UAC clamps: near the base, 2 modern steel scanning clamps (STEEL_GRAY) gripping
     the stone, with cyan sensor LEDs (0.35,0.95,1.00) and thin cables snaking off.
  6. Foot rubble: a small ')' -like debris heap at the base.
  Optional polish: 2-3 tiny floating rock chips near the crown (drawn statically,
  offset from the body) implying levitation.
PULSE: the groove glow may brighten/dim via the global-tick/flicker hook by scaling
  the sprite's tint (NEVER reallocate the Pixmap in render()).

--- PROP '(' — DRILL RIG (billboard, 0.85x) ---
  Skid base (STEEL_GRAY, dust-caked ochre). Motor housing box with cooling-vent
  slats + grimy amber worklight (0.90,0.65,0.20). Angled hydraulic arm (2 segments,
  hoses drawn as curved dark lines) holding a large SPIRAL AUGER bit: a cone of
  helical flighting, STEEL_GRAY with OCHRE_DUST crust, tip pointed down-forward.
  Control-lever cluster (small). Body color safety-orange (0.85,0.45,0.12) + gray.
  Jammed variant (~30%): red fault LED (0.95,0.20,0.15) + place a spark ':' or 'e'
  decal on an adjacent tile.

--- PROP ')' — RUBBLE MOUND (billboard, 0.45x) ---
  Low wide irregular heap: 8-14 overlapping angular rock chunks (SLATE_GRAY /
  BASALT_BLACK, top-lit lighter), OCHRE_DUST fill between, a half-buried TIMBER_BROWN
  beam poking out at an angle, optional snapped drill-bit shard (STEEL_GRAY). Matte,
  dusty. No glow.

--- PROP '/' — FLOODLIGHT MAST (billboard, 0.90x) ---
  Tripod base + small generator box (STEEL_GRAY, green power LED 0.20,0.85,0.35,
  coiled cable). Tall thin pole. Crossbar at top with 2-4 halogen lamp heads
  (rounded rectangles) glowing FLOOD_WHITE (0.95,0.97,1.00) with a soft additive
  bloom halo, aimed downward. This is the room's cold key light — sprite should read
  as the brightest cool element, opposing the monolith's warm glow.

--- DECAL ',' — GRAVEL SCATTER (0.16x) ---
  Flat irregular blotch: 30-60 tiny rock-chip specks (SLATE_GRAY / OCHRE_DUST) with
  a faint dust smear and 1-2 boot-scuff streaks. Matte, no glow. Same ground band as
  '.'/'O'/'e'.

--- DECAL '_' — CATWALK GRATING (0.30x, walkable) ---
  Rectangular grating plate: diamond-plate / bar-grid pattern (STEEL_GRAY lines on a
  near-black ground so the DARK GAPS read as the pit below); riveted edge rails
  (lighter STEEL_GRAY); a hazard-stripe (HAZARD_YELLOW / black) along the long
  edges; worn tread wear-marks (brighter scuff). A faint VEIN_BLOOD glow bleeds up
  through the grid gaps (the vein continues into the pit). Reads slightly raised.

--- DECAL ':' — BORE TAILINGS (0.16x) ---
  Two small conical powder piles (OCHRE_DUST, top-lit) dusted with 6-10 faint
  VEIN_ORANGE glow-flecks; plus 2-3 dark round core-sample bore holes (near-black
  circles with a thin bright rim). Placed next to drill rigs. Glow-flecks + round
  holes distinguish it from plain ',' gravel.


GENERATOR ALGORITHM (step-by-step for LevelGenerator.java)
==========================================================
Add RoomType.EXCAVATION_SITE with a decorateExcavationSite() method following the
existing RoomType decoration pattern. The generator carves the octagon into a
reserved rectangular room slot, then decorates radially.

Inputs: reserved room rectangle (interior columnCount x rowCount), the run RNG, the
door/entrance side(s) chosen by the connector pass.

1. SIZE GATE: require interior footprint >= 11x11 (prefer 11x11 or 13x13, odd side).
   If the slot is smaller, fall back to a different RoomType (do not force it).

2. CARVE OCTAGON:
   - Fill the whole interior with lit floor ' '.
   - Ring the perimeter with bedrock 'o' walls.
   - Chamfer each of the 4 corners: let chamferSize C = floor(sideLength / 4). For
     each corner, for tileColumn/tileRow within C of that corner where
     (cornerOffsetColumn + cornerOffsetRow) < C, set the tile to bedrock 'o' wall.
     This staircases the diagonal, producing the octagon silhouette.

3. STRATA REVEAL: pick 1-2 contiguous perimeter segments (2-3 'o' tiles each) on a
   NON-entrance side (bias to the wall directly opposite the primary door) and
   convert them to strata/vein wall '['. Never convert an entrance-adjacent tile.

4. CENTRAL PLINTH: define a centered square of side P = sideLength - 6 (so P is 5 or
   7). Fill every plinth tile with shoring wall '{' (solid). This is the raised pit
   rim / rock plug.

5. CUT THE CROSS CATWALK: within the plinth, convert the center row and the center
   column (each 1 tile wide) from '{' to catwalk grating '_'. The center tile of the
   plinth is where the two lanes cross. This yields four cardinal bridges through
   solid stone. Guarantee each catwalk arm connects the plinth edge to the outer
   ring floor (extend one tile of '_' just past the plinth edge if needed).

6. MONOLITH: place '}' on the exact center tile (the catwalk crossing).

7. DRILL RIGS: place 2-4 '(' in the OUTER RING, positioned near the mouths of the
   catwalk arms (just outside the plinth), facing inward. ~30% rolled "jammed"
   (spawns an adjacent ':' or 'e').

8. FLOODLIGHT MASTS: place 2-4 '/' in the outer ring, spaced evenly against/near the
   bedrock shell (leave 1 tile clearance so they don't block the wall-hug path).

9. RUBBLE: scatter 2-4 ')' mounds in the outer ring for cover / sightline breaks;
   keep them off the catwalk arms and out of the single guaranteed loop path.

10. DECAL PASS: scatter ',' gravel across the outer-ring floor (density higher within
    2 tiles of any drill rig); place ':' bore tailings adjacent to each drill rig;
    ensure catwalk arms are all '_' (already placed step 5).

11. LIGHTING: outer ring floor stays lit ' ' near floodlight masts; drop 1-3 'u'
    unlit or 'f' flicker tiles in ring gaps far from masts for dread. The plinth is
    solid (no floor). The catwalk arms stay lit ' '/'_' (the monolith self-lights).

12. ENTRANCES: exactly 1 door gap in the octagon shell (2 opposite gaps allowed for a
    through-room). Connector pass wires it into the corridor graph.

13. ENEMY SPAWNS: place 2-4 spawn markers behind rubble mounds and at catwalk-arm
    mouths (see BALANCE). NAVIGABILITY CHECK: the outer ring must be a full loop and
    every catwalk arm must reach the ring — verify the monolith tile is reachable
    from the entrance via flood-fill before committing the room (roguelike pillar 2:
    no isolated cells).


PLACEMENT RULES (frequency, depth-gating, spacing)
==================================================
  - RARITY: RARE set-piece. At most 1 per generated level; frequently 0. LOW weight
    in the RoomType roll table. It is a landmark, not filler.
  - DEPTH GATE: floors 4+. This is where the dig "found" the invasion — it belongs to
    mid/late descent, thematically the cause the player uncovers. (Route-map: gate to
    deeper regions; never on floor 1.)
  - SPACING: minimum spacing from other set-piece rooms (RESEARCH_LAB, boss arena,
    shop/safe room) — do not stack two landmarks adjacently. Never the start room.
  - SLOT SIZE: only spawn if a >=11x11 interior slot exists (see step 1).
  - SYMBOL CONTAINMENT: bedrock 'o', strata '[', shoring '{', monolith '}', catwalk
    '_' and bore tailings ':' should appear ONLY in EXCAVATION_SITE rooms (keeps the
    biome read exclusive). Drill rig '(', rubble ')', floodlight mast '/', gravel ','
    MAY leak sparingly into adjacent maintenance/mining corridors (1-2 tiles) as a
    breadcrumb hinting a dig is near — optional polish, keep rare.


GAMEPLAY LOOP / ATMOSPHERE (roguelike pillars)
==============================================
1. Player enters via one octagon door. Room READ: rock walls + white floodlights +
   a red glow rising from a central stone mass = "this is the dig; something is at
   the middle, and reaching it means walking a bridge."
2. The outer ring is the safe(ish) loop: cover from rubble mounds ')', cold light
   from masts '/'. Ranged/melee enemies contest the ring.
3. To reach the MONOLITH '}' the player must step onto a narrow catwalk arm '_'. On a
   catwalk the player is COMMITTED and EXPOSED: single-file, no cover, and — crucially
   — standing on a catwalk arm places the player on the same tile-row or tile-column
   as anything sitting at that arm's mouth. Given the cardinal-line ranged rule
   (isSameCardinalLine), a Vortex Eye / Eye Tyrant parked at an arm mouth gets a
   clean shot down the bridge. Advancing to the relic is a deliberate risk. (Pillar 4:
   meaningful choice under pressure; Pillar tactical positioning.)
4. THE MONOLITH is an interactable relic/shrine node (phase 2 — ties to
   roguelike_order_14 artifacts). Facing it and interacting offers a double-edged
   trade: touch the stone to claim an ARTIFACT / boon, but the disturbance WAKES the
   dig — spawns an ambush wave from the arm mouths and/or converts a strata seam into
   a fresh vein. Take the power, pay the alarm. (Pillar 3: emergent; Pillar 1: a
   death here is legible — "I woke it for the relic and got pinned on the bridge.")
   Phase 1 fallback: monolith is a pure atmospheric prop and the reward (H / A /
   keycard / artifact pickup) simply sits at its foot; the bridge exposure still
   makes claiming it tense.
5. EMOTIONAL ARC: descent-into-awe (a genuinely ancient, beautiful, terrible thing)
   then dread (the UAC clamps and floodlights prove humans were HERE, poking it). The
   room reframes the whole run — the invasion wasn't an accident, it was excavated.

Atmosphere payoff ties directly to the pillars: unique readable biome (information
management — you learn "rock + red glow = the dig, expect a bridge gauntlet"),
committed positioning (tactical depth), and an optional relic-for-alarm bargain
(meaningful choice + emergent consequence).


BALANCE NOTES (all PLACEHOLDER — flag for tuning)
=================================================
  - Room spawn weight: LOW; cap 1 per level; floors 4+. (PLACEHOLDER)
  - Solid-prop budget: keep total blocking props (', ) / plus plinth mass) navigable —
    outer ring must remain a full walkable loop. Aim <= 8 free-standing props.
  - Drill rigs '(': 2-4; ~30% jammed variant. Rubble ')': 2-4. Masts '/': 2-4.
  - Catwalk arms: exactly 4, each 1 tile wide (the exposure is the point — do not
    widen to 2, it removes the gauntlet).
  - Enemy spawns: 2-4. Thematic fit = things the dig disturbed. Suggested: a ranged
    "arm-mouth" guard (Eye Tyrant '2' or Vortex Eye 'V') positioned to punish the
    bridge, plus 1-2 melee (Gore Biter '3' / Blight Corruptor '*') in the ring. On
    deeper floors add a heavy (Shell Brute '4' / Iron Stalker '!').
  - Relic wake-ambush (phase 2): spawn 2-3 enemies at arm mouths on interaction.
    Tune so a prepared player survives and an over-extended one does not.
  - 'f'/'u' dread tiles: 1-3 max in ring gaps; do not strobe-spam.
  - Emissive discipline: red (vein/monolith) is the ONLY warm glow; white (masts) the
    ONLY cool key light; everything else is dry earth tone. Keep the two lights
    spatially separated (red = center/pit, white = outer ring) so the contrast reads.
