TITLE: Medkit & Field Medicine System
CREATED: 2026-05-29

OVERVIEW
========
The first healing system for the marine. Two tiers of medical supply spawn on
the floor as walkable decal props (UAC field stim-packs and full wall-mounted
medkits), get picked up automatically into a tiny carried stash, and are spent
on a dedicated HEAL action that costs exactly one turn. Because using a medkit
burns a turn while every enemy in the room gets a free swing, healing is never
"free": the player must read the fight and decide whether to step, shoot, or
patch up. This is the Doom RPG "press a key, pick up the bonus" pickup loop
crossed with DCSS's quaff-under-fire tension — and it is the missing
counterweight to the damage the enemy and hazard systems already deal.

This doc owns: the medical pickup props, the PlayerInventory medical stash, the
HEAL turn action, and the heal-application numbers. It deliberately does NOT
re-define max HP, the Vitality stat, or the HUD heal feedback — those are owned
by levelup-stim-boon-choice-progression.txt and scanline-crt-hud-marine-vitals
.txt respectively. This system CONSUMES Player.applyHealing(int) (defined in the
HUD doc) and is clamped by maxHealth (defined in the progression doc).


GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
ACQUIRING (automatic, on entering the tile — Doom RPG style):
1. Player steps onto a tile holding a medical pickup ('h' stim or 'H' medkit).
2. On move-completion, BEFORE the tick fires, World checks the destination tile
   for a pickup (same hook order the tick doc uses: settle door -> pick up ->
   fireTick). Reuse that exact ordering so the world sees the consumed pickup.
3. The pickup type maps to a heal-charge value. If the marine's medical stash
   has room (carry cap not exceeded), the charge is added and the prop tile is
   converted to plain floor (the decal disappears).
4. A toast routes through the HUD banner ("STIM-PACK +1" / "FIELD MEDKIT +1").
5. If the stash is already FULL, the pickup is NOT consumed: it stays on the
   tile, and a "MED STASH FULL" toast fires. The player can leave and return.
   (Anti-waste rule — never silently destroy medicine the player can't hold.)
6. Walking onto a pickup is NOT a separate action — it happens during the
   normal step. The step is the only tick. Picking up costs no extra turn.

HEALING (deliberate, costs one turn):
1. Player is IDLE and presses HEAL (default key: R — "Recover"; NUM keys 1/2/3
   are reserved by the boon overlay, so HEAL gets a letter).
2. PlayerController checks: do we have at least one charge of ANY tier, and is
   currentHealth < maxHealth? If either fails -> reject, brief red flash of the
   medkit HUD slot, NO turn consumed (a wasted heal at full HP is a known
   roguelike trap; we forbid it).
3. If valid: select the SMALLEST sufficient tier first (spend a stim before a
   full medkit when a stim alone tops you off — avoids overheal waste). See
   BALANCE for the selection rule.
4. The HEAL action animates over PLAYER_HEAL_DURATION (same action-lock model as
   move/fire). During this the marine is locked, exactly like reloading.
5. On completion: call Player.applyHealing(healAmount) (HUD doc method; clamps
   to maxHealth, fires PlayerHealed event -> green sparkle + bar glide up),
   decrement the stash, then finishAction(true, TickCause.HEAL) -> the tick
   fires -> EVERY enemy takes its turn and may hit the marine.
6. Net tension: you regain N HP but eat one round of incoming damage. Healing
   early (safe) wastes the buffer; healing late (under fire) risks dying mid-
   animation. This is the decision the system exists to create.


ITEM TIERS
==========
TIER 1 — STIM-PACK ('h'): the common drip-feed heal.
  - Small, single-use injector. Heals MEDKIT_STIM_HEAL HP (placeholder 8).
  - Drops frequently: from supply rooms, lockers, and occasionally enemy
    corpses (Scavenger boon synergy). The "I found a little something" beat.
  - Cheap to spend; you'll often pop one between fights to stay topped.

TIER 2 — FIELD MEDKIT ('H'): the rare panic button.
  - Bulky UAC wall medkit / full kit. Heals MEDKIT_FULL_HEAL HP (placeholder 25)
    OR heals to MEDKIT_FULL_HEAL_FRACTION of max, whichever the developer picks
    (see OPEN QUESTIONS). The "save it for when I'm bleeding out" item.
  - Rare: 0-2 per floor, hand-placed by the level designer near boss/objective
    rooms or behind a keycard door.
  - Same one-turn cost as a stim — so the medkit isn't slower, just stronger and
    scarcer. The tension is scarcity, not action economy.

Both tiers share ONE carry stash with a combined cap (see PICKUP MECHANICS).
They are tracked as separate counts but compete for the same inventory pressure.


TILE SYMBOLS (new chars needed)
===============================
Add two WALKABLE DECAL PROP chars (player can walk over them; rendered as flat
billboards, same family as 'm' corpse / 's' shotgun / '.' blood):

  'h' — STIM-PACK pickup.  Height 0.20x wall (small, near floor).
        Visual: white injector / small white box with a red cross, faint cyan
        UAC glow underneath. Sits flat-ish like the dropped shotgun.

  'H' — FIELD MEDKIT pickup. Height 0.30x wall (chunkier than a stim).
        Visual: white/olive hard case, bold red cross on the lid, small green
        status LED. Reads instantly as "bigger heal" from across a room.

Both are NON-SOLID (Level.isWall returns false) so the player walks onto them to
trigger pickup, exactly like 'm'/'s'/'.'/'O'. Do NOT add them to the wall set.
A wall-mounted variant is cosmetic only; logically it still occupies a floor
tile the player steps onto. NO new wall chars, NO grid-format change beyond
these two decal symbols.


PICKUP MECHANICS
================
CARRY MODEL — small stash, deliberate scarcity:
  - Combined cap: MEDKIT_TOTAL_CARRY_CAP charges across both tiers (placeholder
    4). Example legal states: 4 stims; or 2 stims + 2 medkits; or 1 medkit + 3
    stims. The cap is on TOTAL charges, not per-tier, so a medkit "costs" the
    same slot as a stim — picking up a fat medkit means carrying fewer stims.
  - Optional per-tier sub-cap (medkits capped at MEDKIT_FULL_CARRY_CAP = 2) to
    stop the player hoarding only big kits. Flag as tunable.
  - When full, pickups are left on the floor (see GAMEPLAY LOOP step 5). They
    persist; the tile is unchanged until a charge frees up.

PICKUP RESOLUTION ORDER (must match tick doc):
  In the move-completion path: settle door -> resolve medical pickup -> resolve
  other pickups (ammo/keycard) -> finishAction(true, MOVE). The pickup mutates
  inventory and clears the tile BEFORE the tick, so enemies react to the post-
  pickup world state on the same turn.

NO MANUAL "PICK UP" KEY: stepping on the tile is the whole interaction. Keeps
the Doom RPG instant-grab feel and avoids a second turn-cost.

BALANCE NOTES
=============
All numbers are PLACEHOLDERS pending playtest against enemy damage output.

- STIM heal = 8, MEDKIT heal = 25. Against a starting pool around 20-30 HP (set
  by the progression doc's Vitality base), a stim is a "patch" and a medkit is a
  "full reset." Tune STIM and MEDKIT together with PLAYER_STARTING_HP.
- ONE-TURN COST is the core balance lever, not the heal size. Healing always
  gives enemies a free turn; in a 3-enemy room that may cost more HP than a stim
  restores, which correctly discourages panic-healing mid-swarm and rewards
  retreating to a cleared corridor first. This is the intended tension.
- SMALLEST-SUFFICIENT SELECTION: chooseHealTier() spends a STIM if a stim alone
  brings you to >= maxHealth - MEDKIT_OVERHEAL_SLACK (or if no medkit exists);
  otherwise spends a MEDKIT. Prevents wasting a 25-heal kit to top off 5 HP.
  Provide an OPEN QUESTION on whether the player should choose manually instead.
- CARRY CAP = 4 keeps medicine precious; the player can't turtle behind an
  infinite stash. If runs feel too punishing, raise the cap before raising heal
  values (cap changes the strategic texture less than heal-size changes do).
- FULL-HP HEAL FORBIDDEN: pressing HEAL at max HP costs no turn and just blips
  the HUD — protects the player from a fat-finger that would otherwise gift the
  enemies a free round for zero gain.
- DROP RATES (placeholder): stim spawn ~1 per 2 rooms + ~10% enemy-corpse drop
  (scales with the Scavenger boon); medkit 0-2 per floor, hand-placed. Keep
  total available healing per floor under the total damage a careful player
  takes per floor, so HP is a managed resource, not a faucet.


INTERACTIONS
============
1. PROGRESSION / VITALITY (levelup-stim-boon-choice doc): maxHealth and the
   Vitality "heal-to-full on level-up" are owned there; this system only ever
   calls applyHealing(amount) and is clamped by that maxHealth. The Vitality
   level-up heal and a field medkit must not double-count — both go through the
   same clamp, so they compose safely. A future "Field Medic" boon could grant
   +1 carry cap or +50% medkit potency, reading MEDKIT_* constants.
2. HUD (scanline-crt-hud doc): consumes the existing PlayerHealed event (green
   sparkle, bar glide), the toast banner (pickup + full-stash messages), and
   adds one MEDKIT count slot. No new event bus — reuse GameEvents.PlayerHealed.
3. TICK SYSTEM (global-tick-event doc): HEAL is a new TickCause; the heal
   completes, the tick fires, enemies act. The pickup-before-tick ordering
   matches the doc's settle-door -> pickup -> fireTick rule exactly.
4. ENEMIES (enemy_system doc): the one-turn heal cost means healing in melee
   range of a Corruptor invites a hit; the Vortex Eye's ranged threat makes
   "heal now or dodge now" a genuine dilemma. Scavenger-boosted corpse drops let
   aggressive play partly self-fund healing.
5. HAZARDS (hazard-chain-reactions doc): standing in fire/toxic and healing is a
   trap — the hazard tick during the HEAL turn can out-damage the heal. The
   Pyromaniac boon's halved fire damage interacts here (heal becomes viable in
   fire). Oil pools ('O') under a medkit make for cruel, readable placements.
6. LEVEL DESIGN: 'h'/'H' give the level designer a pacing tool — a stim before a
   hard room signals "you'll need this"; a medkit behind a keycard door rewards
   exploration. Pairs with the keycard-doors doc for gated medical caches.


OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
1. FIELD MEDKIT — flat heal (25) or percentage/heal-to-full? Recommendation:
   flat 25 for v1 (predictable, scales cleanly with maxHealth tuning); revisit a
   heal-to-full variant if max HP pools grow large late-run.
2. AUTO tier-selection (smallest-sufficient) vs MANUAL choose (separate keys for
   stim vs medkit)? Recommendation: AUTO for v1 (one HEAL key, less UI); add a
   manual "use medkit" key only if playtesters want to deliberately bank stims.
3. Should picking up over the carry cap PARTIALLY fill a kit (no, charges are
   discrete) — confirmed discrete. Leave the pickup on the floor instead.
4. Enemy-corpse stim drops: rolled by the enemy system on death or by this
   system listening to a kill event? Recommendation: enemy system rolls it (it
   owns the loot table) and calls inventory.addMedical(STIM); this doc just
   provides the addMedical API and the drop-rate constant.
5. Should HEAL be interruptible if the marine would die from the post-heal
   enemy turn? No — turn integrity is sacred; the heal resolves, then enemies
   act, then death is evaluated. The player chose to heal under fire.
6. Does a medkit cure status effects (bleed/toxic) if those ship later, or only
   restore HP? Recommendation: HP only for v1; a separate "antitoxin" item or a
   medkit upgrade boon can own cleanse later.
7. PLAYER_HEAL_DURATION longer than a move (0.18 vs 0.12) for weight — confirm
   in playtest it doesn't feel sluggish under pressure.
