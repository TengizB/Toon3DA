TITLE: Weapon System Order 9 — Positional & Situational Abilities
---

WHAT WAS BUILT
==============
Six new ON_HIT/ON_KILL weapon abilities covering positional bonuses, first-hit
bonuses, consecutive-hit ramp, AoE kill splash, and percentage max-HP damage.

ABILITIES
=========
POINT_BLANK       (ON_HIT)  — bonus damage % when target is within 1 tile.
MARKSMANS_PATIENCE(ON_HIT)  — per-tile bonus % beyond MARKSMAN_MIN_DISTANCE (2),
                              capped by MARKSMAN_TOTAL_BONUS_CAP.
OPENING_SALVO     (ON_HIT)  — bonus % damage on the first hit against a full-HP
                              target (targetWasFullHp captured before applyDamageTo).
RHYTHM            (ON_HIT)  — consecutive hits on the SAME enemy ramp damage
                              (rhythmStacks, rhythmTargetId on Weapon). Resets on
                              miss, target-switch, and when the target dies.
STATIC_DISCHARGE  (ON_KILL) — AoE splash to 4 orthogonal adjacent enemies on kill.
                              resolvingDischarge guard prevents infinite chains.
RESONANT_ROUNDS   (ON_HIT)  — flat bonus = pct × target.maxHealth (scales with elites).

ARCHITECTURE NOTES
==================
- hitDistance comes from FireResult.distanceTiles (already computed by
  marchShot), passed through dispatchHitCallbacks() — no extra player-tile
  state storage needed.
- RHYTHM resets on miss (Weapon.fire()), target-switch (updateRhythm detects
  id mismatch), and kill (AbilityResolver.onKill → resetRhythmIfTarget).
- STATIC_DISCHARGE secondary kills call onKill() recursively but the
  resolvingDischarge flag (try/finally) prevents further splash chains.
- OPENING_SALVO requires pre-damage HP check: solved via isAtFullHp() added
  to EnemyHitTarget interface with EnemyManager override.

WHAT IS NOT IMPLEMENTED
=======================
- WeaponRoller (order-11) must assign these abilities when generating loot.
  Until then, abilities can only be set via hardcoded WeaponProfile entries.
- RHYTHM and STATIC_DISCHARGE have no HUD indicator for current stack count.
  A future HUD pass (order-12 or later) could add a small counter.
