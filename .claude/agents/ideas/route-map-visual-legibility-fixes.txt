TITLE: Route Map — Nav Console Legibility Fixes (border + CRT + contrast)
CREATED: 2026-07-10
---

PREREQUISITE: The "FACILITY NAV" route-select console already ships (route-map order-4 LOOK,
order-5 icons, order-6 touch). This is a POLISH / BUG-FIX pass on that existing console, NOT a
new feature. Nothing here changes layout math, selection flow, or the graph — only the visual
treatment constants and a couple of small draw-order tweaks that make the schematic readable.

OWNS (files touched)
====================
- core/.../util/RouteMapConstants.java   — the visual-spec constants (all values live here first).
- core/.../render/RouteMapOverlayRenderer.java — drawCrtPass(), drawScrim(), and (optional) a new
  opaque "screen" fill for the map viewport band.

ABSOLUTE CONSTRAINT (unchanged): every pixel is procedurally drawn — ShapeRenderer primitives +
built-in BitmapFont. No textures, no FrameBuffer in the game loop (viewport-safe rule). These
fixes are all constant nudges and existing-primitive draws; none of them break that contract.

WHY (source: player screenshot, depth 0 -> 1, "UNKNOWN SECTOR")
==============================================================
The console renders, but the map is hard to read. Two problems the player called out, plus
several contributing causes found in the renderer:

  (1) "Parts of the roadmap are overlaid by a black border that does not look nice."
  (2) "The screen-like effect is too strong and reduces clear visibility."

Everything below is confirmed against RouteMapOverlayRenderer.drawCrtPass() /
drawScrim() and the RouteMapConstants CRT + node-card values.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 1 — The "black border" over the map  (player complaint #1)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CAUSE: drawCrtPass() paints a VIGNETTE — four opaque-ish black rectangles hugging the map
viewport edges:

    VIGNETTE_ALPHA = 0.34      // 34% black
    VIGNETTE_BAND  = 64f       // 64 world-units thick on EACH edge

The top band (y 576..640) and bottom band (150..214) sit directly on top of the top-most and
bottom-most node rows. Because node rows are laid out right up to the viewport edges
(MAP_BOTTOM_PAD = 46, and the lookahead rows climb to MAP_VIEWPORT_TOP_Y), the top row of "?"
cards gets its upper third painted over with 34% black — that is the "black border cutting the
cards" in the screenshot. The left/right bands do the same to the outer lanes.

Made worse by a layout pinch at the top: MAP_VIEWPORT_TOP_Y = 640 but TITLE_PLATE_BOTTOM_Y = 648
(only an 8 px gap), while MAP_CULL_MARGIN_TOP = 96 lets a card centre climb to y = 736 — i.e.
ABOVE the title plate. So a distant top row can draw partly behind the title plate / hazard trim
AND under the vignette top band at the same time. That stack reads as a hard black edge slicing
the cards.

FIX (pick A; B is the stronger version):
  A. Kill the top and bottom vignette bands entirely and soften the sides. The vignette adds
     almost nothing on a flat schematic and costs legibility:
        VIGNETTE_ALPHA : 0.34  -> 0.12
        VIGNETTE_BAND  : 64f   -> 40f
     …and in drawCrtPass() draw ONLY the left and right bands (delete the two full-width top and
     bottom rect() calls). Rationale: horizontal edge darkening is what eats the card rows; thin
     side darkening alone still gives the faint "tube curvature" hint without touching content.
  B. (Recommended) Remove the vignette bands completely (drop all four rect() calls) and instead
     lean on the new opaque screen panel from ISSUE 3 for the "inside a display" feel. Cleaner and
     one fewer moving part. Keep the constants but set VIGNETTE_ALPHA = 0f so the pass no-ops and
     the values stay documented.

  Also close the top pinch so no row can hide under the title plate:
        MAP_CULL_MARGIN_TOP : 96f -> ~24f   (a card centre may no longer sit above the plate)
     OR nudge MAP_VIEWPORT_TOP_Y down to ~624 so there's real breathing room under the plate.
     Verify the top lookahead row still fully fits after the change (it is already shrunk by the
     DISTANT_SCALE perspective fade, so this is safe).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 2 — CRT "screen effect" too strong  (player complaint #2)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CAUSE: three stacked treatments in drawCrtPass()/flicker() over-darken the hologram:

    SCANLINE_ALPHA   = 0.10    // dark line every 3 px across the ENTIRE viewport
    SCANLINE_SPACING = 3f      // very dense -> a near-continuous grey wash
    FLICKER_AMPLITUDE = 0.02   // whole-hologram dim that multiplies EVERY node/label/conduit alpha

With SCANLINE_SPACING = 3 and a 1 px line, one third of every card is covered by a 10%-black line,
and flicker() further scales down all content alpha every frame. On the low-contrast cards (see
ISSUE 4) this is what "reduces clear visibility."

FIX (tune down, don't remove — the CRT vibe is the console's identity):
        SCANLINE_ALPHA   : 0.10 -> 0.05
        SCANLINE_SPACING : 3f   -> 4f      (fewer, thinner-feeling lines; keeps the texture)
        FLICKER_AMPLITUDE: 0.02 -> 0.012   (subtler breathing; labels stop shimmering)
     SYNC_FLASH is already rare/cheap (every 4.5 s, alpha 0.06) — leave it, or drop to 0.04.
     Keep SCANLINE_SCROLL_SPEED as-is; motion is fine, it's the darkness that hurt.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 3 — The frozen 3D world bleeds through the map (found while investigating)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CAUSE: drawScrim() lays a single navy scrim at OVERLAY_SCRIM_ALPHA = 0.72 over the whole screen,
so 28% of the paused 3D scene shows through UNDER the hologram. In the screenshot this is the
faint green "EQUIPPED / RAILGUN" HUD text and wall shapes ghosting behind the cards — visual
noise that fights the schematic and lowers contrast on the translucent cards.

FIX: give the map its OWN clean display surface instead of relying on the see-through scrim.
Add a near-opaque dark "screen" panel filling just the map viewport band, drawn AFTER the scrim
and BEFORE the glow/conduits/cards. New constants:

        MAP_SCREEN_FILL_ALPHA = 0.90f          // near-opaque phosphor screen
        // colour: reuse palette.scrimNavy (or a slightly cooler near-black, e.g. a new
        // palette.screenBlack ≈ (0.03, 0.05, 0.07)) so the cyan hologram pops.

In render(), between drawScrim() and drawBezel()/drawGlowPass(), draw one filled rect:
        (0, MAP_VIEWPORT_BOTTOM_Y) .. (worldWidth, MAP_VIEWPORT_TOP_Y)
This is the single biggest readability win: the schematic now sits on a real screen, the world
stops bleeding through, and the additive glow reads against a consistent dark backdrop. Keep the
outer OVERLAY_SCRIM_ALPHA the same (the world OUTSIDE the map band can still ghost through the
bezel — that's fine and diegetic).

NOTE: draw the screen panel with NORMAL alpha blending BEFORE the additive glow pass so the glow
still adds on top. Do not draw it after the cards.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 4 — Node cards are barely legible (found while investigating)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CAUSE: the card bodies are almost transparent and the glow blobs swamp them:

    NODE_CARD_BODY_ALPHA = 0.22    // card fill is 22% -> the "?" and labels float on near-nothing
    GLOW_RADIUS = 92f, GLOW_RING_COUNT = 5, GLOW_RING_ALPHA = 0.13   // big soft additive discs

In the screenshot the AVAILABLE nodes are giant green/blue glow BLOBS with the actual card + label
lost inside them. The glow was meant to be a halo, not a fog bank.

FIX (do this together with ISSUE 3 — on the new opaque screen these can stay tasteful):
        NODE_CARD_BODY_ALPHA : 0.22 -> ~0.34   (card reads as a solid-ish panel, label has a base)
        GLOW_RADIUS          : 92f  -> ~70f     (halo hugs the card instead of bleeding two cards over)
        GLOW_RING_ALPHA      : 0.13 -> ~0.10    (a touch dimmer so the halo doesn't wash the label)
     Leave FOCUS_GLOW_BOOST as-is so the focused card still clearly pops.
     Optional: bump NODE_LABEL drop-shadow contrast is unnecessary once the body alpha is up.

IMPLEMENTATION ORDER (fastest path to the win)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Add MAP_SCREEN_FILL_ALPHA + the opaque screen-panel rect in render() (ISSUE 3). Biggest
   single readability jump; also makes the rest safe to tune.
2. Remove/zero the vignette top+bottom bands and close the top cull pinch (ISSUE 1).
3. Soften scanlines + flicker (ISSUE 2).
4. Raise card body alpha, shrink glow (ISSUE 4).
5. Re-run on desktop (./gradlew lwjgl3:run), portal into a ROUTE_SELECT, eyeball: no black edge
   slicing the top card row, world no longer ghosts through the map band, "?" labels crisp,
   glow is a halo not a blob. Then confirm on an Android build (target platform).

DO NOT
======
- Do NOT remove the CRT treatment wholesale — the phosphor/scanline identity is the console's
  charm; the fix is DIALLING IT DOWN, not deleting it.
- Do NOT introduce a per-frame FrameBuffer for the screen panel or glow — the opaque rect + the
  existing additive-disc glow are enough and stay viewport-safe.
- Do NOT touch selection/commit logic, layout math, or the graph — this is a pure visual pass.
- Do NOT hardcode any of the new values in the renderer — add them to RouteMapConstants first.
