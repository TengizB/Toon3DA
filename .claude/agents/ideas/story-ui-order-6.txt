TITLE: Story UI 6 — Codex, pacing, anti-skip & accessibility (wrap-up)
---

PURPOSE
The opt-in archive plus the whole-system rules that keep story UI from annoying the
player: pacing budget, anti-skip philosophy, and accessibility. Build last; it ties
orders 1–5 together. Depends on all of them.

PART A — THE CODEX (the one place long text is allowed)
- A quiet, out-of-the-way menu that ARCHIVES everything already said, plus the FULL text
  of any log the player chose to interact with. Nothing here is forced.
- Purpose: the ~20% who love lore can read for hours; the ~80% never open it and still
  get the whole story from barks/exchanges.

  ACCESS
  - From the pause/menu overlay (a book/terminal icon). Never interrupts play.
  - Optional: a subtle "NEW" dot when an entry unlocks, to invite (not force) a look.

  LAYOUT (readable, scrollable, thumb-friendly)
  - Left/top: category tabs — e.g. LOGS, PEOPLE, THE PLANET, THE ORGANIZATION, ORA,
    MEMORIES (your original self). Right/body: scrollable entry list → entry detail.
  - Big tap targets, large body text, generous line spacing, dark panel. Smooth scroll,
    no horizontal scroll. Long entries scroll within their own panel.
  - Each entry: title, source, full text, and (for logs) the AI's take shown at top.

  UNLOCKS & REWARD (pay them for engaging — 08 lever #3)
  - Entries unlock from log pickups, probe choices, and story beats.
  - Completion perks: small cosmetic rewards or a tiny meta bonus for filling a category.
    Never required; never gameplay-critical.
  - MEMORIES category fills as the block lifts region by region — the codex itself shows
    the player reassembling their true past.

PART B — PACING BUDGET (anti-overwhelm)
- Barks: hard rate-limit (order-2). No chatter. Silence is allowed and good.
- Exchanges: ~2–4 per region max (order-5). Never back-to-back.
- No story UI during intense combat spikes — queue it for the lull right after.
- If in doubt, cut. Under-telling beats over-telling for this audience.

PART C — ANTI-SKIP PHILOSOPHY (make them WANT it, don't trap them)
- Never trap the player in text. One tap always advances. Frustration makes skimming.
- We earn attention, not force it:
    • one line at a time (short = read without deciding to),
    • the AI is funny and the player bonds with her,
    • choices make the player a participant (order-4),
    • curiosity gaps ("...huh") and reading-for-loot pull them forward.
- Mandatory beats ride unskippable channels (boot card, first-appearance barks, the core
  ending exchange), so even a pure skimmer cannot miss the spine of the story.
- A player who skips everything optional should STILL understand: who they are, what the
  Organization does, and what choice they face at the core. Verify this "skimmer path."

PART D — ACCESSIBILITY & SETTINGS
- Story-text size / hold-time setting (slow, comfortable default).
- Nothing critical conveyed by color alone (always name chip + icon).
- Timers off by default; if any timed moment exists, it has a disable toggle and a clear
  default option (order-4).
- High-contrast panels; no flashing; combat screen-shake never disturbs story panels.
- Left/right-handed friendly: story panels centered, clear of both thumb clusters.

PART E — TELEMETRY (optional, for tuning)
- Log which barks/exchanges were seen, skipped fast, or read; which choices were picked;
  codex open rate. Use it to tune pacing and prune lines players ignore. No PII.

DEFINITION OF DONE
- Codex opens from the menu, lists unlocked entries by category, scrolls readably, shows
  "NEW" on unlock, and rewards completion. A "skim everything" playthrough still conveys
  the core story on unskippable channels. All accessibility settings function.
