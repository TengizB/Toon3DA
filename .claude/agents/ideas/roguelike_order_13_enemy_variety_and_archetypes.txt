TITLE: Enemy Variety — Six Demonic Archetypes (Floors 1-10+)
---

OVERVIEW
========
Six distinct enemy archetypes that fill out the bestiary the turn-based combat
core (enemy_system.txt) was built to run. Each is a different ANSWER to a
different question the player asks the tile grid: "can I outrun it? can I out-
range it? does the wall protect me? is it worth the ammo?" Together they cover
the role wheel — tank, ranged poke, charger, swarm/flyer, area-denial floater,
and elite bruiser — so any floor's monster mix poses a layered tactical problem
rather than one repeated threat.

This document OWNS: the six archetype stat blocks, their AI behavior, XP rewards
(mirrored into order_11's GameBalance), drop tables, and floor-appearance bands.
It does NOT re-define the turn engine, the perception/chain-alert phases, the
DDA LOS check, or Player.applyDamage — those are owned by enemy_system.txt
(IMPLEMENTED). Each archetype is a parameterization of that existing Enemy model
plus, where noted, one new behavior flag.

SHARED MODEL RECAP (from enemy_system.txt — every archetype uses these knobs)
=============================================================================
  - hp, attackDamage, attackType {MELEE, RANGED, BOTH}
  - moveEveryNTurns (movement throttle; attacks are NOT throttled when adjacent)
  - alertRadius (Chebyshev proximity wake) + LOS wake
  - rangedRange (tiles) for ranged/both; hitscan in tile space, needs clear LOS
  - states DORMANT/ALERTED/CHASING/ATTACKING
NEW FLAGS introduced by this doc (small additions to Enemy):
  - boolean ignoresWalls           (Lost Soul: flies; pathing + minimap special)
  - boolean chargeAttacker         (Pinky: locks a cardinal line and rushes)
  - boolean canBeKnockedBack       (order_15 melee knockback gate; default true)
  - MovementPattern {CHASE, PATROL, AMBUSH, CHARGE, FLOAT} (perception/idle flavor)

ARCHETYPE 1 — ZOMBIE  (spawn '1')   ROLE: slow attrition wall
=============================================================================
VISUAL: shambling reanimated UAC security guard in tattered armor, exposed ribs,
  one dragging leg. Desaturated green-grey skin, dim flashlight still clipped to
  the chest. Sprite sways slightly when dormant; lurches forward one tile per two
  turns when chasing. assets/textures/enemies/enemy_zombie.png
HP: 60        DAMAGE: 14 (melee)      ATTACK TYPE: MELEE, range 1 (Chebyshev==1)
MOVEMENT: CHASE, moveEveryNTurns = 2 (slow — Corruptor cadence).
XP REWARD: 8
AI BEHAVIOR: dormant until proximity/LOS wake. Once alerted, never loses interest;
  greedy-steps toward the player every OTHER turn but SWINGS every turn it is
  adjacent (you cannot out-DPS it by clinging — you must create distance). The
  tutorial threat: teaches "back-pedal and reload" because it is slow enough to
  kite but tanky enough to demand commitment.
RESISTANCES: none. Vulnerable to everything (incl. status). The honest baseline.
DROPS: 25% Stim pack '+', 10% Armor shard 'a'. (Roll on death.)
FLOORS: 1-6 (the bread-and-butter early threat; thins out by mid-game).

=============================================================================
ARCHETYPE 2 — IMP  (spawn '2')   ROLE: ranged poke / sightline punisher
=============================================================================
VISUAL: hunched leathery brown demon, glowing orange palms it cocks back before
  hurling a fireball. Spindly limbs, white eyes. Telegraph: palms flare bright one
  frame before the shot (readable tell). enemy_imp.png
HP: 18        DAMAGE: 8 (ranged fireblast)   ATTACK TYPE: RANGED, range 5
MOVEMENT: CHASE/KITE, moveEveryNTurns = 1 (fast — Vortex Eye cadence).
XP REWARD: 6
AI BEHAVIOR: wakes on LOS at long range (it sees you first). If LOS clear and
  within range -> FIRE (hitscan in tile space, can be dodged via AGILITY, blocked
  by walls/columns). If too close it KITES one tile away to restore range; if too
  far it closes. The "break LOS behind a corner" trainer. A fireball can apply
  Burning (order_14) at a low chance — see RESISTANCES note.
RESISTANCES: takes HALF fire damage; immune to Burning self-inflict. Normal to
  everything else.
DROPS: 15% Stim pack '+'. Low drop rate (they are common chaff).
FLOORS: 1-8 (the persistent ranged annoyance throughout).

=============================================================================
ARCHETYPE 3 — PINKY DEMON  (spawn '3')   ROLE: straight-line charger
=============================================================================
VISUAL: bulky pink hairless bull-demon, enormous jaw, tiny legs, no ranged
  capacity. Lowers its head and snorts (telegraph) one turn before it charges.
  enemy_pinky.png
HP: 45        DAMAGE: 18 (melee, +knockback on charge hit)   ATTACK TYPE: MELEE 1
MOVEMENT: CHARGE, moveEveryNTurns = 1 normally; CHARGE special below.
XP REWARD: 10
AI BEHAVIOR (chargeAttacker = true): when alerted and the player lies on a clear
  CARDINAL line (same row or column, unobstructed by wall/door/enemy), the Pinky
  enters a one-turn WIND-UP (snort telegraph, no move), then on the next turn
  RUSHES in a straight line covering up to PINKY_CHARGE_TILES (default 4) tiles in
  a single turn, stopping on the tile adjacent to the player (or the first wall)
  and dealing damage + knockback if it reaches melee range. If NOT on a clean
  line, it behaves as an ordinary moveEveryNTurns=1 chaser, repositioning to line
  itself up. The "don't stand in its lane; round a corner to cancel the charge"
  enemy. Charge cancels if the lane is broken during wind-up (door closes,
  another enemy steps in, player strafes out of the row/column).
RESISTANCES: resists knockback (canBeKnockedBack = false — it's too heavy);
  normal to all damage/status.
DROPS: 20% Stim '+', 15% Armor shard 'a'.
FLOORS: 2-9.

=============================================================================
ARCHETYPE 4 — LOST SOUL  (spawn '4')   ROLE: flying swarm / wall-ignoring harasser
=============================================================================
VISUAL: a flaming flying skull, no body, trailing embers, mouth agape. Bobs in
  place when dormant. enemy_lost_soul.png
HP: 12        DAMAGE: 6 (melee bite on contact)   ATTACK TYPE: MELEE 1
MOVEMENT: FLOAT, ignoresWalls = true, moveEveryNTurns = 1 (fast).
XP REWARD: 5
AI BEHAVIOR: the ONLY archetype that pathfinds THROUGH walls — it flies, so its
  greedy step ignores Level.isWall for MOVEMENT (still cannot occupy the player's
  tile; still cannot pass the OUTER world boundary). This breaks the "hide behind
  a wall" instinct the Imp teaches: a Lost Soul comes straight at you through
  geometry. Low HP makes it a one-shot for most weapons, but it spawns in PACKS
  (level generator places 2-4 per spawn cluster) and the chain-alert scream wakes
  them together. Tactically it forces the player to actually KILL rather than
  evade. MINIMAP: rendered with a distinct "flying" marker (see VISUAL) and is
  NOT hidden by fog-of-war walls between it and the player at close range,
  reflecting that it ignores walls (consistent with fog-of-war-minimap.txt rules
  — flyers reveal through one wall thickness within alertRadius).
RESISTANCES: immune to knockback while flying (canBeKnockedBack = false). Takes
  +25% fire? No — it IS fire; immune to Burning, normal to everything else.
DROPS: none (chaff). Rare 5% Stim from a pack.
FLOORS: 3-10 (escalating swarm pressure deeper in).

=============================================================================
ARCHETYPE 5 — CACODEMON  (spawn '5')   ROLE: hovering area-denial acid floater
=============================================================================
VISUAL: a giant floating spherical red demon, one huge central eye, a maw of
  jagged teeth, tiny horns. Hovers, drifts. Cheeks puff before an acid spit
  (telegraph). enemy_cacodemon.png
HP: 70        DAMAGE: 12 (ranged acid spit, applies Poisoned)   ATTACK TYPE: RANGED 4
MOVEMENT: FLOAT (slow drift), moveEveryNTurns = 2. Does NOT ignore walls (too big
  to phase — it's a hovering blob, not an incorporeal skull). ignoresWalls=false.
XP REWARD: 16
AI BEHAVIOR: a slow, tanky ranged threat. Wakes on LOS, drifts to maintain a
  medium range, and spits acid (hitscan, needs LOS) that deals direct damage AND
  applies the Poisoned status (order_14, stacks) — so even after you break LOS the
  poison ticks. It does not kite aggressively (slow), so the counter is to close
  the gap fast (it is bad in melee) OR break LOS and out-wait the poison. Punishes
  ranged-only, sit-back play; rewards aggression — the inverse of the Imp.
RESISTANCES: immune to its own Poison; resists poison damage by 50% (it's made of
  the stuff). Normal to fire/physical.
DROPS: 25% Stim '+', 20% Armor shard 'a', 5% Field medkit 'H'.
FLOORS: 4-10.

=============================================================================
ARCHETYPE 6 — HELL KNIGHT  (spawn '6' — NEW)   ROLE: armored elite, melee + ranged
=============================================================================
VISUAL: tall muscular grey-brown humanoid demon, goat legs, armored shoulders,
  glowing green fists it can cock back for a ranged plasma-like bolt OR swing in
  melee. The "you are in trouble now" silhouette. enemy_hell_knight.png
HP: 120       DAMAGE: 22 melee / 16 ranged bolt   ATTACK TYPE: BOTH
MOVEMENT: CHASE, moveEveryNTurns = 1 (it is fast for its size — relentless).
XP REWARD: 35
AI BEHAVIOR (attackType BOTH): the elite. If adjacent -> heavy melee swing
  (22, knockback-resistant). If not adjacent but within HELL_KNIGHT_RANGE_TILES
  (default 4) with LOS -> green-fist ranged bolt (16). Otherwise closes
  aggressively every turn. There is no safe range: kiting eats bolts, closing eats
  swings. The intended counter is BURST + cover dance + using the floor geometry,
  not raw evasion. Spawns sparingly (1, occasionally 2 per floor) as a mini-elite;
  the level generator should treat it as a "budget spike" placement, often
  guarding a keycard or the exit.
RESISTANCES: 25% physical damage reduction (armored); knockback-resistant
  (canBeKnockedBack = false); normal to fire/poison. NOT immune to Stun, but
  Stun duration on it is halved (heavy frame).
DROPS: 60% Armor shard 'a' OR Security vest 'A', 30% Field medkit 'H', plus a
  guaranteed XP lump (already in xpReward). Often gates a pickup behind itself.
FLOORS: 5-10+ (and as the standard non-boss elite thereafter).

BALANCE NOTES (all numbers placeholder)
=======================================
- HP/damage chosen against the existing shotgun (coeff 0.18, ~20 point-blank):
  Imp/Lost Soul die in one shell, Zombie ~3, Pinky ~3, Caco ~4, Hell Knight ~6
  (before perks/stats). This keeps the elite genuinely threatening.
- Each archetype must answer a DIFFERENT question, so no two share a counter:
  Zombie = distance, Imp = cover, Pinky = lane-break, Lost Soul = must-kill,
  Caco = aggression, Hell Knight = burst+geometry. Mixing two creates a real
  puzzle (kite the Zombie while a corner blocks the Imp, etc.).
- Telegraphs (palm flare, snort, cheek puff) are the readability contract: every
  hit the player takes should be one the telegraph warned about. Critical for the
  "I understand why I died" pillar.

INTERACTIONS
============
1. TURN ENGINE (enemy_system.txt): all six are parameterizations of the existing
   Enemy + a few new flags (ignoresWalls, chargeAttacker, canBeKnockedBack,
   MovementPattern). The two-phase turn, chain-alert, and red-alert pulse are
   unchanged.
2. STATUS EFFECTS (order_14): each archetype's RESISTANCES block maps directly to
   that doc's resist/immunity table (Imp half-fire, Caco half-poison, Lost
   Soul/Hell Knight knockback-immune, etc.). order_14 owns the mechanism.
3. XP/PERKS (order_11): xpReward feeds awardExperience on death; the table above
   replaces the placeholder values.
4. STATS (order_12): player STRENGTH/MARKSMANSHIP scale damage DEALT to these
   enemies; Hell Knight's 25% physical reduction and Pinky knockback-immunity are
   read by the damage/knockback pipeline.
5. MELEE (order_15): knockback pushes only canBeKnockedBack==true enemies one
   tile; Pinky/Lost Soul/Hell Knight ignore it. Chainsaw vs Hell Knight is the
   high-risk melee payoff.
6. FOG OF WAR / MINIMAP (fog-of-war-minimap.txt): Lost Soul's ignoresWalls grants
   it the through-wall minimap reveal at close range.
7. LEVEL GENERATION: floor bands + threat budget drive spawn placement; Hell
   Knight as a keycard/exit guard.

OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
1. New spawn digit '6' for Hell Knight, or place elites via a generator pass on an
   existing digit? Recommend the generator pass (avoids a symbol commit) unless
   hand-authored levels need to place Hell Knights explicitly.
2. Lost Soul through-wall movement vs raycasting: the BILLBOARD must not render
   through solid walls (z-buffer handles occlusion) even though its LOGIC ignores
   walls — confirm EnemyRenderer occludes it correctly when it is mid-wall. Likely
   render it only when its tile is in player LOS; it "phases out" while inside a
   wall, "phases in" when it emerges.
3. Pinky charge through a closing door / other enemy mid-rush: cancel and snort
   again, or barrel through? Recommend cancel (lane broken) — keeps it readable.
4. Does the Cacodemon's poison stack with multiple Cacos? Per order_14 Poisoned
   stacks; two Cacos could stack-poison fast. Cap stacks (order_14 owns the cap).
5. Should the existing implemented names (Corruptor/Vortex Eye/Ghoul/Crawler/
   Revenant) be kept as alternate-skin variants of these archetypes for variety,
   or fully replaced? Recommend keep them as RESKINS sharing the same AI (a
   Corruptor IS a Zombie skin) so existing levels still work.
