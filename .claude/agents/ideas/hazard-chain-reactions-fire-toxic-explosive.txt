OVERVIEW
========
A turn-based environmental hazard layer that turns the level itself into a
weapon. Explosive barrels (`B`) detonate, throwing fire onto adjacent tiles;
oil pools (`O`) ignite into spreading fire; fire damages and panics anything
standing in it; radioactive barrels (`b`) rupture into lingering toxic gas.
The pillar is emergent complexity from simple rules: a single shotgun pellet
into a barrel beside an oil slick can wipe a corridor of zombies — or cook the
marine who fired it. Every hazard is a tile-space cellular automaton ticked
once per turn, fully consistent with the existing grid + turn engine.

This is the first feature that makes positioning and the level layout a
combat resource rather than just scenery. It reuses props and decals that
already exist in the tile spec (`B`, `b`, `O`) and the planned weapon-damage
code path, so it adds depth without inventing new art.


GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
HAZARDS ARE TILE-LAYER STATE, TICKED ONCE PER ENEMY PHASE.
A new HazardField owns a parallel grid of HazardCell state laid over the Level.
It ticks exactly once per full turn (during the ENEMIES_ACTING phase from the
turn engine), so hazards advance in lockstep with everything else. No real-time
fire.

IGNITION (player-driven, the interesting decision):
1. Player fires a weapon (shotgun pellet ray) or punches/melee a barrel tile.
2. Weapon damage resolution already marches tile-by-tile (see pump-action
   shotgun doc). When the marched tile contains a hazard prop, route the hit to
   HazardField.onTileDamaged(tileColumn, tileRow, damage, source) INSTEAD of /
   in addition to a normal enemy-hit.
3. Explosive barrel `B` reaching 0 HP -> EXPLODE event queued for next tick.
4. Oil pool `O` taking any FIRE-typed damage, or being adjacent to a FIRE cell,
   -> becomes a BURNING cell.
5. Radioactive barrel `b` reaching 0 HP -> RUPTURE event (toxic gas) queued.

PER-TURN HAZARD TICK (HazardField.tick(level, enemies, player)):
1. Resolve queued EXPLODE events first (instantaneous, this tick):
   - Deal EXPLOSION_DAMAGE to player/enemy on the barrel tile and the 4
     cardinal neighbours (cross pattern, not diagonals — grid-consistent).
   - Each affected FLOOR/decal tile (not walls) is set to FIRE with
     FIRE_LIFETIME_TURNS. Walls block the blast: a neighbour that is a wall is
     not set on fire and shields the tile beyond it.
   - If a neighbour tile contains another `B`, it takes EXPLOSION_DAMAGE ->
     chain detonation queued for the NEXT tick (one-tick delay = visible
     "domino" cascade, very readable, very Doom).
   - If a neighbour contains `O`, that oil ignites this tick.
2. Resolve queued RUPTURE events: set the barrel tile + 4 neighbours to TOXIC
   gas with GAS_LIFETIME_TURNS. Toxic does not spread further on its own; it
   only dissipates.
3. Spread fire: any FLOOR tile cardinally adjacent to a BURNING tile that also
   contains `O` oil becomes BURNING (fire follows oil trails). Plain floor does
   NOT catch fire — only oil carries flame. This keeps spread bounded and
   designer-controllable via oil placement.
4. Apply standing damage: any actor (player or enemy) whose tile is FIRE takes
   FIRE_DAMAGE_PER_TURN; any actor on TOXIC takes TOXIC_DAMAGE_PER_TURN.
   Enemies standing in fire also gain a PANIC flag (see INTERACTIONS).
5. Decrement every hazard cell's remaining lifetime by 1. At 0, FIRE -> SCORCHED
   floor decal (permanent cosmetic blackening), TOXIC -> clears to floor.

PLAYER EXPERIENCE
=================
The player learns to read the room before shooting. A barrel is no longer
scenery; it is a question: "Do I lure the zombie conga line next to that
explosive barrel and pop it, or is the blast going to reach me too?" Oil trails
become fuses the player can deliberately exploit ("shoot the barrel at the far
end, watch the fire run down the oil into the pack"). The standout feeling is
the domino cascade — barrels chaining one tile per turn, each detonation
lighting the next, a slow-motion grid explosion you set off with one shell.

The flip side is dread: walking through a flickering, oil-streaked maintenance
corridor knowing a single stray shot or a panicking enemy could turn the floor
to flame. Death by your own barrel must feel earned ("I shot point-blank into
the barrel I was standing next to") — never random.


TECHNICAL NOTES
===============

NEW PACKAGE & CLASSES
---------------------
- ge.tbegvadze.toon3d.hazard.HazardField   — owns the parallel hazard grid;
                                              tick(), onTileDamaged(), query
                                              methods getHazardAt(col,row).
- ge.tbegvadze.toon3d.hazard.HazardCell    — value object: HazardType type,
                                              int turnsRemaining,
                                              DamageSource source.
- ge.tbegvadze.toon3d.hazard.HazardType    — enum: NONE, FIRE, TOXIC, SCORCHED.
- ge.tbegvadze.toon3d.hazard.PropHealth    — sparse map of barrel HP keyed by
                                              packed (column,row) int; barrels
                                              are not full enemies, just
                                              destructible props.
- ge.tbegvadze.toon3d.hazard.HazardEvent   — value object for queued EXPLODE /
                                              RUPTURE events (column, row, type).

HAZARD GRID STORAGE
-------------------
- byte[] per cell is enough: pack HazardType ordinal (low bits) + turnsRemaining
  (high bits) into one byte/short, OR keep a small HazardCell[] sized to the
  level. Pre-allocate at level load; never allocate during tick or render.
- Walls and solid props are looked up from Level, not duplicated here. Hazards
  only ever live on walkable tiles (floor + decal tiles).

TURN ENGINE INTEGRATION
-----------------------
- TurnEngine (zombie doc) calls HazardField.tick(...) exactly once, at the START
  of the ENEMIES_ACTING phase, BEFORE enemies decide their turns. This ordering
  matters: an enemy should react to the fire that is already on its tile this
  turn (it should try to flee a burning tile, not walk onto it next turn).
- Damage applied by HazardField uses the same player/enemy HP fields and the
  same death handling as weapon damage. Reuse, do not duplicate.

DAMAGE TYPING
-------------
- Introduce a tiny DamageSource/DamageType concept (PHYSICAL, FIRE, TOXIC,
  EXPLOSION). Only FIRE-typed damage ignites oil. The shotgun pellet is
  PHYSICAL — so a normal shot does NOT ignite oil, but DOES damage/detonate a
  barrel. A future flamethrower / incendiary round would be FIRE-typed and
  ignite oil directly. This keeps the door open for build diversity.

EVENT QUEUE
-----------
- HazardField holds an explodeQueue and ruptureQueue (pre-sized array-backed
  ring buffers, no per-turn allocation). Queued this turn, resolved next tick,
  giving the one-tick domino delay. Guard against infinite loops: a tile can
  only be set on fire once per tick (dirty-flag pass).

GAMEMATH ADDITIONS (with derivation comment blocks)
---------------------------------------------------
- packTileKey(tileColumn, tileRow) -> int (column * levelWidth + row)
- cardinalNeighbourOffsets() — the four (dColumn,dRow) cross offsets, as a
  static final int[][] in Constants (NOT a fresh allocation).
- No trig needed; this is all integer grid logic.

CONSTANTS ADDITIONS (placeholders — tune in playtest)
-----------------------------------------------------
  EXPLOSIVE_BARREL_HP            = 1     (one pellet pops it)
  RADIOACTIVE_BARREL_HP         = 4     (tougher; must be deliberately broken)
  EXPLOSION_DAMAGE              = 12    (lethal to zombies, badly hurts player)
  FIRE_DAMAGE_PER_TURN          = 3
  TOXIC_DAMAGE_PER_TURN         = 2
  FIRE_LIFETIME_TURNS           = 3
  GAS_LIFETIME_TURNS            = 5
  FIRE_SPREAD_ONLY_ON_OIL       = true  (design flag, documents the rule)
  HAZARD_CHAIN_DELAY_TURNS      = 1     (domino visible delay)
  EXPLOSION_NOISE_RADIUS_TILES  = 8     (feeds noise/alert system)


LEVEL / TILE IMPLICATIONS
=========================
- NO new tile chars required. Uses existing `B` (explosive barrel), `b`
  (radioactive barrel), `O` (oil pool). This is intentional — the feature
  weaponizes art that already exists.
- New COSMETIC decal char (optional, low priority): `:` SCORCHED floor, the
  permanent black mark left after fire burns out. If not added, FIRE simply
  reverts to its original floor char on expiry.
- Level designers gain a real combat-design vocabulary:
    * A `B` next to a `O` trail laid through a corridor = a player-triggerable
      trap line.
    * Two or three `B` in a line = a domino cascade waiting for one pellet.
    * `b` radioactive barrels in a dead-end side room reward greedy looters
      with a toxic-cloud punishment if they break one carelessly.
- Generator guidance: barrels should sit on or adjacent to walkable tiles the
  player and enemies actually traverse, never sealed behind walls where the
  blast does nothing. Oil trails should connect a barrel to a likely combat
  tile (chokepoint, doorway) to make the fuse meaningful.

Example test snippet (player can pop the barrel and watch fire run the oil):

  xp        x
  x OOOOO B x      <- shoot B (right), fire runs left along the oil
  x   Z Z   x      <- two zombies caught in the oil trail
  xxxxxxxxxxx


VISUAL DESIGN
=============
- FIRE cell: animated additive orange/yellow billboard on the tile floor,
  flickering at real-time speed (like the existing `f` flickering floor — reuse
  that animation timing). Casts a warm shade bump on nearby walls via the same
  WallRenderer brightness-bump hook the muzzle flash uses (lab lighting +
  muzzle-flash precedent). Fire is bright; it should genuinely light the corridor.
- EXPLOSION: a single-frame full-tile flash + screen shake (reuse ScreenShake
  from the shotgun doc, higher intensity) + brief screen brighten. One punchy
  "WHUMP" sample.
- TOXIC gas: semi-transparent sickly green billboard fog over the tile, gently
  rising; the existing radioactive-barrel green palette. Slightly desaturates /
  green-tints the player's view when the player tile is TOXIC.
- SCORCHED floor: dark charred decal, no animation. Pure atmosphere/breadcrumb
  ("a fight happened here").
- HUD: when the player is on FIRE or TOXIC, the marine-vitals HUD shows a
  status icon (flame / biohazard) and the screen edge pulses the matching
  colour, distinct from the generic red damage flash.
- Billboard ordering: hazard floor sprites render with enemy sprites in the
  painter's-algorithm pass (after walls, before mini-map), z-buffer clipped per
  the existing SpriteRenderer.


BALANCE NOTES
=============
- EXPLOSION_DAMAGE = 12 one-shots an 8-HP zombie and removes ~60% of a 20-HP
  marine — barrels must feel genuinely dangerous to both sides, or the decision
  is trivial. Flag as the primary tuning knob.
- FIRE_DAMAGE_PER_TURN = 3 over FIRE_LIFETIME_TURNS = 3 = up to 9 damage if an
  actor stands in it the whole time; enemies should usually flee after one tick,
  so realistic fire damage to a fleeing zombie is ~3-6. The player must never be
  forced to walk through fire with no alternative route (generator rule).
- Chain caps: cap total simultaneous active FIRE/EXPLODE cells per tick
  (placeholder 64) to bound worst-case cost and prevent a whole-floor inferno
  from one shot in a pathological level.
- Radioactive barrels are higher-HP and lower-reward-by-default so the player
  has to CHOOSE to break them (e.g. to deny a corridor to a melee horde) rather
  than popping them reflexively.
- All numbers above are placeholders pending playtest.


INTERACTIONS
============
- ZOMBIE + TURN ENGINE (zombie doc): enemies on a FIRE tile gain PANIC for one
  turn — they move to a random adjacent NON-FIRE walkable tile instead of
  chasing (overrides greedy-step pathing). This produces the classic "burning
  monster runs around" moment and lets fire herd enemies. Fire/explosion/toxic
  damage routes through the same enemy HP + DEAD handling.
- PUMP-ACTION SHOTGUN (shotgun doc): the pellet ray is the primary ignition
  source. Add a barrel/oil branch in fire() resolution: if a marched tile holds
  a hazard prop, call HazardField.onTileDamaged(...). Penetration rule: a pellet
  stops at a barrel it destroys (the barrel is now gone, but the blast continues
  the chain). EXPLOSION_NOISE feeds the planned noise/alert radius.
- NOISE / RED ALERT (zombie + red-alert docs): an EXPLODE event emits a large
  NoiseEvent (EXPLOSION_NOISE_RADIUS_TILES), waking enemies across the floor and
  intensifying the red-alert pulse — explosions are loud and have consequences.
- LAB LIGHTING + FLICKERING FLOOR (lighting docs): fire reuses the flicker
  animation cadence and the wall brightness-bump hook; unlit `u` tiles become
  briefly visible while fire burns there.
- FUTURE ITEMS: a Radiation Suit negates TOXIC damage; an incendiary shell or
  flamethrower deals FIRE-typed damage that ignites oil directly; a fire
  extinguisher (Doom RPG improvised weapon) could clear FIRE cells in front of
  the player — strong build-defining synergies.


OPEN QUESTIONS
=============
1. Should plain floor ever catch fire, or strictly only oil? Recommendation:
   only oil (and the explosion's direct cross), to keep spread bounded and
   designer-authored. Revisit if fire feels too contained.
2. Do walls fully block explosions, or do thin (1-tile) walls let blast through?
   Recommendation: walls fully block — consistent with LOS, simplest to reason
   about.
3. Should the player be able to push/kick a barrel one tile to reposition it?
   Recommendation: defer to a future "kick" action; tempting but expands scope.
4. Permanent SCORCHED decal or revert-to-floor on burnout? Recommendation:
   add `:` scorched decal if cheap; otherwise revert. Cosmetic only.
5. Does toxic gas drift (spread one tile/turn) or stay put? Recommendation:
   stay put for v1 (predictable); drifting gas is a great later upgrade.
6. Friendly-fire on the player from their own explosion — always on?
   Recommendation: yes, always. The danger to self is the entire decision.
