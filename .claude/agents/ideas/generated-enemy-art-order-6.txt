OVERVIEW
--------
The five UNDEAD - Ghoul, Crawler, Revenant, Vortex Eye, Blight Corruptor - rebuilt as animated, reactive
creatures. In the premise they are the facility's own staff, killed by the catastrophe and the Deepworks'
conditions and dragged back into motion by raw soul bleeding through the galleries: victims, not monsters.
They are people, so the kit gets a HUMANOID RIG (one skeleton, five bodies, five ways of moving wrong),
cloth, and the family's new glow ink, SOUL.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - Kit growth:
      * humanoid rig: head, neck, torso, pelvis, arms with hands, legs with feet, driven by joint-angle pose
        parameters - so walk cycles, reaches and collapses are keyframes, not new drawings.
      * cloth: Organization work overalls (desaturated slate), hazmat rubber (dull olive-grey - not green,
        G5), lab white (livery), security armour (gunmetal/darkmetal); a cloth-fold carve helper; cloth
        that lags its limb by a frame (secondary motion).
      * dead-skin ramp (ash grey, cold undertone); the SOUL emissive ramp (pale bone-white, faint cold tint,
        low saturation so it never reads as the purple "special" colour - G5).
  - Five briefs -> five rigs with the full clip set, DORMANT + WOUNDED idles, and ability clips:
      Vortex Eye        DEBUFF_PLAYER (the vortex tightens and lashes)
      Blight Corruptor  SUMMON (spore pods split and the raised dead are "released" from them)
  - Effects (effects.py): soul wisps (hit), cloth scraps + soul burst (death), the Vortex Eye's soul bolt
    projectile, spore puffs for the Corruptor.
  - Owner gate (the Ghoul alone first - see RISKS), five registry rows, earlier creatures byte-identical, docs.

OUT
  - Facial expressions as acting; gore; wounds as the main read (tone: restraint).
  - SUMMON rules (the raised dead use these same types and therefore these sprites automatically).

VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
GHOUL (CHAFF, 0.90 walls, melee shambler)
  A labourer in torn overalls, head lolled, a lanyard ID badge on the chest, soul light in the eyes and the
  slack mouth. MOTION: idle = swaying, head rolling; move = a dragging, patient shamble, one foot scuffing;
  attack = a clumsy two-handed grab; hurt = it rocks back and keeps coming; death = folds at the knees.
CRAWLER (CHAFF, 0.55 walls, fast scuttler)
  A lab technician on all fours, spine arched high, head thrust forward, long splayed arms, a torn lab coat
  dragging. Full anatomy (guide 12.6). MOTION: idle = a twitching crouch; move = a fast, unnatural
  scuttle; attack = a leap-swipe; death = it skids flat.
REVENANT (BRUISER, 1.05 walls, fast heavy melee)
  Organization security in riot armour - pauldrons, a cracked visor with soul light behind it, a shock
  baton, a stencilled unit number. MOTION: idle = an alert combat stance; move = a heavy charging stride;
  wind-up = the baton raised high; attack = an overhead smash; hurt = armour sparks, barely rocks; death =
  drops to its knees, then forward.
VORTEX EYE (CHAFF, 0.55 walls, short-range caster, DEBUFF_PLAYER)
  A researcher's head and shoulders held aloft by a slow vortex of soul light pouring from the eye sockets.
  Hover baked into the canvas. MOTION: idle = the vortex turning, the head bobbing; attack = the vortex
  tightens and lashes (soul bolt from the eye anchor, short reach); death = the vortex unravels and the
  head drops.
BLIGHT CORRUPTOR (SOLDIER, 1.00 walls, durable carrier, SUMMON)
  A quarantine worker in a full hazmat suit, swollen from inside by spore growth bursting through in soft
  soul-lit pods, a decontamination sprayer tank fused to its back. MOTION: idle = laboured breathing, pods
  pulsing; move = a slow heavy trudge; summon = pods split and puff; death = it collapses and the pods burst.

DECISIONS
---------
  D1  OWNER OVERRIDE (2026-10-22, the /implement request; supersedes VISUAL DESIGN and AS2 where they clash): no human
      face is ever visible - at most glowing eyes; every undead is covered (armour, hazard suit, hood, respirator,
      helmet). One enemy is a TALL, THIN humanoid with disproportionately long legs, arms and fingers, in a hazard suit
      and a gas mask with ONE glass visor (never two lenses) and glowing RED eyes; others may wear two-lens masks.
      Built as the GHOUL. The red eyes are an owner-approved exception to G5 (material `redeye`).
  D2  OWNER GATE ORDER (same request): the concept still of every enemy is shown and approved BEFORE any animation is
      made; this replaces the Ghoul-alone early read of A1 / RISKS.
  D3  GHOUL MASK (owner, 2026-10-22, after the first concept): a LONG gas mask. The owner supplied a reference image of
      a figure in a hazard suit and gas mask, to be used as a loose reference only: ignore the device on its chest and
      do not make the in-game enemy look too similar to it. Built as a long ribbed rubber respirator snout hanging to
      the chest, ending in a filter canister, plus taped glove and boot cuffs; the slate suit, the single visor, the red
      eyes and the long limbs stay ours.
  D4  CONCEPTS APPROVED (owner, 2026-10-22: "Approved, now continue working on idea"): the five concept stills - the
      Ghoul with the long mask (75aa234), Crawler, Revenant, Vortex Eye, Blight Corruptor (3b1bc07) - are approved.
      Animation, effects and the clip gate follow (A1 met: the concepts were read before any clip was built).
  D5  REVENANT WEAPON (owner, 2026-10-22, at the clip gate): the baton hand "is cursed" - it deformed and moved in
      impossible ways - and the owner does not want this enemy to hold a club. Redesigned: a tall RIOT SHIELD on the left
      arm replaces the baton, the right hand is a plain closed gauntlet that is never articulated, and the attack is a
      SHIELD BASH (braced -> drawn back -> the shield driven at the viewer). WIND_UP = crouched behind the raised shield.
      VISUAL DESIGN's "shock baton" and "overhead smash" are superseded.
  D6  GHOUL BUILD (owner, 2026-10-22): "too thin, looks weak" -> thicker (no muscles), then heavier legs, then heavier
      again with the body and the arms ~12% longer (approved). A version matched to the owner's reference image of a
      hazard-suit figure was judged "fat" and reverted. Approved: BULK 1.6 (body, arms), LEG_BULK 1.8, spine 65, arms 50/47.
  D7  ALL APPROVED (owner, 2026-10-22: "Approved now. You have my approve for everything. Now implement the idea
      fully"): the five undead - Ghoul (D6), Crawler, Revenant (riot shield, D5), Vortex Eye, Blight Corruptor - their
      clips, the undead effect set and the SUMMON runtime row. A2 met.
