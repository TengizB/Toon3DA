OVERVIEW
--------
The last four textures and the most important ones: the Void Shroud (the only regular DEMON) and the
three bosses - the Overseer (MACHINE), the Corruptor (ABERRATION) and the Hell Baron (DEMON). Two of
the bosses have never had their own art at all: the Corruptor and the Hell Baron draw a placeholder
quadrant of the blight sheet today. Bosses use the large 240x360 canvas (G4) because they are drawn
1.6-2.0 walls tall.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - Four briefs -> void_shroud.png (160x240), overseer.png, corruptor.png, hell_baron.png (240x360).
  - Kit growth: a SHADOW material (near-black charcoal with ember undertones - readable only through
    its rims and core, never violet), smoke-taper helper (a body that dissolves into dithered wisps
    toward the floor line), and canvas-size support for 240x360 (the stencil and every helper take
    absolute pixel coordinates already; sheet.py must handle mixed sizes).
  - Owner gate (G11) - the bosses are gated INDIVIDUALLY, each with an in-engine boss-arena shot.
  - Four registry rows (the boss rows replace today's placeholder regions).
  - Earlier sprites byte-identical; docs.

    so it inherits the new art for free), arena levels.
  - Changing boss heightMultiplier values (1.80 / 1.60 / 2.00).
  - Multiple boss frames per phase (AS3 - single frame).

VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
DESIGN BRIEF - VOID SHROUD (DEMON, SOLDIER, 0.80 walls, melee flanker)
  The planet's pain given a shape: a tall, hooded drift of shadow-stuff with an ember core where a chest
  would be, one long arm reaching out to the side and forward - the flank, the reach around you - and a
  body that thins into smoke above the floor. Readable on a dark floor only by its rims (cyan rim from
  the facility light, magma from its core): make the outline do the work.

DESIGN BRIEF - THE OVERSEER (MACHINE boss, 1.80 walls, ranged, telegraphed phases)
  The game's story says the Overseer is not a person - "It's a desk, not a person"; the chair is the
  cruelty. So the boss is the CHAIR made into a war machine: a monumental administrative chassis - a
  console-throne of Organization gunmetal and livery panels, a bank of cyan screens and lenses where a
  face would be, filing-arm manipulators, cables running up out of the canvas into the ceiling. Nobody
  sits in it. The cyan ink at its most intense: this is the source of the facility's light.

DESIGN BRIEF - THE CORRUPTOR (ABERRATION boss, 1.60 walls, ranged, telegraphed)
  Where the breach began: a towering mass of harvested tissue grown around the ruin of a containment
  tank, the tank's ribs and glass shards still embedded in it. Many eyes (order 3's eyeball helper) in
  rows down its front, all aimed at the player - it "tells you what's coming" by looking. Ichor ink,
  the flesh ramps of order 3; the aberrations are visibly its offspring.

DESIGN BRIEF - THE HELL BARON (DEMON boss, 2.00 walls, melee, read the mark)
  The planet's immune champion - an antibody the size of a room. Armoured in the planet's own crust
  (sculpted per order 5's stone rule, never slabs), an ember core, immense forearms built for the heavy,
  telegraphed strike. Deliberately NOT the stock horned devil: its menace is mass and patience. One
  Organization artefact embedded in it like a splinter - a drill bit, broken off in its shoulder: the
  wound that woke the planet.
