OVERVIEW
--------
Three environment objects, chosen by the owner, are rebuilt with the kit and animated: the COMPUTER TERMINAL
('T', the story's LOG terminal), the BIO-POD ('&' / sprite prop_biopod, which becomes a floor-to-ceiling
SPECIMEN TUBE) and the EXIT PORTAL ('>'). Because they are the first props, this order also builds what every
later prop order reuses: the prop bake in the kit (props.py), the neon materials, the prop sheet format and
the PropRenderer path that draws an animated, glowing generated prop.

RULES
-----
  R1  A registered prop is drawn ONLY from its sheet; the per-level EnvironmentTextureSet / fixed texture map
      no longer carries a picture for it.
  R2  Frame index = floor((time + phase) / frameSeconds) mod frameCount, phase from the tile (GameMath), so
      neighbouring copies desynchronise. No allocation per frame.
  R3  Body pass = the existing prop pass unchanged (shade x tile brightness x mood grade, muzzle light,
      occluder record, contact shadow), sampling the frame's rect. The portal body stays ungraded (it was
      emissive before - art-direction section 5).
  R4  Glow pass = the frame's glow rect, NORMAL blend, colour white (ungraded, unshaded), alpha =
      GameMath.wallGlowDistanceAlpha(...) x the floor's light multiplier (BLACKOUT dims it) - the wall glow
      rule, so walls and props glow alike. Same wall z-test and nearer-sprite test as the body.
  R5  A sheet that fails to load is logged and the prop is skipped (not drawn) - never a crash.
  R6  Everything loaded is disposed with the PropRenderer (one owner).


VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
Common: smooth bake, the kit's light rig (key upper-left, cyan rim), cold steel + lab-white bodies
(gunmetal, darkmetal, livery, steel_lt, frost), neon only on emissive parts, a soft bloom halo around the neon
baked into the glow sheet. Each prop carries one Organization stencil (serial).

COMPUTER TERMINAL ('T', 0.80 wall height, canvas 160 x 200, 16 frames @ 8 fps, loops)
  A free-standing Organization console. A heavy darkmetal pedestal with a lab-white (livery) front panel
  and a stencilled serial, a sloped keyboard deck, and on top a deep steel CRT housing angled at the viewer.
  - SCREEN: black glass with a text IMITATION in bright PHOSPHOR GREEN - lines of pseudo-glyphs (seeded 3x5
    glyph blocks, varying line lengths, a header bar), faint scanlines, scrolling up one line every two
    frames, a block cursor blinking on the last line.
  - BUTTONS: a row of backlit keys on the deck, a few lit neon_cyan and rad lime.
  - LAMPS: three indicator lamps on the housing (phosphor green steady, neon_cyan, rad lime blinking).
  Animated: screen scroll + cursor + lamp blink. Nothing else moves.

SPECIMEN TUBE / BIO-POD ('&' sprite prop_biopod, FULL wall height - floor to ceiling, canvas 112 x 256,
24 frames @ 8 fps, loops)
  A floor-to-ceiling containment cylinder. A steel plinth on the floor (vents, a stencil, a rad lime hazard
  ring light), a tall glass tube of glowing NEON CYAN fluid, a steel collar and conduits running up INTO the
  ceiling (the top row of the canvas is the ceiling line).
  - SPECIMEN: a curled grown creature (no human face - series rule from the undead) hanging on a feeding
    line, flesh tinted by the cyan light, slowly drifting up and down and turning a little, tendrils trailing.
  - BUBBLES rise through the fluid (seeded columns, wrapping), the fluid glow breathes, the ring light pulses.
  - Glass: vertical sheen streaks over the fluid; bands of steel and neon_cyan ring lights at both ends.

EXIT PORTAL ('>', FULL wall height, canvas 224 x 256, 16 frames @ 12 fps, loops)
  A transit ring-gate: a heavy steel torus standing in a floor cradle, clamped to the ceiling, its rim set
  with rad lime emitter lamps; inside the ring a NEON VIOLET vortex - spiral arms turning, a white-hot core,
  sparks - spilling a violet bloom past the rim. Hazard chevrons on the cradle.
  Animated: the vortex turns (seamless loop), the rim lamps chase.


TECHNICAL NOTES
---------------
  - Kit: props.py (beside walls.py) imports kit; one function per prop returns its frames
    [(body RGBA, glow RGBA)]; write_prop() packs them into the frame grid and writes the three files.
    Screen text and the vortex are painted into the rendered frame (body + glow) by numpy after the kit
    render, like kit.stencil - generated in code, seeded.
  - Manifest <id>.prop.json: {"version":1,"id","frameWidth","frameHeight","frameCount","columns",
    "frameSeconds"}. Frame k sits at column k % columns, row k / columns.
  - render/propvisual: GeneratedPropRegistry.bootstrap() rows: spriteId "prop_terminal" -> "terminal",
    "prop_biopod" -> "bio_pod", fixed char '>' -> "portal". GeneratedPropSheets loads the registered sheets
    once per PropRenderer and answers sheetForSprite(id) / sheetForChar(c). GeneratedPropSheet holds the two
    textures (Nearest), the manifest numbers and a per-frame ColumnOpacity for the occluder record.
  - PropRenderer.render: before resolving a texture, ask the sheets; when a sheet answers, the column loop
    samples (frameX + column, frameY + row) of the sheet; aspect = frameWidth / frameHeight.
  - Constants (RenderConstants): PROP_SHEET_DIRECTORY, PROP_HEIGHT_TERMINAL 0.80, PROP_BIOPOD_HEIGHT 1.0,
    PROP_GLOW_* reuse WALL_GLOW_FADE_* (one rule). GameMath: propFrameIndex(time, phase, frameSeconds, count).
  - Deleted: PropRenderer.generateTerminalTexture / generateBioPodTexture / generatePortalTexture and their
    TextureGeneratorRegistry rows; the '>' entry of the fixed texture map.
