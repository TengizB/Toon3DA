OVERVIEW
========
The ATMOSPHERIC PROCESSING PLANT is the facility's air-and-coolant guts: the ventilation /
gas-scrubbing hub that keeps the whole UAC installation breathable. Its perimeter is armored in
hazard-striped bulkheads ('h' = wall_hazard) broken by floor-to-ceiling ventilation grille banks
('v' = wall_vent); inside sit rows of scrubber pumps ('%' = generator), racks of PRESSURIZED VOLATILE
GAS CYLINDERS ('E' = explosive barrel), and leaking coolant drums ('g' = radioactive barrel), all
standing in a floor slick with spilled coolant ('O' = oil) and thermal-burn scorch ('e' = energy
scorch) where the pipes ruptured. It fills a clear catalog gap: every existing special room is either a
CLEAN facility FUNCTION (medical, armory, cryo, server, power plant, command, containment, research,
observatory), a demonic AFTERMATH (gore nest), or GENERIC storage (salvage, supply). None is the raw
INDUSTRIAL UTILITY / HAZARDOUS-MATERIALS space — the dangerous, unglamorous machinery room. Its
identity is built entirely from WALLS + FLOOR DECALS + a few reused props, ZERO new symbols, ZERO new
sprites (RECIPE B only; the gore nest is the exact precedent). It claims the two currently-unclaimed
signature walls 'h' and 'v' as its face, so both reservations are free.

Its mechanical hook is what makes it distinct from the power plant (which is about energy/generators):
this is the game's CHAIN-REACTION HAZARD ROOM. Real explosive barrels are packed inside on purpose, so
the whole space is a volatile arena where one stray shot — yours or an enemy's — can cascade.

GAMEPLAY LOOP
=============
1. Generator places rooms and runs the seeded weighted roulette (STEP A). On a floor at depth >= 2 with a
   candidate room >= 6x6 interior and no plant yet placed, ATMOSPHERIC_PLANT competes like any blueprint.
2. If selected -> build() stamps the room: floor lighting bias, hazard/vent wall theming, then the plant
   prop-and-leak pass (deterministic minimums + a bounded scatter; see TECHNICAL NOTES).
3. Player enters through a corridor whose far wall is yellow-black HAZARD STRIPES, not plain steel, with a
   dark ventilation grille bank strobing under flickering light. It reads instantly as "industrial / do
   not linger" before any enemy is seen.
4. Inside: a bank of scrubber pumps '%' along the back wall, a cluster of pressurized gas cylinders 'E'
   flanking them, leaking coolant drums 'g' in a corner, and a floor carpeted in coolant slick 'O' and
   scorch 'e'. A walkable lane is always left between the machinery and the door approach.
5. THE HAZARD DECISION: because 'E' cylinders are LIVE explosive barrels (handled by
   ExplosiveBarrelManager at load time), the room is a powder keg. If enemies pack in near the cylinders,
   the player can pop one from the doorway for a chain-reaction wipe — or get punished if an enemy shot
   (or the player's own careless spray) detonates the cluster while the player is inside the blast lane.
6. Player fights through, loots the global pickup pass, and exits. The plant is a memorable, high-tension
   "watch your line of fire" landmark on the minimap and in memory.

PLAYER EXPERIENCE
=================
Tension and opportunity, not dread. The gore nest says "this section is lost"; the plant says "this
section is DANGEROUS — to everything in it." The hazard-stripe far wall and strobing vent grilles are an
instant, wordless "volatile" read: the player slows, clocks the barrels, and starts thinking about lines
of fire. The decision it creates is a risk/reward positioning puzzle unique among the rooms: bait the
enemy pack next to the gas cluster and detonate for a huge economy swing (ammo saved, pack wiped), or
play it safe and clear from the corridor because standing in a barrel-lined kill-box while a ranged enemy
holds your cardinal line is how you die to your own environment. It rewards the "read the room, mind your
muzzle" instinct and gives explosive weapons (grenade launcher, rockets) a signature stage.

TECHNICAL NOTES
===============
Recommendation: SIGNATURE room (RoomKind.SIGNATURE). It must PIN the hazard and vent walls so the
striped/grille face actually renders (a GENERIC room has an empty demand and could not claim 'h'/'v');
pinning is the whole point.

NO NEW SYMBOLS, NO NEW SPRITES. All seven symbols and their sprites already exist (verified against
docs/tile-symbols.txt and tileset/TilesetRegistries — 'h'->wall_hazard, 'v'->wall_vent, '%'->prop_generator,
'E'->prop_explosive_barrel, 'g'->prop_radioactive_barrel, 'O'->decal_oil, 'e'->decal_energy_scorch are all
normal flexible registry entries; only '@' is a FIXED-sprite exception). This is RECIPE B (a signature
room with a bespoke prop pass), NOT RECIPE A.

EXACT DEMAND SET (the room's symbolDemand().requiredSprites — all legacy bindings so the allocator
reserves the intended art via order-4 Step 2). This is the COMPLETE set of flexible symbols build() may
ever stamp — the RoomBlueprintBuildDemandTest asserts the stamped set EQUALS this set across 400 seeds,
so build() must reliably stamp EACH of these every seed and NOTHING else flexible:
    WALLS (the signature face — 2):
      'h' -> "wall_hazard"   (primary perimeter: yellow-black hazard-striped bulkhead — the room's face)
      'v' -> "wall_vent"     (accent: ventilation grille banks — one guaranteed per wall)
    SOLID PROPS (reused; the machinery — 3):
      '%' -> "prop_generator"          (scrubber / pump stacks along the back wall)
      'E' -> "prop_explosive_barrel"   (pressurized volatile gas cylinders — the chain-reaction hazard)
      'g' -> "prop_radioactive_barrel" (leaking coolant / chemical drums)
    FLOOR DECALS (the environmental heart — 2):
      'O' -> "decal_oil"           (spilled coolant slick — dense carpet)
      'e' -> "decal_energy_scorch" (thermal / chemical burn scorch at ruptured pipe seams)
Total = 2 walls + 3 props + 2 decals = 7 symbols. Modest and each reliably placed (see RELIABILITY below).

RELIABILITY RULE (mandatory — so the build-demand test passes over 400 seeds on a 16x16 room):
Every declared symbol is stamped by a DETERMINISTIC minimum that does NOT depend on RNG succeeding, and
the build stamps NOTHING outside the seven. Concretely:
  - 'h': after carving, set EVERY interior-facing perimeter wall tile to 'h' as the default (guaranteed
    many). The bounded fill knob only chooses which tiles STAY plain 'x' vs become 'h'; keep at least the
    four wall-run tiles adjacent to corners always 'h' so 'h' cannot vanish on any seed.
  - 'v': deterministically overwrite the MIDPOINT tile of each of the four wall runs to 'v'
    (LEVEL_GEN_ATMO_PLANT_VENT_PER_WALL, default 1). Zero RNG dependence -> 'v' present every seed.
  - '%': place a deterministic line of LEVEL_GEN_ATMO_PLANT_PUMP_COUNT (2) generators against the back
    interior wall row.
  - 'E': place a deterministic cluster of LEVEL_GEN_ATMO_PLANT_GAS_CYLINDER_COUNT (3) explosive barrels
    flanking the pump bank.
  - 'g': place LEVEL_GEN_ATMO_PLANT_DRUM_MIN (1) radioactive drum in a fixed near-corner slot.
  - 'O': the coolant slick is a GUARANTEED carpet — always stamp at least the ring immediately around the
    pump/cylinder cluster, then a bounded scatter (LEVEL_GEN_ATMO_PLANT_COOLANT_CHANCE) over the interior.
  - 'e': always stamp at least one scorch ring tile at the ruptured-seam positions (adjacent to the gas
    cluster), then a bounded scatter (LEVEL_GEN_ATMO_PLANT_SCORCH_CHANCE).
  Decals ('O'/'e') are walkable -> no collision risk. Solid props ('%'/'E'/'g') MUST use the existing
  shared prop-placement clearance guard (leave a 1-tile lane, never block the sole path or a door approach)
  — reuse it, do not invent a new one. Do NOT stamp crates, blood, or any other flexible symbol anywhere.

Clash safety: '%', 'E', 'g', 'O' are also demanded by sibling signature rooms (power plant, gore nest,
etc.) but ALL to the SAME sprite id — the allocator's Step-2 clash resolution only reroutes on
same-symbol/DIFFERENT-sprite, so there is no clash. 'h' and 'v' are currently claimed by NO signature
room (they live only in the generic-variety / harsh pools), so reserving them here is free. 'e'
(decal_energy_scorch) is likewise unclaimed by any signature room today.

'E' NOTE (important for the implementer): 'E' is a normal flexible SOLID_PROP in the registry, so it is
valid in the demand and the build-demand test sees it stamped in the grid. Separately, at LOAD time the
generator/ExplosiveBarrelManager turns each 'E' into a live explosive-barrel entity — which is the whole
mechanical point of this room. The build-demand test inspects the grid straight out of build() (before
that load-time extraction), so 'E' is present when asserted. Confirm 'E' is in the flexible SOLID_PROP
budget set before wiring (it is registered exactly like 'g'); if for any reason 'E' is treated as FIXED
in SymbolBudget, drop it from the demand and substitute 'C' (prop_crate), losing only the chain-reaction
hook.

Prop layout concept (placeAtmosphericPlantProps):
  - Identify the interior "back" wall row (the wall run opposite the door approach, or simply the top
    interior row if none is special). Place the pump bank '%' (PUMP_COUNT, default 2) along it, spaced one
    tile apart, each with the shared clearance guard.
  - Flank the pump bank with the gas cylinder cluster 'E' (GAS_CYLINDER_COUNT, default 3): e.g. one tile
    to each side of the pump line plus one in front, forming a tight volatile pocket. Guard clearance so
    the cluster never seals the room or a door.
  - Place the coolant drum(s) 'g' (DRUM_MIN, default 1) in a fixed near-corner slot away from the door.
  - Coolant slick 'O': stamp the guaranteed ring around the pump/cylinder pocket, then a bounded scatter
    over the rest of the interior at COOLANT_CHANCE — the floor should read as flooded/leaking.
  - Scorch 'e': stamp the guaranteed seam-scorch tiles adjacent to the gas cluster, then a light scatter
    at SCORCH_CHANCE near the machinery — burn marks where hot pipes vented.
  - PATHING RULE (mandatory): never fully seal the walkable lane between the machinery and the door and
    never block a door approach. Only '%'/'E'/'g' are solid; use the existing clearance check for them.

VISUAL DESIGN
=============
  - Walls: hazard wall 'h' (yellow-black caution chevrons on riveted bulkhead) dominates the perimeter,
    broken by ventilation grille 'v' banks at each wall midpoint (dark slatted vents, faint airflow shimmer).
    The room reads INDUSTRIAL and DANGEROUS at a glance — a silhouette no other room shares.
  - Floor: bias toward dim 'l' and flickering 'f' lighting via assignFloorLighting for the ATMOSPHERIC_PLANT
    tag — the plant's failing power makes hazard lights strobe. The flicker sells "malfunctioning utility"
    and makes the coolant slick catch and lose the light.
  - Machinery: the scrubber pumps '%' (boxy generator stacks) form a back-wall bank; the gas cylinders 'E'
    (red-banded pressure barrels) cluster in front as the obvious volatile focal point; coolant drums 'g'
    (green-glow radioactive barrels) leak in the corner.
  - Palette: caution yellow + black (hazard stripes) + gunmetal grille grey + toxic green (drums) + oily
    blue-black coolant sheen + charcoal scorch. Cold industrial, distinct from the power plant's electric
    blue/energy glow and the gore nest's wet reds.
  - No HUD changes. Purely environmental. Reads instantly on the raycast far wall from the doorway.

INTERACTIONS
============
  1. ExplosiveBarrelManager / chain reactions: this is the marquee interaction. The 'E' cluster becomes
     live barrels at load; the existing chain-reaction/explosive-barrel system does all the work. The room
     is the natural home for the hazard-chain-reactions feature (see hazard-chain-reactions idea) and gives
     the grenade launcher / railgun / rockets a signature "detonate the pocket" stage. No new hazard code
     needed — the room just seeds a dense barrel cluster the existing system already handles.
  2. Encounter budget / faction: pairs best with a mixed pack that WANTS to close on the player (melee or a
     ranged enemy holding a cardinal line), so the player is tempted to lure them into the cylinder pocket.
     Spawn placement stays the generator's global FIXED-symbol pass; the room only sets the volatile stage.
     Cardinal-line kiting from the doorway + one shot on the cluster is the intended power play.
  3. Hazard tiles (fire 'i' / toxic 'q'): the plant is an ideal hand-seed or runtime target for a toxic
     leak 'q' — a ruptured scrubber venting gas — reinforcing the "do not linger" read. Its walkable coolant
     floor + flickering light make it the natural home for area-denial hazards.
  4. Freed-symbol mechanic: on floors WITHOUT a plant, 'h' and 'v' fall back into the generic-variety pool
     exactly as today (no regression); when the plant IS placed, its demand reserves them so a random
     corridor never accidentally sprouts a lone hazard-stripe wall next to a placed plant.
  5. Power plant contrast: deliberately distinct from the reactor/power room — that room is about ENERGY
     (generators/cores as power source); this room is about AIR/COOLANT PROCESSING and VOLATILITY. Even
     though both can use '%', the plant's identity is the hazard-stripe/vent wall face + the live 'E' cluster,
     which the power plant does not have. Keeps the two industrial rooms readable as different beats.
  6. Fog-of-war / minimap: a rare, visually loud landmark (the "barrel room") aids player mental-mapping and
     synergises with any minimap-memory feature.

OPEN QUESTIONS
==============
  - GAS_CYLINDER_COUNT: 3 punchy vs 2 safer vs 4 lethal. The chain must reward a smart detonation without
    being an unavoidable death sentence if the player is caught inside — playtest against the actual barrel
    blast radius before shipping.
  - MIN_DEPTH 2 vs 1: is floor 1 too early to hand the player a live barrel room, or is it a great early
    "learn the hazard" beat? Leaning 2 so the entrance floor stays calm; consider 1 if playtests want it.
  - Should one guaranteed pickup (ammo/stim) sit inside the cylinder pocket to actively bait the risky
    detonation play, or stay purely atmospheric and let the global pickup pass decide? (Leaning atmospheric.)
  - Wall theming: reuse the shared themeNewRoomWallsForRoom switch with an ATMOSPHERIC_PLANT case (less code)
    vs a dedicated themeAtmosphericPlantWallsForRoom (cleaner if the deterministic vent-midpoint logic
    diverges from the other rooms' sparse accenting). Dedicated helper is safer given the guaranteed-vent rule.
  - Does the flickering floor bias hurt readability of the coolant slick? May need a few 'l' dim tiles around
    the machinery so the volatile cluster stays legible at a glance.
