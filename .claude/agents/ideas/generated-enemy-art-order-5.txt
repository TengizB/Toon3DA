OVERVIEW
--------
The four regular ABERRATIONS - Plague Hulk, Eye Tyrant, Gore Biter, Mire Wraith - rebuilt as animated,
reactive creatures. Aberrations are the containment breach: harvested planet tissue that escaped the
tanks and kept growing. Soft, fused, wet flesh is what the kit's smooth union does best, and flesh moves
beautifully (jiggle, sag, pulse), so this family should be the series' most alive. (The Corruptor boss is
order 8.)

VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
PLAGUE HULK (SOLDIER, 0.95 walls, melee tank, AREA_STRIKE, self-destruct finisher)
  A mass of tissue that grew in a containment tank until it burst it; it still wears the tank's broken
  steel collar ring around its middle, flesh bulging over and under it. Wide, no neck, two enormous
  forearms knuckled on the floor. Ichor blisters across one shoulder.
  MOTION: idle = heavy breathing, flesh rolling over the collar; move = a lurching knuckle-walk with
  jiggle; slam = both fists high (wind-up) then down with a squash; hurt = the mass ripples; priming =
  swelling, blisters brightening each turn; death = it sags and spreads; self-destruct death = it bursts.
EYE TYRANT (CHAFF, 0.55 walls, ranged straight-line sniper, DEBUFF_PLAYER, renderer-hovered)
  One huge eye that was being studied, hanging in a cradle of fleshy lids and petals; a surgical speculum
  (gunmetal) still holds the lids open; nerve tendrils trail beneath. The ichor iris points at the player.
  MOTION: idle = the pupil saccades, petals breathe, tendrils sway; alert = the iris snaps onto you;
  wind-up = the pupil narrows to a line; attack = the iris flares on the emit frame; hurt = the lids try to
  close against the speculum; death = the eye clouds over and the cradle wilts.
GORE BITER (CHAFF, 0.85 walls, melee swarmer)
  A lean runner that is mostly mouth: the head splits vertically into a toothed jaw, a low forward crouch on
  long digitigrade legs, ribs through thin flesh, a pen ear-tag. Built to be seen five at a time: simple.
  MOTION: idle = twitchy, the jaw clacking; move = a fast loping run (the pack must not sync - R3 phase
  offsets); attack = a lunging bite with the jaw splitting wide; death = it skids and folds.
MIRE WRAITH (SOLDIER, 0.80 walls, ranged acid artillery, DEBUFF_PLAYER)
  A drooping half-liquid thing from the facility sump: a translucent sagging belly of glowing acid glands,
  a hunched draped body, a long spout aimed forward, a length of drainage hose fused into its back.
  MOTION: idle = the belly sloshes and the glands pulse; wind-up = the belly draws up, glands brighten;
  attack = the spout spits (emit anchor); hurt = it splashes and re-forms; death = it slumps into a puddle
  that stays as its remains.
