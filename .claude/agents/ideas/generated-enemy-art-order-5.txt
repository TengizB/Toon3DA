OVERVIEW
--------
Replaces the four GOLEM textures - Auric Sentinel, Cinderforge Colossus, Rimeshell Lancer, Verdant
Spiresower. In the premise golems are the planet's own crust and mantle rising to wall off and crush
the infection - the player. This is the family the 2026-10-21 prototype FAILED at (the Mantle Sentinel:
polygon slabs over a glowing backing, read as bricks, rejected as the weakest sprite). So this order
first fixes HOW the kit makes stone, proves it on one golem with the owner, then builds the rest.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - Kit growth (stone done right):
      * SCULPTED ROCK: rock masses as irregular blobs (several merged ellipsoids with low k), with
        fracture lines CARVED into them (a seeded crack-path helper producing carve chains) and chipped
        edges (small carved bites along the silhouette). No straight-edged rectangles as body parts.
      * CRYSTAL material: faceted (a facet helper that quantises the normal into a few planes per
        crystal), high specular, translucent inner glow like the `sac` material.
      * element ramps: auric (metallic gold-brown with warm-white highlights - must not read as the
        UI/semantic yellow), rime (frost white with grey-blue shadows, low saturation - not the
        defence blue, not FACILITY_CYAN), verdant (olive and moss, desaturated - not the health green),
        plus a SAP glow ramp (warm cream) for the Spiresower.
  - The Cinderforge Colossus FIRST as the stone prototype (owner early read), then the other three.
  - Owner gate (G11); four registry rows; earlier sprites byte-identical; docs.

OUT
  - The renderer's golem overlays - they stay and the sprites are designed AROUND them (see VISUAL
    DESIGN): the Auric shard ring, the Cinderforge crust plates + molten seams + heat haze, the
    Rimeshell shell-open and frost tints, the Auric exposed tint.
  - The Spiresower's SPIRES: they are runtime tiles (':') with their own prop texture, not part of the
    enemy texture. Out of scope for this series.
  - Mechanics, numbers, names (G10).

VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
DESIGN BRIEF - CINDERFORGE COLOSSUS (BRUISER, 1.15 walls, melee, AREA_STRIKE; crust re-hardens)
  The prototype golem. A hunched forge of the planet's mantle: a body of molten rock, sculpted and
  slumped, heaviest at the shoulders, with a few cooled basalt ridges. The renderer lays dark crust
  plates over it as it hardens and burns seams between them - so the BODY is mostly the hot state:
  mid-orange molten surfaces, not white-hot (the crust tint must still darken it). Two thick arms
  ending in fused, club-like hands for the area slam. A broken Organization shackle on one wrist.

DESIGN BRIEF - AURIC SENTINEL (SOLDIER, 1.00 walls, ranged; shards = armour and ammo)
  A geode the planet split open: a rough dark-rock outer body cracked down the front, the inside lined
  with auric crystal, a hot core deep in the split. Upright and symmetrical, arms held out from the body
  - as if holding the ring of shards the renderer draws around it. NO shards in the sprite (R3); leave
  clear air around the head and shoulders where the ring orbits.

DESIGN BRIEF - RIMESHELL LANCER (SOLDIER, 0.85 walls, ranged lance; armoured until it fires)
  A cold body around a hot core: a closed shell of frost-rime plates (sculpted, not slabs) folded over
  its front like shut petals, a slit down the middle where the magma lance-core shows through, the lance
  emitter as a molten point in that slit. Low, braced stance. The shell-open tint the renderer adds
  must read against the frost.

DESIGN BRIEF - VERDANT SPIRESOWER (SOLDIER, 0.80 walls, melee; spires heal it)
  Stone overgrown: a squat rock body furred with moss and lichen, crystalline spire-buds pushing out of
  its back and shoulders (the spires it sows - SAP glow at their tips), roots trailing from its feet into
  the floor line. Slow and rooted; the only golem that looks alive in the plant sense.
