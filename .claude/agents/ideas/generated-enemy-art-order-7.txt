OVERVIEW
--------
The four GOLEMS - Auric Sentinel, Cinderforge Colossus, Rimeshell Lancer, Verdant Spiresower - rebuilt as
animated, reactive creatures. Golems are the planet's own crust and mantle rising to crush the infection:
the player. This is the family the 2026-10-21 prototype FAILED at (slabs over a glow, read as bricks), so
the order first fixes how the kit makes stone, proves it on one golem with the owner, then builds the rest.
Stone that MOVES - grinding, cracking, re-hardening - is also where the reactions pay off most.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - Kit growth (stone done right):
      * SCULPTED ROCK: irregular masses (several merged ellipsoids, low k), fractures CARVED in by a seeded
        crack-path helper, chipped silhouettes; no straight-edged rectangle as a body part.
      * CRYSTAL material: faceted normals (a facet helper quantising the normal into planes), high specular,
        translucent inner glow.
      * element ramps: auric (metallic gold-brown, warm-white highlights - not the semantic yellow), rime
        (frost white, grey-blue shadows, low saturation - not the defence blue, not FACILITY_CYAN), verdant
        (olive and moss - not the health green), SAP glow (warm cream).
  - The Cinderforge Colossus FIRST as the stone prototype (owner early read), then the other three.
  - Each with the full clip set, DORMANT + WOUNDED idles and their mechanic clips:
      Auric Sentinel        shard absorb (a jolt as a shard takes the hit), shard launch (arms fling out)
      Cinderforge Colossus  AREA_STRIKE slam; a cooling/hardening loop and a crust-shatter reaction
      Rimeshell Lancer      shell OPEN (the petals part on WIND_UP), lance fire, reseal
      Verdant Spiresower    sowing (a spire-bud flung down), healing pulse when a spire heals it
  - Effects: rock chips + dust (hit), ember spray (Cinderforge), frost shards (Rimeshell), gold crystal
    shards (Auric), sap motes + leaf grit (Verdant); death bursts of their own stone.
  - Owner gate, four registry rows, earlier creatures byte-identical, docs.

OUT
  - The renderer's golem overlays stay and the art is designed AROUND them, not baked in: the Auric shard
    ring, the Cinderforge crust plates/seams/heat haze, the Rimeshell open/frost tints, the Auric exposed
    tint. (Order 9 may fold them into the new layer if that reads better - not this order.)
  - The Spiresower's spires are runtime TILES with their own prop texture - not enemy art.
  - Mechanics, numbers, names (G10).

VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
CINDERFORGE COLOSSUS (BRUISER, 1.15 walls, melee, AREA_STRIKE, crust re-hardens) - the prototype
  A hunched forge of the planet's mantle: a slumped body of molten rock, heaviest at the shoulders, a few
  cooled basalt ridges; two thick arms ending in fused club hands; a broken Organization shackle on one
  wrist. The body is the HOT state (mid-orange, not white-hot) so the renderer's crust can darken it.
  MOTION: idle = a slow seething, molten surface creeping; move = ground-shaking steps; slam = both clubs
  up, down, with a squash; hurt = splashes of melt; death = it cools to black and cracks apart.
AURIC SENTINEL (SOLDIER, 1.00 walls, ranged; shards = armour and ammo)
  A geode the planet split open: a rough dark-rock outer body cracked down the front, the inside lined with
  auric crystal, a hot core deep in the split; upright, arms held out as if holding the shard ring the
  renderer draws. NO shards in the sprite; clear air around head and shoulders.
  MOTION: idle = the core pulses, the arms hold the ring; attack = arms fling forward; absorb = a jolt
  through the crystal; death = the geode closes, then shatters.
RIMESHELL LANCER (SOLDIER, 0.85 walls, ranged lance; armoured until it fires)
  A closed shell of frost-rime plates (sculpted) folded over its front like shut petals, a slit down the
  middle where the magma lance-core shows. MOTION: idle = frost breath, plates creak; WIND_UP = the petals
  part and the core blazes (held - this IS the opening the player must exploit); attack = the lance fires
  from the core anchor; reseal = petals close; death = the shell shatters into frost.
VERDANT SPIRESOWER (SOLDIER, 0.80 walls, melee; spires heal it)
  Stone overgrown: a squat rock body furred with moss and lichen, crystalline spire-buds pushing out of its
  back (SAP glow at their tips), roots trailing from its feet into the floor line.
  MOTION: idle = buds sway, roots flex; sow = it hurls a bud down; heal = the buds pulse in time with a
  spire's heal; death = moss withers, the stone crumbles.
