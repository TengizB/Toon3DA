Part of the generated-enemy-art series. Requires orders 1-6 (every EnemyType has a generated sprite).
Read generated-enemy-art.txt first.


OVERVIEW
--------
Finishes the series. Two jobs: (1) give the generated enemies back the light they lost - the kit's
glow is translucent, the engine alpha-cuts it, and the mood grade + fog dim glowing eyes and cores
like any surface, which breaks art-direction pillar V6 ("things that glow and mean something are never
tinted"); (2) delete every trace of the old image-model textures.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - EMISSIVE LAYER:
      * kit.py writes, beside each sprite, <sprite>_glow.png: the emissive pixels only (binary alpha,
        same canvas) - generated from the same functions, so it can never drift from the body.
      * GeneratedEnemySprites rows gain the glow path; EnemyRenderer draws it as an additive re-draw of
        the visible sprite columns after the body (the same per-column pattern the muzzle light already
        uses), at full colour, NOT graded and NOT fogged (V6), respecting the wall z-buffer and the
        SpriteOccluderBuffer exactly as the body does.
      * a per-depth glow fade constant in RenderConstants so a distant enemy's eyes are a point of
        light, not a flare.
  - CLEANUP:
      * delete the nine old PNGs (enemy_sheet_blight, enemy_sheet_infernal, enemy_sheet_elemental_golems,
        enemy_corruptor, enemy_vortex_eye, enemy_ghoul, enemy_crawler, enemy_revenant,
        enemy_boss_overseer) and their EnemyConstants paths, the buildTextureRegions quadrant code, the
        per-texture fields and the boss placeholder mapping. The registry becomes the ONLY source.
      * EnemySpriteSheets: the hard alpha-cut existed to clean the image models' baked halos; generated
        sprites are binary already, so the conditioning pass is removed (load time only drops). The
        cache, Nearest filter and fallback stay.
      * a load-time warning (Gdx.app.error) naming any EnemyType without a registry row.
  - Series check and docs: art-direction section 9 rewritten, section 16 completed (emissive layer),
    docs/enemy-system.txt texture notes, CLAUDE.md render/ list, the enemy-art SKILL.md, the series
    master file's roster marked done.

OUT
  - Animation (series AS3 - a separate future series), new enemies, renderer overlays other than glow.
  - Soft/translucent halos (no custom shader, V2; the glow is binary + additive).


GAMEPLAY LOOP
-------------
Unchanged.


RULES
-----
  R1  Emissive pixels draw unshaded and unfogged; everything else about the enemy is graded as before.
  R2  No enemy texture remains that was not written by bestiary.py.
  R3  No allocation in render(); the glow pass adds no per-frame objects.


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
-----------------
In a dark far corridor the first thing you see of an enemy is its eyes, its emitter or its core - the
"threats read above the world" pillar, finally true for enemies.


TECHNICAL NOTES
---------------
  - EnemyRenderer: one extra pass per visible enemy over its columns (blend GL_SRC_ALPHA, GL_ONE), batched
    with the existing muzzle-light additive pass where possible to keep blend switches at two.
  - Measure with run-game --probe cpu before and after (same session, docs/art-direction section 11
    method); the glow pass must not add more than the muzzle light's measured cost.
  - The kit change must leave every body PNG byte-identical (only new _glow files appear).


VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
No new art. The glow files are derived from the approved sprites.


BALANCE NOTES / BALANCE ADMISSION
---------------------------------
None - adds no balance-bearing content.


INTERACTIONS
------------
Mood grade (AtmosphereGrade), fog, muzzle light, wall z-buffer, SpriteOccluderBuffer, hit flash (glow
stays on during the flash), death (the glow goes with the body), boss afterimages (no glow - they are
memories, not light).


EDGE CASES
----------
A missing _glow file means no glow for that enemy, never a crash. GL context loss: same unmanaged-
texture path as the bodies. A frozen-world overlay (pause, codex): glow is drawn with the body as usual.


ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
-------------------
  A1  At 8+ tiles in cool_steel and corrupted_violet, an enemy's emissives are visibly brighter than its
      body and the same colour under every mood (screenshots of the Warden and the Harvest Tick).
  A2  `ls assets/textures/enemies/` shows only generated/; no EnemyConstants path names an old file.
  A3  Every EnemyType draws its generated sprite; the load log has no missing-row warning.
  A4  Frame cost of the glow pass measured and recorded in the IMPLEMENTATION NOTE.
  A5  Every body PNG is byte-identical to its order's approved version. Build green.
