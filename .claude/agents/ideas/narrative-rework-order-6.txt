TITLE: Narrative rework 6 — The line rewrite: cut, fix, keep
---

PURPOSE
The cleanup pass. Applies order-1's doctrine to every string that ships today: cuts the lines
that do no job, rewrites the ones that assume unrevealed facts or land as noise, and keeps
(with sharpening) the ones that work. This is the order that answers "some messages do not
make sense — remove them; some jokes stay, refined."

READ FIRST
- order-1 (D1 the three jobs, D4 the joke policy, D8 cut is the default)
- order-3 (no term before its intro), order-4 and 5 (which replace whole sections outright)
- assets/story/story-strings.properties

============================================================
THE THREE VERDICTS
============================================================
  CUT      does none of the three jobs, or its only content is a joke that references nothing.
  REWRITE  the moment is worth a line but this text is wrong: unrevealed fact, wrong voice,
           unparseable, or jargon ahead of its ladder rung.
  KEEP     does a job and passes the joke policy. Some are sharpened; the id stays.

POOL-DEPTH CONSTRAINT (do not break it while cutting)
Every REPEATABLE trigger must keep at least 4 rows per region band — BarkSystem's no-repeat
memory (STORY_BARK_RECENT_MEMORY = 10) silences a moment entirely when its pool empties. So
cutting a weak row obliges either a replacement row or the demotion of the whole moment.
Where this order cuts below four, it says what replaces it.

B. CUT (with the reason)
============================================================
  story.bark.floor.rings.5   "Air's breathable down here. You're welcome, I suppose."
        No job. She did not make the air; nothing in the fiction suggests it would be
        otherwise. A joke with no observation under it (D4).
  story.bark.log.galleries.3 "A form for reporting screaming. Box was ticked."
        KEEP the idea, CUT the duplicate: story.bark.log.galleries.1/.2 already carry the
        "the paperwork knew" beat, and the codex entry says it better. Retained as codex only.
  story.bark.kill.rings.2    "Textbook. Efficient. The yield thanks you."
        Uses "yield" three regions before its ladder rung (order-3) and says nothing else.
  story.bark.kill.galleries.4 "That was loud. Let's pretend nothing else heard it."
        Implies a noise-aggro mechanic the game does not have. A line that describes a rule
        that is not real is worse than no line.
  story.bark.lowhealth.rings.2 "You're leaking. The Cradle bill for this is mine."
        "Cradle" in Region 1 (ladder violation) and "the bill is mine" is not true of her.
  story.bark.idle.mid.2      "The resonance stops when you stop. Did you notice?"
        Describes a phenomenon the player cannot observe. Unparseable in play.
  story.bark.backtrack.deep.2 "It moves the rooms. I'm fairly sure it moves the rooms."
        The rooms do not move. Do not tell the player the world does something it does not do.
  story.exchange.deep.duty.*  (the "ahead of schedule" check-in)
        Three exchanges float free of any region beat; this is the weakest, it blocks play,
        and "ahead of schedule" contradicts an endless descent. Cutting one blocking beat is
        cheap and the layer is better for it.
  story.sample.*  (four order-1 showcase lines)
        Dev-only sample rows still shipping in the player-facing table. Move to test fixtures.

Nine cuts. Two of them (kill.rings.2, kill.galleries.4) drop those pools to four and three

E. THE EXCHANGES — three fixes
============================================================
  1. story.exchange.rings.teaching.prompt
       old  Quick thing while it's quiet. How are you doing with all this?
       new  While it's quiet - do you actually remember doing this before? Because your file
            says you have.
       Answers: "Some of it." / "None of it." / "Does it matter?"
       The first choice ever offered still decides nothing mechanically, but it is now ABOUT
       something the player was told sixty seconds earlier (order-2's gap), so answering is a
       thought rather than a mood check.
  2. story.exchange.galleries.arrival.prompt — keep, but drop "defence works" (a term nobody
       has met): "These floors were built for cutting things up, not for keeping anyone out.
       Does that land wrong for you too?"
  3. Cut story.exchange.deep.duty.* (section B). The catalog goes from 18 to 17 rows, still
       inside the 20-row budget, and the two surviving floating check-ins (warm, quiet) cover
       both leanings plus neutral.

============================================================
F. THE STANDING RULES THIS PASS ESTABLISHES
============================================================
  - No line describes a mechanic the game does not have.
  - No line uses a sense ORA does not have (smell, taste, pain).
  - No line celebrates or scores the player's death, ever, in any region.
  - No line states a number the HUD already states.
  - A joke is the DELIVERY of an observation, never the payload.
  - When a pool is cut below four rows, either replace or demote the moment. Never leave a
    moment able to go silent.
