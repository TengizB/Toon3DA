OVERVIEW
--------
The SUMP WRETCH is a new CHAFF archetype for the early floors: a slow, weak, hunched sump creature
that crawls up to you and spits point-blank. When it dies, any cause of death, it BURSTS. Acid
splashes the four tiles orthogonally next to it and poisons whatever is standing there, the player
and other enemies alike. Then the creature melts into a small live acid puddle for a few turns. It
asks the player one readable, Doom-RPG question: where is it when it dies? Kill it a step away and
it costs nothing. Let it hug you and it costs you HP. Kill it next to its pack and it fights for you.

Raw request (owner, 2026-10-22): keep the rejected first Mire Wraith sprite (now the generated
creature `sump_wretch`) and make a NEW low-level enemy from it "that will splash acid when killed".
Underlying need: the art is good but read as weak, so give it a role where looking weak is
correct. It is fodder whose danger is its corpse, not its claws. The design gives the player a
cheap early lesson in positioning and a two-sided tool against packs.


SCOPE
-----
IN
  - EnemyType.SUMP_WRETCH, with role CHAFF, family ABERRATION (trait FLESH inherited), melee, slow,
    no special move-set and no heavy.
  - The DEATH SPLASH: on death, every living host on the 4 orthogonal neighbour tiles gets POISONED
    stacks (one count for the player, a larger one for enemies). The death tile becomes a live
    toxic pool ('q') for a short, dedicated lifetime.
  - Archetype data on EnemyType (defaults 0, so every other archetype is unchanged):
    deathSplashPlayerStacks(), deathSplashEnemyStacks(), deathPuddleTurns().
  - ONE shared death-hazard method on HazardManager, called by BOTH World and SimWorld. It replaces
    the two duplicated listener lambdas, so the game and the balance simulator can never drift.
  - New FIXED spawn symbol 'o', under the CLAUDE.md STRICT RULE.
  - Pricing: the splash adds a TP term (a new GameMath formula), and the splash is declared as a
    K11 hazard row.
  - The ORA archetype line (story.bark.enemy.sump_wretch), in the properties file AND in
    BarkStrings.
  - Visual wiring: one EnemyVisualRegistry row SUMP_WRETCH -> "sump_wretch". An ACID_SPLASH burst
    (the aberration set's existing burst) plays on each splash tile. The legacy-sprite fallback is
    the Mire Wraith's legacy region.
  - Encounter pool membership (EncounterBudgetPlanner.SPAWN_POOL), so generated floors field it.

OUT (deliberately not doing now)
  - Any change to the art. The rig, clips, effects and remains ship as they are. The art's
    DEBUFF_PLAYER (acid volley) clip stays unused, because chaff has no special.
  - A ranged attack. A ranged enemy kites to RANGED_KITE_MIN_TILES = 2, so it would never be
    adjacent when it dies and the splash would never matter to the player. Melee is the design.
  - Acid detonating or igniting barrels. Acid is not fire or explosive. A barrel that KILLS a
    wretch still triggers its splash, because the splash fires on death by any cause.
  - A floor danger marker or a "danger tile" for the splash. That system means "a HEAVY is coming"
    and the TACTICAL sim policy reads it. The splash is not a heavy (see BALANCE ADMISSION).
  - Direct (non-status) splash damage, a vignette flash, or a new sound. The existing TOXIC_SPILL
    sound already plays when the puddle appears, and the family DEATH voice plays.
  - A new TeachingTopic or tutorial. ORA's first-sight archetype line is the teaching.
  - A max-spawn-depth gate. No chaff has one. The R-LADDER keeps it relevant deeper down.
  - Placing it in hand-made levels. The symbol works in them, but no level is edited.
  - Changing the Plague Hulk's death cloud or the Mire Wraith in any way. The Hulk's existing
    behaviour moves into the shared method byte-for-byte.
  - Fixing LevelLoader's spawn-character list, which is missing '}' (see RISKS). Report it as
    short-lane work.


GAMEPLAY LOOP
-------------
  1. A wretch wakes (ALERT) and shuffles toward the player. It moves only on every second enemy turn
     (moveEveryNTurns 2), the same pace as the Ghoul.
  2. If it reaches an orthogonally adjacent tile, it attacks every turn (cadence 1). This is an
     ordinary melee hit, played with its spit ATTACK clip.
  3. The player kills it, by weapon, DoT, barrel, hazard, relic corpse burst or another wretch's
     poison. In the SAME resolution as the death (EnemyManager's death path, after the drop is
     placed and where the death-hazard hook already fires):
       a. SPLASH. For each of the 4 orthogonal neighbours of the death tile, every LIVING host
          standing there gets POISONED stacks: the player SUMP_WRETCH_SPLASH_PLAYER_STACKS (3),
          an enemy SUMP_WRETCH_SPLASH_ENEMY_STACKS (4). Stacks add to any poison already there, up
          to POISON_MAX_STACKS, through the same applyPoisonStacks path a toxic tile uses.
       b. PUDDLE. The death tile becomes a toxic tile ('q') for SUMP_WRETCH_PUDDLE_LIFETIME_TURNS
          (3) turns. From then on it is an ordinary toxic tile: two-sided, stacks per turn stood,
          may creep at HAZARD_TOXIC_SPREAD_CHANCE, never overwrites fire.
       c. FEEDBACK. An ACID_SPLASH burst plays on the death tile and on each non-wall neighbour.
          The puddle's appearance plays TOXIC_SPILL through the existing hazard visual listener.
  4. Poison ticks in the ordinary status phase from then on. On the player the splash costs
     3+2+1 = 6 stack-turns, which is 12 HP at 130 max HP (about 9%). A splashed enemy that dies of
     the poison bursts in turn, if it is a wretch.
  5. Optimal play: back-step one tile while it is adjacent (it is slow, so it usually cannot follow
     at once), then kill it from 2 tiles. Better still, kill it while it stands next to its pack mate.


RULES
-----
  R1  SUMP_WRETCH is CHAFF, ABERRATION, melee (range 1, isRanged false), moveEveryNTurns 2, attack
      cadence 1, positional POSITIONAL_MULT_MELEE, move-set MOVESET_NONE, heavyWindUp null,
      minSpawnDepth 1, spawnChar 'o'.
  R2  The splash fires on EVERY death of a type whose deathSplashPlayerStacks() or
      deathSplashEnemyStacks() is > 0, whatever the cause. Today that is only SUMP_WRETCH.
  R3  Splash footprint: the 4 orthogonally adjacent tiles only. Never diagonals, never range 2, and
      not the death tile itself (nothing living stands there at that moment).
  R4  The splash applies status only. It deals no direct damage, so one death can NEVER cause
      another death inside the same resolution. Chains run only through later poison ticks, which
      bounds them and keeps them readable.
  R5  On the player, the splash is NOT a hit. Armour does not shield it and does not pay the
      ARMOUR_STATUS_COST, GUARD does not reduce it, and the relic first-hit shield is not spent.
      This is the same rule as standing on a toxic tile: position is the counter-play.
  R6  Poison immunity and resistance (StatusResistance) apply as usual. An immune host is
      unaffected.
  R7  Puddle: the death tile is ignited as TOXIC with lifetime deathPuddleTurns() through a new
      HazardManager.igniteToxic(column, row, lifetimeTurns) overload. Eligibility is the existing
      rule (Level.isHazardSpreadable). It is skipped if a pickup or keycard drop landed on the tile
      or if the tile is already FIRE. A tile that is already TOXIC has its lifetime set to
      deathPuddleTurns() (the existing ignite() behaviour). The
      splash in R3 still happens whether or not the puddle can be placed.
  R8  Acid never interacts with explosive barrels (no detonation, no ignition).
  R9  Plague Hulk behaviour is unchanged: the ordinary cloud at HAZARD_PLAGUE_HULK_DEATH_CLOUD_RADIUS,
      and the massive cloud when selfDestructMassive. The Cinderforge last-fire tile is unchanged.
      Both move unchanged into the shared method.
  R10 The splash is resolved in the simulation (headless). The ACID_SPLASH burst is cosmetic and is
      fired through a listener that is null in SimWorld.


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
-----------------
Floor 1 or 2: a hunched, glowing, dripping thing slumps toward you, slowly. ORA's first-sight line:
"It bursts when it dies. Not next to you - next to its friends." A player who kills it at arm's
length sees the splash, gets POISONED (3) on the status row, and loses about 9% HP over three turns.
That is a cheap, legible lesson with the cause on screen: a green splash on your tile and the
poison icon. A player who listens learns one step back, then shoot.

Then comes the decision the enemy exists for. A Gore Biter pair arrives with a wretch between them.
Do you kill the wretch now, while it is next to the biters (poisoning both, possibly melting a
wounded second wretch), or kill the biters first? The wretch is a grenade the enemy carries for you.

Thumb-only: nothing new to press. Forward, Back, Strafe and Fire already express "step away, then
shoot".

  (R-TELEGRAPH). It is a fraction of max HP, so it does not go stale with depth.

  and chains. Deeper floors raise enemy HP, so the share drops (about 11% of a depth-10 Gore
  Biter). This is deliberate: it is an early-floor tool that fades.

INTERACTIONS
------------
  - EnemyManager death path: the existing enemyDeathHazardListener hook is the only entry point.
    Drops are placed first, then WILDFIRE, then the hook. So a carried pickup on the death tile
    blocks the puddle (R7), and a burning wretch both passes its burn (WILDFIRE) and splashes.
  - Plague Hulk toxic cloud: when a wretch dies on a tile that is already toxic, the existing
    ignite() SETS that tile's lifetime to the new value (3), whatever was left. This is accepted
    as-is: it can shorten a Hulk cloud tile from up to 6 remaining turns to 3, which is rare and
    harmless. The builder must not add special-casing.
  - Fire: a wretch dying on a fire tile gets no puddle (fire is never overwritten) but still splashes.
  - Barrels: no interaction from acid (R8). A barrel blast that kills a wretch triggers its splash.
  - Relics: the CORPSE_BLAST burst and the splash are independent. A kill made by a corpse burst
    still splashes (the splash is not a corpse burst, so the no-chain rule of R8 relics is
    untouched). The first-hit shield and the damage-taken multiplier are not involved (R5).
  - Status system: POISONED stacks, decay and cap are unchanged. Enemies with poison resistance or
    immunity resist it.
  - Encounter planner: one more chaff in the pool shifts every generated floor's composition
    slightly. That is why both gates run at CP5.
  - Floor twists: HUNTED picks its hunter by its own rules. If a wretch is ever the hunter, it
    splashes on death as normal. SALVAGE/BLACKOUT are unaffected.
  - Narrative: the first-sight archetype line is REACTIVE (held through a combat spike), as are all
    non-boss archetype lines. A player may be splashed before hearing it. That is acceptable at 9%,
    and the cause is on screen.
  - Sim: SimWorld calls the same HazardManager method, so the bands measure the real splash. The
    TACTICAL policy is NOT taught to back off. Its splash losses are what the 0.5 adjacent-share
    assumption prices.


EDGE CASES
----------
  - The wretch is killed by the player's own shot while adjacent: the player is splashed (that is
    the lesson).
  - The player dies this turn: the splash still resolves, and death handling is unchanged.
  - Two wretches adjacent, one dies: the other gets 4 stacks. If it was already wounded, it dies on
    a later poison tick and splashes in turn. No same-resolution recursion (R4).
  - A wretch dies from a poison tick during the status phase: it splashes there. Hosts the loop has
    not reached yet may tick their new stacks this phase. No ConcurrentModification is allowed,
    because the splash never removes from the enemy list.
  - The death tile holds a pickup, a key, or the exit: no puddle; the splash still happens.
  - A neighbour is a wall or closed door: nothing to hit; no burst is drawn on a wall tile.
  - An open door tile neighbour with a host on it is splashed (it is orthogonally adjacent).
  - Floor transition the turn it dies: the HazardManager is rebuilt per floor, so the puddle dies
    with the floor. Player poison carries over exactly as any poison does today.
  - Hard-pause overlay (level-up, relic offer, pause) opens the same turn: the sim resolved
    already; the cosmetic burst plays when rendering resumes, or has already finished. Nothing is
    queued.
  - App paused: no state outside the existing hazard and status state.
  - Hand-made level with 'o': spawns a wretch (LevelLoader + EnemyManager mapping). It is not
    depth-gated.
  - Generated vs hand-made: same behaviour.
  - Boss floors: no change; no boss summons wretches.

ASSUMPTIONS
-----------
  AS1  Name "Sump Wretch" (display) / SUMP_WRETCH (enum) / sump_wretch (creature id). It is cheap to
       rename later: one displayName and one string.
  AS2  CHAFF, not a weak SOLDIER. The danger is its corpse, so its body must be cheap: 2 hits.
  AS3  Melee, not ranged. A ranged chaff kites at 2 tiles and would never splash the player, which
       would make the core mechanic moot for the player.
  AS4  Slow (moves every 2 turns). This is what makes "step back, then shoot" a real, learnable
       answer.
  AS5  The splash is POISON (reusing POISONED and the toxic hazard path), not a new ACID status or a
       direct hit. It reuses K10, keeps the two-sided hazard model, and needs no new HUD icon.
  AS6  Footprint is the orthogonal plus (4 tiles), matching cardinal-only game geometry. No
       diagonals.
  AS7  Two-sided by design (hazards in this game always are). Enemies take MORE stacks than the
       player (4 vs 3), mirroring fire's enemy multiplier, so using it against a pack is worth it.
  AS8  Not shielded by armour, guard or the relic first-hit shield. It is a hazard, not a hit; the
       counter-play is position.
  AS9  The puddle is the death tile only, for 3 turns. The cross-shaped toxic cloud stays the Plague
       Hulk's signature, and the two enemies must not read the same.
  AS10 No floor danger marker. The heavy-danger system means "a big hit is coming", and the
       splash's 9% does not earn it.
  AS11 Acid does not touch barrels.
  AS12 No max spawn depth (no chaff has one). It is "low-level" by role and by the fading enemy-side
       poison share, not by a gate.
  AS13 Credits 5, the chaff standard.
  AS14 TP prices the splash at DEATH_SPLASH_ADJACENT_SHARE 0.5: half of wretch deaths are assumed
       to happen next to the player. The sim validates this.
  AS15 No new sound. TOXIC_SPILL plus the family DEATH voice carry the moment.
  AS16 The unused DEBUFF_PLAYER clip in the art stays unused.
  AS17 The tacticalVerb is a code string (not localised), like every other archetype's. The player
       hears the ORA line, not the verb.
  AS18 It joins chaff PAIRS / BROOD by role. No new group template.
