OVERVIEW
--------
Defines the game's interface vocabulary once, as render/UiKit: a chamfered panel,
corner brackets, a plate with four states, a segmented meter, a divider and shadowed
text, all coloured from util/ArtDirection's two inks. It then applies the kit to
everything on screen DURING PLAY: the HUD panels, the touch buttons, the mini-map, the
boss bar, event text and damage numbers. These are the UI pixels in every gameplay
screenshot.

What was SAID: "HUD and menus ... consistent". What is NEEDED: today the HUD is steel
with LED green, the touch buttons phosphor green, the mini-map grey, and the boss bar,
event text and damage numbers each their own. The player's suit interface should
look like one designed object, in SUIT ink (amber on gunmetal: ORA's colour, because
she is the suit).

RULES
-----
  R1  PANEL. UiKit.panel(shapes, x, y, width, height, fillR/G/B, fillAlpha,
      borderR/G/B): a rectangle with all four corners chamfered at 45 degrees by
      UI_CHAMFER, filled, with a UI_BORDER_THICKNESS outline in the border colour.
      Drawn with ShapeRenderer filled triangles/rects and lines. No allocation.
  R2  BRACKETS. UiKit.brackets(shapes, x, y, width, height, inkR/G/B, alpha): four
      L-shaped corner marks, each arm UI_BRACKET_LENGTH long and UI_BRACKET_THICKNESS
      thick, inset UI_BRACKET_INSET from the panel edge. This is the series'
      signature motif.
  R3  PLATE (any tappable). UiKit.plate(shapes, x, y, width, height, PlateState,
      ink): chamfered with UI_PLATE_CHAMFER. States:
        IDLE      fill UI_PANEL       alpha (caller's), border UI_LINE
        PRESSED   fill UI_PANEL_RAISED alpha (caller's pressed alpha), border = ink, full alpha
        SELECTED  fill UI_PANEL_RAISED, border = ink, plus brackets in ink
        DISABLED  fill UI_PANEL at 0.5x alpha, border UI_LINE at 0.5x alpha
      PlateState is an enum carrying its fill/border choice as per-constant data (no
      switch).
  R4  METER. UiKit.meter(shapes, x, y, width, height, fraction, segments, onR/G/B,
      trackR/G/B): `segments` cells separated by UI_METER_GAP. Lit cells use on-colour;
      the partial last cell is lit proportionally from its left edge.
  R5  DIVIDER. UiKit.divider(shapes, x, y, width, inkR/G/B, alpha): a 1-unit line with
      a 6-unit ink tick at its left end.
  R6  SHADOWED TEXT. UiKit.text(batch, font, text, x, y, r, g, b, alpha): draws text
      offset by UI_TEXT_SHADOW_OFFSET (down-right, in world units, scaled by the
      font's current scale) in UI_INK_BLACK at UI_TEXT_SHADOW_ALPHA 0.85, then the
      text. A GlyphLayout-free overload uses font.draw directly. No allocation (the
      caller passes a pre-built CharSequence or StringBuilder).
  R7  GEOMETRY CONSTANTS (ArtDirection): UI_CHAMFER 8, UI_PLATE_CHAMFER 12,
      UI_BORDER_THICKNESS 2, UI_BRACKET_LENGTH 14, UI_BRACKET_THICKNESS 3,
      UI_BRACKET_INSET 4, UI_METER_GAP 2, UI_TEXT_SHADOW_OFFSET 1.5,
      UI_TEXT_SHADOW_ALPHA 0.85, UI_PANEL_ALPHA_HUD 0.82 (= today's HUD_PANEL_ALPHA,
      which then references it), UI_SCRIM_ALPHA 0.75 (used from order 5).
  R8  INK RULE. Everything in this order is SUIT ink: accents SUIT_AMBER, secondary
      accents SUIT_AMBER_DIM, neutrals UI_*. FACILITY_CYAN appears only where the
      facility is speaking: the mini-map EXIT marker (the facility's way out) and the
      NAV OFFLINE plate's text on BLACKOUT floors. Semantic colours only where they
      carry their meaning (V5).
  R9  HUD (hud/HudRenderer):
      - Left and right panels: UiKit.panel in UI_PANEL at UI_PANEL_ALPHA_HUD, border
        UI_LINE, brackets SUIT_AMBER_DIM. The bevel light/dark lines are removed (the
        chamfer + brackets replace them).
      - Labels (HP, AR, ...) UI_TEXT_SECONDARY at TYPE_CAPTION. Values UI_TEXT_PRIMARY.
      - HP meter: keep the existing green->red health ramp and tick count, with the
        ramp's endpoints from ArtDirection.HEALTH_GREEN -> CombatPalette.DAMAGE;
        track UI_INK_BLACK.
      - AR meter: ARMOUR_BLUE, track UI_INK_BLACK.
      - Ammo digits: HEAVY face (order 3), UI_TEXT_PRIMARY; low ammo (existing low
        threshold) SUIT_AMBER; empty CombatPalette.DAMAGE. Clip pips SUIT_AMBER, track
        UI_INK_BLACK.
      - Melee meter: SUIT_AMBER (no longer green: green is reserved for health/buff).
      - XP bar: CREDITS_GOLD (earned things are gold), track UI_INK_BLACK.
      - Weapon slot strip: active slot = plate SELECTED in SUIT_AMBER; filled = IDLE;
        empty = DISABLED.
      - LED_GREEN accents become SUIT_AMBER, except where green means "healthy"
        (then HEALTH_GREEN).
      - Status icons: unchanged colours (semantic, already sourced from CombatPalette).
      - Credits readout: CREDITS_GOLD. BLACKOUT NAV OFFLINE plate: UiKit.panel +
        FACILITY_CYAN_DIM text (R8).
      - Face box frame: UiKit.panel with brackets. The portrait is untouched.
  R10 TOUCH BUTTONS (input/touch/TouchControllerRenderer): each button's outline
      becomes a chamfered square of the same 96x96 (or its existing size) with
      corner chamfer = TOUCH_BUTTON_CORNER_RADIUS (16). The constant is renamed
      TOUCH_BUTTON_CHAMFER with the same value. Hit testing is unchanged and does not
      read this constant (verify).
        idle     fill UI_PANEL alpha TOUCH_FILL_ALPHA_IDLE, rim UI_LINE alpha
                 TOUCH_RIM_ALPHA, icon UI_TEXT_PRIMARY alpha TOUCH_ICON_ALPHA_IDLE
        pressed  fill UI_PANEL_RAISED alpha TOUCH_FILL_ALPHA_PRESSED, rim SUIT_AMBER
                 alpha 1, icon SUIT_AMBER alpha TOUCH_ICON_ALPHA_PRESSED
        locked   icon alpha TOUCH_ICON_ALPHA_LOCKED, rim UI_LINE (as today)
      Contextual buttons (INSPECT, USE) and PAUSE use the same style. USE keeps its
      prominence with permanent SUIT_AMBER brackets. The switch-hint outline keeps its
      existing amber pulse and constants. The phosphor-green constants are deleted.
      Icon shapes are unchanged.
  R11 MINI-MAP (render/LevelRenderer): frame UiKit.panel (UI_INK_BLACK fill at 0.70,
      border UI_LINE) + brackets SUIT_AMBER_DIM; walls UI_TEXT_SECONDARY at 0.85;
      grid lines UI_LINE at 0.35; props UI_TEXT_DISABLED; barrel dot keeps its pulse
      in CombatPalette.DAMAGE; enemies CombatPalette.DAMAGE (pulse unchanged); player
      marker + facing wedge SUIT_AMBER; exit FACILITY_CYAN (pulse unchanged); shop
      CREDITS_GOLD; doors keep their keycard colours; weapon dots SUIT_AMBER. Fog of war
      and every geometry constant unchanged.
  R12 BOSS BAR (render/BossHudRenderer): panel + brackets; name HEAVY UI_TEXT_PRIMARY at
      TYPE_LABEL; epithet UI_TEXT_SECONDARY at TYPE_CAPTION; health meter
      CombatPalette.DAMAGE with BOSS_HEALTH_SEGMENTS 20; phase threshold ticks
      SUIT_AMBER; the existing heal flash and state labels keep their semantic
      colours.
  R13 EVENT TEXT (render/EventTextRenderer + EventTextSystem colour table): every line
      drawn through UiKit.text (shadowed). Colour mapping by code: WHITE ->
      UI_TEXT_PRIMARY, GREY -> UI_TEXT_SECONDARY, GOLD -> SUIT_AMBER, AMBER ->
      SUIT_AMBER, GREEN -> HEALTH_GREEN, BLUE -> CombatPalette DEFENSE, ARMOUR ->
      ARMOUR_BLUE, RED -> CombatPalette.DAMAGE. The byte codes and every call site are
      unchanged; only the colour table they index changes.
  R14 DAMAGE NUMBERS (render/ImpactEffectRenderer): HEAVY face, shadowed (R6), colours
      semantic (damage dealt UI_TEXT_PRIMARY, crit/heavy SUIT_AMBER, blocked
      CombatPalette DEFENSE, if those categories exist; existing categories map 1:1,
      no new category).
  R15 TYPE SCALE ADOPTION. On these screens, every text draw uses one of
      ArtDirection.TYPE_* (order 3 R8) times the screen's existing global factor. No
      raw literal scales remain in these files.


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
-----------------
The bottom of the screen now reads as one instrument: dark gunmetal, amber brackets,
bone-white numbers. Green and red only ever mean health and harm. The thumb buttons
match the HUD instead of glowing a different green. A player who has never read
anything learns the language early: amber is mine, cyan is the facility's.

VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
The signature: a dark gunmetal slab, 45-degree cut corners, four small amber L-brackets.
Bracket arms are 14 units long, short enough to read as a frame and not a box. The
amber appears in exactly three roles: brackets, the active/pressed state, and the
player's resource numbers (ammo, the player marker). Everything else is neutral, so
amber never becomes wallpaper. Contrast target: UI_TEXT_PRIMARY on UI_PANEL is roughly
12:1, and UI_TEXT_SECONDARY on UI_PANEL roughly 5:1. Both clear 4.5:1 (computed from
the palette in docs/art-direction.txt).

ASSUMPTIONS
-----------
  AS1  The suit is the player's interface, and ORA (whose accent is already amber
       0.98/0.78/0.30) is the suit. So the suit ink is amber. That ties the fiction to
       the chrome for free.
  AS2  Chamfered corners read "industrial facility" better than the current rounded
       touch buttons. A 16-unit chamfer on a 96-unit button is as thumb-friendly as a
       16-unit radius.
  AS3  Ammo, melee charge and the player marker are "the player's own resource" and
       use amber. HP and armour keep their universal semantic colours.
  AS4  XP and credits share gold because both are earned rewards.
  AS5  The HP ramp keeps its existing tick count. Segmenting it differently would
       change how players read their health at a glance, which is not in scope.
  AS6  The interim state where the story bark panel keeps its old look until order 6
       is acceptable for one order.


DECISIONS
---------
  D1  Owner instruction: decide by default and ask nothing.  ->  All choices are the
      designer's.  (2026-10-19)
  D2  Two inks, suit vs facility  ->  Amber = the player's suit (HUD, controls, the
      player's own screens). Cyan = what the facility shows you. Diegetic, and easy to
      learn without being told.  (2026-10-19)
  D3  Scanlines / CRT / chromatic aberration (scanline-crt-hud-marine-vitals.txt)  ->
      Not adopted. On phone screens they read as compression artefacts and they cost
      legibility. The identity comes from shape (chamfer + brackets) and ink.
      (2026-10-19)
  D4  Touch hit areas  ->  Frozen. A visual pass must never move a control under a
      player's thumb.  (2026-10-19)
