Order 3 of 8 in the balance-overhaul series (index: balance-overhaul.txt).
Depends on order 1 (logs, dials) and order 2 (ExpectedPlayer, ladder, fractional

OVERVIEW
--------
The owner: "Sometimes there are too many resources and other times too little",
"levels are big but empty", "rarely there are groups, I wish there were groups",
"I die on the cave levels because they have no medkits", "usually I arrive with
full HP", "I feel no difference between elite and normal". The cause is
structural: three generators place loot with three different codes built from
independent per-room chances (CavernGenerator only places medkits inside a
MEDICAL_BAY chamber that may not exist; LevelGenerator hard-codes a 0.70 stim
chance), enemy drops are independent rolls, the encounter budget buys the SAME
number of bodies at every depth by construction, and early maps are the full
80x45 grid.

This order replaces all of it with two planners every generator shares:
  - an ENCOUNTER planner that spends a growing body budget on GROUP TEMPLATES
    placed as one unit in one room, with a first fight close to the start;
  - a SUPPLY planner that derives ammo, heals, armour, credits and weapon drops
    from the floor's ACTUAL roster (its demand) and the node type, places them
    deterministically, guarantees a heal floor on every floor, and treats enemy
    drops as planned carriers instead of dice.
Maps shrink in the early regions so the same fights happen in less walking, and
COMBAT / ELITE / CACHE floors get contents that feel and read different.

GAMEPLAY LOOP
-------------
  1. Route map: the player taps an ELITE node; its card reads "ELITE — HEAVY
     RESISTANCE / REWARD: WEAPON +2 LV, RARE OR BETTER". A CACHE reads "LIGHT
     RESISTANCE / SUPPLIES". A COMBAT node reads "RESISTANCE / STANDARD".
  2. Floor 1 COMBAT: within ~12 steps the player meets a group of three Gore
     Biters in the first room off the start; the map is compact (~450 walkable
     tiles); 12-16 enemies in 4-5 groups; one medkit is guaranteed in the first
     half of the map, and enough ammo for the roster sits along the way.
  3. The player leaves at roughly 55-80% HP — not full — having spent most of the
     floor's ammo; the next floor's supply is sized to ITS roster, not to luck.
  4. A cave floor at depth 12 has the same heal floor and the same supply-to-
     demand ratio as a rooms floor at depth 12.


RULES
-----
Supply planner
  S1  Every generator builds a floor through the SAME pipeline: layout -> slot
      list (SupplySlotProvider) -> encounter plan -> supply plan (SupplyPlanner)
      -> placement. No generator places a pickup by its own rule. Hand-made
      levels (.txt) are unaffected.
  S2  AMMO: demandUnits(type) derives from the planned roster's total eHP at
      depth d divided by the expected player's per-unit damage (ExpectedPlayer,
      order 2). Planned ammo = demand * spec.ammoRatio - expected carrier drops.
      Split: SUPPLY_CARRIED_SHARE (0.70) across the ammo types of weapons the
      player carries at floor build, SUPPLY_OFF_TYPE_SHARE (0.30) across the
      rest (so a found weapon is usable). Rounded to whole boxes.
  S3  HEALS: expectedIncoming = GameMath.floorExpectedIncomingDamage(roster, d,
      expected player) (the existing heal-economy model, re-based on order 2).
      Planned heal value = expectedIncoming * (1 - spec.drainTarget), expressed
      in fractions of expected max HP and converted to 'H' (0.45) and '+' (0.18)
      pickups; armour ('a'/'A') = spec.armourShare of that value.
  S4  HEAL FLOOR: every floor that is not BOSS, REST or REGION_GATE carries heal
      value >= SUPPLY_HEAL_FLOOR_FRACTION (0.45 of expected max HP — at least one
      'H' or its equivalent), all of it REACHABLE WITHOUT any keycard, and at
      least half of it within the first half of the floor by walk distance from
      the start.
  S5  TRACKING: the placed supply of every category is within +/- SUPPLY_TRACK_
      TOLERANCE (0.10) of plan (only rounding to whole pickups may move it).
      There are no independent per-room or per-kill chance rolls for ammo, heals
      or armour anywhere.
  S6  CARRIERS: the planner assigns a share of the ammo/heal plan
      (SUPPLY_CARRIER_SHARE, 0.25) to specific spawned enemies as their drop
      (deterministic from the floor seed). A carrier drops its item on death,
      exactly once. Unkilled carriers keep their loot — skipping fights costs
      supply by design.
  S7  SPREAD: no single room/chamber holds more than SUPPLY_MAX_ROOM_SHARE (0.35)
      of any category; pickups prefer slots on the way to the exit and in or
      next to group rooms (rewarding the fight), with the S4 half-early rule.
  S8  CREDITS: credit chips per floor = spec.creditScale * the depth credit
      curve, placed by the planner (replacing the 3-7 chip roll).
  S9  WEAPONS: weapon drops per floor = spec.weaponDrops at level offsets and a
      tier floor from the spec. CADENCE: across any two consecutive non-boss
      floors the player is offered at least one weapon at level >= d (replaces
      GUARANTEED_UPGRADE_PER_REGION; tracked in RunStats).
  S10 NODE SPECS (NodeSupplySpec, one register() row per RouteNodeType; defaults,
      numbers live in BalanceConfig):
        type    threat  ammoRatio drainTarget armourShare credits weapons
        COMBAT  1.00    0.85      0.20        0.20        1.0     1 @ -1..0
        ELITE   1.60    0.85(+vault) 0.30     0.30 (+'A') 1.6     1 @ +1..+2,
                                                                  tier >= region
                                                                  min + 1, gated
                                                                  behind the
                                                                  anchor group
        CACHE   0.35    1.50      -0.25 (net  0.40        0.8     1 @ 0, 50%
                                  gain)
        SHOP    0.35    1.00      0.00        0.20        1.0     0 (2 machines)
        REST / EVENT / MYSTERY / BOSS / REGION_GATE: today's contents expressed
        as a spec (order 7 retunes REST/EVENT/MYSTERY; BOSS keeps R-BOSS-AMMO's
        arena budget as its ammo plan; REGION_GATE threat 0).
      REGION_TP_BUDGET_MULTIPLIER (the region danger dial) still multiplies
      threat.

Encounter planner
  E1  BODIES: floor body target grows with depth, independent of per-enemy cost:
      COMBAT d1 12-16 bodies rising linearly to 22-28 at d25 (BODY_TARGET_MIN/
      MAX at d1 and d25), times spec.threat and the region dial. The TP budget
      becomes a CAP on total threat, the body target the thing being filled.
  E2  GROUPS: the roster is built from EncounterGroupTemplate rows (register()
      rows, level package). Default templates:
        PACK        3-5 chaff, one type
        FIRETEAM    2 soldiers + 2 chaff
        ESCORT      1 bruiser + 2-3 chaff
        BATTERY     1-2 ranged soldiers + 2 melee chaff in front of them
        WARBAND     1 mini-elite + 2 soldiers        (ELITE nodes; depth >= 3)
        HUNTER      1 bruiser or flanker alone       (max 20% of groups)
      Each template declares role slots, min depth and node eligibility; the
      archetype for each slot is drawn from the depth/region roster pool.
  E3  A group spawns as ONE unit in ONE room (or cavern chamber / corridor
      pocket), clustered within LEVEL_GEN_PACK_CLUSTER_RADIUS. Per-group threat
      cap GROUP_TP_FRACTION_CAP (0.35 of the floor cap); per-room cap follows the
      group (a room holds at most one group unless it is LARGE).
  E4  SHAPE: on COMBAT and ELITE floors >= 75% of enemies are in groups of >= 2,
      at least 2 groups have >= 3 members, and at most 3 enemies are alone.
  E5  FIRST CONTACT: on COMBAT and ELITE floors a group of >= 2 is placed in a
      room 1-3 room-hops from the start (caverns / corridors: <= 18 walk tiles),
      never in the start room. On the run's FIRST floor: <= 12 walk tiles.
  E6  FOOTPRINT: the combat generators accept LevelGenConfig.targetWalkableTiles
      and build to it (+/- 15%): region A 350-550, B 450-650, C/D/E 500-750.
      ROOMS_MST reduces room count, CAVERN its fill area, LINEAR_CORRIDOR its
      spine length. ELITE floors use the lower half of their region's range.
  E7  DENSITY: enemies per 100 walkable tiles — COMBAT [2.2, 4.0], ELITE
      [3.0, 5.0], CACHE / SHOP [0.6, 1.5].

Communication
  C1  Route node card shows a one-line threat word and a one-line reward
      promise per node type (strings are UI chrome in the route overlay's
      existing string table, not story lines): COMBAT "RESISTANCE" / "STANDARD
      SUPPLY"; ELITE "HEAVY RESISTANCE" / "WEAPON +2 LV, RARE+"; CACHE "LIGHT
      RESISTANCE" / "SUPPLIES"; SHOP "LIGHT RESISTANCE" / "FABRICATORS".
  C2  The ELITE reward weapon sits behind the anchor group (in or past its room)
      and shows its rarity glow on the ground (existing ground sprite tint).


PLAYER EXPERIENCE
-----------------
Floors are shorter and busier; the first fight comes quickly; fights come in
groups that ask for positioning. Supply stops being a lottery: a floor gives
what its fights cost, minus a bite the player must absorb with skill, and a
player who skips fights also skips the carriers' loot. Elite is visibly the
risk-for-a-weapon floor, cache the breather. Nothing new to teach: the route
card says it.

EDGE CASES
----------
  - Too few slots (tiny cave chamber set): planner merges pickups into larger
    ones (two '+' -> one 'H') before dropping any; if still short, it places in
    any reachable floor tile; heal floor S4 is never dropped.
  - Carrier killed by a hazard or another enemy: still drops (on death, any
    cause). Carrier summoned? Summons are never carriers.
  - Carrier inside a wall-adjacent tile when it dies: drop uses the existing
    ground-item placement fallback.
  - A player carrying no ranged weapon: the 70% carried share goes to the
    types of the expected player's weapons.
  - Keycard never found: nothing essential lies behind it (S4).
  - Floor with fewer rooms than groups: groups merge into LARGE rooms or the
    body target is reduced to fit; E4 shape rules are checked after.
  - Generated vs hand-made levels: hand levels keep their authored pickups.

ASSUMPTIONS
-----------
  AS1  Supply depends on the floor's demand and node, never on the player's
       current state (fairness = consistency; the owner wants earned wins).
  AS2  Leaving a normal floor at ~55-80% HP is the right target ("usually full
       HP" is the complaint); REST and CACHE are where HP comes back.
  AS3  Enemy drops as planned carriers (deterministic) are acceptable; players
       will not notice the difference except that drops stop being streaky.
  AS4  12-16 enemies on floor 1 in a ~450-tile map is "not boring"; the body
       target grows to 22-28 by depth 25. Tunable with ENEMY_COUNT (order 1).
  AS5  Smaller early maps are wanted (owner: "large but empty"); exploration
       stays rewarding because supply is placed near fights and on the exit path.
  AS6  Group templates use existing archetypes and AI; mixed-type groups are
       fine for the existing pack-coherence assumption (chaff still never spawns
       alone: every template with chaff has >= 2).
  AS7  The ELITE reward (weapon +1..+2 LV, rare or better for the region) is the
       differentiator the owner is missing; the route card must promise it.
  AS8  REST/EVENT/MYSTERY keep today's contents until order 7.
  AS9  No new symbols: every placement uses existing pickup and spawn symbols.
