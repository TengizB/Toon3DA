OVERVIEW
--------
The old enemies fail twice. Their textures were made by image models, so no two share a light, a
palette, an outline or a resolution and each looks pasted in from another game. And they are STILL
IMAGES: no idle, no walk, no attack, no flinch, no death - the renderer fakes life by scaling a picture
up during a lunge and flashing it white when hit.

Now that the art is generated in code we can fix both. This series REBUILDS THE ENEMY VISUAL LAYER: every
enemy is re-designed from the story, sculpted by the kit, rigged and animated with a full clip set,
and driven by a new runtime that reacts to every event the simulation produces - waking, moving,
telegraphing, attacking, being hit, resisting, staggering, burning, dying - with effects generated
from the creature's own materials. The goal is not "make the existing renderer use new textures"; the
owner explicitly allows rewriting the enemy visual code from scratch (D8).

On 2026-10-21 the method was prototyped: the WARDEN (machine) and the HARVEST TICK (insect) were
approved as still images and committed; a stone golem was rejected.

SERIES RULES (every order, by reference)
----------------------------------------
  G1  ORIGINALITY (guide N2). The old enemy textures are not reference material - never opened for
      inspiration, traced, recoloured or re-created. Designs come from story/00-premise.md, the type's
      role(), tacticalVerb(), abilities, and ORA's line (story.bark.enemy.<type>).
  G2  GENERATED IN CODE (guide N1). Every frame, glow layer, effect particle and manifest is written
      by the enemy-art skill's scripts. Deterministic; no hand edits.
  G3  ONE STYLE LOCK (guide N3, section 3). Never overridden per creature.
  G4  CANVAS (guide section 6). 160x240 regular, 240x360 boss; heightMultiplier() is never changed to
      fix art.
  G5  SEMANTIC COLOURS SACRED; family inks per guide 4.4.
  G6  SILHOUETTE FIRST; size reads role; the attack is on the body (guide 5.1).
  G7  ONE STORY DETAIL per creature, on the body (guide N6).
  G8  MOTION IS MANDATORY. A creature ships only with its full standard clip set (guide 7.3), its
      ability clips, its reactions (guide 8) and its family effects (guide 9). A still image is not
      a finished enemy.
  G9  REACTIONS ARE COSMETIC. No clip, reaction or effect changes a rule, a number, a turn, the
      action lock or input. The simulation stays headless; it talks to visuals only through a
      listener seam (order 2), never by importing a renderer.
  G10 NAMES, STATS, ABILITIES, SOUNDS, ORA LINES, SPAWN SYMBOLS DO NOT CHANGE.
  G11 OWNER APPROVAL GATE (guide N7, section 11). Contact sheet + clip GIFs + in-engine captures, then
      STOP until the owner answers; the answer goes into that order's DECISIONS.
  G12 NO TESTS (Testing Policy). Not balance-bearing; verified by build + run-game captures.
  G13 PERFORMANCE IS PART OF DONE. No allocation in render(); atlases load per floor and are disposed;
      each runtime order measures frame time with run-game --probe against the order before it.

Why this order: orders 1-3 build and prove the whole pipeline on the two creatures the owner already
likes, so every later order is "content only". Families then go from the kit's strongest material
(chitin, flesh) to its hardest (humanoids, then stone, which already failed once), and the bosses come
last with everything learnt. Until order 9 the game runs with a mix: migrated types on the new
runtime, the rest on the legacy path - always building, always playable.

ASSUMPTIONS (series-wide)
-------------------------
  AS1  Warden -> ACID_DRONE (the only non-boss MACHINE: a hovering ranged harasser). Harvest Tick ->
       SHELL_BRUTE (INSECT BRUISER, a heavy straight-line charger). Name tags keep the current names.
  AS2  FRONT-FACING billboards only. Enemies are drawn facing the player, as in Doom RPG; directional
       (4/8-angle) sprites would multiply every clip by 4-8 for little gain in a first-person,
       turn-based crawler where enemies come at you. Revisit only if the owner asks.
  AS3  Pre-rendered frames, not a runtime skeleton (guide 7.2).
  AS4  The kit stays offline Python (guide 2).
  AS5  Atlases are loaded per floor for the types that can appear on it (family-coherent floors make
       that a handful), not all at app start.
  AS6  A kit change regenerates every earlier creature byte-identical unless the owner approved a change.
