VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
AI CORE NODE ('J', FULL wall height - floor to ceiling, canvas 144 x 256, 24 frames @ 0.1 s, loops)
  A tall steel compute column. A plinth on the floor (vents, a rad hazard strip), a darkmetal chassis between
  two gunmetal side rails, a collar at the top with cable bundles running up INTO the ceiling.
  - WINDOW: a tall dark glass window down the front. Inside, a stack of thin MEMORY WAFERS lit NEON CYAN at
    their edges, threaded by a bright vertical DATA SPINE; data pulses climb the spine (seamless loop) and on up
    the cables; in the middle of the stack a pulsing CORE in RAD LIME with a white-hot heart - the one warm
    accent, so the archive reads as alive and distinct from the cyan tube and the cyan portal.
  - LEDs: columns of small status LEDs down both side rails blinking in phosphor green and cyan, an occasional
    rad lime one.
  - A lab-grey readout plate under the window with a small phosphor bar graph that moves.
  - Story detail: the stencil PTN-ARC (pattern archive) on the plinth.
  Static body (one kit render); everything that moves is painted per frame.
