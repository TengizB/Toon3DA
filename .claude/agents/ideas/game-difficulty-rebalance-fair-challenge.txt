TITLE: Game Difficulty Rebalance — Fair Hard Challenge
CREATED: 2026-06-21
---

OVERVIEW
========
The game is currently "unfairly hard": even flawless tactical play cannot reliably
reach the first boss, because the survivability budget (HP, armor, heals) is too small
relative to enemy damage, the XP curve is too slow to deliver meaningful level-ups before
the player is overwhelmed, and depth scaling compounds too fast. This document rebalances
the whole survivability + threat + progression economy so that a CAREFUL player (uses
line-of-sight cover, kites enemies one at a time, manages ammo and heals) can reach
floor 5-7 on a good first run, while a RUSHING player who walks into two or more enemies
at once still dies. Hardness and tactical depth are preserved — only the math is made fair.

This is a pure-numbers rebalance. No new mechanics, no new tile symbols, no new classes.
Every change is a constant edit in one of four files.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY — HARD BUT FAIR
=================================
1. The player should always understand WHY they died. Deaths come from positioning
   mistakes (multiple enemies in line of sight, getting cornered, running out of ammo),
   not from arithmetic the player could never have beaten.
2. One-on-one fights are winnable with correct play. Two-on-one is dangerous.
   Three-on-one is usually lethal. This is the tactical constraint we protect.
3. Cover and kiting are the core skills. Because enemies only act once per player turn
   and ranged enemies need a clear cardinal line, breaking LOS is always the right answer
   when overwhelmed. The numbers must give the player enough HP to survive the 1-2 hits
   it takes to reach a corner.
4. Resources are tight but not starving. A heal should be worth using. Ammo should run
   low if you spray, but not strand you with no kills.
5. Level-ups arrive BEFORE the difficulty wall, and each one is felt.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT THE PROPOSAL MISSED — CORRECTED MATH
======================================================
Three facts in the codebase change the combat math from the rough estimate in the
original proposal. The numbers below are tuned around the ACTUAL systems:

A. SHOTGUN HAS DISTANCE FALLOFF, not flat 50.
   SHOTGUN_DAMAGE = 50, SHOTGUN_DAMAGE_DROP_COEFF = 0.18 (WeaponConstants.java).
   Damage drops with range, so "one-shots everything" is only true at point-blank.
   This is GOOD for tactics (you want enemies close to maximize damage, which is risky)
   and we do NOT touch it. Just be aware kiting at max range trades safety for more shots.

B. TOUGHNESS APPLIES A SECOND DAMAGE-REDUCTION LAYER after armor.
   GameBalance.TGH_REDUCTION_PER_POINT = 1 HP shaved per TOUGHNESS point, floor of
   TGH_MIN_DAMAGE = 1. A MARINE starts at TGH 2 → -2 HP off every hit that reaches HP.
   TGH also adds HP: TGH_HP_PER_POINT = 5, so a MARINE's effective max HP is base+10.
   The rebalance leaves TOUGHNESS alone; it is already a fair mitigation layer. The
   survival math below assumes a MARINE (TGH 2) for realism, i.e. -2 per HP-bound hit.

C. ACTUAL CURRENT HP VALUES differ from the proposal in two enemies:
   - MIRE_WRAITH_MAX_HEALTH is 45 (proposal said 60). We reduce 45 -> 38.
   - EYE_TYRANT_MAX_HEALTH is 18 (proposal said 20). We leave it at 18.
   All other current values match the proposal.

ARMOR + TOUGHNESS DAMAGE PIPELINE (how a hit resolves)
======================================================
For an incoming raw hit of D against a player WITH armor remaining:
   armorAbsorb   = round(D * ARMOUR_ABSORB_FRACTION)   // 0.50
   hpPortion     = D - armorAbsorb
   hpDamage      = max(TGH_MIN_DAMAGE, hpPortion - TGH_REDUCTION_PER_POINT * TGH)
   armor        -= armorAbsorb   (clamped at 0)
Once armor is depleted, the full D goes to the hpPortion path (no 50% absorb), still
reduced by TOUGHNESS. This pipeline is unchanged by this rebalance — we only retune
the pool sizes and the raw D values.

=======================================================================
VERIFICATION MATH
=======================================================================
All player-side math below assumes a fresh MARINE on floor 1:
  effective max HP = 130 + TGH(2)*5 = 140 HP
  max armor        = 75
  TOUGHNESS flat reduction = -2 per HP-bound hit (floor 1)
Floor 1 = no depth scaling (multiplier 1.0).

(1) GORE_BITER encounter at floor 1, with 1 stim medkit.
    GORE_BITER raw D = 7. With armor up:
      armorAbsorb = round(7*0.5) = 4 ; hpPortion = 3 ; hpDamage = max(1, 3-2) = 1 HP
      (armor drains 4 per hit; 75 armor lasts ~18 hits)
    GORE_BITER has 18 HP, moves every turn. Shotgun at range 1-2 deals ~40-50 -> ONE-SHOT.
    A careful player who waits in a corridor and fires as the biter steps adjacent kills
    it on turn 1, taking 0 damage. Even if the biter gets one bite in first:
      worst case while armored: 1 HP. While unarmored: max(1, 7-2) = 5 HP.
    A single biter is never a threat. A stim heal (18) trivially covers any chip taken.
    PASS. A pack of 3 biters reaching melee simultaneously = 3-15 HP/turn — survivable
    long enough to retreat and funnel them into a corridor one at a time. RUSHING into
    the open with 3 biters and reloading a 1-shell shotgun is still deadly. Tactical
    constraint preserved.

(2) Can a careful player KITE an IRON_STALKER at floor 1? (the stress test)
    IRON_STALKER: 95 HP, melee 16, ranged 11, range 4, moves every turn.
    Shotgun at point-blank ~50, with falloff ~38-44 at range 2-3.
    Kite pattern in a corridor: shoot, step back (breaking the cardinal line denies the
    ranged shot whenever the player rounds a corner), shoot again.
    Shots to kill: ceil(95 / 44) = 3 shots (at modest range) or 2 at point-blank (~50+50).
    Worst realistic case — open corridor, no corner, stalker melees every other turn:
      turn 1: shoot (stalker ~95->~51), reload — stalker melee 16:
        armorAbsorb 8, hpPortion 8, hpDamage max(1,8-2)=6 HP ; armor 75->67
      turn 2: shoot (~51->~7), reload — stalker melee 16: 6 HP ; armor 67->59
      turn 3: shoot -> dead.
    Total taken: ~12 HP and ~16 armor out of 140/75. Comfortable.
    If the player uses a corner to break LOS during reload turns, they take 0-6 HP total.
    PASS — an Iron Stalker is a tense but clearly winnable elite, not a wall.
    Under OLD numbers this same fight cost ~33 HP out of 100 with worse RNG, which is why
    stacking it with any second enemy was lethal. Now there is headroom for a mistake.

(3) Floor 5 SHELL_BRUTE sanity check (the old "dies in 7 hits" problem).
    Depth-5 damage multiplier = 1.06^4 = 1.262.
    SHELL_BRUTE raw D at floor 5 = round(13 * 1.262) = 16.
    With armor: armorAbsorb 8, hpPortion 8, hpDamage max(1,8-2) = 6 HP per hit (armored).
    Unarmored: max(1, 16-2) = 14 HP per hit.
    A level-4ish player at floor 5 (see XP math) has ~140 base HP + ~50-75 from level-ups
    = ~190-215 effective HP and a refreshed armor pool from vests. Even unarmored that is
    13+ brute hits to die, and the brute has only 38 * 1.08^4 = ~52 HP -> 1-2 shotgun
    shots to kill. The "7 hits to death" cliff is gone; the fight is now a fair trade.
    OLD floor-5 brute did 29 raw -> 14.5 HP/hit on a 100 HP pool = the unfair wall. FIXED.

(4) XP MATH — kills to reach each level.
    Formula: xpRequired(level) = XP_BASE * level ^ XP_CURVE_EXPONENT
    New constants: XP_BASE = 50, exponent = 1.3.
      level 1 -> 2: 50 * 1^1.3 =  50 XP
      level 2 -> 3: 50 * 2^1.3 = 123 XP
      level 3 -> 4: 50 * 3^1.3 = 207 XP
      level 4 -> 5: 50 * 4^1.3 = 302 XP
      level 5 -> 6: 50 * 5^1.3 = 406 XP
    Kills to reach each level (mixed floor-1/2 fodder; GORE_BITER=10, SHELL_BRUTE=18,
    EYE_TYRANT=10, plus the occasional MIRE_WRAITH=22 / IRON_STALKER=55):
      Reach LVL 2: 50 XP  = 5 gore biters, OR 1 stalker. ~half of floor 1. FAST — the
        first level-up lands while the player is still learning the floor. GOOD.
      Reach LVL 3: +123 XP cumulative 173 = ~floor 1 fully cleared + a few floor-2 kills.
      Reach LVL 4: +207 XP cumulative 380 = clearing floor 2 (a typical floor yields
        150-250 XP with brutes and an elite). Player hits ~LVL 4 entering floor 3.
      Reach LVL 5: +302 XP cumulative 682 = by end of floor 3 / into floor 4.
    Result: the player is roughly (floor + 1) in level through the early game, so each new
    floor is met with a fresh level-up's worth of HP/armor/damage. This is exactly the
    "level-ups arrive before the wall" goal.
    COMPARISON: old curve needed ~14 gore-biter kills just for LVL 2 (80 XP / 6 each) and
    ~38 more for LVL 3 — the player was perpetually under-leveled. FIXED.

(5) Level-up payoff feel.
    LEVEL_UP_HP_BONUS 25 on a 130 base = +19% max HP per HP pick (was +15 on 100 = +15%,
    but on the NEW larger pools the old +15 would have felt smaller). +25 is a clearly
    felt jump: by LVL 5 an HP-focused player is near 240 effective HP. ARMOR +18 and
    DAMAGE +8 are similarly meaningful (the +8 flat damage helps shotgun reach lethal
    breakpoints against scaled HP at depth).

(6) Medkit worthwhileness.
    Stim 18 on 130 max = 13.8% — a real mid-fight top-off, not the old 8 (6.2%) that was
    rounding error against 10-damage hits. Full 50 = 38.5% of max — a genuine recovery
    between fights. Carry cap 6 total (up to 3 large) gives a careful hoarder ~150-204 HP
    of banked healing across a run without trivializing damage.

=======================================================================
PLAYER EXPERIENCE
=======================================================================
Before: every encounter felt like a coin flip the player would eventually lose; deaths
felt arbitrary because the HP pool was too thin to absorb even a single tactical mistake,
and the player was always under-leveled.
After: the player feels like a competent marine who CAN win fights they play well, and who
dies when they get greedy — pull two patrols at once, fight in the open instead of a
doorway, or burn all their shells before clearing a room. The decision the rebalance
creates: "Do I push for the next kill (XP, ammo drop at 40%) or retreat to heal and
reset LOS?" That risk/reward tension is the game. Hardness is preserved; arbitrariness
is removed.

BALANCE NOTES (tuning guidance / placeholders)
=======================================================================
- These are first-pass tuned values, not final. The intended target is: a SKILLED first-
  good-run reaches floor 5-7. If playtests show the player reaching the boss too easily,
  the cheapest lever to re-tighten is ENEMY_DAMAGE_SCALE_PER_DEPTH (1.06 -> 1.07) and
  MEDKIT_TOTAL_CARRY_CAP (6 -> 5), NOT raising base enemy damage (that re-breaks floor 1).
- If the player still dies on floor 1-2, the safest buffer is MEDKIT_STIM_HEAL (18 -> 22)
  and ARMOUR_ABSORB_FRACTION (0.50 -> 0.55), since both help the careful player most.
- Do NOT lower enemy HP further than listed — fights are already snappy (1-3 shots); going
  lower removes the kiting requirement and collapses the tactical layer.
- XP exponent 1.3 is the key "leveling feels good" lever. If late floors feel like the
  player out-levels the danger, raise exponent to 1.35 (steeper) rather than cutting
  rewards.
- All ULTRA/NIGHTMARE difficulty stat bases (STAT_BASE_*) are UNCHANGED; this rebalance
  targets the MARINE baseline. Verify that NIGHTMARE (TGH 1, -1 reduction, +5 HP) is still
  hard-but-fair under the new enemy damage — it should be, since the raw D cut helps every
  difficulty proportionally.

INTERACTIONS
=======================================================================
1. TOUGHNESS stat system (GameBalance TGH_*): the -2 flat reduction at MARINE stacks on top
   of the lowered raw damage, so low-damage hits (GORE_BITER 7) become near-chip while
   armored. This is intended — fodder should be trivial 1-on-1; the danger is volume.
   Re-confirm TGH_MIN_DAMAGE=1 floor still applies so nothing becomes fully immune.
2. Armor pickups (ARMOUR_SHARD/VEST) + the larger PLAYER_MAX_ARMOR=75: vests (now +35) and
   shards (+8) refill a bigger pool, making armor a meaningful between-fight resource to
   seek out. Pairs with the 40% ammo drop to make exploration worthwhile.
3. Weapon-level abilities (Lifesteal, Hemorrhage Harvest, Second Wind, Bulwark) all read
   off the new HP/armor pools and unchanged ability constants. Larger pools mean Lifesteal
   % returns slightly more raw HP; Second Wind's 30%-HP threshold now triggers at 42 HP
   instead of 30 — a touch more generous, which is fine and on-theme.
4. Boss (Overseer 250 HP, laser 20, charge 30): against the new ~190-215 HP / 75-armor
   level-4+ player, a charge (30 raw -> ~13 HP armored / ~28 unarmored) is a serious but
   survivable hit. The 250 HP (from 300) keeps the DPS check reachable with the larger
   start/box ammo so the player doesn't run dry mid-boss. Re-verify boss ammo economy in
   playtest; if the player runs out, raise AMMO_BOX_SHELLS again before lowering boss HP.
5. Procedural level generation (LevelGenConstants): enemy COUNT per floor is unchanged, so
   the survivability buffer assumes current spawn density. If a future change increases
   enemies-per-room, revisit MEDKIT carry cap and start ammo.
