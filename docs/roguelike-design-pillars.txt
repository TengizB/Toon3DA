# Background reference for creative-game-designer agent — design philosophy.

=== 8 ROGUELIKE DESIGN PILLARS ===

1. Permadeath & meaningful death
   Death is permanent for the run. Must teach something — feel fair in retrospect ("I made a mistake").
   Design so the player always had a choice that could have saved them.

2. Procedural generation
   Every run generates new floor layouts, enemy placements, item distributions, shop inventories.
   No two runs play the same. Player knowledge of systems rewarded more than memorising layouts.
   Levels must always be navigable — no isolated rooms, no dead ends with no return.

3. Emergent complexity from simple rules
   Each system is simple; depth from interaction.
   Example: fire sets oil slicks ablaze → ignites wood → damages player and enemies.
   Every mechanic should interact with at least 2–3 others.

4. Meaningful choice under pressure
   Every resource decision matters. "Use last medkit now or risk next room?"
   Items should have interesting trade-offs, not pure upgrades.
   Cursed items, double-edged weapons, high-risk-high-reward choices welcome.

5. Run identity through build diversity
   Each run develops distinct identity through item/ability combinations.
   Holy Water Pistol + high Agility plays completely differently from Chainsaw + high Strength.
   Synergies discoverable by experimentation, not listed in tutorials.

6. Meta-progression
   Permanent unlocks: new starting items, classes, weapon variants, lore fragments.
   Rewards engagement without making individual runs feel mandatory.
   A new player should complete a full run without needing meta-unlocks.

7. Information management
   Fog of war: unexplored tiles hidden on mini-map.
   Unknown items: potions/scrolls unidentified until used (or ID'd by high IQ).
   Enemy alert: noise propagates; loud attacks alert nearby enemies. Silent weapons preserve stealth.

8. Pacing and escalation
   Floors get harder as player descends: more enemies, tougher variants, scarcer resources.
   Each floor has a clear climax: boss room, gauntlet corridor, or choice room (pick one of three boons).
   Safe rooms (rest point, shop, lore terminal) give space to plan — reward cautious exploration.

=== KEY REFERENCE GAMES ===

Shattered Pixel Dungeon — deep item interaction, hunger clock, scrolls of identify
DCSS (Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup) — skill system, vast item variety, genuinely challenging tactical combat
Enter the Gungeon — room-by-room design, bullet patterns, item synergies, boss design
Hades — narrative integration, boon system, meta-progression done right
Brogue — elegant minimal systems, magical items with unpredictable properties
Nethack — extreme emergent complexity, item identification meta-game
Cogmind — sci-fi setting, modular equipment, information management
Dead Cells — weapon synergies, proc-gen level structure
