# Enemy System — Turn-Based AI Design & Implementation Reference

## Overview

Enemies are turn-based entities that act once after every player action.
Two archetypes: Corruptor (melee bruiser) and Vortex Eye (ranged kiter).
Dormant until alerted by proximity or line-of-sight.

## Turn Sequence

ONE FULL TURN:

1. Player taps a touch button (move, rotate, fire, strafe, etc.).
2. PlayerController enters its action state (MOVING, ROTATING, FIRING, INTERACTING).
3. Animation plays for its duration (PLAYER_MOVE_DURATION, etc.).
   No enemy logic runs during this window.
4. finishAction(causesTick, cause) snaps the player to their settled position.
5. If causesTick: tickEventBus.fireTick() dispatches to all subscribers in order:
     a. WeaponReloadSubscriber — advances weapon reload counter.
     b. EnemyTurnSubscriber   — calls enemyManager.takeTurn(playerTile, player).
6. EnemyManager runs three phases (see below).
7. fireTick() returns. PlayerController is back to IDLE.

Tick-causing actions: forward/back/strafe tile step (MOVE tick), FIRE button (FIRE tick).
Non-tick actions: left/right rotation, door interaction, wall-blocked moves (never reach finishAction).

See docs/tick-system.txt for the full tick architecture.

## Enemy Simulation Phases (EnemyManager.takeTurn)

### Phase A — Perception
For every DORMANT enemy:
  - Chebyshev distance to player <= ALERT_RADIUS_TILES (4) → alert immediately.
  - OR Chebyshev <= LOS_MAX_RANGE_TILES (8) AND hasLineOfSight → alert.

### Phase A2 — Chain Alert (BFS)
Newly alerted enemies propagate wakeup to DORMANT enemies within
CHAIN_ALERT_RADIUS_TILES (5) via breadth-first search.
Chain alert does NOT require LOS — represents a scream traveling through the room.
The first wakeup sets World.gameState.redAlert = true (drives the red wall pulse).

### Phase B — Action
Every ALERTED/CHASING/ATTACKING enemy acts once:

  MELEE enemies (PLAGUE_HULK, GORE_BITER, SHELL_BRUTE, IRON_STALKER, VOID_SHROUD, CINDERFORGE_COLOSSUS,
  VERDANT_SPIRESOWER):
    - Manhattan distance == 1 (cardinal adjacent only, never diagonal) → swing.
    - Else → step toward player via greedy Manhattan minimizer.
    - Move cooldown governed by moveEveryNTurns() per type.
    - CINDERFORGE_COLOSSUS is a plain greedy chaser (no bespoke plan branch): its identity is in two
      PASSIVE hooks, not in clever AI. It HARDENS (gains flat armour) on every turn it takes no damage,
      and it IGNITES the tile it just vacated, so ducking into cover to reload lets it cool while the
      corridor behind you burns. Its danger is the board state it creates, not outsmarting the player.
    - VERDANT_SPIRESOWER (computeSowerPlan) is the first enemy that EDITS THE LEVEL. On its sow cadence
      (SPIRESOWER_SOW_CADENCE_TURNS), under its live-spire cap, it commits a WIND_UP that resolves a turn
      later into a bespoke SOW (executeSow → SpireManager stamps solid, sight-blocking crystal spires that
      heal it while they stand). Otherwise it swings if adjacent, else steps toward the player PREFERRING a
      step that keeps a living spire between it and them (stepTowardPreferringCover). See MECHANICAL VERB
      HOOKS below.

  RANGED enemies (EYE_TYRANT, MIRE_WRAITH, ACID_DRONE, VORTEX_EYE, AURIC_SENTINEL, RIMESHELL_LANCER):
    - Distance < RANGED_KITE_MIN_TILES (2) → flee first, then re-evaluate.
    - In range AND has LOS AND on same cardinal line → fire in place (no move).
    - Out of range OR no LOS OR not on cardinal line → step toward player.
    - AURIC_SENTINEL inverts the kiting rule (computeSentinelPlan): with a shard in the ring it STANDS
      AND SHOOTS at any range, even point-blank; it only kites once the ring is EMPTY, because then it
      cannot attack at all. Its aggression level is therefore a readout of its shard count.
    - RIMESHELL_LANCER (computeLancerPlan) NEVER kites: off cooldown with a clean cardinal lane it commits
      a WIND_UP charge (opening its ice shell for the punish window), then fires a piercing lane beam a
      turn later via executeLanceBeam; otherwise it steps toward alignment or braces. See MECHANICAL VERB
      HOOKS below.

## CRITICAL — Ranged Attack Cardinal Line Rule

Ranged enemies can ONLY fire at the player when they share the same tile column
(same vertical line) or the same tile row (same horizontal line):

    isSameCardinalLine(enemyCol, enemyRow, playerCol, playerRow)
    = (enemyCol == playerCol) OR (enemyRow == playerRow)

Diagonal attacks are NEVER allowed. This is enforced in EnemyManager.java by
isSameCardinalLine() called before every ranged attack attempt.

When designing or implementing any new ranged enemy archetype, this constraint
is mandatory. The AI movement steers ranged enemies toward cardinal alignment so
they can fire — but the fire check itself always verifies cardinal alignment first.

## Enemy AI State Machine

  DORMANT    — inactive; only perception phase touches it.
  ALERTED    — just woke this turn; transitions immediately into CHASING/ATTACKING.
  CHASING    — moving toward player.
  ATTACKING  — dealt damage this turn.

States only advance forward — no enemy ever returns to DORMANT.

## Movement AI

Greedy cardinal stepper: evaluate all four cardinal directions, pick the one with
smallest Manhattan distance to the player that passes isPassableForEnemy() checks.

Passability rules:
  - level.isBlockedAt() (walls, solid props, closed doors) → blocked.
  - occupancy[col][row] (another enemy is there this turn) → blocked.
  - Player's tile → blocked (attack instead of stepping onto them).

Greedy-stuck mitigation: if stuck for STUCK_TURNS_BEFORE_WIGGLE (2) consecutive turns,
pick a random legal cardinal step (deterministic RNG seed 12345, reproducible).

## Line of Sight

GameMath.tileLineOfSightClear(fromColumn, fromRow, toColumn, toRow, solidTest):
  Parametric sampling: advance step/maxSteps along both axes simultaneously,
  testing max(|dc|,|dr|)−1 intermediate tiles. A tile is opaque if:
    - Level.isWall(cell) OR
    - DoorManager.blocksSight (CLOSED/CLOSING door) OR
    - Level.isPropSolid(cell) (lockers, terminals, barrels, crates).
  Decals and floor-light tiles are transparent.

Pre-check: if Chebyshev > LOS_MAX_RANGE_TILES (8), return false without walking.

## Level Placement

Spawn characters (replaced with ' ' at load time — underlying tile becomes floor):
  '1' → Corruptor
  '2' → Vortex Eye

LevelLoader records EnemySpawnPoint(spawnChar, tileColumn, tileRow) before rewriting.
EnemyManager reads level.getEnemySpawnPoints() to build the initial enemy list.

Placement guidance:
  Corruptors ('1'): chokepoints, doorways, unlit rooms — obstacles the player MUST engage.
  Vortex Eyes ('2'): open rooms with long sightlines, near columns ('P') for LOS cover.
  Mixed pack (1 Corruptor + 2 Vortex Eyes in a room behind a door): signature encounter.

## Combat Integration

Player damages enemies:
  Shotgun.marchShot() walks facing tiles; on first tile with a living enemy,
  enemyHitTarget.applyDamageTo(enemy, damageAtDistance(dist)) is called.
  On HP <= 0: enemy removed from list, corpse 'm' stamped into level grid.

Enemy damages player:
  Player.applyDamage(amount) is called from EnemyManager's EXECUTE phase (phaseBExecute),
  running the action the enemy COMMITTED on its previous turn (see "Pre-Committed Intent Model").
  Player.health starts at PLAYER_MAX_HEALTH (100).
  Player.isDead() returns true when health reaches 0.

## Rendering

EnemyRenderer (render/EnemyRenderer.java):
  - Mirrors PropRenderer: same projection math, same painter's-algorithm depth sort,
    same per-column z-buffer occlusion test, zero per-frame allocations.
  - Textures loaded from asset paths at construction; procedural fallbacks if absent.
  - DORMANT enemies rendered with shade *= DORMANT_SHADE_DAMPEN (0.7) — visibly darker.
  - VORTEX EYE drawBottom raised by VORTEX_EYE_HOVER_OFFSET_FRACTION (0.25) × lineHeight.
  - Inserted in World.render() after propRenderer.render(), before weaponHudRenderer.render().

## Tick Wiring

  EnemyManager is driven by EnemyTurnSubscriber, a package-private adapter in world/.
  World constructor registers two subscribers on TickEventBus (in order):
    1. new WeaponReloadSubscriber(inventory)      — reload advances first
    2. new EnemyTurnSubscriber(enemyManager, gameState) — enemies act second
  World sets: playerController.setTickEventBus(tickEventBus)
  World sets: playerController.setEnemyManager(enemyManager)  — for weapon hit-target only
  PlayerController.finishAction(true, TickCause.MOVE or FIRE) fires the bus.
  EnemyTurnSubscriber calls enemyManager.takeTurn(playerTile, player)
    and sets gameState.redAlert = true when anyAlerted().

  See docs/tick-system.txt for the full tick architecture.

## Enemy Family Classification (bestiary taxonomy)

Every archetype declares a FAMILY on EnemyType (enemy/EnemyFamily.java) alongside its role:

    EnemyType.PLAGUE_HULK.family()  ->  EnemyFamily.ABERRATION

FAMILY vs ROLE — two orthogonal axes:
  - family() = WHAT the thing is    (undead / insect / machine / demon / aberration / golem)
  - role()   = what JOB it does     (chaff / soldier / bruiser / mini-elite / boss)
An UNDEAD floor still fields chaff, a soldier and an anchor; the family only changes the flavour
of the bodies filling those roles.

  family       archetypes
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ABERRATION   Plague Hulk, Eye Tyrant, Gore Biter, Mire Wraith, The Corruptor (boss)
  UNDEAD       Ghoul, Crawler, Revenant, Vortex Eye, Blight Corruptor
  INSECT       Shell Brute, Iron Stalker
  MACHINE      Acid Drone, The Overseer (boss)
  DEMON        Void Shroud, Hell Baron (boss)
  GOLEM        Rimeshell Lancer, Auric Sentinel, Cinderforge Colossus, Verdant Spiresower (Q3 of the
               shared elemental-golem sheet)

STATUS: DESCRIPTIVE ONLY. Nothing reads family() yet — no spawn, budget, AI or render path branches
on it, so adding the taxonomy changed zero behaviour and every archetype stays spawnable on every
floor exactly as before.

STAGED FOR: the FAMILY-COHERENT FLOOR rule — a generated floor picks ONE family and draws its whole
roster from that family, so a level reads as a single infestation instead of a random bestiary dump.
When that lands it must be a FILTER inside EncounterBudgetPlanner's roster selection (drop candidate
archetypes whose family != the floor's family), never a switch on EnemyType. A family must therefore
carry enough archetypes to fill the role wheel — chaff + soldier + one anchor — before it can be
chosen as a floor family; GOLEM is the first family authored to that standard.

RULE: family() is ABSTRACT on EnemyType. A new archetype cannot compile without stating its family,
so the taxonomy can never silently fall out of date.

## Tactical Roles & Verbs (balance idea 4 — Tactical Combat Depth)

Every enemy archetype carries TACTICAL METADATA on EnemyType (the single source of truth):
role(), positionalMultiplier(), attackCadenceTurns(), spawnChar(), tacticalVerb(), and the
derived baseThreatPoints() (via GameMath.threatPoints). The VERB is the one-sentence reason the
player must fight that archetype DIFFERENTLY — the design rule that a new enemy is NOT approved
until you can state its verb in one sentence and that verb is DISTINCT from every existing enemy.
"It has more HP" is not a verb. Any ranged behaviour must respect the cardinal-line constraint
(see isSameCardinalLine in EnemyManager).

THE VERB REACHES THE PLAYER (narrative-rework order-5). It used to be pure design documentation
with no caller in the build. It is now the SOURCE OF TRUTH behind the bestiary voice: ORA says a
plain-words translation of each archetype's verb the first time the player sees that archetype, and
narrative/BestiaryCatalog reads tacticalVerb() at bootstrap, so an archetype that ships without one
fails the build instead of shipping a line about nothing. The verbs below are written in DESIGNER
voice (caps prefix, em dashes, too long for a two-line panel); the shipped line is the translation,
in assets/story/story-strings.properties under story.bark.enemy.<type>. Changing a verb here means
rewriting that line in the same commit — one behaviour per line, and never a number.
A NEW ARCHETYPE OR FAMILY THEREFORE NEEDS ITS ORA LINES: see docs/story-ui-system.txt, ADD AN ENEMY.

  archetype     spawn  role        verb (the reason to fight it differently)
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Gore Biter     '3'   CHAFF       SWARMER: don't get surrounded — funnel the pack into a chokepoint.
  Eye Tyrant     '2'   CHAFF       SNIPER: break its line — step off its row/column to deny the shot.
  Acid Drone     '$'   SOLDIER     HARASSER: corner it — cut off its kite path with level geometry.
  Void Shroud    '^'   SOLDIER     FLANKER: keep your back covered — rotate to face it.
  Mire Wraith    '5'   SOLDIER     ARTILLERY: prioritise it — don't let its acid DOT stack.
  Plague Hulk    '1'   SOLDIER     TANK: kill at range — don't grind it down in melee.
  Shell Brute    '4'   BRUISER     CHARGER: sidestep its charge, then punish the recovery.
  Iron Stalker   '!'   MINI_ELITE  MINI-ELITE: commit resources or avoid — a deliberate mid-floor spike.
  Auric Sentinel '('   SOLDIER     SENTINEL: its shards are armour AND ammo — strip them with rapid
                                   fire, or bait it into spending them.
  Cinderforge C. '{'   BRUISER     FORGE: keep the pressure on — it re-hardens the moment you stop
                                   shooting, and it burns the ground behind it.
  Rimeshell Lancer '[' SOLDIER     LANCER: armoured until it fires — bait the lance, step off the lane,
                                   then burst it through the open shell.
  Verdant Spiresower '}' SOLDIER   SOWER: break the spires first — they heal it and wall you in, and it
                                   will never die while one is standing.

Two BRUISERS, two different failures: the Shell Brute is a POSITIONING puzzle (sidestep the lane, punish
the recovery); the Cinderforge Colossus is a TEMPO puzzle (never let the damage stop). The Rimeshell
Lancer is a TIMING puzzle (shoot on the RIGHT turn — when its shell is open, not while it is sealed).

FLOOR BANDS — EnemyType.minSpawnDepth() (default 1) is a DATA gate the EncounterBudgetPlanner applies to
BOTH its fill pool and its anchor pool, never a switch. Two archetypes raise it, each because it asks a
question floor 1 cannot answer: the Auric Sentinel (3) asks "WHAT ARE YOU HOLDING" — unanswerable on
floor 1 with a single starting weapon — and the Cinderforge Colossus (2) asks "CAN YOU KEEP FIRING
without ducking into cover to reload", which is a real choice only once you have a weapon and know its
reload. Because the Colossus is a BRUISER ANCHOR (chosen at most once per floor as the largest anchor
that fits the reserve), its 90-TP body does not fit the tight floor-2 anchor reserve, so in practice it
begins anchoring from floor 3; the DATA gate opening at 2 is deliberate and hand-authored levels are NOT
gated — a level file may place '(' or '{' at any depth deliberately.

ENCOUNTER BUDGET (Pillar 1) — the floor generator (LevelGenerator) SPENDS a per-depth Threat-Point
budget instead of rolling enemies at random, via EncounterBudgetPlanner:

  floorThreatPointBudget(depth) = FLOOR_BASE_THREAT_POINT_BUDGET * depthThreatScale(depth)
                                                                   (GameMath.floorThreatPointBudget)

Each enemy's TP cost is scaled by the SAME depth curve (GameMath.enemyThreatAtDepth), so the enemy
COUNT stays roughly constant across floors while each enemy gets stronger — difficulty rises through
per-enemy power, never an exploding horde, and the depth curve is never double-counted.

Composition rules the planner enforces (BalanceConfig SECTION 11 knobs):
  - Reserve ENCOUNTER_ANCHOR_BUDGET_FRACTION_MIN..MAX (15–30%) for ONE anchor (bruiser/mini-elite)
    that defines the floor's hardest room; the anchor is placed in the DEEPEST room and is exempt
    from the per-room cap. Rarely (ENCOUNTER_ELITE_ANCHOR_FLOOR_CHANCE, depth-gated) a floor is a
    deliberate elite gauntlet whose mini-elite anchor exceeds that reserve.
  - No single non-anchor TYPE exceeds ENCOUNTER_MAX_SINGLE_TYPE_FRACTION (40%) of the budget (variety).
  - No single non-anchor ROOM exceeds ENCOUNTER_PER_ROOM_TP_FRACTION_CAP (35%) of the budget.
  - Every roster contains both RANGED and MELEE archetypes (the core ranged/melee tension).

Verify the planned roster per depth with BalanceReport (the ENCOUNTER BUDGET section), the same
dev-only auditor that prints the weapon/enemy/scarcity living tables.

PRE-COMMITTED INTENT MODEL (strategy-combat-order-1) — the spine of the strategy-combat series.
Enemies no longer decide-and-act in one step. EnemyManager.takeTurn() runs THREE phases:

  A  phaseA           — perception: dormant enemies wake on proximity/LOS, then chain-alert BFS.
  B  phaseBExecute    — EXECUTE: every alerted enemy runs the action it COMMITTED last turn, via
                        executePlan() → execute{Melee,Ranged,WindUp,Move}. Fairness re-validation
                        (R1-R6) happens here: a committed attack only lands if the player is STILL
                        in reach / on the line — otherwise it whiffs (no damage, no free re-plan),
                        so "bait the swing, step away, punish" is the core counter. A committed MOVE
                        that is blocked quietly re-paths (low stakes). A stun cancels the action (R6).
  C  phaseCCommit     — COMMIT: every alerted enemy reads the now-resolved board and computes its
                        NEXT action via computeNextPlan() → compute{Melee,Charger,Flanker,Ranged}Plan,
                        storing it in enemy.plannedAction (verb + target + predictedDamage). ALL of B
                        runs before ANY of C, so no enemy re-plans on a same-turn ally's move.

Because the plan is written at the END of the enemy turn, a fresh, readable intent is visible for
the ENTIRE following player turn (order-2 draws the icon + predicted-damage number). A freshly-woken
enemy has plan.committed == false, so it does nothing on its wake turn except commit — one full turn
of warning before its first action. Enemies act one telegraphed action per turn (a kiting ranged
enemy flees OR fires OR advances, never move-and-fire in the same turn).

MECHANICAL VERB HOOKS (Pillar 2) — the compute*Plan step ENFORCES the tactical verbs, not just
labels them:

  - SHELL_BRUTE — CHARGER (computeChargerPlan / canBeginCharge / performCharge). When a clear,
    unobstructed cardinal lane to the player opens within SHELL_BRUTE_CHARGE_TRIGGER_MIN..MAX_TILES,
    the brute COMMITS a WIND_UP (IntentVerb.WIND_UP, EnemyState.WINDING_UP, triggerTelegraph(),
    chargeDirectionColumn/Row captured) — a rim-flash telegraph shown for the whole player turn. The
    next turn executeWindUp() rushes straight down the committed lane and, if it ends cardinally
    adjacent, hits for base * SHELL_BRUTE_CHARGE_DAMAGE_MULTIPLIER. If the player sidestepped off the
    lane the rush whiffs and the brute is left in a punishable recovery (skipNextAction). Counterplay:
    "sidestep the charge, then punish."
  - VOID_SHROUD — FLANKER (computeFlankerPlan / isBehindPlayerFacing / stepTowardTile). When not
    adjacent it commits a MOVE toward the tile directly behind the player's facing rather than toward
    the player; when it strikes from that blind side executeMeleeAttack recomputes the bonus live and
    deals base * VOID_SHROUD_FLANK_DAMAGE_MULTIPLIER. Counterplay: "rotate to face it / keep your back
    covered."
  - PLAGUE_HULK — AREA DENIAL. On death (EnemyManager.EnemyDeathHazardListener, wired in World) it
    leaves a lingering toxic cloud (HazardManager.spawnToxicCloud, radius
    HAZARD_PLAGUE_HULK_DEATH_CLOUD_RADIUS). Counterplay: "kill at range — don't melee into its cloud."
  - PLAGUE_HULK — SELF_DESTRUCT finisher (.claude/agents/ideas/plague-hulk-self-destruct.txt). Once
    HP drops to or below PLAGUE_HULK_SELF_DESTRUCT_HP_PERCENT it PRIMES (sticky —
    shouldPrimeOrContinueSelfDestruct/commitSelfDestruct pre-empt every other branch in
    computeNextPlan, including the scripted move-set) and braces (EnemyState.SELF_DESTRUCTING, a
    sickly-green SPECIAL telegraph, no Block gained) for
    PLAGUE_HULK_SELF_DESTRUCT_BRACE_TURNS turns. If it survives the countdown it detonates: a
    wall-stopped cardinal cross blast (applySelfDestructBlast) against the player plus a MASSIVE
    toxic cloud (PLAGUE_HULK_SELF_DESTRUCT_TOXIC_RADIUS_TILES). Killing it before detonation leaves
    only the ordinary minimal cloud instead — the EnemyDeathHazardListener carries a
    selfDestructMassive flag so World picks the right radius. Counterplay: "burst it down before the
    countdown hits zero, or get out of the blast radius." Because the detonation removes the enemy
    from inside phaseBExecute's own iteration, the kill is deferred via
    EnemyManager.pendingExecuteDeaths and applied in a second pass after the phaseB loop finishes.
  - BLIGHT_CORRUPTOR — SUMMON (order-5) spawns VORTEX_EYE (not GHOUL) on empty adjacent tiles, each
    spawn marked with a green portal ring-pulse + spark burst (ImpactEventListener.onEnemySpawned).
  - AURIC_SENTINEL — SHARD ECONOMY (.claude/agents/ideas/elemental-golem-auric-sentinel.txt). Its
    orbiting shards are simultaneously its armour and its ammunition, so the counter to its offence and
    the counter to its defence are the SAME action. Implemented as plain state + a damage-pipeline hook,
    NOT as a SpecialAbility verb, so the SpecialAbility catalogue and BalanceSchema.SPECIAL_VERB_PRICING
    are untouched and the verb COVERAGE rule stays green. Four touch points:
      ABSORB  Enemy.applyDamage, at the TOP — ahead of Block and flat armour, so the full mitigation
              order is SHARD -> Block -> armor -> HP. While shardCount > 0 any single incoming damage
              INSTANCE is NULLIFIED (not reduced) and costs one shard, whatever its size: a railgun slug
              and a chaingun tick are worth exactly the same. Because it is per-instance, a weapon that
              resolves as several applyDamage calls (a Chaingun burst, a multi-tile march, an ability's
              bonus hit) strips several shards on one trigger pull — that reward FALLS OUT of the rule
              rather than being special-cased. Enemy.applyDoTDamage deliberately does NOT absorb, so
              burns/poison/hazard fire bite straight into HP: the safety valve that keeps a status build
              from ever being hard-countered.
      SPEND   EnemyManager.executeRangedAttack calls Enemy.spendShardForShot BEFORE the fairness
              re-validation, so a committed shot the player dodges still costs a shard (a real bait).
              With an empty ring the committed shot cannot happen at all — the enemy is disarmed.
      REGROW  EnemyManager.advanceShardRegrowth at the end of every enemy turn (including stunned turns
              and turns it fired), +1 shard per AURIC_SHARD_REGEN_TURNS up to AURIC_MAX_SHARDS, so a
              passive player faces a permanently full ring.
      READ    EnemyRenderer pass 1.55 draws one additive gold quad per LIVE shard orbiting the
              billboard. The ring IS the readability contract — remaining armour and remaining ammo are
              the same number, legible with no HUD, no bar and no text. An absorbed hit plays a GOLD
              flash INSTEAD of the usual white hit flash (and no damage number), which is the single
              most important effect on the archetype: without it the enemy reads as a bullet sponge
              rather than a puzzle. A shardless body drops to a dull violet matte — the "hit it NOW" cue.
    TP pricing: absorption is priced as an ARMOUR POOL (EnemyType.armorPool -> 70), because absorbing a
    hit IS extra effective HP; see BalanceConfig.AURIC_SHARD_ARMOR_POOL for the derivation and for the
    stated RISK that one averaged pool cannot express a loadout-dependent eHP swing.
  - CINDERFORGE_COLOSSUS — COOLING CRUST + MOLTEN TRAIL
    (.claude/agents/ideas/elemental-golem-cinderforge-colossus.txt). THE GAME PUNISHES HESITATION: it
    hardens while you hide and burns the ground you would have retreated across. Like the Sentinel it is
    plain state + two PASSIVE hooks, NOT a SpecialAbility verb, so the SpecialAbility catalogue and
    BalanceSchema.SPECIAL_VERB_PRICING are untouched and the COVERAGE rule stays green. Touch points:
      CRUST   Enemy.crustStacks (0..CINDERFORGE_CRUST_MAX_STACKS). GAIN: at the END of the Colossus's own
              turn (EnemyManager.advanceCrustCooling), if it took NO damage since its previous turn,
              +1 stack, each adding CINDERFORGE_CRUST_ARMOR_PER_STACK to Enemy.armor. LOSE: ANY damage
              shatters the WHOLE shell (Enemy.shatterCrust), removing the armour. The shatter fires in
              BOTH damage entry points — applyDamage AND applyDoTDamage — because "any damage" must
              include a burn/bleed tick, or a status build could not express the counterplay. The crust
              rides on the existing `armor` field, so it shatters LAST (order: SHARD -> [x shatter bonus]
              -> Block -> armor(crust) -> HP -> shatter): the shell protects the very blow that breaks
              it, and breaking a FULLY cooled shell amplifies that hit by CINDERFORGE_CRUST_SHATTER_-
              MULTIPLIER (the payoff for coming back out). Crust only accrues once ALERTED, so a sleeper
              never wakes pre-hardened. TP pricing: TIME-AVERAGED flat reduction (EnemyType.flatReduction
              -> 3, ~1 stack of uptime), because the modelled player attacks every turn — see
              BalanceConfig.CINDERFORGE_CRUST_AVERAGED_FLAT_REDUCTION.
      TRAIL   EnemyManager.executeMove ignites the VACATED tile after the move resolves, via the shipped
              HazardManager.igniteFire(col, row, CINDERFORGE_TRAIL_FIRE_TURNS) — a real 'i' fire tile, so
              it spreads, chain-detonates barrels, and burns ANY host (player AND the Colossus's own
              allies) for free. It never ignites the tile it STANDS on (the player is never denied a melee
              tile without warning), and a per-Colossus live-tile cap (Enemy trail ring buffer,
              CINDERFORGE_TRAIL_MAX_LIVE_TILES) stops a long chase carpeting a floor. The trail's threat
              is priced in the HAZARDS channel (BalanceReport's Fire 'i' row), NOT folded into the enemy
              TP — folding it in both places would double-count it.
      READ    EnemyRenderer: the sprite tint is driven by the crust level (blazing orange at 0 stacks ->
              charcoal at a full shell, eased over CINDERFORGE_TINT_LERP_SECONDS so a shatter FLASHES back
              to hot), plus one dark PLATE per live stack (a literal armour count on the body) with a
              glowing molten seam, plus a heat-haze shimmer at the feet. A cooled Colossus keeps a hot
              orange RIM (CINDERFORGE_RIM_MIN_RED) so "hardened" never reads as "asleep" (the dormant
              dampen). ImpactEventListener adds a quiet ember tell on cooling, a loud ember burst +
              shake + (full-shell) white flash on shatter, a distance-scaled footfall shake, and an ember
              COLLAPSE on death (which also drops one last fire tile, via the death-hazard hook in World).
  - RIMESHELL_LANCER — RIME LANCE + ICE SHELL (.claude/agents/ideas/elemental-golem-rimeshell-lancer.txt).
    ARMOURED OR DANGEROUS, NEVER BOTH: the shell that makes it the toughest non-elite must OPEN for the
    lance to fire, so the puzzle is TIMING — shoot it on the RIGHT turn. Built from the EXISTING
    WIND_UP -> ATTACK pair (exactly like the Shell Brute charge), so NO SpecialAbility verb is added and
    BalanceSchema.SPECIAL_VERB_PRICING / COVERAGE are untouched. Touch points:
      CHARGE  computeLancerPlan: off cooldown (Enemy.lanceCooldownTurns == 0) with a clean cardinal firing
              line, it commits a WIND_UP, LOCKS the lane (Enemy.lanceDirectionColumn/Row, mirroring the
              charger's chargeDirection), and OPENS the shell (Enemy.openShell zeroes the live Enemy.armor)
              for the whole of the following player turn — the punish window. triggerTelegraph draws the
              warning in the existing WIND_UP intent frame with the predicted-damage number.
      FIRE    executeWindUp branches by type to executeLanceBeam: a PIERCING beam swept straight down the
              LOCKED lane out to attackRangeTiles, stopping at the first wall / closed door / solid prop
              (isLanceBlocked), never at a body. It hits the player for the full lance damage ONLY if they
              are still on the committed lane (a sidestep wastes it — the counter, and it works because the
              lane was locked a turn earlier), and splashes every OTHER enemy in the lane for
              RIMESHELL_LANCE_FRIENDLY_FIRE_FRACTION (0.5) — golems do not care about each other, so a pack
              pulled into the lane thins itself out. The shell RESEALS (Enemy.resealShell) and the cooldown
              arms (RIMESHELL_LANCE_COOLDOWN_TURNS) at the end of the turn either way.
      SHELL   Enemy.armor IS the live shell: set to type.baseArmor() (RIMESHELL_SHELL_ARMOR = 7) at spawn,
              zeroed on the charge commit, restored after the beam. Per-INSTANCE; the type-level
              flatReduction() is the TIME-AVERAGED price only (RIMESHELL_SHELL_AVERAGED_FLAT_REDUCTION = 5,
              the shell is down ~1 turn of every ~3) and the two are NEVER wired together.
      READ    EnemyRenderer: a slow cyan FROST pulse while sealed, and a hot orange HEAT wash the whole
              turn the shell is open (Enemy.isShellOpen) — the "hit it NOW" cue, legible even with the
              intent icon off-screen. ImpactEventListener sweeps an orange-white BEAM + muzzle frost ring +
              shake on fire (onLanceBeam), and a cold blue-white SHATTER with one hot core flash on death
              (onFrostShatter).
    TP pricing: an ordinary ranged SOLDIER — eHP through the shared flat-reduction primitive on the
    AVERAGED shell yields TP ~41 (mid-low SOLDIER band); friendly fire and the lane-lock are
    player-favouring and priced at zero. See BalanceConfig.RIMESHELL_SHELL_AVERAGED_FLAT_REDUCTION.
  - VERDANT_SPIRESOWER — SOW SPIRES + LEY-LINE REGEN (.claude/agents/ideas/elemental-golem-verdant-spiresower.txt).
    The first enemy that EDITS THE LEVEL. Built from plain state + a bespoke WIND_UP -> sow hook (the Shell
    Brute charge precedent) plus a damage-pipeline-adjacent regen tick, so NO SpecialAbility verb is added
    and the BalanceSchema COVERAGE rule is untouched. The spire TILES live in hazard/SpireManager (reached
    through the entity/SpireHitTarget seam), NOT on the enemy.
      SOW     computeSowerPlan: on SPIRESOWER_SOW_CADENCE_TURNS, under SPIRESOWER_MAX_LIVE_SPIRES, it
              selectSowTargets (deterministic seeded RNG) up to SPIRESOWER_SPIRES_PER_SOW walkable tiles
              within SPIRESOWER_SOW_RADIUS_TILES, then commits a WIND_UP telegraphed a full player turn
              ahead. A turn later executeWindUp branches by type to executeSow, which re-validates each
              tile and SpireManager.sowSpire stamps a solid ':' crystal spire (SPIRESOWER_SPIRE_HIT_POINTS
              HP, decays after SPIRESOWER_SPIRE_LIFETIME_TURNS). A spire blocks movement, LOS and shots
              (Level.isSpire, checked in isBlockedAt / the enemy LOS + isLanceBlocked / Weapon.isShotBlocking-
              Cover), so it costs ranged golem allies their lanes exactly as it costs the player their
              sightline.
      GUARD   NON-OPTIONAL: selectSowTargets rejects any tile that SpireManager.wouldSealExit reports would
              cut the player off from a level exit — a flood fill from the player with the candidate (and
              the tiles already chosen this sow) treated as solid. A spire that seals a dead end turns a
              puzzle into a softlock; the guard forbids it. Pinned by level.SpireReachabilityTest.
      HEAL    advanceSowerRegen at the END of the sower's turn: +SPIRESOWER_REGEN_PER_SPIRE HP per LIVING
              spire within SPIRESOWER_LEY_RANGE_TILES (capped at maxHealth). "Shoot the golem and it heals;
              shoot the spires and it stops" — the whole decision. A weapon hit that stops on a spire tile
              routes through EnemyHitTarget.damageSpireAt (Weapon.hitSpireCover) into the SpireManager; a
              destroyed spire FLINCHES its sower, and the sower's death WITHERS every spire it planted
              (killEnemy -> shatterSpiresOf), restoring the room.
      READ    livingSpireCount (cached on the enemy) drives the eye-glow scale (dim at zero, blazing at the
              cap); the ley-lines and spire/birth/shatter bursts are procedural (ImpactEventListener
              onSpireBorn/onSpireShattered).
    TP pricing: the regeneration is priced as an ARMOUR POOL (SPIRESOWER_REGEN_ARMOR_POOL = 20) fed to
    GameMath.enemyEffectiveHitPoints, yielding TP ~62 (upper-mid SOLDIER band). The spires' TERRAIN value
    is deliberately UNPRICED — it hurts the golem's own family as much as the player, and the model has no
    positional-denial channel. See BalanceConfig.SPIRESOWER_REGEN_ARMOR_POOL.

All hooks respect the cardinal-line constraint; the charge only fires down a true cardinal lane, and the
lance only ever commits down a true row or column and never re-targets after commit.

TERRAIN HAZARDS (Pillar 3) — HazardManager (hazard pkg) owns a two-sided fire/toxic chain-reaction
system, ticked once per turn by HazardTickSubscriber (before the status tick so a tile bites the
same turn you stand in it). Tiles 'i' (fire) and 'q' (toxic) apply the existing BURNING/POISONED
status to ANY host on them — player AND enemies. Fire spreads along spreadable floor/stain tiles
and chain-detonates explosive barrels (and a detonation can ignite fire back onto its neighbours);
toxic is a static area-denial pool. Knobs in BalanceConfig SECTION 12; per-tile Threat-Point cost
(GameMath.hazardTileThreatPoints) is printed by BalanceReport's HAZARDS section so a hazard room's
contribution to the floor TP is auditable.

TELEGRAPH AUDIT (Pillar 5) — BalanceReport's TELEGRAPH AUDIT section checks every attack against
TELEGRAPH_MAX_UNTELEGRAPHED_HIT_FRACTION (25% of reference eHP ≈ 51 dmg): no UN-telegraphed attack
may exceed it. The only big single hits (Shell Brute charge, boss slams) are telegraphed wind-ups;
ranged attacks are "telegraphed" by the cardinal-line tell. See
.claude/agents/ideas/balance_order_4_tactical_combat_depth.txt.
