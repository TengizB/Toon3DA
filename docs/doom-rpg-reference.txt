# Background reference — game inspiration. Not needed at runtime; agents check this for creative/design decisions.

=== DOOM RPG (2005) ===

Developed by id Software / Fountainhead Entertainment for mobile. Primary design reference.

Visual style:
- Pseudo-3D first-person raycasting. Enemies/objects are billboard sprites always facing player, scaled by distance.
- Flat 2D tile grid. No polygons. Hand-crafted levels. Episodic: UAC facility on Mars.

Movement:
- Cardinal only, one tile at a time. Step forward/backward, strafe left/right, rotate 90° left/right.
- Player always occupies exactly one tile.

Combat (turn-based):
- Every action (step, rotate, attack, item use) costs exactly one turn.
- After player acts, every enemy in level acts once.
- Stat-based — no twitch skill. Weapon weakness per enemy type; correct weapon = bonus damage.

Weapons:
- Fists, Pistol, Shotgun, Super Shotgun, Chainsaw, Rocket Launcher, Plasma Rifle, BFG 9000.
- Improvised melee: Fire Axe, Fire Extinguisher (effective vs specific enemies).
- Chainsaw/fists infinite-use melee. BFG costs many cells.

Enemies:
- Zombie, Sergeant, Imp, Demon, Hell Knight, Baron of Hell, Cacodemon, Lost Soul, Revenant, Arachnotron, Spider Mastermind.
- Each has weapon weakness, attack type (melee/ranged), movement pattern.
- Alert nearby enemies when they spot the player.

Progression & economy:
- XP from kills → level-up → choose Strength / Health Max / Agility.
- Credits from environment → spend at computer terminals (medkits, ammo, upgrades).
- Pet mechanic: mind-control collar captures Hellhound — melee weapon + living shield.

Level design:
- Hand-crafted, episodic (5 episodes). Not procedural.
- Secrets: displaced wall segments identical to solid walls. Searching reveals hidden rooms.
- Colored key cards unlock matching doors. Terminals for shops/lore/hints.
- Environments: UAC offices, maintenance corridors, demon-infested zones, hellscapes.

Inventory:
- Slot-based. Medkit (small/large), Stimpack, Armor Shard, Security Armor, Combat Armor, Backpack.
- Ammo: Bullets, Shells, Rockets, Plasma Cells.
- Special: Berserk Pack (melee ×mult for N turns), Invulnerability Sphere, Radiation Suit.

=== DOOM II RPG (2009) ===

Sequel for iOS. Same raycasting renderer, updated for larger screens. Billboard sprites.

Character classes (chosen at run start):
  Sergeant  | Strength High | Agility Med | IQ Low  | Melee tank, chainsaw specialist
  Major     | Strength Med  | Agility High| IQ Med  | Balanced; best dodge/accuracy
  Scientist | Strength Low  | Agility Low | IQ High | Hacking, bargaining, scouting

Stats:
- Strength: melee damage, carry capacity bonus.
- Agility: dodge chance, attack accuracy.
- IQ: minigame success rate, cheaper shop prices, faster item identification.

Weapons (additions over Doom RPG):
- Holy Water Pistol: two water streams → fear (enemies flee, can't attack, N turns). Refill at any water source.
- BFG: splash damage hits all in a line. Costs 10 plasma cells.
- Improvised: sentry bots (deployable one-use turrets), plumbing fixtures thrown as projectiles, toilets (high damage, humorous).
- Chainsaw damage scales with current Strength.

Enemies:
- 11 base types, multiple colour-coded difficulty variants per type (e.g. Imp → Demon Imp → Hell Imp → Arch Imp).
- 4 end-of-episode bosses. Every class has weapon weakness.

Turn-based system:
- Same one-action-per-turn model. Actions: move N/S/E/W, rotate, attack, use item, talk to NPC, ransack corpse, skip turn.
- Ransacking corpses: yields ammo, credits, occasionally rare items.

Level structure:
- 9 hand-crafted episodes + secret levels.
- Hidden blue crates (require IQ/searching) with rare equipment.
- Pressure plates, floor hazards, trap doors.
- NPC quests with credit/item rewards.

Minigames:
- Hacking: puzzle to unlock terminals. Success scales with IQ.
- Bargaining: negotiate shop prices. Higher IQ = better deals.
- Scout bot: maps nearby area. Range/accuracy scales with IQ.

=== TARGET GENRE: TURN-BASED ROGUELIKE ===

Doom RPG DNA + roguelike structure:
  Perspective     | First-person pseudo-3D raycasting       | —
  Movement        | One tile/turn, cardinal directions       | —
  Combat          | Turn-based, stat + weapon vs enemy type  | —
  Progression     | XP → level-up → stats grow              | Per-run only; death resets
  Levels          | —                                        | Procedurally generated each run
  Continuity      | Fixed episodes, save & resume           | Permadeath — one life per run
  Meta-progress   | —                                        | Unlocks persist across runs
  Loot            | Fixed item placement                    | Randomised drops and shops
  Character       | Fixed protagonist                       | Class selection each run
