================================================================================
 ENGAGEMENT ANALYSIS — toon3D vs. Shattered Pixel Dungeon
 What makes SPD engaging, what toon3D lacks, and what to build next.
 Date: 2026-06-24
================================================================================

PURPOSE
-------
toon3D and Shattered Pixel Dungeon (SPD) share a skeleton: tile grid, turn-based,
roguelike, permadeath, procedural floors. They differ in skin (first-person
raycast shooter vs. top-down sprite crawler). The user's observation is correct:
SPD is gripping and toon3D is not yet. The difference is NOT graphics. It is
STRUCTURE. This document explains why, grounded in toon3D's actual code.


================================================================================
PART 1 — WHAT toon3D ALREADY HAS (and has done well)
================================================================================

toon3D is content-rich and polished at the moment-to-moment level:

  Combat content
    - 10+ weapons: pistol, shotgun, double-barrel, plasma, rocket, chaingun,
      assault rifle, railgun, incinerator, + melee (fist/knife/hammer/chainsaw).
    - 11 enemy types (PLAGUE_HULK, EYE_TYRANT, GORE_BITER, SHELL_BRUTE,
      MIRE_WRAITH, IRON_STALKER, ACID_DRONE, VOID_SHROUD, OVERSEER, CORRUPTOR,
      HELL_BARON) across two AI archetypes (melee bruiser / ranged kiter).
    - Status effects: BURNING, POISONED, BLEED, STUNNED, BLINDED, SLOWED.

  Systems
    - Turn/tick engine (TickEventBus) — clean, correct, extensible.
    - Inventory, ammo as finite resource, weapon pickup/switch.
    - XP + level-up (3 attributes: STRENGTH/AGILITY/TOUGHNESS; 3 boons:
      HP/ARMOR/DAMAGE).
    - Permadeath + death screen, credits/currency, keycard doors.
    - 3 procedural generators (rooms, linear corridor, cellular cave) + stairs.

  Game feel (this is genuinely strong)
    - Impact effects, screen shake, hit vignettes, kill flashes, enemy hit
      reactions, health bars, red-alert pulse, ability feedback visuals.

CONCLUSION: toon3D is not lacking content or polish. It is lacking the
INTERLOCKING SYSTEMS OF MEANINGFUL CHOICE that turn content into a game you
can't put down. SPD has roughly the same amount of "stuff" — but every piece
of stuff feeds a decision.


================================================================================
PART 2 — WHY SHATTERED PIXEL DUNGEON IS ENGAGING (the 7 engines)
================================================================================

SPD's grip comes from seven engines that all feed each other. Each one is a
loop that generates "just one more floor" tension. toon3D is missing or only
half-has most of them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENGINE 1 — A GOAL THAT CREATES AN ARC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPD: descend 26 floors, retrieve the Amulet of Yendor, climb back out. Every
run has a beginning, middle, and end. You always know how far you've come and
how far is left. The arc is the single biggest reason runs feel like stories
instead of sessions.

toon3D: NO WIN CONDITION EXISTS (verified — no victory/finalFloor/amulet code).
You descend stairs forever until you die. There is no destination, so there is
no progress, no climax, no "almost made it." A run is just play-until-death.
THIS IS THE #1 ENGAGEMENT KILLER.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENGINE 2 — BUILD DIVERSITY / SYNERGY (runs feel different)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPD: 4 classes x subclasses, rings, wands, artifacts, enchantments, alchemy.
A run "becomes" something — a frost-wand mage, a thrown-weapon rogue, a
regen-tank warrior. Items COMBINE (Ring of Elements + Wand of Lightning;
Horn of Plenty + hunger build). You discover synergies by experimenting. Each
run develops a distinct identity (this is Pillar 5 in the project's own docs).

toon3D: weapons are essentially a linear power ladder (pistol < shotgun <
plasma...). Attributes are FLAT boosts (HP/ARMOR/DAMAGE — pure upgrades, no
trade-offs). Nothing combines. There is no "build." Whatever you pick up, you
play the same way: face enemy, shoot. The project's own pillars demand
"interesting trade-offs, not pure upgrades" — the current level-up violates
this directly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENGINE 3 — ESCALATING CLIMAXES (bosses)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPD: a boss every 5 floors (Goo, Tengu, DM-300, Dwarf King, Yog). Each is a
distinct puzzle with an arena, a mechanic, and a reward. They chunk the run
into acts and give you a "checkpoint feeling" of mastery.

toon3D: bosses are NOT IMPLEMENTED (boss-encounter-facility-overseer.txt and
roguelike_order_10 both NOT IMPLEMENTED — though an OVERSEER enemy type and
HELL_BARON exist as enum values, there is no boss-floor encounter system).
Floors are tonally identical from floor 1 to floor N. No climax = no pacing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENGINE 4 — MEANINGFUL CHOICE UNDER SCARCITY (the economy)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPD: gold is scarce; shops force agonizing choices (buy the +health potion or
save for the upgrade scroll?). Items are scarce; you can't have everything.
Identify-or-gamble decisions. The hunger clock forces you to keep moving and
spend resources. Every floor poses "spend now or save?".

toon3D: credits/currency exist but there is NO SHOP to spend them on (verified —
roguelike_order_11 shop NOT IMPLEMENTED; the "shop" grep hits are unrelated).
Ammo scarcity exists, which is good, but there is no spend/save tension, no
economy, no opportunity cost. Currency with nothing to buy is a dead system.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENGINE 5 — DISCOVERY & THE IDENTIFICATION META-GAME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPD: potions/scrolls are UNIDENTIFIED. Drinking an unknown potion is a gamble
(healing? or burning?). Over many runs you learn the system, not the layout.
Plus secret rooms, special floor variants, locked vaults — exploration is
always rewarded with a chance of something.

toon3D: everything is known. A medkit is a medkit, ammo is ammo. No unidentified
items, no gambles, no "what's behind the locked door" payoff. Secret rooms NOT
IMPLEMENTED. Exploration yields nothing surprising, so there is no reason to
explore carefully — you just walk to the stairs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENGINE 6 — TACTICAL TERRAIN (emergent combat)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPD: combat is positional. Water conducts lightning and douses fire; fire
spreads through grass; chasms drop enemies a floor; toxic gas fills rooms;
caltrops, traps, doorways as chokepoints. Two enemies with the same HP play
completely differently because of how they interact with terrain and each other.

toon3D: combat is "stand in line, shoot, enemy walks at you." The cardinal-line
ranged rule is a nice constraint, but there is no terrain interaction. The
hazard-chain-reaction system (fire/toxic/explosive) is IN PROGRESS and is the
single most promising lever toon3D already has pointed at this engine — it
should be finished. Right now enemies are mostly HP sponges differentiated by
stats, not by tactics.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENGINE 7 — META-PROGRESSION (the reason to come back after death)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPD: badges, unlockable subclasses, alchemy recipes, a growing journal. Death
is never wasted — you always unlocked or learned something. This is what makes
permadeath bearable and makes you hit "new run" instantly.

toon3D: StatsStore only saves raw stats (load/save/update — no unlock keys).
salvage-cache-meta-progression.txt is NOT IMPLEMENTED. Death gives you nothing
permanent. There is no pull back into the game after a loss.


================================================================================
PART 3 — THE CORE DIAGNOSIS
================================================================================

SPD is engaging because of a CHAIN OF DECISIONS, not because of its art:

  goal (descend to Amulet)
    -> forces you forward, creating an arc
  build diversity (items/classes that synergize)
    -> makes each run a different puzzle you must solve
  scarce economy + identification gamble
    -> makes every pickup and every shop a real decision
  terrain-driven tactics
    -> makes every fight a positioning problem, not a damage race
  boss climaxes every few floors
    -> chunks the run into memorable acts
  meta-progression
    -> makes death productive and pulls you back

toon3D has built the BODY (combat, rendering, enemies, weapons, feel) but not
the NERVOUS SYSTEM (the decision chain above). The player has nothing to decide
except "which weapon kills fastest," and no reason to care whether they reach
floor 5 or floor 50. Engagement comes from MEANINGFUL CHOICE UNDER PRESSURE
TOWARD A GOAL. toon3D currently has pressure (enemies, ammo) but no meaningful
choice and no goal.


================================================================================
PART 4 — WHAT toon3D LACKS (ranked by engagement impact)
================================================================================

TIER 1 — RUN STRUCTURE (build these first; they create the "game")
  1. WIN CONDITION + FINAL FLOOR. A reachable goal (e.g. "reach Sector 10 and
     destroy the core / escape"). Without this nothing else has stakes.
     -> roguelike_order_19_win_condition_and_final_floor.txt (NOT IMPLEMENTED)
  2. DIFFICULTY / FLOOR SCALING. Enemies, density, and loot must escalate so
     depth means something and creates real pressure.
     -> roguelike_order_16_difficulty_and_floor_scaling.txt (NOT IMPLEMENTED)
  3. BOSS FLOORS. A climax every N floors to chunk the run into acts.
     -> boss-encounter-facility-overseer.txt, roguelike_order_10 (NOT IMPLEMENTED)

TIER 2 — MEANINGFUL CHOICE (this is what makes it re-playable)
  4. BUILD DIVERSITY. Replace flat HP/ARMOR/DAMAGE boons with branching, mutually
     exclusive upgrades that change HOW you play and that SYNERGIZE with weapons
     (e.g. "Overload: plasma chains to adjacent enemies" vs "Executioner: +50%
     damage to bleeding targets"). Weapon levels/tiers/abilities are designed
     but NOT IMPLEMENTED. This converts the linear power ladder into builds.
     -> weapon-levels-tiers-abilities.txt, levelup-stim-boon-choice-progression.txt
  5. SHOP / ECONOMY. Give credits a purpose: a merchant room each act with a
     scarce, tempting inventory. Creates spend-vs-save tension.
     -> roguelike_order_11_shop_and_merchant_room.txt (NOT IMPLEMENTED)
  6. ARTIFACTS / RELICS. Rare, run-defining passive items with trade-offs (the
     SPD ring/artifact slot). The single best generator of "build identity."
     -> roguelike_order_14_artifacts_and_special_relics.txt (NOT IMPLEMENTED)

TIER 3 — DISCOVERY & TACTICS (depth and texture)
  7. FINISH HAZARD CHAIN REACTIONS (fire/toxic/explosive terrain). The highest-
     value half-built system — turns fights into positioning puzzles.
     -> hazard-chain-reactions-fire-toxic-explosive.txt (IN PROGRESS)
  8. SECRET ROOMS + ENVIRONMENTAL TRAPS. Reward careful exploration.
     -> secret-rooms, pressure-plate-trap-tiles, roguelike_order_12/13 (NOT IMPL)
  9. UNIDENTIFIED ITEMS (sci-fi flavor: unmarked vials / salvaged tech that
     must be used or scanned to ID). Adds the gamble meta-game. (Not yet designed
     — would be a new idea doc; fits Pillar 7 "Information management".)

TIER 4 — RETENTION (the reason to relaunch)
  10. META-PROGRESSION / UNLOCKS. Permanent unlocks funded by salvage so death
      is productive.
      -> salvage-cache-meta-progression.txt (NOT IMPLEMENTED)
  11. RUN STATISTICS / LEADERBOARD + MAIN MENU / NEW RUN SETUP. Framing that
      makes a run feel like an attempt at something.
      -> roguelike_order_17 / _18 (NOT IMPLEMENTED)

TIER 5 — SECONDARY POLISH
  12. ENEMY VARIETY WIRING. 11 types exist but enemy-variety-and-archetypes is
      still IN PROGRESS; ensure each type has a DISTINCT TACTICAL ROLE (flanker,
      summoner, exploder, support) not just different HP/damage numbers.
      -> roguelike_order_7 (IN PROGRESS), lurker/sentry/resonant-pylon (NOT IMPL)
  13. FOG OF WAR on the minimap (the first-person view is already a natural fog,
      but the minimap reveals too much).
      -> fog-of-war-minimap.txt (NOT IMPLEMENTED)


================================================================================
PART 5 — RECOMMENDED SEQUENCE (smallest change -> biggest engagement gain)
================================================================================

If only three things get built, build these, in order:

  STEP 1 — GIVE THE RUN A GOAL AND A SHAPE.
    Win condition + final floor (#1) + floor scaling (#2) + one boss (#3).
    Effort: medium. Payoff: enormous. This alone converts "play until death"
    into "a run with stakes." Nothing else matters until the run has an arc.

  STEP 2 — MAKE CHOICES MATTER.
    Branching synergistic level-up boons / weapon abilities (#4) + a shop (#5).
    Effort: medium. Payoff: this is what creates replayability and "builds."
    Now two runs feel different and every pickup is a decision.

  STEP 3 — MAKE FIGHTS TACTICAL.
    Finish hazard chain reactions (#7) and give 3-4 enemies distinct tactical
    roles (#12). Effort: low-medium (much is half-built). Payoff: combat stops
    being a damage race and becomes positioning.

Everything in Tier 4 (meta-progression, leaderboard, menu) is the retention
layer — valuable, but only after a single run is fun end-to-end. Do not build
retention for a loop that isn't yet engaging.


================================================================================
ONE-SENTENCE TAKEAWAY
================================================================================
Shattered Pixel Dungeon is engaging because every floor forces a meaningful
decision on the way to a clear goal; toon3D has built a great-feeling combat
sandbox but no goal to reach, no build to shape, and no economy or terrain to
make choices matter — so add a WIN CONDITION, SYNERGISTIC UPGRADES + A SHOP, and
TERRAIN-DRIVEN COMBAT, in that order, before anything else.
