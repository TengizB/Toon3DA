# 05 — Descent Structure

The reveal is staged top-to-bottom so the player's *understanding rots gradually* —
clean at the surface, grey by the labs, inverted by the wound, undeniable at the core.
The enemies never change; only what you know about them does. This maps onto the
existing route-map regions; region names below are narrative placeholders.

The rule of the staging: **the Organization owns the story at the top; the planet
takes it back on the way down.** Every stratum, the briefing loses a little authority
and the Choir gains a little voice.

## Region 1 — The Habitation Rings *(surface strata)*
**Understanding: clean good vs. evil.**

- Mood: an evacuated company town gone wrong. Familiar, corporate, recently lived-in.
- Enemies: **undead** (risen crew), **machine** (rogue drones).
- The planet: silent. At most, *wrongness* — a watched feeling, a sign misread for a
  beat.
- The Organization: **procedural.** Flat directives. You are equipment.
- What you believe: the briefing, entirely. Contaminant below, purge it, save the
  surface. You are the good guy and everything confirms it.
- Seed for later: a few undead wear operator serials close to your own. You don't
  register it yet.

## Region 2 — The Harvesting Galleries *(facility strata)*
**Understanding: the first crack.**

- Mood: industrial abattoir. Something was being *grown and cut* here at scale.
- Enemies: **insect** (escaped specimens), more **machine**.
- The planet: first **whispers** — single wrong-language words, dismissible.
- The Organization: **corrective.** It flags "auditory artifacts," blames the deep,
  tells you to disregard.
- What you learn: the specimens weren't invaders — they were **harvested from the
  planet's own tissue.** Logs describe extraction, yield, tolerances — the language of
  a factory, not a defense. First real doubt: *who was doing what to whom?*

## Region 3 — The Choir Labs *(mid-deep strata)*
**Understanding: the frame starts to invert.**

- Mood: the resurrection heart of the Deepworks — Cradle banks, pattern archives.
- Enemies: **machine** (failed Cradle-prints — wrong, half-formed *you*s), **undead**.
- The planet: **address.** It speaks to you in full for the first time, and it knows
  you — names you by what you did, not by your serial. It begins handing back memory.
- The Organization: **coercive.** It threatens the Cradle — *complete the objective or
  no reprint will be authorized.* The mask of "for your benefit" comes off.
- What you learn: **how you come back** — the Choir, the Cradle, that you are printed
  from a pattern the planet holds. And that **you are not an external operator.** You
  worked here. The block starts to fail.

## Region 4 — The Wound *(deep strata)*
**Understanding: full inversion.**

- Mood: no longer facility. The Deepworks gives way to the being's raw body, drills
  still buried in living tissue.
- Enemies: **demon** (immune response, pain) and **golem** (immune response, body),
  thick and rising. You are attacking a body's defenses and it starts to *feel* wrong.
- The planet: near-**communion.** It shows you the Catastrophe — that its immune
  response killed your original self, and that it has been hunting *you specifically*
  all along, and now grasps that you don't remember, and grieves.
- The Organization: **denial.** It begins writing you off — pattern unrecoverable,
  reprint authorization pending revocation.
- What you learn: the monsters at depth are the being's **pain and body**, not deeper
  evil. And the real shape of the "kill order": you were never sent to kill it — you
  were sent to **re-chain** it, because the Organization needs it alive and screaming
  to keep the Cradles running (see `04-the-organization.md`).

## Region 5 — The Core *(the being's mind)*
**Understanding: everything, and a choice with no clean answer.**

- Mood: inside the mind itself. Not a boss arena — a confrontation with a truth.
- The planet: total **communion.** It gives you the last blocked memory: the discovery,
  the liberation, your death by its own hand. You know what you were, what you did,
  what they did to you, and what the mission really is.
- The Organization: its final transmission — the revocation, the threat, or silence.
- What happens: no monster to defeat resolves this. **You choose** (see
  `06-endings.md`). Each choice costs something real. None is marked correct.

## Pacing note

The five regions above are narrative acts, not level counts — the route map can string
many procedurally-generated floors within each act. The **beats that must land in
order** are: (1) belief, (2) first doubt at the harvest, (3) "I am the one who comes
back / I worked here" at the labs, (4) "the monsters are the victim / the order is
re-chaining" at the wound, (5) "I caused this / I died freeing it / now I choose" at
the core. Everything else is texture.
