# The Planet — Voice

**Voice:** vast, slow, non-human, and — for most of the game — *not comforting and not
clearly trustworthy.* It is grief and accusation before it is mercy. It addresses you
by **what you did**, never by your serial. It recognizes you from the first step but
stays silent until you are deep enough, near enough its core, for it to reach past the
Organization's block.

Four stages (see `03-the-planet.md`). The voice should feel like *remembering*, not
like being spoken to — as if the words are surfacing from inside you, because they are.

---

## Stage 1 — Wrongness *(Region 1)*
**No voice. Environment only.** The planet cannot speak this shallow. It can only make
the world feel watched.

- A cleared corridor *feels* occupied when you re-enter it.
- A wall sign reads, for one frame, as a word it is not — then corrects.
- Ambient: a low resonance under the machinery that stops when you stop moving.

(No text lines. This stage is entirely design/audio. Its absence is the point.)

---

## Stage 2 — Whisper *(Region 2)*
Single words, wrong-language, half-heard. Easy to blame on the deep. Ambiguous: saving
you, or getting into you?

- "…down…"
- "…again…"
- "…you *smell* the same…"
- "…I know this weight…"  *(said of your footsteps)*
- "…not the first time you came to cut me…"

The player should be unsure whether to be afraid of the voice. Good.

---

## Stage 3 — Address *(Region 3: The Reliquary)*
It knows you now. Not by serial — by deed. Still short, broken lines, delivered a few at
a time. It starts handing back memory.

- "There you are. New face. Same weight."
- "You know what they burn to wake you? Me."
- "A piece of me. Every time you die."
- "You are not from the surface. You worked here."
- "They took part of you, too. I kept it. I never forgot."

The turns to land: the player is fuelled by the planet's stolen soul, and what the
planet returns is its *own memory* of you — it never forgot. Keep every line short.

---

## Stage 4 — Communion *(Region 4 → Core)*
It gives you everything — but still in short, plain lines, one after another. Here, and
only here, it can finally be gentle, because now you both know the same grief.

- "You found me. Not a seam. Me."
- "You heard me screaming. No one else did."
- "You set me free once. For one moment I could move."
- "I could not tell your hands from theirs. I killed everything. I killed you."
- "They built you again. They cut out the part that would say no."
- "They don't want me dead. Dead, I bleed nothing."
- "They want me alive. Held. Forever. So they can keep taking pieces."
- "So here you are. Same as before. A weapon at my heart."
- "I won't stop you. I only wanted you to know this time."
- "That's all I ever wanted. To be known."

---

## Writing rules

- **Never grammatical or clean early; never fully monstrous late.** The arc is
  wrongness → suspicion → recognition → grief. Track it.
- **It addresses the deed, never the serial.** "The one who opened me," "you who came
  to cut me," "you who set me free." This is the deliberate mirror of the
  Organization's "Operator #serial" — same person, two names, the identity theme in one
  contrast.
- **It never resolves the identity question.** It calls you the *same* one — that is
  its belief, its grief needs you to be continuous. It is not proof. The Organization's
  "you are a copy" is equally unproven. Keep both unconfirmed
  (`07-themes-and-questions.md`).
- **Restraint.** Cosmic horror dies of over-talking. Fewer, heavier lines. Let silence
  and environment carry Stage 1 entirely.
