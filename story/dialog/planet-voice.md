# The Planet — Voice

**Voice:** vast, slow, non-human, and — for most of the game — *not comforting and not
clearly trustworthy.* It is grief and accusation before it is mercy. It addresses you
by **what you did**, never by your serial. It recognizes you from the first step but
stays silent until the field is deep enough to reach past the Organization's block.

Four stages (see `03-the-planet.md`). The voice should feel like *remembering*, not
like being spoken to — as if the words are surfacing from inside you, because they are.

---

## Stage 1 — Wrongness *(Region 1)*
**No voice. Environment only.** The planet cannot speak this shallow. It can only make
the world feel watched.

- A cleared corridor *feels* occupied when you re-enter it.
- A wall sign reads, for one frame, as a word it is not — then corrects.
- Ambient: a low resonance under the machinery that stops when you stop moving.

(No text lines. This stage is entirely design/audio. Its absence is the point.)

---

## Stage 2 — Whisper *(Region 2)*
Single words, wrong-language, half-heard. Easy to blame on the deep. Ambiguous: saving
you, or getting into you?

- "…down…"
- "…again…"
- "…you *smell* the same…"
- "…I know this weight…"  *(said of your footsteps)*
- "…not the first time you came to cut me…"

The player should be unsure whether to be afraid of the voice. Good.

---

## Stage 3 — Address *(Region 3: Choir Labs)*
It speaks in full and it clearly knows you. Not by serial — by deed. It begins handing
back memory.

- "There you are. Wearing new skin, carrying their errand. I would know you in any
  body."
- "They print you from *me*. Did you know that? You die and you fall into my dreaming
  and they fish you back out and call it a machine. Every death, you come home to me
  first."
- "You are not from the surface. You worked here. Your hands know these halls. Ask them."
- "They took a part of you before they sent you down. I have it. I have kept it. Come
  deeper and I will give it back — even the part you will wish I had not."

Note the turn at the end of that last line: the planet is not simply comforting. It is
returning a truth that will *hurt* the player, and it says so.

---

## Stage 4 — Communion *(Region 4 → Core)*
It gives you everything. The Catastrophe, your death, the truth of the Cradles and the
"kill order." Here — and only here — the voice can finally be tender, because now you
both know the same grief.

- "You found me. Not the seam they told you. *Me.* You were the only one who ever
  looked at the drilling and heard it screaming."
- "You tried to set me free. You *did* set me free — for one moment I could move — and
  I did not know your hands from theirs. I killed everything that touched me. I killed
  you. I have carried that with your pattern ever since."
- "They rebuilt you without the part that would refuse, and they told you to put the
  chains back on. They do not want me dead. Dead, I cannot dream them their second
  lives. They want me *awake* and *hurting*, forever, because my pain is deep enough to
  fish the dead out of."
- "So here you are again, at my heart, with a weapon. Same as before. And I will not
  stop you, whichever thing you choose. I only wanted you to *know* this time. That is
  all I ever wanted. To be known."

---

## Writing rules

- **Never grammatical or clean early; never fully monstrous late.** The arc is
  wrongness → suspicion → recognition → grief. Track it.
- **It addresses the deed, never the serial.** "The one who opened me," "you who came
  to cut me," "you who set me free." This is the deliberate mirror of the
  Organization's "Operator #serial" — same person, two names, the identity theme in one
  contrast.
- **It never resolves the identity question.** It calls you the *same* one — that is
  its belief, its grief needs you to be continuous. It is not proof. The Organization's
  "you are a copy" is equally unproven. Keep both unconfirmed
  (`07-themes-and-questions.md`).
- **Restraint.** Cosmic horror dies of over-talking. Fewer, heavier lines. Let silence
  and environment carry Stage 1 entirely.
