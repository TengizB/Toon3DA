# Log Fragments

Recovered staff and operator logs, seeded per region. These are the **breadcrumb**
layer — the reveal that arrives through the *world* rather than through the planet's
voice or the Organization's orders. They carry the facts the Organization will never
confess: the soul harvest, the truth of the Cradles, the fact that the captive being is
the fuel, and — at the bottom — your own original words.

**Delivery TBD** (audio diaries, text terminals, environmental). For now these are
tone-and-content drafts. Author them as written by *people*, with the small human
textures that make the horror land: routine, fatigue, denial, complicity.

---

## Region 1 — Habitation Rings
Ordinary people, not yet alarmed. Seeds that read as nothing now.

- **Shift log, ring maintenance:** "Third quake this cycle. Geo says the seam's
  'settling.' Settling. It's been settling for ten years. Anyway — filters need
  changing on 4."
- **Personal, unsent:** "The new operators keep washing out below the galleries.
  Company just prints more. I don't ask where the patterns come from anymore. You stop
  asking, down here. It's better."

## Region 2 — Harvesting Galleries
The first true crack. The language of a factory, applied to a body.

- **Yield report:** "Extraction down 4% this quarter. Tissue's scarring faster than we
  can cut. Recommend deeper galleries. The material does not run out — it only gets
  harder to reach. It heals. Note that: it *heals.*"
- **Tech log, uneasy:** "The specimens scream on the same frequency as the ambient
  resonance. Whole gallery, same note. I asked the biologist. She said don't file that.
  I'm filing it."

## Region 3 — The Reliquary
How you come back. What the Cradles are. That the captive being is the fuel, stated by
the people who engineered it.

- **Cradle engineer:** "Every print draws down the reserve. I want this on record
  because no one will say it in a meeting: the 'reserve' is the subject. We are not
  running the Cradles on some inert extract — we are burning *it*, the living thing,
  ounce by ounce, and it does not grow back fast. Keep it alive, keep it chained, keep
  it yielding. That is the whole business. Our immortality is measured in what we take
  out of it. We should at least admit that to ourselves."
- **Archivist:** "Pattern EX-[REDACTED] flagged: recovered from the Catastrophe event,
  high deep-strata competence, unmatched survivability. Approved for reprint. Memory
  redaction: mandatory, regions tagged 'liberation,' 'core sabotage,' 'terminal.' Do
  not print with those intact. It will not cooperate if it remembers."

*(That last log is about you. The player may not realize it on first read.)*

## Region 4 — The Wound
Few logs survive this deep; the ones that do are the last honest voices.

- **Final security transmission:** "Reactor's not the problem. There's no contaminant.
  We drilled into something that can *feel it*, and it woke up, and everything since is
  it trying to get us off. We're not being invaded. We're being *removed.* God, we're
  the infection. We were always the—" *(ends)*

## Region 5 — The Core
Your own original logs. The deepest breadcrumb: the protagonist's own voice, before the
block, before the death. The player reads themselves.

- **Operator log, unlabeled, timestamp pre-Catastrophe:** "I've checked it a hundred
  ways and it's not noise. The core resonance answers the drilling. It *anticipates* it.
  It flinches before the bit lands. That's not a seam. That's a nerve. I've been helping
  torture something older than the sky and calling it a shift."
- **Operator log, final entry before the Catastrophe:** "If anyone finds this — I'm
  going to try to free it. The restraints on the core, the harvest clamps, all of it.
  It'll probably kill me. It doesn't know me from the drills; why would it. But I would
  rather die being the one thing down here that tried, than live being one more hand on
  the knife. If it wakes, tell it — if it can be told anything — that not all of us
  couldn't hear it."

*(Read at the core, with the block already lifted, this is the player learning these
were their own words — and that the being *did* hear, and kept them, and is standing
around you now.)*

## Writing rules

- **The Organization never authored a log that confesses its motive** — its own people
  do, in guilt, off the record. The institution stays clean-handed in its official
  voice (`organization-comms.md`); only individuals break.
- **Seed identity late, never announce it.** The "Pattern EX-[REDACTED]" log and the
  final operator log are *about the player*, but nothing points at it. Let the player
  connect it themselves, ideally on a second read.
- **Human texture is the horror delivery mechanism.** Filters on level 4, don't-file-
  that, "you stop asking down here" — the banality is what makes the atrocity land.
