# Log Fragments

Recovered staff and operator logs, seeded per region. They carry the facts the
Organization will never confess: the soul harvest, the truth of the Cradles, the captive
being as the fuel, and — at the bottom — your own original words.

**Delivery: these are CODEX text (opt-in).** Per `08-storytelling-delivery.md`, the
player does not read these on the floor. When a log is picked up, **the AI gives a
one-line take** (the bark the player actually hears), and the full text below is filed in
the codex for players who choose to dig. So every entry has two parts:

- **LOG** — the full text (codex only; written as a real person: routine, fatigue,
  denial, complicity).
- **AI take** — the single line the AI says out loud on pickup. Short, plain, often a
  curiosity gap. This is what carries the beat to a non-reader.

## Region 1 — Habitation Rings
Ordinary people, not yet alarmed. Seeds that read as nothing now.

- **LOG — Shift log, ring maintenance:** "Third quake this cycle. Geo says the seam's
  'settling.' Settling. It's been settling for ten years. Anyway — filters need
  changing on 4."
  - **AI take:** "Maintenance gripes. Ten years of earthquakes they keep calling
    'settling.' ...Anyway. Filters."
- **LOG — Personal, unsent:** "The new operators keep washing out below the galleries.
  Company just prints more. I don't ask where the patterns come from anymore. You stop
  asking, down here. It's better."
  - **AI take:** "Someone's diary. 'Don't ask where the patterns come from.' ...Huh.
    I've genuinely never asked either."

## Region 2 — Harvesting Galleries
The first true crack. The language of a factory, applied to a body.

- **LOG — Yield report:** "Extraction down 4% this quarter. Tissue's scarring faster
  than we can cut. Recommend deeper galleries. The material does not run out — it only
  gets harder to reach. It heals. Note that: it *heals.*"
  - **AI take:** "It's a mining report. Except the thing they're mining... heals. Rock
    doesn't heal. Moving on. I'd like to move on."
- **LOG — Tech log, uneasy:** "The specimens scream on the same frequency as the ambient
  resonance. Whole gallery, same note. I asked the biologist. She said don't file that.
  I'm filing it."
  - **AI take:** "The bugs and the room hum the same note. Somebody was told not to
    write that down. So they wrote it down. Good for them."

## Region 3 — The Reliquary
How you come back. What the Cradles are. That the captive being is the fuel, stated by
the people who engineered it.

- **LOG — Cradle engineer:** "Every print draws down the reserve. I want this on record
  because no one will say it in a meeting: the 'reserve' is the subject. We are not
  running the Cradles on some inert extract — we are burning *it*, the living thing,
  ounce by ounce, and it does not grow back fast. Keep it alive, keep it chained, keep
  it yielding. That is the whole business. Our immortality is measured in what we take
  out of it. We should at least admit that to ourselves."
  - **AI take:** *(quiet)* "...I read it. The thing they burn to bring you back. It's
    the planet. It's alive. We knew that one already, didn't we. I just didn't want to."
- **LOG — Archivist:** "Pattern EX-[REDACTED] flagged: recovered from the Catastrophe
  event, high deep-strata competence, unmatched survivability. Approved for reprint.
  Memory redaction: mandatory — regions tagged 'liberation,' 'core sabotage,' 'terminal.'
  Do not print with those intact. It will not cooperate if it remembers."
  - **AI take:** "Some operator they rebuilt with the memory cut out. Deep-strata
    veteran. Survives anything. ...Survives anything. Huh. Remind you of anyone?"

*(That archivist log is about YOU. The AI take dangles it without saying so — the player
connects it, ideally on a second read.)*

## Region 4 — The Wound
Few logs survive this deep; the ones that do are the last honest voices.

- **LOG — Final security transmission:** "Reactor's not the problem. There's no
  contaminant. We drilled into something that can *feel it*, and it woke up, and
  everything since is it trying to get us off. We're not being invaded. We're being
  *removed.* God, we're the infection. We were always the—" *(ends)*
  - **AI take:** "There's no contaminant. There never was. We're the thing it's trying
    to get rid of. ...I've been calling *you* the cure this whole time."

## Region 5 — The Core
Your own original logs. The deepest breadcrumb: the protagonist's own voice, before the
block, before the death. The player reads themselves.

- **LOG — Operator log, pre-Catastrophe:** "I've checked it a hundred ways and it's not
  noise. The core answers the drilling. It *flinches* before the bit lands. That's not a
  seam. That's a nerve. I've been helping torture something older than the sky and
  calling it a shift."
  - **AI take:** "This one's old. Same operator, over and over. ...Wait. This
    handwriting. This is you. This was always you."
- **LOG — Operator log, final entry:** "If anyone finds this — I'm going to try to free
  it. The clamps, the restraints, all of it. It'll probably kill me. It doesn't know me
  from the drills. But I'd rather die as the one thing down here that tried than live as
  one more hand on the knife. If it wakes — tell it not all of us couldn't hear it."
  - **AI take:** *(very quiet)* "...You already did this once. You freed it. It killed
    you. And they brought you back to put it in chains again. Oh. Oh, no."

*(Read at the core, with the block already lifted, this is the player learning these
were their own words — and that the being *did* hear, and kept them, and is standing
around you now.)*

## Writing rules

- **The Organization never authored a log that confesses its motive** — its own people
  do, in guilt, off the record. The institution stays clean-handed in its official
  voice (`organization-comms.md`); only individuals break.
- **Seed identity late, never announce it.** The "Pattern EX-[REDACTED]" log and the
  final operator log are *about the player*, but nothing points at it. Let the player
  connect it themselves, ideally on a second read.
- **Human texture is the horror delivery mechanism.** Filters on level 4, don't-file-
  that, "you stop asking down here" — the banality is what makes the atrocity land.
- **The AI take is the real delivery; the LOG is the reward.** Write the take first —
  it must carry the beat alone, because most players only hear the take. The full log is
  for the codex divers, and picking one up should also pay them (a cache, a map secret,
  a small buff) per `08` lever #3.
- **Keep AI takes to one or two short lines, plain words.** Same rules as every bark
  (`ai-assistant.md`).
